# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

```
          RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 3 p.m., on Tuesday, September 26, 2023.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 36 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, September 26, 2023, at 3 p.m.
```
