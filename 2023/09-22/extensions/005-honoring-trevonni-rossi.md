# HONORING TREVONNI ROSSI

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        HONORING TREVONNI ROSSI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 22, 2023

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Trevonni Rossi 
from Canon City for his acceptance into the Juilliard Summer Dance 
Intensive, an exclusive and highly competitive program.
  Even at his young age, Mr. Rossi has an extensive dance career that 
extends far beyond his years. He was named Mr. Dance America at a 
prestigious regional dance competition for two consecutive years, 
earned the World Dance Championship Finalist award in 2019, and ranked 
fourth in the world at the Soloist World Dance Pageant in 2022.
  Mr. Rossi's relentless drive, passion, and exceptional skills have 
been the driving force behind his success. It is an honor for me to 
extend my heartfelt congratulations to him on his well-deserved 
acceptance to the prestigious Juilliard Summer Dance Intensive. As he 
begins this exciting journey, our community is wishing him the best and 
waiting to see all he achieves in the future.

                          ____________________
```
