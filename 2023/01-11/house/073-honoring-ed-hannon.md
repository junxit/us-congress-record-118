# HONORING ED HANNON

**Speaking:** Mooney, Alexander X. (M001195) [R-WV]

```
                           HONORING ED HANNON

  (Mr. MOONEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MOONEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to highlight the incredible 
volunteer work of my constituent, Ed Hannon, of Charles Town, West 
Virginia.
  Ed began volunteering for fire and rescue services in 1978 at the age 
of 16. Ed took a job as a paid first responder in Arlington, Virginia, 
in 1983 but continued to give his time volunteering.
  When he moved to Jefferson County in 1993, he joined the Citizens 
Fire Company. Jefferson County has no paid fire and EMS personnel, so 
they rely on dedicated volunteers like Ed to provide these critical 
services.
  Ed ran his final calls on December 31, completing 44 years of 
dedicated volunteer service. I wish Ed well in retirement and thank him 
for his service to the citizens of Jefferson County.

                          ____________________
```
