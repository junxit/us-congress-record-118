# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5983

**Measures:** H.R. 5983

```
            By Mr. LARSEN of Washington:
        H.R. 5983.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Federal Water Pollution Control Act to restore a 
     national minimum standard of protection for the water 
     resources of the United States
```
