# HONORING WE ARE THE 22

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                         HONORING WE ARE THE 22

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. HILL. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to honor ``We Are The 
22'', an Arkansas-based veteran suicide intervention group.
  ``We Are The 22'' was founded in 2017 by Mikel Brooks, a Little Rock 
resident and decorated Army veteran with two tours in Afghanistan.
  Brooks, who had become addicted to pain medications in 2009 while 
recovering from injuries sustained in combat, founded the group 
following his own suicide attempt.
  In just six years, ``We Are The 22'' has successfully responded to 
more than 430 calls from troubled veterans, has over 130 highly trained 
volunteers, and has become a trusted resource for local and state 
authorities.
  I am proud to recognize the work of this dedicated organization and 
hope that they continue to serve as a symbol of hope for all veterans 
who may be struggling.
  I thank Mr. Brooks for his service to our Nation and state.

                          ____________________
```
