# CONGRATULATING SENIORS FROM RIVERVIEW HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
           CONGRATULATING SENIORS FROM RIVERVIEW HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. HILL. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to congratulate 21 
rising and graduated seniors from Riverview High School in Searcy.
  Each of these gifted students have taken and passed a dual-credit 
history course from their high school in partnership with Arkansas 
State University-Beebe.
  With this complete, they will already be ahead in their college 
education and be set for success wherever they may go.
  I was delighted to welcome these talented students to our Nation's 
Capital just a few weeks ago and show them the history of the United 
States firsthand.
  To make the trip, these young men and women tirelessly raised the 
funds themselves by working concession stands and hosting car washes.
  Their hard work inside and outside of the classroom will ensure that 
they achieve excellence in their future endeavors.
  It's wonderful to see the hard work of our students and I wish them 
continued success in their future education.

                          ____________________
```
