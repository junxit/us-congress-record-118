# MICHAEL SCHENCK

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                            MICHAEL SCHENCK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, this month, it was my absolute 
honor to attend the retirement party of Master Gunnery Sergeant Michael 
Schenck. Born a South Jersey native, Schenck embarked on his remarkable 
Marine Corps journey in 1995. From early days at Parris Island, he rose 
through the ranks with fierce dedication. While deployed to Okinawa as 
his first duty station, he quickly excelled and earned promotions. 
After transferring to Camp Lejeune, he became Corporal and served in 
the 22nd Marine Expeditionary Unit across Europe and Africa. His 
journey continued as he joined G Battery, achieved Staff Sergeant, and 
served as a Civil Affairs Team Leader during Operation Iraqi Freedom. 
He further contributed to logistics planning and earned Gunnery 
Sergeant rank in 2006. His roles expanded, leading as the military-to-
military chief for operations across Europe and Africa. Mastering 
logistics, he became Master Sergeant in 2011 and was promoted to Master 
Gunnery Sergeant in 2015. His awards, including the Legion of Merit and 
Navy Commendation Medal, testify to his exceptional service and 
commitment. Michael Schenck's journey epitomizes Marine Corps values 
and excellence and I hope he enjoys his retirement with his wife and 
two daughters. God Bless Master Gunnery Sergeant Michael Schenck and 
God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
