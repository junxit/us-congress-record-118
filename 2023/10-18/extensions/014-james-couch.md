# JAMES COUCH

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                              JAMES COUCH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. HILL. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to congratulate James 
Couch, who is one of only two students from Arkansas to receive a 
Horatio Alger scholarship this year.
  The scholarship program is one of the largest in the country, 
providing educational funding to exceptional students who have overcome 
significant adversity.
  Mr. Couch just finished his junior year at North Little Rock High 
School where he certainly proved to be an exceptional student.
  His involvements include Student Council President, Little Rock Kappa 
League President, and member of Beta Club, Math Honors Society, Key 
Club, and National Honor Society. He also placed third in the state 
wrestling competition.
  In an interview with Arkansas Democrat Gazette, Mr. Couch talked 
about the application process. He wrote about his definition of the 
American dream and losing a loved one, his aunt. I commend his 
dedication to write about these important moments of his life.
  He commented that his guidance counselor motivated him to apply for 
the scholarship. This kind of mentorship and encouragement truly makes 
a difference in schools across our state.
  Mr. Couch hopes to attend college in Washington, D.C. and ultimately 
become a surgeon. What an exceptional goal to dedicate one's life to 
caring for others.
  Congratulations again to Mr. Couch.

                          ____________________
```
