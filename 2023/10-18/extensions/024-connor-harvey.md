# CONNOR HARVEY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                             CONNOR HARVEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, this month, I had the 
opportunity to attend Connor Harvey's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. 
Connor has been scouting for ten years, beginning as a cub scout, and 
then crossing over as a boy scout in 2017, quickly rising through the 
ranks. He has earned over thirty merit badges and has held several 
leadership positions in the troop including Historian, Patrol Leader, 
and Quartermaster. For Connor's

[[Page E992]]

Eagle Scout project, he made enhancements to Veterans Memorial Park in 
Egg Harbor Township. The project involved the installation of 
additional pavers to expand the standing room for ceremonies conducted 
at the park. He is a junior at Atlantic County Institute of Technology 
specializing in the Computer-Aided Design and Drafting program and is a 
member of the school's rowing team. Connor should be proud of his 
accomplishments with the Boy Scouts, and it was a pleasure to attend 
his Eagle Scout Court of Honor. God Bless Connor, and God Bless our 
United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
