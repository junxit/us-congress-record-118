# LAST SALUTE

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                              LAST SALUTE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, today, I want to honor the 
mission and dedication of the Last Salute. Last Salute, a nonprofit 
veteran's organization, is dedicated to providing profound and 
dignified funeral ceremonies that pay homage to the honor and 
dedication of military service. Their services adhere to the high 
standards expected by both families and deserving service members. 
These ceremonies encompass various elements, including the dignified 
transfer of remains through military transport, a Civil War cannon 
artillery salute, the solemn tolling of a memorial bell three times, a 
three-volley rifle salute, the rendition of Taps, and the meticulous 
folding and presentation of a memorial flag. Furthermore, Last Salute 
commemorates fallen individuals by placing their prayer card or image 
into their Memorial Prayer Box, signifying their enduring membership 
within the honor guard. Last Salute provides its services at no cost 
and relies solely on donations to sustain its vital mission of honoring 
the fallen and upholding the values of military service. Their services 
help honor the sacrifice of our fallen soldiers in the most commendable 
way, and I thank them for their dedication to their mission. God Bless 
Last Salute and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
