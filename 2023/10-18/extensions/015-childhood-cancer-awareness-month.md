# CHILDHOOD CANCER AWARENESS MONTH

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                    CHILDHOOD CANCER AWARENESS MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, today, I want to shine a 
spotlight on an issue that tugs at our hearts and demands our 
attention--Childhood Cancer Awareness Month. It's a time when we unite 
to acknowledge the brave young souls and their families who face an 
unimaginable battle against this devastating disease. But in the face 
of adversity, we witness incredible courage, strength, and resilience. 
Childhood Cancer Awareness Month serves as a call to action. It reminds 
us that while we cannot control the occurrence of this disease, we have 
the power to change its outcome. We can support research to find new 
treatments, increase awareness to foster early detection, and provide 
emotional and financial support to families in need. In this battle, I 
want to particularly commend South Jersey natives Carey Italiano and 
June Sieber for their tireless efforts and dedication for this cause. 
It is because of good people like them that we stand a chance in this 
fight. It is important we shift our focus to this important cause, and 
together, let's stand up for the brave children fighting this battle 
and strive for a brighter, cancer-free future for all our children. God 
Bless those fighting to bring awareness to this disease and God Bless 
the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
