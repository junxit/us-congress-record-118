# DANIEL COHEN

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                              DANIEL COHEN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 18, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, this past August, it was my 
honor to attend the Eagle Scout Court of Honor ceremony for Daniel 
Cohen. The trail to Eagle Scout is marked by milestones and 
achievements, each one a testament to Daniel's unwavering 
determination. From those early days as a Cub Scout to rising through 
the ranks, to earning merit badges and honing skills, he has 
demonstrated a thirst for knowledge and a willingness to embrace 
challenges. Daniel makes giving back to his community a priority and 
that is evident through his Eagle Scout Project. In an effort to help 
retire flags in a respectful way, Daniel built a flag retirement box 
for his local VFW. I know Daniel has a bright future ahead of him and I 
wish him all the best. God Bless Daniel Cohen and God Bless the United 
States of America.

                          ____________________
```
