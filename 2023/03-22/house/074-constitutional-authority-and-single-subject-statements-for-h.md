# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1702

**Measures:** H.R. 1702

```
            By Mrs. GONZALEZ-COLON:
        H.R. 1702.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress has the authority tpo enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article IV Section 3 Clause 2 of the United 
     States Constitution which provides:
       The Congress shall have Power to dispose of and make all 
     needful Rules and Regulations respecting the Territory or 
     other Property belonging to the United States(. . .)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Regulation of contracting of the Puerto Rico Financial 
     Oversight and Management Board.
```
