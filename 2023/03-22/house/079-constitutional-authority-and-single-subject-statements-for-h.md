# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1707

**Measures:** H.R. 1707

```
            By Mr. ISSA:
        H.R. 1707.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would reduce, from 14 years to 30 months, the 
     period of time during which car companies can enforce design 
     patents on the cosmetic, exterior parts, which most typically 
     get damaged in an accident, against alternative suppliers.
```
