# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 30

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill of the following title in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 30.--An act to authorize major medical facility projects 
     for the Department of Veterans Affairs for fiscal year 2023, 
     and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 101-509, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Majority Leader, announces the reappointment of 
the following individual to serve as a member of the Advisory Committee 
on the Records of Congress:
  Denise A. Hibay of New York.

                          ____________________
```
