# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1717

**Measures:** H.R. 1717

```
           By Mr. NEGUSE:
       H.R. 1717.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establish an interagency task force between the U.S. Patent 
     and Trademark Office and the Food and Drug Administration for 
     purposes of sharing information with respect to patents.
```
