# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1704

**Measures:** H.R. 1704

```
            By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
        H.R. 1704.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To nearshore certain manufacturing in the Western 
     Hemisphere to reduce dependency on the People's Republic of 
     China and to decrease illegal immigration.
```
