# CONGRATULATING THE FULLERTON COLLEGE MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Steel, Michelle (S001135) [R-CA]

```
       CONGRATULATING THE FULLERTON COLLEGE MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM

  (Mrs. STEEL asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. STEEL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Fullerton 
College men's basketball team on winning the California Community 
College Athletic Association's State championship.
  The Hornets beat the City College of San Francisco Rams by a score of 
83-73.
  The win marks the fifth time the Hornets have brought home the State 
title since the school basketball team was founded in 1927.
  The game was an exciting one, rounding out the team's season record 
to 30-1 and concluding their 25-game win streak.
  On behalf of the Fullerton community and the 45th Congressional 
District, I extend my warmest congratulations to the Hornets for this 
extraordinary accomplishment.
  You make us all proud.

                          ____________________
```
