# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1732

**Measures:** H.R. 1732

```
           By Mr. WOMACK:
       H.R. 1732.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The Congress shall have power to lay and collect taxes, 
     duties, imposts and excises, to pay the debts and provide for 
     the common defense and general welfare of the United States; 
     but all duties, imposts and excises shall be uniform 
     throughout the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would make a technical correction to the tax code 
     to help the military and their families by adding BNAs to the 
     list of qualified tax-exempt military benefits.
```
