# COMMUNICATION FROM THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Measures:** H.Res. 5

```
     COMMUNICATION FROM THE SPEAKER OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kiley) laid before the House the 
following communication from the Honorable Kevin McCarthy, Speaker of 
the House of Representatives:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                    June 23, 2023.

     through Monday, July 10, 2023, as a ``district work period'' 
     under section 3(z) of House Resolution 5.

     Speaker of the House of Representatives.
```
