# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4339

**Measures:** H.R. 4339

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 4339.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to ensure 
     enhanced Federal funding made available to States under the 
     Medicaid program is equitably distributed to political 
     subdivisions.
```
