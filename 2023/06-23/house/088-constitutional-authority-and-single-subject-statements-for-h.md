# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4348

**Measures:** H.R. 4348

```
           By Mr. NEGUSE:
       H.R. 4348.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude the 
     portion of a lump-sum social security benefit payment that 
     relates to periods prior to the taxable year from the 
     determination of household income.
```
