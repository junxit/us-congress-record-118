# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor David Bullock, Luke 4:18 Fellowship, Mobile, Alabama, offered 
the following prayer:
  Heavenly Father, maker of Heaven and Earth, redeemer, generous 
provider, we are desperately dependent upon You for all things. Bestow 
Your wisdom on this body and its Members to know and do Your will for 
our Nation, to abandon unrighteous agendas, to hear and heed Your 
voice, and to make decisions according to Your Holy Word so that we may 
practice kindness, develop unity, and walk humbly before You. Bring 
about unity in our Nation through the power of the holy spirit.
  May we, in reverence and through repentance of our sins, return to 
You. As the psalmist said, ``The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit; 
a broken and a contrite heart, O God, You will not despise.'' Father, 
open up Heaven's windows to bless this Nation as we humble ourselves 
before You.
  In the name of Jesus, we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
