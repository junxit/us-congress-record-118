# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4318

**Measures:** H.R. 4318

```
            By Mr. PALMER:
        H.R. 4318.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 4 Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the National Voter Registration Act of 1993 to 
     eliminate reliance on the change of address information 
     provided by the United States Postal Service for the purpose 
     of removing ineligible voters from the official list of 
     voters in a State, and for other purposes.
```
