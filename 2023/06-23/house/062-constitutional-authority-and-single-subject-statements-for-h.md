# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4321

**Measures:** H.R. 4321

```
            By Mr. BABIN:
        H.R. 4321.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Prohibiting federal funding to bail out states or 
     municipalities that would implement reparation programs.
```
