# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4338

**Measures:** H.R. 4338

```
           By Mr. LaHOOD:
       H.R. 4338.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       U.S. Constitution Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2: ``The 
     Congress shall have Power to dispose of and make all needful 
     Rules and Regulations respecting the Territory or other 
     Property belonging to the United States . . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would amend the National Trails System Act to 
     designate a trail of approximately 2,400 miles extending from 
     Chicago, Illinois, to Santa Monica, California, as the Route 
     66 National Historic Trail.
```
