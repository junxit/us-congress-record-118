# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4335

**Measures:** H.R. 4335

```
           By Mr. KILMER:
       H.R. 4335.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill requires a Federal Housing Administration mortgage 
     notice to a veteran to contain comparative rate and fee 
     information about loans available under the Veterans Affairs 
     home loan program.
```
