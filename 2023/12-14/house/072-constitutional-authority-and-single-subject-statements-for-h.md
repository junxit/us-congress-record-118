# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6789

**Measures:** H.R. 6789

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 6789.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Consumer Financial Protection Act of 2010 to 
     clarify standards for UDAAP enforcement actions brought by 
     the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection, and for other 
     purposes.
```
