# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6793

**Measures:** H.R. 6793

```
            By Mr. BRECHEEN:
        H.R. 6793.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To ensure that the proposed rule by the Department of 
     Energy titled ``Energy Conservation Program: Energy 
     Conservation Standards for Ceiling Fans'' (88 Fed. Reg. 
     40932) shall have no force or effect.
```
