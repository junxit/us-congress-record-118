# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6783

**Measures:** H.R. 6783

```
           By Mr. SESSIONS:
        H.R. 6783.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 (Commerce Clause)
       [The Congress shall have Power . . . ] To regulate Commerce 
     with foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     the Indian Tribes;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To protect the investment choices of investors by restoring 
     stablility to traditional financial vehicles.
```
