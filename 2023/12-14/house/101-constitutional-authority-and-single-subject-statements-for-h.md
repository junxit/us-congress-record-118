# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6818

**Measures:** H.R. 6818

```
            By Mrs. HINSON:
        H.R. 6818.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Allows an employer that maintains a qualified training 
     program for their employees in a taxable year to be eligible 
     for a tax credit equal to 30 percent of the direct costs 
     incurred by the employer.
```
