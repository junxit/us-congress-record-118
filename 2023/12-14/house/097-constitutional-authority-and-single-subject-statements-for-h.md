# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6814

**Measures:** H.R. 6814

```
            By Mr. GRAVES of Louisiana:
        H.R. 6814.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Preserve marine ecosystems and increases fishing access for 
     anglers by improving longstanding artificial reefing programs 
     for decommissioned energy infrastructure.
```
