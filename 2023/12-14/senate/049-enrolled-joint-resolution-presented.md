# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 32

```
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, December 14, 
2023, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled joint resolution:

       S.J. Res. 32. A joint resolution providing for 
     congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United 
     States Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Consumer 
     Financial Protection relating to ``Small Business Lending 
     Under the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (Regulation B)''.

                          ____________________
```
