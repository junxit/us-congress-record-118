# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1675

**Measures:** H.R. 1675

```
            By Mrs. GONZALEZ-COLON:
        H.R. 1675.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1: to provide for the common 
     Defence and general Welfare of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide a set-aside of funds for the territories under 
     the health profession opportunity grant program under section 
     2008 of the Social Security Act
```
