# HONORING ANITA GRANT

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                          HONORING ANITA GRANT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 21, 2023

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Anita 
Grant, who is retiring after 31 years of service to Lake County as a 
member of the county's legal counsel.
  An Iowa native, Ms. Grant started her career with Lake County, 
California, in 1992 as a Deputy County Counsel. She quickly stood out 
as an exceptional attorney, oftentimes working with county officials on 
issues, like criminal justice and civil rights. Her hard work and 
preparation as a litigator were essential in ensuring strong 
representation for the interests of Lake County at the local, state, 
and federal levels of the legal system.
  Ms. Grant's commitment to public service was unrelenting, especially 
during election years where she would provide an unbiased review and 
explanation of the ballot propositions that have appeared in front of 
Lake County voters. She also helped with the operation of ballot 
counting equipment to make sure all votes could be quickly and 
accurately counted. She also provided special training to officials on 
navigating through the complex set of laws and regulations that they 
had to adhere to while running elections.
  Ms. Grant's leadership, legal expertise, and dedication have made her 
an indispensable member of our community. She always maintained an 
open-door policy for county officials who wanted to hear her advice and 
legal guidance on important matters. Her commitment could be seen in 
her strong work ethic as she rarely missed work, forfeited vacation 
leave, and worked on weekends and holidays to get the job done despite 
the heavy workload she faced.
  Ms. Grant's legal work as County Counsel has helped the residents of 
Lake County by supporting the county's financial well-being. Her 
assistance in supporting better financial circumstances for Lake County 
has also helped the county afford to reliably provide necessary 
services for its residents.
  Mr. Speaker, Anita Grant has been an indispensable part of Lake 
County's community for the past 31 years and her service will be fondly 
remembered as she heads into retirement. Therefore, it is fitting and 
proper that we honor her here today.
```
