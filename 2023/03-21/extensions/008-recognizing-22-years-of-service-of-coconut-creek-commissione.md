# RECOGNIZING 22 YEARS OF SERVICE OF COCONUT CREEK COMMISSIONER BECKY TOOLEY

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
  RECOGNIZING 22 YEARS OF SERVICE OF COCONUT CREEK COMMISSIONER BECKY 
                                 TOOLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 21, 2023

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 22 years of 
service by Coconut Creek Commissioner, Becky Tooley.
  Born in 1948 in Newark, Ohio, Mayor Tooley has a long history of 
service to the community of Coconut Creek. Before her election to the 
Coconut Creek City Commission, she served on the City's Environmental 
Board and the Planning and Zoning Board.
  Her public service continues beyond city government, mentoring the 
youth of Coconut Creek at the Atlantic Technical Center and Coconut 
Creek High School. She also serves on the Boards of numerous community 
organizations, including the Coconut Creek Woman's Club, Alzheimer's 
Family Center Board of Trustees, Northwest Medical Center, Coconut 
Creek Democratic Club, the American Legion Auxiliary Unit 157, and the 
board of the Broward League of Cities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that you join me in recognizing Mayor Becky Tooley 
for her outstanding service to the people of Coconut Creek in Florida's 
23rd Congressional District.
```
