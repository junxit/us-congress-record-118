# CELEBRATING THE 90TH BIRTHDAY OF ROBERT CORNETTI

**Speaking:** McCormick, Richard (M001218) [R-GA]

```
            CELEBRATING THE 90TH BIRTHDAY OF ROBERT CORNETTI

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. RICHARD McCORMICK

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 21, 2023

  Mr. McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, today I pay tribute to Robert D. Cornetti 
of Roswell, Georgia, ``Bob,'' to his friends.
  Mr. Cornetti served in the United States Marine Corps during the 
Korean War, where he received the Good Conduct Medal, the United 
Nations Service Medal, the Korean Service Medal with Three Bronze 
Stars, and the National Defense Service Medal.
  On April 27, 2023, Mr. Cornetti celebrates his 90th birthday. I want 
to extend my congratulations, and the best wishes of Congress to Bob 
and his entire family for this special occasion.
  May America and my great State of Georgia be forever blessed with 
patriots like Robert Cornetti, and may God give him many happy returns.

                          ____________________
```
