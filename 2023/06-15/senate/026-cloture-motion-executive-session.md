# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 30, Natasha C. Merle, of New York, 
     to be United States District Judge for the Eastern District 
     of New York.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Alex Padilla, Tim 
           Kaine, Margaret Wood Hassan, Ben Ray Lujan, Raphael G. 
           Warnock, Tammy Duckworth, Jack Reed, John W. 
           Hickenlooper, Catherine Cortez Masto, Tammy Baldwin, 
           Brian Schatz, Christopher Murphy, Tina Smith, Debbie 
           Stabenow, Sheldon Whitehouse.

                          ____________________
```
