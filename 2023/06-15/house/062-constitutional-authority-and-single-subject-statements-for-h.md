# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4153

**Measures:** H.R. 4153

```
           By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
       H.R. 4153.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I; Section 8; Clause 1 of the Constitution states 
     The Congress shall have the Power To lay and collect Taxes, 
     Duties, Imposts, and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide 
     for the common Defense and general Welfare of the United 
     States . . .
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To create a competitive grant program to support 
     organizations that operate senior legal hotlines.
```
