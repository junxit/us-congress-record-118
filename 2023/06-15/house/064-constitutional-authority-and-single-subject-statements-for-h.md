# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4155

**Measures:** H.R. 4155

```
           By Mr. COLE:
       H.R. 4155.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single single subject of this legislation is:
       The Tribal HUD-VASH Act of 2023 permanently authorizes the 
     Tribal HUD-VASH program and allows for the placement of 
     Native veterans in Formula Current Assisted Stock (FCAS) 
     units without a resulting negative impact on a tribe's Indian 
     Housing Block Grant (IHBG).
```
