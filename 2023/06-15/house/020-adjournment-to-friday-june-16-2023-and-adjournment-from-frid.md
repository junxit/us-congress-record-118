# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JUNE 16, 2023; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 16, 2023, TO TUESDAY, JUNE 20, 2023

**Speaking:** Fry, Russell (F000478) [R-SC]

```
ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JUNE 16, 2023; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 
                  16, 2023, TO TUESDAY, JUNE 20, 2023

  Mr. FRY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. Friday, June 16, 2023; and 
further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet on 
Tuesday, June 20, 2023, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour 
debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from South Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
