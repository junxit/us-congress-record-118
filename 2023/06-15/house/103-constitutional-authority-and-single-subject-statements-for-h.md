# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4194

**Measures:** H.R. 4194

```
           By Ms. WATERS:
       H.R. 4194.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Federal Reserve Act to add additional 
     demographic reporting requirements, to modify the goals of 
     the Federal Reserve System.
```
