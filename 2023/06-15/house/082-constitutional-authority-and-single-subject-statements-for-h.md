# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4173

**Measures:** H.R. 4173

```
           By Mr. LaMALFA:
       H.R. 4173.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To Amend the Agricultural Research, Extension, and 
     Education Reform Act of 1998 to direct the Secretary of 
     Agriculture to establish a specialty crop mechanization and 
     automation initiative.
```
