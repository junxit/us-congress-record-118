# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4141

**Measures:** H.R. 4141

```
           By Mr. FULCHER:
       H.R. 4141.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, providing Congress to ``make all Laws 
     which shall be necessary and proper for carrying into 
     Execution'' the power eneumerated in Article 1 and ``all 
     other Powers vested by [the] Constitution in the Government 
     of the United States, or in any Department or Office 
     thereof.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for the efficient development of wireless, 
     wired, and communication apparatus in relation to federally 
     owned lands.
```
