# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6367

**Measures:** H.R. 6367

```
           By Mr. CLEAVER:
       H.R. 6367.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the Treasury to instruct the 
     United States Executive Director at each international 
     financial institution to use the voice, vote, and influence 
     of the United States to provide financial assistance to 
     countries that receive an influx of civilian, non-combatant 
     displaced persons stemming from the armed conflict that has 
     resulted from Hamas's October 2023 terrorism in Israel, to be 
     used for humanitarian aid for the displaced persons.
```
