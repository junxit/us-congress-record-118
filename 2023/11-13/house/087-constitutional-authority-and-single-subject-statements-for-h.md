# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6389

**Measures:** H.R. 6389

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 6389.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would prohibit the Department of Defense from 
     recouping pay from District of Columbia National Guard 
     members who are federal civilian employees and took 
     encampment leave.
```
