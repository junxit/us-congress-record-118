# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 101

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 101

```
           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.J. Res. 101.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United 
     States to prohibit Members of Congress from receiving 
     compensation for any period during which a Government 
     shutdown is in effect.
```
