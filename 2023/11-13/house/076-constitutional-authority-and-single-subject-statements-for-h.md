# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6378

**Measures:** H.R. 6378

```
           By Mr. ELLZEY:
       H.R. 6378.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution, 
     which states ``[t]he Congress shall have power to lay and 
     collect taxes, duties, imposts and excises, to pay the debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States; but all duties, imposts and excises shall be 
     uniform throughout the United States...''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To permit the remains of any Medal of Honor recipient to 
     lie in honor in the rotunda of the United States Capitol, and 
     for other purposes,
```
