# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6373

**Measures:** H.R. 6373

```
           By Mr. BERGMAN:
       H.R. 6373.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Directs VA to offer annual preventative health evaluations 
     to veterans with a spinal cord disorders and increases access 
     to assistive technologies.
```
