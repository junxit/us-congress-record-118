# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
following nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of Molly 
R. Silfen, of the District of Columbia, to be a Judge of the United 
States Court of Federal Claims for a term of fifteen years.
  Thereupon, the Senate proceeded to consider the nomination.
```
