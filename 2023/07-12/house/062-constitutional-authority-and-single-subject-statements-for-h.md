# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4582

**Measures:** H.R. 4582

```
           By Mr. LAMBORN:
       H.R. 4582.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress shall have the power to lay and collect txes, 
     duties, imposts, and excises; to pay the debts and provide 
     for the common defense and general welfare of the United 
     States; but all duties, imposts and excises shall be uniform 
     throughout the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       related to the prevention of fentanyl poisoning and 
     overdose in America's youth population.
```
