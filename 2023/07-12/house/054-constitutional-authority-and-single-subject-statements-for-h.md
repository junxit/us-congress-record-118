# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4574

**Measures:** H.R. 4574

```
           By Mr. CORREA:
       H.R. 4574.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Direct DHS to expand partnerships with appropriate law 
     enforcement entities in Mexico and Central American and South 
     American countries to combat human smuggling and trafficking 
     operations.
```
