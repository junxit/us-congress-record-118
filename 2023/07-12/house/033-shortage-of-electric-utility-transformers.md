# SHORTAGE OF ELECTRIC UTILITY TRANSFORMERS

**Speaking:** Fulcher, Russ (F000469) [R-ID]

```
               SHORTAGE OF ELECTRIC UTILITY TRANSFORMERS

  (Mr. FULCHER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FULCHER. Mr. Speaker, electric utility transformers provide a 
service that plays a pivotal role in the lives of Americans. Right now, 
there is a critical shortage of those transformers that enable 
America's electric grid. This shortage is keeping homes from being 
completed and hindering grid reliability as power utilities, co-ops, 
and energy producers try to keep up with demand.
  Why is the Department of Energy hurting American consumers more by 
making a transformer shortage worse?
  Mr. Speaker, that is why I rise as a proud supporter of the 
Protecting America's Distribution Transformer Supply Chain Act, which 
will delay the Department of Energy from imposing unnecessary mandates.
  Our power sector, builders, and contractors need a ready supply of 
electric transformers. We must stop the administration from turning the 
lights out on American families, including my constituents in Idaho.

                          ____________________
```
