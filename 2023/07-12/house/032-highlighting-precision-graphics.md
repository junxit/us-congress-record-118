# HIGHLIGHTING PRECISION GRAPHICS

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                    HIGHLIGHTING PRECISION GRAPHICS

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to highlight 
Precision Graphics, a family-owned electronics manufacturing and 
fulfillment facility in Snow Hill, North Carolina, my hometown.
  It fills me with tremendous pride to witness our local business' 
growth and positive impact.
  Precision Graphics, located just a block from my grandmother's home 
where I was raised, holds the resilience and strength of our community. 
While they have encountered supply chain challenges, the outlook is 
improving.
  It is of utmost importance that we continue to support businesses 
like Precision Graphics and our STEM programs. We must restore hope and 
help young people across eastern North Carolina, rural America, and our 
country to live the American Dream.
  Let's come together and work toward empowering our local businesses, 
fostering job creation, and contributing to the prosperity of our 
Nation.

                          ____________________
```
