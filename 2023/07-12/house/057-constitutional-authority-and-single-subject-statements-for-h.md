# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4577

**Measures:** H.R. 4577

```
           By Mr. GALLAGHER:
       H.R. 4577.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Foreign Commerce Clause; Article I, Section 8, Clause 
     3.
       The Necessary and Proper Clause; Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Expanding CFIUS jurisdiction to more real estate 
     transactions and requiring and elevated standard of review 
     for certain real estate transactions by foreign adversary 
     entities near an expanded list of sensitive sites.
```
