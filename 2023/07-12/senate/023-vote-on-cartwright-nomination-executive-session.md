**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                     Vote on Cartwright Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Cartwright nomination?
  Mr. PETERS. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.

  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. 
Fetterman), the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders), and the Senator
```
