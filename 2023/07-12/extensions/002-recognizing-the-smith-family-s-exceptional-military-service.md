# RECOGNIZING THE SMITH FAMILY'S EXCEPTIONAL MILITARY SERVICE

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE SMITH FAMILY'S EXCEPTIONAL MILITARY SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 12, 2023

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the Smith 
family of Johannesburg, Michigan, for an exceptional history of 
military service and for their dedication to serving our nation for 
decades. Tracing their military lineage back to 1812, the Smith family 
has been involved in our Armed Forces for over 150 years and deserve to 
be recognized for their generational sacrifices for the preservation of 
our liberty.
  The Smith family, many of whom are now deceased, consisted of 10 sons 
and 4 daughters, with all of the men serving in our military in varying 
capacities. In both the Navy and Army, Frank Dutton Smith's 10 sons 
began their selfless careers during World War II or shortly thereafter. 
Their family has exemplified a shared sense of duty and sacrifice for 
our country, and they have continued to dedicate themselves to this 
higher calling throughout their lives. I am thankful for extraordinary 
citizens like this family and extend my appreciation to the Smiths for 
all they have done to protect our freedom.
  Generational service, as seen in the Smith family, is a rare and 
commendable ethic that not many families can boast of. We must offer 
well-deserved praise and acknowledgment of their actions while 
celebrating their legacy for years to come. This level of devotion and 
commitment to our nation is inspiring to all and I am pleased to 
acknowledge them for it.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the Smith family of 
Johannesburg, Michigan, for their exceptional service to the United 
States Armed Forces. On behalf of Michigan's First District, I wish 
Marlene, David, and all other family members the best in their future 
endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
