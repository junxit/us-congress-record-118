# HONORING AND COMMENDING ROLAND TRAYNOR

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
                 HONORING AND COMMENDING ROLAND TRAYNOR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD NORCROSS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 12, 2023

  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and commend Roland 
Traynor, Riverfront Stage Dedication.
  Roland Traynor served as the architect of Camden County's free summer 
concert series where he began as a laborer for the Camden County Parks 
Department in 1972. As the architect, Mr. Traynor has provided Camden 
County with decades of access to world-class artists across all genres 
of music.
  For the last 35 years, music has played a huge role in Roland's life. 
Although he retired in 2011, he is brought back each year as a 
consultant to help the county successfully carry-out the summer concert 
series. The series has been held at Cooper River Park in Pennsauken, 
New Jersey and at Haddon Lake Park in Haddon Heights, New Jersey.
  The Camden County Board of Commissioners dedicates the River Stage at 
Wiggins Waterfront Park in Camden, New Jersey in his

[[Page E660]]

name to honor Mr. Traynor's commitment to making entertainment 
accessible to all residents of Camden County. His efforts as the 
architect of the summer concert series are fueled by his beliefs that 
music and entertainment should be the means to drive people to visit 
Camden, New Jersey; keeping him both active and work focused even in 
his time of retirement.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask you to join me in honoring and commending Mr. 
Roland Traynor of Winslow Township, New Jersey, a resident dedicated to 
his community and who has thoroughly served them to the best of his 
ability.
  Be it known to all, that I hereby honor Roland Traynor, and extend 
great appreciation for outstanding service to our community.

                          ____________________
```
