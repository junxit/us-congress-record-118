# HONORING THE SERVICE OF CHANCELLOR JIM YEONOPOLUS

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING THE SERVICE OF CHANCELLOR JIM YEONOPOLUS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 12, 2023

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I'm honored to celebrate and 
recognize the extraordinary work of Chancellor Jim Yeonopolus as he 
enters his richly deserved retirement. His decades of dedication to 
Central Texas College reflect the hard work and commitment of an 
admirable public servant.
  Jim has made substantial contributions to CTC since he launched his 
career at the college 50 years ago. He began as a counselor for student 
services, and through hard work, tenacity, and business acumen, he 
advanced into leadership roles as dean, assistant deputy chancellor, 
deputy chancellor, and interim chancellor. As a proud veteran, he has 
been an integral part of the success of CTC's military education 
operations. His efforts have resulted in CTC being regarded as one of 
Texas' great institutions of higher learning.
  Jim's commitment to community doesn't stop when his workday ends. He 
contributes his energies to trade and civic organizations that help 
make Central Texas a great place to call home. Whether it's the Boy 
Scouts, the United Way, or the Killeen Rodeo Association, Jim goes the 
extra mile to be of service to his fellow Texans.
  While Jim isn't tired of the privilege of service, he knows that 
everything has its season, and the time has come to allow the next 
generation of leaders to take the stage. Stepping away from work won't 
give him much idle time as he remains a vital part of the Central Texas 
community and will spend more time with his beloved wife Nancy.
  Retirement is meant to be celebrated and enjoyed. It is not the end 
of a career, but the beginning of a new adventure. It is my honor to 
recognize the selfless service of Jim Yeonopolus as he starts this new 
chapter of his life. On behalf of Central Texas, I thank him for his 
superb work and wish him the very best.

                          ____________________
```
