# RECOGNIZING ROBERT LaMARCHE FOR 25 YEARS OF SERVICE TO THE ESCANABA DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
  RECOGNIZING ROBERT LaMARCHE FOR 25 YEARS OF SERVICE TO THE ESCANABA 
                      DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 12, 2023

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Robert LaMarche 
for 25 years of service to the city of Escanaba. Throughout his time 
with the Escanaba Department of Public Safety, Robert has shown 
extraordinary commitment and dedication to his community, continually 
putting the safety and well-being of Escanaba residents above his own.
  As Director of the Escanaba Department of Public Safety, Robert has 
played an integral role in the department's continued success and 
effectiveness, and his selfless service to the people of Escanaba has 
left an indelible mark on so many. Since beginning his work in public 
safety in the late 1990s, Robert has become a familiar and trustworthy 
face, and his service will continue to have a positive impact for years 
to come. Our Nation needs more humble, upstanding leaders like Robert 
who protect and uplift our communities in the First District, and I 
cannot thank him--along with every law enforcement officer across our 
Nation--enough for his profound work in keeping our communities safe.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Robert LaMarche for 25 years 
of service to the Escanaba Department of Public Safety. His leadership 
and hard work are greatly appreciated and on behalf of Michigan's First 
District, I extend my gratitude to Robert, his family, his colleagues, 
and wish them the best in their future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
