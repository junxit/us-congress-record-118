# HONORING LINDA F. DAVIS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                        HONORING LINDA F. DAVIS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 12, 2023

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
tenacious and self-motivated leader, Linda F. Davis. She has shown what 
can be done through hard work, dedication, and a desire to achieve.
  Day of Caring is a community event that allows various groups and 
organizations to come together for a day and help different local 
organizations. Davis volunteered at United Way of West Central 
Mississippi's Day of Caring this past April. She is also the Vicksburg 
Post Volunteer of the week.
  Davis is a 39-year employee at Grand Gulf Nuclear Station supporting 
the Engineering Department. She has a daughter named Johnalynn, and in 
her free time, she enjoys volunteering, traveling, watching television 
and family time.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Linda F. 
Davis for her passion and dedication to the Vicksburg Warren Community.

                          ____________________
```
