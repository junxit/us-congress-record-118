# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5746

**Measures:** H.R. 5746

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
        H.R. 5746.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill allows Medicare Advantage organizations to offer 
     certain supplemental benefits, which are currently only 
     available to chronically ill enrollees, to enrollees with 
     low-income or who meet other criteria, such as socioeconomic 
     risk factors
```
