# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5752

**Measures:** H.R. 5752

```
            By Mr. FITZPATRICK:
        H.R. 5752.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       Allow employers to provide families with first dollar 
     coverage of mental health services up to 500 dollars through 
     their Health Savings Account (HSA).
```
