# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5742

**Measures:** H.R. 5742

```
            By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
        H.R. 5742.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power. . .To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would clarify that an employee under the 
     Fair Labor Standards Act does not include an individual who 
     performs activities required as part of a vocational school 
     curriculum or training.
```
