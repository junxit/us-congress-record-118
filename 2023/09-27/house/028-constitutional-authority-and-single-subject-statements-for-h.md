# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5748

**Measures:** H.R. 5748

```
            By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
        H.R. 5748.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Alice Cogswell and Anne Sullivan Macy Act will shine a 
     light on the needs of these students and enhance 
     accountability for the services they require, fulfilling 
     IDEAs promise of an appropriate educational experience.
```
