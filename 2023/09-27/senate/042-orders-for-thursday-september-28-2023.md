# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2023

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 3935

```
                ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2023

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 10 a.m. on 
Thursday, September 28; that following the prayer and pledge, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day and 
morning business be closed; that upon the conclusion of morning 
business, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to proceed to 
H.R. 3935, postcloture, and that all time be considered expired at 
11:45 a.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
