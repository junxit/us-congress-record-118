# CONGRATULATING MR. BRUCE GROTEWIEL ON RECEIVING THE MISSOURI EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES ASSOCIATION'S LIFETIME ACHIEVEMENT AWARD

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
CONGRATULATING MR. BRUCE GROTEWIEL ON RECEIVING THE MISSOURI EMERGENCY 
       MEDICAL SERVICES ASSOCIATION'S LIFETIME ACHIEVEMENT AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 27, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Mr. Bruce Grotewiel on receiving the Missouri 
Emergency Medical Services Association's Lifetime Achievement Award.
  This December, Mr. Grotewiel will mark 50 years of service to the 
greater Jefferson City and Columbia area. His dedication has not gone 
unnoticed, and his service could not be more deserving of this 
recognition. Throughout his career as a first responder, Mr. Grotewiel 
has been on the front lines helping those who need it most, and for 
that we cannot thank him enough. The great courage and moral judgment 
he exhibited over the course of his professional and volunteer career 
should forever be appreciated.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in commending Mr. Grotewiel's five 
decades of service to the people of Missouri.
```
