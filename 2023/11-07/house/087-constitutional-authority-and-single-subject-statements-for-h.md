# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6265

**Measures:** H.R. 6265

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 6265.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To advance nuclear energy by directing the Nuclear 
     Regulatory Commission to update the mission statement of the 
     Commission to ensure licensing and regulation is efficient 
     and is in alignment with the policies stated in the Atomic 
     Energy Act of 1954.
```
