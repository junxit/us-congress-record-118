# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6271

**Measures:** H.R. 6271

```
            By Mr. MILLER of Ohio:
        H.R. 6271.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       ``The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land, as enumerated in Article I, Section 
     8, Clause 14 of the United States Constitution.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To expand agriculture markets through promotion of aviation 
     renewable fuels.
```
