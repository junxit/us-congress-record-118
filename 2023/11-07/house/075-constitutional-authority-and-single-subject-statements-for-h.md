# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6253

**Measures:** H.R. 6253

```
           By Mr. CURTIS:
       H.R. 6253.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To advance nuclear energy by authorizing the Secretary of 
     Energy to make awards to cover regulatory costs relating to 
     licensing certain first-of-a-kind advanced nuclear reactors.
```
