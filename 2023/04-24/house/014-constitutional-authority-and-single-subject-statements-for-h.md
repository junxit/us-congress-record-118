# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2796

**Measures:** H.R. 2796

```

        H.R. 2796.

     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''

       To provide educational resources and host events to promote
```
