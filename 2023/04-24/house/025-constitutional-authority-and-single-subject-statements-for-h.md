# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2807

**Measures:** H.R. 2807

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
        H.R. 2807.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to direct the Department of Transportation to develop and 
     submit to Congress a report containing recommendations to 
     reduce train noise and vibrations near homes and estimates of 
     the costs and benefits of such recommendations.
```
