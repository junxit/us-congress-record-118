# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3691

**Measures:** H.R. 3691

```
           By Mr. ESTES:
       H.R. 3691.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation provides CMS the statutory authority to 
     revise the Medicare fee schedule for Emergency Air Ambulance 
     Services based on the cost data and requires additional data 
     collection to occur every three years, on the fixed and 
     operating costs of providing services, the revenue collected 
     based on transports, and any other data the Secretary wishes 
     collected.
```
