# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3726

**Measures:** H.R. 3726

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 3726.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Education to study student 
     mental health at institutions of higher education and to 
     issue guidance on compliance with the Americans with 
     Disabilities Act of 1990 for mental health and substance use 
     disorder policies of institutions of higher education, and 
     for other purposes.
```
