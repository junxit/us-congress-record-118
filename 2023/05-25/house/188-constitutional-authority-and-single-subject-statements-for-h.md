# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3720

**Measures:** H.R. 3720

```
           By Mr. NEGUSE:
       H.R. 3720.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Labor to award grants to 
     organizations for the provision of transition assistance to 
     members and former members of the Armed Forces who are 
     separated, retired, or discharged from the Armed Forces, and 
     spouses of such members, and for other purposes.
```
