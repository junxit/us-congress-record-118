# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3721

**Measures:** H.R. 3721

```
           By Mr. NEWHOUSE:
       H.R. 3721.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article 1, Section 8 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, and title 39, United 
     States Code, to provide the United States Postal Service the 
     authority to mail alcoholic beverages, and for other 
     purposes.
```
