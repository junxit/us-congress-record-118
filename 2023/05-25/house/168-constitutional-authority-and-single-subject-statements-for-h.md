# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3700

**Measures:** H.R. 3700

```
           By Mr. HUFFMAN:
       H.R. 3700.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for restoration, economic development, 
     recreation, and conservation on Federal lands in Northern 
     California, and for other purposes.
```
