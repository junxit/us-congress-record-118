# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3710

**Measures:** H.R. 3710

```
           By Mrs. McCLAIN:
       H.R. 3710.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to prohibit health 
     professionals from providing false or misleading information 
     with respect to assisted reproduction.
```
