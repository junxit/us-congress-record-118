# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3692

**Measures:** H.R. 3692

```
           By Mr. FITZGERALD:
       H.R. 3692.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires bipartisan membership in the Federal 
     Trade Commission in order for the Commission to act following 
     a vacancy.
```
