# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3702

**Measures:** H.R. 3702

```
           By Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania:
       H.R. 3702.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Article I Section 
     8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow a 
     refundable tax credit against income tax for the purchase of 
     qualified access technology for the blind.
```
