# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3704

**Measures:** H.R. 3704

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 3704.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Help America Vote Act of 2002 to prohibit 
     States from using ranked choice voting to carry out an 
     election for Federal office, and for other purposes.
```
