# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3697

**Measures:** H.R. 3697

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Texas:
       H.R. 3697.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 1 of the United States Constitution 
     provides the Congress of the United States all legislative 
     powers.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Preemption of state laws that prohibit the purchase of real 
     property by citizens of other nations.
```
