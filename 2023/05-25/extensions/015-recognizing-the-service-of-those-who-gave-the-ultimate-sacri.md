# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF THOSE WHO GAVE THE ULTIMATE SACRIFICE MEMORIAL DAY 2023

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF THOSE WHO GAVE THE ULTIMATE SACRIFICE 
                           MEMORIAL DAY 2023

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 25, 2023

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and recognize 
those who paid the ultimate sacrifice in defense of the United States 
of America. On behalf of the United States Congress, and the 
constituents of the 23rd Congressional District, I am proud to 
recognize our brave soldiers' courage, sacrifice, and valor as we come 
together this Memorial Day.
  All gave some, and some gave all. Memorial Day is not a 3-day 
weekend. Instead, it is the duty of a grateful Nation to pause and 
remember those who laid down their life so that we may live free. This 
is the greatest country on earth, and we are the only Nation that 
guarantees the right to life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness as 
the cornerstone of our foundation.
  The Flag of the United States of America shows red, white, and blue. 
Blue symbolizes vigilance, perseverance, and justice--a reminder that 
we must remain watchful and strong. White reminds us to stay true to 
our values and ideals. Red represents valor in honor of the blood shed 
by those who have fought to protect our freedoms.
  On Memorial Day we remember those we lost and honor the veterans who 
are with us today. America is the land of the free because it is the 
home of the brave. I am so humbled by the bravery and honor displayed 
by our men and women in uniform. Whether it be Memorial Day or any 
other day, please know that we as a Nation are grateful.

                          ____________________
```
