# HONORING THE SERVICE OF MAJOR GENERAL TREVOR J. BREDENKAMP

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
       HONORING THE SERVICE OF MAJOR GENERAL TREVOR J. BREDENKAMP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 25, 2023

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, as the proud Co-Chair of the Army 
Caucus, I extend my deepest gratitude for the service of Major General 
Trevor J. Bredenkamp to the United States Congress as the Army's Chief 
Legislative Liaison.
  Under his stewardship, the Army maintained strong relationships with 
members of Congress, committees, and staff. His many accomplishments 
within the Office of the Chief Legislative Liaison are a testament to 
his leadership, particularly in how his team persevered during the 
COVID-19 pandemic.
  Congratulations to Major General Bredenkamp on his selection as the 
Commanding General of the Military District of Washington and Commander 
of the Joint Forces Headquarters-National Capital Region. His 
dedication and experience will undoubtedly result in a disciplined and 
prepared force, entrusted with securing our Nation's Capital.
  I extend my best wishes as he transitions to a new assignment and 
express profound thankfulness to him and his family's service to our 
Nation.

                          ____________________
```
