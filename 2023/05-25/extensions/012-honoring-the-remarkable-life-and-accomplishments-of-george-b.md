# HONORING THE REMARKABLE LIFE AND ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF GEORGE BONFE

```
    HONORING THE REMARKABLE LIFE AND ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF GEORGE BONFE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE STAUBER

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 25, 2023

  Mr. STAUBER Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the remarkable life 
and accomplishments of George Bonfe. George's unwavering can-do spirit 
was indeed an inspiration to us all. Despite suffering a spinal injury 
during an Army leave at the age of 21, which left him paralyzed from 
the waist down, George refused to let this obstacle define his life. 
Instead, he adopted three children, two of whom served in the military, 
one of whom even graduated from West Point. George's resilience was 
further demonstrated by his building of an airplane and becoming a 
pilot from his wheelchair, along with driving an automobile, boat, jet 
skis, snowmobile, and flying an ultralight. He also tirelessly sent 
care packages to deployed troops, including his adopted daughter. 
George was an active member of his community, participating in various 
military events and the Forest Lake Veterans Memorial. Additionally, he 
built a daycare, owned a liquor store, and engaged in numerous 
community service activities. George made a lasting impact on those he 
met, often visiting the Forest Lake American Legion Post No. 225 with 
his daughter. He also attended many funerals of Minnesota fallen 
soldiers.
  George Bonfe serves as a remarkable role model for everyone. Despite 
facing physical limitations, he never allowed them to hinder his 
success; his only obstacle was stairs, but somehow he always made it to 
the top. He dedicated himself to his loved ones, community, and fellow 
veterans, leaving a lasting impact on all those he touched. We are 
grateful for his service to our Nation and his incredible story that 
will continue inspiring future generations. May he rest in peace, 
knowing that his legacy will continue to positively impact the world.

                          ____________________
```
