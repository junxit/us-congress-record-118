# SENATE RESOLUTION 4--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 4

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 4--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
                THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. SCHUMER submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 4

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Patty Murray as
```
