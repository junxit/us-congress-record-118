# TRIBUTE TO DAMAR HAMLIN

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO DAMAR HAMLIN

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, this is a joyful and celebratory day, 
but I would like to begin on a serious note and offer my prayers and 
best wishes to Buffalo Bills safety Damar Hamlin and his family.
  What happened last night was one of the most horrifying things I have 
ever seen watching football, and I watch a lot of it. We are all 
rooting and praying for his recovery.
  Damar, we are with you every step of the way in spirit. You make us 
proud. We can't wait to see you up and healthy again soon.

                          ____________________
```
