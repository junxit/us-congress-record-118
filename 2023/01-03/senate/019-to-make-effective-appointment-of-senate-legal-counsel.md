# TO MAKE EFFECTIVE APPOINTMENT OF SENATE LEGAL COUNSEL

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 8

```
         TO MAKE EFFECTIVE APPOINTMENT OF SENATE LEGAL COUNSEL

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 8, submitted earlier today.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the resolution by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 8) to make effective appointment of 
     Senate Legal Counsel.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed to 
and that the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the 
table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 8) was agreed to as follows:

                               S. Res. 8

       Resolved, That the appointment of Morgan J. Frankel of the 
     District of Columbia to be Senate Legal Counsel, made by the 
     President pro tempore this day, is effective as of January 3, 
     2023, and the term of service of the appointee shall expire 
     at the end of the One-Hundred Nineteenth Congress.

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Pursuant to Public Law 95-521, appoints 
Patricia Mack Bryan as Deputy Senate Legal Counsel for a term of 
service to expire at the end of the 119th Congress.

                          ____________________
```
