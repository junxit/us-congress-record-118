# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 6, 2023, THROUGH MONDAY, JANUARY 23, 2023

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 6, 2023, THROUGH MONDAY, JANUARY 23, 2023

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it convene for a pro forma session 
only, with no business conducted, on the following dates and times and 
that following each pro forma session, the Senate stand in recess until 
the next pro forma session: Friday, January 6, at 1:05 p.m.; Tuesday, 
January 10, at 10 a.m.; Friday, January 13, at 1:30 p.m.; Tuesday, 
January 17, at 1 p.m.; and Friday, January 20, at 1 p.m.; that 
following the January 20 pro forma, the Senate stand in recess until 3 
p.m. on Monday, January 23, 2023; further, I ask that following the 
prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date and 
the Senate proceed to executive session, as provided for under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
