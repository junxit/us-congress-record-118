# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5946

**Measures:** H.R. 5946

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 5946.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would prohibit the Department of Defense (DOD) 
     and the District of Columbia National Guard (DCNG) from 
     transferring personnel and equipment of the DCNG to DOD or 
     the National Guard of a state or territory.
```
