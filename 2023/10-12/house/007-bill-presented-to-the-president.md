# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 5110

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

                                 ______
                                 
  Kevin F. McCumber, Acting Clerk of the House, reported that on 
October 04, 2023, the following bill was presented to the President of 
the United States for approval:

       H. R. 5110. To amend the Elementary and Secondary Education 
     Act of 1965 to clarify that the prohibition on the use of 
     Federal education funds for certain weapons does not apply to 
     the use of such weapons for training in archery, hunting, or 
     other shooting sports.
```
