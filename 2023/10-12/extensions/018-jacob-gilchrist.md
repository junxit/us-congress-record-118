# JACOB GILCHRIST

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                            JACOB GILCHRIST

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 12, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, this June, I had the pleasure 
of celebrating Jacob Gilchrist's achievements with the Scouts. Jacob 
joined the Scouts as a Tjger Cub and after years of hard work and 
dedication, has earned the rank of Eagle Scout. Through earning merit 
badges, participating in outdoor adventures, and developing his 
leadership skills, Jacob has gained valuable knowledge that he will 
carry with him for the rest of his life. For his Eagle Scout Service 
Project, Jacob renovated and repaired a local World War Two monument in 
his local community. Jacob's service does not go unnoticed, and I 
applaud him for his hard work and dedication for improving our South 
Jersey community. I congratulate Jacob and I wish him all the best. God 
Bless Jacob Gilchrist and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
