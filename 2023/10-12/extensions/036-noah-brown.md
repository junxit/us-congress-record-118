# NOAH BROWN

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                               NOAH BROWN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 12, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, today I celebrate the 
accomplishments of Noah Quinton Brown and his recent achievement of the 
rank of Eagle Scout. Noah began scouting at the age of five, following 
in the footsteps of four generations of family before him. Now, having 
reached the highest rank in scouting, we celebrate his achievements. 
During his time in the Scouts, Noah has participated in countless 
fundraising and community service activities along with many different 
camping and hiking adventures. In July of 2022, Noah completed a 60-
mile Trek at Philmont Scout Ranch in Cimarron, New Mexico where he 
achieved the Boy Scout 50 miler award. For his Eagle Scout Project, he 
built benches, a water station, and rehabilitated a pavilion for use by 
spectators at cycling events put on by Camp Edge in Alloway, South 
Jersey. His remains active in the scouts with his current position of 
Junior Assistant Scoutmaster at Troop 60 in Daretown, South Jersey. 
Noah is now a junior at Woodstown High School, consistently earning his 
way on to the

[[Page E965]]

Honor Roll. Outside of scouting, he is an athlete on his school's 
Varsity Track and Field team and a volunteer at his church, all while 
maintaining a job at a local farm. Along with his Eagle Scout award, 
Noah will be presented with the Bronze and Gold palms for his 
remarkable leadership and his willingness to accept responsibility. I 
am incredibly proud of Noah for all his accomplishments in the Scouts 
and I know he has a bright future ahead of him. God Bless Noah and God 
Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
