# CELEBRATING THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF TONI COCKERAM

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
             CELEBRATING THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF TONI COCKERAM

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 12, 2023

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to 
celebrate the remarkable career and retirement of a truly exceptional 
leader, Toni Cockeram.
  After 23 years of dedicated and loyal service to the Golden Isles 
Association of Realtors. Toni is leaving behind a remarkable legacy of 
achievement.
  With prestigious designations such as Realtor Association Certified 
Executive, Home with Diversity and e-PRO certifications, Toni's career 
has been a true example of professional development and education.

[[Page E959]]

  Ms. Cockeram has held significant leadership positions throughout her 
career, including six years as the Georgia Association of Realtors 
Chairman and co-Chairman for the MLS Forum.
  Her leadership experience and achievements are truly too numerous to 
list in just one speech, which speaks volumes about who Toni is as a 
person.
  I congratulate Toni on her amazing career and on her upcoming 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
