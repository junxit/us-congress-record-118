# RECOGNIZING NICK ADAMS

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                         RECOGNIZING NICK ADAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 12, 2023

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise to recognize Nick 
Adams and the incredible work of his organization, the Foundation for 
Liberty and American Greatness, or FLAG. Mr. Adams was born in Sydney, 
Australia, and immigrated to the United States in 2016 as the recipient 
of the Extraordinary Ability Green Card. He became a naturalized 
citizen on December 20, 2021, and I was proud to be there with him for 
that special moment.
  After coming to the United States, Mr. Adams founded the Foundation 
for Liberty and American Greatness, also known as FLAG, dedicated to 
educating Americans about the values and principles that make our 
nation exceptional. To date, FLAG has provided educational resources to 
more than 1.6 million students around the country.
  Mr. Adams is of the Greek-Orthodox faith, is a Stage IV cancer 
survivor, a patriot, and the embodiment of the American Dream. His life 
is a testament to the free spirit that has made America great. I am 
proud to call him friend.

                          ____________________
```
