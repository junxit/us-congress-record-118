# NATHAN AMEY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                              NATHAN AMEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 12, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, this Month, I had the honor of 
attending Nathan Amey's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Nathan first joined 
the Scouts as a Tiger Cub in the first grade and immediately embraced 
the true tenets of scouting. He quickly emerged as a natural leader to 
all and embodied the characteristics of the Scout Law. Nathan is 
someone his troop can count on and is constantly thinking of ways to 
improve the scouting experience for his troop. Whenever the Senior 
Patrol Leader is unable to attend, Nathan is the first person the 
scouts turn to for guidance. His Eagle Scout Project was based on his 
reverence for the American flag as he built a flag retirement pit area 
for the American Legion Post 524. Outside of scouting, he participates 
in countless activities such as playing the cello and piano, 
participating on his school's tennis team, is an active member in Model 
United Nations and French club and so much more; all while maintaining 
his academic standing of Distinguished honors. Nathan is now a 
sophomore at Ocean City High School and continues to excel in all 
facets of his life. Nathan should be proud of his achievements in the 
Scouts, and I wish him all the best in his promising future. God Bless 
Nathan, and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
