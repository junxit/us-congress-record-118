# HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: LISA TINBRINK HOWARD

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
       HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: LISA TINBRINK HOWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 12, 2023

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Lisa Tinbrink Howard as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Lisa is a 
leader who serves her community through her work, advocacy, and 
volunteer efforts. Early in her career. Lisa worked as an engineer, 
where she broke down barriers and was a trailblazer for other female 
engineers. She later founded Tolenas Winery, which she operates with 
her husband in the Suisun Valley. There they provide wine education and 
help others understand the invaluable contributions of local 
agriculture to our community. Lisa has served on the Solano County 
Agricultural Advisory Committee and is Secretary of the Suisun Valley 
Vinters and Growers Association where she works with elected officials 
to advocate for farmers and winemakers. Furthermore, Lisa serves her 
community through her volunteer work, which includes work with the 
local 4H, advocating for women in the wine and agricultural industries, 
and supporting military personnel. She is also a proud and caring 
mother of three children and inspires others with her dedication to her 
faith. family, business, advocacy, and community service. On behalf of 
the people of California's 8th Congressional District. it is my 
pleasure to honor and recognize Lisa Tinbrink Howard for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
