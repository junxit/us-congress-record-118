# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6699

**Measures:** H.R. 6699

```
           By Mr. HORSFORD:
       H.R. 6699.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The End Double Taxation of Successful Consumer Claims Act 
     changes tax law so that plaintiffs that win consumer fraud 
     cases are not liable for taxes on funds awarded to their 
     attorney.
```
