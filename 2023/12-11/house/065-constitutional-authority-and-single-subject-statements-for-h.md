# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6700

**Measures:** H.R. 6700

```
           By Mr. LAMBORN:
       H.R. 6700.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section WIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Children's Bureau of Health and Human 
     Services to collect statistics from the court system on the 
     domestic private adoptions within the United States.
```
