# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6707

**Measures:** H.R. 6707

```
           By Mr. MOYLAN:
       H.R. 6707.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article one of the United States Constitution 
     Congress has the power to enact this legislation.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Repeal the requirements of the Foreign Dredge Act of 1906 
     with respect to dredging and dredged material.
```
