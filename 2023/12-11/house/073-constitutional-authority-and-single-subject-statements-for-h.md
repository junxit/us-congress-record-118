# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6708

**Measures:** H.R. 6708

```
            By Mr. MOYLAN:
        H.R. 6708.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article one of the United States Constitution 
     Congress has the power to enact this legislation.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Army, acting through the 
     Chief of Engineers, to propose a new nationwide permit under 
     the Federal Water Pollution Control Act for dredging 
     projects, and for other purposes.
```
