# RESIGNATION AS MEMBER OF COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE, SPACE, AND TECHNOLOGY

```
  RESIGNATION AS MEMBER OF COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE, SPACE, AND TECHNOLOGY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Lawler) laid before the House the 
following resignation as a member of the Committee on Science, Space, 
and Technology:
                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 11, 2023.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: I hereby resign from the House 
     Committee on Science, Space and Technology.
           Sincerely,
                                                      Ted W. Lieu,
                                               Member of Congress.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the resignation is 
accepted.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
