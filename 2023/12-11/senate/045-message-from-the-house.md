# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 88

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:05 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following joint resolution, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

       H.J. Res. 88. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Department of Education relating 
     to ``Improving Income Driven Repayment for the William D. 
     Ford Federal Direct Loan Program and the Federal Family 
     Education Loan (FFEL) Program''.
```
