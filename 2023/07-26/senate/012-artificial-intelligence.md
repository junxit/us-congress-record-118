# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                        ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, on AI, finally, with so much going on in 
the Senate, I want to remind my colleagues that today we will hold our 
third all-Senate briefing on artificial intelligence. Our presenters 
are Rick Stevens from the Department of Energy's Argonne National Lab, 
Dr. Sethuraman ``Panch'' Panchanathan from the National Science 
Foundation, and Dr. Kathleen Fisher from the Information Innovation 
Office at DARPA. Finally, our moderator will be Dr. Jose-Marie 
Griffiths, member, National Security Commission on Artificial 
Intelligence, and president of Dakota State University. It is a broad 
range of presenters.
  The last few briefings were well-attended. I know people are busy 
today, but please try to make the time. And the Q-and-A was 
surprisingly very direct. We got a lot of answers and learned a lot.
  So I look forward to seeing my colleagues at the briefing for what 
surely will be an illuminating, important discussion, and I thank 
everyone for their good work.

                          ____________________
```
