# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4950

**Measures:** H.R. 4950

```
           By Mr. TRONE:
       H.R. 4950.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish additional requirements for state Medicaid 
     drug-use review programs for individuals who experience 
     opioid-related overdoses.
```
