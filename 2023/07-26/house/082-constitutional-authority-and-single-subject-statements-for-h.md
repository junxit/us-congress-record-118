# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4904

**Measures:** H.R. 4904

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 4904.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establishes certain funds to construct and maintain 
     physical barriers along the southern international border of 
     the United States and award grants to certain organizations 
     addressing the fentanyl crisis.
```
