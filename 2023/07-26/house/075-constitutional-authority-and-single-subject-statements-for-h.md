# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4897

**Measures:** H.R. 4897

```
           By Ms. CHU:
       H.R. 4897.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 3 of Article 1, Section 8 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Labor to promulgate an 
     occupational safety and health standard to protect workers 
     from heat-related injuries and illnesses.
```
