# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4922

**Measures:** H.R. 4922

```
           By Mrs. LUNA:
       H.R. 4922.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Address loopholes in the Lacey Act that need to be 
     addressed including a 2017 D.C. Circuit Court of Appeals 
     decision and granting U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service with 
     emergency designation authority of potentially injurious 
     species.
```
