# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2394

**Measures:** H.R. 2394

```
           By Mr. SWALWELL:
       H.R. 2394.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution, 
     specifically clause 18 (relating to the power to make laws 
     necessary and proper for carrying out the powers vested in 
     Congress).
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Creates a banned fliers list through the Transportation 
     Security Administrative to limit individuals who physically 
     or sexually assault members of in-flight crew.
```
