# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2374

**Measures:** H.R. 2374

```
           By Mr. GARCIA of Illinois:
       H.R. 2374.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article 1 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill makes changes to immigration enforcement, 
     including ending mandatory detention in certain cases.
```
