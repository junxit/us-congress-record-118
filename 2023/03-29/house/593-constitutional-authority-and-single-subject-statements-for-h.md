# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2381

**Measures:** H.R. 2381

```
           By Mr. LARSEN of Washington:
       H.R. 2381.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       As written in Article I, Section 1, ``all legislative 
     powers herein granted shall be vested in a Congress of the 
     United States, which shall consist of a Senate and a House of 
     Representatives.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill restores Fulbright exchanges with China and Hong 
     Kong.
```
