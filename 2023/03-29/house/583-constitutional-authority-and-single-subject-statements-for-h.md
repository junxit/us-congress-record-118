# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2371

**Measures:** H.R. 2371

```
            By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 2371.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority for Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To ensure that older adults and individuals with 
     disabilities are included in disaster preparedness.
```
