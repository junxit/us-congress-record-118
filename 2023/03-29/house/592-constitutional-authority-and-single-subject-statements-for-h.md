# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2380

**Measures:** H.R. 2380

```

       H.R. 2380.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8, the Necessary and Proper
```
