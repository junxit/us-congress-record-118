# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1774

**Measures:** H.R. 1774

```
            By Mr. ALFORD:
        H.R. 1774.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Cluse 18 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to reimburse 
     veterans for the cost of emergency medical transportation to 
     a Federal facility.
```
