# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1799

**Measures:** H.R. 1799

```
           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.R. 1799.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to render an 
     individual, who transfers certain educational assistance, to 
     which the individual is entitled because of an agreement by 
     such individual to serve in the Armed Forces, to a dependent 
     of that individual, and who fails to complete such agreement, 
     solely liable for the overpayment of such educational 
     assistance.
```
