# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1780

**Measures:** H.R. 1780

```
            By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
        H.R. 1780.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill provides coverage under Medicare, Medicaid, and 
     CHIP to full molecular diagnostics and DNA sequencing at the 
     time of cancer diagnosis.
```
