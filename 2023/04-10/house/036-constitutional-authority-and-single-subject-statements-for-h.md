# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2561

**Measures:** H.R. 2561

```
           By Mr. LYNCH:
       H.R. 2561.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII, Clause XVIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will require the Secretary of Transportation to 
     enter into an agreement with the National Academies of 
     Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine to produce a report that 
     identifies and recommends a system of measuring aviation 
     noise.
```
