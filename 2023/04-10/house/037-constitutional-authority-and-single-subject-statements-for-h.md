# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2562

**Measures:** H.R. 2562

```
           By Mr. LYNCH:
       H.R. 2562.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII, Clause XVIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will direct the Administrator of the Federal 
     Aviation Administration to enter into appropriate 
     arrangements with the National Academies of Sciences, 
     Engineering, and Medicine to provide for a report on the 
     health impacts of air traffic noise and pollution.
```
