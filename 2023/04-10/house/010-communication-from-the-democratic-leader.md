# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

**Measures:** H.Res. 5

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                    April 7, 2023.
     Hon. Kevin McCarthy,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker McCarthy: Pursuant to Section 4(b) of House 
     Resolution 5, 118th Congress, I am writing to appoint the 
     following members to the House Democracy Partnership:
       The Hon. Dina Titus of Nevada, Ranking Member.
       The Hon. Gwen Moore of Wisconsin.
       The Hon. Ted Lieu of California.
       The Hon. Norma Torres of California.
       The Hon. Robin Kelly of Illinois.
       The Hon. Terri Sewell of Alabama.
       The Hon. Diana DeGette of Colorado.
       The Hon. Barbara Lee of California.
       The Hon. Ilhan Omar of Minnesota.
       Thank you for your attention to these appointments.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
