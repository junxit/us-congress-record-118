# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2554

**Measures:** H.R. 2554

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 2554.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 14 of the U.S. 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To expand coverage of the Defense Access Road designation 
     to include named roads in the vicinity of the Beale Air Force 
     Base, Travis Air Force Base, and Sierra Army Depot, 
     California.
```
