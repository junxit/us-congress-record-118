# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                    April 7, 2023.
     Hon. Kevin McCarthy,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker McCarthy: Pursuant to Section 4 of the United 
     States Semiquin-
     centennial Commission Act of 2016 (Pub. L. 114-196), I am

     Commissioners to the United States Semiquincentennial 
     Commission:
       The Hon. Bonnie Watson Coleman of New Jersey.
       The Hon. Dwight Evans of Pennsylvania.
       Thank you for your attention to these recommendations.
           Sincerely,

     Democratic Leader.
```
