# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6726

**Measures:** H.R. 6726

```
            By Mr. LUETKEMEYER:
        H.R. 6726.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Securities Act of 1933 to require the 
     accounting principles standard setting body to comply with 
     the Administrative Procedure Act and the Government in the 
     Sunshine Act, to require the head of such body to testify 
     annually before Congress, and for other purposes
```
