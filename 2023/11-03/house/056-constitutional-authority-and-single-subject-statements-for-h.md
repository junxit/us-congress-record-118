# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6227

**Measures:** H.R. 6227

```
           By Mr. SCHWEIKERT:
       H.R. 6227.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to expand 
     the tropical disease product priority review voucher program 
     to encourage prevention and treatment of coccidioidomycosis.
```
