# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6219

**Measures:** H.R. 6219

```
            By Mr. KEAN of New Jersey:
        H.R. 6219.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Administrator of the National Aeronautics 
     and Space Administration to establish a program to identify, 
     evaluate, acquire, and disseminate commercial Earth remote 
     sensing data and imagery in order to satisfy the scientific 
     operational, and educational requirements of the 
     Administration, and for other purposes.
```
