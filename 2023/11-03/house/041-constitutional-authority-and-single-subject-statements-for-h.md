# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6212

**Measures:** H.R. 6212

```
            By Mr. RUTHERFORD:
        H.R. 6212.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Prevent oil and gas drilling and exploration, from 
     occurring off the coast of Florida through 2032.
```
