# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1343

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:31 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1343. An act to codify the Institute for 
     Telecommunication Sciences and to direct the Assistant 
     Secretary of Commerce for Communications and Information to 
     establish an initiative to support the development of
```
