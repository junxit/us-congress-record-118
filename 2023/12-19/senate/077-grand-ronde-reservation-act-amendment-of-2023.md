# GRAND RONDE RESERVATION ACT AMENDMENT OF 2023

**Speaking:** Merkley, Jeff (M001176) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 1722

```
             GRAND RONDE RESERVATION ACT AMENDMENT OF 2023

  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 1722, which was received 
from the House and is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1722) to amend the Grand Ronde Reservation 
     Act, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. MERKLEY. I further ask that the bill be considered read a third 
time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Kelly). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

[[Page S6062]]

  The bill (H.R. 1722) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
