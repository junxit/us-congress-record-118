# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6848

**Measures:** H.R. 6848

```
            By Mr. CRENSHAW:
        H.R. 6848.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to prohibit 
     institutions of higher education from requiring ideological 
     oaths or similar statements, and for other purposes.
```
