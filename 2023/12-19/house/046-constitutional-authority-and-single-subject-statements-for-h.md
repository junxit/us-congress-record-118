# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6878

**Measures:** H.R. 6878

```
            By Mrs. SPARTZ:
        H.R. 6878.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Clean Air Act to exempt nonroad engines and 
     vehicles that are used for agricultural purposes from the 
     emission standards of that Act
```
