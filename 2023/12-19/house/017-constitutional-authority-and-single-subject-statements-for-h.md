# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6849

**Measures:** H.R. 6849

```
            By Mr. CRENSHAW:
        H.R. 6849.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the establishment of a Civilian Climate Corps, 
     American Climate Corps, or any similar program, and for other 
     purposes.
```
