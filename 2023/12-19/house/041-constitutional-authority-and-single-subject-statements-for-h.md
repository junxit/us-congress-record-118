# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6873

**Measures:** H.R. 6873

```
            By Mr. RESCHENTHALER:
        H.R. 6873.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to include 
     information relating to the rate of suicide among covered 
     Reserves in each National Veteran Suicide Prevention Annual 
     Report of the Office Mental Health and Suicide Prevention of 
     the Department of Veterans Affairs.
```
