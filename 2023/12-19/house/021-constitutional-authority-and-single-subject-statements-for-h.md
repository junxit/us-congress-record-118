# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6853

**Measures:** H.R. 6853

```
            By Mr. FEENSTRA:
        H.R. 6853.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the US Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is;
       Amends the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control Act 
     of 1974 to require the Congressional Budget Office to provide 
     to Congress information on payments from the Old-Age and 
     Survivors Insurance Trust Fund and the Disability Insurance 
     Trust Fund
```
