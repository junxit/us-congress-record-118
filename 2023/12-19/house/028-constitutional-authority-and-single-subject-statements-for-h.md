# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6860

**Measures:** H.R. 6860

```
           By Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania:
       H.R. 6860.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would restore the Medicare Seconday Payer Act's 
     protections for End Stage Renal Disease patients by ensuring 
     that private health plans do not discriminate against such 
     patients or adversely classify dialysis as compared to other 
     covered medical services.
```
