# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we stand in awe of You. Lord, when babies die at a 
church school, it is time for us to move beyond thoughts and prayers. 
Remind our lawmakers of the words of the British statesman Edmund 
Burke: All that is necessary for evil to triumph is for good people to 
do nothing.
  Lord, deliver our Senators from the paralysis of analysis that waits 
for the miraculous. Use them to battle the demonic forces that seek to 
engulf us.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
