# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1820

**Measures:** H.R. 1820

```
           By Mr. CALVERT:
       H.R. 1820.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 AND Article IV, Section 3, 
     Clause 2 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to exempt a local 
     recreational airport, located on Army Corp property, from a 
     Corp policy which would then allow the Corp to renew the 
     lease with the City of Corona which expires in in 2036.
```
