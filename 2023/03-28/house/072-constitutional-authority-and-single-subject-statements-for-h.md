# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1842

**Measures:** H.R. 1842

```
           By Mr. SMUCKER:
        H.R. 1842.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause I of the U.S. Constitution

       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to eliminate
```
