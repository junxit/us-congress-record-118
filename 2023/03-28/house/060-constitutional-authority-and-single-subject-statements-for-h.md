# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1830

**Measures:** H.R. 1830

```
           By Mr. CROW:
       H.R. 1830.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I. Section 8, Clause 3, authorizing Congress ``to 
     regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the several 
     States, and with Indian Tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill restricts the sale or transfer of shotguns and 
     rifles across state lines.
```
