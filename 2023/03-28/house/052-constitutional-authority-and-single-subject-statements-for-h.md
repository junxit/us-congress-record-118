# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1822

**Measures:** H.R. 1822

```
           By Mr. BERGMAN:
       H.R. 1822.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Restores criminal penalties on individuals who attempt to 
     take advantage of Veterans, while preserving the right for 
     Veterans to seek assistance from the private sector when 
     preparing their claim to VA.
```
