# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Reed, Jack (R000122) [D-RI]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. REED. Madam President, I have five requests for committees to 
meet during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of 
the Majority and Minority Leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


                      COMMITTEE ON ARMED SERVICES

  The Committee on Armed Services is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Thursday, September 28, 2023, at 9:30 a.m., to 
conduct a hearing on nominations.


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  The Committee on Energy and Natural Resources is authorized to meet 
during the session of the Senate on Thursday, September 28, 2023, at 10 
a.m., to conduct a hearing.


                          COMMITTEE ON FINANCE

  The Committee on Finance is authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Thursday, September 28, 2023, at 10 a.m., to conduct a 
hearing on nominations.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Thursday, September 28, 2023, at 10 a.m., to 
conduct an executive business meeting.


   SUBCOMMITTEE ON CHEMICAL SAFETY, WASTE MANAGEMENT, ENVIRONMENTAL 
                   JUSTICE, AND REGULATORY OVERSIGHT

  The Subcommittee on Chemical Safety, Waste Management, Environmental 
justice, and Regulatory Oversight of the Committee on Environment and 
Public Works is authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on 
Thursday, September 28, 2023, at 10 a.m., to conduct a hearing.

                          ____________________
```
