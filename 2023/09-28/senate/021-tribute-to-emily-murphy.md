# TRIBUTE TO EMILY MURPHY

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO EMILY MURPHY

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Emily Murphy, the 
Suwannee County Teacher of the Year from Suwannee Springcrest 
Elementary School in Live Oak, FL.
  Emily believes Suwannee's schools provide students the best choice to 
further their educational goals. She commits each day to ensure her 
students learn something new and takes pride in showcasing their 
success across Florida.
  Emily enjoys working with her fellow teachers as they develop lesson 
plans suited to meet the needs of their students. She views her job as 
making the first educational impact on her young students and hopes 
they take what they learn in stride. Emily was humbled to receive this 
important recognition because she works alongside many great educators.
  Emily teaches kindergarten at Suwannee Springcrest Elementary and has 
worked in the Suwannee County School District for the past few years.
  I extend my deepest gratitude and best wishes to Emily for her 
commitment to her students. I look forward to hearing about her 
continued good work in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
