# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5781

**Measures:** H.R. 5781

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
        H.R. 5781.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to permit payments 
     to be made to the law firms of court appointed attorneys, and 
     for other purposes abides by the single subject requirement 
     in that the provisions are limited to payments made to court 
     appointed attorneys and law firms.
```
