# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5794

**Measures:** H.R. 5794

```
           By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 5794.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to eliminate 
     conflicts of interest in conduct of quality management and 
     administrative investigations by the Veterans Health 
     Administration
```
