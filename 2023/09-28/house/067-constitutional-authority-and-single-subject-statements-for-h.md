# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5799

**Measures:** H.R. 5799

```
           By Mr. TONY GONZALES of Texas:
       H.R. 5799.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To designate the checkpoint of the United States Border 
     Patrol located on United States Highway 90 West in Uvalde 
     County, Texas, as the ``James R. Dominguez Border Patrol 
     Checkpoint''.
```
