# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mark (K000377) [D-AZ]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 30

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. KELLY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that tomorrow, 
Wednesday, March 1, at 11:30 a.m., the Senate vote on confirmation of 
the Guzman nomination followed by the motion to invoke cloture on the 
Lawless nomination; further, that notwithstanding rule XXII, at 2:15 
p.m., the Senate vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the Grey 
nomination; further, that following the cloture vote on the Grey 
nomination, the Senate proceed to legislative session and proceed to 
the immediate consideration of H.J. Res. 30; that at 4 p.m., the Senate 
vote on passage of the joint resolution and upon disposition of the 
joint resolution, the Senate resume executive session and, 
notwithstanding rule XXII, vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the 
Simmons nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. KELLY. For the information of the Senate, there will be two 
rollcall votes at 11:30 a.m., one rollcall vote at 2:15 p.m., and two 
rollcall votes at 4 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
