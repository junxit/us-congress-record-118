# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1230

**Measures:** H.R. 1230

```
           By Mr. CARSON:
       H.R. 1230.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill directs the Secretary of Agriculture to make 
     grants to States to support the establishment and operation 
     of grocery stores in underserved communities, and for other 
     purposes.
```
