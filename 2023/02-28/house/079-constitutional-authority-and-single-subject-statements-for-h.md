# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1258

**Measures:** H.R. 1258

```
           By Mr. PERRY:
       H.R. 1258.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8 and Amendment 2
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Ensuring gun owners are afforded due process for the 
     preservation of their Second Amendment rights as it relates 
     to determinations at certain federal agencies.
```
