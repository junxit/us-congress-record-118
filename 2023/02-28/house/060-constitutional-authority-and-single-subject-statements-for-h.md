# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1239

**Measures:** H.R. 1239

```
           By Mr. DIAZ-BALART:
       H.R. 1239.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill designates the area between the intersections of 
     16th Street, NW and Fuller Street, NW and 16th Street, NW and 
     Euclid Street, NW in the District of Columbia as Oswaldo Paya 
     Way.
```
