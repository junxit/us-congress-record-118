# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1243

**Measures:** H.R. 1243

```
           By Mr. GOOD of Virginia:
       H.R. 1243.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Restricting the ability of the federal government to 
     partner with the local agencies to perform no-knock raids on 
     law-abiding gun owners.
```
