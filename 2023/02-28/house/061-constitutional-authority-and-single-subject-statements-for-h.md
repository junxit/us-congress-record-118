# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1240

**Measures:** H.R. 1240

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 1240.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 Clause 4 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To transfer administrative jurisdiction of certain Federal 
     lands from the Army Corps of Engineers to the Bureau of 
     Indian Affairs, to take such lands into trust for the 
     Winnebago Tribe of Nebraska, and for other purposes.
```
