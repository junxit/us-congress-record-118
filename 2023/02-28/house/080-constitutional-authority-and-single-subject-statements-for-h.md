# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1259

**Measures:** H.R. 1259

```
           By Ms. SANCHEZ:
       H.R. 1259.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To allow independent music creators to deduct 100 percent 
     of recording production expenses in the year they are 
     incurred, rather than in later years.
```
