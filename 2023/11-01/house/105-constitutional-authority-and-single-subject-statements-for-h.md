# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6170

**Measures:** H.R. 6170

```
            By Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas:
        H.R. 6170.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Ceases immigration functions at the Southern Border until 
     the Secretary of Homeland Security can ensure that no 
     individual on the Federal Terrorist Watch list can enter the 
     United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
