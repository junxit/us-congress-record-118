# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6134

**Measures:** H.R. 6134

```
           By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
       H.R. 6134.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Truth-in-Tuition Act would provide students and parents 
     with transparency as they plan for the future and look for 
     ways to finance college costs.
```
