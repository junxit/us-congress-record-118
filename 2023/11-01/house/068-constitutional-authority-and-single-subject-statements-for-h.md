# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6133

**Measures:** H.R. 6133

```
            By Mr. BRECHEEN:
        H.R. 6133.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Health and Human Services from 
     finalizing, implementing, or enforcing the proposed rule, 
     entitled ``Safe and Appropriate Foster Care Placement 
     Requirements for Titles IV-E and IV-B.''
```
