# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6148

**Measures:** H.R. 6148

```
           By Mr. GRIJALVA:
       H.R. 6148.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To protect Native cultural sites located on Federal land, 
     to improve consultation with Indian Tribes, to bring parity 
     to Indian Tribes with regard to Federal public land 
     management laws, and for other purposes.
```
