# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5412

**Measures:** H.R. 5412

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 5412.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To create a commission to investigate how the United States 
     can maintain democratic traditions while responding to 
     threats posed by foreign and domestic terrorism.
```
