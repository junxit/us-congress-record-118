# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5421

**Measures:** H.R. 5421

```
           By Mrs. WATSON COLEMAN:
       H.R. 5421.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Clause 1 of Section 8 of 
     Article 1 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the 
     earned income tax credit to all taxpayers with dependents and 
     to qualifying students, and for other purposes.
```
