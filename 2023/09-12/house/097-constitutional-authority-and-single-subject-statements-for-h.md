# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5422

**Measures:** H.R. 5422

```
           By Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas:
       H.R. 5422.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Adds an additional function to the responsibilities of the 
     FinCEN domestic liaison program to include an annual small 
     business working group that shares information with small 
     businesses on rules, regulations, and compliance.
```
