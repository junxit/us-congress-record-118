# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5390

**Measures:** H.R. 5390

```
           By Mrs. MILLER-MEEKS:
       H.R. 5390.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Directs the Secretary of Commerce to conduct a study on the 
     feasibility of manufacturing more goods in the United States 
     that are key to our critical infrastructure sector.
```
