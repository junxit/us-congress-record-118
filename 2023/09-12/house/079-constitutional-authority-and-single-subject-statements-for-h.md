# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5404

**Measures:** H.R. 5404

```
           By Mr. FITZGERALD:
       H.R. 5404.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the Attorney General to annually report 
     to Congress on Department of Justice activities to counter 
     national security threats by the Chinese Communist Party.
```
