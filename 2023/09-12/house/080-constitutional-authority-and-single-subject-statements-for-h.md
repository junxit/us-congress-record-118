# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5405

**Measures:** H.R. 5405

```
           By Mr. FITZPATRICK:
       H.R. 5405.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the share of federal funds for cancer 
     research that is allocated to pediatric cancer research to 
     equal the percentage of the U.S. population that is under the 
     age of 18.
```
