# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5392

**Measures:** H.R. 5392

```
           By Mr. DUNN of Florida:
       H.R. 5392.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power ``to regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would alter the review process for Medicare local 
     coverage determinations
```
