# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5402

**Measures:** H.R. 5402

```
           By Ms. DeLAURO:
       H.R. 5402.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will strengthen fundamental protections that 
     allow workers to get the money they have earned through hard 
     work, and it will crack down on corporations that subject 
     workers to these abuses.
```
