                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 124, Jeffrey Irvine Cummings, of 
     Illinois, to be United States District Judge for the Northern 
     District of Illinois.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Brian Schatz, John 
           W. Hickenlooper, Margaret Wood Hassan, Gary C. Peters,

           Menendez, Michael F. Bennet.

nomination of Jeffrey Irvine Cummings, of Illinois, to be United States 
District Judge for the Northern District of Illinois, shall be brought
