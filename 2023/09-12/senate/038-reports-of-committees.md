# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S. 447, S. 1303, S. 1669

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Ms. CANTWELL, from the Committee on Commerce, Science, 
     and Transportation, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 447. A bill to establish a demonstration program for the 
     active remediation of orbital debris and to require the 
     development of uniform orbital debris standard practices in 
     order to support a safe and sustainable orbital environment, 
     and for other purposes.

     disclose comprehensive information to consumers about ticket 
     prices and related fees.

     to issue a rule requiring access to AM broadcast stations in 
     motor vehicles, and for other purposes.
```
