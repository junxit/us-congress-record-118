# SENATE RESOLUTION 336--PROHIBITING THE IMPOSITION OF VACCINATION REQUIREMENTS RELATING TO COVID-19 FOR SENATE PAGES

**Measures:** S.Res. 336

```
   SENATE RESOLUTION 336--PROHIBITING THE IMPOSITION OF VACCINATION 
           REQUIREMENTS RELATING TO COVID-19 FOR SENATE PAGES

  Mr. PAUL submitted the following resolution; which was referred to 
the Committee on Rules and Administration:

                              S. Res. 336

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. PROHIBITION ON COVID-19 VACCINATION REQUIREMENTS 
                   FOR SENATE PAGES.

       A Senate Page or applicant to be a Senate Page may not be 
     required to receive a vaccination for COVID-19.

                          ____________________
```
