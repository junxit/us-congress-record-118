# Vote on Hunt Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Hunt Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Welch). The question is, Will the Senate 
advise and consent to the Hunt nomination?
  Mr. GRASSLEY. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.

Feinstein) is necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator
```
