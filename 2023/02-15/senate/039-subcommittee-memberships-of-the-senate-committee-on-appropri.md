# SUBCOMMITTEE MEMBERSHIPS OF THE SENATE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE 118TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

```
SUBCOMMITTEE MEMBERSHIPS OF THE SENATE COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS FOR 
                           THE 118TH CONGRESS

  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
memberships on the twelve subcommittees of the Committee on 
Appropriations for the 118th Congress be printed in the Record. I thank 
my vice chair on the committee, Senator Collins, for her leadership and 
cooperation with me in making these important assignments.
  There being no objection, the material was ordered to be printed in 
the Record, as follows:
```
