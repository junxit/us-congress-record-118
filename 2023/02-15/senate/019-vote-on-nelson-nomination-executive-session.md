# Vote on Nelson Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Crapo, Mike (C000880) [R-ID], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

                       Vote on Nelson Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Nelson nomination?
  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
