# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, JANUARY 20, 2023, AT 1 P.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, JANUARY 20, 2023, AT 1 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Friday, January 20, 2023, at 1 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1 and 38 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Friday, January 20, 2023, at 1 p.m.
```
