# HONORING THE LIVES OF SERGEANT STEVEN ROBIN AND OFFICER BRANDEN ESTORFFE

**Speaking:** Ezell, Mike (E000235) [R-MS]

```
    HONORING THE LIVES OF SERGEANT STEVEN ROBIN AND OFFICER BRANDEN 
                                ESTORFFE

  (Mr. EZELL asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. EZELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor two 
fallen heroes: Bay St. Louis Police officers, Sergeant Steve Robin and 
Officer Branden Estorffe, who were shot and killed in the line of duty 
this past December.
  Sergeant Robin served 3 years with the Bay St. Louis Police 
Department after prior service with the Hancock County Sheriff's 
Office.
  Officer Estorffe served 2 years with the Bay St. Louis Police 
Department.
  During their law enforcement careers, both men received numerous 
accolades for their dedication to protecting their community. May we 
remember their selfless commitment, their service, and ultimately their 
sacrifice.
  Their families are in the thoughts and prayers of myself, this body, 
and everyone in Mississippi.

                          ____________________
```
