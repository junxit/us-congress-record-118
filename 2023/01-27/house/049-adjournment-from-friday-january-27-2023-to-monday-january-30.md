# ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JANUARY 27, 2023, TO MONDAY, JANUARY 30, 2023

**Speaking:** Rutherford, John H. (R000609) [R-FL]

```
 ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JANUARY 27, 2023, TO MONDAY, JANUARY 30, 2023

  Mr. RUTHERFORD. I ask unanimous consent that when the House adjourns 
today, it adjourn to meet on Monday next, when it shall convene at noon 
for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.

gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```
