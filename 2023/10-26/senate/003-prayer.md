# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, each day, it seems we face new challenges, unexpected 
hurdles, and tragic realities. Have mercy upon us and this land we 
love. Remind us that Your plans for us are better than anything we can 
plan for ourselves.
  Today, as our lawmakers depend on You for help, respond to their 
intercession with Your peace that transcends human understanding. Lord, 
fill them with the joy that comes from the strength You provide those 
who refuse to become weary in doing what is right.
  Remind us all that we are travelers in this life in search of a city 
that You have prepared for us.
  Lord, we mourn the tragic loss of life in the Maine mass shooting.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
