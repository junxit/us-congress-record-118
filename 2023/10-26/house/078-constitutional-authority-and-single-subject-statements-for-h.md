# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6105

**Measures:** H.R. 6105

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 6105.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clauses 3 and 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to deem 
     certain substances to be unsafe for use as food contact 
     substances, and for other purposes.
```
