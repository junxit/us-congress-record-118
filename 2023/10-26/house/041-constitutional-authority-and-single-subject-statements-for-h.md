# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6068

**Measures:** H.R. 6068

```
            By Mr. McCARTHY:
        H.R. 6068.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To allow a period in which members of the clergy may revoke 
     their exemption from Social Security coverage, and for other 
     purposes.
```
