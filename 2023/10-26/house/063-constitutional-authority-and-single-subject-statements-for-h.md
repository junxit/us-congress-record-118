# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6090

**Measures:** H.R. 6090

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 6090.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for the consideration of a definition of 
     antisemitism set forth by the International Holocaust 
     Remembrance Alliance for the enforcement of Federal 
     antidiscrimination laws concerning education programs or 
     activities, and for other purposes.
```
