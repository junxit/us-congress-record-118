# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6070

**Measures:** H.R. 6070

```
            By Mr. AMODEI:
        H.R. 6070.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority for which this bill is 
     authorized is under Article I, Section 8, granting Congress 
     the Power to provide for the common defense.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation authorizes the United States Air Force to 
     establish and use of up to 15 equipment pads at the Nevada 
     Test and Training Range.
```
