# CONGRATULATING ST. CHARLES WEST ON THEIR CLASS 3 GIRLS' CROSS-COUNTRY STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
 CONGRATULATING ST. CHARLES WEST ON THEIR CLASS 3 GIRLS' CROSS-COUNTRY 
                           STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating St. Charles West High School on their victory 
in Missouri's Class 3 Girls' Team Cross-Country Championship.
  By claiming the Class 3 title, the Warriors are now state champions 
for the second consecutive year. Every member of the team performed 
admirably, beating out their closest competition by nearly 100 points. 
The team should be commended for all their hard work throughout the 
past year and for bringing home a state championship to their school 
and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the St. Charles West 
Girls' Cross-Country Team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
