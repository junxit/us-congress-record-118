# HONORING THE PINECREST PATRIOTS ON WINNING THEIR SIXTH GIRLS GOLF STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
HONORING THE PINECREST PATRIOTS ON WINNING THEIR SIXTH GIRLS GOLF STATE 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Pinecrest 
High School Women's Golf Team on winning their second straight state 
championship.
  Last month, for the second straight year and the sixth time in school 
history, the women of Pinecrest High School have brought the North 
Carolina High School Athletic Association championship back home to 
Southern Pines. The Patriots won this year's championship by a hefty 10 
strokes over their closest rival, Northwest Guilford. After this year's 
victory, Pinecrest is tied for the most N.C. High School Athletic 
Association girls' golf titles of all time. Additionally, Pinecrest 
placed three individual golfers in the top 12 and four in the top 17.
  The coaches and players of the Pinecrest Women's Golf Team have 
brought great pride to both their school and all of Moore County. I 
wish them all the best and hope to see them continue their great 
success next season. Go Patriots!
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in congratulating the coaches and 
golfers of Pinecrest High School on their victory in this year's N.C. 
High School Athletic Association 4A girls' golf state championship.
```
