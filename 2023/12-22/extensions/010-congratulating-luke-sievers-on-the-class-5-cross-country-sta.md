# CONGRATULATING LUKE SIEVERS ON THE CLASS 5 CROSS-COUNTRY STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
    CONGRATULATING LUKE SIEVERS ON THE CLASS 5 CROSS-COUNTRY STATE 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Luke Sievers on his victory in Missouri's 
Class 5 Individual Cross-Country Championship.
  Luke claimed the Class 5 title by setting a course-record time of 14 
minutes, 51.9 seconds in the 5,000-meter race to secure his first high 
school cross-country victory and lead the team to a win in the Team 
Championship. Luke should be commended for all his hard work throughout 
the past year and for bringing

[[Page E1257]]

home a state championship to his school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Luke Sievers for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
