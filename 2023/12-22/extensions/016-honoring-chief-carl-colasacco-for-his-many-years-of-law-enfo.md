# HONORING CHIEF CARL COLASACCO FOR HIS MANY YEARS OF LAW ENFORCMEENT SERVICE TO THE PEOPLE OF NORTH CAROLINA

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
  HONORING CHIEF CARL COLASACCO FOR HIS MANY YEARS OF LAW ENFORCMEENT 
                SERVICE TO THE PEOPLE OF NORTH CAROLINA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Chief Carl 
Colasacco on his retirement from the Aberdeen Police Department and to 
thank him for his more than 31 years of law enforcement service.
  Colasacco's esteemed law enforcement career began over three decades 
ago in 1992, when he started as a reserve officer with the Pinebluff 
Police Department. Over the course of his career, he would serve in 
various law enforcement capacities across our community. In 1995, 
Colasacco joined the Aberdeen Police Department, where he steadily rose 
the ranks. He served as a patrol officer, detective, Captain, Deputy 
Chief and, eventually, Chief of Police. The wealth of knowledge and 
experience Colasacco has provided is astounding and I think I speak for 
the whole of our community when I say he will be dearly missed as he 
enters retirement.
  Colasacco's dedication to service and constant willingness to step up 
when needed is commendable and inspiring. It has been an honor serving 
the people of Moore County alongside him and I wish him all the best as 
he begins this next chapter of his life.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in recognizing Chief Carl Colasacco 
for his great service to our community and the great state of North 
Carolina.

                          ____________________
```
