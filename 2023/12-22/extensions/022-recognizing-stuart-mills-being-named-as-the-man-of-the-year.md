# RECOGNIZING STUART MILLS BEING NAMED AS THE MAN OF THE YEAR BY THE MOORE COUNTY COMMUNITY FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
  RECOGNIZING STUART MILLS BEING NAMED AS THE MAN OF THE YEAR BY THE 
                   MOORE COUNTY COMMUNITY FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr, Speaker, I rise today to honor Stuart Mills on being 
named as the Man of the Year by the Moore County Community Foundation.
  Stuart Mills' impact on Moore County is similarly robust and wide 
ranging. He has and continues to work in many important community 
leadership roles, including as Chairman of The Given Tufts Foundation, 
director of Pinehurst's Village Heritage Foundation, as well as board 
Chairman and trustee of The O'Neal School. Likewise, Governor Roy 
Cooper appointed Stuart to North Carolina's Financial Literacy Council 
last year. Stuart's most prominent community role is as Executive 
Director of Partners for Children and Families, a private nonprofit 
organization promoting early childhood development in Moore County.
  Stuart's dedication to the people of Moore County is without 
question. His many years of service are both commendable and inspiring. 
I know I speak for everyone in our community when I say that we are 
grateful for all he has done to improve the place we call home.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in honoring Stuart Mills on being 
named Man of the Year and to thank him for all he has done for the 
people of Moore County.

                          ____________________
```
