# CONGRATULATING FATHER TOLTON ON THEIR CLASS 5 GIRLS' CROSS-COUNTRY STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
  CONGRATULATING FATHER TOLTON ON THEIR CLASS 5 GIRLS' CROSS-COUNTRY 
                           STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Father Tolton Catholic High School on their 
victory in Missouri's Class 5 Girls' Team Cross-Country Championship.
  The Trailblazers claimed the Class 5 title, becoming the only school 
in the state to win state titles in three separate classes. The team 
should be commended for all their hard work throughout the past year 
and for bringing home this state championship to their school and 
community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Father Tolton Girls' 
Cross-Country Team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
