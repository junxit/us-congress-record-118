# CONGRATULATING ST. DOMINIC GIRLS' GOLF ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
     CONGRATULATING ST. DOMINIC GIRLS' GOLF ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the St. Dominic Girls Golf Team on their 
victory in Missouri's Class 3 Team Girls' Golf State Championship.
  The Crusaders claimed the Class 3 title by dominating their 
competition. By the end of the first day, the team held a 17-shot 
advantage and never looked back as they went on to win by 42 strokes. 
The team should be commended for all their hard work throughout the 
past year and for bringing home a state championship to their school 
and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the St. Dominic Girls' 
Golf Team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
