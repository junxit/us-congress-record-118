# CONGRATULATING THE SCHOOL OF THE OSAGE ON THEIR CLASS 2 GIRLS' GOLF STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
  CONGRATULATING THE SCHOOL OF THE OSAGE ON THEIR CLASS 2 GIRLS' GOLF 
                           STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the School of the Osage on their victory in 
Missouri's Class 2 Girls' Team Golf Championship.
  The Indians claimed the Class 2 title for the second consecutive year 
by beating Incarnate Word by 32 strokes. The team should be commended 
for all their hard work throughout the past year and for bringing home 
this state championship to their school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Osage Girls' Golf Team 
for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
