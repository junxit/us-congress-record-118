# CONGRATULATING ROCK BRIDGE CROSS-COUNTRY ON THEIR CLASS 5 BOYS' STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
 CONGRATULATING ROCK BRIDGE CROSS-COUNTRY ON THEIR CLASS 5 BOYS' STATE 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 22, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr.Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Rock Bridge High School on their victory in 
Missouri's Class 5 Boys' Team Cross-Country Championship.
  The Bruins claimed the Class 5 title. having not one but two top-
three finishers. They won by just 8 points, meaning that every student-
athlete's determination was essential in clinching this victory. The 
team should be commended for all their hard work throughout the past 
year and for bringing home another state championship to their school 
and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Rock Bridge Cross-
Country Team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
