# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6892

**Measures:** H.R. 6892

```
            By Mr. McGOVERN:
        H.R. 6892.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1, the Spending Clause, and 
     Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18, the Necessary and Proper 
     Clause.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To expand coverage under Medicare, Medicaid, other 
     specified federal health care programs, and private health 
     insurance to include foods, vitamins, and individual amino 
     acids that are medically necessary for the management of 
     certain digestive and metabolic disorders and conditions.
```
