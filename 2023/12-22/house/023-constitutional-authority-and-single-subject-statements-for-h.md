# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6889

**Measures:** H.R. 6889

```
           By Ms. MALOY:
       H.R. 6889.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 Clauses I and 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend section 1105(a) of title 31, United States Code, 
     to require that annual budget submissions of the President to 
     Congress provide an estimate of the cost per taxpayer of the 
     deficit and of the public debt.
```
