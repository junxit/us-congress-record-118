# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6893

**Measures:** H.R. 6893

```
            By Ms. PORTER:
        H.R. 6893.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the disbursement of funds to entities owned or 
     controlled by individuals with executive or managerial 
     authority over the operations of political committees, and 
     for other purposes.
```
