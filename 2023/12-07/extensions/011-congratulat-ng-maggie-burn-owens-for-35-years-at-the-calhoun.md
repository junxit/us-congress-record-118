# CONGRATULAT[NG MAGGIE BURN OWENS FOR 35 YEARS AT THE CALHOUN COUNTY YMCA

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
  CONGRATULAT[NG MAGGIE BURN OWENS FOR 35 YEARS AT THE CALHOUN COUNTY 
                                  YMCA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, December 7, 2023

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
Maggie Burn Owens for her 35 years of service at the YMCA of Calhoun 
County.
  She currently serves as president and CEO of the YMCA of Calhoun 
County where 3,586 people benefit each year from the programs and 
services offered.
  Under her leadership, the YMCA of Calhoun County has undergone 
extensive efforts to boost financial assistance in order to grow the 
membership of the organization.
  The YMCA was able to offer $129,177 in financial assistance last year 
to children, individuals and families who might otherwise not have been 
able to participate in YMCA programs or memberships.
  We are extremely proud of Maggie and her ongoing commitment to the 
Calhoun County community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Maggie Burn Owens for 
her 35 years of service to the YMCA of Calhoun County.
```
