# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2670, H.R. 6611

```
Committee Meetings
REGIONAL MISSILE DEFENSE ASSETS--ASSESSING COCOM AND ALLIED DEMAND FOR 
CAPABILITIES
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces held a 
hearing entitled ``Regional Missile Defense Assets--Assessing COCOM and 
Allied Demand for Capabilities''. Testimony was heard from John D. 
Hill, Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Space and Missile 
Defense Policy, Department of Defense; Major General Sean Gainey, U.S. 
Army, Director, DAMO-Fires and Joint Capabilities, G-3/5/7; Rear 
Admiral Douglas Williams, U.S. Navy, Acting Director, Missile Defense 
Agency, Department of Defense; and Brigadier General Clair A. Gill, 
U.S. Army, Deputy Director for Regional Operations and Force 
Management, Joint Staff J-3.
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: Full Committee held a 
markup on H.R. 6611, the ``FISA Reform and Reauthorization Act of 
2023''. H.R. 6611 was ordered reported, without amendment.

Joint Meetings
NDAA
Conferees agreed to file a conference report on the differences between 
the Senate and House passed versions of H.R. 2670, to authorize 
appropriations for fiscal year 2024 for military activities of the 
Department of Defense and for military construction, and for defense 
activities of the Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel 
strengths for such fiscal year.
```
