# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6688

**Measures:** H.R. 6688

```
            By Mr. RESCHENTHALER:
        H.R. 6688.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To permit grant funds to be used to identify unidentified 
     human remains without regard as to whether the manner of 
     death is determined to be a homicide.
```
