# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6656

**Measures:** H.R. 6656

```
            By Mr. CALVERT:
        H.R. 6656.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution, specifically clause 1 and clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Directs the Social Security Administration and the 
     Department of Veterans Affairs to introduce a more robust 
     public public phone line policy, that lowers wait time and 
     improves customer service.
```
