# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6662

**Measures:** H.R. 6662

```
            By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
        H.R. 6662.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Transforming Hiring in Rural Industries and Vital 
     Economies Act establishes a federal interagency council to 
     oversee and coordinate investments in rural areas.
```
