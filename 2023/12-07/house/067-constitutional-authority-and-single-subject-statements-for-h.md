# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6677

**Measures:** H.R. 6677

```
            By Mr. LYNCH:
        H.R. 6677.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII, Clause XVIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will amend the National Levee Safety Act of 2007 
     to include hard and soft infrastructure in addition to levees 
     to the approved structures to fund.
```
