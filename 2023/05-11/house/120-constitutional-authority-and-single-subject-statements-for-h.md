# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3246

**Measures:** H.R. 3246

```
           By Mr. MEEKS:
       H.R. 3246.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the imposition of sanctions with respect to 
     any foreign person endangering the integrity or safety of the 
     Zaporzhzhia nuclear power plant.
```
