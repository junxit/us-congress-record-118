# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3277

**Measures:** H.R. 3277

```
           By Mr. WALBERG:
       H.R. 3277.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Energy to assign energy 
     emergency and energy security functions to an Assistant 
     Secretary, including responsibilities with respect to 
     infrastructure and cybersecurity.
```
