# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3272

**Measures:** H.R. 3272

```
           By Ms. STEFANIK:
       H.R. 3272.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the US Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To award a Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to the 
     brave women who served in World War II as members of the U.S. 
     Army Nurse Corps and U.S. Navy Nurse Corps.
```
