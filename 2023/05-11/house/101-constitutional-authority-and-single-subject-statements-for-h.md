# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3227

**Measures:** H.R. 3227

```
           By Mr. ESTES:
       H.R. 3227.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill repeals certain restrictions under Medicare and 
     Medicaid that prohibit the approval of nurse-aide training 
     and competency evaluation programs in skilled nursing 
     facilities that have been subject to specified regulatory 
     actions (e.g., civil penalties) for substandard quality of 
     care.
```
