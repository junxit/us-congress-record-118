# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3255

**Measures:** H.R. 3255

```
           By Mr. PAYNE:
       H.R. 3255.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of congress to make rules for the common defense 
     and general welfare of the United States as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution. 
     Interstate Commerce
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Rail health and safety
```
