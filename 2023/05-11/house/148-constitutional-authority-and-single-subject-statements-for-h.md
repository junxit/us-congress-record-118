# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3274

**Measures:** H.R. 3274

```
           By Ms. STRICKLAND:
       H.R. 3274.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to provide 
     that the 3-month State residency requirement for applicants 
     or naturalized citizenship do not apply with respect to 
     spouses of members of the Armed Forces serving on active duty 
     at a location in the United States, and or other purposes.
```
