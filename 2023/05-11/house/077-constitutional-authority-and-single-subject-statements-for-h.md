# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3203

**Measures:** H.R. 3203

```
           By Mr. BARR:
        H.R. 3203.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To impose sanctions with respect to Chinese producers of 
     synthetic opioids and opioid precursors, to hold Chinese 
     officials accountable for the spread of illicit fentanyl, and 
     for other purposes.
```
