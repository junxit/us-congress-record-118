# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3233

**Measures:** H.R. 3233

```
           By Mr. GARCIA of Illinois:
       H.R. 3233.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article 1 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill will help create a well-prepared and diverse 
     educator workforce. These reforms will help to ensure that 
     students that are the furthest from opportunity have access 
     to diverse teachers who have completed high-quality pathways 
     into the profession.
```
