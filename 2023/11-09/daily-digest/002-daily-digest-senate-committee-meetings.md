# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
AVIATION SAFETY
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Subcommittee on 
Aviation Safety, Operations, and Innovation concluded a hearing to 
examine addressing close calls to improve aviation safety, after 
receiving testimony from Jennifer L. Homendy, Chair, National 
Transportation Safety Board; Timothy L. Arel, Chief Operating Officer, 
Air Traffic Organization, Federal Aviation Administration; Rich Santa, 
National Air Traffic Controllers Association, AFL-CIO, Washington, 
D.C.; Jason Ambrosi, Air Line Pilots Association, International, 
McLean, Virginia; and J. Randolph Babbitt, Babbitt and Associates, LLC, 
Dallas, Texas.
COAL MINE LAND RECLAMATION AND ECONOMIC REVITALIZATION
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine the implementation of federal coal mine land 
reclamation and abandoned coal mine land economic revitalization 
programs, after receiving testimony from Glenda H. Owens, Deputy 
Director, Office of Surface Mining Reclamation and Enforcement, 
Department of the Interior; Rob Rice, West Virginia Department of 
Environmental Protection Division of Land Restoration Deputy Cabinet 
Secretary and Director, Charleston; and Don Newton, Wyoming Department 
of Environmental Quality Abandoned Mine Land Division Administrator, 
Cheyenne.
TAX CODE
Committee on Finance: Committee concluded a hearing to examine how the 
tax code affects high-income individuals and tax planning strategies, 
after receiving testimony from Chye-Ching Huang, New York University 
School of Law Tax Law Center, and Morris Pearl, Patriotic Millionaires, 
both of New York, New York; and William McBride, Tax Foundation, and 
Douglas Holtz-Eakin, American Action Forum, both of Washington, D.C.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee ordered favorably reported the 
nominations of Mustafa Taher Kasubhai, to be United States District 
Judge for the District of Oregon, and Eumi K. Lee, to be United States 
District Judge for the Northern District of California.
```
