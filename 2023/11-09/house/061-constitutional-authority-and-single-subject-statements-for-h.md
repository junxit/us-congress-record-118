# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6337

**Measures:** H.R. 6337

```
            By Mr. GARBARINO:
        H.R. 6337.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to make 
     grants to veterans service organizations for facility and 
     technology improvements, and for other purposes.
```
