# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5228

**Measures:** H.R. 5228

```
            By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 5228.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces, as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the Air Force to develop a plan 
     for the recapitalization of fighter squadrons of the Air 
     National Guard and the Air Force Reserve and to limit 
     modifications to the flying missions of such squadrons until 
     such plan is complete.
```
