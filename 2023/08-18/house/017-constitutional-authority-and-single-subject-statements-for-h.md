# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5227

**Measures:** H.R. 5227

```
            By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 5227.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces, as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Department of Defense from acquiring 
     computers or printers manufactured by or from entities 
     controlled by the government of the People's Republic of 
     China, and for other purposes.
```
