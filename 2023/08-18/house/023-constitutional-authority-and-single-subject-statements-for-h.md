# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5233

**Measures:** H.R. 5233

```
            By Mr. CARTER of Georgia:
       H.R. 5233.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Clause 1 of 
     Section 8 of Article I of the United States Constitution and 
     Amendment XVI of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Passport Act of June 4, 1920, to make certain 
     improvements with respect to expenditure and other 
     authorities, and for other purposes.
```
