# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5232

**Measures:** H.R. 5232

```
            By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 5232.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces, as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States 
     Constitution

[[Page H4193]]

       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to develop a plan to 
     equip and train Iraqi security forces and Kurdish Peshmerga 
     forces, and for other purposes.
```
