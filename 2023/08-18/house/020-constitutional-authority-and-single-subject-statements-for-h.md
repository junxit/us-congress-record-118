# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5230

**Measures:** H.R. 5230

```
            By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 5230.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces, as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to carry out a pilot 
     program under which the Secretary enters into a contract for 
     the calculation, using industry standard machine learning and 
     artificial intelligence algorithms, the monthly rates of 
     basic allowance for housing for certain military housing 
     areas, and for other purposes.
```
