# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5701

**Measures:** H.R. 5701

```
           By Mr. CASAR:
       H.R. 5701.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to prohibit the 
     placement of a photograph of any household member on an 
     electronic benefit card used by such household to purchase 
     food with supplemental nutrition assistance program benefits 
     provided under such Act.
```
