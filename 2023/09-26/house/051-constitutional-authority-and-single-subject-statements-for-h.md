# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5704

**Measures:** H.R. 5704

```
           By Mr. DeSAULNIER:
       H.R. 5704.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To promote specified minimum requirements for local 
     educational agencies and public elementary and secondary 
     schools relating to the prevention and treatment of 
     concussions suffered by students.
```
