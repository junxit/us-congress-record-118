# RECESS UNTIL WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 27, 2023, AT 10 A.M.

```
         RECESS UNTIL WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 27, 2023, AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand in recess under the previous
```
