# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who made us in Your image, thank You for Your sustaining 
power. Because of You, we receive the gift of heartbeats each day. 
Great is Your faithfulness. Lord, enable us to see Your divine image in 
every human being.
  Bless our lawmakers. Bring to the surface the goodness within each of 
them. Keep them safe as You give them the wisdom to do Your will on 
Earth, even as it is done in Heaven.
  Lord, give us all insights into Your will and the courage to do it.
  We pray in Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
