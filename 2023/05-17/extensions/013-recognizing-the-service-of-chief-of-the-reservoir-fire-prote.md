# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF CHIEF OF THE RESERVOIR FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT SCOTT BERRY

**Speaking:** Guest, Michael (G000591) [R-MS]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF CHIEF OF THE RESERVOIR FIRE PROTECTION 
                          DISTRICT SCOTT BERRY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL GUEST

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 17, 2023

  Mr. GUEST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the service of 
Chief of the Reservoir Fire Protection District, Scott Berry. Chief 
Berry is a second-generation fire fighter who began his career as a 
volunteer at the Reservoir Fire Protection District. Over a 34-year 
career, Chief Berry has remained loyal to the same fire district and 
has led firefighters in the Reservoir District since 1996.
  For his exceptional leadership, Chief Berry was elected as the 
President of the Mississippi Fire Chiefs Association in 2012 and was 
selected as Mississippi Fire Chief of the Year in 2022. Chief Berry has 
also contributed to the wellbeing of his community--serving as Chairman 
of the Mississippi Burn Camp for Kids and a CPR/First Aid Instructor. 
Outside of his community service activities, Chief Berry spends his 
time as a dedicated husband to his wife, Christy Lane, and loving 
father to his 2 daughters, Sarah and Rachel.
  On behalf of Mississippi's Third Congressional District and the 
United States House of Representatives, I thank Chief Berry for his 
service to his community and state.

                          ____________________
```
