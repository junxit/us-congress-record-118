# TRIBUTE TO ELLA TANNER

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ELLA TANNER

  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the opportunity 
to express my appreciation to Ella Tanner for her hard work as an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Ella is a native of Virginia. She is currently a senior at McLean 
High School and plans to attend Brigham Young University in the fall. 
She has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made her an 
invaluable asset to our office. The quality of her work is reflected in 
her great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Ella for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.

                          ____________________
```
