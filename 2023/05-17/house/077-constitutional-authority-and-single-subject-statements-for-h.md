# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3397

**Measures:** H.R. 3397

```
            By Mr. CURTIS:
       H.R. 3397.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of the Bureau of Land Management to 
     withdraw a rule of the Bureau of Land Management relating to 
     conservation and landscape health.
```
