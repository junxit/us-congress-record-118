# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3401

**Measures:** H.R. 3401

```
           By Mr. ALFORD:
       H.R. 3401.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8
       The Congress shall provide for the common Defence and 
     general Welfare of the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to conduct a study on 
     infrastructure for B-2 aircraft at Whiteman Air Force Base, 
     Missouri.
```
