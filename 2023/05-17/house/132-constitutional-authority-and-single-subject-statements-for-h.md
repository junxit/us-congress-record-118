# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3452

**Measures:** H.R. 3452

```
            By Ms. VAN DUYNE:
        H.R. 3452.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require that certain loans made to parents on behalf of 
     a dependent student and to graduate students are included in 
     the definition of cohort default rate under the Higher 
     Education Act of 1965, to require the Secretary of Education 
     to report default rates for such loans, and for other 
     purposes.
```
