# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3394

**Measures:** H.R. 3394

```
           By Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi:
       H.R. 3394.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To improve the ability of the Transportation Security 
     Administration to carry out its security mission by ensuring 
     that revenues collected from passengers as aviation security 
     fees are used to help finance the costs of aviation security 
     screening.
```
