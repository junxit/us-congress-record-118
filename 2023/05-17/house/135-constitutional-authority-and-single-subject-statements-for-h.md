# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3455

**Measures:** H.R. 3455

```
            By Mr. WALTZ:
        H.R. 3455.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide that sums 
     in the Thrift Savings Fund may not be invested in securities 
     that are listed on certain foreign exchanges, and for other 
     purposes.
```
