# HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF BYRNE CONSTRUCTION SERVICES

**Speaking:** Granger, Kay (G000377) [R-TX]

```
     HONORING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF BYRNE CONSTRUCTION SERVICES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAY GRANGER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 3, 2023

  Ms. GRANGER. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
Proclamation.
  Whereas, Thos. S. Byrne, a distinguished construction services firm, 
was founded on July 17, 1923, in Fort Worth, Texas, by the visionary 
Thomas Sneed Byrne, a native Texan and an esteemed alumnus of the 
Massachusetts Institute of Technology;
  Whereas, throughout its illustrious history, Byrne has left an 
indelible mark on the Stale of Texas, shaping much of the Texas skyline 
and contributing to the growth and development of communities through 
its commitment to excellence, quality, and service;
  Whereas, from its first milestone project completion in 1928, the 
Montgomery Ward's Regional Headquarters, to its innovative 
contributions recognized with the ``Build/America Award'' in 1972 for 
the revolutionary construction techniques utilized in the Kimbell Art 
Museum in Fort Worth, Byrne has consistently exceeded expectations and 
set new standards for architecturally significant and high-quality 
projects;
  Whereas, in 1995, John Avila, Jr. exemplified his dedication to 
construction and community by acquiring Thos. S. Byrne, where he 
skillfully expanded the company's presence and influence, while 
fostering long-lasting relationships and a warm family atmosphere among 
employees and clients alike growing the firm on the mantra People Build 
Buildings and dedicating our success to the men and women of Byrne;
  Whereas, John Avila's visionary leadership led Byrne to diversify its 
pursuits, resulting in strong portfolios in aviation, municipal, 
healthcare, historic restoration, commercial, education, and high-end 
residential markets, with a pioneering focus on technology in 
construction;
  Whereas, in 2015, a new chapter began as John Avila passed on the 
mantle of Chairman to his sons, Matthew and Paul Avila, who have since 
taken on the roles of CEO and COO respectively, carrying forward the 
legacy of excellence, quality, and service for the next generation;
  Now, therefore, I, Kay Granger, United States Representative, do 
hereby proclaim and commend the remarkable journey of Byrne, from its 
humble beginnings in 1923 to becoming one of the largest construction 
services firms in the state, and a true embodiment of dedication, 
innovation, and community-driven values;
  I call upon the citizens of Texas and beyond to recognize and 
celebrate the immense contributions of Thos. S. Byrne to the built 
environment and cultural fabric of our communities, as well as their 
dedication to delivering projects that have defined the very essence of 
our State;

                          ____________________
```
