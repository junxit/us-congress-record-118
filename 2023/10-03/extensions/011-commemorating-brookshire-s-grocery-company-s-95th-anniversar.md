# COMMEMORATING BROOKSHIRE'S GROCERY COMPANY'S 95TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Gooden, Lance (G000589) [R-TX]

```
     COMMEMORATING BROOKSHIRE'S GROCERY COMPANY'S 95TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LANCE GOODEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 3, 2023

  Mr. GOODEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
Brookshire's Grocery Company on its 95th anniversary and commemorate 
its service to the Texas community. In 1928, Wood T. Brookshire and his 
wife founded their grocery company at a small store in Tyler, Texas. 
The store began with four employees and has grown to over 200 stores 
across several other states. To this day, Brookshire's is led by the 
founder's grandson, Brad Brookshire.
  Brookshire's has grown from its humble beginnings into a thriving 
enterprise known for its philanthropic efforts and dedication to its 
local community. Brookshire's has consistently led hunger relief 
efforts across the South, among other important initiatives. I extend 
my gratitude to Brookshire's not only for its commitment to excellent 
customer service, but consistently going above and beyond to deliver 
the best experience for its customers. I have no doubt Brookshire's 
stores across Texas, Louisiana, Arkansas, and Oklahoma will continue to 
prosper and serve as an inspiration for entrepreneurs. Texans certainly 
look forward to its positive impact on the community for many years to 
come.
```
