# HOUSE BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 7, H.R. 346, H.R. 423, H.R. 1096, H.R. 2544, H.R. 3672, H.R. 3746, H.R. 4004, H.R. 5860

```
       HOUSE BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates he had approved and signed bills and a joint resolution of the 
following titles:

           April 10, 2023:
       H.J. Res. 7. A joint resolution relating to a national 
     emergency declared by the President on March 13, 2020.
           June 3, 2023:
       H.R. 346. An Act to establish a task force on improvements 
     for notices to air missions, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3746. An Act to provide for a responsible increase to 
     the debt ceiling.
           July 26, 2023:
       H.R. 1096. An Act to require the Secretary of the Treasury 
     to mint coins in commemoration of the 250th Anniversary of 
     the United States Marine Corps, and to support programs at 
     the Marine Corps Heritage Center.
           July 28, 2023:
       H.R. 423. An Act to take certain land located in San Diego 
     County, California, into trust for the benefit of the Pala 
     Band of Mission Indians, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3672. An Act to designate the clinic of the Department 
     of Veterans Affairs in Indian River, Michigan, as the ``Pfc. 
     Justin T. Paton Department of Veterans Affairs Clinic''.
           August 7, 2023:
       H.R. 4004. An Act to approve and implement the Agreement 
     between the American Institute in Taiwan and the Taipei 
     Economic and Cultural Representative Office in the United 
     States regarding Trade between the United States of America 
     and Taiwan, and for other purposes.
           September 2, 2023:
       H.R. 2544. An Act to improve the Organ Procurement and 
     Transplantation Network, and for other purposes.
           September 30, 2023:
       H.R. 5860. An Act making continuing appropriations for 
     fiscal year 2024, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
