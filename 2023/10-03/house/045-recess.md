# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 12(a) of rule I, the 
Chair declares the House in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  Accordingly (at 4 o'clock and 48 minutes p.m.), the House stood in 
recess.
```
