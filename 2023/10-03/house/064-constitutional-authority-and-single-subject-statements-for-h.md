# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5877

**Measures:** H.R. 5877

```
           By Mr. FINSTAD:
       H.R. 5877.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Consolidated Farm and Rural Development Act to 
     direct the Secretary of Agriculture to create the USDA 
     Express Guaranteed Farm Ownership and Guaranteed Farm 
     Operating Loan programs, enabling faster access to credit for 
     America's farmers and ranchers.
```
