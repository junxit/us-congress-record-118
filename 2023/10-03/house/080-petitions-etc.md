# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-8. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Legislative 
     Council Service, relative to a letter urging conferees from 
     the United States House and Senate to help people in New 
     Mexico who have been exposed to radiation by supporting the 
     inclusion of the RECA amendments in the National Defense 
     Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2024; which was referred 
     jointly to the Committees on the Judiciary, Education and the 
     Workforce, and Energy and Commerce.
```
