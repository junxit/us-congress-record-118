# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 5, H.Con.Res. 29, H.Con.Res. 52, S. 57, H.Con.Res. 88, H.Con.Res. 105, H.Con.Res. 109, H.Con.Res. 131, H.Res. 229, H.Res. 239

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:
       ML-71. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 131, memorializing the United States Congress 
     to take such actions as are necessary to codify the 
     terminology of ``solar compound'' and ''wind installation'' 
     in lieu of ''solar farm`` and ''wind farm'' to accurately 
     reflect the purpose and function of these renewable energy 
     resources; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.
       ML-72. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent Resolution No. 88, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     as are necessary to compel the United States Food and Drug 
     Administration to fulfill its duties regarding inspection and 
     testing of imported seafood; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       ML-73. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 105, urging and requesting the Louisiana 
     Department of Health and the united States Food and Drug 
     Administration to expand the testing of imported shrimp 
     products for banned drugs, bacteria, and disease and to 
     increase public awareness to the possible health risks 
     associate with imported shrimp; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Energy and Commerce.
       ML-74. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 229, 
     memorializing the United States Environmental Protection 
     Agency to take such actions as are necessary to timely review 
     and grant the state of Louisiana's application for primacy in 
     the administration Class VI injection well permitting and to 
     express support in furtherance thereof to maintain and extend 
     Louisiana's global leadership in transformative energy 
     innovation; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.
       ML-75. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     California, relative to Senate Concurrent Resolution No. 57, 
     extending to the people of the Philippine Province of Bulacan 
     an invitation to join with California in a sister state 
     relationship, in order to encourage and facilitate mutually 
     beneficial educational, economic, and cultural exchanges; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
       ML-76. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 52, urging and requesting the United States 
     Congress and the National Security Council to conduct a 
     formal review of the Status of Forces Agreement between the 
     United States and Japan to ensure that the agreement is in 
     the best interest of the United States and adequately 
     protects American service members in accordance with 
     constitutional rights and the United States Department of 
     Defense policy; ; which was referred to the Committee on 
     Foreign Affairs.
       ML-77. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent Resolution No. 29, 
     urging the President of the United States to reconsider an 
     agreement signed with Ecuador that facilitates growth of 
     certain aquaculture exports from Ecuador to the United 
     States; which was referred to the Committee on Foreign 
     Affairs.
       ML-78. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent Resolution No. 109, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     as are necessary to impose a quota or tariff on imported 
     shrimp, crab meat, and crawfish and enact a buy plan for 
     domestic shrimp, crab meat, and crawfish directly from 
     domestic commercial fishermen; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Ways and Means.
       ML-79. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 5, urging the United States Congress, 
     Department of Defense, and Department of Veterans Affairs to 
     prioritize research and investment in non-technology 
     treatment options for servicemembers and veterans who have 
     psychological trauma as a result of military service; which 
     was referred jointly to the Committees on Armed Services and 
     Veterans' Affairs.
       ML-80. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Resolution No. 239, 
     memorializing the United States Congress to take such actions 
     as are necessary to assist in the establishment of a 
     Louisiana pilot program for the recruitment of new United 
     States Army members to address military recruitment shortage; 
     which was referred jointly to the Committees on the Judiciary 
     and Armed Services.
```
