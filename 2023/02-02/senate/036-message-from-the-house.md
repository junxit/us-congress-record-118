# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 7, H.R. 139

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:55 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill and joint resolution, in which it requests 
the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 139. An act to require Executive agencies to submit to 
     Congress a study of the impacts of expanded telework and 
     remote work by agency employees during the COVID-19 pandemic 
     and a plan for the agency's future use of telework and remote 
     work, and for other purposes

  H.J. Res. 7. Joint resolution relating to a national emergency 
declared by the President on March 13, 2020.

                          ____________________
```
