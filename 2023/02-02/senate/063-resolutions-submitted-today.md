# RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 27, S.Res. 28, S.Res. 29

```
                      RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to the en bloc consideration of the following Senate 
resolutions, which were submitted earlier today: S. Res. 27, S. Res. 
28, and S. Res. 29.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolutions en bloc.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
resolutions be agreed to, the preambles be agreed to, and that the 
motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table, all 
en bloc.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolutions were agreed to.
  The preambles were agreed to.
  (The resolutions, with their preambles, are printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
