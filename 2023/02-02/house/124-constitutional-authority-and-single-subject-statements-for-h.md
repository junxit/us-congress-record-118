# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 808

**Measures:** H.R. 808

```
           By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
       H.R. 808.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article I, section 8 of the United States 
     Constitution, Congress has the power to ``provide for the 
     common Defense and general Welfare of the United State.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The ``Veterans Patient Advocacy Act'' aims to improve 
     veterans' access to advocacy services by ensuring adequate 
     staffing and coverage, especially in rural areas.
```
