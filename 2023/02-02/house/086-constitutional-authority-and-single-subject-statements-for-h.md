# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 776

**Measures:** H.R. 776

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 776.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 1, to provide for the Common Defence 
     (and general welfare) of the United States; Article I, 
     Section 3, to regulate Commerce with foreign nations; Article 
     I, Section 10, to punish felonies and offences against the 
     Law of Nations; Article I, Section 11, to grant letters of 
     reprisal and rules regarding captures.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation allows funds seized from foreign 
     trafficking cartels to be repurposed to build the wall along 
     the border between the United States and Mexico.
```
