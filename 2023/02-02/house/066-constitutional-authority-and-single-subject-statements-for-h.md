# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 756

**Measures:** H.R. 756

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 756.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the Administration for Children and 
     Families to award demonstration grants to foster care 
     stabilization agencies to improve services for foster youth 
     awaiting placement and for other emergency assistance.
```
