# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 775

**Measures:** H.R. 775

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 775.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 allows for the regulation of 
     Commerce with foreign nations; Article I, Section 8, Clause 4 
     grants Congress the right to set forth rules for 
     Naturalization.
       The single subject of this lgislation is:
       This legislation allows for the revocation of any US 
     passport belonging to a member of a foreign terrorist 
     organization.
```
