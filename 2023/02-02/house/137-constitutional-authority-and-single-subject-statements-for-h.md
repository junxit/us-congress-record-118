# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 821

**Measures:** H.R. 821

```
           By Mr. STEWART:
       H.R. 821.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       United States Constitution, Section 1 Article 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require providers of social media platforms to prohibit 
     children under the age of 16 from accessing such social media 
     platforms, and for other purposes.
```
