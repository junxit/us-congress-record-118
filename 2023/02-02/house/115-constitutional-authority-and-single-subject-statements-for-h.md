# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 799

**Measures:** H.R. 799

```
            By Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI:
        H.R. 799.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the sale, lease, or use of recalled motor 
     vehicles by Federal agencies, and for other purposes.
```
