# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 778

**Measures:** H.R. 778

```
 By Mr. DUNCAN: 
H.R. 778. Congress has the power to enact this legislation 
pursuant the following: Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 gives 
Congress the authority to create laws necessary for the proper 
execution of its powers. Also, Article I, Section 8, Clause 4 
allows Congress to make Rules for Naturalization.The single 
subject of this legislation is:


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


       This legislation makes presence in the Terrorist Screening 
     Database as grounds for deportation from the United States.
```
