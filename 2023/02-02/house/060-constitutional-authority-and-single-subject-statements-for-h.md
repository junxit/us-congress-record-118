# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 750

**Measures:** H.R. 750

```
           By Mrs. CAMMACK:
       H.R. 750.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires any person that sells or distributes a 
     mobile application that the Federal Government has prohibited 
     for Government-owned devices to disclose that fact to any 
     individual who downloads, updates, or otherwise uses such 
     application.
```
