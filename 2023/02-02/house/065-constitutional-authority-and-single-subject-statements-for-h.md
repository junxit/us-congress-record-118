# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 755

**Measures:** H.R. 755

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 755.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the Administration for Children and 
     Families to establish a competitive plot grant program for 
     foster care agencies to increase the placement of siblings 
     together in foster care.
```
