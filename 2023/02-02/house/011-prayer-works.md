# PRAYER WORKS

```
                              PRAYER WORKS

  (Mr. VAN ORDEN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. VAN ORDEN. Mr. Speaker, I stand here today to thank the Lord 
Jesus Christ, my constituents, and my colleagues for your thoughts and 
prayers and tender mercies that you have showed my family over these 
last trying months.
  I am here today to report to you that prayer works. As of yesterday, 
our daughter's cancer is in remission.
  Mr. Speaker, I will never forget the kindness shown to me by both 
sides of this aisle, and I humbly remain your servant.
```
