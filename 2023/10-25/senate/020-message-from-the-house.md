# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Res. 810

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 4:55 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to H. Res. 810, resolving that a message be sent to the Senate 
to inform that body that Mike Johnson, a Representative from the State 
of Louisiana, has been elected Speaker of the House of Representatives.
```
