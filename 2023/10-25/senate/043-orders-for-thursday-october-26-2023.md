# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2023

**Speaking:** Manchin, Joe, III (M001183) [D-WV]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 42, S.J.Res. 44, H.R. 4366

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2023

  Mr. MANCHIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Thursday, October 26; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and morning business be closed; that upon the conclusion of 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 198, 
H.R. 4366, as provided under the order of October 24; further, that if 
Senator Paul makes a motion to discharge S.J. Res. 44 from the 
Committee on Foreign Relations, the Senate vote on the motion at 11:30 
a.m.; further, that if the motion to discharge is not agreed to, the 
Senate vote on the Braun amendment No. 1182 to H.R. 4366; finally, that 
upon disposition of the Braun amendment, the Senate move to 
consideration of S.J. Res. 42; that there be up to 30 minutes of debate 
equally divided between the two leaders or their designees; and that 
following the use or yielding back of time, the joint resolution be 
considered read a third time and the Senate vote on passage of the 
joint resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
