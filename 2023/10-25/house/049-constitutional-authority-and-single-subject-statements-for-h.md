# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6051

**Measures:** H.R. 6051

```
            By Mr. HILL:
        H.R. 6051.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Treasury to report on 
     financial institutions' involvement with officials of the 
     Iranian Government, and for other purposes.
```
