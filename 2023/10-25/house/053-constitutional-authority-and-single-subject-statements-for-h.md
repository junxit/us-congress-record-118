# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6055

**Measures:** H.R. 6055

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 6055.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Section 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to provide penalties 
     for communicating threats that target schools.
```
