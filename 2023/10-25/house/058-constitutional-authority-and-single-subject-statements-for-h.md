# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6060

**Measures:** H.R. 6060

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 6060.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit any US assistance from being made available for 
     Palestinian-controlled areas of Judea and Samaria and Gaza
```
