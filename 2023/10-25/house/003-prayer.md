# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Jeremy Leganski, Immaculate Conception Church, Elmhurst, 
Illinois, offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, we praise You, we love You, and we thank You 
for this day.
  Draw near to these Members of Congress. Remind them of their calling 
to serve the true common good. Help them to be instruments of unity, 
peace, and reconciliation in our country and throughout the world. 
Bless them, guide them, and fill them with an abundance of Your grace.
  May they decide everything for the well-being of all, and may they 
never turn aside from Your will.
  May all that they do begin from You and, by You, be brought to 
completion.
  In Your name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
