# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6065

**Measures:** H.R. 6065

```
           By Mr. SMUCKER:
       H.R. 6065.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 3 of section 8 article 1 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish the American Worker Retirement Plan and tax 
     credit to improve the financial security of working Americans 
     by facilitating the accumulation of wealth.
```
