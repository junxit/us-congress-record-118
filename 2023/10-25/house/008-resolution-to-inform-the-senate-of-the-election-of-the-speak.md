# RESOLUTION TO INFORM THE SENATE OF THE ELECTION OF THE SPEAKER

**Speaking:** Scalise, Steve (S001176) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 810

```
                              {time}  1445
     RESOLUTION TO INFORM THE SENATE OF THE ELECTION OF THE SPEAKER

  Mr. SCALISE. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution and ask for 
its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res 810

       Resolved, That a message be sent to the Senate to inform 
     that body that Mike Johnson, a Representative from the State 
     of Louisiana, has been elected Speaker of the House of 
     Representatives.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
