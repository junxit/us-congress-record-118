# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6057

**Measures:** H.R. 6057

```
           By Mrs. McCLAIN:
       H.R. 6057.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation is solely for preventing the US from 
     reestablishing the JCPOA without Iran allowing UN nuclear 
     inspectors from inspecting and confirming that Iran is in 
     100% compliance with the JCPOA.
```
