# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 53, Karla Ann Gilbride, of 
     Maryland, to be General Counsel of the Equal Employment 
     Opportunity Commission for a term of four years.
         Charles E. Schumer, Ben Ray Lujan, Peter Welch, Tina 
           Smith, Tammy Duckworth, Tim Kaine, Richard J. Durbin, 
           Alex Padilla, Raphael G. Warnock, Christopher Murphy, 
           John W. Hickenlooper, Catherine Cortez Masto, Tammy 
           Baldwin, Edward J. Markey, Benjamin L. Cardin, Jack 
           Reed, Mazie K. Hirono.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory 
quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, October 4, be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
