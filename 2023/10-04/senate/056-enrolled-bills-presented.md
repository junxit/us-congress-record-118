# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 112, S. 2795

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, October 4, 2023, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 112. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     strengthen benefits for children of Vietnam veterans born 
     with spina bifida, and for other purposes
       S. 2795. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     extend and modify certain authorities and requirements 
     relating to the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other 
     purposes.
```
