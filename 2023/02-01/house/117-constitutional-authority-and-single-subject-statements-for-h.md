# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 722

**Measures:** H.R. 722

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
       H.R. 722.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To nearshore manufacturing in the Western Hemisphere to 
     reduce dependency on the People's Republic of China and to 
     decrease illegal immigration.
```
