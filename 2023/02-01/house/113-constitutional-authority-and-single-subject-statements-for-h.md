# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 718

**Measures:** H.R. 718

```
           By Mrs. GONZALEZ-COLON:
       H.R. 718.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:

[[Page H630]]

       The Congress has the power to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the 
     U.S. Constitution, which provide as follows:
       -The Congress shall have Power To lay and collect Taxes, 
     Duties, Imposts and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for 
     the common Defense and general Welfare of the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amendment to the definition of critical services eligible 
     for specified recovery funding.
```
