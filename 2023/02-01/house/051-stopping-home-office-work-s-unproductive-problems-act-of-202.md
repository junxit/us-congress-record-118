**Measures:** H.R. 139

     STOPPING HOME OFFICE WORK'S UNPRODUCTIVE PROBLEMS ACT OF 2023

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 139) to 
require Executive agencies to submit to Congress a study of the impacts 
of expanded telework and remote work by agency employees during the 
COVID-19 pandemic and a plan for the agency's future use of telework 
and remote work, and for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays 
were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  This is a 15-minute vote.
