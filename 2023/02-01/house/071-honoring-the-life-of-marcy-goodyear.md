# HONORING THE LIFE OF MARCY GOODYEAR

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF MARCY GOODYEAR

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
honor the life of Marcy Goodyear.
  Marcy was born in 1971 in East Point, Georgia. She moved to Albany in 
1974, where she attended Westover High School.
  In 2009, after living in Albany for many years, Marcy found her way 
to Darien, Georgia, located in the First Congressional District of 
Georgia.
  Marcy loved traveling and spending time with friends and family. She 
enjoyed working at Skippers' Fish Camp with her family as well as her 
time as a city councilwoman with the city of Darien.
  Marcy's smile was infectious, and she never met a stranger. She will 
be dearly missed by all of those who knew her.
  On behalf of myself and everyone in the First Congressional District, 
our thoughts and prayers are with Marcy's family and friends.

                          ____________________
```
