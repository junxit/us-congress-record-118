# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 716

**Measures:** H.R. 716

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 716.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1, Section 8, Article I of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for cost-of-living increases for certain Federal 
     benefits programs based on increases in the Consumer Price 
     Index for the elderly.
```
