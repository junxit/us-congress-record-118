# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
2023 FARM BILL
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine the 2023 Farm Bill, focusing on trade and 
horticulture, after receiving testimony from Alexis Taylor, Under 
Secretary of Trade and Foreign Agricultural Affairs, and Jennifer 
Lester Moffitt, Under Secretary for Marketing and Regulatory Programs, 
both of the Department of Agriculture; and Sarah Charles, Assistant to 
the Administrator, Bureau for Humanitarian Assistance, U.S. Agency for 
International Development.

intelligence matters, receiving testimony from officials of the 
intelligence community.
```
