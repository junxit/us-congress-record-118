Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded a hearing to examine

SeatGeek, and Clyde Lawrence, Lawrence, both of New York, New York; 
Jerry Mickelson, Jam Productions, LLC, Chicago, Illinois; Sal Nuzzo, 
The James Madison Institute, Tallahassee, Florida; and Kathleen 
Bradish, American Antitrust Institute, Washington, D.C.

Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
