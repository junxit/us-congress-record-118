# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3310

**Measures:** H.R. 3310

```
           By Ms. ADAMS:
       H.R. 3310.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To end preventable maternal mortality and severe maternal 
     morbidity in the United States and close disparities in 
     maternal health outcomes.
```
