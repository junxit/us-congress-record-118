# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3283

**Measures:** H.R. 3283

```
           By Mrs. MILLER-MEEKS:
       H.R. 3283.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires National Telecommunications and 
     Information Administration to periodically report to Congress 
     on whether the Departments of Interior and Agriculture have 
     established an online portal for the acceptance, processing, 
     and disposal of the common form application to deploy a 
     communications facility on federal property.
```
