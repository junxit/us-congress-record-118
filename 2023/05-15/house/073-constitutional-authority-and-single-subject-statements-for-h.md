# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3296

**Measures:** H.R. 3296

```
           By Mrs. RODGERS of Washington:
       H.R. 3296.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide that construction, rebuilding, or hardening of 
     communications facilities following a major disaster or an 
     emergency related to a wildfire is not subject to 
     requirements to prepare certain environmental or historical 
     preservation reviews.
```
