# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3293

**Measures:** H.R. 3293

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 3293
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 17 grants Congress the 
     authority to pass laws related to ``needful buildings'' on 
     lands owned by the federal government.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will create an interagency task force to expedite 
     broadband deployments on federal lands.
```
