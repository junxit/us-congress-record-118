# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3337

**Measures:** H.R. 3337

```
           By Mrs. MILLER-MEEKS:
       H.R. 3337.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would incentivize lower carbon emissions from 
     ethanol production and provide a more accurate measurement of 
     the carbon content of biofuels.
```
