# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3339

**Measures:** H.R. 3339

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 3339
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would require the Federal Bureau of Prisons to 
     place District of Columbia residents serving sentences for 
     D.C. Code felonies in BOP facilities within 250 miles of D.C.
```
