# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3351

**Measures:** H.R. 3351

```
           By Mr. VEASEY:
       H.R. 3351.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 Clause 3 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to include 
     expenses for certain nonathletic supplies in the above-the-
     line deduction for eligible educators, and to allow such 
     deduction to interscholastic sports administrators and 
     coaches.
```
