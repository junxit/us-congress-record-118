# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3332

**Measures:** H.R. 3332

```
           By Mrs. McBATH:
       H.R. 3332.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       To lay and collect taxes, duties, imposts and excises, to 
     pay the debts and provide for the common defense and general 
     welfare of the United States; but all duties, imposts and 
     excises shall be uniform throughout the United States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To extend eligibility for the Special Supplemental 
     Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC) for 
     new moms, and for other purposes.
```
