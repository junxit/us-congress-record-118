# Public Bills and Resolutions for H.R. 6211

**Measures:** H.R. 6211

```
           By Mr. ZINKE:
       H.R. 6211.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Homeland Security or the 
     Secretary of State, as applicable, from approving any 
     application for or issuing a non-immigrant or immigrant visa 
     to nationals of Palestine.
```
