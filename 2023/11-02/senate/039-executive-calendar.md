# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
consider the following nominations: all nominations on the Secretary's 
desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, Navy, and Space Force; that 
the nominations be confirmed en bloc; that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate; that no further motions be in order to any of the nominations; 
that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action; and 
that the Senate resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
