# Trump Investigation (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                          Trump Investigation

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, last Friday, the Department of Justice 
announced Donald Trump had been indicted on 37 counts related to his 
mishandling of classified documents. One only has to listen to the 
statements from former Trump National Security Advisor John Bolton and 
Trump's former Attorney General to know just how serious this 
indictment is.
  As I have said before: No one is above the law--including Donald 
Trump. This case must be allowed to play out through the legal process 
without outside political or ideological interference. I encourage both 
supporters and critics of Donald Trump to maintain the peace and let 
the justice system do its work.
```
