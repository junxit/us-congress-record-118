# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4048

**Measures:** H.R. 4048

```
           By Mr. SOTO:
       H.R. 4048.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the Secretary of Homeland Security to adjust 
     the status of certain aliens who are nationals of Venezuela 
     to that of aliens lawfully admitted for permanent residence, 
     and for other purposes.
```
