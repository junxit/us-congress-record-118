# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4004

**Measures:** H.R. 4004

```
           By Mr. SMITH of Missouri:
       H.R. 4004.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this legislation is to approve and 
     implement the Agreement between the American Institute in 
     Taiwan and the Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative 
     Office in the United States regarding Trade between the U.S. 
     and Taiwan, and to establish transparency and consultations 
     requirements for future negotiations.
```
