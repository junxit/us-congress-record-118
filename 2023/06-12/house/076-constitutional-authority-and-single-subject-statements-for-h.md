# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4039

**Measures:** H.R. 4039

```
           By Mr. MORAN:
       H.R. 4039.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the use of funds supporting any activities 
     within the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region of the People's 
     Republic of China.
```
