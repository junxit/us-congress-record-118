# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4042

**Measures:** H.R. 4042

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 4042.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 17 of section 8 of article 1 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would clarify that District of Columbia residents 
     may not be excluded or disqualified from jury service in the 
     D.C. Superior Court based on sexual orientation or gender 
     identity.
```
