# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                    JUNE 12, 2023.
     Hon. Kevin McCarthy
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker McCarthy: Pursuant to Section 114(b) of the 
     John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training and 
     Development Act (2 U.S.C. 1103), I hereby reappoint the 
     Honorable Terri A. Sewell of Alabama to the Board of Trustees 
     for the John C. Stennis Center for Public Service Training 
     and Development for a term of six years.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
