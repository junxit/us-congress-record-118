# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4009

**Measures:** H.R. 4009

```
           By Ms. BARRAGAN:
       H.R. 4009.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill establish emergency paid sick leave and expanded 
     family medical leave for individuals impacted by an
```
