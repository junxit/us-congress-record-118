# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4011

**Measures:** H.R. 4011

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 4011.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires group health plans and pharmacies 
     contracted with the plans to dispense medically necessary 
     oral medications within certain time frames.
```
