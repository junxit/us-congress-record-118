# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 151

**Measures:** H.R. 151

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 151.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       With this legislation, Congress is defending the 2nd 
     Amendment prerogative to keep and bear arms. Also, Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 1 gives Congress the right to lay and 
     collect taxes.
       Single Subject Statement:
       This legislation removes short-barreled shotguns from the 
     definition of firearms for purposes of the National Firearms 
     Act.
```
