# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING THE 118TH CONGRESS

```
                              {time}  1915
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED 
         BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING THE 118TH CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, January 7, 2023.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Patrick McHenry, the 
     Honorable Adrian Smith, the Honorable Robert Wittman, the 
     Honorable Andy Harris, the Honorable Richard Hudson, the 
     Honorable John Joyce, and the Honorable Guy Reschenthaler to 
     act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through the remainder of the One Hundred 
     Eighteenth Congress.
                                                   Kevin McCarthy,
                                                          Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointments are 
approved.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
