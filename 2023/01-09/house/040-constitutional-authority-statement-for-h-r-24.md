# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 24

**Measures:** H.R. 24

```
           By Mr. MASSIE:
       H.R. 24.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is authorized by Article 1, Section 8 of the 
     Constitution, which gives Congress the power ``to coin money, 
     regulate the value therof, and of foreign coin, and fix the 
     standard of weights and measures,'' and ``to provide for the 
     punishment of counterfeiting the securities and current coin 
     of the United States.''
```
