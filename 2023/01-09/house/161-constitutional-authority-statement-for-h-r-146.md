# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 146

**Measures:** H.R. 146

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 146.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, paragraph 14 bestows upon Congress 
     the responsibility to ``Make Rules for the Government.'' This 
     legislation informs one Department of the Government that 
     they are only to fly one flag over our embassies abroad--
     namely the national flag of the United States.
       Single Subject Statement:
       This legislation instructs the State Department to only fly 
     the national flag of the United States over our consulates 
     and embassies abroad.
```
