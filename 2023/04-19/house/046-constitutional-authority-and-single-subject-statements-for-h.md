# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2700

**Measures:** H.R. 2700

```
            By Mr. ALLEN:
        H.R. 2700.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reform the labor laws of the United States, and for 
     other purposes, by amending the National Labor Relations Act.
```
