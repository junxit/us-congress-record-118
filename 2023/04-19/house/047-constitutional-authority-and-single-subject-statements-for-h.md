# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2701

**Measures:** H.R. 2701

```
            By Ms. ESHOO:
        H.R. 2701.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for individual rights relating to privacy of 
     personal information, to establish privacy and security 
     requirements for covered entities relating to personal 
     information, and to establish an agency to be known as the 
     Digital Privacy Agency to enforce such rights and 
     requirements, and for other purposes.
```
