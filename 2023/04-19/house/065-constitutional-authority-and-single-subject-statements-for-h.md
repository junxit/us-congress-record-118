# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2719

**Measures:** H.R. 2719

```
            By Mr. NEWHOUSE:
        H.R. 2719.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act 
     of 1990 to establish a competitive grant program under which 
     the Secretary of Agriculture provides grants to land-grant 
     colleges and universities to support agricultural producers 
     in adopting conservation and innovative climate practices
```
