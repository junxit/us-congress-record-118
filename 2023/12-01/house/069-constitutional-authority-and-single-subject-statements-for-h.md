# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6553

**Measures:** H.R. 6553

```
           By Mr. MAGAZINER:
       H.R. 6553.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the waiver of costs of activities relating to 
     the evacuation of United States citizens endangered by acts 
     of terrorism or war in Israel instigated by Hamas and other 
     Islamist militant groups.
```
