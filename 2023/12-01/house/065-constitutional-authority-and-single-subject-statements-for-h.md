# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6549

**Measures:** H.R. 6549

```
           By Ms. ESHOO:
       H.R. 6549.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Administrator of the Federal Aviation 
     Administration to adjust certain requirements with respect to 
     sovereignty and use of airspace.
```
