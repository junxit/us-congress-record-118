# HOUR OF MEETING

**Speaking:** Smith, Christopher H. (S000522) [R-NJ]

```
                            HOUR OF MEETING

  Mr. SMITH of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet on Monday next, when 
it shall convene at noon for morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for 
legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Nunn of Iowa). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
