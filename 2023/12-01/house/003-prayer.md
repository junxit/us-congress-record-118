# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Pastor Jared Timberlake, Sandhill Christian Baptist Church, 
Wheelersburg, Ohio, offered the following prayer:
  Heavenly Father, we come to You today thankful for another day. We 
are thankful for Your blessings and Your love. God, I ask today that 
You would bring guidance to all of our Representatives and elected 
officials.
  As a Nation, may we always seek Your face and Your will. Lord, I pray 
that You will bring unity to this House and to those that serve. May 
they serve for the common good of our Nation.
  God, I thank You that You have allowed us to live in the greatest 
country in the world. I thank You for blessing America for 247 years.
  Now, God, it is my prayer that America will bless You in all that we 
do, and that we will always be one Nation under God. Protect us and 
guide us in Jesus' name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
