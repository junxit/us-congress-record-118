  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sergio Navarro

  Sergio has achieved great things, all while overcoming adversity and 
challenges along the way. Students like Sergio, who strive to make the 
most of their education, develop crucial skills and a work ethic that 
will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a testament 
to Sergio's hard work, determination, and perseverance at Jefferson 
Jr./Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Sergio Navarro on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
