# DAYANA BANUELOS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                            DAYANA BANUELOS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, December 1, 2023

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dayana Banuelos 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Dayana has achieved great things, all while overcoming adversity and 
challenges along the way. Students like Dayana, who strive to make the 
most of their education, develop crucial skills and a work ethic that 
will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a testament 
to Dayana's hard work, determination, and perseverance at Arvada High 
School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and promising 
future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Dayana Banuelos on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth award.
```
