will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a testament 
to Jesus's hard work, determination, and perseverance at Jefferson Jr./
Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Jesus Figueroa-Arriga on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
