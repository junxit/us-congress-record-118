# JUAN JOSE MORENO SANTOS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        JUAN JOSE MORENO SANTOS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, December 1, 2023

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Juan Jose 
Moreno Santos for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors 
for Youth Award.
  Juan Jose has achieved great things, all while overcoming adversity 
and challenges along the way. Students like Juan Jose, who strive to 
make the most of their education, develop crucial skills and a work 
ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a 
testament to Juan Jose's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Juan Jose Moreno Santos on achieving 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth award.
```
