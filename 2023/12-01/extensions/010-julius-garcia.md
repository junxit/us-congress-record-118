# JULIUS GARCIA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

                                 ______

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, December 1, 2023

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Julius Garcia 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Julius has achieved great things, all while overcoming adversity and 
challenges along the way. Students like Julius, who strive to make the 
most of their education, develop crucial skills and a work ethic that 
will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a testament 
to Julius's hard work; determination, and perseverance at Jefferson 
Jr./Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Julius Garcia on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth award.
