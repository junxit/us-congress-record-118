# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2508

**Measures:** H.R. 2508

```
           By Mr. MURPHY:
       H.R. 2508.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to ensure that 
     public institutions of higher education eschew policies that 
     improperly constrain the expressive rights of students, and 
     to ensure that private institutions of higher education are 
     transparent about, and responsible for, their chosen speech 
     policies.
```
