# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2538

**Measures:** H.R. 2538

```
           By Mr. TAKANO:
       H.R. 2538.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the sale of a firearm to, and the purchase of a 
     firearm by, a person who is not covered by appropriate 
     liability insurance coverage.
```
