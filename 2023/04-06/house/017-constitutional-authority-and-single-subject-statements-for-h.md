# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2480

**Measures:** H.R. 2480

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 2480.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Health and Human Services to 
     issue guidance on coverage under the Medicaid program under 
     title XIX of the Social Security Act of certain pelvic health 
     services furnished during the postpartum period, and for 
     other purposes
```
