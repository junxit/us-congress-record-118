# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rev. John A. Kellogg, Christ Church and Washington Parish, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Gracious God, watch over our country now and in the days ahead. Guide 
and bless our Representatives in their pursuit of faithful 
administration and wise laws. Grant them wisdom and integrity in the 
exercise of their duties. In the complexities of the issues they seek 
to address, give them quiet spirits and courageous hearts. Keep our 
Nation under Your care.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
