# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2497

**Measures:** H.R. 2497

```
           By Mr. HILL:
       H.R. 2497.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To permit an issuer, when determining the market 
     capitalization of the issuer for purposes of testing the 
     significance of an acquisition or disposition, to include the 
     value of all shares of the issuer.
```
