# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2542

**Measures:** H.R. 2542

```
           By Mr. WILLIAMS of New York:
       H.R. 2542.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Infrastructure Expansion Act is a bill that addresses 
     the single subject of precluding absolute liability on 
     federally funded projects.
```
