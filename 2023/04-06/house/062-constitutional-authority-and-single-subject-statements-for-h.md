# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2525

**Measures:** H.R. 2525

```
           By Mr. RUIZ:
       H.R. 2525.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution, to provide for the general welfare and make all 
     laws necessary and proper to carry out the powers of 
     Congress.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill reauthorizes the Veterans Affairs 
     Administration's homelessness programs and allows the VA to 
     provide a referral to health care providers to veterans who 
     have been discharged with a other than honorable discharged.
```
