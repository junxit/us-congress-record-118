# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2494

**Measures:** H.R. 2494

```
           By Mr. GARBARINO:
       H.R. 2494.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to section 8 of Article 1 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would amend the Immigration and Nationality Act 
     to explicitly state that assaulting a law enforcement officer 
     is a deportable offense.
```
