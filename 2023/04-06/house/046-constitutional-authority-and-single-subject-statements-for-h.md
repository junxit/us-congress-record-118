# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2509

**Measures:** H.R. 2509

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 2509.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Transportation to submit a 
     report on helicopter and rotorcraft noise in the District of 
     Columbia.
```
