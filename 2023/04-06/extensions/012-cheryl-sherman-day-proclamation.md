# CHERYL SHERMAN DAY PROCLAMATION

**Speaking:** Pocan, Mark (P000607) [D-WI]

```
                    CHERYL SHERMAN DAY PROCLAMATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MARK POCAN

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 6, 2023

  Mr. POCAN. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
proclamation in honor of Cheryl Sherman.
  Whereas, Cheryl Sherman began her public service to the Village of 
Prairie Du Sac in 1983 by serving on the Plan Commission, and was first 
elected to the Village Board in 1991; and
  Whereas, Cheryl Sherman has played a vital role in her community's 
growth and development through her service on a variety of boards, 
commissions, and committees throughout her tenure; and
  Whereas, Cheryl Sherman has consistently proven herself to be a 
thoughtful, effective, and dependable community leader by continuously 
serving as Village President since 1995; and
  Whereas, Under Cheryl Sherman's leadership, the Village has more than 
doubled its population, expanded its community spaces, entered into a 
variety of future oriented intergovernmental cooperative agreements, 
and seen multiple community projects successfully come to fruition; and
  Now, therefore, I hereby proclaim April 25, 2023 as Cheryl Sherman 
Day in honor of her 40 years of public service to her community, the 
Village of Prairie Du Sac, and the State of Wisconsin. I join her 
friends, family, and colleagues in offering her best wishes on her 
much-deserved retirement.
```
