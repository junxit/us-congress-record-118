# CELEBRATING THE FLORIDA ATLANTIC UNIVERSITY MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM'S HISTORIC FINAL FOUR FINISH IN THE 2023 NCAA DIVISION I MEN'S BASKETBALL TOURNAMENT

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
  CELEBRATING THE FLORIDA ATLANTIC UNIVERSITY MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM'S 
HISTORIC FINAL FOUR FINISH IN THE 2023 NCAA DIVISION I MEN'S BASKETBALL 
                               TOURNAMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 6, 2023

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the historic 
achievement of the Florida Atlantic University Men's Basketball team, 
the Florida Atlantic Owls, in the 2023 NCAA Division I Men's Basketball 
Tournament.
  The Owls have appeared in the NCAA tournament only twice. The first 
time in 2002 and again this year, where for the first time, they 
finished in the Final Four. To celebrate their success, I include in 
the Record their names so their achievements may be remembered forever:
  No. 0, Brenen Lorient from Ocala, Florida
  No. 1, Johnell Davis from Gary, Indiana
  No. 2, Nick Boyd from Garnerville, New York
  No. 3, Giancarlo Rosado from West Palm Beach, Florida
  No. 4, Bryan Greenlee from Gainesville, Florida
  No. 5, Isaiah Gaines from Pensacola, Florida
  No. 11, Michael Forrest from Lauderhill, Florida
  No. 12, Jalen Gaffney from Columbus, New Jersey
  No. 13, Jack Johnson from White Plains, New York
  No. 15, Alijah Martin from Summit, Mississippi
  No. 21, Alejandro Ralat from San Juan, Puerto Rico
  No. 23, Brandon Weatherspoon from Canton, Mississippi
  No. 25, Tre Carroll from Punta Gorda, Florida
  No. 30, Leo Beath from Malibu, California
  No. 50, Vladislav Goldin from Nalchick, Russia
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Florida's 23rd Congressional District, 
please join me in congratulating the FAU men's basketball team's 
historic Final Four finish in the 2023 NCAA Men's Basketball 
Tournament. They should be genuinely proud of their accomplishment.
```
