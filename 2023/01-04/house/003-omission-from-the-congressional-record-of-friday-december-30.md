# OMISSION FROM THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD OF FRIDAY, DECEMBER 30, 2022 AT PAGE H10536

```
OMISSION FROM THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD OF FRIDAY, DECEMBER 30, 2022 AT 
                              PAGE H10536

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, December 2022.
     Governor Roy Cooper,
     State of North Carolina,
     Raleigh, North Carolina.
       Dear Governor Cooper: Please be advised that I will resign 
     from the United States House of Representatives effective 
     December 30, 2022. It has been my high honor and privilege to 
     represent the people of the First Congressional District for 
     more than eighteen years. And I thank you for your friendship 
     and leadership.
       Thank you very much.
           Very truly yours,
                                                G. K. Butterfield,
     Member of Congress.
```
