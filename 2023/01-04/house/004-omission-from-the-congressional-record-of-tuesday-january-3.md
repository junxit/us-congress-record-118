# OMISSION FROM THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD OF TUESDAY, JANUARY 3, 2023 AT PAGE H10549

```
 OMISSION FROM THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD OF TUESDAY, JANUARY 3, 2023 AT 
                              PAGE H10549

                                           Congressman Mike Doyle,


                                          Eighteenth District,

                                  Pennsylvania, December 16, 2022.
     Governor Tom Wolf,
     Harrisburg, PA.
       Dear Governor Wolf: This letter is to inform you of my 
     intention to resign my seat in Congress effective December 
     31, 2022.

       Best wishes to you and your family in all your future 
     endeavors.
           Sincerely,
```
