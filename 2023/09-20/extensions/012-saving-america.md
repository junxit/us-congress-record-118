     spending over the cliff.

     fastest growing component of the budget over the next 
     decade--other than the Green New Deal--is interest on the . .

     trillion, will be like a Pacman swallowing up more and more 
     of the entire budget.

     Almost $8 trillion of low-interest-rate debt will roll over 
     at much higher rates. Instead of paying 2 and 3% interest on 
     bonds, we will be paying close to 5% and maybe more if the 
     Fed has to raise interest rates again. Biden has created a 
     fine fiscal mess, and it will take years to get out of it. 
     Arrington is right: let's start right now.''
