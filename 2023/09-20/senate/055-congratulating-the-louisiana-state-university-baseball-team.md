**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 354

```
CONGRATULATING THE LOUISIANA STATE UNIVERSITY BASEBALL TEAM FOR WINNING 
 THE 2023 NATIONAL COLLEGIATE ATHLETIC ASSOCIATION DIVISION I COLLEGE 
                              WORLD SERIES

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 354, which is at the desk.

       A resolution (S. Res. 354) congratulating the Louisiana

  The resolution (S. Res. 354) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
```
