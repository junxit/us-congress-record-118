# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5582

**Measures:** H.R. 5582

       H.R. 5582.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.

       This legislation proposes measures to rejuvenate white oak
