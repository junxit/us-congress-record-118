# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5608

**Measures:** H.R. 5608

```
           By Mr. STEUBE:
       H.R. 5608.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To allow individuals to elect to receive contributions to a 
     health savings account in lieu of reduced cost-sharing under 
     health insurance obtained through a health insurance 
     Exchange.
```
