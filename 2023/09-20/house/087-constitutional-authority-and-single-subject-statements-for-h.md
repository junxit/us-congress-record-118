# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5606

**Measures:** H.R. 5606

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 5606.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clauses 3 and 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Clean Air Act to prohibit the emission of any 
     greenhouse gas in any quantity from any new electric utility 
     steam generating unit, and for other purposes.
```
