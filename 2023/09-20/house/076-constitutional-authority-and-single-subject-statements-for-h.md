# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5595

**Measures:** H.R. 5595

```
           By Mrs. HINSON:
       H.R. 5595.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establish the Retrospective Review Commission to review 
     federal regulations and recommend repeals, streamline the 
     repeal process for Congress to consider rules the Commission 
     recommends for repeal, and require federal agencies to 
     implement cut-as-you-go for new rules.
```
