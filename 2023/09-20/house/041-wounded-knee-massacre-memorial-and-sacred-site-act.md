# WOUNDED KNEE MASSACRE MEMORIAL AND SACRED SITE ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 3371

```
           WOUNDED KNEE MASSACRE MEMORIAL AND SACRED SITE ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 3371) to direct the 
Secretary of the Interior to complete all actions necessary for certain 
land to be held in restricted fee status by the Oglala Sioux Tribe and 
Cheyenne River Sioux Tribe, and for other purposes.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Georgia (Mr. Collins) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
