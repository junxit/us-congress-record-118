DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION REQUEST AND FUTURE YEARS DEFENSE PROGRAM
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces concluded 
a hearing to examine Department of Defense missile defense activities 
in review of the Defense Authorization Request for Fiscal Year 2024 and 
the Future Years Defense Program, after receiving testimony from John 
Plumb, Assistant Secretary for Space Policy, General Glen D. VanHerck, 
USAF, Commander, Northern Command and North American Aerospace Defense 
Command, Vice Admiral Jon A. Hill, USN, Director, Missile Defense 
Agency, and Lieutenant General Daniel L. Karbler, USA, Commanding 
General, Army Space and Missile Defense Command, and Commander, Joint 
Functional Component Command for Integrated Missile Defense, all of the 
Department of Defense.
