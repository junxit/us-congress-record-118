# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3138

**Measures:** H.R. 3138

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 3138.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1
       The Congress shall have Power to . . . provide for the . . 
     . general Welfare of the United States; . . .
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would allow ESSA funds to be used to 
     provide educators and administrators with culturally 
     competent educational training to support immigrants students 
     and multi-language learners.
```
