# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3154

**Measures:** H.R. 3154

```
           By Mr. McGOVERN:
       H.R. 3154.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 and clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reduce and eliminate threats posed by nuclear weapons to 
     the United States by leading and revitalizing international 
     arms control agreements.
```
