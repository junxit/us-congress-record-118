# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3156

**Measures:** H.R. 3156

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 3156.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would require that, subject to appropriations, 
     the United States Postal Service install security cameras at 
     each postal facility.
```
