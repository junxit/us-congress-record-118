# RECOGNIZING THE 90TH BIRTHDAY OF MARY LITTLE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
              RECOGNIZING THE 90TH BIRTHDAY OF MARY LITTLE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 9, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mary Little of 
Irving, Texas as she celebrated her 90th birthday last Sunday.
  Mary Little is an established woman who has accomplished so much in 
her lifetime. Today, we come together to recognize the 90 years Mary 
has spent with us, and the impact she has and continues to make on our 
lives.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Mary and wishing her a 
happy 90th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
