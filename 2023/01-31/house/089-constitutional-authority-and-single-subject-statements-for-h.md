# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 661

**Measures:** H.R. 661

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 661.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 Clause 4 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require immigration authorities to keep undocumented 
     immigrants in custody if they are accused of killing someone 
     or inflicting serious bodily injury.
```
