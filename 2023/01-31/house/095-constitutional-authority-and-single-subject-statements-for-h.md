# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 668

**Measures:** H.R. 668

```
            By Ms. McCOLLUM:
       H.R. 668.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article I Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill permanently withdraws over 234,000 acres of 
     federal land and waters within the Rainy River Watershed 
     adjacent to the Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness from 
     mineral leasing.
```
