# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 685

**Measures:** H.R. 685

```
           By Mr. STEUBE:
       H.R. 685.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the President, in consultation with the Secretary 
     of the Treasury, to develop and carry out a strategy to seek 
     reinbursement from the People's Republic of China of funds 
     made available by the United States Government to address 
     COVID-19.
```
