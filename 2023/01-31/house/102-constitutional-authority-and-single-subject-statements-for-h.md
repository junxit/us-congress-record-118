# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 675

**Measures:** H.R. 675

```
           By Mr. PALLONE:
       H.R. 675.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: [The Congress shall 
     have Power] To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the India Tribes
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Commercial satellites.
```
