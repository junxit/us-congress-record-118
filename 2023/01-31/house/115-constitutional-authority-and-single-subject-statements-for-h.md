# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 688

**Measures:** H.R. 688

```
           By Mr. STEUBE:
       H.R. 688.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Homeland Security and Secretary 
     of State to require aliens applying for certain visas to 
     disclose if they receive funds from the Government of the 
     People's Republic of China or the CCP.
```
