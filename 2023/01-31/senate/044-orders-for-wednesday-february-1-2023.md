# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 2023

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 2023

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, February 1; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the 
weekly Democratic caucus meeting.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, rollcall votes are expected during 
Wednesday's session.

                          ____________________
```
