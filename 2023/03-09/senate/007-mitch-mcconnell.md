# MITCH McCONNELL

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                            MITCH McCONNELL

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, first, my thoughts this morning are 
with my good friend Leader Mitch McConnell, who is recovering in the 
hospital after an accident last night.
  This morning, I offer a prayer of strength and healing for the leader 
and his family. I called the leader this morning and spoke briefly with 
his staff to extend my prayers and well wishes. My thoughts are also 
with Leader McConnell's family and his team.
  I join every single one of my colleagues in wishing Leader McConnell 
a speedy and full recovery.

                          ____________________
```
