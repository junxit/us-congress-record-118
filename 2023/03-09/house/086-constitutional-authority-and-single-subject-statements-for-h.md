# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1501

**Measures:** H.R. 1501

```
           By Mr. GUEST:
       H.R. 1501.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Homeland Security from 
     operating or procuring unmanned aircraft systems that were 
     produced in certain adversarial nations for the purpose of 
     preventing harmful surveillance activities.
```
