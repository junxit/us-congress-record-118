# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1508

**Measures:** H.R. 1508

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota:
       H.R. 1508.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant

       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution

       To amend chapter 44 of title 18, United States Code, to
```
