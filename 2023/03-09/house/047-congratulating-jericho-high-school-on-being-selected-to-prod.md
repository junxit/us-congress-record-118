# CONGRATULATING JERICHO HIGH SCHOOL ON BEING SELECTED TO PRODUCE "FROZEN"

**Speaking:** Santos, George (S001222) [R-NY]

```
    CONGRATULATING JERICHO HIGH SCHOOL ON BEING SELECTED TO PRODUCE 
                               ``FROZEN''

  (Mr. SANTOS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SANTOS. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to congratulate Jericho High 
School for being granted an exclusive performance of ``Frozen.''
  Jericho is one of 52 schools in the world, and the only one in New 
York, to be selected by Disney to produce the musical. This impressive 
performance by talented students is expected to include a snow machine, 
a projector, puppetry, and even robotics. It will also feature songs 
made famous by New York Third's very own Idina Menzel, who is the voice 
of Elsa in the motion picture.
  I wish the entire cast and crew of Jericho High School the best of 
luck. As for Lauren Marchand, who is playing Elsa, it must be such an 
honor to play her role model and mentor in this role. Break a leg.

                          ____________________
```
