# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

```
                    ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly an 
enrolled joint resolution of the House of the following title, which 
was thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.J. Res. 30. Joint Resolution providing for congressional
```
