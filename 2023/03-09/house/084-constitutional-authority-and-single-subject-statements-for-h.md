# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1499

**Measures:** H.R. 1499

```
           By Mr. GARCIA of Illinois:
       H.R. 1499.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article 1 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill addresses pay, benefits, and labor standards for 
     certain airport service workers. Airport service workers 
     include security officers, food service workers, cleaning 
     staff, ticketing agents, and retail service workers.
```
