# RECOGNIZING THE VALLEY HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM FOR WINNING THE AHSAA CLASS 5A STATE TITLE

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE VALLEY HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' BASKETBALL TEAM FOR WINNING 
                     THE AHSAA CLASS 5A STATE TITLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 9, 2023

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Valley High School boys' basketball team for being undefeated and 
winning the AHSAA Class 5A state title.
  On March 4, 2023, the Valley High Rams played fast, leaving no doubt 
as to who deserves to be state champion, beating Charles Henderson 54-
50 to secure their title win. The game was played at BJCC's Legacy 
Arena in Birmingham, Alabama.
  The Rams finished their championship season a perfect 33-0. Coach 
Marshon Harper led the Rams to become just the 11th boys' team in AHSAA 
history to be undefeated.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Coach Harper, the 
Valley High School boys' basketball team, students, faculty and all the 
fans. Go Rams.

                          ____________________
```
