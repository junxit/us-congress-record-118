# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6123

**Measures:** H.R. 6123

```
            By Mr. PALMER:
        H.R. 6123.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the State Department from charging Americans 
     for the cost associated with evacuation or govenement 
     assisted departure from a crisis situation.
```
