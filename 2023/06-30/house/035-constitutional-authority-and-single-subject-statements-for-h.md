# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4417

**Measures:** H.R. 4417

```
           By Mrs. HINSON:
       H.R. 4417.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit state or local governments from imposing 
     standards on the preharvest production of agricultural 
     products, if such production occurs in another state.
```
