# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4403

**Measures:** H.R. 4403

```
           By Mr. CARTER of Louisiana:
       H.R. 4403.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       the Spending Clause, Article 1, Section 8, Cl. 1 and the 
     Necessary and Proper Clause, Article I, Section 8, Cl. 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to make 
     improvments to the Securing the Cities program.
```
