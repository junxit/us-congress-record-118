# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4395

**Measures:** H.R. 4395

```
           By Mrs. BICE:
       H.R. 4395.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prevent the Department of Energy from eliminating gas

     type of stove, water heater, or furnace they prefer for their
```
