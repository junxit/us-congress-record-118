# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4409

**Measures:** H.R. 4409

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 4409.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation closes loopholes in current acquisition 
     laws, tie financial incentives for contractors to 
     performance, and provide the Department of Defense (DoD) with 
     the information necessary to ensure fair and accurate 
     pricing.
```
