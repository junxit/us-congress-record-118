# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4424

**Measures:** H.R. 4424

```
           By Mr. LaLOTA:
       H.R. 4424.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to study and 
     report on the prevalence of cholangiocarcinoma in veterans 
     who served in the Vietnam theater of operations during the 
     Vietnam era, and for other purposes.
```
