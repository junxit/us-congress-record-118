# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4454

**Measures:** H.R. 4454

```
           By Mr. TURNER:
       H.R. 4454.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 
     to ensure that politically derived information is not used in 
     an application to the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court 
     for an order under title I or III of such Act.
```
