# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4416

**Measures:** H.R. 4416

```
           By Mr. HIGGINS of Louisiana:
       H.R. 4416.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution, Congress 
     has the power ``to make all Laws which shall be necessary and 
     proper for carrying into Execution the foregoing Powers, and 
     all other Powers vested by this Constitution in the 
     Government of the United States, or any Department or Officer 
     thereof''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislaton will prohibit the funding, hiring, and 
     equipping of Federal armed regulatory enforcemen agents for 
     the IRS, DOL, and EPA.
```
