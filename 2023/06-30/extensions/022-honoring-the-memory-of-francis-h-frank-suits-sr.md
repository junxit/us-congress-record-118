# HONORING THE MEMORY OF FRANCIS H. ``FRANK'' SUITS, SR.

**Speaking:** Molinaro, Marcus J. (M001221) [R-NY]

```
         HONORING THE MEMORY OF FRANCIS H. ``FRANK'' SUITS, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MARCUS J. MOLINARO

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 30, 2023

  Mr. MOLINARO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of Frank 
Suits who passed

[[Page E631]]

away on June 22nd of this year. Frank was a devoted father, 
grandfather, great-grandfather, and husband who will be dearly missed 
by all who knew him.
  Frank will long be remembered for the many meaningful contributions 
he made to his business and community. He successfully ran the Suite-
Kote Corporation for many years while also serving on the Homer School 
Board and many other Homer-based community programs.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues in the House join me in 
honoring the memory of Frank Suits. While we mourn his passing, we 
celebrate his life and the legacy he leaves behind. May his life and 
memory always be a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
