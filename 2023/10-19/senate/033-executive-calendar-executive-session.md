# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: all 
nominations placed on the Secretary's desk in the Coast Guard; that the 
nominations be confirmed en bloc; that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate; that no further motions be in order to any of the nominations; 
that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and 
that the Senate resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:

               Nominations Placed on the Secretary's Desk


                           IN THE COAST GUARD

       * PN978 COAST GUARD nominations (2) beginning MATTHEW A. 
     GANS, and ending IAN W. MARKS, which nominations were 
     received by the Senate and appeared in the Congressional 
     Record of September 6, 2023.

                          ____________________
```
