# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5997

**Measures:** H.R. 5997

```
           By Ms. LEE of Nevada:
       H.R. 5997.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and 
     Excises'' in order to ``provide for the . . . general Welfare 
     of the United States.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Energy to further develop and 
     support the adoption of a voluntary streamlined permitting 
     and inspection process for authorities having jurisdiction 
     over the permitting of qualifying distributed energy systems, 
     and for other purposes.
```
