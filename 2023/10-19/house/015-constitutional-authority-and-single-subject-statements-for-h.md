# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5991

**Measures:** H.R. 5991

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 5991.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 and Article IV, Section 3, 
     Clause 2 of the the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Commandant of the Coast Guard and the 
     Commissioner of U.S. Customs and Border Protection to make 
     certain determinations in enforcing the Jones Act, and for 
     other purposes.
```
