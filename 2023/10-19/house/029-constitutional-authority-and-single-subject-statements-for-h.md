# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6005

**Measures:** H.R. 6005

```
            By Mr. RUPPERSBERGER:
       H.R. 6005.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Sec. 8, cl. 1 and Article I, 
     Sec. 8, cl. 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Fairness for Victims of SNAP Skimming Act of 2023 would 
     allow the USDA to reimburse victims of SNAP benefit theft 
     equal to the amount of benefits stolen from the household and 
     would allow for federal reimbursement past September 30th, 
     2024.
```
