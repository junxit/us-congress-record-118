# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5995

**Measures:** H.R. 5995

```
           By Mr. KILMER:
       H.R. 5995.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Grants federal employees who started their careers as 
     temporary workers the opportunity to make catch-up retirement 
     contributions for on time retirement.
```
