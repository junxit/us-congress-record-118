# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2684

**Measures:** H.R. 2684

```
           By Mr. NORMAN:
       H.R. 2684.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Increases the capital loss limit against ordinary income 
     from $3,000 to $13,000 and adjusts annually for inflation.
```
