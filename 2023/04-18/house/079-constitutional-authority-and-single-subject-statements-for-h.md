# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2677

**Measures:** H.R. 2677

```
           By Mr. JOYCE of Ohio:
       H.R. 2677.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the Attorney General to make grants to States 
     and units of local government to reduce the financial and 
     administrative burden of expunging convictions for cannabis 
     offenses.
```
