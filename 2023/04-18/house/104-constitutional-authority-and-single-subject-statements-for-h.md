# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 57

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 57

```
           By Mr. KILEY:
       H.J. Res. 57.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article V
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The following article is proposed as an amendment to the 
     Constitution of the United States. No person shall be a 
     Senator from a State unless such person has been elected by 
     the people thereof.
```
