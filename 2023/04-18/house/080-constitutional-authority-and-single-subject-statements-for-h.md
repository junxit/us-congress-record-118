# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2678

**Measures:** H.R. 2678

```
           By Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI:
       H.R. 2678.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       U.S. Constitution Section 1, Article 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend chapter 131 of title 5, United States Code, to 
     prevent Members of Congress and their spouses and dependent 
     children from trading stocks and owning stocks.
```
