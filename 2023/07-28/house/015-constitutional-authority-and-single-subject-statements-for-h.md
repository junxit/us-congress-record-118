# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5073

**Measures:** H.R. 5073

```
           By Mr. CAREY:
       H.R. 5073.

     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow
```
