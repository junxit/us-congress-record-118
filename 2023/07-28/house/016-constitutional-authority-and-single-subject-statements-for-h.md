# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5074

**Measures:** H.R. 5074

```
           By Mr. CARTER of Georgia:
       H.R. 5074.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the American Taxpayer Relief Act of 2012 to delay 
     implementation of the inclusion of oral-only ESRD related 
     drugs in the Medicare ESRD prospective payment system.
```
