# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5080

**Measures:** H.R. 5080

```
           By Mr. FITZPATRICK:
       H.R. 5080.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Internal Revenue Code to create parity in tax
```
