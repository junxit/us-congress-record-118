# HONORING GEORGE FRASER

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                         HONORING GEORGE FRASER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. LUIS CORREA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 21, 2023

  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of George 
Fraser (AHS Class of 1970), who was one of Anaheim High School's 
greatest sports legends. George passed away suddenly last weekend on 
February 12, 2023.
  As a sophomore, George famously stepped in as quarterback and 
proceeded to lead the Mighty Colonists to the 1967 CIF Football 
Championship. George would go on to star for the Colonist football team 
during his junior and senior seasons, in which Anaheim reached the 4-A 
Division semifinals each year. He became the first and only football 
player to ever be named to the All-CIF-SS 4-A Division team three 
times. George eventually returned to coach football at Anaheim and was 
on staff when the Colonists again reached the CIF finals in 1987 and 
2006, spending more than 20 years coaching at The Colony up to and 
including last season. George also coached youth sports in the City of 
Anaheim for decades. George's legacy will live on in our community and 
through the halls of Anaheim High School. Let us join in mourning the 
loss of this great Colonist who was a tremendous asset to the Anaheim 
Community.

                          ____________________
```
