# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1109

**Measures:** H.R. 1109

```
           By Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas:
       H.R. 1109.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Allows State and Federal regulators to share the results of 
     regulatory exams on bank service companies in order to cut 
     down on duplicative exams.
```
