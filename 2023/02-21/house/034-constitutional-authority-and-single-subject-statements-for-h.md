# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1125

**Measures:** H.R. 1125

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 1125.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require disclosure of the total amount of interest that 
     would be paid over the life of a loan for certain Federal 
     student loans.
```
