# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1136

**Measures:** H.R. 1136

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 1136.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to require the Secretary of the Interior to remove the 
     statue of Albert Pike located near Judiciary Square in the 
     District of Columbia.
```
