# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1135

**Measures:** H.R. 1135

```
           By Mr. MEEKS:
       H.R. 1135.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To grant certain authorities to the President to combat 
     economic coercion by foreign adversaries.
```
