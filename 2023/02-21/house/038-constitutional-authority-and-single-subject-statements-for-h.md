# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1129

**Measures:** H.R. 1129

```

       H.R. 1129.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1 and Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill eliminates a rule that prohibits free-standing
```
