# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1120

**Measures:** H.R. 1120

```
           By Mr. DONALDS:
       H.R. 1120.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art 1, Sec 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill provides for asset- and visa-blocking sanctions 
     for conduct relating to Cuba. It also establishes an 
     interagency task force to facilitate access to uncensored 
     internet in Cuba.
```
