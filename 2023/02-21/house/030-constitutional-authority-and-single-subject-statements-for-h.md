# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1121

**Measures:** H.R. 1121

```
           By Mr. DUNCAN:
       H.R. 1121.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 and Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2.
       To increase American energy production and restore energy 
     leadership by prohibiting the President from declaring a 
     moratorium on the use of hydraulic fracturing unless 
     authorized by Congress.
```
