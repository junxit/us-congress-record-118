# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1107

**Measures:** H.R. 1107

```
           By Mrs. KIM of California:
       H.R. 1107.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of State to take certain actions 
     with respect to the labeling of the People's Republic of 
     China as a developing country, and for other purposes.
```
