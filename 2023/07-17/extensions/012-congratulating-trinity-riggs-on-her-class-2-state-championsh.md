# CONGRATULATING TRINITY RIGGS ON HER CLASS 2 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
     CONGRATULATING TRINITY RIGGS ON HER CLASS 2 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 17, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Russellville's Trinity Riggs on her victory 
in Missouri's Class 2 Shot Put State Championship.
  Trinity claimed the Class 2 title for the Indians, ending her high 
school career with an incredible throw of 11.93 meters. She should be 
commended for all her hard work throughout the past year and for 
bringing home the state championship to her school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Trinity Riggs for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
