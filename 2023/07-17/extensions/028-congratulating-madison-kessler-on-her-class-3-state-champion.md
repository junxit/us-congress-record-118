# CONGRATULATING MADISON KESSLER ON HER CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
    CONGRATULATING MADISON KESSLER ON HER CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 17, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Osage High School's Madison Kessler on her 
victory in Missouri's Class 3 Pole Vault State Championship.
  Madison claimed the Class 3 title in the pole vault competition, 
clearing the impressive height of 3.28 meters. Madison should be 
commended for all her hard work throughout the past year and for 
bringing home the state championship to her school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Madison Kessler for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
