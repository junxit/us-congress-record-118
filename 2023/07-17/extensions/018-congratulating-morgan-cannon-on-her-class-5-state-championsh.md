# CONGRATULATING MORGAN CANNON ON HER CLASS 5 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
     CONGRATULATING MORGAN CANNON ON HER CLASS 5 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 17, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Liberty High School's Morgan Cannon on her 
victory in Missouri's Class 5 Javelin State Championship.
  Morgan claimed the Class 5 title with an incredible throw of 45.21 
meters. She fought through her shoulder pain and singlehandedly won 10 
of her team's 18 points. Morgan should be commended for all her hard 
work throughout the past year and for bringing home the state 
championship to her school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Morgan Cannon for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
