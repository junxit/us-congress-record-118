# CONGRATULATING LUTHERAN ST. CHARLES ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
CONGRATULATING LUTHERAN ST. CHARLES ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 17, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Lutheran St. Charles Track and Field Team 
on their victory in the Class 3 State Championship.
  Lutheran St. Charles claimed the Class 3 title in an impressive team 
effort. With only one member of the team a returner to the state 
competition, the team's plethora of young talent bodes well for its 
future. Although their title seemed secure after 15 events, they 
finished strong and brought home the championship. The Cougars should 
be commended for all their hard work throughout the past year, and I'd 
like to wish the team luck as they move up to succeed in Class 4 next 
year.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Lutheran St. Charles 
Cougars for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
