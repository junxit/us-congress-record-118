# CONGRATULATING OLIVIA ANDREWS ON HER CLASS 2 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
    CONGRATULATING OLIVIA ANDREWS ON HER CLASS 2 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 17, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Father Tolton's Olivia Andrews on her victory 
in Missouri's Class 2 3200 Meter Run State Championship.
  Olivia claimed the Class 2 title with the impressive time of 
11:02:24, achieving a school record and Class 2's fastest time of the 
season in the 3200-meter run. She ended her high school career with a 
bang as she dominated her last race before she heads off to college 
next year. Olivia should be commended for all her hard work throughout 
the past year and for bringing home the state championship to her 
school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Olivia Andrews for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
