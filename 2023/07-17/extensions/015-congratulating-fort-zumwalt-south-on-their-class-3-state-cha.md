# CONGRATULATING FORT ZUMWALT SOUTH ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
 CONGRATULATING FORT ZUMWALT SOUTH ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 17, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Fort Zumwalt South Girls Soccer Team on 
their victory in Missouri's Class 3 Soccer State Championship.
  The team claimed the Class 3 title for the third time in the first 
ever three-peat in girls' soccer by a Missouri public school. Isabel 
Montileone and Audrey Smith scored beautiful first-half goals to give 
the Bulldogs the win as they defeated Smithville 2-1. The team should 
be commended for all their hard work throughout the past year and for 
bringing home the state championship to their school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Fort Zumwalt South 
Girls Soccer Team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
