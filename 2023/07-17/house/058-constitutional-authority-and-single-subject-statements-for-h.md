# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4672

**Measures:** H.R. 4672

```
           By Mr. ADERHOLT:
       H.R. 4672.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The Congress shall have Power to dispose of and make all 
     needful Rules and Regulations respecting the Territory or 
     other Property belonging to the United States; and nothing in 
     this Constituition shall be so construed as to Prejudice any 
     Claims of the United States, or any particular State.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Acquisition of Buildings and Sites
```
