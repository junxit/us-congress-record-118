# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4688

**Measures:** H.R. 4688

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 4688.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to direct the Administrator of the General Services 
     Administration (GSA) to sell Lot 822 of Square 375 at 940 H 
     Street Northwest, including the vacant Daniel Webster School, 
     in downtown D.C. for fair market value and highest and best 
     use.
```
