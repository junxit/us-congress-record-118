# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4679

**Measures:** H.R. 4679

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 4679.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       section 5 of Amendment XIV to the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend chapter 44 of title 18, United States Code, to 
     ensure that all firearms are traceable, and for other 
     purposes.
```
