# MORNING-HOUR DEBATE

```
                          MORNING-HOUR DEBATE

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the order of the House of January 9, 2023, 
the Chair will now recognize Members from lists submitted by the 
majority and minority leaders for morning-hour debate.
  The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with time 
equally allocated between the parties and each Member other than the 
majority and minority leaders and the minority whip limited to 5 
minutes, but in no event shall debate continue beyond 1:50 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
