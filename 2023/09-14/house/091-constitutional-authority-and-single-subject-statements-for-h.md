# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5471

**Measures:** H.R. 5471

```
            By Mr. CORREA:
        H.R. 5471.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To codify protections afforded to Vietnamese refugees in 
     the United States under a 2008 Memorandum of Understanding 
     (MOU) between the U.S. and Vietnam.
```
