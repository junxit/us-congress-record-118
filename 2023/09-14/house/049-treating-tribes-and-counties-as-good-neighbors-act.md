# TREATING TRIBES AND COUNTIES AS GOOD NEIGHBORS ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 1450

```
           TREATING TRIBES AND COUNTIES AS GOOD NEIGHBORS ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 1450) to amend the 
Agricultural Act of 2014 to modify the treatment of revenue from timber 
sale contracts and certain payments made by counties to the Secretary 
of Agriculture and the Secretary of the Interior under good neighbor 
agreements, and for other purposes, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Arkansas (Mr. Westerman) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
