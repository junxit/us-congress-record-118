# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5468

**Measures:** H.R. 5468

```
            By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
        H.R. 5468.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To exempt Children's Savings Accounts from counting against 
     asset limits in TANF, SSI, LIHEAP, and SNAP.
```
