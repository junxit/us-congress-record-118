# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5465

**Measures:** H.R. 5465

```
            By Mr. ALFORD:
       H.R. 5465.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the head of each agency to allow members of 
     Congress access to certain Federal buildings.
```
