# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5487

**Measures:** H.R. 5487

```
           By Mr. HUFFMAN:
       H.R. 5487.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Commerce to establish and carry 
     out a grant program to conserve, restore, and manage kelp 
     forest ecosystems, and for other purposes.
```
