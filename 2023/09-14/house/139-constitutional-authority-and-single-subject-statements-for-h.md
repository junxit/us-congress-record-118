# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5520

**Measures:** H.R. 5520

```
           By Ms. WILLIAMS of Georgia:
       H.R. 5520.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Creates a new grant program, administered by HHS's Office 
     of Minority Health, to expand access to culturally and 
     linguistically appropriate care, encourage innovation, and 
     address persistent health disparities and chronic disease 
     challenges.
```
