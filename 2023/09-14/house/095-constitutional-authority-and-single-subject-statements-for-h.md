# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5475

**Measures:** H.R. 5475

```
            By Mr. FITZGERALD:
        H.R. 5475.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.

       This bill prohibits the issuance of patent to any person or
```
