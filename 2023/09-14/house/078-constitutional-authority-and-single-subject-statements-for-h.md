# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5458

**Measures:** H.R. 5458

```
           By Mr. YAKYM:
       H.R. 5458.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the Patient-Centered Outcomes Research 
     Institute to study the use of home cardiorespiratory monitors 
     (medical devices that track the respiratory effort and heart 
     rate of a sleeping infant) to reduce and prevent sleep-
     related sudden infant death syndrome or sudden unexpected 
     infant death.
```
