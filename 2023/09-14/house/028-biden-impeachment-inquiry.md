# BIDEN IMPEACHMENT INQUIRY

**Speaking:** Miller, Mary E. (M001211) [R-IL]

```
                       BIDEN IMPEACHMENT INQUIRY

  (Mrs. MILLER of Illinois asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute.)
  Mrs. MILLER of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise to address the historic 
impeachment inquiry into Joe Biden.
  Congress must hold Joe Biden accountable for serving as the kingpin 
over a series of shady foreign business deals involving his family 
members. Bank records and extensive evidence show that the Biden family 
received $20 million in cash, diamonds, and sports cars from China, 
Russia, Ukraine, Romania, and other foreign entities.
  Joe Biden was directly involved in this foreign influence-peddling 
scheme, and when Biden's son demanded $5 million in cash from a Chinese 
official, he wrote, ``I am sitting here with my father. . . .''
  Congress has a duty to the American people to protect taxpayers from 
this type of foreign corruption.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are reminded to refrain from 
engaging in personalities toward the President.

                          ____________________
```
