# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5473

**Measures:** H.R. 5473

```
            By Mr. EDWARDS:
        H.R. 5473.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``Regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Eliminates the sunset in the Disaster Recovery Reform Act 
     (DRRA) to the definition of building codes to include the 
     last 2 cycles plus allowing for amendments, and updates the 
     definition of building codes in Section 205 of the Robert T. 
     Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act, Grants 
     to Entities for Establishment of Hazard Mitigation Revolving 
     Loan Funds.
```
