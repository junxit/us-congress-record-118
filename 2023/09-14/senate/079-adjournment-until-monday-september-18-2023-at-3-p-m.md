# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 18, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
        ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 18, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come

September 18, 2023, at 3 p.m.
```
