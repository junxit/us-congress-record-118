# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3604

**Measures:** H.R. 3604

```
            By Mr. DONALDS:
        H.R. 3604.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. 1, Sec. 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the Administrator of the Small Business 
     Administration to license lending institutions to make loans 
     under section 7(a) of the Small Business Act, and for other 
     purposes.
```
