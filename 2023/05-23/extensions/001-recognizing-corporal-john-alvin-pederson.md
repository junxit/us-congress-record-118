# RECOGNIZING CORPORAL JOHN ALVIN PEDERSON

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                RECOGNIZING CORPORAL JOHN ALVIN PEDERSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 23, 2023

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Corporal John 
Alvin Pederson. Corporal Pederson was born near La Crosse, Wisconsin on 
January 15, 1926. He honorably served in the 1st Battalion, 232nd 
infantry Regiment as an Army Corporal in World War II from 1944 to 
1945.
  During his time as a soldier, Corporal Pederson showed bravery, 
resilience, and selflessness. He saved the life of his Assistant Squad 
Leader, Robert Bailey, who was severely wounded, by securing medical 
assistance. He helped capture 8 to 10 German soldiers in a basement 
stronghold, freeing 40 to 50 civilians.
  Corporal Pederson's dedication to our country and his fellow soldiers 
is a testament to his character and integrity. We honor his service and 
express our sincere appreciation for the profound impact he made. He is 
a true hero, and his legacy will be remembered and honored for 
generations to come.
```
