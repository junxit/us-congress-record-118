# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Measures:** S.Res. 110

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, and pursuant to S. 
Res. 110, the Senate stands adjourned until 10 a.m., Thursday, March 
16, 2023, and does so as a further mark of respect to the late James G. 
Abourezk, former Senator from South Dakota.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 7:16 p.m., adjourned until Thursday, March 
16, 2023, at 10 a.m.
```
