# RECOGNIZING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FOUNDING OF THE UNITED STATES NAVAL INSTITUTE

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
RECOGNIZING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE FOUNDING OF THE UNITED STATES 
                            NAVAL INSTITUTE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 5, 2023

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, since its founding in 1873, 
The United States Naval Institute has been a tireless advocate for our 
Nation's Navy, Marine Corps and Coast Guard.
  The United States Naval Institute, which is located at the United 
States Naval Academy in Annapolis, Maryland, provides an independent 
forum for those who work to advance our understanding of sea power 
along with issues critical to our National security.
  This independent, non-partisan and innovative platform was created to 
promote the free exchange of ideas to generate and advance the 
knowledge of America's Sea Services, thereby preserving our naval and 
maritime heritage.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District, I 
congratulate the United States Naval Institute on its 150th Anniversary 
and commend the staff and over 100,000 dedicated supporters worldwide 
for their commitment to our Nation's servicemembers.

                          ____________________
```
