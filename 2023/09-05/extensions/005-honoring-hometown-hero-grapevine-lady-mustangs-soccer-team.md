# HONORING HOMETOWN HERO GRAPEVINE LADY MUSTANGS SOCCER TEAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
       HONORING HOMETOWN HERO GRAPEVINE LADY MUSTANGS SOCCER TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 5, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize our Texas-24 
Hometown Hero, the Grapevine Lady Mustangs soccer team. Earlier this 
year, the Grapevine Lady Mustangs won the Texas 5A UIL Girls Soccer 
State Championships. Over the last few years, the team has overcome 
great adversity. In overcoming these challenges, the team has shown the 
power of hard work and dedication. Our North Texas community is proud 
of our Lady Mustangs, and I thank the team and coaches for setting a 
great example for the next generation of student-athletes.

                          ____________________
```
