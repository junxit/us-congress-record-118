# RECOGNITION OF THE 70TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE OCEAN GROVE HOME OWNERS ASSOCIATION

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

```
  RECOGNITION OF THE 70TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE OCEAN GROVE HOME OWNERS 
                              ASSOCIATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FRANK PALLONE, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 5, 2023

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to congratulate the 
Ocean Grove Home Owners Association on its 70th anniversary this year. 
This milestone is truly deserving of this body's recognition.
  Since its inception, the Ocean Grove Home Owners Association (OGHOA) 
has striven to uphold the aesthetic and historical character of Ocean 
Grove, while maintaining and improving property values. It represents 
and supports the common interests of concerned homeowners, welcoming 
input and open discussion from members and residents. In that endeavor, 
the OGHOA maintains an active role in local government, staying 
informed of government actions and policies, advocating on behalf of 
the community, and encouraging its members to serve on boards and 
commissions that affect homeowners.
  In addition to its preservation and advocacy efforts, the OGHOA 
provides fellowship among its members, gathering socially twice a year 
and encouraging members to participate in community celebrations.
  The Ocean Grove Home Owners Association continues to play a vital 
role in the promotion of Ocean Grove and its homeowners. As it 
celebrates its 70th anniversary, I am honored to recognize the 
contributions of the Ocean Grove Home Owners Association and to extend 
my gratitude for its efforts to preserve the charm of Ocean Grove.

                          ____________________
```
