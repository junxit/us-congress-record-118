# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5337

**Measures:** H.R. 5337

```
            By Mrs. HOUCHIN:
        H.R. 5337.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       U.S. Constitution, Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 
     1974 to clarify the application of prudence and exclusive 
     purpose duties to the exercise of shareholder rights.
```
