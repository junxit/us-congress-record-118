# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5343

**Measures:** H.R. 5343

```
            By Mrs. CAMMACK:
        H.R. 5343.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To appropriate amounts to carry out the Robert T. Stafford 
     Disaster Relief and Emergency Assistance Act and to provide 
     disaster relief.
```
