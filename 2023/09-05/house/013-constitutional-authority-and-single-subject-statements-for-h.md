# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5339

**Measures:** H.R. 5339

```
            By Mr. ALLEN:
        H.R. 5339.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       U.S. Constitution, Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 
     1974 to specify requirements concerning the consideration of 
     pecuniary and non-pecuniary factors, and for other purposes.
```
