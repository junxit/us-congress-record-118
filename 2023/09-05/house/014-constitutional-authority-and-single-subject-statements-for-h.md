# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5340

**Measures:** H.R. 5340

```
            By Mr. BANKS:
        H.R. 5340.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       U.S. Constitution, Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 
     1974 to ensure that pension plans provide notice to 
     participants and beneficiaries on risks associated with 
     certain investments, and for other purposes.
```
