# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5345

**Measures:** H.R. 5345

```
            By Mr. TONY GONZALES of Texas:
        H.R. 5345.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation adds explosive materials as a dangerous or 
     deadly weapon to 18 U.S.C. 111, and increases penalties for 
     using dangerous or deadly weapon to a $500,000 fine, up to 25 
     years in prison, or both. This is up from a $250,000 fine, 
     and up 20 years in prison, or both.
```
