# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S. 359, S. 1080, S. 1425, S. 1871, S. 1973

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. PETERS, from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 1425. A bill to require a report on Federal support to 
     the cybersecurity of commercial satellite systems, and for 
     other purposes (Rept. No. 118-92).
       S. 1871. A bill to create intergovernmental coordination 
     between State, local, Tribal, and territorial jurisdictions, 
     and the Federal Government to combat United States reliance 
     on the People's Republic of China and other covered countries 
     for critical minerals and rare earth metals, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. No. 118-93).
       By Mr. PETERS, from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs, with an amendment:
       S. 1973. A bill to require the purchase of domestically 
     made flags of the United States of America for use by the 
     Federal Government (Rept. No. 118-94).
       By Mr. DURBIN, from the Committee on the Judiciary, with an 
     amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 359. A bill to amend title 28, United States Code, to 
     provide for a code of conduct for justices of the Supreme 
     Court of the United States, and for other purposes.
       S. 1080. A bill to amend the Controlled Substances Act to 
     require electronic communication service providers and remote 
     computing services to report to the Attorney General certain 
     controlled substances violations.
```
