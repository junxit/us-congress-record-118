# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1501, H.R. 3254, H.R. 4366

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1501. An act to prohibit the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security from operating or procuring certain foreign-made 
     unmanned aircraft systems, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3254. An act to amend the Homeland Security Act of 
     2002 to establish a process to review applications for 
     certain grants to purchase equipment or systems that do not 
     meet or exceed any applicable national voluntary consensus 
     standards, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4366. An act making appropriations for military 
     construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
     agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, and 
     for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276d, clause 10 
of rule I, and the order of the House of January 9, 2023, the Speaker 
appoints the following Members on the part of the House of 
Representatives to the Canada-United States Interparliamentary Group: 
Mr. Higgins of New York, Mr. Larsen of Washington, Mr. Morelle of New 
York, Ms. Omar of Minnesota, and Mr. Thanedar of Michigan.
  The message further announced that pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276h, clause 
10 of rule I, and the order of the House of January 9, 2023, the 
Speaker appoints the following Members on the part of the House of 
Representatives to the Mexico-United States Interparliamentary Group: 
Mr. Cuellar of Texas and Mr. Correa of California.
  The message also announced that pursuant to section 4 of the United 
States Semiquincentennial Commission Act of 2016 (Public Law 114-196), 
and the order of the House of January 9, 2023, the Speaker appoints the 
following Members on the part of the House of Representatives to the 
United States Semiquincentennial Commission: Mr. Aderholt of Alabama 
and Ms. Salazar of Florida. And from private life: Ms. Lorna Auerbach 
of Pacific Palisades, California, to fill the existing vacancy theron.
```
