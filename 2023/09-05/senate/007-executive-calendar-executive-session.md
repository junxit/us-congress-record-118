# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
following nomination.
  The clerk will report the nomination.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Philip Nathan Jefferson, 
of North Carolina, to be Vice Chairman of the Board of Governors of the 
Federal Reserve System for a term of four years.
```
