# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5186

**Measures:** H.R. 5186

```
           By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 5186.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill reauthorizes the Voluntary Public Access and 
     Habitat Incentive Program (VPA-HIP) and increases the 
     program's funding by $100 million over 5 years.
```
