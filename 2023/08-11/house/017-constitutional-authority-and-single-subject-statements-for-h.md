# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5188

**Measures:** H.R. 5188

```
           By Mr. GALLAGHER:
       H.R. 5188.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Trade Expansion Act of 1962 to impose 
     limitations on the authority of the President to adjust 
     imports that are determined to threaten to impair national 
     security
```
