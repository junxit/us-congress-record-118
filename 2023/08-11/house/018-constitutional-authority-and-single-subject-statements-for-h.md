# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5189

**Measures:** H.R. 5189

```
           By Mr. MIKE GARCIA of California:
       H.R. 5189.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To condition the receipt of Help America Vote Act (HAVA) 
     grants on states implementing Voter ID laws.
```
