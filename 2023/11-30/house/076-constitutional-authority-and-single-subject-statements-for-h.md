# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6518

**Measures:** H.R. 6518

```
           By Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina:
       H.R. 6518.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Labor to take initiatives to 
     measure the impact of automation on the workforce in order to 
     inform workforce development strategies.
```
