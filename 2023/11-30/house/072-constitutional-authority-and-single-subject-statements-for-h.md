# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6514

**Measures:** H.R. 6514

```
           By Mr. ALFORD:
       H.R. 6514.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require agencies to notify the Archivist of the date on 
     which a final rule is submitted to Congress and the 
     Government Accountability Office.
```
