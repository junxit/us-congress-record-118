# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6540

**Measures:** H.R. 6540

```
           By Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ:
       H.R. 6540.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 relating to the 
     power of Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, 
     and among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Authorizes the Army Corp of Engineers to design and 
     construct modifications to the L-29 Levee component of the 
     Central and Southern Florida Project.
```
