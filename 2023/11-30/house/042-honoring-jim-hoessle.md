# HONORING JIM HOESSLE

**Speaking:** Miller, Max L. (M001222) [R-OH]

```
                          HONORING JIM HOESSLE

  (Mr. MILLER of Ohio asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MILLER of Ohio. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize Vietnam war 
veteran Jim Hoessle. Since 2016, Jim has served on the board of the 
Medina County Veterans Service Commission.
  Jim has spent 10 years as a member of the Honor Guard team for both 
VFW Post 5137 and American Legion Post 202.
  He also serves as a mentor in the Medina County Valor Court where he 
assists veterans who have run into difficulties with the law.
  Jim even serves the youth in Medina County where he works closely 
with the juvenile detention center, selecting opportunities for teens 
to provide community service.
  For the last 8 years, Jim has spent each summer at Buckeye Boys 
State, mentoring high school juniors in local, county, and State 
government.
  Jim's love and service to country has never wavered. His life and 
work for the people of my district, our district, the State of Ohio, 
and our country make a better America.
  Madam Speaker, let us thank Jim Hoessle for his service.

                          ____________________
```
