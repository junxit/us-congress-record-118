# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF CHIEF BRADLEY HAAS

**Speaking:** Kuster, Ann M. (K000382) [D-NH]

```
          HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF CHIEF BRADLEY HAAS

  (Ms. KUSTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KUSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life and 
bravery of New Hampshire's own Bradley Haas, who was killed in the line 
of duty while protecting the staff and patients at New Hampshire 
Hospital on November 17, preventing what likely would have been a 
tragic mass shooting in our psychiatric hospital.
  There is no doubt that Chief Haas' courage saved countless lives. Our 
community, our State, and our country owe him and his family a debt 
that can never be repaid.
  Throughout his life of service, Chief Haas always devoted himself to 
working for others. He will be remembered as many things: a hero, a 
patriot, a community leader, and, most of all, a friend. May he rest in 
peace.

                          ____________________
```
