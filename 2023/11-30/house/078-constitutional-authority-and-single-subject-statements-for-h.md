# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6520

**Measures:** H.R. 6520

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 6520.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``Regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Excludes individuals who are not citizens of the United 
     States from the number of persons used to determine the 
     apportionment of Representatives and the number of electoral 
     votes.
```
