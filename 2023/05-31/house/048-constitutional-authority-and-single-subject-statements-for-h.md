# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3763

**Measures:** H.R. 3763

```
           By Mr. CRAWFORD:
       H.R. 3763.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Water Resources Reform and Development Act of 
     2014 with respect to the application of the Spill Prevention, 
     Control, and Countermeasure rule to certain farms, and for 
     other purposes.
```
