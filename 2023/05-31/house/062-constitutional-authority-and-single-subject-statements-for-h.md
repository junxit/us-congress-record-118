# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3777

**Measures:** H.R. 3777

```
           By Mr. ROUZER:
        H.R. 3777.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and 
     Emergency Assistance Act to provide assistance for common 
     interest communities, condominiums, and housing cooperatives 
     damaged by a major disaster.
```
