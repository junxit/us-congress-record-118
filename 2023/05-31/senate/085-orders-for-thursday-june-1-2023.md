# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 1, 2023

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 45

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 1, 2023

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, June 1; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day and morning business be closed; further, that following the 
conclusion of morning business, the Senate resume consideration of H.J. 
Res. 45; that at 12:15 p.m., the joint resolution be considered read a 
third time and the Senate vote on passage.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
