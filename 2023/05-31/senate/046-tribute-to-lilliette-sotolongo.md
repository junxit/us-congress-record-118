# TRIBUTE TO LILLIETTE SOTOLONGO

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO LILLIETTE SOTOLONGO

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Lilliette Sotolongo, a 
spring 2023 intern in my Miami office, for the hard work she has done 
for my office and the people of Florida.
  Lilliette recently graduated from Florida International University, 
where she majored in political science and international relations. She 
is a dedicated and diligent worker who was devoted to getting the most 
out of her internship experience.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Lilliette for her work with my 
office, and I look forward to hearing of her successes in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
