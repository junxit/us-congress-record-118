# TRIBUTE TO DANIEL ENRIQUE NIETO

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO DANIEL ENRIQUE NIETO

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Daniel Enrique Nieto, a 
spring 2023 intern in my Tallahassee office, for the hard work he has 
done for my office and the people of Florida.
  Daniel is a student at Florida State University, where he is majoring 
in international affairs and political science. He is a dedicated and 
diligent worker who was devoted to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.

and I look forward to hearing of his successes in the years to 
come.●
```
