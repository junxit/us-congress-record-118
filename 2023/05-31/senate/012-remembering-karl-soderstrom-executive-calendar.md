# Remembering Karl Soderstrom (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                      Remembering Karl Soderstrom

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I wanted to start this morning by 
expressing my condolences to my chief of staff, Sharon Soderstrom, who 
lost her father a few days ago. Here is what she had to say about him.
  She said:

       My dad truly was one of the great good gifts of my life. I 
     hope to be back tomorrow.

  I think Sharon pretty well summed it up. Her dad was a special, 
special person, and our thoughts and prayers are with Sharon and her 
family this week.
```
