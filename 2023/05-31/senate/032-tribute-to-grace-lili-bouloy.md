# TRIBUTE TO GRACE LILI BOULOY

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO GRACE LILI BOULOY

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Grace Lili Bouloy, a 
recent intern in my Tallahassee office, for the hard work she has done 
for my office and the people of Florida.
  Grace is a student at Florida State University, where she is majoring 
in political science. She possesses many wonderful qualities that 
include an aptitude to evaluate complex issues, a strong work ethic, 
and a kindness that inspires.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Grace for her work with my office, 
and I look forward to hearing of her successes in the years to 
come.●
```
