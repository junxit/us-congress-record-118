# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4210

**Measures:** H.R. 4210

```
           By Ms. WATERS:
       H.R. 4210.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Closes a loophole that allowed large banks like Signature 
     Bank and First Republic Bank to escape Dodd-Frank's enhanced 
     prudential standards simply because they did not have a bank 
     holding company.
```
