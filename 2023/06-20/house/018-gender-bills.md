# GENDER BILLS

**Speaking:** Ogles, Andrew (O000175) [R-TN]

```
                              GENDER BILLS

  (Mr. OGLES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. OGLES. Madam Speaker, the hypersexualized left is not satisfied 
with its month of sexual pride. Instead, they are going after our 
children, and it is coming to a neighborhood near you.
  From drag shows in schools, to gender-affirming care, to hiding 
sexual orientation and gender identity from parents, the sex-obsessed 
left knows no bounds; which is why I am introducing two bills to 
protect children and to affirm God's creation.
  First, I am introducing the Keep Our Kids Safe Act of 2023, which 
will prohibit use of Federal education funding for drag performances or 
instruction by drag queens in elementary or secondary schools.
  Can you believe that in this time that I am having to introduce 
legislation to protect children from men in dresses?
  The second bill I am introducing is the There Are Two Sexes Act of 
2023, which prohibits Federal funds from being used to create any 
Federal form that has anything other than male or female. America 
should not be funding the radical left's agenda to sexualize children 
in schools.
```
