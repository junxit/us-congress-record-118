# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1210

**Measures:** H.R. 1210

```
           By Mr. DesJARLAIS:
       H.R. 1210.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill authorizes the Department of Health and Human 
     Services (HHS) to restrict migrationand imports from foreign 
     countries to prevent the introduction of illicit drugs into 
     the United States.
```
