# COMMENDING NICHOLAS AYERS ON HIS WORK FOR ALABAMA DISTRICT ONE

**Speaking:** Carl, Jerry L. (C001054) [R-AL]

```
     COMMENDING NICHOLAS AYERS ON HIS WORK FOR ALABAMA DISTRICT ONE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JERRY L. CARL

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, February 27, 2023

  Mr. CARL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Nicholas Ayers for his 
service to Alabama's First Congressional District. A Virginia native, 
Nicholas graduated from George Mason University, where he also received 
his Master of Public Administration. Nicholas has been on the Hill for 
nearly six years and has dedicated his time serving for the U.S. House 
of Representatives. Starting off as a Legislative Intern, Nicholas 
researched information about legislation and issues which affected that 
district. He was then promoted to Staff Assistant, Legislative 
Correspondent, Legislative Aid, and then I hired him on as my 
Legislative Assistant. His extensive knowledge of health care made him 
an asset to my office, and I know wherever he goes next, he will 
continue to be a leader. We wish him all the best in his future 
endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
