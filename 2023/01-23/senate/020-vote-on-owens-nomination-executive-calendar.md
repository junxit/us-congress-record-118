# Vote on Owens Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Duckworth, Tammy (D000622) [D-IL], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Owens Nomination

  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Owens 
nomination?
  Ms. DUCKWORTH. Madam President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Maine (Mr. King) and the 
Senator from California (Mr. Padilla) are necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Utah (Mr. Lee), the Senator from Alaska (Ms. Murkowski), and the 
Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch).
  Further, if present and voting, the Senator from Utah (Mr. Lee) would 
have voted ``nay'' and the Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch) would have 
voted ``nay.''
  The result was announced--yeas 60, nays 35, as follows:
```
