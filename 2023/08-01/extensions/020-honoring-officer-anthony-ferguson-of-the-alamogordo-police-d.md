# HONORING OFFICER ANTHONY FERGUSON OF THE ALAMOGORDO POLICE DEPARTMENT

**Speaking:** Vasquez, Gabe (V000136) [D-NM]

```
 HONORING OFFICER ANTHONY FERGUSON OF THE ALAMOGORDO POLICE DEPARTMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GABE VASQUEZ

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 1, 2023

  Mr. VASQUEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart to honor 
the memory of Anthony Ferguson, a dedicated public servant who was 
killed in the line of duty while serving as a police officer in my 
district.
  Officer Ferguson spent eleven years serving his community as a member 
of the Alamogordo Police Department. As a field training officer in the 
patrol division, he trained countless other officers to protect and 
serve. On July 16, 2023, Officer Ferguson was senselessly killed in 
action while protecting his fellow New Mexicans.
  Officer Ferguson is remembered by his family, friends, and fellow 
officers as loyal and courageous in the performance of his duties. His 
sacrifice in the line of duty serves as a solemn reminder of the 
bravery and commitment of our law enforcement officers. His loss is 
felt across the state, and my deepest condolences go out to all who 
knew and loved him.
  We owe a debt of gratitude to our law enforcement officers that can 
never be fully repaid. We must strive to honor Officer Ferguson's 
memory by continuing to support and appreciate those who put their 
lives on the line every day to keep our communities safe.
  On behalf of the great people of New Mexico's 2nd Congressional 
District, I thank Officer Ferguson for his service, and his ultimate 
sacrifice for the community of Alamogordo. We must not let his bravery 
be forgotten.

                          ____________________
```
