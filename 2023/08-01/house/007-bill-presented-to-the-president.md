# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1096

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Acting Clerk of the House, reported that on July 
26, 2023, the following bill was presented to the President of the 
United States for approval:

       H.R. 1096. To require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint

     States Marine Corps, and to support programs at the Marine
```
