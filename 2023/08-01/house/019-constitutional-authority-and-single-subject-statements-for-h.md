# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5100

**Measures:** H.R. 5100

```
            By Mr. CROW:
        H.R. 5100.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, United States Constitution.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        The single subject of this legislation is to to provide 
     for timely determination and action on applications by a 
     member of one of certain Armed Forces for consideration of a 
     reassignment to a duty station within a certain distance to 
     the residence of a dependent child.
```
