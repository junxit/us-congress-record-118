# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5122

**Measures:** H.R. 5122

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 5122.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 10, United States Code, to include the 
     hardening of infrastructure in support of disaster mitigation 
     in the definition of humanitarian and civic assistance.
```
