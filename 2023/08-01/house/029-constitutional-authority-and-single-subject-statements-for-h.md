# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5110

**Measures:** H.R. 5110

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
       H.R. 5110.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Corrects the Department of Education's interpretation to 
     allow hunting, archery, and other shooting sports to recieve 
     Elementary and Secondary Education Act federal funding.
```
