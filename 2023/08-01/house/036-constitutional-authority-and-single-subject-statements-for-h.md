# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5117

**Measures:** H.R. 5117

```
           By Mr. MOORE of Alabama:
       H.R. 5117.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To make funds available for grants under the COPS program 
     to be used for establishing school-based partnerships between 
     local law enforcement agencies and local school systems by 
     using school resource officers.
```
