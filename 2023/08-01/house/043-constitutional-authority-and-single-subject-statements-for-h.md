# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5124

**Measures:** H.R. 5124

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 5124.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to increase the 
     length of the post-employment ban on lobbying by certain 
     former senior executive branch personnel from one year to 2 
     years.
```
