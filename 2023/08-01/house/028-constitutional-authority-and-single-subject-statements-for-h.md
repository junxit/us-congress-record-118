# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5109

**Measures:** H.R. 5109

```
           By Mr. GALLAGHER:
       H.R. 5109.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Tax Clause; Article I, Section 8, Clause 1.
       The Necessary and Proper Clause; Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill denies tax exemptions to certain taxpayers who 
     invest in PRC companies.
```
