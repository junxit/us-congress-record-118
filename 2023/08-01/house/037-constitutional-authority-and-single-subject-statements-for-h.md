# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5118

**Measures:** H.R. 5118

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 5118.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 17 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would remove the authority of the National 
     Capital Planning Commission to review or approve the 
     development of District of Columbia-owned real property.
```
