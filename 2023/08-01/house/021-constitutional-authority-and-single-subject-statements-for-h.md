# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5102

**Measures:** H.R. 5102

```
            By Mrs. DINGELL:
        H.R. 5102.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        The Constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        To make it easier for individuals living with disabilities 
     with lower incomes to save.
```
