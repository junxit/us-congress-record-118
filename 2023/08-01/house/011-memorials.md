# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 678

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       ML-43. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of Texas, relative to Senate Resolution No. 678, 
     requesting that the Congress of the United States fully 
     reimburse the State of Texas for $9,927,000,000 in expended 
     funds that were used or will be used to meet federal 
     obligations to secure the southern border; which was referred 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
