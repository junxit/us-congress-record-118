# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5112

**Measures:** H.R. 5112

```
           By Ms. JACOBS:
       H.R. 5112.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to establish procedures 
     for the review and reinvestigation of certain allegations of 
     civilian harm that resulted from United States military 
     operations, and for other purposes.
```
