# HONORING HOMETOWN HERO LISA GOODWIN

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                  HONORING HOMETOWN HERO LISA GOODWIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 22, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lisa Goodwin, a 
shepherd of altruism in North Texas. Lisa routinely goes the extra mile 
to give back to our North Texas community through her charitable 
conduct and volunteerism. Lisa has earned a well-deserved reputation 
for going above and beyond to help others, including using her own 
resources to purchase childcare products for families in need. In her 
free time, Lisa visits nursing home residents and refugees in the Fort 
Worth area to offer them companionship. When Lisa is not volunteering 
her time at church, she can be found helping teens register for 
volunteer hours.
  I want to thank Lisa for being a shining beacon of light in our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
