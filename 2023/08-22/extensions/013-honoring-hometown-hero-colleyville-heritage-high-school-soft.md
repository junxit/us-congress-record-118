# HONORING HOMETOWN HERO COLLEYVILLE HERITAGE HIGH SCHOOL SOFTBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING HOMETOWN HERO COLLEYVILLE HERITAGE HIGH SCHOOL SOFTBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 22, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Colleyville Heritage 
High School's Softball Team, who recently won the 2023 UIL Class 5A 
Region I championship. This is the first time in the school history 
that the softball team advanced to the UIL 5A State Tournament. On 
Saturday, May 27, 2023, head coach Allison Conaway led this group of 
talented young women to victory. In the regional finals they beat 
Northwest High School, in the third game of the round with a final 
score of 1 to 0. This team has shown hard work and dedication all 
season and this was a well-deserved victory. I commend this group of 
young women on their UIL Regional championship victory and representing 
the 24th Congressional District of Texas.
```
