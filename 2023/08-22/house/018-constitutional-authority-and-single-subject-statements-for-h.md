# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5251

**Measures:** H.R. 5251

```
            By Mr. FERGUSON:
        H.R. 5251.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution, to 
     ``provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Cemetery Trusts. To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to allow a deduction for investment advisory expenses of 
     certain funeral and cemetery trusts during suspension of 
     miscellaneous itemized deductions.
```
