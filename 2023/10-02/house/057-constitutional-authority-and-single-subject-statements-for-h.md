# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5864

**Measures:** H.R. 5864

```
           By Mr. FITZPATRICK:
       H.R. 5864.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires state Medicaid programs to cover 
     noninvasive prenatal genetic screening for pregnant women. 
     (Noninvasive prenatal genetic screening uses naturally 
     occurring fetal DNA from the mother's blood to screen for 
     genetic abnormalities.)
```
