# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5865

**Measures:** H.R. 5865

```
           By Mr. LaMALFA:
       H.R. 5865.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 and Article IV, Section 3, 
     Clause 2 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establishes a pilot program with the U.S. Army Corps of 
     Engineers to protect Native American burial sites, village 
     sites, and cultural resources.
```
