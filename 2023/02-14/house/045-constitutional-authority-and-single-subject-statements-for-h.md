# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1021

**Measures:** H.R. 1021

```
       By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 1021.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the Interior to remove or 
     permanently conceal the name of Francis Newlands on the 
     grounds of the memorial fountain located at Chevy Chase 
     Circle in the District of Columbia.
```
