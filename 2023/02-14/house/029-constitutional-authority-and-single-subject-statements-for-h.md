# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1005

**Measures:** H.R. 1005

```
           By Mr. DIAZ-BALART:
       H.R. 1005.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill reauthorizes the National Threat Assessment 
     Center (NTAC) within the U.S. Secret Service. It reauthorizes 
     the functions of NTAC through FY2028 and expands them to 
     include the establishment of a national program on targeted 
     school violence prevention, while also advancing the Center's 
     mission to prevent violence in houses of worship, workplaces 
     and more through evidence-based research and training
```
