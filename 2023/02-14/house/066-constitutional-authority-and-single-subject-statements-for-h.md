# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1042

**Measures:** H.R. 1042

```
           By Mrs. RODGERS of Washington:
       H.R. 1042.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 10
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase American energy production and restore energy 
     leadership by preventing imports of uranium from Russia.
```
