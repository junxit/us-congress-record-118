# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 990

**Measures:** H.R. 990

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 990.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To update Medicaid DSH formula rates to prioritize funding 
     for hospitals that provide most care to low-income patients.
```
