# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1023

**Measures:** H.R. 1023

```
           By Mr. PALMER:
       H.R. 1023.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase American energy production and restore energy 
     leadership by repealing section 134 of the Clean Air Act, 
     regarding the greenhouse gas reduction fund.
```
