# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1000

**Measures:** H.R. 1000

```
           By Ms. FOXX:
       H.R. 1000.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 2 of the U.S. Constitution which states 
     ``Each House may determine the Rules of its Proceedings . . 
     .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Changing when and how the Oath of Office is Administered to 
     House Members-elect.
```
