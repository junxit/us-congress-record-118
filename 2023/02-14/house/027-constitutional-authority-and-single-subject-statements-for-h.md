# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1003

**Measures:** H.R. 1003

```
           By Mr. CORREA:
       H.R. 1003.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to carry out a 
     study and clinical trials on the effects of cannabis on 
     certain health outcomes of covered veterans
```
