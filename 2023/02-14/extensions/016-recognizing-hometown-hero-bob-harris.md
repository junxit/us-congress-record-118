# RECOGNIZING HOMETOWN HERO BOB HARRIS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                  RECOGNIZING HOMETOWN HERO BOB HARRIS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 14, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Bob Harris, Co-
founder of Level Pathways. Level Pathways is a nonprofit group in 
Irving that encourages homeless youth by facilitating employment, 
medical, food, clothing, and educational resources.
  Level Pathways was founded to give homeless youth in high school a 
chance to live a new life, graduate, and develop into productive 
citizens. Bob works with multiple organizations and nonprofits to 
provide fundamental resources to curb homelessness. On top of being CEO 
of Level Pathways, Bob also has more than 12 years of experience 
operating Wilderness Trek Christian Camp and Texoma Youth Camp. He has 
been working for nonprofit organizations in North Texas for more than 
30 years. Bob is an active member of our community working to end 
homelessness and allocate structural resources for underprivileged 
youth to excel.
  I thank Bob for going above and beyond for our community. His 
selflessness and dedication do not go unnoticed.
```
