# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 78

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 78

```
           By Mr. McGOVERN:
       H.J. Res. 78.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article V.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       A joint resolution proposing an amendment to the 
     Constitution of the United States relating to the authority 
     of Congress and the States to regulate campaign financing, 
     contributions, and spending.
```
