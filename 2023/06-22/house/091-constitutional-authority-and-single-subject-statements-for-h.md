# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4314

**Measures:** H.R. 4314

```
           By Mrs. WATSON COLEMAN:
       H.R. 4314.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Clause 1 of Section 8 of 
     Article 1 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To improve response to, and preparation for, heat waves and 
     extreme heat.
```
