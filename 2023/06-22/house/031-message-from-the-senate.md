# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 11, H.J.Res. 44

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Byrd, one of its clerks, announced 
that the joint resolution from the House of Representatives (H.J. Res. 
44) entitled ``Joint Resolution providing for congressional disapproval 
under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted 
by the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives relating to 
`Factoring Criteria for Firearms with Attached `Stabilizing Braces' '', 
did fail to pass the Senate.
  The message also announced that the Senate having proceeded to 
reconsider the bill (S.J. Res. 11) entitled ``Joint Resolution 
providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, 
United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
Protection Agency relating to `Control of Air Pollution from New Motor 
Vehicles: Heavy-Duty Engine and Vehicle Standards' '', returned by the 
President of the United States with his objections, to the Senate, in 
which it originated, it was
  Resolved, That the said bill do not pass, two-thirds of the Senators 
present not having voted in the affirmative.

                          ____________________
```
