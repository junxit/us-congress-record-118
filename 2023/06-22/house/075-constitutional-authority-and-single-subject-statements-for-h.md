# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4298

**Measures:** H.R. 4298

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 4298.

       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution

       To prevent Federal funds from being used to sexualize
```
