# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4278

**Measures:** H.R. 4278

```
           By Mr. BOST:
       H.R. 4278.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article 1, Section 8 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amending title 38, United States Code, to modify personnel 
     action procedures with respect to employees of Department of 
     Veterans Affairs.
```
