# CONGRATULATING LENOIR-RHYNE UNIVERSITY MEN'S LACROSSE

**Speaking:** McHenry, Patrick T. (M001156) [R-NC]

```
         CONGRATULATING LENOIR-RHYNE UNIVERSITY MEN'S LACROSSE

  (Mr. McHENRY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McHENRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to salute the Lenoir-Rhyne 
University's men's lacrosse team for bringing home the NCAA Division II 
National Championship in an overwhelming 20-5 victory over Mercyhurst 
College.
  Mr. Speaker, 2 years ago, the Bears made their first appearance in 
the national championship game but came up just short. This time, 
though, they would not be denied. In fact, this wasn't just one 
dominating game for the Bears, it was a dominating season. All of North 
Carolina is proud that Lenoir-Rhyne has brought the national title home 
to Hickory. I am proud to represent Lenoir-Rhyne here in Washington.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Coach Greg, the Lenoir-Rhyne president, 
Dr. Whitt, the entire Lenoir-Rhyne community, and the Greater Hickory 
Metro.
  Go Bears.

                          ____________________
```
