# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4275

**Measures:** H.R. 4275

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 4275.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires entities that acquire or transfer human 
     bodies or body parts for education, research, or the 
     advancement of medical, dental, or mortuary science to 
     register with the Department of Health and Human Services.
```
