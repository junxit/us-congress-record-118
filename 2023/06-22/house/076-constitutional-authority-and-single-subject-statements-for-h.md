# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4299

**Measures:** H.R. 4299

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 4299.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prevent Federal funds from being made that offer an 
     option other than Male or Female to mark the sex of an 
     individual.
```
