# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, in this most holy time of the year, we pray 
for a spirit of gratitude in this Chamber and throughout our Nation. 
May we be truly grateful from the bottom of our hearts for all the good 
You have done for us. We give thanks for the grace of life, which we 
cherish, and we especially give thanks for the gift of eternal life, 
which You offer us as a sheer gift of Your self-sacrificing love. We 
thank You. We bless You. We glorify You. We pray all this in Thy sacred 
name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
