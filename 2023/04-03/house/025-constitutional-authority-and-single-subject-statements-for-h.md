# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2470

**Measures:** H.R. 2470

```
            By Mr. RUIZ:
        H.R. 2470.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution, to provide for the general welfare and make all 
     laws necessary and proper to carry out the powers of 
     Congress.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill expands the allowable uses of grants for 
     Hispanic-serving institutions.
```
