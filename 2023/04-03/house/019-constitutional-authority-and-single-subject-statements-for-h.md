# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2464

**Measures:** H.R. 2464

```
            By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
        H.R. 2464.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article One, Section 8, Clause 14
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Commander of United States Army Special 
     Operations Command to seek to carry out a training program 
     with special operations forces of the Polish Army.
```
