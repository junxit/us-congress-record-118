# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer.
  Good and gracious God, we struggle to allocate funds properly. We 
strive to use our resources for the good of all. Remind us today that 
You are the source of all good. You are the maker of our lives and our 
world, and all things will return to You.
  Help us to keep our eyes fixed on You and on our heavenly goal. Thus, 
we will be guided by Your spirit in the difficult choices of these 
days.
  We pray all this in Thy sacred name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
