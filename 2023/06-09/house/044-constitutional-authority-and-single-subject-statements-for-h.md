# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3964

**Measures:** H.R. 3964

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 3964.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To codify and authorize the ReConnect program and make 
     updates to the ReConnect program's speed requirements and 
     priorities to ensure that rural communities have access to 
     modern internet
```
