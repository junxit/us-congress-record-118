# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3939

**Measures:** H.R. 3939

```
           By Mr. MEEKS:
       H.R. 3939.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for the transfer of Virginia class submarines 
     from the inventory of the Navy to the Government of Australia 
     under Section 21 of the Arms Export Control Act and for 
     support and related training.
```
