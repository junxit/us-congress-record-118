# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3953

**Measures:** H.R. 3953

```
            By Mr. BOYLE of Pennsylvania:
       H.R. 3953.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution under the 
     General Welfare Clause.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation reforms the debt ceiling by authorizing 
     the Treasury to continue paying the nation's bills absent a 
     disapproval resolution from Congress. Secretary of the 
     Treasury shall submit to Congress a written certification 
     when the debt is within $100 billion of a $1 trillion 
     increment and then may borrow to meet existing commitments.
```
