# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3972

**Measures:** H.R. 3972

```
           By Mrs. HINSON:
       H.R. 3972.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend section 1271(b) of the Food Security Act of 1985 
     to direct the U.S. Department of Agriculture to address 
     challenges associated with flooding and drought within the 
     Regional Conservation Partnership Program.
```
