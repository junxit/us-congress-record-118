# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3966

**Measures:** H.R. 3966

```
           By Mr. MIKE GARCIA of California:
       H.R. 3966.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18:
       The Congress shall have power to make all Laws which shall 
     be necessary and proper for carrying into Execution the 
     foregoing Powers, and all other Powers vested by this 
     Constitution in the Government of the United

[[Page H2790]]

     States, or in any Department of Officer thereof.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Improving Atmospheric River Forecasts Act will improve 
     the forecasting of atmospheric rivers.
```
