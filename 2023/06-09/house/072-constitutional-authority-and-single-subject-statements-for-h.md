# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3992

**Measures:** H.R. 3992

```
            By Mr. SHERMAN:
        H.R. 3992.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I. Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constituion: 
     ``To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the 
     several States, and with the Indian Tribes;''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To improve Federal bank supervision and regulation
```
