# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3950

**Measures:** H.R. 3950

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 3950.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require sellers of event tickets to disclose information 
     about ticket prices and related fees to consumers.
```
