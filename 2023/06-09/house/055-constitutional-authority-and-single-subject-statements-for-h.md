# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3975

**Measures:** H.R. 3975

```
           By Mr. KILMER:
       H.R. 3975.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Creates an allowable use of funds to establish IT Service 
     Corps members at AmeriCorps aimed at supporting IT workforce
```
