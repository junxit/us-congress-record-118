# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5926

**Measures:** H.R. 5926

```
           By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
       H.R. 5926.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1: The Congress shall have 
     Power To lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and Excises, 
     to pay the Debts and provide for the common Defence and 
     general Welfare of the United States; but all Duties, Imposts 
     and Excises shall be uniform throughout the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would require a review by the Committee on 
     Foreign Investment in the United States for any investment by 
     a foreign individual that involves the acquisition of U.S. 
     real estate and establishment of a U.S. business on such real 
     estate that would result in the People's Republic of China's 
     direct or indirect control over the U.S. business.
```
