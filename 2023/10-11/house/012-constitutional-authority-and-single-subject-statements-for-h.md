# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5919

**Measures:** H.R. 5919

```
           By Mr. CURTIS:
       H.R. 5919.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Directs the Secretary of the Interior to conduct an 
     assessment to identify locations in National Parks in which 
     there is the greatest need for broadband internet access and 
     cellular service.
```
