# HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ERIN GARCIA-ROBLES

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
        HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: ERIN GARCIA-ROBLES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 11, 2023

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Erin Garcia-Robles as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year.
  Erin is a registered nurse who serves as a staffing supervisor for 
the Behavior Health Unit at Woodland Hospital. She works with patients 
who are struggling with substance abuse and mental health issues. She 
is a compassionate caregiver for suicidal patients and people who have 
been sexually abused. Erin manages intake for some of the most 
vulnerable patients, ensuring that doctors and staff know what care 
they need. She ensures that her patients receive proper placements and 
are connected with family members. She is recognized by her family, 
friends, and colleagues for her caring nature and determined attitude 
which she brings to every interaction. Her dedication to her patients, 
family, and friends is an inspiration to everyone who knows her.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Erin Garcia-Robles for her 
positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
