# RECOGNIZING COMPEER INTERNATIONAL

**Speaking:** Higgins, Brian (H001038) [D-NY]

```
                   RECOGNIZING COMPEER INTERNATIONAL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BRIAN HIGGINS-

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 11, 2023

  Mr. HIGGINS of New York. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, today I am honored 
to recognize an organization with a noble cause, Compeer International. 
For numerous years, Compeer has helped individuals who suffer from 
mental health problems by utilizing the power of friendship and small 
group mentoring services.
  On this World Mental Health Day, Compeer International is marking 
their fifth decade of service to their community. Founded in Rochester, 
NY as a means to treat formerly institutionalized patients, Compeer has 
grown to be a multinational organization, offering services in Canada, 
the U.K., and Australia. Upon receiving the Presidential Recognition 
Award in 1982, they have never looked back; Compeer's ongoing mission 
to expand their services has proven incredibly successful.
  After tireless efforts from countless advocates across our country, 
mental health issues are finally getting the attention they deserve. 
Mental health and substance abuse challenges impact thousands of 
Western New York families. It is imperative that our community leaders 
understand their respective roles in addressing these concerns and 
ensuring that proper resources are readily available. Adults and 
children, alike, suffer from a wide variety of mental health 
challenges, and it is through groups like Compeer that individuals can 
find the appropriate assistance. All ages are encouraged to take 
advantage of one-to-one programs, and their fifty-year history is a 
testament to the dedication of their leadership, staff, and volunteers. 
The events that our community has endured in recent years underscore 
the need to prioritize mental healthcare in Western New York. It can be 
confidently stated that Compeer's comprehensive programs are working to 
find solutions by creating a support system for our neighbors in need. 
It is my sincere honor to recognize Compeer's everimportant work over 
the last fifty years and express my gratitude for all of their future 
efforts as we continue to battle mental health challenges.

                          ____________________
```
