# HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: JUNE MEJIAS

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
            HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: JUNE MEJIAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 11, 2023

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise to recognize the 
contributions of June Mejias as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year.
  June is a dedicated public servant and active member of the Benicia 
community. June's advocacy began with her efforts to understand the 
Crude by Rail issue and educate others on its importance to their 
region. She went on to serve on the Benicia Campaign Finance 
Subcommittee and meet with the Benicia Open Government Commission. She 
has served for many years with the League of Women Voters and would 
often volunteer to visit high schools and help register eligible 
students to vote. She was also selected by the City Council to serve 
for 3 years on the Benicia Parks and Recreation Commission. June 
continues to engage with all levels of government, advocating for 
issues that impact not only her, but the community at large. She 
consistently ensures that her voice and those of her peers continue to 
be heard. June is a role model of civic engagement for anyone looking 
to have an impact on the policies that impact them.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize June Mejias for her positive 
contributions to our community.
```
