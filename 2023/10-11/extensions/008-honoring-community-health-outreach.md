# HONORING COMMUNITY HEALTH OUTREACH

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
                   HONORING COMMUNITY HEALTH OUTREACH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 11, 2023

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, today I rise to honor 
an organization in Florida's 4th District that exemplifies the spirit 
of compassion and service--Community Health Outreach.
  For 35 years, they have been the cornerstone of hope for low-income, 
uninsured families in our northeast Florida community. Their tireless 
efforts have not only provided critical healthcare but also empowered 
countless families, including our brave U.S. Military Veterans, to 
overcome adversity.
  Community Health Outreach is not just a healthcare provider; it's a 
beacon of sustainable change. They've woven a tapestry of support, 
offering resources, guidance, and a lifeline to those who need it most 
as they work to build a healthier society.
  Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I ask that my colleagues in Congress join me 
in recognizing this milestone and celebrating the unsung heroes within 
Community Health Outreach and all those they have touched.
  May their legacy inspire us to uplift the vulnerable, to strengthen 
our communities, and to forge a brighter, healthier future for all 
Americans.

                          ____________________
```
