# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Scott, David (S001157) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 27, H.J.Res. 42, H.R. 1149

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID SCOTT

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 2, 2023

  Mr. DAVID SCOTT of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, had my vote for H.J. Res. 
27, the ``Providing congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 
5 United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Department of the 
Army, Corps of Engineers, Department of Defense and the Environmental 
Protection Agency relating to ``Revised Definition of `Waters of the 
United States' '' been cast, it would be recorded as YEA. I would also 
like the record to show that had my vote for H.J. Res. 42, the 
``Disapproving the action of the District of Columbia Council in 
approving the Comprehensive Policing and Justice Reform Amendment Act'' 
been cast, it would be recorded as NAY. Further, had my vote for H.R. 
1149, the ``Countering Untrusted Telecommunications Abroad Act'' been 
cast, it would be recorded a YEA.
  Had I been present, I would have voted YEA on Roll Call No. 187, NAY 
on Roll Call No. 188, and YEA on Roll Call No. 189.
```
