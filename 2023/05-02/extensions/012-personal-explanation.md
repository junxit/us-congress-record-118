# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Scott, David (S001157) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 734

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DAVID SCOTT

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 2, 2023

  Mr. DAVID SCOTT of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, had my vote for Mace of 
South Carolina Amendment No. 1, to H.R. 734, the ``Protection of Women 
and Girls in Sports Act'' been cast, it would have been recorded as 
NAY. I would also like the record to show that had my vote for Motion 
to Recommit for H.R. 734, the ``Protection of Women and Girls in Sports 
Act'' been cast, it would have been recorded YEA. Lastly, I would like 
the record to show that had my vote for H.R. 734, the ``Protection of 
Women and Girls in Sports Act'' been cast, it would have been recorded 
as NAY.
  Had I been present, I would have voted NAY on Roll Call No. 190, YEA 
on Roll Call No. 191, and NAY on Roll Call No. 192.
```
