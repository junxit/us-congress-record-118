# RECOGNIZING MADDY MALINE FOR EXEMPLARY COMMUNITY SERVICE AND HER STEADFAST COMMITMENT TO MICHIGAN'S FIRST DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
   RECOGNIZING MADDY MALINE FOR EXEMPLARY COMMUNITY SERVICE AND HER 
           STEADFAST COMMITMENT TO MICHIGAN'S FIRST DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 2, 2023

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Maddy Maline of 
Manistique Middle and High School for her exemplary service to her 
community and her astounding achievements in and out of the classroom. 
Maddy has excelled in serving others, earning the rank of Eagle Scout 
and being awarded the Congressional Award for her generous service. 
Maddy has become an invaluable part of Manistique and the surrounding 
area and has had a lasting impact on her peers, garnering the respect 
of many across her community. She deserves our praise for her 
determination and drive in her endeavors to be an asset to Michigan's 
First District.
  Maddy's dedication to bettering herself and her community will 
continue to have an enduring, positive effect on those she encounters. 
As she continues beyond Manistique Middle and High School and expands 
her horizon, we celebrate the connections she has made, the lives she 
has changed, and any future opportunities she will have during this 
monumental shift in her life.
  Mr. Speaker, again it is my honor to recognize Maddy Maline for her 
exceptional community service and the ripple effect of positive change 
she has created as she graduates Manistique High School. On behalf of 
Michigan's First District, I extend my gratitude to Maddy and her 
family and wish them the best in their future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
