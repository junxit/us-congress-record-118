# HONORING THE LIFE OF FIREFIGHTER JASON TUCKER

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
             HONORING THE LIFE OF FIREFIGHTER JASON TUCKER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 2, 2023

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate 
the life of my constituent, Pasco County Firefighter Jason Tucker. 
Jason served our community for 19 years as a firefighter for Pasco 
County Fire Rescue. Being named employee of the year for 2022, an award 
he earned through his dedication to his job, our community, and his 
fellow firefighters.
  He exemplified his strength and character throughout his long and 
courageous battle with cancer. Firefighter Tucker continued to be 
involved with the department, even after his medical retirement in July 
2022, routinely visiting with new-hire classes to teach the next 
generation the importance of cancer prevention. Through this work he 
has paved the way for future firefighters throughout the industry and 
saving countless lives. Throughout treatment he appeared at public 
events to speak about his illness. Jason Tucker will be remembered for 
his life of service and the courage he exemplified. May his memory be 
eternal.

                          ____________________
```
