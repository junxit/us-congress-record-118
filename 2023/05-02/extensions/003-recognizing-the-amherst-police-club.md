# RECOGNIZING THE AMHERST POLICE CLUB

**Speaking:** Higgins, Brian (H001038) [D-NY]

```
                  RECOGNIZING THE AMHERST POLICE CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRIAN HIGGINS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 2, 2023

  Mr. HIGGINS of New York. Mr. Speaker, today I am pleased to recognize 
The Amherst Police Club for their continued dedication to community 
service and safety. This is a model organization that serves as much-
needed support to the heroes in our community.
  In 1819, Timothy S. Hopkins was elected the first supervisor for the 
Town of Amherst. He appointed two Constables, Joseph Hershey and Palmer 
Cleveland. These two men patrolled the present boundaries of Amherst 
(as well as portions of Cheektowaga). By 1883, the number of Town 
Constables had been increased to five. In 1924, the Town of Amherst 
Police and the Village of Williamsville Police merged, and by 1938, the 
department had grown to thirteen men. Since that time, the Town of 
Amherst Police has only continued to grow and meet the needs of their 
citizens in large part due to the help and representation of the 
Amherst Police Club.
  The Amherst Police Club, Inc., is a non-profit organization that 
represents the sworn police officers of the Town of Amherst. They give 
back to the community and sponsor numerous charitable causes, events, 
youth sports teams and youth activities that have a positive impact and 
touch the lives of those they serve and protect. They also host annual 
events for the community to benefit the Amherst Police Benevolent 
Association. The representation of our sworn police officers requires a 
deep understanding and appreciation of dedication to service, and for 
that, Amherst Police Club fits the bill.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing and giving thanks to 
outstanding organizations in the Western New York area, especially to 
the Amherst Police Club as they are honored today for their continued 
dedication and selfless service to their community.
```
