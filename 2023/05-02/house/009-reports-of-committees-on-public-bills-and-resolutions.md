# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 300

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. McCAUL: Committee on Foreign Affairs. House Resolution 
     300. Resolution requesting the President and directing the 
     Secretary of Defense to transmit, respectively, to the House 
     of Representatives copies of all documents indicating any 
     plans for current or future military assistance to Ukraine 
     and documents indicating whether any United States Armed 
     Forces, including special operations forces, are currently 
     deployed in Ukraine (Rept. 118-44). Referred to the House 
     Calendar.
```
