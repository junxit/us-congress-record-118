# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3076

**Measures:** H.R. 3076

```
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 3076.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Sec. 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 36, United States Code, to direct the 
     Secretary of Veterans Affairs to establish a flag for next of 
     kin of veterans who die by suicide.
```
