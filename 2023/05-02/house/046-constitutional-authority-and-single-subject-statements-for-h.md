# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3070

**Measures:** H.R. 3070

```
           By Mr. STEIL:
       H.R. 3070.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires the President, before providing specified 
     sanctions relief to an Iranian person that is on the list of 
     specially designated nationals and blocked persons, to 
     certify to Congress that the person has ceased involvement in 
     terrorism
```
