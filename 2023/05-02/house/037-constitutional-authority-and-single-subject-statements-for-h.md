# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3061

**Measures:** H.R. 3061

```
           By Mr. LaLOTA:
       H.R. 3061.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 49, United States Code, to establish an 
     Aviation Security Checkpoint Technology Fund in the 
     Department of Homeland Security to fund investments in 
     aviation security checkpoint technology.
```
