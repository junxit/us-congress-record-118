# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3075

**Measures:** H.R. 3075

```
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 3075.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Sec. 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Communications Act of 1934 to require 
     providers of cable service and telephone service to charge 
     veterans' organizations residential rates for such services, 
     and for other purposes.
```
