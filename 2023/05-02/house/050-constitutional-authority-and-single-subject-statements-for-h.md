# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3074

**Measures:** H.R. 3074

```
           By Mr. TRONE:
       H.R. 3074.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Medicaid Inmate Exclusion Policy (MIEP) to 
     ensure that states have the option to provide Medicaid 
     coverage for pre-trial detainees.
```
