# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3077

**Measures:** H.R. 3077

```
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 3077.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Sec. 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to establish a 
     counseling program for certain survivor of veterans deceased 
     as the result of suicide.
```
