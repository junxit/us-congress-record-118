# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3039

**Measures:** H.R. 3039

```
           By Mr. BRECHEEN:
       H.R. 3039.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prevent the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration 
     from issuing a rule or regulation requiring speed limiting 
     devices on large commercial vehicles.
```
