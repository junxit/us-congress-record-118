# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3059

**Measures:** H.R. 3059

```
           By Mr. KILMER:
       H.R. 3059.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Expanding eligibility under the Federal Aviation 
     Administration's Airport Improvement Program (AIP) to allow 
     for certain projects related to resiliency against natural 
     disasters and emergency management.
```
