# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3064

**Measures:** H.R. 3064

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 3064.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 17 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would redesignate the Office of Mayor of the 
     District of Columbia as the Office of the Governor of the 
     District of Columbia and redesignate the Council of the 
     District of Columbia as the Legislative Assembly of the 
     District of Columbia.
```
