                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 26

  At the request of Mr. Hagerty, the name of the Senator from West 
Virginia (Mrs. Capito) was added as a cosponsor of S. 26, a bill to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to repeal the amendments made 
to reporting of third party network transactions by the American Rescue 
Plan Act of 2021.

  At the request of Ms. Baldwin, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
(Ms. Stabenow) was added as a cosponsor of S. 106, a bill to amend 
title 38, United States Code, to authorize the Secretary of Veterans 
Affairs to award grants to States to improve outreach to veterans, and 
for other purposes.

  At the request of Ms. Cantwell, the name of the Senator from New

prescription drugs, and for other purposes.

(Mr. Braun) and the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Welch) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 150, a bill to amend the Federal Trade Commission Act
