# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR EN BLOC--S. 1395 and H.R. 2811

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 1395, H.R. 2811

```
     MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR EN BLOC--S. 1395 and H.R. 2811

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, first, a little housekeeping: I 
understand that there are two bills at the desk due for a second 
reading en bloc.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The leader is correct.
  The clerk will read the bills by title for the second time, en bloc.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1395) to temporarily suspend the debt limit 
     through December 31, 2024.
       A bill (H.R. 2811) to provide for a responsible increase to 
     the debt ceiling, and for other purposes.

  Mr. SCHUMER. In order to place the bills on the Calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceedings, en bloc.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The objection having been heard, 
the bills will be placed on the Calendar.

                          ____________________
```
