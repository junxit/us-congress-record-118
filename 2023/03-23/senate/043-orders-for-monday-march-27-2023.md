# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 27, 2023

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 316

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 27, 2023

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, March 27; that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; that following the conclusion of 
morning business, the Senate resume consideration of Calendar No. 25, 
S. 316; further, that the filing

[[Page S942]]

deadline for first-degree amendments be at 3:30 p.m. and the cloture 
motion filed during today's session ripen at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
