# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1759

**Measures:** H.R. 1759

```
           By Ms. MALLIOTAKIS:
       H.R. 1759.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3:
       Congress shall have Power to regulate Commerce with foreign 
     Nations, and among the several States, and with the Indian 
     Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Transportation from 
     implementing a congestion pricing program until an economic 
     impact analysis is completed and made available to the 
     public.
```
