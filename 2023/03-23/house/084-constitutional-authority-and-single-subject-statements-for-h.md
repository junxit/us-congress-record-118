# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1765

**Measures:** H.R. 1765

```
           By Mr. PAYNE:
       H.R. 1765.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the for the common 
     Defense and general Welfare of the United States, as 
     enumerated in Article I, Section 8 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is,
       Nutrition
```
