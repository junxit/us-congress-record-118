# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1753

**Measures:** H.R. 1753

```
           By Mr. ISSA:
       H.R. 1753.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the Constitution of the 
     United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To ensure that certain members of the Armed Forces who 
     served in female cultural support teams receive proper credit 
     for such service.
```
