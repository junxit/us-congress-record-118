# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1760

**Measures:** H.R. 1760

```
           By Mr. MOYLAN:
       H.R. 1760.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article one of the United States Constitution 
     Congress has the power to enact this legislation.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To extend the admission to Guam or the Commonwealth of the 
     Northern Mariana Islands for certain nonimmigrant H-2B 
     workers.
```
