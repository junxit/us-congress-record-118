# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1766

**Measures:** H.R. 1766

       H.R. 1766.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant

       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the US Constitution

       To amend the Federal Power Act to establish a procedure for
