# NOTICE: REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

```
                 NOTICE: REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

  The filing date for the 2023 second quarter Mass Mailing report is 
Tuesday, July 25, 2023. An electronic option is available on Webster 
that will allow forms to be submitted via a fillable PDF document. If 
your office did no mass mailings during this period, please submit a 
form that states ``none.''
  Mass mailing registrations or negative reports can be submitted 
electronically at http://webster.senate.gov/secretary/
mass_mailing_form.htm or e-mailed to OPR_MassMailings@sec.
senate.gov.
  For further information, please contact the Senate Office of Public 
Records at (202) 224-0322.

                          ____________________
```
