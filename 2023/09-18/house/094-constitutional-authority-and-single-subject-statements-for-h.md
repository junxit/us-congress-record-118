# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5550

**Measures:** H.R. 5550

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 5550.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Foreign Relations Authorization Act, Fiscal 
     Years 1990 and 1991 to expand the Presidents authority to 
     deny any individual's admission to the United States as a 
     representative to the United Nations.
```
