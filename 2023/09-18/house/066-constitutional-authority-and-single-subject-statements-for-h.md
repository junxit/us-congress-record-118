# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5522

**Measures:** H.R. 5522

```
           By Mr. HIMES:
       H.R. 5522.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would would authorize sanctions on individuals 
     and entities who facilitate spyware, including spyware that 
     could enable the targeting of United States Government 
     officials or personnel of the intelligence community.
```
