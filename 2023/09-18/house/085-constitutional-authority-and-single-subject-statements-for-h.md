# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5541

**Measures:** H.R. 5541

```
           By Mr. LATTA:
       H.R. 5541.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: Congress shall have the 
     power . . . ``to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide temporary licensing reciprocity for telehealth 
     and interstate health care treatment.
```
