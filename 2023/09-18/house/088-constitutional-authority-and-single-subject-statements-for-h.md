# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5544

**Measures:** H.R. 5544

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 5544.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 9, Clause 7 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires all moneys received by the federal government from 
     any source (like user fees), other than programs where the 
     federal government is holding money for states/localities, 
     are deposited in the Treasury General Fund for appropriation.
```
