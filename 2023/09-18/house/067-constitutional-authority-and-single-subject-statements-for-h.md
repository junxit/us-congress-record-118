# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5523

**Measures:** H.R. 5523

```
           By Ms. WATERS:
       H.R. 5523.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To improve financial access for nations that appear in the 
     Money Laundering volume of the International Narcotics 
     Control Strategy Report (INSCR), issued annually by the 
     Department of State.
```
