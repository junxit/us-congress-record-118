# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 9, S.J.Res. 24

```
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on September 14, 2023, she 
had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled joint resolutions:

       S.J. Res. 9. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife 
     Service relating to ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and 
     Plants; Lesser Prairie-Chicken; Threatened Status With 
     Section 4(d) Rule for the Northern Distinct Population 
     Segment and Endangered Status for the Southern Distinct 
     Population Segment''.
       S.J. Res. 24. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife 
     Service relating to ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and 
     Plants; Endangered Species Status for Northern Long-Eared 
     Bat''.

                          ____________________
```
