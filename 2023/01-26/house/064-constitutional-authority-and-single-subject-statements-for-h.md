# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 540

**Measures:** H.R. 540

```
           By Mrs. KIM of California:
       H.R. 540.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Treasury to pursue more 
     equitable treatment of Taiwan at the international financial
```
