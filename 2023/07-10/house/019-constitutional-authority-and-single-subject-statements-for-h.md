# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4509

**Measures:** H.R. 4509

```
            By Ms. FOXX:
        H.R. 4509.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 
     1974 to require group health plans and health insurance 
     issuers offering group health insurance coverage to only pay 
     claims submitted by hospitals that have in place policies and 
     procedures to ensure accurate billing practices, and for 
     other purposes.
```
