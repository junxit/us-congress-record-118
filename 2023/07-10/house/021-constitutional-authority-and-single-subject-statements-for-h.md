# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4511

**Measures:** H.R. 4511

```
           By Mrs. CAMMACK:
        H.R. 4511.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the National Telecommunications and 
     Information Administration Organization Act to establish the 
     Office of Public Safety Communications.
```
