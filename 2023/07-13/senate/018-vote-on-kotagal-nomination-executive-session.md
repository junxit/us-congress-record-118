# Vote on Kotagal Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mark (K000377) [D-AZ], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Kotagal Nomination

  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Kotagal 
nomination?
  Mr. KELLY. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. 
Fetterman) is necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Tennessee (Mrs. Blackburn), the Senator from Missouri (Mr. 
Hawley), and the Senator from Ohio (Mr. Vance).
  Further, if present and voting: the Senator from Ohio (Mr. Vance) 
would have voted ``nay,'' and the Senator from Missouri (Mr. Hawley) 
would have voted ``nay.''
  The result was announced--yeas 49, nays 47, as follows:
```
