# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4610

**Measures:** H.R. 4610

```
           By Mr. DOGGETT:
       H.R. 4610.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article I of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To conform Federal tax law with the Texas Constitution to 
     allow for the continued benefit to the fullest extent 
     possible from the financial leverage of the Permanent 
     University Fund to the State of Texas.
```
