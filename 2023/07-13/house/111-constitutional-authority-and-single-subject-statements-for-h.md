# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4635

**Measures:** H.R. 4635

```
            By Ms. ROSS:
        H.R. 4635.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to remove the 
     record of default on a loan made, insured, or guaranteed 
     under title IV from a borrower's credit history upon 
     repayment of the full amount due on such loan.
```
