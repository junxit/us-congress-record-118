# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4600

**Measures:** H.R. 4600

```
            By Mr. BARR:
        H.R. 4600.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 to specify 
     requirements concerning the consideration of pecuniary and 
     non-pecuniary factors, to require the Securities and Exchange 
     Commission to conduct a study on climate change and other 
     environmental disclosures in the municipal bond market, and 
     to require the Securities and Exchange Commission to conduct 
     a study on the solicitation of municipal securities business.
```
