# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4597

**Measures:** H.R. 4597

```
            By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
        H.R. 4597.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 4, Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Citizen Vote Protection Act amends the REAL ID Act to 
     require states to include an indication of citizenship on 
     state-issued identification. Nothing in this section would 
     mandate or establish the use of a national identification 
     card or authorize any part of the executive branch to 
     establish or mandate the same.
```
