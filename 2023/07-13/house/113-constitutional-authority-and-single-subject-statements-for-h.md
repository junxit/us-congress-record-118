# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4637

**Measures:** H.R. 4637

```
            By Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ:
        H.R. 4637.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 relating to the 
     power of Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, 
     and among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires background checks for the purchase of ammunition 
     if the buyer does not have a permit to own a firearm.
```
