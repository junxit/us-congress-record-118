# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rev. John Kellogg, Christ Church, Washington Parish, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Loving God, who stills our storms and soothes our hearts, be with 
everyone this evening who waits or works in uncertainty. Give us hope 
for the future, courage to endure what cannot be avoided, and the 
resolve to be the equal of whatever lies ahead. You are God and we need 
You.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
