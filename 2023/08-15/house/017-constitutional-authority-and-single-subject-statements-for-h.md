# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5208

**Measures:** H.R. 5208

```
            By Mr. CARTER of Georgia:
        H.R. 5208.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to clarify the 
     treatment of locum tenens physicians and advanced care 
     practitioners as independent contractors to help alleviate 
     physician shortages, including in underserved areas.
```
