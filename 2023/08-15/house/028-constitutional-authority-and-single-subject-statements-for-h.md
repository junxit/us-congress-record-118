# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5219

**Measures:** H.R. 5219

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
        H.R. 5219.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 17 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:

[[Page H4188]]

       This bill would give the District of Columbia the authority 
     to appoint the members of the D.C. Board of Zoning Adjustment 
     (Board), except when the Board is performing functions 
     regarding an application by a foreign mission with respect to 
     a chancery.
```
