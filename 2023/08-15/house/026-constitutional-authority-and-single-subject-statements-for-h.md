# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5217

**Measures:** H.R. 5217

```
            By Mr. NADLER:
        H.R. 5217.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the National Wildlife Refuge System Administration 
     Act of 1966 to prohibit the possession or use of body-
     gripping traps in the National Wildlife Refuge System, and 
     for other purposes.
```
