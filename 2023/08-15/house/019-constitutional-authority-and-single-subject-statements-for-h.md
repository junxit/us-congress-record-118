# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5210

**Measures:** H.R. 5210

```
            By Mr. EDWARDS:
        H.R. 5210.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory and other Property 
     belonging to the United States.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would provide an EPA emissions exemption for 
     public safety agencies, boat motors with less than 60 
     horsepower.
```
