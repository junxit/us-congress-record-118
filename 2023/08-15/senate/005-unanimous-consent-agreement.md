# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. In my capacity as the Senator from 
Maryland, I ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding the order of 
July 27, 2023, the Senate, having now convened today, not convene at 12 
noon as previously ordered, and that when it adjourns today, it stand 
adjourned as under the previous order until 1 p.m. on Friday, August 
18, 2023, and that all other provisions of the July 27th order remain 
in effect.
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
