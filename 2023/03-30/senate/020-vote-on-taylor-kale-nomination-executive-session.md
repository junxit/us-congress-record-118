# Vote on Taylor-Kale Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Booker, Cory A. (B001288) [D-NJ], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                     Vote on Taylor-Kale Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Taylor-Kale nomination?
  Mr. BOOKER. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays, or whatever 
means yes and no.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Mrs. 
Feinstein), the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. Fetterman), and the 
Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) are necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Wyoming (Mr. Barrasso), the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. 
Cramer), the Senator from Tennessee (Mr. Hagerty), the Senator from 
Kentucky (Mr. McConnell), the Senator from Kansas (Mr. Moran), the 
Senator from Alaska (Ms. Murkowski), and the Senator from Missouri (Mr. 
Schmitt).
```
