                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: All 
nominations placed on the Secretary's desk for the Army, Air Force, 
Marine Corps, Navy, Space Force; that the nominations be confirmed en 
bloc, the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the 
table with no intervening action or debate; further, that no motions be 
in order to any of the nominations; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action and the Senate resume legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
