# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2429

**Measures:** H.R. 2429

```
           By Mrs. LEE of Nevada:
       H.R. 2429.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and 
     Excises'' in order to ``provide for the . . . general Welfare 
     of the United States.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish the Open Access Evapotranspiration (OpenET) 
     Data Program.
```
