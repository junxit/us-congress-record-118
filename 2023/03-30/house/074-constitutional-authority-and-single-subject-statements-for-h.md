# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2421

**Measures:** H.R. 2421

```
           By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 2421.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional Authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To improve responses to sexual violence against students 
     with disabilities.
```
