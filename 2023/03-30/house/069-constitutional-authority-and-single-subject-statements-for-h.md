# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2416

**Measures:** H.R. 2416

```
           By Mr. BURGESS:
       H.R. 2416.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Public Health Service Act to require 
     the Department of Health and Human Services to award grants 
     to certain trauma centers to enable military trauma care 
     providers and trauma teams to provide trauma care and related 
     acute care at those trauma centers.
```
