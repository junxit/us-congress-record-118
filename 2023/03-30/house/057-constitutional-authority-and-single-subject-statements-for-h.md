# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2404

**Measures:** H.R. 2404

```
           By Mr. SANTOS:
       H.R. 2404.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       US Constitution, Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the availability of funds to provide assistance 
     to foreign countries that criminalize or discriminate based 
     on gender, and for other purposes.
```
