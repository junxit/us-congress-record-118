# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2437

**Measures:** H.R. 2437

```
           By Mr. MURPHY:
       H.R. 2437.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To revise the boundaries of a unit of the John H. Chafee
```
