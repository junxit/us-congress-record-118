# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2434

**Measures:** H.R. 2434

```
           By Mrs. MILLER-MEEKS:
       H.R. 2434.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would improve fuel efficiency and performance of 
     vehicles, and reduce carbon emissions, through the greater 
     use of ethanol.
```
