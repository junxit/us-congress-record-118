# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2405

**Measures:** H.R. 2405

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
       H.R. 2405.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The North Dakota Trust Lands Completion Act of 2023 abides 
     by the single sublect requirement in that the provisions are 
     limited to relinquishment and in lieu selection of land and 
     minerals in the State of North Dakota.
```
