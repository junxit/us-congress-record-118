# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2426

**Measures:** H.R. 2426

```
           By Mr. TONY GONZALES of Texas:
       H.R. 2426.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, clause 1 and Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Office of the Administration for Children & 
     Families to evaluate the protocols that states have developed 
     to comply with the reporting requirements for locating a 
     child missing from foster care.
```
