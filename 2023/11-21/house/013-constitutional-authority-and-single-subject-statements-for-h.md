# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6460

**Measures:** H.R. 6460

```
           By Mr. BRECHEEN:
       H.R. 6460.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Health and Human Services from 
     issuing, finalizing, implementing, or enforcing any rule or 
     guidance to facilitate abortions or access to abortions for 
     an unaccompanied alien child.
```
