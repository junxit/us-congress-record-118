# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6461

**Measures:** H.R. 6461

```
           By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
       H.R. 6461.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To ensure that claims for benefits under the Black Lung 
     Benefits Act are processed in a fair and timely manner, to 
     better protect miners from pneumoconiosis (commonly known as 
     ``black lung disease''), and for other purposes.
```
