# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6467

**Measures:** H.R. 6467

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 6467.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to require all federal agencies to include in their annual 
     budget requests to Congress the amount they spend on 
     advertising contracts with small disadvantaged businesses and 
     businesses owned by minorities and women.
```
