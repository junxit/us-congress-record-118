# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6471

**Measures:** H.R. 6471

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 6471.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 to 
     prohibit contributions and donations by foreign nationals in 
     connection with State or local ballot initiatives or 
     referenda and recall elections.
```
