# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6468

**Measures:** H.R. 6468

```
           By Mr. PALLONE:
       H.R. 6468.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3: [The Congress shall 
     have Power] To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, sand 
     among the several states, and with the Indian Tribes;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide that certain water beads products shall be 
     considered banned hazardous products under section 8 of the 
     Consumer Product Safety Act, and for other purposes.
```
