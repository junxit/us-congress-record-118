# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 627

**Measures:** H.R. 627

```
           By Mr. FALLON:
       H.R. 627.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Voter Registration Act of 1993 to prohibit the 
     registration of individuals not providing proof of United 
     States Citizenship and applying criminal penalty for the 
     attempt to do so.
```
