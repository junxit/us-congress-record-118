# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5168

**Measures:** H.R. 5168

```
            By Mr. MOYLAN:
        H.R. 5168.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article one of the United States Constitution 
     Congress has the power to enact this legislation.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the President to award the Medal of Honor to 
     Joseph M. Perez for acts of valor as a member of the Army 
     during the Vietnam War.
```
