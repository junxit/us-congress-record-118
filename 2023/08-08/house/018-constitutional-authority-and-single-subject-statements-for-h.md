# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5171

**Measures:** H.R. 5171

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
        H.R. 5171.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would allow federal employees who reach the pay 
     cap in their pay system to receive the base and locality pay 
     adjustments they would otherwise be entitled to.
```
