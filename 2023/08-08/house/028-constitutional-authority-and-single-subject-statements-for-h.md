# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5181

**Measures:** H.R. 5181

```
            By Ms. TOKUDA:
        H.R. 5181.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to modify the 
     thrifty food plan to apply to the urban and rural parts of 
     the State of Hawaii.
```
