# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4660

**Measures:** H.R. 4660

```
           By Ms. STEFANIK:
       H.R. 4660.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the US Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit foreign influence from U.S. institutions of 
     higher education and stop U.S. taxpayer dollars from funding 
     research that benefits the development of our adversaries' 
     military capabilities.
```
