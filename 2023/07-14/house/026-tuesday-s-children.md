# TUESDAY'S CHILDREN

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                           TUESDAY'S CHILDREN

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, yesterday, I had two 
special visitors, Seth and Bronson.
  Seth and Bronson participated in the career shadow day with Tuesday's 
Children.
  Tuesday's Children formed in the aftermath of the terrorist attacks 
on Tuesday, September 11, 2001. They meet individuals and families of 
servicemembers where they are on their grief journey following a loss 
due to military service, mass violence, or acts of terrorism.
  This is the fifth year Seth has joined me and the first year Bronson 
has joined me. Tuesday's Children uses its experience and expertise to 
help our military families work through their own losses.
  Mr. Speaker, I hope Seth and Bronson enjoyed being my shadow. I sure 
enjoyed their visit. I can't wait to see all that they accomplish.

                          ____________________
```
