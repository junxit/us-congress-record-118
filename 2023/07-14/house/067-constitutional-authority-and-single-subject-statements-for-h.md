# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4651

**Measures:** H.R. 4651

```
           By Mr. LOUDERMILK:
       H.R. 4651.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clauses 1, 2, and 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Treasury to issue a report 
     containing information on extraordinary measures available 
     when the debt of the United States Government approaches the 
     statutory limit.
```
