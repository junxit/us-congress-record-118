# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4649

**Measures:** H.R. 4649

```
           By Mr. LOUDERMILK:
       H.R. 4649.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require disclosures from certain federal financial 
     regulators when major rulemakings implement the policies of, 
     or conform with, certain non-governmental organization.
```
