# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4764

**Measures:** H.R. 4764

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
       H.R. 4764.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: [The Congress shall 
     have Power] To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Develops an identity and labeling standard for agriculture 
     product known as ``honey''.
```
