# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4802

**Measures:** H.R. 4802

```
            By Mr. RASKIN:
        H.R. 4802.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 (Necessary and Proper 
     Clause)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit employers from using non-disclosure agreements 
     (NDAs) to silence victims of workplace misconduct.
```
