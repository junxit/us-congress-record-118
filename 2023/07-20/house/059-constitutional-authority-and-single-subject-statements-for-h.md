# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4773

**Measures:** H.R. 4773

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 4773.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill authorizes the Centers for Disease Control and 
     Prevention and state public health departments to fund 
     detection and testing activities, such as wastewater 
     surveillance to detect synthetic opioids, including fentanyl.
```
