# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4801

**Measures:** H.R. 4801

```
            By Mr. PHILLIPS:
        H.R. 4801.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec.  8, cl. 3 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill establishes the Compassionate Capitalist Award to 
     recognize organizations that substantially benefit the well-
     being of their employees, stakeholders, and communities.
```
