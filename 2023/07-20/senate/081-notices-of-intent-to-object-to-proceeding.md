# NOTICES OF INTENT TO OBJECT TO PROCEEDING

**Measures:** S. 1080, S. 1199, S. 1207

```
               NOTICES OF INTENT TO OBJECT TO PROCEEDING

  I, Senator Chuck Grassley, intend to object to proceeding to the 
nomination of Tanya J. Bradsher, of Virginia, to be Deputy Secretary of 
Veterans Affairs, dated July 20, 2023.
  I, Senator Ron Wyden, intend to object to proceeding to S. 1080, a 
bill to amend the Controlled Substances Act to require electronic 
communication service providers and remote computing services to report 
to the Attorney General certain controlled substances violations, dated 
July 20, 2023.
  I, Senator Ron Wyden, intend to object to proceeding to S. 1199, a 
bill to combat the sexual exploitation of children by supporting 
victims and promoting accountability and transparency by the tech 
industry, dated July 20, 2023.
  I, Senator Ron Wyden, intend to object to proceeding to S. 1207, a 
bill to establish a National Commission on Online Child Sexual 
Exploitation Prevention, and for other purposes, dated July 20, 2023.

                          ____________________
```
