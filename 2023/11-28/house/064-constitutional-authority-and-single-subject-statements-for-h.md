# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6489

**Measures:** H.R. 6489

```
           By Mrs. PELTOLA:
       H.R. 6489.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3.
       The single subject of this legislation is;
       To amend the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act to provide 
     that Village Corporations shall not be required to convey 
     land in trust to the State of Alaska for the establishment of 
     Municipal Corporations.
```
