# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6490

**Measures:** H.R. 6490

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 6490.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to limit 
     the penalty for late enrollment under part B of the Medicare 
     Program to 15 percent and twice the period of no enrollment, 
     and to exclude periods of COBRA, retiree, and VA coverage 
     from such late enrollment penalty.
```
