# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

meet during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of

the Senate on Tuesday, December 5, 2023, at 10 a.m., to conduct a 
hearing.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, December 5, 2023, at 10 a.m., to
```
