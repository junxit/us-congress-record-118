# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6591

**Measures:** H.R. 6591

```
           By Mr. ELLZEY:
       H.R. 6591.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution, 
     which states ``[t]he Congress shall have power to lay and 
     collect taxes, duties, imposts and excises, to pay the debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States; but all duties, imposts and excises shall be 
     uniform throughout the United States''.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends section 8(a) of the Small Business Act to require 
     the Administrator of the Small Business Administration to 
     regularly reassess the asset and net worth thresholds for 
     qualifying as an economically disadvantaged individual.
```
