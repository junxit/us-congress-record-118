# HONORING THE CAREER OF MS. JOYCE ALLEGRUCCI

**Speaking:** Davids, Sharice (D000629) [D-KS]

```
              HONORING THE CAREER OF MS. JOYCE ALLEGRUCCI

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SHARICE DAVIDS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 5, 2023

  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
career of Ms. Joyce Allegrucci, a dedicated public servant for the 
state of Kansas. Over a long and lasting career, Ms. Allegrucci served 
as Assistant Secretary of the Kansas Department of Social and 
Rehabilitation Services for Children and Family Policy, where she 
fiercely advocated for policies to make Kansas a safer place where 
children and families can thrive.
  She's also supported other public officials on the state level to 
ensure fair and equitable

[[Page E1167]]

policies are at the fore, having served as chief of staff to former 
Governor Kathleen Sebelius and on Governor Laura Kelly's transition in 
2018.
  Ms. Allegrucci is an example to us all of what it means to commit 
yourself to public service. We in Kansas owe her a tremendous debt of 
gratitude, and it is a privilege to honor her here today. I 
congratulate Ms. Allegrucci on an impressive, successful career, and 
wish her a peaceful retirement.

                          ____________________
```
