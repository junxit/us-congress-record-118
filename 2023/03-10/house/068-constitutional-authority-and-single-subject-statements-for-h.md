# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1550

**Measures:** H.R. 1550

```
           By Mr. LaTURNER:
       H.R. 1550.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to 
     restore and standardize work requirements for able-bodied 
     adults enrolled in the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance 
     Program.
```
