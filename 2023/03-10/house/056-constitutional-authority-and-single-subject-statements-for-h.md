# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1538

**Measures:** H.R. 1538

```
            By Mr. CARSON:
        H.R. 1538.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Small Business Act to provide for contracting 
     preferences and other benefits for emerging business 
     enterprises, and for other purposes.
```
