# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1544

**Measures:** H.R. 1544

```
            By Mr. TONY GONZALES of Texas:
        H.R. 1544.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV Section 3 clause 2: ``The Congress shall have 
     Power to dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory or other Property 
     belonging to the United States;''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To adjust the boundary of Big Bend National Park in the 
     State of Texas.
```
