# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1546

**Measures:** H.R. 1546

```
            By Mr. JACKSON of Texas:
        H.R. 1546.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Prohibit federal funds from being used to implement any 
     obligations of the United States under the World Health 
     Organization (WHO)'s Global Pandemic Treaty.
```
