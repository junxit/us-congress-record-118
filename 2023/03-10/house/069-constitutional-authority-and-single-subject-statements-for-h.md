# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1551

**Measures:** H.R. 1551

```
           By Mr. LaTURNER:
       H.R. 1551.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Social Security Act to implement a 
     minimum work requirement for able-bodied adults enrolled in 
     State Medicaid programs.
```
