# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1541

**Measures:** H.R. 1541

```
            By Mr. CROW:
        H.R. 1541.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``The Congress shall have Power to regulate Commerce with 
     foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with the 
     Indian Tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill allows Small Business Development Centers (SBDCs) 
     to provide assistance to small businesses regarding 
     apprenticeship, pre-apprenticeship, and job training 
     programs.
```
