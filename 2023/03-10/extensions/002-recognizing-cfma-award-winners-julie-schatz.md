# RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--JULIE SCHATZ

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
              RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--JULIE SCHATZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 10, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Julie Schatz of 
Comp Group. Julie is a leader in the complex, intricate industry of 
construction management. She has been identified and awarded Associate 
of the Year by the Construction Financial Management Association 
(CFMA).
  CFMA is a figurehead in the construction industry by virtue of its 
support for financial managers through networking, educational, and 
informational services. CFMA grants more opportunities and resources to 
empower advanced, innovative development of infrastructure in the 
prosperous district of TX-24. The Associate of the Year award goes to 
an individual that substantially impacts the DFW chapter of CFMA. Julie 
has established her commitment, contribution, and influence in the 
industry as she has emerged as a prominent leader. Her unwavering work 
ethic and devotion to advocating for her clients yield noteworthy 
results in the expedition of development projects.
  Congratulations to Julie on earning this high recognition for all her 
impressive work.

                          ____________________
```
