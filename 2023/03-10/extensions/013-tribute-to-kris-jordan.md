# TRIBUTE TO KRIS JORDAN

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO KRIS JORDAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 10, 2023

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to commend to the House the 
memory of Kristopher W. Jordan of Ostrander, who died unexpectedly at 
the age of 46 on February 25. Kris ably served the people of Delaware 
County in the Ohio House of Representatives.
  Kris was a man of strong faith and strong conservative credentials. 
He was a leader of the pro-life movement in the General Assembly and a 
champion of smaller government throughout his time in government 
service, which included 8 years in the Ohio Senate and 6 as Delaware 
County Commissioner.
  His dedication and connection to the people of his district was so 
strong that he would knock on thousands of doors each election season 
even when running unopposed.
  Mr. Speaker, we are grateful that good men like Kris Jordan are 
willing to dedicate themselves to public service. We offer his family 
and especially his 3 children--Macy, David, and Everly--our deepest 
condolences at this difficult time.

                          ____________________
```
