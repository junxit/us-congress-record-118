# RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--TIMOTHY HESS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
              RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--TIMOTHY HESS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 10, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Timothy Hess of 
Azteca-Omega Group. Timothy is a champion in the multidimensional, 
elaborate industry of construction management. He is a distinguished 
awardee for Executive of the Year from the Construction Financial 
Management Association (CFMA).
  CFMA is a figurehead in the construction industry by virtue of its 
support for financial managers through networking, educational, and 
informational services. CFMA grants more opportunities and resources to 
empower advanced, innovative development of infrastructure in the 
prosperous district of TX-24. Timothy has made a significant impact on 
his company, industry, and colleagues in North Texas. He has been 
recognized by CFMA as a leader within the community that empowers

[[Page E212]]

his cohorts and initiates an efficient, responsible workplace 
atmosphere. Timothy's exceptional professionalism has permitted his 
company, community, and industry to flourish.
  Congratulations to Timothy on earning this high recognition for all 
his impressive work.

                          ____________________
```
