# RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--KYRA COKER

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
               RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--KYRA COKER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 10, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kyra Coker of 
National Roofing Partners. Kyra is a pioneer in the intricate, 
multidimensional industry of construction management. She is an 
esteemed awardee for Construction Financial Professional of the Year 
from the Construction Financial Management Association (CFMA).
  CFMA is a figurehead in the construction industry by virtue of its 
support for financial managers through networking, educational, and 
informational services. CFMA grants more opportunities and resources to 
empower advanced, innovative development of infrastructure in the 
prosperous district of TX-24. Kyra goes above and beyond by fostering 
innovative perspectives, judicious contributions, and collaboration 
within her company and community. She has been acknowledged as a 
prominent leader and mentor to her company. Kyra's remarkable 
professionalism and sense of community have allowed her company to 
thrive.
  Congratulations to Kyra on earning this high recognition for all her 
impressive work.

                          ____________________
```
