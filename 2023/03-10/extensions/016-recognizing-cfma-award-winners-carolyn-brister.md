# RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--CAROLYN BRISTER

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
            RECOGNIZING CFMA AWARD WINNERS--CAROLYN BRISTER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 10, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Carolyn Brister 
of Momentum Mechanical. Carolyn is a groundbreaker in the complex, 
multifaceted industry of construction management. She is an honorable 
awardee of the Chairman's Coin and the Bob Moulder Award from the 
Construction Financial Management Association (CFMA).
  CFMA is a figurehead in the construction industry by virtue of its 
support for financial managers through networking, educational, and 
informational services. CFMA grants more opportunities and resources to 
empower advanced, innovative development of infrastructure in the 
prosperous district of TX-24. Carolyn paves the way to progress the 
mission of CFMA off the back of her prudent contributions and momentous 
partaking. The Chairman's Coin is awarded to exceptional professionals 
that exemplify their service and distinction to CFMA. Carolyn has 
earned her marks amongst an elite group of associates.
  Congratulations to Carolyn on earning this high recognition for all 
her impressive work.
```
