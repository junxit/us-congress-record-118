# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6443

**Measures:** H.R. 6443

```
           By Mr. ISSA:
       H.R. 6443.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 , Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Jamul Indian Village Land Transfer Act is a bill to 
     take certain land in the State of California into trust for 
     the benefit of the Jamut Indian Village of California Tribe, 
     and for other purposes.
```
