# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6445

**Measures:** H.R. 6445

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 6445.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the definition of ``audiology services'' 
     in the Medicare statute, which specifies the services that 
     audiologists may provide, to include all services already 
     covered by Medicare that are also within an audiologist's 
     scope of practice
```
