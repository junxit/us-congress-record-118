# HONORING SANDHILLS COMMUNITY COLLEGE COURT AND BEACH VOLLEYBALL TEAMS ON THEIR SUCCESS IN THE CLASSROOM

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
 HONORING SANDHILLS COMMUNITY COLLEGE COURT AND BEACH VOLLEYBALL TEAMS 
                   ON THEIR SUCCESS IN THE CLASSROOM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 15, 2023

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the student 
athletes of Sandhills Community College's (SCC) Court and Beach 
Volleyball Teams on being recognized as Academic All-Americans by the 
National Junior College Athletic Association (NJCAA).
  SCC has a proud history of both academic and athletic excellence and 
is a regional leader on both fronts. I was proud to see so many SCC 
student athletes carry on this great tradition and be named to the 
2022-2023 Academic Teams of the Year.
  In particular, I would like to recognize the SCC Court and Beach 
Volleyball teams and the following members of these teams on their 
achievement of this honor. Student athletes, Emma Brown, Lannah 
Huneycutt, Keke Lawrence, Alyssa Maragelis, Anna Ty Ivey, Jalasia Ray, 
Mackenzie Swett, Rachel Burley, Olivia Barlowe and Britta Martin.
  Their great success in the classroom is proof that each of them truly 
embody what it means to be a student athlete. I think I speak for 
everyone in our community when I say they have made Pinehurst and the 
whole Moore County proud.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in recognizing the SCC Court and 
Beach Volleyball teams for this great achievement and to wish them 
continued success both on the court and in the classroom.

                          ____________________
```
