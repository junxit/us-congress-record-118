# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6843

**Measures:** H.R. 6843

```
            By Mr. SCALISE:
        H.R. 6843.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following: Article IV, Section 3, clause 2 provides 
     Congress with the power to ``dispose of and make all needful 
     rules and Regulations respecting the Territory and other 
     Property belonging to the United States.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill modifies the boundary area for the Atchafalaya 
     National Heritage Area.
```
