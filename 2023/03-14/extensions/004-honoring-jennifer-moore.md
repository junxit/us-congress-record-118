# HONORING JENNIFER MOORE

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                        HONORING JENNIFER MOORE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Jennifer Moore as 
part of Women's History Month.
  Jennifer has dedicated her life to representing people who have been 
injured as a result of medical negligence, harmful drugs and devices, 
and other personal injuries. As the founder of her own law firm, she 
never backs down from a fight and is always willing to give a voice to 
the voiceless. I thank Jennifer for her tireless advocacy in our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
