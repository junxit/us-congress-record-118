# HONORING REBECCA JACKSON

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                        HONORING REBECCA JACKSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Rebecca Jackson as 
part of Women's History Month.
  In 1998, Rebecca Jackson made history as the first woman elected 
Jefferson County judge executive. Her decades-long commitment to 
Louisville as an educator, activist, and public servant is nothing 
short of inspirational. I thank Rebecca for her service to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
