# HONORING DR. KIMBERLY BOLAND

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                      HONORING DR. KIMBERLY BOLAND

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. Kimberly Boland 
for her leadership at the University of Louisville.
  Dr. Boland is the chair of the University of Louisville's Department 
of Pediatrics, and is a leader in the improvement of medical education 
both in the community and across our country. She also serves as the 
chief of staff for North Children's Hospital ensuring our children have 
access to the best care. Her dedication to community advocacy has set a 
generation of medical students up for success. I thank Dr. Boland for 
everything she does for Louisville.

                          ____________________
```
