# HONORING THE MEMORY OF JAMES CRAWFORD

**Speaking:** Molinaro, Marcus J. (M001221) [R-NY]

```
                 HONORING THE MEMORY OF JAMES CRAWFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MARCUS J. MOLINARO

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. MOLINARO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of James 
Crawford who passed away on February 28th of this year. James was a 
devoted father, husband, and man of faith who will be dearly missed by 
all who knew him.
  James will long be remembered for many meaningful contributions he 
made to his community. He was founder and director of both Community 
Faith Partners and the Herald Examiner, a newspaper he published with 
his wife Nancy for 32 years.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues in the House join me in 
honoring the memory of James Crawford. While we mourn his passing, we 
celebrate his life and the legacy he leaves behind. May his life and 
memory always be a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
