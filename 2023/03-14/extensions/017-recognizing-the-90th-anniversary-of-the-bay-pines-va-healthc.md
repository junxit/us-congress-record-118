# RECOGNIZING THE 90TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BAY PINES VA HEALTHCARE SYSTEM

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE 90TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BAY PINES VA HEALTHCARE SYSTEM

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 90th anniversary 
of the Bay Pines VA Healthcare System. With the opening of its first 
domiciliary in 1933, Bay Pines VA became the cornerstone for Veteran 
healthcare throughout Tampa Bay. Now, the Bay Pines VA Healthcare 
System proudly serves more than 110,000 Veterans annually across the 
Southwest region of Florida. Since its inception, its team of dedicated 
professionals have worked tirelessly to foster an experience that is 
built on respect and transparency. The team also strives to remain 
Veteran-focused in all decision-making. As our community celebrates 
this important milestone, I'd like to congratulate Director Paul Russo 
and his entire team for their efforts to ensure all Veterans receive 
the care they've earned and deserve.

                          ____________________
```
