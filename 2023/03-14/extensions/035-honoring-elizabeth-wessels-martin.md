# HONORING ELIZABETH WESSELS-MARTIN

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                   HONORING ELIZABETH WESSELS-MARTIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Elizabeth Wessels-
Martin for her tremendous service to Louisville.
  Her work as the CEO of the Center for Women and Families has helped 
so many community members at some of the most vulnerable times in their 
lives. Louisville's Center for Women and Families serves community 
members who are victims of intimate partner violence and sexual 
assault, offering them safety and comfort. Elizabeth's dedication to 
addressing domestic violence and helping victims recover and rebuild 
their lives is inspiring. I thank her for her service.

                          ____________________
```
