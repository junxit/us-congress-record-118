# HONORING DENISE CLAYTON

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                        HONORING DENISE CLAYTON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Denise Clayton for 
her incredible service to Kentucky's Third District.
  As a Louisville native and graduate of the University of Louisville's 
Brandeis School of Law, Denise was the first Black woman to serve on 
the Kentucky Court of Appeals, which she currently serves on as chief 
justice. Her dedication to break down barriers serves as an inspiration 
to many across Louisville. I thank Denise for her commitment to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
