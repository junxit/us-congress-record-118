# HONORING PHOEBE WOOD

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                          HONORING PHOEBE WOOD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Phoebe Wood as part 
of Women's History Month.
  As one of Louisville's most active angel investors, Phoebe was named 
the 2016 Business First Louisville Woman of Achievement, and was named 
to the Junior Achievement Kentuckiana Business Hall of Fame in 2019. 
She currently chairs the board of trustees for the American Printing 
House for the Blind, demonstrating her continued commitment to some of 
our most vulnerable communities. I thank Phoebe for her service to 
Kentucky's Third District.

                          ____________________
```
