# HONORING KARINA BARILLAS

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                        HONORING KARINA BARILLAS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Karina Barillas for 
her dedication to Louisville's Latin American community.
  A native of Guatemala, Karina came to the United States as a 
Fulbright Scholar at the University of Louisville. She has spent her 
career advocating for women's rights, and as executive director of La 
Casita Center in my district, champions the well-being of our city's 
Latin American community.
  She is a steadfast champion for women and immigrant rights, and I 
thank her for her service to Louisville.

                          ____________________
```
