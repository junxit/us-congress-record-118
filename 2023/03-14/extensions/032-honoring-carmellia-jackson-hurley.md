# HONORING CARMELLIA JACKSON-HURLEY

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                   HONORING CARMELLIA JACKSON-HURLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Carmellia Jackson-
Hurley for her commitment to the Louisville community.
  As executive director of Louisville Girls Leadership, she provides 
young girls in Louisville opportunities to grow their self-development 
and leadership skills through education, advocacy, and action. 
Carmellia also works as a therapist, helping members of our community 
heal.
  Her work to inspire the next generation of women leaders means a 
great deal to me and so many Louisvillians. I thank Carmellia for her 
service.

                          ____________________
```
