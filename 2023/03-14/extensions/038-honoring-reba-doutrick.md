# HONORING REBA DOUTRICK

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                         HONORING REBA DOUTRICK

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Reba Doutrick for 
her lifelong commitment to making Louisville a better place to live.
  Reba has been an activist throughout her life, having worked with the 
Louisville Arena Authority, Harbor House, Louisville Redevelopment 
Authority, Louisville Red Cross, Salvation Army, Boys and Girls Club, 
and Southwest Hospital. I thank Reba for her tireless dedication to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
