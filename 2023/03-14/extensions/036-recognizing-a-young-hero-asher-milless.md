# RECOGNIZING A YOUNG HERO, ASHER MILLESS

**Speaking:** Emmer, Tom (E000294) [R-MN]

```
                RECOGNIZING A YOUNG HERO, ASHER MILLESS

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOM EMMER

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 14, 2023

  Mr. EMMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a very brave young 
man from the Sixth Congressional District. In November, 4-year-old 
Asher Milless saved his mother's life during a medical emergency.
  Rachel Milless, Asher's mother, was at her home in Orrock Township 
when she suddenly collapsed in their kitchen. Asher sprung into action, 
calling his grandmother for help, who was able to contact Asher's 
father.
  After speaking with Asher's grandmother, Asher's father, Tyler, 
called 9-1-1 with help on the way, Asher unlocked the front door so 
paramedics could get inside. Amazingly, Asher was even able to get his 
mother's nebulizer working and comfort her until help arrived.
  Once on the scene, paramedics were able to stabilize Rachel and get 
her to the hospital. Without Asher's quick thinking, Rachel likely 
wouldn't be alive today.
  As a result of his quick thinking and courageous actions, Asher was 
recently awarded the Life Saving Award by the Sherburne County 
Sheriff's Office for his outstanding work and I congratulate him on his 
award.

                          ____________________
```
