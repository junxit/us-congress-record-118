# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1603

**Measures:** H.R. 1603

```
           By Mrs. RODGERS of Washington:
       H.R. 1603.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase American energy production and restore energy 
     leadership by repealing costly programs that would make homes 
     more expensive and take away homeowners' energy choices.
```
