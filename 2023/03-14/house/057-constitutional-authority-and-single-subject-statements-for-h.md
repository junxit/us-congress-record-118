# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1609

**Measures:** H.R. 1609

```
           By Mr. STEIL:
       H.R. 1609.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To revise the effective date for reductions or 
     discontinuances of Department of Veterans Affairs 
     compensation, dependency and indemnity compensation, and 
     pensions.
```
