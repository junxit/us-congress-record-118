# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1604

**Measures:** H.R. 1604

```
           By Mr. ROSENDALE:
       H.R. 1604.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation prohibits labeling meat products ``Product 
     of U.S.A.'' unless the animal was exclusively born, raised, 
     and slaughtered in the United States.
```
