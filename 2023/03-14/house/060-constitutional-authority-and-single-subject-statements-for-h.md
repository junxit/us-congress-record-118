# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1612

**Measures:** H.R. 1612

```
           By Mr. WOMACK:
       H.R. 1612.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 Clause 3:
       The Congress shall have the power to regulate Commerce with 
     foreign Nations, and among States, and with the Indian 
     Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To clarify the definition of the term ``tipped employee''.
```
