# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1578

**Measures:** H.R. 1578

```
           By Mr. GRIJALVA:
       H.R. 1578.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       U.S. Const. art. I, Sec. Sec. 1 and 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation provides grants to States and districts to 
     create comprehensive school-wide action plans, develop 
     rigorous curriculum, provide professional development for 
     school personnel, and implement student supports.
```
