# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1585

**Measures:** H.R. 1585

```
           By Mr. LaMALFA:
       H.R. 1585.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the US Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       require a State receiving funds pursuant to title II of the 
     Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 to implement a 
     State policy to prohibit a school employee from conducting 
     certain social gender transition interventions.
```
