# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1596

**Measures:** H.R. 1596

```
           By Mr. MOULTON:
       H.R. 1596.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Repealing Section 401 of the Economic Growth, Regulatory 
     Relief, and Consumer Protection Act (Public Law 115-174).
```
