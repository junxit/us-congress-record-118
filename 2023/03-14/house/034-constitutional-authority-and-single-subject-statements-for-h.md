# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1586

**Measures:** H.R. 1586

```
           By Mr. LaMALFA:
       H.R. 1586.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the US Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Allows the Secretaries of the Interior and Agriculture, and 
     other public entities, to use a fire retardant, chemical, or 
     water for fire suppression, control, or prevention activities
```
