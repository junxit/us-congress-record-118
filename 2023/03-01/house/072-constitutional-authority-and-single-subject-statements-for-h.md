# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1287

**Measures:** H.R. 1287

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 1287.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 Clause 4 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish mandatory minimum purchase volumes for packers 
     through ``approved pricing mechanisms'', establish a maximum 
     penalty for covered packers of $90,000 for mandatory minimum 
     violations, and to create a publicly available library of 
     marketing contracts.
```
