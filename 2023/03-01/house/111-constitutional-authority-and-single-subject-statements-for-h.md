# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1326

**Measures:** H.R. 1326

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 1326.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To modify the limitation on the deduction by individuals of 
     certain State and local taxes and to provide coverage for 
     hearing and vision care under the Medicare program.
```
