# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1311

**Measures:** H.R. 1311

```
           By Mrs. McCLAIN:
       H.R. 1311.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to direct the 
     Secretary of Education to publish requirements for financial 
     aid offers to be provided by institutions of higher education 
     to enrolled and prospective students.
```
