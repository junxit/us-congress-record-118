# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1288

**Measures:** H.R. 1288

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 1288.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would amend the Chemical and Biological Weapons 
     Control and Warfare Elimination Act of 1991 to impose 
     sanctions against governments of foreign states that engage 
     in an act or acts of gross negligence with respect to state 
     owned, operated, or directed chemical or biological programs.
```
