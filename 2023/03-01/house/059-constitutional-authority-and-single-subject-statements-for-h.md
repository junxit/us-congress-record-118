# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1274

**Measures:** H.R. 1274

```
           By Mr. AMODEI:
       H.R. 1274.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to authorize Appropriations under 
     Article 1, Section 9 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation will reauthorize the Lake Tahoe 
     Restoration Act through 2034.
```
