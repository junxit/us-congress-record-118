# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1299

**Measures:** H.R. 1299

```
            By Mr. KEATING:
        H.R. 1299.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        Legislation to award a Congressional Gold Medal to the 
     group of heroic participants in the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising 
     who led an armed resistance against Nazi occupiers and fought 
     to preserve and protect the Jewish culture.
```
