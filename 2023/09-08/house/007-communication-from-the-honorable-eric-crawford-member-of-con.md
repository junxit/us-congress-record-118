# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE ERIC CRAWFORD, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE ERIC CRAWFORD, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Eric Crawford, Member of Congress:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, August 30, 2023.
     Hon. Kevin McCarthy,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mister Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I, the Honorable Eric A. ``Rick'' 
     Crawford, U.S. Representative for the 1st Congressional 
     district of Arkansas, have been served with a subpoena for 
     documents and testimony issued by the United States District 
     Court for the Southern District of New York.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     partially consistent with the privileges and rights of the 
     House.
           Sincerely,
                                        Eric A. ``Rick'' Crawford,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
