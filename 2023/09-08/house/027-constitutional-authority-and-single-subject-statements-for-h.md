# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5364

**Measures:** H.R. 5364

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 5364.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2, The Congress shall have 
     Power to dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory or other Property 
     belonging to the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The purpose of the bill is to require the U.S. Forest 
     Service to construct barriers and alter trail routes to 
     protect old growth trees in the Joyce Kilmer-Slickrock 
     Wilderness Area.
```
