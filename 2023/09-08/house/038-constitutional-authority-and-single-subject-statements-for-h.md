# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5375

**Measures:** H.R. 5375

```
           By Mr. MEEKS:
       H.R. 5375.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require a strategy for bolstering engagement and 
     cooperation between the United States, Australia, India, and 
     Japan and to seek to establish a Quad Intra-Parliamentary 
     Working Group to facilitate closer cooperation on shared 
     interests and values.
```
