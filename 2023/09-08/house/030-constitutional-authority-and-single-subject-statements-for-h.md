# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5367

**Measures:** H.R. 5367

```
           By Mr. GOLDEN of Maine:
       H.R. 5367.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 ``The Congress shall have 
     Power to . . . provide for the . . . general Welfare of the 
     United States; . . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Small Business Act to require an annual report 
     on entrepreneurial development programs.
```
