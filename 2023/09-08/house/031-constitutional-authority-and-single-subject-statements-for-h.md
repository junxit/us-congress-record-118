# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5368

**Measures:** H.R. 5368

```
           By Ms. GREENE of Georgia:
       H.R. 5368.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit through December 31, 2024, the imposition of a 
     mask mandate on passengers of air carriers or public transit 
     and in educational settings within the United States, and for 
     other purposes.
```
