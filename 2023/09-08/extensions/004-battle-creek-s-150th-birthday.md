# BATTLE CREEK'S 150TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
                     BATTLE CREEK'S 150TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE FLOOD

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 8, 2023

  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, I wish to honor Battle Creek, Nebraska as 
they celebrate their

[[Page E816]]

150th birthday. One hundred fifty years ago in August 1873, Battle 
Creek was founded. Sitting along the Elkorn River Valley in Madison 
County, Battle Creek has grown through the years, welcoming new 
families, businesses, and visitors.
  With a population of a few more than 1,000 people, they exhibit the 
best of what small town Nebraska has to offer: A great quality of life, 
family-owned businesses, and great recreation. They're one of the first 
stops on the Cowboy Trail. And Battle Creek is proud of its community 
members--both new and old. One outstanding example is Rose O'Neill, the 
cartoonist who imagined the Kewpie Doll, which has gained fame around 
the world. As Battle Creek reaches this sesquicentennial birthday, 
there is much to celebrate.
```
