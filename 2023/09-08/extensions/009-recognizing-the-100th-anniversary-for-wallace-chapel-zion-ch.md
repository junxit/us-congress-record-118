# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY FOR WALLACE CHAPEL ZION CHURCH

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
    RECOGNIZING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY FOR WALLACE CHAPEL ZION CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 8, 2023

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise to extend my warmest 
congratulations to the Wallace Chapel AME Zion Church on their 100-year 
anniversary.
  For a century, the Wallace Chapel has been a pillar of strength and a 
source of inspiration within our community. Their unwavering commitment 
to serving others and being a source of hope has left an indelible mark 
on our communities.
  The first congregation was established in 1923 when Adelaide Edney 
longed for a church of her choice in Summit, New Jersey. That summer, 
more than 30 people were regularly attending the church services, 
building the strong community that Wallace Chapel is known for today. 
From worshiping in the hallways to having their own property, Chapel 
Wallace remains a historic, cultural and sacred place to celebrate and 
worship the Lord.
  As we celebrate this milestone, may we recognize the importance of 
this church for our community and the positive impact they have made 
towards other churches in the area.
  Once again, congratulations on reaching this incredible milestone, 
and may God's grace continue to shine upon them.

                          ____________________
```
