# CELEBRATING JOYCE GARDNER BAKER ON HER BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
            CELEBRATING JOYCE GARDNER BAKER ON HER BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 8, 2023

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to an 
individual who embodies the spirit of selfless service, dedication, and 
commitment. Joyce Gardner Baker will be celebrating a milestone 
birthday on September 16, 2023, and today, I wish to honor her 
remarkable journey.
  Joyce, originally from the Midwest, relocated to St. Petersburg in 
1985. There she married her college sweetheart, Rick, and brought with 
her a fervor to serve and make a difference. From her professional 
endeavors at PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) to her influential role as 
St. Petersburg's First Lady, Joyce's contributions have been 
invaluable. Joyce's passion for service is evident in her unwavering 
dedication to both the community and church. Yet, above all, she holds 
her roles as a loving wife and dedicated mother to Julann and Jacob 
closest to her heart.
  Mr. Speaker, it's individuals like Joyce Gardner Baker who are the 
pillars of our communities. I wish to convey our deepest appreciation 
for her years of service, for her unwavering spirit, and for the 
countless lives she has touched.
  May she continue to inspire, serve, and be a beacon of light for many 
more years to come.

                          ____________________
```
