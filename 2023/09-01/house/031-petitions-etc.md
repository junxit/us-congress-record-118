# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** S. 1514, H.R. 3170

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-6. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Pennsylvania 
     State Corrections Officers Association, relative to a letter 
     expressing support of S. 1514 and H.R. 3170, the Homes for 
     Every Local Protector, Educator, and Responder (HELPER) Act; 
     to the Committee on Financial Services.
       PT-7. Also, a petition of City of Miami, Florida, relative 
     to Resolution No. R-23-0309, expressing continued support for 
     Ramon Saul Sanchez's application for permanent resident 
     status (``PRS'') and urging the United States Citizenship and 
     Immigration Services (``USCIS'') to reevaluate and grant 
     Ramon Saul Sanchez's application for PRS; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.
```
