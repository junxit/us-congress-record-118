# Constitutional Authority and Single Subjects Statements for H.R. 5323

**Measures:** H.R. 5323

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 5323.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     which gives Congress the power ``to regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, among states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The purpose of this bill is to withhold federal highway 
     funding from States and Tribes that have legalized use and 
     possession of marijuana for recreational purposes.
```
