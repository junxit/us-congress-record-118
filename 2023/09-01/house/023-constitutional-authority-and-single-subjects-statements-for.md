# Constitutional Authority and Single Subjects Statements for H.R. 5330

**Measures:** H.R. 5330

```
           By Ms. JACOBS:
       H.R. 5330.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish the Department of Defense Military Housing 
     Readiness Council to review and make recommendations to the 
     Department of Defense (DOD) regarding policies for privatized 
     military housing.
```
