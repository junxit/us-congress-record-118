# Constitutional Authority and Single Subjects Statements for H.R. 5320

**Measures:** H.R. 5320

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 5320.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 14: Congress shall have the 
     authority to ``make rules for the government and regulation 
     of the land and naval forces''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       rules related to department of defense management
```
