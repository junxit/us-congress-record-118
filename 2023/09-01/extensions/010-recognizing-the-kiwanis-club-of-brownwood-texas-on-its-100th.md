# RECOGNIZING THE KIWANIS CLUB OF BROWNWOOD, TEXAS ON ITS 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Pfluger, August (P000048) [R-TX]

```
     RECOGNIZING THE KIWANIS CLUB OF BROWNWOOD, TEXAS ON ITS 100TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. AUGUST PFLUGER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 1, 2023

  Mr. PFLUGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Brownwood, 
Texas Kiwanis Club's continued service to children in their community 
for 100 years.
  The Brownwood Charter has helped thousands of children in their 
community and across the globe, dedicating countless resources to 
improving children's health, education, and development. Their servant 
leadership has helped impact children's lives from infancy to early 
adulthood.
  They have assisted needy children through scholarships, blood drives, 
leadership opportunities, and more. For 100 years, their selflessness 
and guidance have been inspiring, and their mission will continue to 
provide children and the Brownwood community the help and opportunities 
they deserve to have a shining future.
  I thank the Brownwood Charter for their selfless service and 
dedication to improving their community for 100 years. Their work is 
emblematic of the values that make our Nation the greatest country in 
the world.

                          ____________________
```
