# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6419

**Measures:** H.R. 6419

```
           By Mr. BEAN of Florida:
       H.R. 6419.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Equal Protection Clause of the Fourteenth Amendment
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Higher Education: Prohibits federal funds from supporting 
     any activity, by or in an institution of higher education, 
     that urges support for, endorses, espouses, encourages, 
     organizes for, or promotes a Foreign Terrorist Organization 
     or its terrorist- activities.
```
