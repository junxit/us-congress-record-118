# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6435

**Measures:** H.R. 6435

```
           By Mrs. RODGERS of Washington:
       H.R. 6435.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Improving coordination between federal, state, and local
```
