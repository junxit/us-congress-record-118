# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6426

**Measures:** H.R. 6426

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
       H.R. 6426.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires federal agencies to report annually 
     regarding federal funds provided for activities conducted by 
     or with certain Chinese individuals and entities.
```
