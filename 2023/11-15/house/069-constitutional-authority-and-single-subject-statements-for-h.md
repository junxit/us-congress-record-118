# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6438

**Measures:** H.R. 6438

```
           By Ms. SHERRILL:
       H.R. 6438.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution of 
     the United States of America.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reauthorize the expired tax incentive that allowed 
     developers to fully deduct the costs of environmental 
     remediation of brownfields in the year the costs were 
     incurred.
```
