# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6432

**Measures:** H.R. 6432

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 6432.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Prohibits the use of funds to implement, administer, or 
     enforce any rule, policy, guidance, recommendation, or 
     memoranda requiring any federal employee or contractor to use 
     another person's preferred pronouns.
```
