# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, we thank You for this Thanksgiving season. We are 
grateful for life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
  Lord, we cling to Your promise in Isaiah 54:17, which tells us no 
weapon turned against us will succeed. Your promises are great, 
precious, and true.
  Today, use our lawmakers to protect the oppressed, to preserve 
freedom, and to speak the truth with compassion.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
