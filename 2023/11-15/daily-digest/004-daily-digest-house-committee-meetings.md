CONGRESSIONAL COMMISSION ON THE STRATEGIC POSTURE OF THE UNITED STATES
Committee on Armed Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Congressional Commission on the Strategic Posture of the United 
States''. Testimony was heard from Madelyn R. Creedon, Chair, 
Congressional Commission on the Strategic Posture of the United States; 
and Jon L. Kyl, Vice Chair, Congressional Commission on the Strategic 
Posture of the United States.

OVERSIGHT OF THE PRUDENTIAL REGULATORS
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Oversight of the Prudential Regulators''. Testimony was heard from 
Michael Barr, Vice Chairman for Supervision, Board of Governors of the 
Federal Reserve System; Martin Gruenberg, Chairman, Federal Deposit 
Insurance Corporation; Todd Harper, Chairman, National Credit Union 
Administration; and Michael Hsu, Acting Comptroller, Office of the 
Comptroller of the Currency, Department of the Treasury.
CRYPTO CRIME IN CONTEXT: BREAKING DOWN THE ILLICIT ACTIVITY IN DIGITAL 
ASSETS
Committee on Financial Services: Subcommittee on Digital Assets, 
Financial Technology and Inclusion held a hearing entitled ``Crypto 
Crime in Context: Breaking Down the Illicit Activity in Digital 
Assets''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
THE FUTURE OF NAGORNO-KARABAKH
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on Europe held a hearing 
entitled ``The Future of Nagorno-Karabakh''. Testimony was heard from 
James O'Brien, Assistant Secretary, Bureau of European and Eurasian 
Affairs, Department of State; and Alexander Sokolowski, Deputy 
Assistant Administrator, Bureau for Europe and Eurasia, U.S. Agency for 
International Development.
WORLDWIDE THREATS TO THE HOMELAND
Committee on Homeland Security: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Worldwide Threats to the Homeland''. Testimony was heard from 
Alejandro Mayorkas, Secretary, Department of Homeland Security; 
Christopher Wray, Director, Federal Bureau of Investigation; and 
Christine Abizaid, Director, National Counterterrorism Center, Office 
of the Director of National Intelligence.

H.R. 6070 were ordered reported, as amended. H.R. 4094 was ordered
