# CELEBRATING BAPS SWAMINARAYAN SANSTHA DIWALI

**Speaking:** Bush, Cori (B001224) [D-MO]

```
              CELEBRATING BAPS SWAMINARAYAN SANSTHA DIWALI

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. CORI BUSH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 15, 2023

  Ms. BUSH. Mr. Speaker, St. Louis and I rise to celebrate the joyous 
occasion of Diwali on November 12, 2023 and share our heartfelt 
appreciation for BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha Diwali. The Festival of 
Lights stands as a beacon of hope, faith, and the triumph of good over 
evil, sharing its enlightening messages of positivity and communal 
unity.
  The significance of Diwali extends far beyond its luminous 
festivities; it represents a time for communities to come together, 
reflect on the values of compassion and righteousness, and spread the 
light of joy and goodwill. The Mandir's steadfast commitment to 
upholding the traditions and values of Diwali serves as an exemplary 
beacon of spiritual enlightenment and community service.
  As we embrace the festivities of this occasion, let us cherish the 
timeless wisdom embedded in Diwali's celebration, and let its radiant 
light guide us toward a path of unity, compassion, and prosperity.
  We are eager to join hands with the BAPS Swaminarayan Mandir in 
welcoming our community to celebrate Diwali, to revel in its rich 
cultural heritage, and to partake in the collective spirit of joy and 
harmony that this festival embodies.
  May the divine blessings of Diwali illuminate our hearts and homes, 
and may the ensuing year be filled with prosperity, peace, and 
spiritual fulfillment for all.

                          ____________________
```
