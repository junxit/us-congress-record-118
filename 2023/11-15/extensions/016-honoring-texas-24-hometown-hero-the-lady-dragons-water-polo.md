# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO THE LADY DRAGONS WATER POLO TEAM

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
    HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO THE LADY DRAGONS WATER POLO TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 15, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Southlake Carroll's Water Polo Team, the Lady Dragons.
  In only their second year as a UIL program, the Southlake Carroll 
Lady Dragons Water Polo team has already achieved back-to-back 
appearances at the UIL 6A state tournament. This year, they took 2nd 
place at the State Championships.
  The Lady Dragons finished their 2023 season as District Champions, 
Regional Champions, and State Runner-Up, achieving a 23-2 overall 
record for the season.
  Congratulations to the Southlake Carroll Lady Dragons Water Polo team 
on an amazing season and all of their accomplishments. I look forward 
to cheering them on next season and wish all graduating seniors the 
best of luck.

                          ____________________
```
