# HONORING TEXAS TECH UNIVERSITY WALL OF HONOR, MILITARY & VETERANS TRIBUTE WALK AT PFLUGER FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Pfluger, August (P000048) [R-TX]

```
   HONORING TEXAS TECH UNIVERSITY WALL OF HONOR, MILITARY & VETERANS 
                   TRIBUTE WALK AT PFLUGER FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. AUGUST PFLUGER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 15, 2023

  Mr. PFLUGER. Mr. Speaker, it is with immense pride and gratitude that 
I join in honoring the November 17, 2023 dedication of the new Texas 
Tech University Wall of Honor,

[[Page E1106]]

Military & Veterans Tribute Walk at Pfluger Fountain. The names of 
Texas Tech alumni who fought in World War II, the Korean War, the 
Vietnam War, the Gulf War, the Iraqi and Afghanistan Wars, and others, 
can now be found at the memorial.
  This powerful tribute encapsulates the unwavering commitment and 
sacrifices of our military service members and veterans. It stands as a 
testament to Texas Tech's commitment to honor and remember the 
generations of Texas Tech military service members and veterans who 
have served to protect and defend our freedom.
  As Texas Tech reaches its centennial milestone, I commend Texas Tech 
leadership, the Texas Tech Alumni Association, and the Texas Tech 
Military & Veterans Alumni Network for this heartfelt recognition of 
the veterans who have played an integral role in the 100-year history 
of the university.
  May this memorial serve as a perpetual reminder of the courage and 
selflessness embodied by our Texas Tech military and veterans.

                          ____________________
```
