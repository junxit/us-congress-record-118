# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4487

**Measures:** H.R. 4487

```
           By Mrs. GONZALEZ-COLON:
       H.R. 4487.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 4, Clause 1 informs the Congress that 
     the primary authority to set election law and to administer 
     federal elections rests with the States and not with the 
     Congress.
       Article I, Section 5, Clause 1
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 17
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish certain requirements with respect to the 
     inclusion of a photograph or digital image of the likeness of 
     an individual who is eligible to vote in a District of 
     Columbia election in the poll book which contains the name of 
     the individuals who are eligible to vote in the District of 
     Columbia election, and for other purposes.
```
