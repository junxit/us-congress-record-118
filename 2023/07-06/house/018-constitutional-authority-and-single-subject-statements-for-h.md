# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4474

**Measures:** H.R. 4474

```
            By Mr. CAREY:
        H.R. 4474.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Help America Vote Act of 2002 to confirm the 
     requirement that States allow access to designated 
     congressional election observers to observe the election 
     administration procedures in elections for Federal office.
```
