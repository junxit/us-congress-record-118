# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4471

**Measures:** H.R. 4471

```
            By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
        H.R. 4471.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Article I Section 
     8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Prohibits an entity of the Federal government from 
     collecting or requiring the submission of information on the 
     identification of any donor to a tax-exempt organization.
```
