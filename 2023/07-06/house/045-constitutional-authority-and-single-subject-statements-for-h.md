# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4501

**Measures:** H.R. 4501

```
           By Ms. TENNEY:
       H.R. 4501.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to prohibit 
     501(c){3) organizations from providing direct funding to 
     official election organizations.
```
