# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4497

**Measures:** H.R. 4497

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 4497.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to expand the mission of NPS to include the promotion of 
     active use of its parks located in urban areas, such as 
     playgrounds, pedestrian- and bicycle-friendly infrastructure, 
     sports and recreation facilities, community events, 
     programming and concessions.
```
