# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4467

**Measures:** H.R. 4467

```
            By Mr. BRECHEEN:
        H.R. 4467.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, and Article 4, Section 4.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Department of Homeland Security to submit a 
     report on services performed by contractors along the border 
     with Mexico.
```
