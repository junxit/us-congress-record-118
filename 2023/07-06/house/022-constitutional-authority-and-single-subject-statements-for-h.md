# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4478

**Measures:** H.R. 4478

```
            By Mr. CLOUD:
        H.R. 4478.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The power granted to Congress under Article I, Section 8 of 
     the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reform practices and policies within the State 
     Department as it relates to cases of U.S. citizens being 
     arbitraritly detained abroad.
```
