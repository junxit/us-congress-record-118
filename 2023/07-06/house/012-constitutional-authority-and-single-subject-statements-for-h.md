# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4468

**Measures:** H.R. 4468

```
           By Mr. WALBERG:
        H.R. 4468.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Administrator of the Environmental 
     Protection Agency from finalizing, implementing, or enforcing 
     a proposed rule with respect to emissions from vehicles and 
     to preserve consumer choice.
```
