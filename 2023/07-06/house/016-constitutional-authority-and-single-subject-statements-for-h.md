# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4472

**Measures:** H.R. 4472

```
            By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
        H.R. 4472.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Article I Section 
     8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Promotes election integrity, voter confidence, and faith in 
     elections by protecting political speech and reforming 
     campaign finance rules.
```
