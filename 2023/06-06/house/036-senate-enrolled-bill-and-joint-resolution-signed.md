# SENATE ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 11, S. 777

```
            SENATE ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  The Speaker announced his signature to an enrolled bill and Joint 
Resolution of the Senate of the following titles:

       S. 777.--An act to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2023, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans, and for other purposes.
       S.J. Res. 11.--A joint resolution providing for 
     congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United 
     States Code, of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
     Protection Agency relating to ``Control of Air Pollution From 
     New Motor Vehicles: Heavy-Duty Engine and Vehicle 
     Standards''.
```
