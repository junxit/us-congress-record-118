# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3858

**Measures:** H.R. 3858

```
           By Mr. ISSA:
       H.R. 3858.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill prohibits the President from negotiating or 
     concluding any withdrawal, suspension, waiver, or 
     modification to the Agreement on Trade-Related Aspects of 
     Intellectual Property Rights without explicit authorization 
     from Congress.
```
