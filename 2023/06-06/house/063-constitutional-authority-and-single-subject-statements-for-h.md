# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3857

**Measures:** H.R. 3857

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
       H.R. 3857.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article One, Section Eight, Clause Three
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Arms Export Control Act to provide to the 
     United Kingdom an exemption for licensing of defense items 
     for export in the absence of a relevant bilateral agreement.
```
