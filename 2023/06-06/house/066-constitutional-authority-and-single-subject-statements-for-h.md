# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3860

**Measures:** H.R. 3860

```
           By Ms. KUSTER:
       H.R. 3860.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the United States 
     Constitution, the Taxing and Spending Clause: ``The Congress 
     shall have Power To lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts 
     and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for the common 
     Defense and general Welfare of the United States . . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would allow Medicaid to provide insurance 
     coverage for people who are incarcerated.
```
