# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3868

**Measures:** H.R. 3868

```
            By Mr. NEGUSE:
        H.R. 3868.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Attorney General to include a data field in 
     the National Missing and Unidentified Persons System to 
     indicate whether the last known location of a missing person 
     was confirmed or was suspected to have been on Federal land, 
     and for other purposes.
```
