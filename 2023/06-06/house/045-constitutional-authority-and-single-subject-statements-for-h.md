# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3839

**Measures:** H.R. 3839

```
            By Mr. DUNN of Florida:
        H.R. 3839.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power ``to regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill alters the FDA review process for complex generic 
     drug applications.
```
