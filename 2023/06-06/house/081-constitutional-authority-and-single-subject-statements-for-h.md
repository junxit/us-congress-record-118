# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3875

**Measures:** H.R. 3875

```
            By Ms. SHERRILL:
        H.R. 3875.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       ARTICLE I, SECTION 8, CLAUSE 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to expand 
     the scope of practitioners eligible for payment for 
     telehealth services under the Medicare program, and for other 
     purposes.
```
