# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3746

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on June 2, 2023, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker pro tempore (Mr. McHenry) has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3746. An act to provide for a responsible increase to 
     the debt ceiling.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the enrolled bill was signed on June 2, 2023, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, by the Acting President pro tempore (Ms. Duckworth).

                          ____________________
```
