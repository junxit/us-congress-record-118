# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our Father, in a chaotic and violent world, we look to You as 
our mighty fortress. Have mercy upon our Nation and world. Help us all 
to remember how You have led us in the past.
  Guide our Senators so that they clearly understand Your desires, and 
give them the wisdom to obey. Lord, provide our lawmakers with daily 
strength to honor You with their service. Remind them that they will 
answer to You for the way they use their talents to serve others.
  Inspire all of our hearts to honor You with faithfulness and 
humility.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
