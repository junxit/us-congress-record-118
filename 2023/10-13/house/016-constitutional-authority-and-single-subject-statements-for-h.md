# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5958

**Measures:** H.R. 5958

```
           By Ms. SPANBERGER:
       H.R. 5958.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:

[[Page H5014]]

       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The Congress shall have Power . . . To regulate Commerce 
     with foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require pharmaceutical companies to disclose the list 
     price of their drug in direct-to-consumer advertising.
```
