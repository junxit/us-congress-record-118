# COMMEMORATING TEXAS STATE REPRESENTATIVE BELL ON THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF INTEX

**Speaking:** Gooden, Lance (G000589) [R-TX]

```
 COMMEMORATING TEXAS STATE REPRESENTATIVE BELL ON THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY 
                                OF INTEX

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LANCE GOODEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. GOODEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise to commemorate 
the extraordinary service of Texas State Representative Gregory Keith 
Bell from Forney, Texas, and his company, Intex Electrical Contractors, 
founded in 1983 when Mr. Bell was the young age of twenty-one. Rep. 
Bell continues to serve as President and CEO of Intex, working with an 
outstanding leadership team.
  Due to the extraordinary effort of Rep. Bell, Intex is celebrating 
its 40th anniversary serving the Metroplex. The company has received 
numerous awards for its excellent work and has been recognized as one 
of the top one hundred fastest-growing private companies in North 
Texas. The leadership team brings over two hundred years of industry 
experience. Intex is a Certified Minority Business Enterprise and was 
ranked Top Specialty Contractor in 2005. I am proud to represent Rep. 
Bell, his family, and the team at Intex in Washington, and I am proud 
he represents my family and me in Austin.
```
