# GINO MARTIGNETTI

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                            GINO MARTIGNETTI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, this June, I had the honor of 
celebrating Gino Martignetti's achievement of the rank of Eagle Scout. 
Gino joined the Scouts at the age of eleven, beginning as a Tiger Cub 
Scout and quickly rising through the ranks. After transitioning to the 
Boy Scouts, Gino continued to thrive with the Scouts, and he has now 
achieved the prestigious rank of Eagle Scout. He is an involved member 
of the Scouting community having held several leadership positions over 
the years, including Chaplin's Aide, Den Chief, Assistant Patrol 
Leader, and Quartermaster. For his Eagle Scout Service Project, Gino 
generously donated his time and his efforts to build a gaga ball pit 
for Guardian Angels Regional School in Gibbstown, South Jersey. Gino 
will be continuing his education at Rowan College of Gloucester County 
starting this fall where he will be participating in their Physical 
Therapy Assistant Program.
  It is my privilege to recognize his achievements and I wish him the 
best in all his future endeavors. God Bless Gino Manignetti and God 
Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
