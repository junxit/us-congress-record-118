# HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TAMURI RICHARDSON

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
         HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TAMURI RICHARDSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Tamuri Richardson as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year.
  Tamuri is a dedicated and passionate leader and advocate who has 
touched the lives of countless members of the Solano County community. 
Tamuri is President of the Solano Black Chamber of Commerce where she 
works to support, uplift, and build Black businesses. Under her 
leadership, the chamber has expanded its membership, growing from 50 
paid participants to over 700, established the first Fairfield 
Juneteenth festival, established a Business Entrepreneurship Academy 
providing mentorship to over 25 new businesses, and secured multiple 
grants to assist local small businesses. She has also led the way in 
building relationships between the Chamber and elected officials, 
allowing her to advocate effectively for the needs of businesses in her 
community.
  Tamuri is also a business owner herself as President and CEO of 
TLeanne Speaking Pros. She is an international best-selling author, 
motivational speaker, and mental health advocate who is known for her 
strength and resiliency.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Tamuri Richardson for her 
positive contributions to our community.
```
