# IDEAL INSTITUTE GRADUATION

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                       IDEAL INSTITUTE GRADUATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, today I celebrate the 
achievements of the Ideal Institute of Technology Class of 2023. 
Through hard work and dedication, these graduates have achieved an 
incredible milestone, and they should be incredibly proud of their 
accomplishments. By choosing the Ideal Institute of Technology, these 
students allowed themselves to gain real-world experience through the 
hands-on approach that the Institute is known for. The Institute has 
ensured their graduates have every tool they need to be successful in 
this next phase of life. As they take the next step, I know without a 
doubt these graduates will continue to make our South Jersey community 
proud.
  I extend my sincere congratulations to these graduates and wish them 
all the best in their future endeavors. God Bless the graduates of 
Ideal Institute of Technology, and God Bless our United States of 
America.

                          ____________________
```
