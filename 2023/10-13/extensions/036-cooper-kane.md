# COOPER KANE

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                              COOPER KANE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, this April, I had the pleasure 
of attending Cooper Kane's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Cooper entered 
scouting at the age of six and immediately felt at home with his troop. 
He participated in the National Youth Leadership Training through the 
Scouts which ignited his passion for leadership. He soon after became a 
staff member and participated in the National Advanced Youth Leadership 
Experience in New Mexico. For his final step in completing his rank of 
Eagle Scout, Cooper built a gaga pit, which is an outdoor group ball 
game, for the Ventnor Middle School students to enjoy. Cooper will be 
attending the University of Alabama in the fall and will be pursuing a 
degree in Mechanical Engineering.
  Cooper should be proud of his achievements with the Boy Scouts, and 
it was my honor to attend his Eagle Scout Court of Honor. God Bless 
Cooper, and God Bless our United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
