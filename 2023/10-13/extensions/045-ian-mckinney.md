# IAN McKINNEY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                              IAN McKINNEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, last month, I had the honor of 
attending Ian McKinney's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Ian began his 
scouting experience as a Tiger Cub and after many years and 
transformative experiences, has now earned the rank of Eagle Scout. 
Throughout the years, Ian has gained valuable knowledge and useful 
skills, made possible by his participation in the merit badge program 
and his steady involvement with the troop. To achieve the rank of Eagle 
Scout, Ian developed and built a community educational garden to enrich 
his school's STEM program, leaving a lasting impact on fellow and 
future classmates.
  Ian's dedication to the Scouts has been admirable and he should be 
proud of all that he has achieved with them. God Bless Ian McKinney and 
God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
