# HONORING WHITE CANE AWARENESS DAY

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
                   HONORING WHITE CANE AWARENESS DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to recognize White 
Cane Awareness Day.
  White Cane Awareness Day, celebrated annually on October 15th, serves 
as a powerful reminder of the significance and empowerment that the 
white cane symbolizes for individuals with visual impairments. This day 
raises needed awareness of the challenges faced by the blind and 
visually impaired community, while also highlighting their remarkable 
achievements and contributions to our society.
  I urge all of my colleagues to join me in recognizing White Cane 
Awareness Day and honor the achievements of individuals who inspire us 
with their resilience, their strength, and their spirit.

                          ____________________
```
