# HONORING CARLOS ACUNA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
           HONORING CARLOS ACUNA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 2023

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, Carlos Acuna, a Colombian by 
birth, is a seasoned attorney with 15 years of legal experience who has 
made a significant impact on the Hispanic community in Orlando, 
Florida. As the founder and director of Acuna-Araujo

[[Page E972]]

Law, he has been an unwavering source of support for his community.
  A decade ago. Carlos chose Orlando as his home and established Acuna-
Araujo Law, with a clear mission: to assist the Colombian and Hispanic 
communities. His firm has become a beacon of hope, enabling community 
members to address legal matters in Colombia without the need for 
travel. His passionate commitment to defending the rights and well-
being of his clients has earned him their trust.
  Carlos Acuna also serves as the president of the Colombian-American 
Chamber of Commerce in Orlando. Under his leadership, the organization 
has worked tirelessly to strengthen trade and cultural ties between 
Colombia and the United States in the region. He has promoted business 
collaboration and economic growth, benefiting Colombian and American 
entrepreneurs alike.
  In addition to his achievements in the legal and business fields, 
Carlos has dedicated himself to serving the Hispanic community in 
Orlando during his 10-year tenure in the city. He has offered free 
legal advice to those facing challenges, demonstrating his commitment 
to social justice and support for those in need.
  His passion for the community and dedication to justice have made him 
an exemplary community leader. Carlos not only works within the Chamber 
of Commerce but also actively engages in activities that promote 
Colombian and Hispanic culture in Orlando, proudly sharing the richness 
of his heritage.
  In summary, Carlos Acuna is a Colombian attorney with an outstanding 
legal career and a deep commitment to the Hispanic community in 
Orlando, Florida. His leadership in the Colombian-American Chamber of 
Commerce, his passion for justice, and his solidarity with the 
community make him a role model and tireless advocate for the rights 
and well-being of those around him. His legacy will endure as a beacon 
of hope and support in Central Florida.
```
