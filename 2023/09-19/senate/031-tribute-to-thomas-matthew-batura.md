# TRIBUTE TO THOMAS MATTHEW BATURA

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO THOMAS MATTHEW BATURA

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Thomas Matthew Batura, a 
summer 2023 intern with my Orlando office, for the hard work he has 
done for my office and the people of Florida.
  Thomas is currently studying at the University of Central Florida, 
where he is completing a master's degree in public administration. He 
is a dedicated and diligent worker who was devoted to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.

and I look forward to hearing of his successes in the years to 
come.●
```
