# TRIBUTE TO FIONA FERRERO

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO FIONA FERRERO

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Fiona Ferrero, a spring 
and summer 2023 intern with my Orlando office, for the hard work she 
has done for my office and the people of Florida.

and diligent worker who was devoted to getting the most out of her
```
