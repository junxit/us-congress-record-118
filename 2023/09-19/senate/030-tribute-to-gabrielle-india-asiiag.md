# TRIBUTE TO GABRIELLE INDIA ASIIAG

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                   TRIBUTE TO GABRIELLE INDIA ASIIAG

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Gabrielle India Asiiag, 
a spring and summer 2023 intern with my Orlando office, for the hard 
work she has done for my office and the people of Florida.

dedicated and diligent worker who was devoted to getting the most out 
of her internship experience.

office, and I look forward to hearing of her successes in the years to 
come.●
```
