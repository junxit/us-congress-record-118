# TRIBUTE TO MAEGAN SMARKUSKY

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO MAEGAN SMARKUSKY

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Maegan Smarkusky, a 
summer 2023 intern with my Gulf Coast regional office, for the hard 
work she has done for my office and the people of Florida.
  Maegan is a recent graduate of J.W. Mitchell High School and will 
attend Florida State University, where she will major in political 
science. She is a dedicated and diligent worker who was devoted to 
getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Maegan for her work with my office 
during these challenging times, and I look forward to hearing of her 
continued good work in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
