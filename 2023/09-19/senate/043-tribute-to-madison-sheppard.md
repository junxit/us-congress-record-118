# TRIBUTE TO MADISON SHEPPARD

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO MADISON SHEPPARD

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Madison Joelle Sheppard, 
a summer 2023 intern with my Gulf Coast regional office, for the hard 
work she has done for my office and the people of Florida.
  Madison is currently a student at Florida State University, where she 
is majoring in international affairs and political science. She is a 
dedicated and diligent worker who was devoted to getting the most out 
of her internship experience.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Madison for her work with my office 
during these challenging times, and I look forward to hearing of her 
continued good work in the years to come.●
```
