# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5578

**Measures:** H.R. 5578

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 5578.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress shall have Power . . . To regulate Commerce 
     with foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This Part-Time Worker Bill of Rights Act modifies various 
     employment and leave rules with respect to part-time workers 
     to ensure their fair treatment.
```
