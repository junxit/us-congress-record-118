# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5575

**Measures:** H.R. 5575

```
           By Mr. POSEY:
       H.R. 5575.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       A bill to direct the Secretaries of Health and Human 
     Services, Defense, and Veterans Affairs to end American over 
     dependence on Chinese pharmaceuticals by encouraging the 
     growth of a robust domestic medicine supply chain for generic 
     drugs, to empower the Food and Drug Administration to issue 
     boxed warnings in the case of critical contamination.
```
