# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5557

**Measures:** H.R. 5557

```
           By Mr. HIMES:
       H.R. 5557.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would authorize sanctions on individuals and
```
