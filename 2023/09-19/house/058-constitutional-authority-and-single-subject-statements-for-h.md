# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5567

**Measures:** H.R. 5567

```
           By Mr. JOYCE of Ohio:
       H.R. 5567.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit elementary and secondary schools from accepting 
     funds from or entering into contracts with the Government of 
     the People's Republic of China and the Chinese Communist 
     Party.
```
