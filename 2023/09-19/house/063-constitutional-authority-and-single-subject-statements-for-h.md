# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5572

**Measures:** H.R. 5572

```
           By Ms. OCASIO-CORTEZ:
       H.R. 5572.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 , Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the National and Community Service Act of 1990 to 
     establish a Civilian Climate Corps to help communities 
     respond to climate change and transition to a clean economy, 
     and for other purposes.
```
