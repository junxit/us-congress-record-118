# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5565

**Measures:** H.R. 5565

```
           By Mr. FITZGERALD:
       H.R. 5565.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires a report on the Chinese Communist 
     Party's use of the Belt and Road Initiative to undermine 
     U.S.-led international order.
```
