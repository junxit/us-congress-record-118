# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5566

**Measures:** H.R. 5566

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of Georgia:
       H.R. 5566.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 1.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends Title 28, United States Code, to provide 
     for 18-year terms of active service for justices of the 
     Supreme Court, to regularize the nomination of justices, and 
     for other purposes.
```
