# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church, Washington Parish, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, on this day of remembrance, we give You thanks for all 
those who have laid down their lives in the service of our country. We 
also ask that You send down upon our Representatives in Congress Your 
spirit of wisdom, charity, and justice; that with steadfast purpose 
they may faithfully serve in their offices to promote the well-being of 
all people.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
