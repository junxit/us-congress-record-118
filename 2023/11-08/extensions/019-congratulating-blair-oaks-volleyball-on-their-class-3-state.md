# CONGRATULATING BLAIR OAKS VOLLEYBALL ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
      CONGRATULATING BLAIR OAKS VOLLEYBALL ON THEIR CLASS 3 STATE 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 8, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Blair Oaks High School on their

[[Page E1077]]

victory in Missouri's Class 3 Volleyball State Championship.
  The team claimed the Class 3 title for the third straight year by 
beating the Ursuline Bears in a tightly contested match. After falling 
behind early, Seniors Gabby Horn and Autumn Bax led the Lady Falcons to 
an improbable comeback in the final three sets. The team should be 
commended for all their hard work throughout the past year and for 
bringing home another state championship to their school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Blair Oaks volleyball 
team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
