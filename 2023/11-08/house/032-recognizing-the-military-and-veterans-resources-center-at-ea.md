# RECOGNIZING THE MILITARY AND VETERANS RESOURCES CENTER AT EAST CAROLINA UNIVERSITY

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
RECOGNIZING THE MILITARY AND VETERANS RESOURCES CENTER AT EAST CAROLINA 
                               UNIVERSITY

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, as we prepare for Veterans 
Day and as it is Military Family Appreciation Month, I wish to 
highlight work taking place at East Carolina University.
  ECU has unveiled a new Military and Veterans Resource Center, a 
beacon of support for our active military, veterans, and their 
families, those who have served our Nation with unwavering dedication.
  This resource center is more than just bricks and mortar. It embodies 
our commitment to honor and assist those who have sacrificed so much 
for our Nation.
  The resource center offers many amenities, including study and 
wellness rooms, academic workshops, and peer advising.
  The East Carolina University Military and Veterans Resource Center 
will help our servicemembers, veterans, and their families create a 
brighter future in eastern North Carolina.
```
