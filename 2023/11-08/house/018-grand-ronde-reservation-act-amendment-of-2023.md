# GRAND RONDE RESERVATION ACT AMENDMENT OF 2023

**Measures:** H.R. 1722

```
             GRAND RONDE RESERVATION ACT AMENDMENT OF 2023

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 1722) to amend the Grand Ronde Reservation Act, and 
for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from Oregon (Ms. Salinas) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
