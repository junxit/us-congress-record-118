# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6284

**Measures:** H.R. 6284

```
           By Mr. FLOOD:
       H.R. 6284.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To have the Secretary of Health and Human Services 
     establish a working group for the purpose of recommending 
     standardized measurements for loneliness and isolation.
```
