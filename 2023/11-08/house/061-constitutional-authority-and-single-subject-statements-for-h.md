# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6306

**Measures:** H.R. 6306

```
           By Mr. MILLS:
       H.R. 6306.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the State Department Basic Authorities Act of 1956 
     to prohibit the acquisition or lease of a consular or 
     diplomatic post built or owned by an entity beneficially 
     owned by the People's Republic of China, and for other 
     purposes.
```
