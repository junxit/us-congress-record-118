# PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

```
                        PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

  The following petition or memorial was laid before the Senate and was 
referred or ordered to lie on the table as indicated:

       POM-1. A resolution adopted by the Legislature of Rockland 
     County , New York, supporting the 9/11 Responder and Survivor 
     Health Funding Correction Act and urging the United States 
     Congress to fully fund and appropriate the necessary funding 
     for the World Trade Center Health Program to pay for the 
     current and future health care needs of 9/11 responders; to 
     the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions.
```
