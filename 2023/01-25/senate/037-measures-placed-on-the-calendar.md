# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 4, H.R. 22, H.R. 23, H.R. 26

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills and joint resolution were read the second time, 
and placed on the calendar:

       S.J. Res. 4. Joint resolution removing the deadline for the 
     ratification of the Equal Rights Amendment.
       H.R. 22. An act to prohibit the Secretary of Energy from 
     sending petroleum products from the Strategic Petroleum 
     Reserve to China, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 23. An act to rescind certain balances made available 
     to the Internal Revenue Service.
       H.R. 26. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, to 
     prohibit a health care practitioner from failing to exercise 
     the proper degree of care in the case of a child who survives 
     an abortion or attempted abortion.
```
