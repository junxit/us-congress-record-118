# Text of House Amendment 93

**Speaking:** Pallone, Frank (P000034) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 21

H.R. 21

                        Offered By: Mr. Pallone

       Amendment No. 93: Page 3, after line 9, insert the
