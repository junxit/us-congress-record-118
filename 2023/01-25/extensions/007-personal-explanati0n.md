# PERSONAL EXPLANATI0N

**Speaking:** Chu, Judy (C001080) [D-CA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATI0N

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 25, 2023

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, on January 21, 2023, a mass shooting in 
Monterey Park, California, claimed the lives of 11 people in my 
district. I remained in my community to respond to this crisis and 
participate in a vigil for the lives lost during this tragedy. I was 
unable to be in Washington for votes on January 24, 2023.
  Had I been present, I would have voted: YEA on Roll Call No. 32, and 
YEA on Roll Call No. 33.
```
