# ANGEL NOEMI RODRIGUEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 6, 2023

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Angel Noemi 
Rodriguez for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Angel has achieved great things, all while overcoming adversity and 
challenges along the way. Students like Angel, who strive to make the 
most of their education, develop crucial skills and a work ethic that 
will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a testament 
to Angel's hard work, determination, and perseverance at Jefferson Jr./
Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Angel Noemi Rodriguez on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth award.
