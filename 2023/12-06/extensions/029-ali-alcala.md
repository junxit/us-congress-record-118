  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ali Alcala for

Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Ali Alcala on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth award.
