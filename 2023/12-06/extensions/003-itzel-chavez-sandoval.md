  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Itzel Chavez-

to Itzel's hard work, determination, and perseverance at Jefferson Jr./
Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Itzel Chavez-Sandoval on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth award.
