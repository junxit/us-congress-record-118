# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                 December 6, 2023.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House, Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to clause 5(a)(4)(A) of rule X 
     of the Rules of the House of Representatives, I designate the 
     following Members to be available to serve as Members of the 
     Investigative Subcommittee established by the Committee on 
     Ethics during the 118th Congress:
       Hon. Nanette Barragan of California, Hon. Troy Carter of 
     Louisiana, Hon. Ed Case of Hawaii, Hon. Madeleine Dean of 
     Pennsylvania, Hon. Bill Foster of Illinois, Hon. Chrissy 
     Houlahan of Pennsylvania, Hon. Ayanna Pressley of 
     Massachusetts, Hon. Greg Stanton of Arizona.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
