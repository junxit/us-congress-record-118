# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6649

**Measures:** H.R. 6649

```
            By Mr. THANEDAR:
        H.R. 6649.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill mandates the U.S. Customs and Border Protection 
     to streamline and expedite non-motorized border crossings, 
     including bicycles and pedestrians, on the Gordie Howe 
     International Bridge. It also requires a report from the 
     Comptroller General within two years of the bridge's opening, 
     evaluating the progress and suggesting improvements or 
     incentives for such non-motorized traffic
```
