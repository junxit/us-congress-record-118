# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6650

**Measures:** H.R. 6650

```
            By Mr. THANEDAR:
        H.R. 6650.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill allows investors a business-related tax credit 
     for investment in a Community Development Financial 
     Institution (CDFI). The applicable percentage of such credit 
     is 3% for the first 10 years of investment in a CDFI with a 
     1% increase after the initial credit allowance date and for 
     investments without a fixed term or duration.
```
