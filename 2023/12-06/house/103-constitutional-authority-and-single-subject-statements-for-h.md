# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6642

**Measures:** H.R. 6642

```
            By Mr. POSEY:
        H.R. 6642.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill requires the disclosure of any foreign support 
     from a country of concern or an entity of concern provided to 
     a recipient or covered individual after receiving a federal 
     research and development award.
```
