             CHOICE IN AUTOMOBILE RETAIL SALES ACT OF 2023

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1(c) of rule XIX, further 
consideration of the bill H.R. 4468 to prohibit the Administrator of 
the Environmental Protection Agency from finalizing, implementing, or 
enforcing a proposed rule with respect to emissions from vehicles, and 
for other purposes, will now resume.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.


                           Motion to Recommit

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the motion to 
recommit.
  The Clerk read as follows:

       Ms. Stevens of Michigan moves to recommit the bill H.R. 
     4468 to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.

  The material previously referred to by Ms. Stevens is as follows:

     amendment:

     to limited availability of new motor vehicles based on engine 
     type), and make such conforming changes as may be necessary.
