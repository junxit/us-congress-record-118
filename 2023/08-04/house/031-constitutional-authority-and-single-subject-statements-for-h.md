# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5152

**Measures:** H.R. 5152

```
           By Mr. NEGUSE:
        H.R. 5152.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to establish a program 
     to ensure, under certain conditions, that members of the 
     Armed Forces are automatically enrolled in benefits and 
     services under the laws administered by the Secretary of 
     Veterans Affairs for which such members are eligible.
```
