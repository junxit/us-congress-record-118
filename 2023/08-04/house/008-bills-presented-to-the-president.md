# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 423, H.R. 3672, H.R. 4004

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Acting Clerk of the House, reported that on July 
27, 2023, the following bills were presented to the President of the 
United States for approval:

       H.R. 423. To take certain land located in San Diego County, 
     California, into trust for the benefit of the Pala Band of 
     Mission Indians, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3672. To designate the clinic of the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs in Indian River, Michigan, as the ``Pfc. 
     Justin T. Paton Department of Veterans Affairs Clinic''.
       H.R. 4004. To approve and implement the Agreement between 
     the American Institute in Taiwan and the Taipei Economic and 
     Cultural Representative Office in the United States regarding 
     Trade between the United States of American and Taiwan, and 
     for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
