# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5146

**Measures:** H.R. 5146

```
           By Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania:
        H.R. 5146
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Commerce to conduct a study on 
     the gig economy and the impact the gig economy has on the 
     United States overall economy, including the impact that 
     State laws have had on the gig economy.
```
