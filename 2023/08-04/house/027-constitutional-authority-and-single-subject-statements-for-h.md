# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5148

**Measures:** H.R. 5148

```
           By Mr. NEGUSE:
        H.R. 5148.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to establish a 
     centralized database to record and maintain information 
     relating to training performed by members of the Armed 
     Forces.
```
