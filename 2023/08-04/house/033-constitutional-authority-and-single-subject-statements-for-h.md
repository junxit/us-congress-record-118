# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5154

**Measures:** H.R. 5154

```
           By Ms. OCASIO-CORTEZ:
       H.R. 5154.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article I of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to 
     promulgate regulations with respect to regional and 
     interregional transmission planning, and for other purposes.
```
