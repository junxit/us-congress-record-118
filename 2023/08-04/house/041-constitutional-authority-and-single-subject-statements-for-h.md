# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5162

**Measures:** H.R. 5162

```
           By Ms. TOKUDA:
       H.R. 5162.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amending the National Agricultural Research, Extension, and 
     Teaching Policy Act of 1977 to extend education grant 
     programs for Alaska Native serving institutions and Native 
     Hawaiian serving institutions.
```
