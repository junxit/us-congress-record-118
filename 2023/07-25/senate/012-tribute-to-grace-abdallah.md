# TRIBUTE TO GRACE ABDALLAH

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO GRACE ABDALLAH

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Grace Abdallah, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Grace is a graduate of O'Gorman High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of Kansas in Lawrence, KS, 
where she is pursuing a degree in business. She is a hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Grace for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
