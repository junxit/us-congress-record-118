# TRIBUTE TO NOAH ROISUM

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO NOAH ROISUM

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Noah Roisum, an intern 
in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has done for 
me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
weeks.

[[Page S3518]]

  Noah is a graduate of Rapid City Christian High School in Rapid City, 
SD. Currently, he is attending Northwestern College in Orange City, IA, 
where he is pursuing degrees in international business and political 
science. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Noah for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
