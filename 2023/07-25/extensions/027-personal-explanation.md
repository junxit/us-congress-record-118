# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Courtney, Joe (C001069) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 3935

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOE COURTNEY

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 25, 2023

  Mr. COURTNEY. Mr. Speaker, I was absent from the chamber on July 20, 
2023 due to official travel with President Biden to the Philadelphia 
Shipyard. Had I recorded my vote, I would have voted:
  ``Yes'' on Roll Call No. 361, on the question of agreeing to the 
Amendments En Bloc No. 4 offered by Mr. Graves (OH);
  ``No'' on Roll Call No. 362, on the question of agreeing to amendment 
No. 76 offered by Mr. Perry; ``No'' on Roll Call No. 363, on the 
question of agreeing to amendment No. 77 offered by Mr. Perry; and
  ``Yes'' on Roll Call No. 364, on the question of passage of H.R. 
3935, the Securing Growth and Robust Leadership in American Aviation 
Act. This bipartisan bill will improve safety, travel experience, and 
consumer protections for passengers and contribute to our economic 
recovery by addressing workforce shortages in the aviation sector.
```
