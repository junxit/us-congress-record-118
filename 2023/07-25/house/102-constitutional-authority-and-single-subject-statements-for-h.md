# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4872

**Measures:** H.R. 4872

```
            By Mr. JOHNSON of Louisiana:
        H.R. 4872.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize Customs and Border Protection officers to 
     conduct activities on federal wilderness land in order to 
     secure our borders.
```
