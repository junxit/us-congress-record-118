# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4887

**Measures:** H.R. 4887

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 4887.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power . . . To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Makes violations of certain terms of service and policies 
     by social media platforms and online marketplaces unfair or 
     deceptive acts or practices subject to enforcement by the 
     Federal Trade Commission and not protected by Section 230.
```
