# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4846

**Measures:** H.R. 4846

```
           By Mr. ARRINGTON:
       H.R. 4846.

     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the US Constitution

       Requires Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) to report
```
