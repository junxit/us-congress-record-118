# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4865

**Measures:** H.R. 4865

```
            By Mr. GALLAGHER:
        H.R. 4865.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Foreign Commerce Clause; Article I, Section 8, Clause 
     3.
       The Necessary and Proper Clause; Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill requires Treasury to submit a report on a wide 
     range of PRC influence at the IDB and requires the use of the 
     US voice, vote, and influence at the IDB to counter PRC 
     influence.
```
