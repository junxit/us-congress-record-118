# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4871

**Measures:** H.R. 4871

```
            By Ms. JACOBS:
        H.R. 4871.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:

[[Page H3952]]

       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To modify and expand the annual report required under the 
     Elie Wiesel Genocide and Atrocities Prevention Act of 2018 
     and require additional review of United States assistance 
     provided to certain countries identified as being at risk of 
     atrocities by such report.
```
