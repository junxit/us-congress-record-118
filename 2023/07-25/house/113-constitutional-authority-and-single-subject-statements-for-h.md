# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4883

**Measures:** H.R. 4883

```
            By Mr. MURPHY:
        H.R. 4883.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to require 
     the disclosure of certain ownership information relating to 
     health care provider and pharmacy ownership, and for other 
     purposes.
```
