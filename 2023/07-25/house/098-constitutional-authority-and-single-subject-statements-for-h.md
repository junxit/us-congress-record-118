# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4868

**Measures:** H.R. 4868

```
            By Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas:
        H.R. 4868.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To revise requirements related to the terms and lending 
     limits of certain credit union loans.
       Specifically, the bill (1) allows the National Credit Union 
     Administration Board to lengthen the maturity term of certain 
     loans made by a credit union, and (2) raises the credit limit 
     for credit union member businesses under which such loans are 
     excluded from overall credit union lending limits.
```
