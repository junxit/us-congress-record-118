# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been presented 
under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 45, Patrice H. Kunesh, of 
     Minnesota, to be Commissioner of the Administration for 
     Native Americans, Department of Health and Human Services.
         Charles E. Schumer, Bernard Sanders, Alex Padilla, 
           Richard J. Durbin, Tim Kaine, Margaret Wood Hassan, Ben 
           Ray Lujan, Raphael G. Warnock, Tammy Duckworth, Jack 
           Reed, John W. Hickenlooper, Catherine Cortez Masto, 
           Tammy Baldwin, Brian Schatz, Christopher Murphy, Tina 
           Smith, Debbie Stabenow.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory 
quorum calls for the cloture motions filed today, March 2, be waived.

                          ____________________
```
