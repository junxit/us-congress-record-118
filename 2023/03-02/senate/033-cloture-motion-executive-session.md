# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been presented 
under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 31, Robert Stewart Ballou, of 
     Virginia, to be United States District Judge for the Western 
     District of Virginia.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Catherine Cortez 
           Masto, Sheldon Whitehouse, Sherrod Brown, Margaret Wood 
           Hassan, Raphael G. Warnock, Gary C. Peters, Jack Reed, 
           Christopher A. Coons, Brian Schatz, Tina Smith, Ben Ray 
           Lujan, Elizabeth Warren, Martin Heinrich, Christopher 
           Murphy, Tammy Baldwin, Alex Padilla.

                          ____________________
```
