# IN MEMORY OF OLAVON FARMER-GRIFFIN

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                   IN MEMORY OF OLAVON FARMER-GRIFFIN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 20, 2023

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize Olavon Farmer-
Griffin, a long-time resident of Detroit in Michigan's 12th District 
Strong, as we mourn her recent passing.
  Born in 1939 in Oklahoma City, Olavon Farmer-Griffin knew from an 
early age that she wanted to pursue a career in music. She got her 
start singing gospel music in Oklahoma City with an all-girls gospel 
group called The Holy Juniorettes. As a gifted singer known as Lady 
Olavon, she traveled from coast to coast and everywhere between, 
performing in clubs and venues such as the renowned Apollo Theatre. She 
worked with artists such as Ray Charles, Sam Cooke, Etta James, and the 
Whispers. Lady Olavon's example inspired others to fulfill their 
artistic dreams. Above all, Olavon loved her family dearly. She was 
proud of her four sons James, Ardie, Clyde, and Ra. We thank Lady 
Olavon for her musical legacy and the impact she has had on our 
cultural history and district.
  Please join me in recognizing Ms. Olavon Farmer-Griffin's numerous 
contributions to our community as we honor her memory.
```
