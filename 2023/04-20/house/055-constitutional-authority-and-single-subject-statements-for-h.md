# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2743

**Measures:** H.R. 2743

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 2743.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Reserve Act to prohibit certain 
     financial service providers who deny fair access to financial 
     services from using taxpayer funded discount window lending 
     programs, and for other purposes.
```
