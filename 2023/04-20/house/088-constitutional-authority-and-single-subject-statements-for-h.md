# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2776

**Measures:** H.R. 2776

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 2776.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To eliminate the Treasury Department's Advisory Committee 
     on Racial Equity and prevent Treasury from re-establishing 
     any substantially similar committee moving forward.
```
