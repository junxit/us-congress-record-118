# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2773

**Measures:** H.R. 2773

```
           By Mr. McHENRY:
       H.R. 2773.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3:
       To regulate commerce with states, other nations, and Native 
     American tribes.
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18:
       Authority to create laws that are necessary and proper to 
     carry out the laws of the land
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the definition of an accredited investor.
```
