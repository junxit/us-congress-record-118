# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2745

**Measures:** H.R. 2745

```
           By Mr. BERGMAN:
       H.R. 2745.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Allows claims in federal or state court against foreign 
     states that conduct or participate in cyberattacks against 
     U.S. nationals.
```
