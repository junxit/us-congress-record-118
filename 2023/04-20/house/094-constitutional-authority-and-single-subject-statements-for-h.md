# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2782

**Measures:** H.R. 2782

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 2782.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To specify the standards governing claims of consciously 
     parallel pricing coordination in civil actions under the 
     Sherman Act, and to clarify the meaning of contract, 
     combination in the form of trust or otherwise, or conspiracy 
     under the Sherman Act.
```
