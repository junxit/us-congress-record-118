# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2775

**Measures:** H.R. 2775

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 2775.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To redirect funding from nuclear weapons programs once the 
     president certifies to Congress that all countries possessing 
     nuclear weapons have begun the verifiable and irreversible 
     elimination of such weapons under the Treaty on the 
     Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons.
```
