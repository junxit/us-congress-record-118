# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2741

**Measures:** H.R. 2741

```
           By Mr. GRAVES of Missouri:
       H.R. 2741.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1, Clause 3, and Clause 18 of 
     the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide the Coast Guard with the necessary resources to 
     fulfill its missions and to ensure national defense.
```
