# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2737

**Measures:** H.R. 2737

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 2737.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the President to suspend the entry of aliens 
     into the United States when the average number of 
     ``encounters'' exceeds a certain number.
```
