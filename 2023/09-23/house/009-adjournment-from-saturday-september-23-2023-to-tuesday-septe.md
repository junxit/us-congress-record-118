# ADJOURNMENT FROM SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2023, TO TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2023

**Speaking:** Massie, Thomas (M001184) [R-KY]

```
 ADJOURNMENT FROM SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2023, TO TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 
                                26, 2023
```
