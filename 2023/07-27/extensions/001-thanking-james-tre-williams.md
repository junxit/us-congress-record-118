# THANKING JAMES (TRE) WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
                     THANKING JAMES (TRE) WILLIAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 27, 2023

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. James (Tre) 
Williams, for his work as an intern in my D.C. office. I would like to 
take this opportunity to thank him for his hard work and recognize the 
meaningful contributions he has made to the office this summer.
  Tre is from St Louis, Missouri, and is a rising sophomore at Loyola 
Marymount University where he is studying business marketing. Tre 
enjoys playing intramural basketball and making music during his free 
time.
  Interns work with staff to serve constituents in North Carolina's 
11th congressional district. Tre was chosen from a competitive pool of 
applicants and exceeded every expectation with his positive attitude 
and impressive work ethic.
  It was a pleasure to work with Tre, and I am so proud to have such an 
intelligent and ambitious intern representing my office. Tre 
exemplified the spirit of public service and represents the best that 
our Nation has to offer. I congratulate Tre on this successful 
completion of his internship, and on behalf of the people of Western 
North Carolina, I thank him.

                          ____________________
```
