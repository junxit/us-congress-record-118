# CELEBRATING THE ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF JAMARI McIVORY

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
           CELEBRATING THE ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF JAMARI McIVORY

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 27, 2023

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
accomplishments of Savannah Christian's own, Jamari McIvory.
  Jamari is a rising senior at Savannah Christian, playing three 
sports: track, football, and basketball.
  In May, he won the 2023 Georgia Highschool Associations Class 3A 
State Title in both the 200 and 400-meter races, running a lightning-
fast 21.65 and 48.1 seconds, respectively.
  After winning these championships, Jamari was named the Coastal 
Empire Boy's Track and Field Athlete of the Year by the Savannah 
Morning News.
  Just last week, he won his second AAU Nationals 400-meter run in Las 
Vegas, besting the competition by over two seconds.
  At the end of the month, Jamari will travel to the 2023 AAU Track and 
Field Junior Olympics at Drake University in Indiana.
  Bring the title home to the Coastal Empire.

                          ____________________
```
