# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4968

**Measures:** H.R. 4968

```
           By Mr. BURGESS:
       H.R. 4968.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to exempt 
     qualifying providers of services from prior authorization 
     requirements under Medicare Advantage plans, and for other 
     purposes.
```
