# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4981

**Measures:** H.R. 4981

```
            By Mrs. CHAVEZ-DeREMER:
        H.R. 4981.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit authority to provide financial assistance for, 
     or approve, tolling on Interstate Route 5 or Interstate Route 
     205 in the State of Oregon under certain tolling programs.
```
