# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5003

**Measures:** H.R. 5003

```
           By Mr. HORSFORD:
       H.R. 5003.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Break the Cycle of Violence Act would provide federal 
     grants to communities for evidence-informed community 
     violence intervention and prevention programs designed to 
     interrupt cycles of violence.
```
