# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5028

**Measures:** H.R. 5028

```
           By Ms. MOORE of Wisconsin:
       H.R. 5028.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Taxing and Spending Clause
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would ensure that federal funds provided to

     inappropriately used to separate children from their parents
```
