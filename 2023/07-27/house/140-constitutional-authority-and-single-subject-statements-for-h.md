# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5011

**Measures:** H.R. 5011

```
           By Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania:
       H.R. 5011.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1 of the U.S. Constitution

       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 and the Small
```
