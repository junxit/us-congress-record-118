# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5063

**Measures:** H.R. 5063

```
            By Ms. VELZQUEZ:
        H.R. 5063.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The Congress shall have power. . . to regulate Commerce 
     with foreign Nations, and

[[Page H4147]]

     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Small Business Act to extend the period during 
     which credit for contracting with Puerto Rico businesses and 
     covered territory businesses are double counted.
```
