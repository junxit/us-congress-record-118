# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4970

**Measures:** H.R. 4970

```
           By Mr. LaMALFA:
       H.R. 4970.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.

       To exclude from gross income payments made to compensate
```
