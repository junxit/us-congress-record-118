# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4959

**Measures:** H.R. 4959

           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.R. 4959.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2 (Property Clause)

       To require to Comptroller General to submit to Congress a
