# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4988

**Measures:** H.R. 4988

```
            By Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina:
        H.R. 4988.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to 
     modernize the methods of authenticating controlled substances 
     in the pharmaceutical distribution supply chain.
```
