# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4961

**Measures:** H.R. 4961

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
       H.R. 4961.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: [The Congress shall 
     have Power] To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for operating hours for ports of entry between 
     the United States of America and Canada.
```
