# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5014

**Measures:** H.R. 5014

```
           By Mr. LANDSMAN:
       H.R. 5014.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Employing Veterans to Feed America Act would ensure 
     that veterans have a pathway to meaningful and rewarding 
     employment following their military service, particularly in 
     positions relating to agriculture, conservation, and 
     nutrition.
```
