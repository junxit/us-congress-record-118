consider the following nominations: all nominations placed on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Foreign Service, Marine Corps,

to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order to 
any of the nominations; that the President be notified of the Senate's 
action.
