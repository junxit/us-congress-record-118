# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1160

**Measures:** H.R. 1160

```
            By Mr. WALBERG:
        H.R. 1160.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase American energy production and restore energy
```
