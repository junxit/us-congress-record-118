# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1151

**Measures:** H.R. 1151

```
            By Mr. MEEKS:
        H.R. 1151.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To hold the People's Republic of China accountable for the 
     violation of United States airspace and sovereignty with its 
     high-altitude surveillance balloon.
```
