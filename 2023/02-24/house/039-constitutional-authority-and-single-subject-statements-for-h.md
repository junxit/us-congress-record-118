# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1177

**Measures:** H.R. 1177

```
            By Mr. DONALDS:
       H.R. 1177.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art 1, Sec 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill prohibits the Department of Labor from restricting 
     the type of investments that self-directed 401(k) account 
     investors can choose to invest in through a brokerage window.
```
