# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1156

**Measures:** H.R. 1156

```
            By Ms. SPANBERGER:
        H.R. 1156.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires the Treasury Department to issue a report on the 
     effects of reforms to China's financial sector on the U.S. 
     and global financial system, describes the U.S. policies to 
     protect U.S. interests during these reforms, analyzes risks 
     to U.S. financial stability and the global economy from 
     China, and provides recommendations for how the U.S. can 
     mitigate these risks.
```
