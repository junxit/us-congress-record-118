# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1170

**Measures:** H.R. 1170

```
            By Mr. ARRINGTON:
       H.R. 1170.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would expand eligibility of HSA-FSA/HRA accounts, 
     to pay for storing healthy blood cells for future treatment.
```
