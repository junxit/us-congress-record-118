# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1813

**Measures:** H.R. 1813

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 1813.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would require the Federal Bureau of Prisons (BOP) 
     to provide information to the District of Columbia government 
     on individuals convicted of felonies under D.C. law in BOP 
     custody.
```
