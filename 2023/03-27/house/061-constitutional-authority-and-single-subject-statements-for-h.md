# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1809

**Measures:** H.R. 1809

```
           By Mr. KEATING:
       H.R. 1809.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant

       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Legislation to require the development of strategies and
```
