# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1816

**Measures:** H.R. 1816

```
           By Mr. WALBERG:
       H.R. 1816.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to ensure campus 
     access at pubic institutions of higher education for 
     religious groups.
```
