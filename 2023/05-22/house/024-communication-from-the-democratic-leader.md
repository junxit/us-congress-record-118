# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                     May 18, 2023.
     Hon. Kevin McCarthy,
     Speaker of the House,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to section 9803(d)(l)(C) and (E) 
     of the James M. Inhofe National Defense Authorization Act for 
     Fiscal Year 2023 (Pub. L. 117-263), I am pleased to appoint 
     the following Member to the Commission on Reform and 
     Modernization of the Department of State:
       The Honorable Mike Quigley of Illinois.
       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.
```
