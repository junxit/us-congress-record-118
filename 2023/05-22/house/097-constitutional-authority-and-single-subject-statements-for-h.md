# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3591

**Measures:** H.R. 3591

```
           By Mr. ROUZER:
       H.R. 3591.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To Amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to permanently 
     bar aliens who are ordered removed after failing to appear at 
     a removal proceeding, absent exceptional circumstances, from 
     becoming permanent residents of the United States
```
