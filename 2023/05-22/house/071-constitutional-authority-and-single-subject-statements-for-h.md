# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3565

**Measures:** H.R. 3565

```
           By Mrs. RODGERS of Washington:
       H.R. 3565.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the Federal Communications Commission to grant 
     a license or construction permit through a system of 
     competitive bidding and use proceeds from future systems 
     bidding to fund several programs related to communications 
     and technology.
```
