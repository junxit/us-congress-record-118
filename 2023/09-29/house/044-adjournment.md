# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Houchin, Erin (H001093) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.Res. 742

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mrs. HOUCHIN. Madam Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 7 o'clock and 55 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order and pursuant to House Resolution 742, 
the House adjourned until tomorrow, Saturday, September 30, 2023, at 10 
a.m. as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable 
Dianne Goldman Berman Feinstein.
  Thereupon, (at 7 o'clock and 55 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until tomorrow, Saturday, September 30, 
2023, at 10 a.m. as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late 
Honorable Dianne Goldman Berman Feinstein.
```
