# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5853

**Measures:** H.R. 5853

```
            By Mr. OGLES:
        H.R. 5853.

[[Page H4912]]

        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide that Members of Congress may not receive pay 
     after October 1 of any fiscal year in which Congress has not 
     approved a concurrent resolution on the budget and passed the 
     regular appropriations bills.
```
