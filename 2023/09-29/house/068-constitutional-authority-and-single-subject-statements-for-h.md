# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5847

**Measures:** H.R. 5847

```
            By Mr. LaTURNER:
        H.R. 5847.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution, specifically clause 18 (relating to the 
     power to make all laws necessary and proper for carrying out 
     the powers vested in Congress).
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a grant program at the U.S. Department of 
     State to support the capacity of disability rights 
     organizations and families of children with disabilities 
     abroad to advocate for necessary reforms.
```
