# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5835

**Measures:** H.R. 5835

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 5835.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``Regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Increases the Appalachian Development Highway System from 
     3,090 miles to 3,105 miles for improvements to and the 
     expansion of Corridor K in North Carolina.
```
