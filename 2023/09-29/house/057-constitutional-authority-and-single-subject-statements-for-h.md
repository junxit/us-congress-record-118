# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5836

**Measures:** H.R. 5836

```
           By Mr. GALLAGHER:
       H.R. 5836.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the immediate family members of the President, 
     Vice President, and cabinet officials from working for
```
