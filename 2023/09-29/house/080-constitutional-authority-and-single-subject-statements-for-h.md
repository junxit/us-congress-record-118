# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5859

**Measures:** H.R. 5859

```
            By Ms. WILSON of Florida:
        H.R. 5859.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Clause 18 of 
     Section 8 of Article I of the United States Constitution and 
     Amendment XVI of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Transportation to establish a 
     program to provide grants to protection and advocacy systems 
     to advocate for individuals with disabilities to access 
     transportation, and for other purposes.
```
