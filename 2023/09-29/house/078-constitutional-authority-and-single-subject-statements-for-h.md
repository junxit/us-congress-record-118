# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5857

**Measures:** H.R. 5857

```
            By Mr. VAN DREW:
        H.R. 5857.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Sec. XIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide that no Federal funds may be used for the U.S. 
     Immigration and Customs Enforcement Secure Docket Card 
     Program, and for other purposes.
```
