# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5841

**Measures:** H.R. 5841

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
       H.R. 5841.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 9, Clause 7 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Should there be a lapse in appropriations, the U.S. Customs 
     and Border Protection and U.S. Immigration and Customs 
     Enforcement will still have available funds in order for its 
     uniformed and civilian personnel to carry out their 
     respective duties.
```
