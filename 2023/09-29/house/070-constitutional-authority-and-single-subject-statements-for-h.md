# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5849

**Measures:** H.R. 5849

```
            By Mr. LUETKEMEYER:
        H.R. 5849.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 and Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 3.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow refunds
```
