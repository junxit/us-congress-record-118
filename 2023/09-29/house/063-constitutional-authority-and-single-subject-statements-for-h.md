# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5842

**Measures:** H.R. 5842

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
       H.R. 5842.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 9, Clause 7 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Should there be a lapse in appropriations, the US Coast 
     Guard will still have available funds in order for its 
     uniformed and civilian personnel to carry out their 
     respective duties.
```
