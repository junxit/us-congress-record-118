# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5854

**Measures:** H.R. 5854

```
            By Ms. PORTER:
        H.R. 5854.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to require 
     complete and accurate data set submissions from Medicare 
     Advantage organizations offering Medicare Advantage plans 
     under part C of the Medicare program to improve transparency, 
     and for other purposes.
```
