# TRIBUTE TO ERIN CAMPBELL

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ERIN CAMPBELL

● Mr. OSSOFF. Mr. President, I rise to commend a champion of 
Georgia's families. In October 2015, Erin Campbell valiantly founded 
the Athens Area Diaper Bank with a mission to help families in need. 
Since then, her work has helped countless families access some of the 
most basic, yet vital, supplies a newborn and their families need. 
Working with partner nonprofits, agencies, and organizations in their 
six-county service area, Ms. Campbell and the Athens Area Diaper Bank 
work to collect donations and distribute diapers and other supplies to 
those in need.
  In 2022, the Athens Area Diaper Bank celebrated its 1 millionth 
diaper distributed, an incredible feat. Working closely with Ms. 
Campbell and the Athens Area Diaper Bank team, I brought Republicans 
and Democrats together to deliver new funding for the Athens Area 
Diaper Bank and their partner organization, Helping Mamas, enabling 
them to serve more rural communities in the Athens-Clarke County Area.
  As Georgia's U.S. Senator, I commend Erin Campbell for her 
inspirational service to the Athens area and thank her and the entire 
Athens Area Diaper Bank team for their work.●

                          ____________________
```
