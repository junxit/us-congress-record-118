# BRIAN AND KEVIN MURPHY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                         BRIAN AND KEVIN MURPHY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 17, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, today, I would like to honor 
Brian and Kevin Murphy for graduating high school and 8th grade. Brian 
has graduated from Rustin High School with honors and headed to Penn 
State University in the fall where he will study Business. Kevin has 
graduated 8th grade with honors from St. Simon and Jude School. Kevin 
will continue his journey at Rustin High School next year. Both Brian 
and Kevin have recently assisted their baseball teams in winning their 
Championship games. Two amazing young men with incredible futures ahead 
of them. I know their family is very proud of them, especially their 
Aunt Allie. God Bless Brian and Kevin and God Bless the United States 
of America.

                          ____________________
```
