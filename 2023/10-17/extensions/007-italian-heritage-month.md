# ITALIAN HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                         ITALIAN HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 17, 2023

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, as the Congressman from South 
Jersey, I am proud to celebrate Italian Heritage Month. This month is a 
time to honor the rich contributions of Italian Americans to our 
nation's culture, history, and progress. Italian Americans have left an 
indelible mark on our Nation, from the arts and sciences to public 
service and community building. Their dedication and resilience have 
strethened the fabric of America. In New Jersey, we are fortunate to 
have a vibrant Italian American community that has enhanced our state's 
identity and unity. Italian Heritage Month serves as a reminder of the 
enduring spirit of the Italian American community and of the many 
heritages that make up our great country. I extend my heartfelt 
gratitude to the Italian American community in South Jersey and across 
the United States. Their enduring legacy of hard work, creativity, and 
resilience serves as a testament to the limitless opportunities that 
America offers to those who seek a better life. May Italian Heritage 
Month inspire us to appreciate the diverse tapestry of our Nation and 
remind us that, together, we can continue to make America a beacon of 
hope and opportunity for all. God bless America.

                          ____________________
```
