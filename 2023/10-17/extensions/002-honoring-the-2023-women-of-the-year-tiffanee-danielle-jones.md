# HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TIFFANEE DANIELLE JONES

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
      HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: TIFFANEE DANIELLE JONES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 17, 2023

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Tiffanee Danielle Jones as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year.
  Tiffanee is the District Director for California State Assemblymember 
Lori Wilson of the 11th assembly district. She is also the owner and 
operator of Forward Education LLC., an educational consulting and 
management business. Tiffanee began her career with the State of 
Georgia Department of Corrections where she was instrumental in 
developing a cross agency Juvenile Diversion Program with the County 
Probation, high schools, and the District Attorney's Office in 
Gainesville. Upon returning home to Solano County in 2009, Tiffanee 
worked as an outreach consultant for Oakland Unified School District 
where she assisted multiple school sites in the development and 
implementation of programs for identified low performing students. She 
was the founding member of the West Oakland Health and Safety 
Collaborative, a cross agency collaboration focused on educating the 
community in the areas of violence prevention, health awareness, and 
social justice. Tiffanee continues her work serving students and 
families in the City of Vallejo as the Co-Director for the Willie B. 
Adkins Scholars Program, a college preparation program focused on 
increasing African American enrollment in and completion of college. 
Through her compassionate and generous public service, Tiffanee has 
touched the lives of thousands of people within our district and 
beyond.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Tiffanee Jones for her 
positive contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
