# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5970

**Measures:** H.R. 5970

```
           By Mr. NEGUSE:
       H.R. 5970.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Indian Self-Determination and Education 
     Assistance Act to allow the Secretary of Agriculture to enter 
     into self-determination contracts with Indian Tribes and 
     Tribal organizations to carry out supplemental nutrition 
     assistance programs.
```
