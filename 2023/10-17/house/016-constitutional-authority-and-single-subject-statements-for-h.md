# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5966

**Measures:** H.R. 5966

```
           By Mr. BEYER:
       H.R. 5966.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require that all digital asset transactions conducted on 
     trading platforms that occur off the publicly viewable 
     blockchain be reported to trade repository registered with 
     the Commodity Futures Trading Commission.
```
