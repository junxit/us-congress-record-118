# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5967

**Measures:** H.R. 5967

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 5967.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``Regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Social Security Act to increase integrity of the 
     unemployment system by reviewing and strengthening the 
     Benefits Accuracy Measurement (BAM) audit program, and 
     requiring states, as a condition of federal funding, 
     establish an interview requirement and give employers an 
     avenue to voluntarily report UI recipients who violate work 
     search or interview requirements.
```
