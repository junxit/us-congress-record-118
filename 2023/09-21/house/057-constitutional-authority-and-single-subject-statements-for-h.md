# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5641

**Measures:** H.R. 5641

```
           By Mrs. KIGGANS of Virginia:
       H.R. 5641.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Allows the Department of Defense to use advance 
     appropriated funds for the purpose of ensuring that active-
     duty members of the US Armed Services are paid through FY24.
```
