# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5622

**Measures:** H.R. 5622

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 5622.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress shall have power to lay and collect taxes, 
     duties, imposts and excises, to pay the debts and provide for 
     the common defense and general welfare of the United States; 
     but all duties, imposts and excises shall be uniform 
     throughout the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To make grants to the Girl Scouts of the United States of 
     America, the Boy Scouts of America, the National 4-H Council, 
     and the National FFA Organization to establish pilot projects 
     to expand the programs carried out by the organizations in 
     rural areas and small towns, and for other purposes.
```
