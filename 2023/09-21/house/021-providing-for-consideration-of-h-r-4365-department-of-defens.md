fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, and for other purposes;

restrictions on the export and import of natural gas; and providing for 
consideration of the resolution (H. Res. 684) condemning the actions of 
Governor of New Mexico, Michelle Lujan Grisham, for subverting the 
Second Amendment to the Constitution and depriving the citizens of New 
Mexico of their right to bear arms, on which the yeas and nays were 
ordered.
