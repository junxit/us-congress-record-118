# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5621

**Measures:** H.R. 5621

```
           By Mr. ALFORD:
       H.R. 5621.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18, of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reauthorize the Wildfire and Hurricane Indemnity 
     Program-plus (WHIP+) to provide indemnity payments to 
     producers affected by qualifying natural disasters.
```
