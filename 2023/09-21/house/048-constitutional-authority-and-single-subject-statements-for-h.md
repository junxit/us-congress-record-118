# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5632

**Measures:** H.R. 5632

```
           By Mr. FITZGERALD:
       H.R. 5632.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill directs the Administrator of USAID to make 
     training available to non-adversarial governments on 
     assessing, analyzing, and mitigating the risk of adversarial 
     government lending.
```
