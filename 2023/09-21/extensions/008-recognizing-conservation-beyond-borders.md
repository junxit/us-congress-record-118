# RECOGNIZING CONSERVATION BEYOND BORDERS

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
                RECOGNIZING CONSERVATION BEYOND BORDERS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 21, 2023

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the work of 
Conservation Beyond Borders and support their mission to combat illegal 
poaching. Their work helps to also combat other illicit activities 
including the trafficking of humans, arms, and narcotics across the 
globe.
  I am proud that this important organization is led by an outstanding 
Minnesotan, Dr. Nicole Benjamin-Fink. I'm especially grateful for

[[Page E875]]

the approach Conservation Beyond Borders and Dr. Benjamin-Fink take to 
incorporate rural communities that live alongside wildlife into their 
work.
  I commend Conservation Beyond Borders for their tireless work and 
advocacy to ensure wildlife across the world have the opportunity to 
flourish. I look forward to continuing to work with Conservation Beyond 
Borders and Dr. Nicole Benjamin-Fink.

                          ____________________
```
