# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
following nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The senior assistant executive clerk read the nomination of the 
following named officer for appointment as Chief of Staff of the Army 
and appointment in the United States Army to the grade indicated while 
assigned to a position of importance and responsibility under title 10, 
U.S.C., sections 601 and 7033: to be General, Gen. Randy A. George.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Iowa.
```
