# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, JANUARY 17, 2023, AT 1 P.M.

```
           RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, JANUARY 17, 2023, AT 1 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 1 p.m. on Tuesday, January 17, 2023.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1:30 and 30 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, January 17, 2023, at 1 p.m.
```
