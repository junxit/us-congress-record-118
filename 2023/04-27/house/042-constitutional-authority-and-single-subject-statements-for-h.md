# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2927

**Measures:** H.R. 2927

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 2927.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would reduce the holding period for equine assets 
     to be considered long term capital gains to 12 months, 
     putting them on a level playing field with other similar 
     assets.
```
