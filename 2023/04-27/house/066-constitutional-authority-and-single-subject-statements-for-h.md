# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2951

**Measures:** H.R. 2951

```
            By Mr. JAMES:
        H.R. 2951.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to expand 
     prohibitions under the clean vehicle credit on battery 
     components manufactured or assembled by corporations 
     associated with foreign entities of concern, and for other 
     purposes.
```
