# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2926

**Measures:** H.R. 2926

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 2926.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would make the three-year depreciation schedule 
     permanent for racehorses; regardless of their age when put 
     into service.
```
