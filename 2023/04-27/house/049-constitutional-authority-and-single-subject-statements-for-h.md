# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2934

**Measures:** H.R. 2934

```
            By Mr. COLE:
       H.R. 2934.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 4, Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit states from accepting or using funds from 
     private entities to manipulate administration of and to 
     influence the outcome of federal elections.
```
