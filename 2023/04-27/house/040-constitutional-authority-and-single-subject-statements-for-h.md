# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2925

**Measures:** H.R. 2925

```
           By Mr. AMODEI:
       H.R. 2925.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make

[[Page H2086]]

     all rules and regulations respecting property belonging to 
     the United States under Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill provides clarity that the ancillary uses (support 
     facilities, tailings, etc) inherently accompany exploration 
     and extraction activities for other mining-support 
     activities.
```
