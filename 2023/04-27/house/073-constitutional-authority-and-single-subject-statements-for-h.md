# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2958

**Measures:** H.R. 2958

```
            By Mr. LAMBORN:
        H.R. 2958.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Art. 1, Sec. 8 ``To regulate commerce with foreign nations 
     . . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Modifies Section 301(a) of the Iran Threat Reduction and 
     Syria Human Rights Act of 2012 to close loopholes and require 
     regular reviews of sanctions to ensure sanctions are applied 
     to intended, individuals, organizations, and entities.
```
