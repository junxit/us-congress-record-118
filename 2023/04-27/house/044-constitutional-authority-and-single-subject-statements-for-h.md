# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2929

**Measures:** H.R. 2929

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 2929.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint 
     commemorative coins in recognition of the 100th anniversary
```
