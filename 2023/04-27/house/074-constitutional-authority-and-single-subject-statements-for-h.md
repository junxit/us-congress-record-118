# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2959

**Measures:** H.R. 2959

```
            By Mr. LaTURNER:
        H.R. 2959.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 which provides Congress the power to 
     lay and collect taxes, duties, imposts, and excises to pay 
     the debts and provide for the common defence and general 
     welfare of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To expand access to and uses of funds for Health Savings 
     Accounts and Flexible Spending Accounts.
```
