# TRIBUTE TO KODY SMOOT

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO KODY SMOOT

  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the opportunity 
to express my appreciation to Kody for his hard work as an intern in 
the Energy and Natural Resources Committee. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Kody is a native of Idaho. He recently graduated from Boise State 
University, where he obtained a bachelor's degree in health informatics 
and information management. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, 
which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of 
his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last several 
months.
  I want to thank Kody for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
