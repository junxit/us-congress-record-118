# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been presented 
under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 176, Brendan Abell Hurson, of 
     Maryland, to be United States District Judge for the District 
     of Maryland.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Raphael G. 
           Warnock, Mazie K. Hirono, Jeanne Shaheen, Elizabeth 
           Warren, Catherine Cortez Masto, Margaret Wood Hassan, 
           Jack Reed, Mark Kelly, Tammy Duckworth, Chris Van 
           Hollen, Amy Klobuchar, Jeff Merkley, Alex Padilla, John 
           Fetterman, Robert P. Casey, Jr., Sherrod Brown.

                          ____________________
```
