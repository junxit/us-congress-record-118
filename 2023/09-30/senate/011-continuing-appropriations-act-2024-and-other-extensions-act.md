# CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2024 AND OTHER EXTENSIONS ACT--MOTION TO PROCEED

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 5860

```
CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2024 AND OTHER EXTENSIONS ACT--MOTION TO 
                                PROCEED

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask that it be in order to make a 
motion to proceed to H.R. 5860.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I move to proceed to H.R. 5860, which was received from 
the House and is at the desk.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       Motion to proceed to H.R. 5860, a bill making continuing 
     appropriations for fiscal year 2024, and for other purposes.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I know of no further debate on the 
motion.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there further debate?
  If not, the question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
```
