# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

**Measures:** S.Res. 393

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, OCTOBER 3, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order and pursuant to S. 
Res. 393, the Senate stands adjourned until 3 p.m. on Tuesday, October 
3, 2023, and does so as a further mark of respect to the late Duncan 
McLauchlin ``Lauch'' Faircloth, former Senator from the State of North 
Carolina.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:46 p.m., adjourned until Tuesday, October 
3, 2023, at 3 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
