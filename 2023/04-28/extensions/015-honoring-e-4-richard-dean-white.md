# HONORING E-4 RICHARD DEAN WHITE

**Speaking:** Harshbarger, Diana (H001086) [R-TN]

```
                    HONORING E-4 RICHARD DEAN WHITE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DIANA HARSHBARGER

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 28, 2023

  Mrs. HARSHBARGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor my constituent 
E-4 Richard Dean White of Mountain City, Tennessee.
  E-4 Richard White served in the U.S. Air Force from 1971 until 1975 
and was one of 70 chosen to serve in the USAF Security Command. He was 
stationed at many air force bases such as Lackland, TX Goodfellow, 
Iraklion in Crete, Greece, and Andrews Air Force Base. He was present 
on August 9, 1974, when President Nixon resigned and departed from 
Andrews Air Force Base.
  Following his discharge, E-4 White attended college and pursued a 
career in Law Enforcement, continuing his calling to serve.
```
