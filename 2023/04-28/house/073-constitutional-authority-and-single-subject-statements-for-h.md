# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3015

**Measures:** H.R. 3015

```
           By Mr. LaLOTA:
       H.R. 3015.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reduce the numer of members of the Armed Forces 
     stationed in certain foreign countries with which the United 
     States does not have a proper and enforceable Status of 
     Forces Agreement, and for other purposes.
```
