# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3029

**Measures:** H.R. 3029

```
           By Mr. SMUCKER:
       H.R. 3029.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States,
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation amends the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to allow individuals with direct primary care service 
     arrangements to remain eligible individuals for purposes of 
     health savings accounts.
```
