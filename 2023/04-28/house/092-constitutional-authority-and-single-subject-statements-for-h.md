# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3034

**Measures:** H.R. 3034

```
           By Ms. VAN DUYNE:
       H.R. 3034.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Department of State to revoke passports for 
     certain individuals who fail to make child support payments.
```
