# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3011

**Measures:** H.R. 3011

```
           By Mr. KILMER:
       H.R. 3011.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Creation of Mental Health Task Force at the Department of 
     Defense to develop and provide recommendations for a holistic 
     strategy to address mental health issues agency wide.
```
