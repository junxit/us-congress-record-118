# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3005

**Measures:** H.R. 3005

```
           By Mr. GARBARINO:
        H.R. 3005.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to section 8 of Article I of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation will correct the current ambiguity in 
     federal law regaridng the jurisdiction of the Postal Police 
     and will once again allow Postal Police Officers to engage in 
     street duty and patrols to stem the tide of increased 
     criminal activity against postal employees and assets.
```
