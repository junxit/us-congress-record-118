# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3025

**Measures:** H.R. 3025

```
           By Ms. PLASKETT:
       H.R. 3025.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for no net increase in the total acreage of 
     Federal land in the Virgin Islands National Park.
```
