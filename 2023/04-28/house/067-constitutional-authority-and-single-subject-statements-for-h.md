# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3009

**Measures:** H.R. 3009

```
           By Mr. JOYCE of Ohio:
       H.R. 3009.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to carry out a pilot 
     program to pre-program suicide prevention resources into 
     certain smart devices issued to members of the Armed Forces.
```
