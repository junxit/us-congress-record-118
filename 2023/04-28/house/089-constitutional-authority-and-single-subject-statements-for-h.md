# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3031

**Measures:** H.R. 3031

```
           By Ms. STANSBURY:
       H.R. 3031.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To designate as wilderness certain Federal portions of the 
     red rock canyons of the Colorado Plateau and the Great Basin 
     Deserts in the State of Utah for the benefit of present and 
     future generations of people in the United States.
```
