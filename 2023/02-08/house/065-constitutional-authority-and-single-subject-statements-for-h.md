# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 879

**Measures:** H.R. 879

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 879.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to require the Secretary of the Interior to remove the 
     Andrew Jackson statue and marble base in Lafayette Square in 
     the District of Columbia.
```
