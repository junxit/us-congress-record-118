# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 880

**Measures:** H.R. 880

```
           By Ms. SHERRILL:
       H.R. 880.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution of 
     the United States of America.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establish grant funding for the creation of Continuing 
     Medical Education programming specific to cardiovascular 
     disease and health in women.
```
