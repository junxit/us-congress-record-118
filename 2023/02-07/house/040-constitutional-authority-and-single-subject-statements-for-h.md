# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 865

**Measures:** H.R. 865

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 865.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To posthumously award the Congressional Gold Medal to the 
     African Americans who served with Union forces during the 
     Civil War.
```
