# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 863

**Measures:** H.R. 863

```
           By Mr. MILLS:
       H.R. 863.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends title 18, U.S.C., to prohibit a publishing 
     house from knowingly furnishing sexually explicit material to 
     a school or an educational agency, and to prohibit federal 
     funds from being provided to a school that obtains or an 
     educational agency that distributes sexually explicit 
     material.
```
