# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 29

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 29

```
           By Mr. MANN:
       H.J. Res. 29.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Congressional Review Act pertaining to the listing of the 
     Lesser Prairie-Chicken under the Endangered Species Act.
```
