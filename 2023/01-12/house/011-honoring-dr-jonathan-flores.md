# HONORING DR. JONATHAN FLORES

**Speaking:** De La Cruz, Monica (D000594) [R-TX]

```
                      HONORING DR. JONATHAN FLORES

  (Ms. De La CRUZ asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. De La CRUZ. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to stand here and 
celebrate the induction of Chief of Police Dr. Jonathan Flores from 
Alton, Texas, to the National Law Enforcement Officer Hall of Fame.
  Chief Flores has been both progressive and innovative by growing his 
partnerships within the community and surrounding agencies. His hard 
work and perseverance clearly demonstrate his dedication to the 
community. This momentous occasion truly reflects his great impact both 
as a law enforcement officer and chief.
  On behalf of Texas' 15th Congressional District, I have the honor to 
thank Chief Jonathan Flores for his life of service to our community 
and to our country.

                          ____________________
```
