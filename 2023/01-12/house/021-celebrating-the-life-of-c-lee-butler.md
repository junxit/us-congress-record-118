# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF C. LEE BUTLER

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                 CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF C. LEE BUTLER

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
celebrate the life of C. Lee Butler of Savannah, Georgia.
  Lee was born in Chicago in 1926 and graduated from New Trier High 
School in Winnetka, Illinois, in 1944.
  Five days after his 18th birthday, he enlisted in the U.S. Army and 
served on the Italian front in World War II. While in law school at 
Northwestern University, Lee met and married Betty Stanley. Lee and 
Betty have four children, Darsey, Julia, Malcolm, and Margaret.
  In 1970, Lee founded his own business, the Fiduciary Services 
Corporation, now the Fiduciary Group, a successful investment 
management firm. Lee served at Memorial Medical Center and on the board 
of St. Joseph's Hospital of Savannah.
  He was a member of Wesley Monumental United Methodist Church since 
first coming to Savannah in 1957.
  On behalf of myself and the entire First District of Georgia, we are 
sorry for the loss of this great American.
```
