# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3458

**Measures:** H.R. 3458

```
           By Mr. RUTHERFORD:
       H.R. 3458.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution [Page H205]
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Create a streamlined reinstatement process, requiring the 
     Secretary of Defense to reinstate all servicemembers who were 
     separated solely for denying the DoD COVID-19 vaccine mandate 
     and had requested an exemption to the policy.
```
