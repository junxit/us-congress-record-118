# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3542

**Measures:** H.R. 3542

```
           By Ms. TOKUDA:
       H.R. 3542.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amending the National Agricultural Research, Extension, and
```
