# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3546

**Measures:** H.R. 3546

```
           By Mr. WALTZ:
       H.R. 3546.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Attorney General, in coordination with the 
     President's Interagency Task Force to Monitor and Combat 
     Trafficking in Persons, to study the prevalence and instances 
     of human trafficking at adult entertainment clubs in the 
     United States, and for other purposes.
```
