# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3552

**Measures:** H.R. 3552

```
           By Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas:
       H.R. 3552.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To transfer the United States Secret Service from the 
     Department of Homeland Security to the Department of the 
     Treasury
```
