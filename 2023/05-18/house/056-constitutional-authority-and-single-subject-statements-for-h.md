# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3464

**Measures:** H.R. 3464

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 3464.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Affordable Homeownership Access Act aims to achieve 
     regulatory relief so that private capital provided by seller 
     financers can help solve the affordable homeownership crisis.
```
