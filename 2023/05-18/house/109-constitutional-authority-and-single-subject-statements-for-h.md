# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3517

**Measures:** H.R. 3517

```

       H.R. 3517.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Preserves the authority of the Secretary of the military
```
