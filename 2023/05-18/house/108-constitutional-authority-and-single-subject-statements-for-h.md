# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3516

**Measures:** H.R. 3516

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 3516.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Clause 1 of 
     Section 8 of Article I of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Waives the interview requirement for returning H-2A visa 
     workers who are going back to their former employer and who 
     were previously approved for a visa.
```
