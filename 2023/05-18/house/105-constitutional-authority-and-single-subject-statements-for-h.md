# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3513

**Measures:** H.R. 3513

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 3513.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To extend the availability of funds for Emergency 
     Assistance to Non-Public Schools under the Education 
     Stabilization Fund and the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021.
```
