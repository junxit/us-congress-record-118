# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3504

**Measures:** H.R. 3504

```
            By Mrs. KIGGANS of Virginia:
        H.R. 3504.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation only pertains to: requiring, for a five 
     year period, VA to share with HVAC and SVAC a report 
     including information on police staffing vacancies, security 
     weaknesses, and data associated with criminal activities on 
     VA campuses.
```
