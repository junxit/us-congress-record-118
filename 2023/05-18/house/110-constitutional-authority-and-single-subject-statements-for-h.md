# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3518

**Measures:** H.R. 3518

```
           By Mr. McCAUL:
       H.R. 3518.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To conserve global bear populations by prohibiting the 
     importation, exportation, and interstate trade of bear 
     viscera and bear viscera products.
```
