# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3554

**Measures:** H.R. 3554

```
           By Mr. ZINKE:
       H.R. 3554.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to expand access to

     Veterans Affairs to include certain veterans seeking mental
```
