# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 19, H.J.Res. 1017

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-4. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Tennessee, relative to House Joint Resolution 
     No. 19, urging the United States Congress to enact 
     legislation that will make state child abuse registries 
     public records; to the Committee on Education and the 
     Workforce.
       ML-5. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Oklahoma, relative to House Joint Resolution 1017, requesting 
     the Congress of the United States call a convention of the 
     states to propose amendments to the Constitution of the 
     United States; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       ML-6. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Oklahoma, relative to House Joint Resolution 1017, requesting 
     the Congress of the United States call a convention of the 
     states to propose amendments to the Constitution of the 
     United States; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
