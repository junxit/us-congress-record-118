# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3499

**Measures:** H.R. 3499

```
            By Mr. ISSA:
        H.R. 3499.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 5, United States Code, to provide direct 
     hire authority to appoint individuals to Federal wildland 
     firefighting and firefighting support positions in the Forest 
     Service or the Department of the Interior, and for other 
     purposes.
```
