# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3493

**Measures:** H.R. 3493

```
            By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
        H.R. 3493.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article One, Section Eight, Clause Three
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 
     1990 to require an opportunity to correct an alleged 
     violation as a precondition to commencing a civil action with 
     respect to a place of public accommodation or a commercial 
     facility.
```
