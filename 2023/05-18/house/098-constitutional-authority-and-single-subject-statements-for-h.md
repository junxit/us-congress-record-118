# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3506

**Measures:** H.R. 3506

```
            By Mr. KILEY:
        H.R. 3506.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To make technical amendments to update statutory references 
     to certain provisions classified to title 7, title 20, and 
     title 43, United States Code, and to correct related 
     technical errors.
```
