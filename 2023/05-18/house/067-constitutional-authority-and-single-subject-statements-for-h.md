# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3475

**Measures:** H.R. 3475

```
            By Mr. BUCHANAN:
        H.R. 3475.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the US Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation bans the slaughter, transport, possession, 
     purchase, sale, or donation of equines or their parts for 
     human consumption.
```
