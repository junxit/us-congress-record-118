# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3520

**Measures:** H.R. 3520

```
           By Mrs. MILLER-MEEKS:
       H.R. 3520.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S.C.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Veteran Care Improvement Act will strengthen the VA health

     requirements, and better defining access for residential
```
