# Text of Senate Amendment 90

**Speaking:** Hirono, Mazie K. (H001042) [D-HI]

```
  SA 90. Ms. HIRONO proposed an amendment to the resolution S. Res. 
209, recognizing the significance of Asian American, Native Hawaiian, 
and Pacific Islander Heritage Month as an important time to celebrate

Pacific Islanders to the history of the United States; as follows:

       In the eighth whereas clause, in the matter preceding

     American and Pacific Islander community'' and insert
```
