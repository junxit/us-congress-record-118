# TRIBUTE TO ABIGAIL JOHNSON

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ABIGAIL JOHNSON

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Abigail Johnson, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Abigail is a graduate of Roosevelt High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of South Dakota in 
Vermillion, SD, where she is pursuing degrees in political science and 
psychology. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the 
most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Abigail for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
