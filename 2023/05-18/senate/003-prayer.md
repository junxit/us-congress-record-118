# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray. Eternal God, our help and strength, we bow in awe and 
reverence before You. You are the mighty fortress in whom we find 
refuge. You do for us more than we can ask or imagine.
  Lord, strengthen our Senators for today's journey and all the 
changing scenes of their lives. Help them to bear in mind that You will 
empower them to meet every challenge. Give to them the abiding 
awareness that nothing can disturb their peace if they put their trust 
in You. May our Senators live in the sure faith that You can enable 
them to live worthy of Your grace.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
