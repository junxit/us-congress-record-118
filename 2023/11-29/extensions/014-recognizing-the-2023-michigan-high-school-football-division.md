# RECOGNIZING THE 2023 MICHIGAN HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL DIVISION 6 STATE CHAMPIONS KINGSLEY HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE 2023 MICHIGAN HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL DIVISION 6 STATE 
                     CHAMPIONS KINGSLEY HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 29, 2023

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the Kingsley 
High School Varsity Football team for their recent victory in the 
Division 6 State Championship. With a 38-24 victory over Almont High 
School, the Kingsley

[[Page E1137]]

Stags charged on to the school's first ever State Championship.
  Head Coach Time Wooer's unique approach led them to a dominant 12-2 
season and an ultimate State Championship victory. In the team's 
exemplary win, the Stags were led by elusive running back Eli Graves. 
Finishing the game with 210 rushing yards, 4 touchdowns, and 3 two-
point conversions, Graves is the first running back in Michigan 
Football State Championship history to account for 30 points in a 
championship game.
  Coach Wooer aptly summed up the spirit and composition of his team to 
the Detroit Free Press after the game, stating, ``the one 
characteristic when you get a team of this caliber is the character, 
and the morals and the ethics and just the leadership.'' The Kingsley 
High School Varsity Football team did indeed exude immense talent, 
teamwork, and dedication which secured them the State Championship.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate the Kingsley High School 
Varsity Football team for their well-deserved State Championship. I 
wish the team, families, and staff all the best in their future 
endeavors. Go Stags.

                          ____________________
```
