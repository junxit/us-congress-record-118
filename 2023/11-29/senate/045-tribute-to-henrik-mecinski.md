# TRIBUTE TO HENRIK MECINSKI

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO HENRIK MECINSKI

  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the opportunity 
to express my appreciation to Henrik for his hard work as an intern in 
the Senate Republican Conference. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Henrik is a native of New Jersey. He attends George Washington 
University, where he studies international affairs as well as law and 
society. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made him an 
invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected in 
his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Henrik for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
