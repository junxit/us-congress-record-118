# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6503

**Measures:** H.R. 6503

```
            By Mr. GRAVES of Missouri:
        H.R. 6503.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution, 
     clause 1, clause 2, clause 3, and clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 49, United States Code, to extend 
     authorizations for the airport improvement program, to amend 
     the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the funding and 
     expenditure authority of the Airport and Airway Trust Fund, 
     and for other purposes.
```
