# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3636

**Measures:** H.R. 3636

```
            By Mr. GAETZ:
        H.R. 3636.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to require that 
     online credit card contributions to a political organization 
     require a billing address and credit verification value.
```
