# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3632

**Measures:** H.R. 3632

```
            By Mr. CARTER of Texas:
        H.R. 3632.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the housing of illegal immigrants on any 
     military installation within the United States.
```
