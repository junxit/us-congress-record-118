# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3649

**Measures:** H.R. 3649

```
           By Mr. MURPHY:
       H.R. 3649.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U. S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to establish a 
     pilot program to furnish hyperbaric oxygen therapy to a 
     veteran who has a traumatic brain injury or post-traumatic 
     stress disorder.
```
