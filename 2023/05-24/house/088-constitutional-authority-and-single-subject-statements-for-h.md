# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3630

**Measures:** H.R. 3630

```
            By Mr. CAREY:
        H.R. 3630.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit certain entities from barring a student athlete 
     from participating in intercollegiate athletics as a result 
     of such student athlete entering into an endorsement 
     contract, and for other purposes.
```
