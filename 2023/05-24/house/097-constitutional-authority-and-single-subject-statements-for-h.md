# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3639

**Measures:** H.R. 3639

```
           By Mrs. HINSON:
       H.R. 3639.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Child Care and Development Block Grant of 1990 
     to expand eligibility for after hours and nontraditional 
     hours child care programs and establishes a program to award 
     competitive grants for eligibile after hours and 
     nontraditional hours child care programs and providers.
```
