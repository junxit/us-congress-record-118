# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 42, H.J.Res. 44

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Proceedings will resume on questions 
previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Passage of H.J. Res. 44; and
  Passage of H.J. Res. 42, the objections of the President to the 
contrary notwithstanding.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Pursuant to clause 9 of rule XX, the second electronic vote will be 
conducted as a 5-minute vote.

                          ____________________
```
