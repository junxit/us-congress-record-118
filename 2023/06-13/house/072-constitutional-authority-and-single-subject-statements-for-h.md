# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4063

**Measures:** H.R. 4063

```
           By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 4063.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reauthorize a pilot program for public health 
     laboratories to detect fentanyl and other synthetic opioids.
```
