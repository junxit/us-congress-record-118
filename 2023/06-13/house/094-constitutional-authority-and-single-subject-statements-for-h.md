# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4085

**Measures:** H.R. 4085

```
           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.R. 4085.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 (Commerce Clause)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Rail Inspector Safety Act
       This bill authorizes an additional $3,000,000 for the 
     National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB), intended to 
     increase the number of investigators in rail from 14 to 29.
```
