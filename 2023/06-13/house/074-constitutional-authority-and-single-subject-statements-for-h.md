# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4065

**Measures:** H.R. 4065

```
            By Mrs. GONZALEZ-COLON:
        H.R. 4065.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8, Clauses 17 and 18:
       ``To exercise exclusive legislation in all cases 
     whatsoever, over . . . the seat of the government of the 
     United States. . .
       To make all laws which shall be necessary and proper for 
     carrying into execution the foregoing
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To obtain and direct the placement in the Capitol or on the 
     Capitol Grounds of a statue to honor American humanitarian 
     and star athlete Roberto Clemente of Puerto Rico.
```
