# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4053

**Measures:** H.R. 4053

```
           By Ms. KUSTER:
       H.R. 4053.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the United States 
     Constitution, the Taxing and Spending Clause:
       ``The Congress shall have Power To lay and collect Taxes, 
     Duties, Imposts and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for 
     the common Defense and general Welfare of the United States. 
     . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires an evaluation of buprenorphine-naloxone 
     combination products in regards to their scheduling under the 
     Controlled Substances Act.
```
