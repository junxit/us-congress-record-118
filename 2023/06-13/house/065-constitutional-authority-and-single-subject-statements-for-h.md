# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4056

**Measures:** H.R. 4056

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 4056.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill allows states to receive federal Medicaid payment 
     for services provided to foster care children in qualified 
     residential treatment programs.
```
