# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Measures:** S.Res. 100

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, pursuant to S. Res. 
100, the Senate stands adjourned until 10 a.m. on Thursday, March 9, 
and does so as a further mark of respect to the late James Thomas 
Broyhill, former Senator from North Carolina.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 8:08 p.m., adjourned until Thursday, March 
9, 2023, at 10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
