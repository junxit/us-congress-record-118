# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1435

**Measures:** H.R. 1435

```
           By Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania:
       H.R. 1435.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Clean Air Act to prevent the elimination of 
     the sale or use of internal combustion engines.
```
