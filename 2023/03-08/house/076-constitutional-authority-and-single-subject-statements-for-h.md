# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1452

**Measures:** H.R. 1452

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 1452.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1, Section 8, Article I of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Frank LoBiondo Coast Guard Authorization Act 
     of 2018 to direct the Commandant of the Coast Guard to 
     provide certain data related to water quality, and for other 
     purposes.
```
