# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1437

**Measures:** H.R. 1437

```
           By Mr. ROSE:
       H.R. 1437.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize livestock producers and their employees to 
     take black vultures in order to prevent death, injury, or 
     destruction to livestock.
```
