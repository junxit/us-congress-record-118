# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1467

**Measures:** H.R. 1467

```
           By Ms. MENG:
       H.R. 1467.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution [page H10170]
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Social Security Administration to increase an 
     individual's monthly Social Security benefit amount in 
     accordance with the locality-based comparability payment rate 
     applicable to the federal locality-pay area in which the 
     individual resides.
```
