# MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 21, SYRIA WAR POWERS RESOLUTION

**Speaking:** Massie, Thomas (M001184) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 20, H.Con.Res. 21

```
MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF H. CON. RES. 21, SYRIA WAR 
                           POWERS RESOLUTION

  Mr. MASSIE. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order at any time to consider H. Con. Res. 21 in the House if called up 
by the chair of the Committee on Foreign Affairs or his designee; that 
the concurrent resolution be considered as read; that the previous 
question be considered as ordered on the concurrent resolution to 
adoption without intervening motion except for 1 hour of debate equally 
divided among and controlled by Representative McCaul of Texas, 
Representative Meeks of New York, and Representative Gaetz of Florida 
or their respective designees; and that the provisions of section 7 of 
the War Powers Resolution, 50 U.S.C. 1546, shall not apply to H. Con. 
Res. 20.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Kentucky?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
