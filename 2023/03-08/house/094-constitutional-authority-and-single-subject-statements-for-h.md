# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1470

**Measures:** H.R. 1470

```
           By Mr. NORMAN:
       H.R. 1470.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the use of Federal funds for abortion through 
     financial or logistical support to individuals traveling to 
     another State or country to receive an abortion.
```
