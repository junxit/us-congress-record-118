# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1428

**Measures:** H.R. 1428

```
           By Mr. Trone:
       H.R. 1428.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To repeal Section 297B(e)(3)(B) of the Agricultural 
     Marketing Act of 1946 (7 U.S.C. 1639p(e)(3)(B)) and eliminate 
     the 10-year drug felony prohibition for hemp farmers.
```
