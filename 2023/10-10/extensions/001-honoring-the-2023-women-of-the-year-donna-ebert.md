# HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DONNA EBERT

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
            HONORING THE 2023 WOMEN OF THE YEAR: DONNA EBERT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 10, 2023

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker pro tempore, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Donna Ebert as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year.
  As an active Ambassador to the Fairfield-Suisun Chamber of Commerce, 
Donna has led teams of local business leaders to forge connections with 
one another. Donna has also been key player in organizing the chamber's 
``lunch mob'' where member businesses host chamber members and help 
substantially increase the revenue of the businesses. Donna is also 
involved with the Travis Regional Armed Forces committee where she is 
committed to improving the lives of the men and women serving at Travis 
Air Force Base. She supports Air Shows, golf tournaments, and the many 
other events that benefit the base and surrounding community. Donna 
also serves her community as an active member of Solano County 
Soroptimist International helping to support women through education, 
empowerment, and opportunity. Donna's enthusiasm, hard work, and 
dedication to serving her community serve as an inspiration to all her 
peers.
  On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional District, 
it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Donna Ebert for her positive 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
