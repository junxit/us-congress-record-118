# RECOGNIZING ASHLEY HAINES

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING ASHLEY HAINES

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 10, 2023

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker pro tempore, I rise today in recognition of 
Ashley Haines and her service to Virginia's First District and the 
Nation.
  Ashley earned her bachelor's degree from James Madison University, 
where she majored in Public Policy with a minor in Political 
Communication. Prior to joining my office, Ashley interned on the 2021 
Virginia Governor's Primary Race and was a Legislative Aide on the 
Virginia House District 26 campaign.
  Ashley joined my office in 2021 as my Communications Assistant and 
worked her way up to Deputy Press Secretary. Ashley's outstanding work 
ethic and willingness to learn has made her an invaluable member of 
Team Wittman. From coordinating telephone town halls, arranging 
interviews with local sources, and crafting press releases, Ashley has 
contributed tremendously to providing the best constituent service in 
the Commonwealth.
  I thank Ashley for her contributions and dedicated service over the 
past two years. Her positive attitude and openness to new challenges 
will be dearly missed in my office. I wish her the best as she 
continues her journey as Communications Director for Congressman 
Derrick Van Orden.
  Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I ask you to join me in recognizing Ashley 
Haines for her service to Virginia's First District and the Nation. May 
God bless Ashley as she continues her career in public service.

                          ____________________
```
