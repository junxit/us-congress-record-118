# HONORING KRISTEN BENNETT

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
                        HONORING KRISTEN BENNETT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 10, 2023

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker pro tempore, I rise today to honor Ms. 
Kristen Bennett for her invaluable work as a Congressional Intern 
serving in my Hendersonville office. I would like to take this 
opportunity to thank her for her hard work and recognize the meaningful 
contributions she made at the office this year.

[[Page E946]]

  Interns work with staff to serve constituents in North Carolina's 
11th Congressional district. Kristen was chosen from a competitive pool 
of applicants and exceeded every expectation.
  It was a pleasure to work with Kristen, and I am proud to have such 
an intelligent and ambitious intern representing my office. Kristen 
exemplified the spirit of public service and represents the best that 
Western North Carolina has to offer. I congratulate her on the 
successful completion of her internship.

                          ____________________
```
