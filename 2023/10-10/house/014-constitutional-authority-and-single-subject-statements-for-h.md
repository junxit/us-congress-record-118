# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5917

**Measures:** H.R. 5917

```
            By Mr. GALLAGHER:
        H.R. 5917.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Sanctioning the Use of Civilians as 
     Defenseless Shields Act to modify and extend that Act and 
     require reporting by the Department of Defense on countering 
     the use of human shields.
```
