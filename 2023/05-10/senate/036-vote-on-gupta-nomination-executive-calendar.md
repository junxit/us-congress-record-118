# Vote on Gupta Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Moran, Jerry (M000934) [R-KS], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Kansas.
  Mr. MORAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
scheduled vote occur immediately.

nomination?
  Mr. MORAN. I ask for the yeas and nays.

Feinstein) is necessarily absent.
```
