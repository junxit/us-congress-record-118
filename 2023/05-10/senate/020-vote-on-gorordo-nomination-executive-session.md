# Vote on Gorordo Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reed, Jack (R000122) [D-RI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Gorordo Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Gorordo nomination?
  Mr. REED. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Mrs. 
Feinstein) and the Senator from New York (Mrs. Gillibrand) are 
necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Alabama (Mr. Tuberville).
```
