# MEASURES DISCHARGED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 23, S.J.Res. 24

```
                          MEASURES DISCHARGED

  The following joint resolutions were discharged from the Committee on 
Environment and Public Works, by petition, pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 802(c), 
and placed on the calendar:

       S.J. Res. 23. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the National Marine Fisheries 
     Service relating to ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and 
     Plants; Regulations for Listing Endangered and Threatened 
     Species and Designating Critical Habitat''.
       S.J. Res. 24. Joint resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife 
     Service relating to ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and 
     Plants; Endangered Species Status for Northern Long-Eared 
     Bat''.

                          ____________________
```
