# Private Bills and Resolutions for H.R. 3173

**Measures:** H.R. 3173

```
           By Mr. AMODEI:
       H.R. 3173.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of land and naval forces, as enumerated in Article 
     I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill conveys federal lands to several counties and 
     cities in northern Nevada for public purposes and economic 
     development, and designates lands for wilderness and other 
     conservatior purposes.
```
