       H.R. 3201.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, clause 4 of the Constitution provides 
     that Congress shall have power to establish an uniform Ruleof 
     Naturalization.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The subject of this bill is limited to immigration and 
     citizenship.
