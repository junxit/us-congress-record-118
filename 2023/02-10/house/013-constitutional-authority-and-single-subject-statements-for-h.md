# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 975

**Measures:** H.R. 975

```
           By Mr. BUCHANAN:
       H.R. 975.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. 1, Sec. 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To make transitional compensation available to dependents 
     of members of the Armed Forces convicted of dependent abuse 
     in Federal or State court and dependents of members accused 
     of dependent abuse who have forfeited all pay and allowances 
     for an unrelated offense.
```
