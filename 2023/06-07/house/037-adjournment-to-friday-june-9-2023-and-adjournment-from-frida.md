# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JUNE 9, 2023; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 9, 2023, TO MONDAY, JUNE 12, 2023

**Speaking:** Arrington, Jodey C. (A000375) [R-TX]

```
                              {time}  1845
ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JUNE 9, 2023; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 
                   9, 2023, TO MONDAY, JUNE 12, 2023

  Mr. ARRINGTON. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 9 a.m. on Friday, June 9, 
2023; and further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to 
meet on Monday, June 12, 2023, when it shall convene at noon for 
morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
