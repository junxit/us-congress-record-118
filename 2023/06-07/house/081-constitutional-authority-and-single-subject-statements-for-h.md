# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3919

**Measures:** H.R. 3919

```
           By Mr. NEHLS:
       H.R. 3919.

[[Page H2781]]

       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Asylum Claims Improvement Act would prevent judges from 
     taking the mental health of an alien into account when 
     considering these criminal individuals' asylum claims or 
     whether to withhold their removal by amending Section 
     208(b)(2) of the Immigration and Nationality Act to add a 
     ``Mental Health Consideration.''
```
