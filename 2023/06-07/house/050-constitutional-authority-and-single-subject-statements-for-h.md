# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3888

**Measures:** H.R. 3888

```
            By Mr. BAIRD:
        H.R. 3888.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec. 8. Clause 1: The Congress shall have Power To 
     lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and Excises, to pay 
     the Debts and provide for the common Defence and general 
     Welfare of the United States; but all Duties, Imposts and 
     Excises shall be uniform throughout the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To strengthen existing benefits for certain descendants of 
     veterans exposed to herbicide agents.
```
