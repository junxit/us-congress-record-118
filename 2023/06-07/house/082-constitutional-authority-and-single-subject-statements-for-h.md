# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3920

**Measures:** H.R. 3920

```
           By Mr. NEHLS:
       H.R. 3920.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Prioritizing the Removal of Migrants Act would codify a 
     2017 Executive Order signed by President Trump, in order to 
     limit this prosecutorial discretion and ensure the faithful 
     execution of the immigration laws of the United States 
     against all removable aliens.
```
