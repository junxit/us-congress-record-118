# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 698

**Speaking:** McBath, Lucy (M001208) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 698

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 698

  Mrs. McBATH. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 698, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Cicilline of Rhode Island, for 
the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kean of New Jersey).
  Is there objection to the request of the gentlewoman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
