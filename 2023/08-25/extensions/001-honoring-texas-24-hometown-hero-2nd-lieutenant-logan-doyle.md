# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO 2ND LIEUTENANT LOGAN DOYLE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
       HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO 2ND LIEUTENANT LOGAN DOYLE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 25, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 2nd Lieutenant 
Logan J. Doyle. A native of Carrollton, Texas, Logan recently graduated 
from West Point Academy and has been commissioned as a 2nd Lieutenant 
in the U.S. Army. On July 2nd Lieutenant Doyle began his career as an 
aviation officer in Alabama, at Fort Novosel. Prior to his graduation 
from West Point, 2nd Lieutenant Doyle served as an intern in my 
Washington D.C. office and was an excellent member of our team. He was 
a very hard worker, and I enjoyed my time getting to know him. 
Lieutenant Doyle is a recipient of the General William E. Read 
Pentathlete Award which is rewarded to the cadets who achieve a Term 
Quality Point Average of 4.00 or greater and a letter grade of A- or 
better in all academic, physical, military science, and military 
development courses. He was also awarded the Superintendent's Award for 
Achievement for being in the top 6-20 percent of his class across all 
programs and the Sewell Tappan Tyng Memorial Award for having the 
highest rating in nuclear Engineering. I would like to congratulate 
Lieutenant Doyle on his graduation and thank him for his heart of 
service.

                          ____________________
```
