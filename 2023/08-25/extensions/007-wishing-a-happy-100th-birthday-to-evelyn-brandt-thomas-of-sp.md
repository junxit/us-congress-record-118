# WISHING A HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY TO EVELYN BRANDT THOMAS OF SPRINGFIELD, ILLINOIS

**Speaking:** Budzinski, Nikki (B001315) [D-IL]

```
WISHING A HAPPY 100TH BIRTHDAY TO EVELYN BRANDT THOMAS OF SPRINGFIELD, 
                                ILLINOIS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NIKKI BUDZINSKI

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 25, 2023

  Ms. BUDZINSKI. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Evelyn Brandt Thomas of 
Springfield, on her 100th Birthday.
  In 1953, Evelyn, along with her younger brother Glen Brandt, founded 
Brandt Fertilizer, a venture that later evolved into BRANDT 
Consolidated, Inc. However, Evelyn's contributions to Central Illinois 
extend far beyond her business success. Her philanthropic efforts have 
touched numerous institutions of higher learning, such as the 
University of Illinois Springfield and Lincoln Land Community College. 
Evelyn's generosity has also benefited nonprofits, including the 
Education Center for the Visually Impaired.
  Evelyn's dedication to ensuring access to education for women in the 
Springfield area serves as an inspiration to our community. She has 
particularly focused on empowering women in the fields of business and 
agriculture. Her commitment to fostering young talent in these fields 
has helped to strengthen the next generation's passion for agriculture, 
business, and service to Central Illinois.
  Evelyn is the embodiment of selflessness and community service. Her 
tireless work over seven decades has not only transformed the lives of 
those she has worked with but also transformed the lives of countless 
residents of downstate Illinois.
  I know I join countless others offering the very best birthday wishes 
to Evelyn on this special day and I thank her for her contributions to 
Central Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
