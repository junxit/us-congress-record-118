# IN MEMORY OF SSGT. DARREN JAMES BRETON

**Speaking:** Wittman, Robert J. (W000804) [R-VA]

```
                 IN MEMORY OF SSGT. DARREN JAMES BRETON

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 25, 2023

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in remembrance of the life of 
Staff Sgt. Darren James ``DJ'' Breton, who has recently passed away at 
age 32, and to honor his service to the Nation.
  Born in Wiesbaden, Germany, DJ embarked on a remarkable journey that 
took him across the globe and led him to proudly serve his country 
through his role as a crew chief in the United States Air Force.

[[Page E798]]

  His passion for sports, particularly hockey, became the heartbeat of 
his life. DJ generously shared his love for the game by dedicating his 
time to the Youth Hockey League, where he not only nurtured young 
talents but also found immense joy. To both the youths he mentored and 
the family he deeply cherished, DJ brought a lighthearted sense of 
humor and an essence of selflessness, dependability, and respect for 
others.
  DJ will be remembered for his legacy of service, care, and joy. He is 
survived by his loving father Dennis; his devoted mother, Kimberly; and 
his 2 adoring sisters, Cassandra and Hanna. DJ was, and is, well-loved 
and highly respected amongst his family and community, and his memory 
will not soon be forgotten.
  I pray that his loved ones may be comforted as they mourn his loss. 
Mr. Speaker, I ask that you join me in honoring the life and legacy of 
Staff Sgt. Darren James Breton.
```
