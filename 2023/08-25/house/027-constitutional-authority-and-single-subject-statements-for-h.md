# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5283

**Measures:** H.R. 5283

```
            By Ms. MALLIOTAKIS:
        H.R. 5283.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, Clauses 1 and 18
       The single subject of this legislation is;
       To prohibit the use of Federal funds to provide housing to 
     specified aliens on any land under the administrative 
     jurisdiction of the Federal land management agencies
```
