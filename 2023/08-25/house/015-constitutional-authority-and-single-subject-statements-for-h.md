# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5266

**Measures:** H.R. 5266

```
            By Mr. BACON:
        H.R. 5266.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 1 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of the Bureau of Prisons to develop 
     and implement a strategy to interdict fentanyl and other 
     synthetic drugs in the mail at Federal correctional 
     facilities.
```
