# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5265

**Measures:** H.R. 5265

```
            By Mr. ALFORD:
        H.R. 5265.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Small Business Act to require a report on the 
     performance of the Office of Rural Affairs, and to require a 
     report on the memorandum of understanding between the Small 
     Business Administration and the Department of Agriculture 
     entered into on April 4, 2018.
```
