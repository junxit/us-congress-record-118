```
        APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS AND INDIVIDUAL TO UNITED STATES 
                     SEMIQUINCENTENNIAL COMMISSION

Semiquincentennial Commission Act of 2016 (Pub. L. 114-196), and the 
order of the House of January 9, 2023, of the following Members on the

  Mr. Aderholt, Alabama

  Ms. Lorna Auerbach, Pacific Palisades, California, to fill the
```
