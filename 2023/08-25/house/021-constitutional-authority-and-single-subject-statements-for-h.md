# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5273

**Measures:** H.R. 5273

```
            By Mr. GARBARINO:
        H.R. 5273.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill allows a registered investment company to exclude 
     from the calculation of acquired fund fees and expenses those 
     incurred indirectly from investment in a business development 
     company.
```
