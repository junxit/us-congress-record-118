# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5286

**Measures:** H.R. 5286

```
           By Mr. ROY:
       H.R. 5286.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This prohibits the Department of Veterans Affairs from 
     transferring veterans' information to the Federal Bureau of 
     Investigation's National Instant Criminal Background Check 
     System (NICS) solely because they have received a disability 
     rating for a mental illness.
```
