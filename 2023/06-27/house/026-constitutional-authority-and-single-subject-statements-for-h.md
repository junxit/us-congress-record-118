# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4376

**Measures:** H.R. 4376

```
            By Mr. GARBARINO:
        H.R. 4376.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the department in which the 
     Coast Guard is operating to study and report to the Congress 
     regarding recreational vessel operator training.
```
