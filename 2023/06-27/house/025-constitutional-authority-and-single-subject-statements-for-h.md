# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4375

**Measures:** H.R. 4375

```
            By Mr. FROST:
        H.R. 4375.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 and 18 of the U.S. 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act 
     to provide coverage of medically necessary home resiliency 
     services under Medicare.
```
