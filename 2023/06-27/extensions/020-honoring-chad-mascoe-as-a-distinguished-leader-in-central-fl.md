# HONORING CHAD MASCOE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING CHAD MASCOE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                        CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 27, 2023

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Chad Mascoe has been a pillar in the community 
for over 20 years. He is a native of Kissimmee, Florida who always 
strives to pour into the community. Chad was born and raised in 
Kissimmee and attended Osceola High School. He was a member of the 1998 
Osceola High School state champion football team.
  He continued his education at Southwest Mississippi Community College 
then transferred to the University of Central Florida and played 
football at both schools.
  Chad is the CEO of Athletes United Group which is an organization 
that strives to focus on creating opportunities for all student-
athletes to develop skills through peer interaction and competition. 
Chad has taken young men from Kissimmee on college tours, 7-on-7 
tournaments, and to several community service events throughout the 
years. Each year he hosts a Feed the Homeless tournament in which he 
collects and donates goods to various homeless shelters in Central 
Florida.
  Chad Mascoe is very passionate about what he does and always makes 
sure that no kids are left out. His gentle disposition has a way of 
rubbing off on those around him.
```
