# CONGRATULATING SALLY CHEN'S SERVICE TO THE NEW DEMOCRAT COALITION

**Speaking:** Kuster, Ann M. (K000382) [D-NH]

```
   CONGRATULATING SALLY CHEN'S SERVICE TO THE NEW DEMOCRAT COALITION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ANN M. KUSTER

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 27, 2023

  Ms. KUSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sally Chen, the 
New Democrat Coalition Senior Member Services Advisor. Sally is leaving 
the Coalition to work for the White House Office of Legislative 
Affairs.
  During her 2 years with the Coalition, Sally played an integral role 
in growing our Member Services portfolio, amplifying our communications 
work, and ensuring that staffers from our

[[Page E623]]

98 Member offices are connected to the resources and professional 
development opportunities they need to succeed in their roles.
  In particular, Sally helped Member offices bolster their mail and 
constituent outreach programs and provided support to legislative staff 
in Coalition Member offices, helping them move their legislative 
priorities through committee and the House.
  Having had the pleasure of working closely with Sally during my last 
five months as Chair of the New Democrat Coalition, I have seen 
firsthand how Sally's dedication, work ethic, and `can do' attitude has 
elevated our Coalition to new heights.
  Although her departure is bittersweet, I extend my sincere best 
wishes for her continued success at the White House.

                          ____________________
```
