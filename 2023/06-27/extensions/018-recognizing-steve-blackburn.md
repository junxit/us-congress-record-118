# RECOGNIZING STEVE BLACKBURN

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING STEVE BLACKBURN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 27, 2023

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the retirement of 
Steve Blackburn from Glasgow, Kentucky after 43 years of service with 
the T. Marzetti Company.
  Steve began his career as a temporary employee in the processing 
department in their first manufacturing plant on Indianola Avenue in 
Columbus, Ohio. He has proven to be an important team member at the T. 
Marzetti Company, being instrumental in the greenfield startup in Horse 
Cave, Kentucky in 2006 and Sister Schubert's in 2007. Nearly a half 
century later, Steve will retire as the Senior Director of Operations.
  A loving father of 4 and grandfather of 6, Steve plans on enjoying 
retirement with his wife of nearly 14 years, Angela. Congratulations to 
Steve on his hard-earned retirement.

                          ____________________
```
