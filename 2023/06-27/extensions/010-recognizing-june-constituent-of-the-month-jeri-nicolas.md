# RECOGNIZING JUNE CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH JERI NICOLAS

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
         RECOGNIZING JUNE CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH JERI NICOLAS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 27, 2023

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize Jeri 
Nicolas as my June Constituent of the Month. As an employee of the 
North County LGBTQ Resource Center in Oceanside, Jeri embodies the 
Center's mission by advocating for resources and promoting educational 
programs to assist members of the LGBTQ+ community and create a more 
equitable and safer region.
  Jeri has more than two decades of experience doing this work. Jeri 
also uses her background as a native Hawaiian to promote the 
preservation of Hawaiian culture and conduct outreach to LGBTQ+ 
individuals in the Asian American and Pacific Islander (AAPI) 
community. I have no doubt she will continue to make a difference in my 
district and across our greater region.
  During Pride Month, I am proud to celebrate the exceptional efforts 
of individuals like Jeri Nicolas who spend their time helping others 
and contribute to our unique and beautiful district. I'm so grateful 
for CA-19 residents who dedicate their lives to the betterment of our 
residents and community, and I'm proud to honor Jeri Nicolas as my June 
Constituent of the Month.

                          ____________________
```
