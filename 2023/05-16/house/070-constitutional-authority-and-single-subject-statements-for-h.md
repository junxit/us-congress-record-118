# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3372

**Measures:** H.R. 3372

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota:
       H.R. 3372.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution.

       This bill establishes a safety data collection pilot
```
