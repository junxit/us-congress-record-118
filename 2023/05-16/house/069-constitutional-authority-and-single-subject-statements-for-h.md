# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3371

**Measures:** H.R. 3371

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota:
       H.R. 3371.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to direct the Secretary of the Interior to complete all 
     actions necessary for certain land to be held in restricted 
     fee status by the Oglala Sioux Tribe and Cheyenne River Sioux 
     Tribe.
```
