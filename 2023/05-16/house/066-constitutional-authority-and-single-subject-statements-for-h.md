# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3368

**Measures:** H.R. 3368

```
           By Mr. GOLDEN of Maine:
       H.R. 3368.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section

[[Page H2378]]

     8, Clause 18 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to publish a 
     rule to implement the requirement that the Secretary be 
     permitted to waive the limitation in law on reimbursement of 
     veterans receiving domiciliary care in State homes.
```
