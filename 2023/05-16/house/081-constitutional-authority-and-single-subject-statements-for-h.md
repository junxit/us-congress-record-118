# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3383

**Measures:** H.R. 3383

```
            By Mr. PHILLIPS:
        H.R. 3383.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec. 8, cl. 3 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 
     to establish an employee equity investment facility within 
     the Small Business Administration's Small Business Investment 
     Company program.
```
