# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3361

**Measures:** H.R. 3361

```
           By Mr. CARDENAS:
       H.R. 3361.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Assistant Secretary of Commerce for 
     Communications and Information to submit a report on the 
     digital divide and to update the Indicators of Broadband Need 
     mapping application.
```
