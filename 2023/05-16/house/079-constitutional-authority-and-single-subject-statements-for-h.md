# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3381

**Measures:** H.R. 3381

```
            By Mr. NEHLS:
       H.R. 3381.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill establishes a formal DOJ program to support the 
     community outreach, public education, and officer safety and 
     wellness programs of the National Law Enforcement Officers 
     Memorial Fund, including programs to honor law enforcement, 
     compile data on officer fatalities and injuries, increase 
     public understanding and support for law enforcement, enhance 
     officer safety and wellness, and programs to bring law 
     enforcement closer to the communities they serve.
```
