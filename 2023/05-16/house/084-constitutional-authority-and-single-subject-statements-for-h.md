# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3386

**Measures:** H.R. 3386

```
            By Ms. PORTER:
        H.R. 3386.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Bottles and Breastfeeding Equipment Screening 
     Act to require hygienic handling of breast milk and baby 
     formula by security screening personnel of the Transportation 
     Security Administration and personnel of private security 
     companies providing security screening, and for other 
     purposes.
```
