# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3376

**Measures:** H.R. 3376

```
           By Mr. LANDSMAN:
       H.R. 3376.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Enhancing COPS Hiring Program Grants for Local Law

     used for recruitment and retention bonuses, as well as 
     simplifying the grant application process.
```
