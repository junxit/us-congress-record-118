# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3370

**Measures:** H.R. 3370

```
           By Mr. HIMES:
       H.R. 3370.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       ``US. Const. art. I Sec. 8'' U.S. Const. art. I, Sec. 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit funds available for the United States Armed 
     Forces to be obligated or expended for introduction of United 
     States Armed Forces into hostilities, and for other purposes.
```
