# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3364

**Measures:** H.R. 3364

```
           By Ms. DeLAURO:
       H.R. 3364.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Bipartisan legislation helping to expand WIC by closing the 
     nutrition gap among children.
```
