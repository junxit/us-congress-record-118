# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3387

**Measures:** H.R. 3387

```
            By Ms. PORTER:
        H.R. 3387.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Public Health Service Act, the Employee 
     Retirement Income Security Act of 1974, and the Internal 
     Revenue Code of 1986 to provide for certain health coverage 
     of newborns.
```
