# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2817

**Measures:** H.R. 2817

```
            By Mr. AMODEI:
        H.R. 2817.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to authorize appropriations under 
     Article I, Section 9 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will save Federal funds by authorizing changes to 
     the composition of circulating coins.
```
