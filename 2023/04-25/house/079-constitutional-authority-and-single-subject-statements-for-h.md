# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2831

**Measures:** H.R. 2831

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 2831.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 3 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To award posthumously a Congressional Gold Medal to Robert 
     Parris Moses, in recognition of his achievements and 
     contributions to advancing American education and civil 
     rights.
```
