# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2828

**Measures:** H.R. 2828

```
           By Mr. CONNOLLY:
       H.R. 2828.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause I of section 8 of article I of the Constitution, and 
     clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Directs OPM to require coverage of assisted reproductive 
     treatments in Federal Employees Health Benefits program 
     plans.
```
