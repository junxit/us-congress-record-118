# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2842

**Measures:** H.R. 2842

```
           By Ms. MALLIOTAKIS:
       H.R. 2842.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Department of Housing and Urban Development 
     to inspect certain public housing developments to ensure 
     compliance with housing quality and safety standards, and for 
     other purposes.
```
