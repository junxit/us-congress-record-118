# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2857

**Measures:** H.R. 2857

```
           By Mrs. SPARTZ:
       H.R. 2857.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution

[[Page H1950]]

       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Assistant Secretary for Planning and

     conduct an annual study on health care competition and 
     consolidation at the State level.
```
