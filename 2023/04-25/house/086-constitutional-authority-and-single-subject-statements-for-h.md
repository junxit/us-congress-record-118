# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2838

**Measures:** H.R. 2838

```
           By Mr. GRAVES of Louisiana:
       H.R. 2838.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 6 empowers Congress to coin 
     money.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would provide for the minting of a single 
     commemorative coin to celebrate the 75th anniversary of the 
     founding of NASCAR. The bill has no other provisions.
```
