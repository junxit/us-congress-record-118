# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2812

**Measures:** H.R. 2812

```
            By Mr. HIMES:
        H.R. 2812.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the Securities and Exchange Commission 
     to study and report on the costs encountered by small- and 
     medium-sized companies when undertaking initial public 
     offerings and certain offerings exempt from securities 
     registration requirements.
```
