# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2863

**Measures:** H.R. 2863

```
           By Mrs. SPARTZ:
       H.R. 2863.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act and title 
     XXVII of the Public Health Service Act to address incorrect 
     billing by off-campus hospital locations.
```
