# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 937

**Measures:** H.R. 937

```
           By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
       H.R. 937.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power . . . To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reaffirm the sovereignty of tribal governments by 
     protecting their right to determine for themselves the 
     appropriate labor practices within their jurisdiction, 
     instead of being forced to comply with employer requirements 
     under the National Labor Relations Act.
```
