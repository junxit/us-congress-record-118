# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 889

**Measures:** H.R. 889

```
            By Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania:
        H.R. 889.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Article I Section 
     8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill excludes from gross income, for income tax 
     purposes, certain broadband grants made for broadband 
     deployment.
```
