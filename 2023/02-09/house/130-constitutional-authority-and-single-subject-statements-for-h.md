# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 957

**Measures:** H.R. 957

```
           By Ms. SPANBERGER:
       H.R. 957.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code to increase from 
     $3,000 to $6,000 the amount excludible from the gross income 
     of public safety officers for distributions from governmental 
     retirement plans for health and long-term care insurance.
```
