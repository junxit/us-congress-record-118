# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 965

**Measures:** H.R. 965

```
           By Ms. TOKUDA:
       H.R. 965.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amending the Food, Agriculture, Conservation, and Trade Act 
     of 1990 to support and improve grants to combat plant pests 
     and other threats that affect coffee plants.
```
