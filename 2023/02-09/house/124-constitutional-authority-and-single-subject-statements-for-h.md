# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 951

**Measures:** H.R. 951

```
           By Mr. SCHIFF:
       H.R. 951.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Consitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit educational institutions that require students 
     to provide information with respect to their menstrual cycles 
     from receiving Federal funds.
```
