# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 929

**Measures:** H.R. 929

```
            By Mr. KILMER:
        H.R. 929.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 1 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and 
     Excises'' in order to ``provide for the . . . general Welfare 
     of the United States.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Land Into Trust for the Puyallup Tribe
```
