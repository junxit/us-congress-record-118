# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 953

**Measures:** H.R. 953

```
           By Ms. SHERRILL:
       H.R. 953.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution of 
     the United States of America.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a network of federally-supported, locally-
     administered child care options to improve economic, child 
     development, and employment outcomes for children and their 
     families.
```
