# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 908

**Measures:** H.R. 908

```
           By Ms. ESHOO:
       H.R. 908.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 4, clause 1 of the Constitution;
       Article I, Section, 8, clause 1 of the Constitution; and
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Disclosure of the tax returns of the president, vice 
     president, and candidates for those offices.
```
