# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 917

**Measures:** H.R. 917

```
            By Mr. TONY GONZALES of Texas:
        H.R. 917
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Restricts Russia, China, Iran, or North Korea from buying 
     U.S. land within 100 miles of a U.S. military installation or 
     50 miles from a military operation area.
```
