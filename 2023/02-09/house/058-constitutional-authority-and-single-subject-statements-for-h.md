# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 885

**Measures:** H.R. 885

```
            By Mr. DOGGETT:
        H.R. 885.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article I of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to provide transparency into how taxpayer dollars are spent 
     on pharmaceutical research and development and the terms of 
     funding agreements.
```
