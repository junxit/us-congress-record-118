# Unanimous Consent Agreement (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reed, Jack (R000122) [D-RI], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      Unanimous Consent Agreement

  Mr. REED. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following the 
11 a.m. vote today, the Senate recess until 1 p.m.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REED. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
