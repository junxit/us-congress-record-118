**Speaking:** Peters, Gary C. (P000595) [D-MI]

**Measures:** S. 299

```

  Mr. PETERS. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk due for a second reading.

       A bill (S. 299) to amend title 31, United States Code, to
```
