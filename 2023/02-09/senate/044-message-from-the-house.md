# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 185

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 1:07 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered

Senate:

       H.R. 185. An act to terminate the requirement imposed by

     Prevention for proof of COVID-19 vaccination for foreign
```
