# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4199

**Measures:** H.R. 4199

```
            By Mr. SOTO:
        H.R. 4199.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Agricultural Research, Extension, and 
     Education Reform Act of 1998 to establish and carry out a 
     pilot program to use blockchain technology in food safety and 
     labeling.
```
