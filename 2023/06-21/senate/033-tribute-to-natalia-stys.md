# TRIBUTE TO NATALIA STYS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO NATALIA STYS

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Natalia Stys, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Natalia is a graduate of Creighton University in Omaha, NE, having 
earned a degree in psychology. Currently, she is pursuing a law degree 
at the University of South Dakota Knudson School of Law in Vermillion, 
SD. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out 
of her internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Natalia for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
