# AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

**Speaking:** Bennet, Michael F. (B001267) [D-CO]

```
              AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF ESCORT COMMITTEE

  Mr. BENNET. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
President of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the 
part of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the 
House of Representatives to escort His Excellency Narendra Modi, Prime 
Minister of the Republic of India, into the House Chamber for the joint 
meeting at 4 p.m. on Thursday, June 22, 2023.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
