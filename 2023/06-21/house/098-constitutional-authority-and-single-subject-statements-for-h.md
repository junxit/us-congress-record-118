# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4265

**Measures:** H.R. 4265

```
           By Mr. RYAN:
       H.R. 4265.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 2
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Directs the Comptroller General of the United States to 
     conduct a study to assess the Department of Defense's ability 
     to mitigate insider threats and the unauthorized release of 
     classified information and systems.
```
