# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4254

**Measures:** H.R. 4254

```
            By Mr. MAST:
        H.R. 4254.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       A prohibition on contributions to international 
     organizations that advocate for sexual activity by persons 
     who are younger than the domestically prescribed minimum age 
     of consent.
```
