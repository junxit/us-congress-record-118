# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4255

**Measures:** H.R. 4255

```
            By Mr. MAST:
        H.R. 4255.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To ensure that Foreign Service officers are evaluated and 
     given opportunities for advancement based on their 
     conformance to merit system principles, to require the review 
     of Performance Improvement Plans during tenure and promotion 
     appraisals of Foreign Service officers, and to eliminate the 
     requirement for the inclusion of a public member on selection
```
