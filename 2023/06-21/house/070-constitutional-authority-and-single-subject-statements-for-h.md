# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4237

**Measures:** H.R. 4237

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 4237.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 and the 
     Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to specify 
     requirements concerning the consideration of pecuniary and 
     non-pecuniary factors.
```
