# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1425

**Measures:** H.R. 1425

```
            By Mr. TIFFANY:
        H.R. 1425.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article II, Section 2, Clause 2.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requiring any agreement related to pandemic response

     ratification.
```
