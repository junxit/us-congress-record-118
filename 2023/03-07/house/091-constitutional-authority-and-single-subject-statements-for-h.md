# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1401

**Measures:** H.R. 1401

```
           By Mr. GUEST:
       H.R. 1401.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Commissioner of U.S. Customs and Border 
     Protection to review and update policies and manuals at least 
     every three years related to inspections at ports of entry to 
     help prevent the smuggling of drugs and humans along the 
     border.
```
