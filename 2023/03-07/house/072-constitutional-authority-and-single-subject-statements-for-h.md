# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1382

**Measures:** H.R. 1382

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 1382.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To change the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection into 
     an independent agency named the Consumer Financial 
     Empowerment Agency, to transition the Agency to the regular 
     appropriations process, and for other purposes.
```
