# CALLING ON THE GOVERNMENT OF THE RUSSIAN FEDERATION TO IMMEDIATELY RELEASE UNITED STATES CITIZEN PAUL WHELAN

**Measures:** H.Res. 272

```
  CALLING ON THE GOVERNMENT OF THE RUSSIAN FEDERATION TO IMMEDIATELY 
               RELEASE UNITED STATES CITIZEN PAUL WHELAN

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of Rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the question on suspending the rules and 
agreeing to the resolution (H. Res. 272) calling on the Government of 
the Russian Federation to immediately release United States citizen 
Paul Whelan.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Texas (Mr. McCaul) that the House suspend the rules and 
agree to the resolution.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
