# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 72

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 72

```
           By Ms. WILLIAMS of Georgia:
       H.J. Res. 72.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Constitution to prohibit slavery in the United 
     States by eliminating the Exceptions Clause in the 13th 
     Amendment.
```
