# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4137

**Measures:** H.R. 4137

```
           By Ms. STEFANIK:
       H.R. 4137.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require that federal agencies procure and display 
     American flags that are 100% made in the United States using 
     only materials from America.
```
