# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4102

**Measures:** H.R. 4102

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 4102.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide individuals battling addiction with the 
     stability and assistance needed to rebuild their lives.
```
