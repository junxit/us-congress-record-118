# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4110

**Measures:** H.R. 4110

```
           By Ms. DeLAURO:
       H.R. 4110.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To empower the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) with new 
     authority to allow the investigation of corporate livestock 
     feedlots and confinement operations implicated in foodborne 
     illness outbreaks.
```
