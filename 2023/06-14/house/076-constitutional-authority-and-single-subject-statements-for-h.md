# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4096

**Measures:** H.R. 4096

```
           By Mr. CARTER of Georgia:
       H.R. 4096.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to expand the 
     application of Medicaid State programs to monitor 
     antipsychotic medications to all Medicaid beneficiaries.
```
