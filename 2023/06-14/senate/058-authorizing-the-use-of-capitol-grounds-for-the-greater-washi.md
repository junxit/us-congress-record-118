# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF CAPITOL GROUNDS FOR THE GREATER WASHINGTON SOAP BOX DERBY

**Speaking:** Cortez Masto, Catherine (C001113) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 43

```
AUTHORIZING THE USE OF CAPITOL GROUNDS FOR THE GREATER WASHINGTON SOAP 
                               BOX DERBY

  Ms. CORTEZ MASTO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to the consideration of H. Con. Res. 43, which was 
received from the House and is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 43) authorizing the 
     use of the Capitol Grounds for the Greater Washington Soap 
     Box Derby.

  Thereupon, the Senate proceeded to consider the concurrent 
resolution.
  Ms. CORTEZ MASTO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 43) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
