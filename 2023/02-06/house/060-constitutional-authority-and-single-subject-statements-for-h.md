# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 838

**Measures:** H.R. 838

```
           By Mr. LaHOOD:
       H.R. 838.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       United States Constitution Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1: 
     ``The Congress shall have Power To lay and collect Taxes . . 
     .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide a time-limited tax credit to encourage the 
     replacement or modernization of ineffecient, outdated freight 
     railcars.
```
