# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 845

**Measures:** H.R. 845

```
           By Ms. SANCHEZ:
       H.R. 845.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 
     to create a demonstration project to fund additional 
     secondary school counselors in troubled title I schools to 
     reduce the dropout rate.
```
