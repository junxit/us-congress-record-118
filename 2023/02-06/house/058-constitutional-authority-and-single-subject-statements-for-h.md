# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 836

**Measures:** H.R. 836

```
           By Mr. ISSA:
       H.R. 836.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would redesignate Gravelly Point Park, located 
     along the George Washington Memorial Parkway in Arlington 
     County, Virginia, as the Nancy Reagan Memorial Park.
```
