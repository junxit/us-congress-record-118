# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6908

**Measures:** H.R. 6908

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 6908
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would allow breastfeeding individuals summoned 
     for jury service in the federal courts and in the Superior 
     Court of the District of Columbia to be excused from service 
     upon the individual's request.
```
