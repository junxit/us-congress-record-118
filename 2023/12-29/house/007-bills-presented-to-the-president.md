# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1722, H.R. 2389, H.R. 6503

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Acting Clerk of the House, reported that on 
December 26, 2023, the following bills were presented to the President 
of the United States for approval:

       H.R. 1722. To amend the Grand Ronde Reservation Act, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 2389. To amend the Siletz Reservation Act to address 
     the hunting, fishing, trapping, and animal gathering rights 
     of the Confederated Tribes of Siletz Indians, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 6503. To amend title 49, Unites States Code, to extend 
     authorizations for the airport improvement program, to amend 
     the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the funding and 
     expenditure authority of the Airport and Airway Trust Fund, 
     and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
