# RECOGNIZING JACK AND JILL OF AMERICA, OAKLAND COUNTY

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
          RECOGNIZING JACK AND JILL OF AMERICA, OAKLAND COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 13, 2023

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize Jack and Jill of 
America's Oakland County Michigan Chapter. For 45 years, they brought 
leadership and mentorship programming to African-American young people 
across Oakland County.
  Founded in 1978, the Oakland County Chapter of Jack and Jill of 
America was the realization of a dream of two mothers, Robbie Johnson 
and Bobbye Wilhite, who were looking for more opportunities for their 
young children. The 39 charter members were united in seeking social, 
cultural, educational, recreational, and community service 
opportunities for their children. The Oakland County Chapter is 1 of 
262 chapters nationwide.
  Education, civic service, health, cultural awareness, and recreation 
are the keystones of Jack and Jill of America's programming. Their goal 
is to give children of color opportunities to develop as leaders and 
have access to opportunities that will allow growth, development, and a 
better quality of life. Jack and Jill members work on service projects 
that are aligned with its five values and are hands-on opportunities 
for young people to become involved with their communities.
  Please join me in recognition of Jack and Jill of America's Oakland 
County Chapter, as they celebrate 45 years of service, education, and 
excellence.
```
