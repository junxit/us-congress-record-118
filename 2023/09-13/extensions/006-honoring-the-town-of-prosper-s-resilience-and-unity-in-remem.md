# HONORING THE TOWN OF PROSPER'S RESILIENCE AND UNITY IN REMEMBRANCE OF 9/11

**Speaking:** Self, Keith (S001224) [R-TX]

```
 HONORING THE TOWN OF PROSPER'S RESILIENCE AND UNITY IN REMEMBRANCE OF 
                                  9/11

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KEITH SELF

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 13, 2023

  Mr. SELF. Mr. Speaker, on September 11, 2001, our Nation witnessed a 
tragic and unprecedented attack that forever changed the course of 
history.
  Under the leadership of Mayor David Bristol, Police Chief Doug 
Kowalski, and Fire Chief Stuart Blasingame, the town of Prosper 
commemorates September 11th each year with a solemn and heartfelt 
Remembrance Ceremony.
  Prosper's commitment to honoring the sacrifices made and the lives 
lost on September 11, 2001, reflects the enduring spirit of the 
American people and serves as a poignant reminder of the importance of 
community, compassion, and service in times of adversity.
  It is with much appreciation that I honor the town of Prosper's 
dedication to preserving the memory of 9/11 and foster a sense of unity 
and resilience within its community.

                          ____________________
```
