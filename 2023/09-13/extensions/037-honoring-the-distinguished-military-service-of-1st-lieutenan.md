# HONORING THE DISTINGUISHED MILITARY SERVICE OF 1ST LIEUTENANT KAREN LOUISE NUNAN

**Speaking:** Harshbarger, Diana (H001086) [R-TN]

```
  HONORING THE DISTINGUISHED MILITARY SERVICE OF 1ST LIEUTENANT KAREN 
                              LOUISE NUNAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DIANA HARSHBARGER

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 13, 2023

  Mrs. HARSHBARGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Marine Corps 
Officer, 1st Lieutenant Karen Louise Nunan from Russellville, Tennessee 
for her distinguished military service during the Vietnam War Era. 1st 
Lieutenant Nunan began her career in the United States Marine Corps in 
1972, where she specialized in data processing. During her assignment 
at Camp Lejeune (NC), she served on the committee that designed the 
first, inaugural base-wide computer network. After four years of 
valiant service to our country, 1st Lieutenant Nunan was honorably 
discharged in 1976.
  The 1st Lieutenant remains an active member within her community. As 
a veteran's advocate, she currently serves as Commander of American 
Legion Post 52 in Morristown, as well as Chairperson of the Women 
Veterans Committee for the American Legion 1st District and East 
Division.
  Please join me in thanking 1st Lieutenant Karen Louise Nunan for her 
leadership and honorable military service to our country.

                          ____________________
```
