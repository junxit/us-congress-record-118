# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
IMMIGRATION
Committee on the Budget: Committee concluded a hearing to examine how 
immigration fuels economic growth and our competitive advantage, after 
receiving testimony from Britta Glennon, University of Pennsylvania 
Wharton School, Philadelphia; David J. Bier, Cato Institute, Robert 
Rector, The Heritage Foundation, and Ronil Hira, Howard University, all 
of Washington, D.C.; and Laurens van Beek, Integrated DNA Technologies, 
Kessel-Lo, Belgium.
EXTREME HEAT AND WEATHER
Committee on Environment and Public Works: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine the effects of extreme heat and weather on 
transportation, after receiving testimony from David M. Hondula, City 
of Phoenix, Arizona; Travis Parsons, Laborers' Health and Safety Fund 
of North America, Washington, D.C.; and Aimee Flannery, Jacobs 
Solutions, Herndon, Virginia.
PGA TOUR-LIV DEAL
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Permanent 
Subcommittee on Investigations concluded a hearing to examine the PGA 
Tour-LIV deal, focusing on the Saudi Arabian Public Investment Fund's 
investments in the United States, after receiving testimony from Ben 
Freeman, Quincy Institute for Responsible Statecraft, and Joey Shea, 
Human Rights Watch, both of Washington, D.C.;

[[Page D870]]

and Brian Murphy, Logically AI, Arlington, Virginia.
FEDERAL BUREAU OF PRISONS OVERSIGHT
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded an oversight hearing to 
examine the Federal Bureau of Prisons, after receiving testimony from 
Colette S. Peters, Director, Federal Bureau of Prisons, Department of 
Justice.
INTELLIGENCE
  Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
