# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5440

**Measures:** H.R. 5440

```
           By Mr. HIMES:
       H.R. 5440.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 to prohibit 
     assistance to foreign governments that engage in the use of 
     foreign commercial spyware to target United States persons, 
     and for other purposes.
```
