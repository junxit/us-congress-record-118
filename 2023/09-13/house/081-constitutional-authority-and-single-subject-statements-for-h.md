# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5454

**Measures:** H.R. 5454

```
           By Ms. VELAZQUEZ:
       H.R. 5454.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The Congress shall have power . . . to regulate Commerce 
     with foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Would add the Administrator of the Small Business 
     Administration to the Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council 
     and require better coordination and harmonization of 
     regulations impacting small businesses.
```
