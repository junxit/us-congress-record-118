# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5434

**Measures:** H.R. 5434

```
           By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 5434.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for 12-month continuous enrollment of 
     individuals under the Medicaid program and CHIP.
```
