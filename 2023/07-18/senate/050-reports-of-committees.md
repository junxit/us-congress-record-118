# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S. 567, S. 728, S. 1094, S. 1664

```
                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. SANDERS, from the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions, without amendment:
       S. 567. A bill to amend the National Labor Relations Act, 
     the Labor Management Relations Act, 1947, and the Labor-
     Management Reporting and Disclosure Act of 1959, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. 728. A bill to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 
     1938 to provide more effective remedies to victims of 
     discrimination in the payment of wages on the basis of sex, 
     and for other purposes.
       By Mr. DURBIN, from the Committee on the Judiciary, without 
     amendment:
       S. 1094. A bill to provide a temporary safe harbor for 
     publishers of online content to collectively negotiate with 
     dominant online platforms regarding the terms on which 
     content may be distributed.
       By Mr. SANDERS, from the Committee on Health, Education, 
     Labor, and Pensions, without amendment:
       S. 1664. A bill to allow Americans to earn paid sick time 
     so that they can address their own health needs and the 
     health needs of their families.

                          ____________________
```
