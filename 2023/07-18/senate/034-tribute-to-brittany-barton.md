# TRIBUTE TO BRITTANY BARTON

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO BRITTANY BARTON

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Brittany for her hard work as an intern in 
the Senate Republican Conference. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Brittany is a native of Evanston. She attends Brigham Young 
University, where she studies business. She has demonstrated a strong 
work ethic, which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Brittany for the dedication she has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of 
our team. I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.

                          ____________________
```
