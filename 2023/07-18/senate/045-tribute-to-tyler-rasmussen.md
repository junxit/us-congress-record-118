# TRIBUTE TO TYLER RASMUSSEN

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO TYLER RASMUSSEN

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Tyler Rasmussen, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has 
done on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Mr. 
Rasmussen is a graduate of Brookings High School in Brookings, SD. 
Currently, he is attending South Dakota State University, where he 
studies agricultural business and legal studies. Mr. Rasmussen is a 
dedicated and diligent individual who has been devoted to getting the 
most out of his internship experience. Mr. Rasmussen has been a true 
asset to my office. I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Mr. 
Rasmussen for all of the work he has done and wish him continued 
success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
