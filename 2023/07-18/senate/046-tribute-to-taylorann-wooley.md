# TRIBUTE TO TAYLORANN WOOLEY

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO TAYLORANN WOOLEY

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize TaylorAnn Wooley, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has 
done on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Ms. Wooley 
is a graduate of Wright Junior Senior High School in Wright, WY. 
Currently, she is attending Black Hills State University, where she 
studies political science and international studies. Ms. Wooley is a 
dedicated and diligent individual who has been devoted to getting the 
most out of her internship experience. Ms. Wooley has been a true asset 
to my office. I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Ms. Wooley 
for all of the work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
