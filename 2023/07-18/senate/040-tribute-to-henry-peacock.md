# TRIBUTE TO HENRY PEACOCK

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO HENRY PEACOCK

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Henry for his hard work as an intern in the 
Energy and Natural Resources Committee. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Henry is a native of Virginia. He attends Trinity School at 
Meadowview. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made him 
an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected 
in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Henry for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
