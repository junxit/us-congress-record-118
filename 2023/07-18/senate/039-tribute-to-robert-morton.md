# TRIBUTE TO ROBERT MORTON

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ROBERT MORTON

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Robert for his hard work as an intern in my 
Washington, DC, office. I recognize his efforts and contributions to my 
office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Robert is a native of Big Horn. He attends Sewanee University, where 
he studies psychology. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which 
has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work 
is reflected in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Robert for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
