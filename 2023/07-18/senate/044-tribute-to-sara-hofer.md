# TRIBUTE TO SARA HOFER

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO SARA HOFER

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Sara Hofer, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has done 
on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Ms. Hofer is a 
graduate of Bridgewater-Emery High School in Emery, SD. Currently, she 
is attending Dordt University, where she studies biology and political 
science. Ms. Hofer is a dedicated and diligent individual who has been 
devoted to getting the most out of her internship experience. Ms. Hofer 
has been a true asset to my office. I extend my sincere thanks and 
appreciation to Ms. Hofer for all of the work she has done and wish her 
continued success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
