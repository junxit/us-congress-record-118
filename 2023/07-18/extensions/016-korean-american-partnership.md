# KOREAN-AMERICAN PARTNERSHIP

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                      KOREAN-AMERICAN PARTNERSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 18, 2023

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, July 27th marks the 70th 
Anniversary of the signing of the Korean Armistice Agreement, which 
formally ended the Korean War.
  As Co-Chair of Congressional Caucus on Korea, I was grateful to have 
joined my colleagues in welcoming new Ambassador of the Republic of 
Korea to the United States, His Excellency Hyun-Dong Cho and the Korean 
National Assembly ruling party delegation to the U.S. Capitol for a 
breakfast recognizing veterans of the Korean War.
  I valued the opportunity to have met with 6 members of the ruling 
People Power Party Leader, Assemblyman Gi-Hyeon Kim, members of the 
National Assembly Chul-Gyu Lee, Seok-Ki Kim, Sang-Bum Yoo, Min-Kuk 
Kang, and Chairman of the International Relations Committee of the 
People Power Party and former National Assemblyman Jae-Young Lee.
  Visible proof of affection by Americans for the people of the 
Republic of Korea was the Joint Session of Congress speech by President 
Yoon Suk Yeol.

                          ____________________
```
