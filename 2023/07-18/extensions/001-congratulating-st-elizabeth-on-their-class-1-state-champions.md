# CONGRATULATING ST. ELIZABETH ON THEIR CLASS 1 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
    CONGRATULATING ST. ELIZABETH ON THEIR CLASS 1 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 18, 2023

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating St. Elizabeth's Baseball Team on their 
victory in Missouri's Class 1 Championship game.
  The Hornets claimed the Class 1 title for the third time in 5 years 
and fifth in their program history. In an exhilarant game, the team 
broke open the 1-1 score with an incredible 5 runs in the top of the 
third inning. They never trailed and played so well that the game was 
cut short at the end of the sixth inning due to the ``run rule'' mercy 
rule. The Hornets should be commended for all their hard work 
throughout the past year and for bringing home the state championship 
to their school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the St. Elizabeth Baseball 
Team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
