# RECONGIZING THE RETIREMENT OF THOMAS "TOMMY" CARLISLE

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
        RECONGIZING THE RETIREMENT OF THOMAS ``TOMMY'' CARLISLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 18, 2023

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
retirement of Mr. Thomas ``Tommy'' Carlisle following his 37-year 
career at Anniston Army Depot. Mr. Carlisle's service includes travel 
throughout the United States as well as 4 deployments overseas 
supporting the mission of the U.S. Army.
  Tommy has served as a supervisor in 4 divisions including Fielding, 
Reciprocating Drive Train, Weapons and Track Systems. Additionally, 
Tommy has served as a Division Chief in

[[Page E690]]

3 divisions including Logistics Management (Fielding), Reciprocating 
and Tracked Systems. In 2010, he was named Deputy Director of 
Production where he served for 8 years before becoming selected to 
serve as the Director of Production. Building on this service, he was 
selected as Deputy to the Commander at Anniston Army Depot on September 
29, 2019.
  Mr. Carlisle is a native and resident of Lincoln, Alabama. Tommy has 
been married for 34 years to his wife, Susan. Together, they have 2 
children as well as 2 grandchildren. Tommy is an avid University of 
Alabama fan who enjoys tailgating and traveling to games. Additionally, 
Tommy enjoys being on the river, riding his Harley and socializing with 
his friends.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the retirement of Mr. 
Thomas Carlisle for his 37-year career at Anniston Army Depot.

                          ____________________
```
