# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4699

**Measures:** H.R. 4699

```
            By Ms. BUSH:
        H.R. 4699.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establishing a Division on Community Safety within the 
     Health and Human Services to fund, promote and otherwise 
     facilitate public health approaches to public safety.
```
