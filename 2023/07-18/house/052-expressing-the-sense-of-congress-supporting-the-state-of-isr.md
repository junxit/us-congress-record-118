  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I missed votes today due to

declared by the President on October 27, 2006, ``nay'' on rollcall No. 
334, H.J. Res. 70--Relating to a national emergency declared by the 
President on February 25, 2011, ``nay'' on rollcall No. 335, H.J. Res. 
74--Relating to a national emergency declared by the President on May 
16, 2012, ``nay'' on rollcall No. 336, H.J. Res. 71--Relating to a 
national emergency declared by the President on May 22, 2003, ``nay'' 
on rollcall No. 337, H.J. Res. 79--Relating to a national emergency 
declared by the President on May 11, 2004, and ``yea'' on rollcall No. 
338, H. Con. Res. 57--Expressing the sense of Congress supporting the
