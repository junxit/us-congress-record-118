# Business Before the Senate (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       Business Before the Senate

  Mr. President, finally, on upcoming Senate business, today, the 
Senate gavels back in for the start of a busy week, a very busy work 
period.
  There is so much we must do on lowering costs and improving lives of 
American families, strengthening healthcare, lowering prescription drug 
costs, preserving America's competitiveness, and protecting America's 
fundamental rights. We will get to work on these issues, particularly 
starting off with the fire bill, which will come before us this week.
  I yield the floor.
```
