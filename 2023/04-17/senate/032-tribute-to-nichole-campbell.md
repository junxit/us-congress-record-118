# TRIBUTE TO NICHOLE CAMPBELL

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO NICHOLE CAMPBELL

● Mr. OSSOFF. Madam President, I rise to commend Nichole 
Campbell, a leader in the Augusta community working to empower women in 
business and give back to others.
  In 2018, Ms. Campbell founded The Sisters Social, Inc., a nonprofit 
organization that aims to unite women of different backgrounds and 
provide entrepreneurial education, professional networking, and 
community outreach. After seeking out an organization of women business 
owners and failing to find one in her community, Ms. Campbell stepped 
up to found The Sisters Social, Inc., for women in Augusta and the 
Central Savannah River Area, CSRA. During the pandemic, the group 
provided countless business owners with invaluable advice during a 
challenging time for many.
  As Georgia's U.S. Senator, I commend and recognize Nichole Campbell 
on her leadership and great successes empowering women business owners 
and giving back to her community.●

                          ____________________
```
