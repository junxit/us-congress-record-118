# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2633

**Measures:** H.R. 2633

```
           By Mr. SANTOS:
       H.R. 2633.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       US Constitution, Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of State to submit to Congress a 
     report on the designation of MS-13, Queen Nation, Sinaloa, 
     Cartel Jalisco Nueva Generacion, and Beltran Leyva 
     Organization as foreign terrorist organizations, and for 
     other purposes.
```
