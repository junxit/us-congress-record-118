# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, APRIL 27, 2023, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY YOON SUK YEOL, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOREA

**Speaking:** Mast, Brian J. (M001199) [R-FL]

```
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, APRIL 27, 
2023, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY YOON 
              SUK YEOL, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOREA

  Mr. MAST. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Thursday, April 27, 2023, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Yoon Suk Yeol, President of 
the Republic of Korea.

                          ____________________
```
