# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2638

**Measures:** H.R. 2638

```
           By Mr. GIMENEZ:
       H.R. 2638.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant

       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Cuban-American Unrecognized Bravery Act of 2023--To direct 
     the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to treat certain 
     individuals who participated in the invasion of the Bay of 
     Pigs, Cuba, in April, 1961, as veterans for the purposes of 
     eligibility for benefits under laws administered by the 
     Secretary
```
