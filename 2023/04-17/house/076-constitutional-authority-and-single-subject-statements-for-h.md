# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2654

**Measures:** H.R. 2654

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 2654.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Administrator of the Federal Aviation 
     Administration to take certain actions to prohibit air 
     carriers from reducing the size of passenger seats on 
     aircraft, and for other purposes.
```
