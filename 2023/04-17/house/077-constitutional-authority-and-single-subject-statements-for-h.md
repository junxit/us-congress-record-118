# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2655

**Measures:** H.R. 2655

```
           By Mr. SCHNEIDER:
       H.R. 2655.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would award a Congressional Gold Medal to Captain 
     James A. Lovell, Jr. for his service to the United States, 
     most notably his leadership in the Apollo Space Program as 
     captain of Apollo 13.
```
