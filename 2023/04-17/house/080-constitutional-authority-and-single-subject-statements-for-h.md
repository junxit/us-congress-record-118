# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2658

**Measures:** H.R. 2658

```
           By Mr. TORRES of New York:
       H.R. 2658.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to impose criminal 
     penalties on the President for engaging in actions to 
     interfere with the determination of electoral votes in a 
     presidential election, and for other purposes.
```
