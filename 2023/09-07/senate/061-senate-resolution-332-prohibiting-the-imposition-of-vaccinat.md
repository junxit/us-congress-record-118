# SENATE RESOLUTION 332--PROHIBITING THE IMPOSITION OF VACCINATION, TESTING, AND MASKING REQUIREMENTS RELATING TO COVID-19 FOR SENATE PAGES

**Measures:** S.Res. 332

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

   SENATE RESOLUTION 332--PROHIBITING THE IMPOSITION OF VACCINATION, 
TESTING, AND MASKING REQUIREMENTS RELATING TO COVID-19 FOR SENATE PAGES

  Mr. PAUL submitted the following resolution; which was referred to 
the Committee on Rules and Administration:

                              S. Res. 332

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. PROHIBITION ON COVID-19 VACCINATION, TESTING, AND 
                   MASKING REQUIREMENTS FOR SENATE PAGES.

       A Senate Page or applicant to be a Senate Page may not be 
     required to--
       (1) show proof of COVID-19 vaccination status;
       (2) receive a vaccination for COVID-19;
       (3) undergo testing for COVID-19 without cause; or
       (4) wear a mask.
```
