# Vote on Gomez Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Markey, Edward J. (M000133) [D-MA], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA]

Will the Senate advise and consent to the Gomez nomination?
