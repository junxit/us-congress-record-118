# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3087

**Measures:** H.R. 3087

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 3087.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 3 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Food and Nutrition Act of 2008 to exempt 
     workforce training dollars as income for supplemental 
     nutrition assistance program beneficiaries.
```
