# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3109

**Measures:** H.R. 3109

```
           By Mr. NEHLS:
       H.R. 3109.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress shall have the power to lay and collect Taxes, 
     Duties, Imosts, and Excises to pay the Debts and provide for 
     the common Defence and general welfare of the United States; 
     but all Duties, Imposts and Excises shall be uniform 
     throughout the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Transportation to rescind 
     certain no fly zone restrictions, and for other purposes.
```
