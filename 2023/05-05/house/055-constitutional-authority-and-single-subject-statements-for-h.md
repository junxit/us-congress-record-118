# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3121

**Measures:** H.R. 3121

```
           By Mr. SWALWELL:
       H.R. 3121.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, specifically clause 18 related to the 
     power to make all laws necessary and proper for carrying out 
     the powers vested in Congress.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establishes new criminal offenses for intentional acts (or 
     attempts to commit acts) that cause bodily injury to a 
     journalist in the process of newsgathering.
```
