# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3116

**Measures:** H.R. 3116

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 3116.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power . . . To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Power Act and the Natural Gas Act with 
     respect to the enforcement of certain provisions, and for 
     other purposes.
```
