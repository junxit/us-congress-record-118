# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3103

**Measures:** H.R. 3103

```
            By Ms. McCOLLUM:
        H.R. 3103.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will ensure that no U.S. dollars provided to 
     Israel are used for the detention or mistreatment of 
     Palestinian children and their families.
```
