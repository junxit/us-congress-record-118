# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CAPTAIN TAYLOR BAZE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
          HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO CAPTAIN TAYLOR BAZE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 29, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Captain Taylor 
Baze, who is retiring from the Coppell Fire Department.
  Over the last 30 years, Captain Baze has demonstrated excellent fire 
service to the residents of Coppell, Texas. Captain Baze's influence 
over the City of Coppell Fire Department will still be felt in his 
absence. He was an excellent leader and considered a brother by many. 
His legacy will not be forgotten as he leaves the fire department 
better off for having him as a captain.
  I thank Captain Baze for his leadership and service to our community 
and wish him a happy retirement.

                          ____________________
```
