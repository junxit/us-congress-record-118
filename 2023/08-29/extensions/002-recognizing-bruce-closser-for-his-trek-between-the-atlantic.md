# RECOGNIZING BRUCE CLOSSER FOR HIS TREK BETWEEN THE ATLANTIC AND PACIFIC OCEANS

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
RECOGNIZING BRUCE CLOSSER FOR HIS TREK BETWEEN THE ATLANTIC AND PACIFIC 
                                 OCEANS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 29, 2023

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize Bruce 
Closser for his impressive bicycle trek between the Atlantic and 
Pacific Oceans. After months of training, Bruce became the oldest 
individual to complete this 3-month journey at the age of 78. As a 
resident of Marquette and the great state of Michigan, Bruce shines as 
both a pillar of health and an example of ongoing resilience.
  After being diagnosed with Parkinson's disease in the spring of 2019, 
Bruce has worked to maintain his active lifestyle. Despite his illness, 
he has defied all expectations in conquering the 4,200-mile trek 
between the United States' major coasts. His achievement is officially 
recognized by Guinness World Records and will be remembered by 
individuals throughout the world. As lifelong Yooper, Bruce has always 
valued the outdoors and its numerous activities. In addition to 
cycling, he is an avid skier, spending many winters at Marquette 
Mountain. Beyond enjoying nature, Bruce is deeply involved with the 
Parkinson's Foundation. His efforts have included raising awareness, 
educational advocacy, and fundraising. To the local communities of the 
Upper Peninsula and our Congressional District, Bruce is truly an 
inspiration.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize Bruce Closser for this 
impressive accomplishment. On behalf of Michigan's First District, I 
extend my gratitude to Bruce and wish him and his family the best in 
their future endeavors.
```
