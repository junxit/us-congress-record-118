# HONORING HOMETOWN HERO MADDY SEXTON

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                  HONORING HOMETOWN HERO MADDY SEXTON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 29, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize our Texas-24 
Hometown Hero, Maddy Sexton of Southlake Carroll. Earlier this year, 
Maddy was in a car heading to school with a friend when her friend 
began to experience a medical emergency. Maddy demonstrated maturity 
beyond her years and handled the situation perfectly--she immediately 
pulled over, called Southlake Fire, and helped take care of her friend 
before Southlake Fire quickly arrived.
  I thank Maddy for going above and beyond to look out for her friend 
through her vigilance, quick thinking, and maturity. It is an honor to 
join Southlake DPS in recognizing Maddy for her contributions to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
