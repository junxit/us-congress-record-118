# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5313

**Measures:** H.R. 5313

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
        H.R. 5313.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 17 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would permit the Chair of the Council of the 
     District of Columbia to transmit legislation to Congress in 
     the form of the Chair's choosing, including electronic form.
```
