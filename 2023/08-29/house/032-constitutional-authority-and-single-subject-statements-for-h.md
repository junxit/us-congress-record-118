# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5316

**Measures:** H.R. 5316

```

        H.R. 5316.

       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution

       To amend section 412 of the Federal Food, Drug, and
```
