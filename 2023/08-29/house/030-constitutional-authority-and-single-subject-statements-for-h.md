# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5314

**Measures:** H.R. 5314

```
            By Mr. PERRY:
        H.R. 5314.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Eliminates the judicially-created loophole that exempts 
     union-related violence and extortion from prosecution under 
     the Hobbs Act.
```
