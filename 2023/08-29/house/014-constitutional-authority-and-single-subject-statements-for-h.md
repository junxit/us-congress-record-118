# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5298

**Measures:** H.R. 5298

```
            By Mr. BACON:
        H.R. 5298
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 14: Congress shall have the 
     authority to ``make rules for the government and regulation 
     of the land and naval forces''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       rules related to department of defense management
```
