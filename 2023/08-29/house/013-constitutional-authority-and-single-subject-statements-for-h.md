# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5297

**Measures:** H.R. 5297

```
            By Mr. BACON:
        H.R. 5297.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 14: Congress shall have the 
     authority to ``make rules for the government and regulation 
     of the land and naval forces''
       The single subject of this legislation is
       rules related to the award of defense small business 
     contracts
```
