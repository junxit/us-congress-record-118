# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 126, Monica Ramirez Almadani, of 
     California, to be United States District Judge for the 
     Central District of California.
         Richard J. Durbin, Brian Schatz, John W. Hickenlooper, 
           Margaret Wood Hassan, Gary C. Peters, Mark Kelly, Jack 
           Reed, Tammy Duckworth, Christopher Murphy, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, Catherine Cortez Masto, Mazie K. Hirono, 
           Benjamin L. Cardin, Jeanne Shaheen, Tammy Baldwin, 
           Angus S. King, Jr., Alex Padilla, Robert Menendez, 
           Michael F. Bennet.
```
