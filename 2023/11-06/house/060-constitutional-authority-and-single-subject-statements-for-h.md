# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6241

**Measures:** H.R. 6241

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 6241.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, clause 2
       ``The Congress shall have Power to dispose of and make all 
     needful Rules and Regulations respecting the Territory or 
     other Property belonging to the United States''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires certain data collection and reporting on space 
     utilization, occupancy, and availability of office space 
     leased by an executive agency.
```
