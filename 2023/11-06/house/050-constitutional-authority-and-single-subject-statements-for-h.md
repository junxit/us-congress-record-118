# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6231

**Measures:** H.R. 6231

```
           By Mr. IVEY:
       H.R. 6231.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to require a 
     prioritized policy issuance review process for the Department 
     of Homeland Security.
```
