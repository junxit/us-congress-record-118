# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6243

**Measures:** H.R. 6243

```
           By Mr. GALLEGO:
       H.R. 6243.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article 1, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Labor to issue an occupational 
     safety and health standard regarding opioid overdose reversal 
     medication and employee training.
```
