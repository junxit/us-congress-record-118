# CONGRATULATING SENIOR PASTOR CHUL LEE ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
        CONGRATULATING SENIOR PASTOR CHUL LEE ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 6, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank Senior Pastor Chul 
Lee for his service with the Dallas Central Methodist Church and to 
congratulate him on his retirement.
  Pastor Lee's exceptional leadership at the Dallas Central Methodist 
Church has brought about remarkable initiatives that have made a 
significant impact on our community. One of the standout achievements 
during his tenure has been the church's dedication to providing 
scholarships to various segments of our community. Over the past 15 
years, these scholarships have totaled an impressive $500.000, making 
it possible for numerous young individuals to pursue their educational 
aspirations. These segments include students attending university from 
the Irving Police Department, students from the firefighting community, 
and students from Irving Senior High School who are seeking higher 
education.
  Pastor Sung Chul Lee's unwavering commitment to our community's 
welfare serves as a testament to his profound dedication to the well-
being of our citizens and his faith. Through his leadership and the 
contributions of the Dallas Central Methodist Church, they have greatly 
improved the lives of numerous Irving residents. It is my honor to 
congratulate Senior Pastor Sung Chul Lee on his successful career and 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
