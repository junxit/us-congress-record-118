# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Posey, Bill (P000599) [R-FL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL POSEY

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, November 6, 2023

  Mr. POSEY. Mr. Speaker, I wish to clarify that it was my intent to be 
recorded as YEA on Rollcall No. 572, on Amendment No. 66 to the Fiscal 
Year 2024 Interior and related agencies appropriations bill offered by 
Rep. Harshbarger of Tennessee on Thursday, November 2, 2023. I believed 
that my vote was recorded as YEA at that time, but I apparently did not 
complete execution of my vote. I wish to make it clear that I intended 
to vote for the amendment. The amendment would have provided that: 
``None of the funds made available by this Act shall be available for 
the United States Board on Geographic Names.''
```
