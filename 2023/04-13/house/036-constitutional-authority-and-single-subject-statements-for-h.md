# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2598

**Measures:** H.R. 2598

```
           By Mr. JOYCE of Ohio:
       H.R. 2598.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a Commission on the Federal Regulation of 
     Cannabis to study a prompt and plausible pathway to the 
     Federal regulation of cannabis.
```
