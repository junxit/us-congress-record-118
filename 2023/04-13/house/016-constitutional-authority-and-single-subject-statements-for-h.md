# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2578

**Measures:** H.R. 2578

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 2578.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 to increase 
     the exemption from registration threshold for certain 
     investment advisers of private funds to reflect the change in 
     inflation.
```
