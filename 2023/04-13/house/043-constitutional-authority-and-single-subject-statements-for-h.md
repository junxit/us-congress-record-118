# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2605

**Measures:** H.R. 2605

```
           By Mr. McHENRY:
       H.R. 2605.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3:
       To regulate commerce with states, other nations, and Native 
     American tribes.
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18:
       Authority to create laws that are necessary and proper to 
     carry out the laws of the land
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Updates the mandatory registration threshold of 2,000 or 
     more holders of record shall exclude Qualified Institutional 
     Buyers (QIBs) and institutional accredited investors.
```
