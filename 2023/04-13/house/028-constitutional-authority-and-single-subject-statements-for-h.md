# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2590

**Measures:** H.R. 2590

```
           By Mr. GARBARINO:
       H.R. 2590.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill directs the SEC to finalize its 2020 proposed 
     exemption from broker registration requirements for 
     ``finders'' who help issuers raise capital in private markets 
     from accredited investors.
```
