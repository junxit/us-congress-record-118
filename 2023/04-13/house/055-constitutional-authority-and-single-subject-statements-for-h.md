# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2617

**Measures:** H.R. 2617

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 2617.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 49, United States Code, to require air 
     carriers to provide passengers experiencing a delay or 
     cancellation with alternative transportation on another air 
     carrier.
```
