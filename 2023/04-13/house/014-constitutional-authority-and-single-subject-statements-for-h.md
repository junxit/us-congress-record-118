# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2576

**Measures:** H.R. 2576

```
           By Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas:
       H.R. 2576.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Expands the research report exception. to include reports 
     about any issuer that undertakes a proposed offering of 
     public securities, not just emerging growth companies.
```
