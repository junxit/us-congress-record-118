# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2613

**Measures:** H.R. 2613

```
           By Mr. MENENDEZ:
       H.R. 2613.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 49, United States Code, to require that a 
     voluntary agreement with respect to commercial air tour 
     operations over a national park considers the well being of 
     communities overflown by aircraft involved in such 
     operations, and for other purposes.
```
