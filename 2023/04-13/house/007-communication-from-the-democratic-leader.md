# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                    April 7, 2023.
     Hon. Kevin McCarthy,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker McCarthy: Pursuant to the National Foundation 
     on the Arts and the Humanities Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 955(b)

       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,

     Democratic Leader.
```
