# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2589

**Measures:** H.R. 2589

```
           By Ms. CRAIG:
       H.R. 2589.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill prohibits funds made available for the official 
     travel of a Member of Congress or other officer or employee 
     of the legislative branch from being used for airline 
     accommodations that are not coach-class accommodations.
```
