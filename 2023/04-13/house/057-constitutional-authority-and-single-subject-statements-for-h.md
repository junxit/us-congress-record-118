# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 2619

**Measures:** H.R. 2619

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 2619.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide a 
     lookback rule in the case of certain Federally declared 
     disasters for amounts related to earned income for purposes 
     of determining certain tax credits.
```
