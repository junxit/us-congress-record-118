# HONORING THE LIFE OF JAN SMITH

**Speaking:** LaHood, Darin (L000585) [R-IL]

```
                     HONORING THE LIFE OF JAN SMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 13, 2023

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and legacy of 
Jeannette ``Jan'' Smith of Peoria, Illinois, who passed away on March 
11, 2023 at the age of 88.
  A native of Peoria, Illinois, Jan Smith attended Bradley University 
where she was actively involved as a member of the Phi Kappa Phi Honor 
Society and Gamma Phi Beta Sorority, as well as a member of the Dean's 
List. She graduated summa cum laude from Bradley University in 1975.
  Jan's commitment to her community led her to become a teacher in 
Peoria area schools. In 1980, she left her teaching career and founded 
the Family Resources Center, which provided free-loan resources that 
taught about the sanctity of life. Her dedication to education and for 
the respect of life led her to serve at the Family Resources Center for 
over 35 years. For her tireless service, she received a papal honor in 
the form of the Pro Ecclesiae et Pontifice Medal as well as the Pere 
Marquette Award from the Diocese of Peoria.
  I want to offer my condolences to the Smith family and the people of 
Peoria on the loss of a great woman. Jan's leadership, commitment, and 
dedication has made a lasting impact on the Central Illinois community 
that will last for generations.
```
