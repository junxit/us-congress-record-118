# RECOGNIZING TODD DeGARMO

**Speaking:** Wenstrup, Brad R. (W000815) [R-OH]

```
                        RECOGNIZING TODD DeGARMO

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRAD R. WENSTRUP

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 13, 2023

  Mr. WENSTRUP. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize and congratulate my 
good friend Todd DeGarmo on his acceptance of the 2023 William Howard 
Taft Medal from the University of Cincinnati for notable achievement in 
the field of architecture.
  A graduate of UC, Todd put his blood, sweat, and tears into a career 
in architecture that has spanned nearly 35 years. Todd has worked for 
and led STUDIOS Architecture, overseeing numerous projects and 
renovations during his tenure.
  Todd founded the company's New York office in 1995 and, ten years 
later, was named the first CEO of STUDIOS. Under his stewardship, 
STUDIOS opened new offices in the United States and around the globe, 
including in Mumbai, India and Lyon, France.
  I want to commend Todd for his career at STUDIOS and his many 
meritorious projects that earned him the 2023 William Howard Taft 
Medal. Todd deserves this.

                          ____________________
```
