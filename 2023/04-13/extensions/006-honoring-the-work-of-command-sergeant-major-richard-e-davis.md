# HONORING THE WORK OF COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR RICHARD E. DAVIS

**Speaking:** Kelly, Trent (K000388) [R-MS]

```
      HONORING THE WORK OF COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR RICHARD E. DAVIS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TRENT KELLY

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 13, 2023

  Mr. KELLY of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
upcoming retirement of Richard E. Davis, a superb Soldier and citizen 
who has served our Nation for forty-one years, including, since October 
2021, serving as the Command Sergeant Major of Camp Shelby, 
Mississippi, the largest Reserve Component training site in the United 
States.
  A native of Petal, Mississippi, Command Sergeant Major Davis began 
his military career in the Mississippi Army National Guard on April 5, 
1982, as a 62B, Construction Equipment Repairman. During his career, 
Command Sergeant Major Davis served in all levels of responsibility, 
including Track Vehicle Repairman, 3656th Maintenance Company, Camp 
Shelby, Mississippi; Armament Maintenance Supervisor, Headquarters, 
Headquarters Company, 106th Support Battalion, Monticello, Mississippi; 
Senior Auto Repairman, 3656th Maintenance Company, Camp Shelby, 
Mississippi; Battalion Maintenance Operation Noncommissioned Officer, 
Headquarters, 223rd Engineer Battalion, WestPoint, Mississippi; First 
Sergeant, Forward Support Command, 223rd Engineer Battalion, in 
Aberdeen, Mississippi, the unit with which he deployed to Afghanistan 
in support of Operation Enduring Freedom from 2011 through 2012.
  Upon return from overseas deployment, Command Sergeant Major Davis 
was assigned as the Mississippi National Guard Environmental Supervisor 
with Joint Force Headquarters, Mississippi National Guard, in Jackson, 
Mississippi. After completing his staff tenure, Command Sergeant Major 
Davis returned to the line units he knew and loved serving as the 
Command Sergeant Major, 890th Engineer Battalion, Gulfport, 
Mississippi. Following a successful assignment as the battalion level, 
Command Sergeant Major Davis was assigned as the 168th Engineer Brigade 
Command Sergeant Major from June 2018 until September 2021, after which 
time he was assigned as the Command Sergeant Major of Camp Shelby Joint 
Forces Training Center in 2021.
  During his career, Command Sergeant Major Davis completed all levels 
of military education including Primary Leadership Development Course, 
the Basic Noncommissioned Officers Course, the Advanced Noncommissioned 
Officers Course, and, ultimately, the Sergeant Major Academy, as well 
as numerous other maintenance and training courses. Command Sergeant 
Major Davis distinguished himself as a tactical and technical expert 
during his deployment to Afghanistan and while serving at Camp Shelby, 
supporting mobilizing and deploying units, and, of course, numerous 
State Emergency Operations.
  Command Sergeant Major Davis's military awards include the Bronze 
Star Medal; Meritorious Service Medal; Army Commendation Medal; Army 
Achievement Medal; Army Reserve Component Achievement Medal; National 
Defense Service Medal; Global War on Terrorism Medal; Humanitarian 
Service Medal; Armed Forces Reserve Medal; Noncommissioned Professional 
Development Ribbon with a numeral IV; Army Service Ribbon; Overseas 
Service Ribbon; Mississippi Medal of Efficiency; Mississippi War Medal; 
Mississippi Emergency Service Medal; Mississippi Service School Medal; 
and Mississippi Longevity Medal.
  I commend Command Sergeant Major Davis and his family for their 
dedicated support to our Army, our Soldiers, and this Nation. I 
congratulate him on an extremely successful career, his dedication to 
service, and wish him a fulfilling and restful retirement.
```
