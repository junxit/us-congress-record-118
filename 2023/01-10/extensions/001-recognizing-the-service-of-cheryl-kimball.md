# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF CHERYL KIMBALL

```
               RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF CHERYL KIMBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 10, 2023

  Mr. WITTMAN, Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Cheryl 
Kimball and her service to Virginia's First District and the Nation.
  Prior to joining my office, Cheryl spent over a decade working on 
Capitol Hill.
  She came to D.C. in 1988 to work for Senator Bennett Johnston from 
her home state of Louisiana. In the subsequent years, she worked for 
the Senate Commerce Committee and Senate Committee on Foreign Relations 
as well as Congressman John Mica from Florida.
  Cheryl joined my Stafford office in July 2016 as a District 
Representative and served in that role until December 2022. She 
contributed tremendously to providing the best constituent service in 
the Commonwealth and the Nation, helping countless constituents with 
their problems. She also effectively represented myself, my team, and 
my work throughout the Fredericksburg region and the First District. 
Her work has been invaluable.
  I would like to thank Cheryl for her contributions and dedicated 
service over the last 6 years. Overall, she has nearly 20 years of 
federal service. She has been a key member of my district office team, 
and I wish her the best as she enters her well-deserved retirement.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask you to join me in recognizing Cheryl Kimball for 
her service to Virginia's First District and the Nation. May God bless 
Cheryl in whatever she decides to do next.

                          ____________________
```
