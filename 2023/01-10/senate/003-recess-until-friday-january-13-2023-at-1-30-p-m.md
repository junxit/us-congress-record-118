# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, JANUARY 13, 2023, AT 1:30 P.M.

```
          RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, JANUARY 13, 2023, AT 1:30 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 1:30 p.m. on Friday, January 13, 2023.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 31 seconds a.m., stands in recess 
until Friday, January 13, 2023, at 1:30 p.m.
```
