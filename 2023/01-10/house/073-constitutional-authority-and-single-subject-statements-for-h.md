# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 247

**Measures:** H.R. 247

```
            By Mr. ESTES:
        H.R. 247.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2
       The Congress shall have power to dispose of and make all 
     needful rules and regulations the territory or other property 
     belonging to the United States.
```
