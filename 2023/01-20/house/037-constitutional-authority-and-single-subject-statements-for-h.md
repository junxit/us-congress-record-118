# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 421

**Measures:** H.R. 421

```
           By Mr. GOOD of Virginia:
       H.R. 421.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to extend protection to unborn 
     children and their mothers under the Commerce Clause of 
     Section 8 or Article 1. This authority also comes from the 
     Equal Protection and Due Process Clauses of Section 1 and the 
     Enforcement Clause of Section 5, of the 14th Amendment.
```
