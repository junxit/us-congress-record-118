# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 23, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JANUARY 23, 2023, AT 3 P.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 3 p.m. on Monday, January 23, 2023.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1 and 30 seconds p.m., recessed until 
Monday, January 23, 2023, at 3 p.m.
```
