# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1070

**Measures:** H.R. 1070

```
            By Mr. CARTER of Georgia:
        H.R. 1070
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 3 of Section 8 of article I of the U.S. Constitution 
     provides Congress with the power to regulate commerce.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase American energy production and restore energy 
     leadership by providing the owner or operator of a critical 
     energy resource facility an interim permit under subtitle C 
     of the Solid Waste Disposal Act that is subject to final 
     approval by the Administrator of the Environmental Protection 
     Agency.
```
