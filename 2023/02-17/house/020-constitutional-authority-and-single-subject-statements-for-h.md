# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1062

**Measures:** H.R. 1062

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
       H.R. 1062.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8 clause 18 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Eliminating a Quantifiably Unjust Application of the 
     Law (EQUAL) Act abides by the single subject requirement in 
     that the provisions are limited to the elimination of 
     sentencing disparities for federal criminal offenses 
     involving crack and powder cocaine.
```
