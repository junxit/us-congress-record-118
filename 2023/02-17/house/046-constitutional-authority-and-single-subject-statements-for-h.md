# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1088

**Measures:** H.R. 1088

```
            By Ms. LEE of California:
        H.R. 1088.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Awarding a Congressional Gold Medal, postmordumly, to 
     Congresswoman Shirley Chisholm who was the first woman to run 
     for President and the first African American woman ever 
     elected to Congress.
```
