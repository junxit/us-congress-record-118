# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 34

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 34

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.J. Res. 34.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Expressing the sense of Congress that a woman's ability to 
     engage in interstate travel and receive services available in 
     another state that would otherwise not be available in her 
     own state is protected under Article IV of the Constitution.
```
