# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1068

**Measures:** H.R. 1068

```
            By Mr. BUCSHON:
        H.R. 1068
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This resolution is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase American energy production and restore energy 
     leadership by requiring the DOE to assess and strengthen our 
     critical energy resource supply chain.
```
