# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1058

**Measures:** H.R. 1058

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
       H.R. 1058.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase American energy production and restore energy 
     leadership by streamlining the permitting process of cross-
     border pipelines and electric transmission lines.
```
