# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4532

**Measures:** H.R. 4532

```
           By Mr. ALFORD:
       H.R. 4532.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8
       The Congress shall provide for the common Defence and 
     general Welfare of the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To limit the use of Federal funds authorized by the 
     National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2024 for 
     the Department of Defense Countering Extremism Work Group.
```
