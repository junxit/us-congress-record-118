# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4540

**Measures:** H.R. 4540

```
           By Mr. CUELLAR:
       H.R. 4540.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Safe Drinking Water Act to establish a program 
     to provide grants to suppliers of water for the purpose of 
     making infrastructure improvements to public water systems.
```
