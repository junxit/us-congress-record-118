# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4564

**Measures:** H.R. 4564

```
           By Ms. TENNEY:
       H.R. 4564.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would prevent the federal government from 
     entering into contracts with entities that engage in a 
     boycott of the State of Israel, a boycott of businesses or 
     individuals involved in transactions with the State of Israel 
     or a boycott of companies duly authorized, licensed, or 
     established under the laws of the State of Israel.
```
