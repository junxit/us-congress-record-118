# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4551

**Measures:** H.R. 4551

```
           By Mr. LOUDERMILK:
       H.R. 4551.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To limit conditions under which the SEC may require 
     National Securities Exchanges and their members to provide 
     personally identifiable information through the Consolidated 
     Audit Trail.
```
