# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 4533

**Measures:** H.R. 4533

```
           By Mr. ALFORD:
       H.R. 4533.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8
       The Congress shall provide for the common Defence and 
     general Welfare of the United States;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Defense to assess, and submit a 
     report to Congress on, civilian staffing at the Department of 
     Defense Office for Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion, and for 
     other purposes.
```
