# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3783

**Measures:** H.R. 3783

```
            By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
        H.R. 3783.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1
       The Congress shall have Power to . . . provide for the . . 
     . general Welfare of the United States; . . .
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would close all Mink farms and pay farmers the 
     fair market price of their farms.
```
