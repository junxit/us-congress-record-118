# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 3785

**Measures:** H.R. 3785

```
            By Mr. GARAMENDI:
        H.R. 3785.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill requires construction inspection functions for 
     Federal-aid highways to be conducted by public employees, 
     with a limited, 1-year exception if the State or local 
     transportation agency lacks the staffing to conduct these 
     inspections
```
