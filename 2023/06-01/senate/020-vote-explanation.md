# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Bennet, Michael F. (B001267) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 45

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. BENNET. Mr. President, I was necessarily absent for rollcall vote 
No. 133, Confirmation of Darrel Papillion of LA to be U.S. District 
Judge. Had I been present for the vote, I would have voted yea.

would have voted nay.

Resolution, H.J. Res. 45, forces Federal student loan borrowers across

months of credit towards Public Service Loan Forgiveness and Income-
```
