# HONORING THE MEMORY OF LETIZIA TAGLIAFIERRO

**Speaking:** Molinaro, Marcus J. (M001221) [R-NY]

```
              HONORING THE MEMORY OF LETIZIA TAGLIAFIERRO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MARCUS J. MOLINARO

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 1, 2023

  Mr. MOLINARO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of 
Letizia Tagliafierro who passed away on May 23rd of this year. Letizia 
was a devoted public servant and native of Saugerties, New York, who 
will be dearly missed by all who knew her.
  Letizia will long be remembered for her dedication to public service. 
Throughout her career, she worked tirelessly for the people of New York 
in the NY State Assembly, Erie County District Attorney's Office, and 
as NYS Inspector General.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues in the House join me in 
honoring the memory of Letizia Tagliafierro. While we mourn her 
passing, we celebrate her life and the legacy she leaves behind. May 
her life and memory always be a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
