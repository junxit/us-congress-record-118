# RECOGNIZING KATHERINE MERRITT FOR HER ACCEPTANCE TO THE UNITED STATES MILITARY ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Allen, Rick W. (A000372) [R-GA]

```
 RECOGNIZING KATHERINE MERRITT FOR HER ACCEPTANCE TO THE UNITED STATES 
                            MILITARY ACADEMY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RICK W. ALLEN

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 1, 2023

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate my constituent, 
Katherine Merritt, upon her acceptance to the United States Military 
Academy at West Point.
  As a future cadet, she will be joining the ranks of the world's most 
fearsome fighting force--the United States Army--guided by duty, honor, 
and country.
  The service and sacrifice of America's military personnel have 
safeguarded our Nation since its inception, defending our way of life 
from Lexington to Normandy.

[[Page E512]]

  And throughout our history, American soldiers have demonstrated 
tremendous courage and strength, beating back tyranny to the ends of 
the Earth.
  I was glad to sponsor Katherine's nomination and I am confident that 
her talents will distinguish her among her peers both during her time 
at West Point and thereafter.
  Congratulations to Katherine. I know that she will make this Nation 
proud.

                          ____________________
```
