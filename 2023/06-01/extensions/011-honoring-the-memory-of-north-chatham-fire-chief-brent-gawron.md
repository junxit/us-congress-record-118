# HONORING THE MEMORY OF NORTH CHATHAM FIRE CHIEF BRENT GAWRON

**Speaking:** Molinaro, Marcus J. (M001221) [R-NY]

```
      HONORING THE MEMORY OF NORTH CHATHAM FIRE CHIEF BRENT GAWRON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MARCUS J. MOLINARO

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 1, 2023

  Mr. MOLINARO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of Brent 
Gawron who passed away suddenly on May 14th of this year. Brent was a 
devoted father, husband, and Fire Chief of North Chatham, New York. He 
will be dearly missed by all who knew him.
  Brent will long be remembered for his contributions to the town of 
North Chatham. A true leader, he spent his life serving his community. 
Together with his wife, Erinn, he built a strong and loving family.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues in the House join me in 
honoring the memory of Brent Gawron. While we mourn his passing, we 
celebrate his life and the legacy he leaves behind. May his life and 
memory always be a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
