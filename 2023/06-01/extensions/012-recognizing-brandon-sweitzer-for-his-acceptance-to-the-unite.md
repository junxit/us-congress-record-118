# RECOGNIZING BRANDON SWEITZER FOR HIS ACCEPTANCE TO THE UNITED STATES AIR FORCE ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Allen, Rick W. (A000372) [R-GA]

```
 RECOGNIZING BRANDON SWEITZER FOR HIS ACCEPTANCE TO THE UNITED STATES 
                           AIR FORCE ACADEMY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RICK W. ALLEN

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, June 1, 2023

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate my constituent, 
Brandon Sweitzer, upon his acceptance to the United States Air Force 
Academy.
  As a future cadet, he will be joining the ranks of the world's most 
fearsome fighting force, the United States Air Force.
  Guided by the motto, ``Aim High . . . Fly-Fight-Win,'' the U.S. Air 
Force is the world's preeminent force in air, space, and cyberspace.
  Throughout our history, American airmen have demonstrated tremendous 
courage and strength, beating back tyranny to the ends of the Earth.
  I was honored to sponsor Brandon's nomination and I am confident that 
his talents will distinguish him among his peers both during his time 
at the Air Force Academy and thereafter.
  Congratulations to Brandon. I know that he will make this Nation 
proud.

                          ____________________
```
