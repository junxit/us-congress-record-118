# HONORING TARRANT COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR G.K. MAENIUS

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
           HONORING TARRANT COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR G.K. MAENIUS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 6, 2023

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker Pro Tempore, I rise today to recognize 
G.K. Maenius, long-time Tarrant County Administrator, embodies what it 
means to selflessly serve your community. Over his 35-year tenure as 
County Administrator, G.K. Maenius guided Tarrant County through 
numerous challenges, four county judges, and a doubling in population 
from 1.1 million in 1988 to 2.2 million today.
  With a staff over 4,600 and operating budget of $900 million, he 
masterfully managed personnel and taxpayer dollars, creating a county 
with a reputation marked by efficiency and fiscal responsibility. The 
years he spent supporting businesses, spearheading bond programs, and 
modernizing county technology have not gone unnoticed
  I thank G.K for consistent commitment to the welfare of our 
community. I wish him the best in his well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
