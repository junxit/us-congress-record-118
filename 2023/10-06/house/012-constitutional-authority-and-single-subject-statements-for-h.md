# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 5895

**Measures:** H.R. 5895

```
           By Ms. ADAMS:
       H.R. 5895.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The HBCU Arts Act will increase investment in HBCU arts 
     education, ensuring students of color access to affordable 
     arts and culture programs designed to increase the 
     conservation, study, and display of works by Black artists.
```
