# RIO SAN JOSE AND RIO JEMEZ WATER SETTLEMENTS ACT OF 2023

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S. 595

```
        RIO SAN JOSE AND RIO JEMEZ WATER SETTLEMENTS ACT OF 2023

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Madam President, today, I placed a hold on S. 595, the 
Rio San Jose and Rio Jemez Water Settlements Act of 2023. The 
legislation is not paid for and would violate multiple budget 
enforcement rules. According to the Congressional Budget Office, the 
bill would increase the deficit by $1.7 billion.
```
