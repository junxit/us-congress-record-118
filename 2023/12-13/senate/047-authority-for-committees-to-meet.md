# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I have four requests for committees to 
meet during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of 
the Majority and Minority Leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


           COMMITTEE ON COMMERCE, SCIENCE, AND TRANSPORTATION

  The Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation is authorized 
to meet during the session of the Senate on Wednesday, December 13, 
2023, at 2:30 p.m., to conduct a subcommittee hearing.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Wednesday, December 13, 2023, at 10 a.m., to 
conduct a hearing on nominations.


                       COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The Committee on the Judiciary is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Wednesday, December 13, 2023, at 3 p.m., to 
conduct a hearing.


                    SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  The Select Committee on Intelligence is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Wednesday, December 13, 2023, at 2:30 p.m., to 
conduct a closed briefing.

                          ____________________
```
