# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6751

**Measures:** H.R. 6751

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 6751.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       section 5 of Amendment XIV to the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint 
     commemorative coins in recognition of the life and legacy of 
     Roberto Clemente.
```
