# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6772

**Measures:** H.R. 6772

```
            By Ms. TENNEY:
        H.R. 6772.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       It would allow individuals that took at least one year out 
     of the workforce without receiving an earned income, for the 
     purposes of caring for a family member, to make catch-up 
     contributions in years prior to age 50 to their 401(k) plans, 
     individual retirement accounts (IRAs), and other eligible 
     retirement accounts.
```
