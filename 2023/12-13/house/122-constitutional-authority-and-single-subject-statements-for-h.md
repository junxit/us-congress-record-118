# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6770

**Measures:** H.R. 6770

```
            By Mrs. SYKES:
        H.R. 6770.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act 
     to ensure the safety of infant and toddler food by requiring 
     manufacturers or food processors to sample and test for 
     contaminants in infant and toddler food.
```
