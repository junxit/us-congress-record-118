# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6754

**Measures:** H.R. 6754

```
           By Mrs. HAYES:
       H.R. 6754.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the establishment of a comprehensive school-
     based violence prevention program to assist youth at highest 
     risk for involvement in gun violence in local communities and 
     schools, and for other purposes.
```
