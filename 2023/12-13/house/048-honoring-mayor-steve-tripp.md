# HONORING MAYOR STEVE TRIPP

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                       HONORING MAYOR STEVE TRIPP

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Steve Tripp 
for his 30 years of service to the residents of the town of Ayden, 
North Carolina, including the past 20 years as mayor.
  Mayor Tripp has been instrumental in addressing critical housing 
needs and growth while reducing the town's utility rates. He is also 
known for his faith.
  Mayor Tripp's dedication has set up the town of Ayden for decades and 
decades of success, and we are so grateful for him and his many 
contributions to eastern North Carolina.
  I extend my most profound blessings to him and his wife, Susan.

                          ____________________
```
