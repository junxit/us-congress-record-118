# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6769

**Measures:** H.R. 6769

```
            By Ms. STEFANIK:
        H.R. 6769.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require all companies identified under the Holding 
     Foreign Companies Accountable Act to retain independent 
     auditors to ensure they are fully compliant with U.S. law.
```
