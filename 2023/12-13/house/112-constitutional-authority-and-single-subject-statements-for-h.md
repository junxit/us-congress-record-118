# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6760

**Measures:** H.R. 6760

```
           By Mr. LUETKEMEYER:
       H.R. 6760.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority on which this bill is based is 
     Congress's power under the Spending Clause in Article 1, 
     Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit United States contributions to the 
     intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change, the United Nations 
     Framework Convention on Climate Change, and the Green Climate 
     Fund.
```
