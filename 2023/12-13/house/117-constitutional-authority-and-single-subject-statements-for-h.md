# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6765

**Measures:** H.R. 6765

```
            By Ms. PORTER:
        H.R. 6765.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To create a coordinated domestic wildlife disease 
     surveillance framework for State, Tribal, and local 
     governments to monitor and respond to wildlife disease 
     outbreaks to prevent pandemics, and for other purposes.
```
