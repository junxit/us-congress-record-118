# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6737

**Measures:** H.R. 6737

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 6737.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces, as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the Air Force to develop a force 
     design for the Air Force and Space Force.
```
