# RECOGNIZING MRS. JENNIFER POOLE, CONSTITUENT SERVICES REPRESENTATIVE, ON THE OCCASION OF HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Loudermilk, Barry (L000583) [R-GA]

```
 RECOGNIZING MRS. JENNIFER POOLE, CONSTITUENT SERVICES REPRESENTATIVE, 
                   ON THE OCCASION OF HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BARRY LOUDERMILK

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 3, 2023

  Mr. LOUDERMILK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to Mrs. 
Jennifer Poole, on the occasion of her retirement after 20 years of 
service to the United States House of Representatives.
  Mrs. Poole began her career as a single mom on March 3, 2003, with 
the Office of Congressman John Linder (GA-07), and continued her 
service with the Office of Congressman Tom Price (GA-06) and then the 
Office of Congresswoman Karen Handel (GA-06). Mrs. Poole has been an 
invaluable member of my staff since 2017. She has worked cases related 
to every federal agency through the years, but excelled in working with 
Social Security, Internal Revenue Service, and Medicare cases. She has 
successfully completed thousands of cases, with immeasurable benefits 
being returned to our 11th Congressional District constituents. Guided 
by her faith, Jennifer has made a significant difference to Georgia 
constituents during some of their most challenging times.
  Jennifer serves with uncommon zeal and tenacity; but more than that, 
her compassion and kindness are extraordinary. She exemplifies the very 
best of public service, and is an inspiration for us all. It is because 
of her dedication and impact to the community, that she will be greatly 
missed by our office and constituents. We wish her nothing but the best 
in retirement, allowing her to spend more time with her husband Scott, 
and children, Frankie and Mollie. I urge my colleagues to join me in 
recognizing Mrs. Poole for her 20 years of exemplary service.
  I thank Jennifer. God speed.

                          ____________________
```
