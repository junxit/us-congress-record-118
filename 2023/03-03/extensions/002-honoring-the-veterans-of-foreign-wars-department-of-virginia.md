# HONORING THE VETERANS OF FOREIGN WARS DEPARTMENT OF VIRGINIA ON THEIR CENTENNIAL CELEBRATION

**Speaking:** Spanberger, Abigail Davis (S001209) [D-VA]

```
 HONORING THE VETERANS OF FOREIGN WARS DEPARTMENT OF VIRGINIA ON THEIR 
                         CENTENNIAL CELEBRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ABIGAIL DAVIS SPANBERGER

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 3, 2023

  Ms. SPANBERGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Veterans 
of Foreign Wars Department of Virginia on their centennial celebration 
on March 4, 2023.
  For 100 years, the Virginia VFW has worked hard to protect and fight 
for our veterans to ensure that they receive the benefits that they 
have earned and deserve. They support veterans, servicemembers, and 
their families by providing opportunities through education, 
scholarships, and living assistance.
  I thank the VFW for their relentless advocacy and support of those 
who sacrificed for our country.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating the Veterans of Foreign 
Wars Department of Virginia on 100 years of service to our veterans and 
to making the Commonwealth of Virginia a better place for our veterans 
and their families.

                          ____________________
```
