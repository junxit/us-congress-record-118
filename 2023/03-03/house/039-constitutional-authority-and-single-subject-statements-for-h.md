# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1362

**Measures:** H.R. 1362

```
           By Mr. PFLUGER:
       H.R. 1362.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of the Interior and the Secretary 
     of Agriculture from issuing a moratorium on issuing new oil 
     and gas leases and drill permits on certain Federal lands.
```
