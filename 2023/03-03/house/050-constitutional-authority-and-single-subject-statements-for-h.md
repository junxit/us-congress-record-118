# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1373

**Measures:** H.R. 1373

```
           By Mr. STEWART:
       H.R. 1373.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit any head of a Federal agency from providing 
     assistance to the Wuhan Institute of Virology and other 
     laboratories in certain other foreign nations.
```
