# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1336

**Measures:** H.R. 1336

```
            By Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania:
        H.R. 1336.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to modify and 
     reform rules relating to investigations and whistleblowers, 
     and for other purposes.
```
