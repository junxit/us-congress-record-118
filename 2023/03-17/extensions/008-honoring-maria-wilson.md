# HONORING MARIA WILSON

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                         HONORING MARIA WILSON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 17, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Maria Wilson for her 
dedication to Kentucky's Third District.
  As a graduate of Family Scholar House, Maria continues to work hard 
to ensure the organization can help women in need and get support to 
succeed in life. She continues to serve her community by uplifting 
those in Louisville who need it most. I thank Maria for her commitment 
to Louisvillians.

                          ____________________
```
