# HONORING JACKIE FLOYD

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                         HONORING JACKIE FLOYD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 17, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Jackie Floyd as part 
of Women's History Month.
  As a civil rights activist and icon, Jackie Floyd has been a lifelong 
advocate for the Russell neighborhood and its community members. She 
has inspired generations of Louisvillians to get engaged and come 
together to work toward a common goal. Jackie is a steadfast champion 
of equality and I thank her for her tremendous dedication to 
Louisville.

                          ____________________
```
