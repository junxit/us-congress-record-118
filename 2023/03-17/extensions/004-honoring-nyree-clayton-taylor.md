# HONORING NYREE CLAYTON-TAYLOR

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                     HONORING NYREE CLAYTON-TAYLOR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 17, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Nyree Clayton-Taylor 
for her steadfast service to our community.
  Nyree is the creator of the Hip Hop N2 Learning Real Young Prodigy's 
Program, empowering students to use hip hop to create change that 
impacts the lives of Black children across Kentucky. She was also named 
the 2019 Kentucky Elementary Teacher of the Year. Nyree's dedication to 
bring about change and impact young lives is inspiring, and I thank her 
for all she does for Louisville.

                          ____________________
```
