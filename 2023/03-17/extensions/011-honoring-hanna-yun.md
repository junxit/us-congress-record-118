# HONORING HANNA YUN

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                           HONORING HANNA YUN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 17, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Hanna Yun for her 
service to Louisville and the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
  Having grown up in Korea, Hanna is a strong voice for Korean and 
immigrant communities in Kentucky's Third District. She is currently 
serving her second term as the President of the Korean American 
Association of Kentuckiana, and is an activist, volunteer, and leader 
in outreach. I thank Hanna for her commitment to Louisville's immigrant 
communities.

                          ____________________
```
