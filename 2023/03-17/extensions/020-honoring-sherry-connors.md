# HONORING SHERRY CONNORS

**Speaking:** McGarvey, Morgan (M001220) [D-KY]

```
                        HONORING SHERRY CONNORS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MORGAN McGARVEY

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 17, 2023

  Mr. McGARVEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Sherry Connors as 
part of Women's History Month.
  As the first female elected Mayor of Shively in Jefferson County, 
Sherry has broken down barriers for women in local politics across 
Kentucky. She has helped break down barriers and serves as an 
inspiration to many. I thank Sherry for her service to our community 
and her work as a dedicated public servant.

                          ____________________
```
