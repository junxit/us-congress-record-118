# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1656

**Measures:** H.R. 1656

```
           By Mr. SMUCKER:
       H.R. 1656.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Artile I, Section 8, Clause I of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a federal tax credit for chartible donations 
     by individual and corporate taxpayers to qualifying non-
     profit organizations providing workforce development, 
     apprenticeship programs, career and technical education, and 
     other workforce training.
```
