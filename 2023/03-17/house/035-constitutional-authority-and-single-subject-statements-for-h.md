# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1634

**Measures:** H.R. 1634

```
            By Mr. JOHNSON of Ohio:
        H.R. 1634.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to claise 7 of Rule XII of the rules of the House 
     of Representatives, the following statement is submitted 
     regarding the specific powers granted to Congress in the 
     Constitution to enact the accompanying bill or joint 
     resolution. Congress has the power to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to the following: Article I, Section 8 of the 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill adds podiatrists as covered physicians under the 
     Medicaid program and revises certain documentation 
     requirements related to Medicare coverage of therapeutic 
     shoes for individuals with diabetes.
```
