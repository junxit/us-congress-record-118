# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1654

**Measures:** H.R. 1654

```
           By Mr. SCHIFF:
       H.R. 1654.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Consitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to impose a 
     higher rate of tax on bonuses and profits from sales of stock 
     received by executives employed by failing banks that were 
     closed and for which the Federal Deposit Insurance 
     Corporation has been appointed as conservator or receiver.
```
