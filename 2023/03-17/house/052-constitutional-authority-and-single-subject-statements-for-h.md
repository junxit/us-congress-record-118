# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1651

**Measures:** H.R. 1651

```
           By Mr. PHILLIPS:
       H.R. 1651.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec. 8, cl. 3 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Small Business Act to require the 
     Small Business Administration to establish a registration 
     system for 7(a) loan agents.
```
