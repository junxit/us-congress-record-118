# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 1618

**Measures:** H.R. 1618

```
            By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
        H.R. 1618.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       the establishment of a single commission to study 
     sustainability of the Medicare and Social Security programs.
```
