# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6403

**Measures:** H.R. 6403

```
           By Mr. GAETZ:
       H.R. 6403.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec. 8, Cl. 1, Spending Clause--amending extent law 
     (FERPA), pursuant to the same.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       amending FERPA to enable disclosure of certain student 
     educational records to parents.
```
