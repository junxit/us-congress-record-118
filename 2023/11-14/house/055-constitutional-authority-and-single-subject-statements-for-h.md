# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6411

**Measures:** H.R. 6411

```
           By Mr. MAGAZINER:
       H.R. 6411.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       A bill to encourage the research and use of innovative 
     materials and associated techniques in the construction and 
     preservation of the domestic transportation and water 
     infrastructure system, and for other purposes.
```
