# TRIBUTE TO NOAH McNAUGHTON

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO NOAH McNAUGHTON

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Noah McNaughton, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has 
done on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Mr. 
McNaughton is a graduate of Lyman High School in Presho, SD. Currently, 
he is attending Dakota State University, where he studies cyber 
leadership and intelligence. Mr. McNaughton is a dedicated and diligent 
individual who has been devoted to getting the most out of his 
internship experience. Mr. McNaughton has been a true asset to my 
office. I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Mr. McNaughton 
for all of the work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
