# TRIBUTE TO LITAMARIE STANTON

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO LITAMARIE STANTON

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Litamarie Stanton, the 
Citrus County Teacher of the Year from Citrus High School in Inverness, 
FL.
  From an early age, Lita wanted to help children and be a teacher. 
Lita designs her lesson plans to ensure her students have a successful 
and enjoyable high school experience. Teaching is not a job she takes 
for granted; she knows she can change lives. She also enjoys working 
with other teachers whose classroom she once sat in, finding it to be 
an incredible experience.
  For the past 7 years, Lita has taught algebra I inclusion and honors 
to 9th and 10th grade students at Citrus High

[[Page S5505]]

School. Prior to that, she taught math at Inverness Middle School for 6 
years. Lita is also involved in school activities, serving as 
homecoming and prom coordinator, leading the student government, 
varsity cheer coach, girls' tennis coach, and graduation coordinator.
  I extend my deepest gratitude and best wishes to Lita for her 
commitment to her students. I look forward to hearing about her 
continued good work in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
