# TRIBUTE TO AMELIA MULLIN

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO AMELIA MULLIN

● Mr. ROUNDS. Mr. President, today I recognize Amelia Mullin, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has done 
on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Ms. Mullin is a 
graduate of Forest Charter School in Truckee, CA. Currently, she is 
attending American University, where she is majoring in international 
studies. Ms. Mullin is a dedicated and diligent individual who has been 
devoted to getting the most out of her internship experience. Ms. 
Mullin has been a true asset to my office. I extend my sincere thanks 
and appreciation to Ms. Mullin for all of the work she has done and 
wish her continued success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
