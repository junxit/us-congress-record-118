# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2023

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 43

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 2023

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 10 a.m. 
on Wednesday, November 15; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; further, 
that if a motion to proceed to Calendar No. 244, S.J. Res. 43, is made, 
the motion be agreed to and that at 2:30 p.m. the joint resolution be 
considered read a third time and the Senate vote on passage of the 
joint resolution.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
