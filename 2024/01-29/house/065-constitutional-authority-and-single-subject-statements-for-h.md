# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7110

**Measures:** H.R. 7110

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 7110.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       section 5 of Amendment XIV to the Constitution.

       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to require
```
