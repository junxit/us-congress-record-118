# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7114

**Measures:** H.R. 7114

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 7114.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would provide funding to states to establish and 
     expand prekindergarten programs in public and public charter 
     schools for three- and four-year-old children.
```
