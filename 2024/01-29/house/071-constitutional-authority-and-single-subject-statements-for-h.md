# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7116

**Measures:** H.R. 7116

```
           By Mr. PALLONE:
       H.R. 7116.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3: [The Congrss shall 
     have Power] To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several states, and with the Indian Tribes;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To strengthen certain provisions relating to restrictions 
     on robocalls and telemarketing, and for other purposes.
```
