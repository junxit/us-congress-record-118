# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1633

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1633

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1633

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 1633, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Johnson of Ohio, for the purposes of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
```
