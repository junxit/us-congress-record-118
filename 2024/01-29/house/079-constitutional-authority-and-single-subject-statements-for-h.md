# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7124

**Measures:** H.R. 7124

```
           By Mr. SOTO:
       H.R. 7124.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Communications Act of 1934 to direct the 
     Federal Communications Commission to promulgate regulations 
     that require providers of voice service to offer a robocall-
     blocking service at no additional charge to the customer, and 
     for other purposes.
```
