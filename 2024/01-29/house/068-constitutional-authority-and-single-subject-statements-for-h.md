# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7113

**Measures:** H.R. 7113

```
           By Mr. MOSKOWITZ:
       H.R. 7113.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 3(d)(1) of rule XIII of the Rules of the

     Constitution.
```
