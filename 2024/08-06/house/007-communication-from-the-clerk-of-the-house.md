# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 120, S. 211, S. 2781, S. 3938, S. 4199, S. 4235, S. 4367

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington DC, August 2, 2024.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on August 2, 2024, at 12:52 
     p.m.
       That the Senate passed S. 211.
       That the Senate passed S. 2781.
       That the Senate passed S. 3938.
       That the Senate passed S. 4199.
       That the Senate passed S. 4235.
       That the Senate passed S. 4367.
       That the Senate agreed to without amendment H. Con. Res. 
     120.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                                    Lisa P. Grant,
     Deputy Clerk.

                          ____________________
```
