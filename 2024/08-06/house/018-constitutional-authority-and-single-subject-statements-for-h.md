# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9309

**Measures:** H.R. 9309

```
           By Mr. HILL:
       H.R. 9309.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for regulatory sandboxes that permit certain 
     persons to experiment with artificial intelligence without 
     expectation of enforcement actions.
```
