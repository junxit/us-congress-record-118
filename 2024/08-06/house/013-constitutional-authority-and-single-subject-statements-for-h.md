# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9304

**Measures:** H.R. 9304

```
           By Ms. CARAVEO:
       H.R. 9304.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Constitutional Authority--Necessary and Proper Clause (Art. 
     I, Sec. 8, Clause 18)
       THE U.S. CONSTITUTION
       ARTICLE I, SECTION 8: POWERS OF CONGRESS
       CLAUSE 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of the National Cancer Institute to 
     conduct a review of the current state of stomach cancer 
     incidence, prevention, screening, awareness, and future 
     public health importance, and for other purposes.
```
