# REMEMBERING DAVID STEIN

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
                        REMEMBERING DAVID STEIN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 6, 2024

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, today, we remember David A. Stein, 
who passed away on July 27, 2024, at the age of 85. A Jacksonville 
native, David spent his entire life in the city he loved, leaving an 
indelible mark on his community as an entrepreneur, business leader, 
and philanthropist. Known for his leadership at Burger King and 
Applebee's franchises, David also co-founded T.L. Cannon Companies. He 
passionately supported numerous educational and community 
organizations, including the Jewish Community Alliance, Greenwood 
School, and River Garden. David, alongside his devoted wife Linda, 
championed education and mentorship through initiatives like the Stein 
Fellowship. His generosity extended to many causes, enhancing the 
quality of life in Jacksonville. David's warmth, laughter, and 
boundless energy endeared him to all who knew him. As we celebrate his 
life, we honor his commitment to making a difference and his unwavering 
dedication to his beloved Jacksonville.
```
