# RECOGNIZING MARLEY LINGAR

**Speaking:** Westerman, Bruce (W000821) [R-AR]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MARLEY LINGAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BRUCE WESTERMAN

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 6, 2024

  Mr. WESTERMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend the 
exceptional accomplishments and aspirations of Ms. Marley Lingar, a 
dedicated student at Cass Job Corps, within the bounds of our esteemed 
Fourth Congressional District. Marley is currently enrolled in the 
prestigious Forestry Conservation and Firefighting Program, where she 
demonstrates remarkable leadership and commitment.
  Marley holds the position of vice president in her dormitory at Cass, 
showcasing her ability to lead and inspire her peers. She also 
participates in various student-led organizations on campus, including 
the Student Government Association, where she actively contributes to 
the betterment of her community through service and civic engagement. 
Marley's leadership and efforts to make a positive difference are truly 
commendable. She lives by the words of her instructor, Mr. Wade Kelly, 
who encourages students to ``learn to push past what you thought were 
your limits.'' This mantra has clearly guided Marley in achieving 
significant milestones.
  Recently, Marley passed the rigorous Pack Test, qualifying her to 
join firefighting teams. This achievement is particularly noteworthy as 
she is the first female in over a decade to join the fire program at 
Cass Job Corps. Her grit and determination are evident, and her success 
paves the way for future generations of women in firefighting. Growing 
up in the close-knit Ozark community, Marley has a deep-rooted desire 
to contribute positively to her hometown. She is driven by the goals of 
making her family proud and having a lasting impact on the community 
she holds dear. Post-graduation, Marley aspires to pursue advanced 
training for a dispatcher role in forestry, further demonstrating her 
commitment to public service and her passion for the field.
  Marley's accomplishments and aspirations serve as an inspiration to 
us all. Her story is a testament to the power of perseverance, 
leadership, and community spirit. I am confident that she will continue 
to achieve great things and make significant contributions to her field 
and her community.
  I ask my esteemed colleagues to join me in recognizing and 
celebrating Marley Lingar for her achievements and wishing her 
continued success as she embarks on the next chapter of her journey. 
Marley is truly a testament to the limitless potential of our Nation's 
youth, and I have no doubt that she will continue to make us proud in 
her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
