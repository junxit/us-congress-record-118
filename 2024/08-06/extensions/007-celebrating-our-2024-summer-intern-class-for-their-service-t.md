# CELEBRATING OUR 2024 SUMMER INTERN CLASS FOR THEIR SERVICE TO CALIFORNIA'S 20TH DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
     CELEBRATING OUR 2024 SUMMER INTERN CLASS FOR THEIR SERVICE TO 
                       CALIFORNIA'S 20TH DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 6, 2024

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge and celebrate the 
remarkable interns who have been working in service of the 20th 
District of California this summer. These exceptional young 
individuals--who joined us in our Washington, D.C. office and our 
Clovis, California office--have shown our team their energy, 
commitment, and strong drive to effect positive change. As our nation 
navigates uncertain times and many young people feel out of touch with 
public service, their enthusiasm and resolve give us great hope for the 
future.
  With their summer in Congress reaching its close, I want to extend my 
heartfelt thanks to Jacob Cohen, Gabriella Gutierrez, and Ayush 
Nallapally for their work in our Washington, D.C. office, and Luca 
Fanucchi and Braden McConaghy for their service in our Clovis, 
California office. Their hard work and dedication have been invaluable, 
and I hope that the experiences gained here will serve them well in 
then future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
