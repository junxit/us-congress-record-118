# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7451

**Measures:** H.R. 7451

```
            By Ms. TLAIB:
        H.R. 7451.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 1 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill establishes a pilot program to provide financial 
     and nonfinancial housing assistance to certain homeless 
     individuals, to provide for a study of the effects of the 
     pilot program, and for other purposes.
```
