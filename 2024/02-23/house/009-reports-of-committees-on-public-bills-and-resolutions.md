# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 4396, H.R. 4984, H.R. 5527, H.R. 7102, H.R. 7103, H.R. 7104, H.R. 7129

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     7102. A bill to establish an Office of Native American 
     Affairs within the Small Business Administration, and for 
     other purposes (Rept. 118-394). Referred to the Committee of 
     the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. STEIL: Committee on House Administration. H.R. 4396. A 
     bill to amend the Help America Vote Act of 2002 to prohibit 
     noncitizen voting in District of Columbia elections, and for 
     other purposes (Rept. 118-395). Referred to the Committee of 
     the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     7103. A bill to amend the Small Business Act to require 
     Federal agencies to testify and report on scores received 
     under the scorecard program for evaluating Federal agency 
     compliance with small business contracting goals, to testify 
     for failure to meet Governmentwide contracting goals, and for 
     other purposes (Rept. 118-396). Referred to the Committee of 
     the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. COMER: Committee on Oversight and Accountability. H.R. 
     5527. A bill to amend section 1078 of the National Defense 
     Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2018 to increase the 
     effectiveness of the Technology Modernization Fund, and for 
     other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 118-397). Referred 
     to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the 
     Union.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     7104. A bill to require the National Small Business 
     Development Center Advisory Board to submit an annual report 
     on the activities of the Board and other information, and for 
     other purposes (Rept. 118-398). Referred to the Committee of 
     the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 
     7129. A bill to amend the Small Business Act to establish the 
     Office of Whistleblower Awards, and for other purposes (Rept. 
     118-399). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 4984. A 
     bill to amend the District of Columbia Stadium Act of 1957 to 
     provide for the transfer of administrative jurisdiction over 
     the Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium Campus to the 
     Administrator of General Services and the leasing of the 
     Campus to the District of Columbia for purposes which include 
     commercial and residential development, and for other 
     purposes; with amendments (Rept. 118-400 Pt. 1). Referred to 
     the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. COMER: Committee on Oversight and Accountability. H.R. 
     4984. A bill to amend the District of Columbia Stadium Act of 
     1957 to provide for the transfer of administrative 
     jurisdiction over the Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium 
     Campus to the Administrator of General Services and the 
     leasing of the Campus to the District of Columbia for 
     purposes which include commercial and residential 
     development, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 
     118-400 Pt. 2). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the state of the Union.


                         DISCHARGE OF COMMITTEE

       Pursuant to clause 2 of rule XIII, the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure discharged from further 
     consideration. H.R. 4984 referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
```
