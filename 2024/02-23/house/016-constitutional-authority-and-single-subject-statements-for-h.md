# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7435

**Measures:** H.R. 7435

```
            By Mr. FOSTER:
        H.R. 7435.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the 
     United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the IRS to establish a program to allow 
     taxpayers to download forms relating to individual income tax 
     returns that are pre-populated with return information 
     reported to the IRS.
```
