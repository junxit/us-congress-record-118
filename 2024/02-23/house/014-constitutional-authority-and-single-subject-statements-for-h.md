# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7433

**Measures:** H.R. 7433

```
            By Mr. D'ESPOSITO:
        H.R. 7433.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Homeland Security--To amend the Homeland Security Act of 
     2002 to establish a transnational repression hotline and 
     conduct a transnational repression public service 
     announcement campaign, and for other purposes.
```
