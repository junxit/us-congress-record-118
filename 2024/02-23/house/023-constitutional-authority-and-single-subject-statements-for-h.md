# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7442

**Measures:** H.R. 7442

```
            By Mr. MOYLAN:
        H.R. 7442.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article one of the United States Constitution 
     Congress has the power to enact this legislation.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Medicare Portability Study Act directs GAO to produce a 
     study on the cost-savings of Medicare coverage for U.S. 
     citizens in the Philippines.
```
