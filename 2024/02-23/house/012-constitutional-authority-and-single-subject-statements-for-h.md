# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7431

**Measures:** H.R. 7431

```
            By Mr. ARRINGTON:
        H.R. 7431.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill holds state welfare agencies who receive TANF 
     funds accountable to the same statutes governing improper 
     payments that federal agencies are held to.
```
