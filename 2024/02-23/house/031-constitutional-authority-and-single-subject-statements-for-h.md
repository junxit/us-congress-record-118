# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7450

**Measures:** H.R. 7450

```
            By Ms. STEFANIK:
        H.R. 7450.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit payment card networks and covered entities from 
     requiring the use of or assigning merchant category codes 
     that distinguish a firearms retailer from general-merchandise 
     retailer or sporting-goods retailer
```
