# HONORING NADINE TAYLOR AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING NADINE TAYLOR AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                        CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, entrepreneur and hair care specialist, Nadine 
Taylor has been a pillar in the Kissimmee community for more than 26 
years.
  Originally from Manchester, Jamaica, Nadine moved from Boston to 
Kissimmee to pursue her dreams of owning her own hair care facility ``A 
person's hair is a vital part of their personality,'' she says. ``I 
love taking care of the needs of all of my clients.''
  Nadine specializes in a variety of hair textures, is a master 
colorist, and delights in creating hairstyles for her vast clientel. 
Nadine is gifted in making each client feel beautiful.
  Her philosophy is: When a person looks good, they feel good and can 
produce good things. Nadine is a wife, mother, and grandmother, and 
makes everyone feel as if they are family no matter where they come 
from.
```
