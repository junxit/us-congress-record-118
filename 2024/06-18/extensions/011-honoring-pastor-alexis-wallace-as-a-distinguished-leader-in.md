# HONORING PASTOR ALEXIS WALLACE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
  HONORING PASTOR ALEXIS WALLACE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                  FLORIDA FOR CARIBBEAN HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Pastor Alexis Wallace is a bridge builder. 
Throughout his ministry, he has been dedicated to developing uncommon 
and challenging relationships in a wide variety of areas.
  He is the founder and president of the Jewish Christian Network, 
which has members from every continent. Pastor Alexis is also the 
president and founder of Hagiazo Healing Outreach, a ministry with the 
unique mission of bridging the gap between faith and science to address 
sickness from a spiritual perspective.
  Pastor Alexis is the founder of Global Evangelistic Center, co-
pastoring this church in Kissimmee, Florida, with his wife, Evelina. 
The couple has two children a daughter, Alexia, who is a federal 
attorney, and a son, Sydney, a recent radiography graduate and 
practitioner.
  Global Evangelistic Center was at ground zero in Abaco, Bahamas, with 
a medical mission shortly after Hurricane Dorian devastated the island. 
GEC coordinated the mission with a team of doctors and much-needed 
medical supplies that impacted the lives of many.
  Pastor Alexis attended Oral Roberts University. He has been a 
publisher of newspapers and magazines, as well as the executive 
producer of international Christian TV programs that focused on Israel 
and the Caribbean and reached millions.
```
