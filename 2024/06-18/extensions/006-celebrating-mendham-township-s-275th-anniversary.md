# CELEBRATING MENDHAM TOWNSHIP'S 275TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
            CELEBRATING MENDHAM TOWNSHIP'S 275TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 18, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate and 
congratulate Mendham Township on their 275th anniversary. I am 
extremely grateful to be able to be here today and celebrate this 
incredible milestone with the Mendham Township community. Over the past 
275 years, Mendham Township has been forward-thinking in preserving the 
environment, creating a strong sense of community, and supporting 
excellence in education. For almost three centuries, the township has 
become home to families and retirees alike who enjoy parks, streams and 
playgrounds, annual events that date back decades, and award-winning 
schools. As we reflect on its history, we can use the past centuries as 
a reminder of Mendham Township's dedication to fostering a community 
loved by all. I am honored to have such an amazing township in the 7th 
Congressional District, and I look forward to what is to come.

                          ____________________
```
