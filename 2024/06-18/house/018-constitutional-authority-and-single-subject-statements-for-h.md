# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8775

**Measures:** H.R. 8775

```
           By Mr. CRENSHAW:
        H.R. 8775.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide Congress with an assessment on the ability of 
     critical infrastructure owners and operators to operate 
     critical systems in a manual operating mode during cyber 
     incidents.
```
