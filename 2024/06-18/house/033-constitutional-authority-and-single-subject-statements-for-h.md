# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8790

**Measures:** H.R. 8790

```
            By Mr. WESTERMAN:
        H.R. 8790.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To expedite under the National Environmental Policy Act of 
     1969 and improve forest management activities on National 
     Forest System lands, on public lands under the jurisdiction 
     of the Bureau of Land Management, and on Tribal lands to 
     return resilience to overgrown, fire-prone forested lands.
```
