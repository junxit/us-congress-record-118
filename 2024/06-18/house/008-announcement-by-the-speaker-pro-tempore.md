# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 1292, H.Res. 1293

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair would inform the House that, 
pursuant to H. Res. 1292 and H. Res. 1293, the Speaker has certified to 
the United States Attorney for the District of Columbia the refusal of 
Merrick B. Garland to produce documents, records, and materials to the 
Committees on the Judiciary and Oversight and Accountability as
```
