# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8784

**Measures:** H.R. 8784

```
            By Ms. MALOY:
        H.R. 8784.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require each agency to evaluate the permitting system of 
     the agency, to consider whether a permit by rule could 
     replace that system, and for other purposes.
```
