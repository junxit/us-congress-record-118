# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8778

**Measures:** H.R. 8778

```
           By Mr. CAREY:
        H.R. 8778.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To waive the fee for the issuance of a passport for a 
     family member of a member of the Armed Forces who is in a 
     hospital or medical facility abroad, and for other purposes.
```
