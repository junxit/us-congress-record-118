# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8786

**Measures:** H.R. 8786

```
            By Mr. MOYLAN:
        H.R. 8786.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1
       The single subject of this legislation is;
       To allow certain foreign air carriers to stop in Guam or 
     the Northern Mariana Islands in the course of transportation 
     of passengers or cargo in either direction between a place in 
     the United States and a place outside the United States, and 
     for other purposes.
```
