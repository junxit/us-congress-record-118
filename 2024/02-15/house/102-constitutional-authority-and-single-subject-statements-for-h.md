# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7394

**Measures:** H.R. 7394

```
       By Mr. TAKANO:
       H.R. 7394.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Ensure defendants have access to source code and other 
     information necessary to exercise their confrontational and 
     due process rights when algorithms are used to analyze 
     evidence in their cases.
```
