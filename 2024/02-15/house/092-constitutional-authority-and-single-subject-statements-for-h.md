# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7384

**Measures:** H.R. 7384

```
           By Mr. McCAUL:
       H.R. 7384.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Artile I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill reauthorizes a Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

     of drugs that treat rare pediatric diseases.
```
