# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7385

**Measures:** H.R. 7385

```
           By Ms. McCLELLAN:
       H.R. 7385.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United States 
     Constitution: ``Congress shall have the power . . . to 
     establish post offices and post roads.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill names a United States Post Office in Petersburg, 
     Virginia after John Mercer Langston, the first African-
     American elected to Congress from Virginia.
```
