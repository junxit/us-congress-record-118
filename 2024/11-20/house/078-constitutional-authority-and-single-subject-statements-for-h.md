# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10182

**Measures:** H.R. 10182

```
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER:
        H.R. 10182.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to waive the 10-
     percent additional tax on early distributions from qualified 
     retirement plans in the case of withdrawals for family 
     caregiving expenses.
```
