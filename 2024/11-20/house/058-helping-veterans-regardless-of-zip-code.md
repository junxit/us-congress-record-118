# HELPING VETERANS REGARDLESS OF ZIP CODE

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                HELPING VETERANS REGARDLESS OF ZIP CODE

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, during a Veterans Service 
Officer Roundtable held in my district earlier this year, a constituent 
expressed concern about the difficulty he and his fellow veterans had 
traveling to access healthcare. In response, we took action and 
introduced the Rural Veterans Transportation to Care Act, which expands 
the Department of Veterans Affairs' Highly Rural Transportation Grants 
program and helps veterans in remote areas travel to the VA or VA-
authorized healthcare facilities for their appointments.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge Congress to pass this essential legislation. We 
must help our veterans, regardless of their ZIP Code or crossroads.

                          ____________________
```
