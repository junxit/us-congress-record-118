# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10179

**Measures:** H.R. 10179

```
           By Ms. CARAVEO:
       H.R. 10179.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Constitutional Authority--Necessary and Proper Clause (Art. 
     I, Sec. 8, Clause 18)
       THE U.S. CONSTITUTION
       ARTICLE I, SECTION 8: POWERS OF CONGRESS
       CLAUSE 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to 
     award grants to States to develop and expand the 
     industrialized construction of innovative residential 
     dwelling units, and for other purposes.
```
