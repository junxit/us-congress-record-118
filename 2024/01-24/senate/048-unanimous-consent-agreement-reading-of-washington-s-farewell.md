# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--READING OF WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding the order of January 24, 1901, the traditional reading 
of Washington's Farewell Address take place on Monday, February 26, 
2024, following the prayer and pledge.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
