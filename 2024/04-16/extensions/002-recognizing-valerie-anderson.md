# RECOGNIZING VALERIE ANDERSON

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING VALERIE ANDERSON

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 16, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions of one of my constituents, Valerie Anderson, who 
has served as an active member of Premier Community HealthCare's 
Patient and Community Advisory Council since July 2021 and on the Board 
of Directors since January 2022. She brings a wealth of experience to 
the organization with her background as the former Director of Housing 
and Special Programs at the Pasco County Housing Authority as well as 
more than two decades working with the American Cancer Society. She is 
often the first person to step up to take a leadership role for Premier 
programs and events. For the past two years, Valerie has served as the 
Event Chair of Premier's Annual Community Impact Breakfast raising 
nearly $100,000 each year to support the organization's mission to 
expand accessible healthcare for all. Valerie is also a Premier patient 
and has family members and loved ones who have benefited from Premier's 
medical, dental and behavioral health services. She is a champion for 
advocating for the need for quality, affordable healthcare and 
understands the impact it makes on the overall health of our community. 
In addition to her volunteer work, she also financially supports the 
Federally-Qualified Community Health Center's events. Valerie is now 
the Chair of Premier's Community Engagement and Stewardship Board 
Committee. Under her leadership, Premier is going to continue to expand 
its reach to build additional resources. Valerie humbles herself and 
always finds time to volunteer for other local non-profit organizations 
who share similar visions and missions including: the African American 
Club, Dress for Success, and Africa's Children's Heart. She is always 
available to help those in need.
  It is clear that Ms. Anderson has made a profoundly positive impact 
on our community, and I am grateful for her service.

                          ____________________
```
