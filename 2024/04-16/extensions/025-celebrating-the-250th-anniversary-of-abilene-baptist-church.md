# CELEBRATING THE 250TH ANNIVERSARY OF ABILENE BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Allen, Rick W. (A000372) [R-GA]

```
      CELEBRATING THE 250TH ANNIVERSARY OF ABILENE BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RICK W. ALLEN

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 16, 2024

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 250th 
anniversary of the Abilene Baptist Church in Georgia's 12th District.
  Established near Augusta, Georgia and founded by the pioneering 
Baptist minister Daniel Marshall, the centuries-long history of Abilene 
continues to bring hope and unity of God's word to our communities.
  As we reflect on 250 years of faith, fellowship, and service, we also 
remember the journey that has brought us to this moment--through the 
many challenges, countless blessings, and cherished milestones.
  This anniversary is not just a marker of time, but a testament to the 
unwavering dedication and commitment of each member of this 
congregation.
  One of my favorite passages in the Bible is God's instructions to 
Joshua in Joshua 1:8: ``Do not let this book of law depart from your 
lips, be careful to do what it says, meditate on it day and night, and 
you will be prosperous and successful.''
  This anniversary is undoubtedly a celebration, but also a reminder 
that God has used this congregation in a powerful way and continues to 
do so.
  I express my deepest gratitude to those who have contributed to the 
years of success of Abilene Baptist Church. May God bless each and 
every one of them.

                          ____________________
```
