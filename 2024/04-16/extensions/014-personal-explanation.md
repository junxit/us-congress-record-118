  Ms. ESHOO. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to be present during Roll Call 
vote No. 120 through 123 on April 15, 2024 because my flight to

present: on Roll Call vote No. 120, I would have voted YES; on Roll 
Call vote No. 121, I would have voted YES; on Roll Call vote No. 122, I 
would have voted YES; and on Roll Call vote No. 123, I would have voted 
NO.

appeared: vote YES; on Roll Call vote No. 122, I would have votes 
YES; and on Roll Call vote No. 123, I would have voted NO.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: voted YES; on 
Roll Call vote No. 122, I would have voted YES; and on Roll Call 
vote No. 123, I would have voted NO.
