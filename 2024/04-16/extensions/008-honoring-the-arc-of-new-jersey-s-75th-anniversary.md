# HONORING THE ARC OF NEW JERSEY'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
           HONORING THE ARC OF NEW JERSEY'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 16, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my 
warmest congratulations to the Arc of New Jersey as they celebrate 
their 75th anniversary.
  For the past 75 years, the Arc of New Jersey has admirably supported 
individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities. They have 
made a significant impact by securing quality education and healthcare, 
equipping disabled individuals with vital workplace skills, and 
advocating for this community at all levels of government. The Arc of 
New Jersey has certainly improved the quality of life for thousands of 
New Jersey residents, and the Seventh District is both proud and 
grateful to have such an outstanding organization operating in our 
communities.
  I wish the Arc of New Jersey a wonderful 75th anniversary. I thank 
them again for their dedication and passion to making our communities 
safer, healthier, and more inclusive places to live and work. I have no 
doubt that this organization will continue to thrive in the future.

                          ____________________
```
