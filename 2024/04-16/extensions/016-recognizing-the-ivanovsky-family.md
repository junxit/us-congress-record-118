# RECOGNIZING THE IVANOVSKY FAMILY

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                    RECOGNIZING THE IVANOVSKY FAMILY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 16, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions made in our community by the lvanovsky Family. The 
Twistid Arts Initiative was born out of the desire to raise awareness 
of and increase access to all forms of visual and performing arts in 
Citrus County. Prior to the creation of this non-profit organization, 
youth in Citrus County had limited opportunities for exposure to 
successful arts-based careers. In response, this non-profit 
organization was developed to provide innovative programming, hands-on 
education, and immersive events throughout the community.
  The lvanovsky Family has jumped in to help at every Twistid Arts 
event that has been held to date. They are extremely dedicated to the 
cause and are willing to do even the most menial tasks to ensure events 
and programs run smoothly. Part of what makes our country so great is 
the willingness of ordinary Americans to go above and beyond to 
selflessly give back to others. Volunteerism is one of the cornerstones 
of our society and part of the fabric that unites us.
  It is clear that the Ivanovsky Family is making a profoundly positive 
impact on our community, and I am grateful for their service.
```
