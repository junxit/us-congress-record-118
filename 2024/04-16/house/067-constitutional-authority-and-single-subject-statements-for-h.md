# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8023

**Measures:** H.R. 8023

```
           By Ms. KELLY of Illinois:
       H.R. 8023
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Federal Trade Commission to include in the 
     report of the Commission under the Elder Abuse Prevention and 
     Prosecution Act certain information relating to scams 
     disproportionately impacting seniors during emergencies, and 
     for other purposes.
```
