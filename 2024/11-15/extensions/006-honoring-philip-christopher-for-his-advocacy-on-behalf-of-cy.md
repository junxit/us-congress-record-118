# HONORING PHILIP CHRISTOPHER FOR HIS ADVOCACY ON BEHALF OF CYPRUS AND HELLENIC ISSUES

**Speaking:** Malliotakis, Nicole (M000317) [R-NY]

```
 HONORING PHILIP CHRISTOPHER FOR HIS ADVOCACY ON BEHALF OF CYPRUS AND 
                            HELLENIC ISSUES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. NICOLE MALLIOTAKIS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 15, 2024

  Ms. MALLIOTAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and recognize Mr. 
Philip Christopher, a leader and steadfast advocate for freedom and 
justice for the Cypriot people. For over 50 years, Mr. Christopher has 
championed the cause of Cyprus, dedicating his life to the struggle for 
the reunification of Cyprus, the rights of the Cypriot people, and the 
broader Hellenic community.
  Mr. Christopher is the founder and President of the Pancyprian 
Association of America and the International Coordinating Committee 
``Justice for Cyprus'' (PSEKA). Through these organizations, he has 
tirelessly promoted Cypriot sovereignty and human rights, advocating on 
behalf of Cyprus and Hellenic issues with passion and dedication. As a 
refugee, who was forced to flee his beautiful homeland of Kyrenia, 
Cyprus following the illegal occupation of the island nation by the 
Turkish military in 1974, no one is more passionate and dedicated to 
these issues than he is. His efforts have left a lasting impact, as he 
has worked to ensure that the voice of the Greek Cypriot community is 
heard in the halls of Congress and across the United States.
  Philip Christopher's contributions have not gone unrecognized. He has 
received numerous honors, including the Republic of Cyprus Medal of 
Honor, the title of Honorary Mayor of Kyrenia, and honorary 
citizenships from multiple Cypriot communities. He was also the first 
Greek Cypriot to receive the prestigious Ellis Island Medal of Honor in 
the United States. In 2024, he served as Parade General Chairman of the 
NYC Greek Independence Day Parade. These awards and recognition are a 
testament to his exemplary commitment to his community and his 
invaluable role as a bridge between Cypriot and American interests.
  Beyond his advocacy, Mr. Christopher has demonstrated significant 
achievements as an executive in the telecommunications industry and as 
a board member of various respected organizations. His dedication to 
excellence in both his professional and community endeavors is an 
inspiration.
  Today, I commend Philip Christopher for his enduring dedication to 
Cyprus and the Hellenic community and express gratitude for his 
tireless efforts to further the cause of justice and freedom. His 
legacy stands as a beacon of leadership and commitment that strengthens 
the bonds between Cyprus and the United States.
```
