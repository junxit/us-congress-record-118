# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10136

**Measures:** H.R. 10136

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 10136.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article 1, Section 8, 
     Clause 18 of the Constitution of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would realign reimbursement for certain office-
     based interventions with high-cost supplies under the 
     Medicare Physician Fee Schedule.
```
