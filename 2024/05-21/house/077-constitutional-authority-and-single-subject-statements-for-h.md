# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8481

**Measures:** H.R. 8481

```
            By Mr. MAST:
        H.R. 8481.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would extend the Department of Veterans Affairs 
     emergency community care notification deadline from 72-hours 
     after emergency care starts, to 72-hours after discharge of 
     receiving such care.
```
