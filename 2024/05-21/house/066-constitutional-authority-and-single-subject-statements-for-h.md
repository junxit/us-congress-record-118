# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8470

**Measures:** H.R. 8470

```
           By Ms. BUSH:
       H.R. 8470.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services to 
     make awards to increase or improve access to comprehensive 
     mental and behavioral health services for individuals exposed 
     to violent encounters involving law enforcement personnel.
```
