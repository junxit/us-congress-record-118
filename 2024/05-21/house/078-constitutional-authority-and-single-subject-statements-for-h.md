# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8482

**Measures:** H.R. 8482

```
            By Mr. MOORE of Utah:
        H.R. 8482.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 7, Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will seek to deter conflict in the Taiwan Strait 
     by establishing a policy to revoke Permanent Normal Trade 
     Relations (``PNTR'') with the People's Republic of China 
     (``PRC'') if the PRC government invades Taiwan.
```
