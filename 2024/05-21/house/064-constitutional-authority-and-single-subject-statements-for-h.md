# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8468

**Measures:** H.R. 8468

```
           By Mr. YAKYM:
       H.R. 8468.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would prohibit students and faculty from 
     receiving federal student loan forgiveness if they are 
     expelled or terminated for a hate crime, disorderly conduct, 
     trespassing, creating a public disturbance, or violating 
     titles IV or VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964.
```
