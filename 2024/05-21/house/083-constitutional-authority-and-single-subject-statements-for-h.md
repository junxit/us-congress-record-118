# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8487

**Measures:** H.R. 8487

```
            By Mr. VAN DREW:
        H.R. 8487.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title II of the Social Security Act to exempt 
     widows and widowers of police officers, firefighters, and 
     correctional officers from the government pension offset.
```
