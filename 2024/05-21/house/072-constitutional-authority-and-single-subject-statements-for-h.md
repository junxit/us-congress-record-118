# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8476

**Measures:** H.R. 8476

```
           By Mr. CRAWFORD:
       H.R. 8476.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the Army to designate the Deputy 
     Under Secretary of the Army as principal official responsible 
     for explosive ordnance disposal.
```
