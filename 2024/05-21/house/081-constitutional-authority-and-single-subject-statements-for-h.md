# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8485

**Measures:** H.R. 8485

```
            By Mr. RASKIN:
        H.R. 8485.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 9, Clause 8: No Title of Nobility shall 
     be granted by the United States. And no Person holding any 
     Office of Profit or Trust under them, shall, without the 
     Consent of the Congress, accept of any present, Emolument, 
     Office, or Title, of any kind whatever, from any King, 
     Prince, or foreign State.
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       This legislation enforces the Constitution's Foreign 
     Emoluments Clause.
```
