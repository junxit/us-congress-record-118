# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 8369

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 8369

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 8369

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk that is due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill by 
title for the second time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 8369) to provide for the expeditious delivery 
     of defense articles and defense services for Israel and other 
     matters.

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, in order to place the bill on the 
calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further 
proceeding.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the 
bill will be placed on the calendar.
```
