# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9088

**Measures:** H.R. 9088

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 9088.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would require the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) 
     to make publicly available the forms organizations that self-
     declare under Section 501(c)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code 
     file with the IRS.
```
