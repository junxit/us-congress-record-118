# MOMENT OF SILENCE IN HONOR OF COREY COMPERATORE

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mike (K000376) [R-PA]

```
            MOMENT OF SILENCE IN HONOR OF COREY COMPERATORE

  (Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 
tragic loss we had of a person in Pennsylvania during the Trump rally, 
Corey Comperatore, who was a husband, a father of two, and a volunteer 
fireman.
  His whole life was that of service. He was very tragically one of the 
people who was struck by a sniper's bullet. Corey died on the spot.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask to take a moment of silence to honor his memory, 
the sacrifice, and the terrible things his family is going through 
right now.

                          ____________________
```
