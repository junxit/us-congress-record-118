# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9090

**Measures:** H.R. 9090

```
           By Ms. STEFANIK:
       H.R. 9090.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the US Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Would require the reporting and public disclosure of every 
     penny from a government grant or contract that is provided to 
     an entity located in a foreign country of concern including 
     North Korea, Communist China, Russia, and the Islamic 
     Republic of Iran.
```
