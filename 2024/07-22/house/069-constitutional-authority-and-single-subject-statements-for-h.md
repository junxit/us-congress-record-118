# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9080

**Measures:** H.R. 9080

```
           By Mr. BRECHEEN:
       H.R. 9080.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Director of the United States Secret Service

     Special Agents and Uniformed Division Officers.
```
