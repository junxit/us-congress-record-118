# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9083

**Measures:** H.R. 9083

```
           By Mr. LATTA:
       H.R. 9083.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This resolution is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Energy Policy and Conservation Act to require 
     States to include supporting the physical security, 
     cybersecurity, and resilience of local distribution systems 
     in State energy security plans.
```
