# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8037

**Measures:** H.R. 8037

```
           By Ms. UNDERWOOD:
       H.R. 8037.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill provides statutory authority for the IMPROVE

     supports research on improving maternal health outcomes with 
     a particular emphasis on mitigating maternal health
```
