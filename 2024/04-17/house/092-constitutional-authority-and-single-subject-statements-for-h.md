# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8051

**Measures:** H.R. 8051

```
           By Mr. NORMAN:
       H.R. 8051.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the consideration in the House of 
     Representatives of any legislation containing an earmark.
```
