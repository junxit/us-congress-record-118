# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8033

**Measures:** H.R. 8033

```
           By Mr. LUETKEMEYER:
       H.R. 8033.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 5, United States Code, to clarify the 
     information required to be included in a certification by an 
     agency that a rule will not have a significant economic 
     impact on a substantial number of small entities.
```
