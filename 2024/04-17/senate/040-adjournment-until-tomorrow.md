# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I move that it stand adjourned under the previous 
order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 7:12 p.m., adjourned until Thursday, April 
18, 2024, at 12 noon.
```
