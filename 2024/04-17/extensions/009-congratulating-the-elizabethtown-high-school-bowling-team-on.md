# CONGRATULATING THE ELIZABETHTOWN HIGH SCHOOL BOWLING TEAM ON THEIR CHAMPIONSHIP WIN

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
  CONGRATULATING THE ELIZABETHTOWN HIGH SCHOOL BOWLING TEAM ON THEIR 
                            CHAMPIONSHIP WIN

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 17, 2024

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the 
Elizabethtown High School Bowling Team on winning the Pennsylvania 
State High School Bowling Championship.
  The nail biting, back and forth matchup was a hard-fought battle of 
skill, technique, and determination. The final few frames ended in a 
series of splits that drove up the anticipation in the alley. Finally, 
the Elizabethtown Bears were able to seal the deal by five pins.
  Bowling requires a unique set of athletic capabilities and teamwork 
to strike your way to a victory.
  The Elizabethtown Bowling Team should be extraordinarily proud of 
this success and all their accomplishments throughout the season.
  I congratulate them on their State Championship victory and wish them 
all the best of luck in the future.

                          ____________________
```
