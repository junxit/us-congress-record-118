# RECOGNIZING ADRIONNA HIGUERA MARTINEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                 RECOGNIZING ADRIONNA HIGUERA MARTINEZ

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Adrionna 
Higuera Martinez for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors 
for Youth Award.
  Adrionna has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Adrionna, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Adrionna's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of 
a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Adrionna Higuera Martinez on achieving 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
