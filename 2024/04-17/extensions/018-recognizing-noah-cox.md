# RECOGNIZING NOAH COX

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                          RECOGNIZING NOAH COX

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Noah Cox for 
earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Noah has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Noah, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Noah's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Arvada K-8 and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and promising 
future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Noah Cox on achieving the Arvada Wheat 
Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
```
