# RECOGNIZING KIMO BULL

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING KIMO BULL

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kimo Bull for 
earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Kimo has overcome many challenges along their journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Kimo, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Kimo's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Arvada K-8 and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and promising 
future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Kimo Bull on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
