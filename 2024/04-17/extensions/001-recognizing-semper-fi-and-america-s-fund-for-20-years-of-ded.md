# RECOGNIZING SEMPER FI AND AMERICA'S FUND FOR 20 YEARS OF DEDICATED SERVICE

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
  RECOGNIZING SEMPER FI AND AMERICA'S FUND FOR 20 YEARS OF DEDICATED 
                                SERVICE

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 17, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
remarkable achievements of Semper Fi & America's Fund as it celebrates 
20 years of unwavering service to our Nation's critically wounded, ill, 
and injured service members, veterans, and military families.
  For two decades, Semper Fi & America's Fund has stood as a pillar of 
hope and support, providing critical assistance, resources, and care to 
those who have bravely served our country. Through their tireless 
efforts and unwavering commitment, they have made an indelible mark on 
the lives of countless individuals, empowering them to navigate the 
challenges they face and to recover, transition, and thrive with 
independence and grace.
  Their comprehensive programs and services, including the Service 
Member and Family Support, Integrative Wellness, and Transition 
programs have been instrumental in addressing the unique needs of our 
veterans and their families, offering them hope, healing, and renewed 
purpose.
  I am deeply grateful for the invaluable contributions Semper Fi & 
America's Fund has made to our communities and our country. Their 
selfless commitment to our service members, veterans, and military 
families embodies the very essence of patriotism and compassion, and it 
serves as an example for us all.
  I extend my heartfelt congratulations to Semper Fi & America's Fund, 
its dedicated staff, volunteers, and supporters on this significant 
milestone. May they continue to inspire hope, foster healing, and honor 
the sacrifices of our veterans for years to come.

                          ____________________
```
