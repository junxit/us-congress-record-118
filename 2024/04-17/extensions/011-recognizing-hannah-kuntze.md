# RECOGNIZING HANNAH KUNTZE

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING HANNAH KUNTZE

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Hannah Kuntze 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Hannah has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Hannah, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Hannah's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Arvada High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Hannah Kuntze on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
