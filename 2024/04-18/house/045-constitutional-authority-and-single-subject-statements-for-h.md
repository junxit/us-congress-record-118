# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8060

**Measures:** H.R. 8060

```
           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.R. 8060.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 4
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to require the 
     notification of appropriate elected officials prior to the 
     placement of refugees in a State.
```
