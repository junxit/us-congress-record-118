# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, we find our refuge in You. You have been our help in 
ages past. You have been our shelter from life's storms, filling our 
hearts with Your divine peace as You provide us with an inheritance for 
eternity. You are our hope for the years to come.
  Today, use our Senators for Your glory. May they remember that You 
weigh their motives, direct their steps, and make even their enemies be 
at peace with them. Lord, permit Your power to work in them to 
accomplish Your purposes on Earth.
  And Lord, as we approach the Passover season, we praise You for Your 
redemptive power in our world.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
