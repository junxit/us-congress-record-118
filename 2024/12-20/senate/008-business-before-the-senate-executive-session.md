# Business Before the Senate (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                       Business Before the Senate

  Mr. President, now on Senate business, as the Senate continues 
working on government funding, the Senate has other matters to attend 
to on the floor.
  Today, the Senate is scheduled to vote on two more district judges: 
Ben Cheeks to be the district judge for the Southern District of 
California and Serena Murillo to be the district judge for the Central 
District of California.
  For the information of my colleagues, our first vote will be at 11 
o'clock to advance the Cheeks nomination. If cloture is invoked, the 
Senate will then vote to confirm Judge Cheeks

[[Page S7248]]

sometime this afternoon. And after that, we will proceed with a 
rollcall vote to advance the Murillo nomination. It is my hope that we 
can finish voting on these two judicial nominees by the end of the day.
```
