# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10558

**Measures:** H.R. 10558

```
            By Mr. VARGAS:
        H.R. 10558.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant

       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution.
```
