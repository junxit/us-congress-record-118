# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10548

**Measures:** H.R. 10548

```
            By Ms. DeLAURO:
        H.R. 10548.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the United States Constitution and its 
     subsequent amendments, and further clarified and interpreted 
     by the Supreme Court of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to protect children's health by denying any deduction 
     for advertising and marketing directed at children to promote 
     the consumption of food of poor nutritional quality.
```
