# HONORING THE MICHIGAN BLACK CAUCUS OF LOCAL ELECTED OFFICIALS

**Speaking:** Slotkin, Elissa (S001208) [D-MI]

```
     HONORING THE MICHIGAN BLACK CAUCUS OF LOCAL ELECTED OFFICIALS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELISSA SLOTKIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. SLOTKIN. Mr. Speaker, today I honor the Michigan Black Caucus of 
Local Elected Officials, a cornerstone of leadership, collaboration, 
and advocacy in Michigan since its founding in 2004. For two decades, 
the Caucus has provided a forum for local elected officials to network, 
mentor, and support one another, fostering stronger and connected 
communities throughout Michigan.
  First organized in March 2004 during the Michigan Municipal League 
Conference in Lansing, the Caucus began with 45 Black local elected 
officials before officially becoming an affiliate of the Michigan 
Municipal League later that year. The organization has grown in scope 
and impact under the leadership of its Presidents--Clara Shephard of 
Muskegon, Lois Allen-Richardon of Ypsilanti, and its current President, 
Brenda F. Moore of Saginaw. Together, these leaders have guided the 
Michigan Black Caucus of Local Elected Officials in its mission to 
amplify the voices of African American municipal officials and promote 
equitable governance across the state.
  Through leadership development, data-informed policy, and its 
committees, the Caucus ensures that the needs of Michigan's diverse 
communities are heard and addressed. Its collaboration with state 
partners as well as national organizations has advanced policies that 
improve outcomes and representation for the African-American community. 
Additionally, its scholarships for high school students help ensure 
that the next generation of leaders has the tools they need to succeed 
in higher education.
  The Caucus also serves as an important resource for its members, 
offering workshops, regional conferences, and a network of officials 
who share ideas and solutions to challenges faced by local governments. 
Through these efforts, it strengthens Michigan's cities, towns, and 
villages, empowering local governments to deliver meaningful progress 
and opportunity to their residents.
  The Michigan Black Caucus of Local Elected Officials exemplifies the 
power of collaboration and shared purpose. By fostering equity, 
supporting emerging leaders, and advocating for policies that uplift 
the communities they serve, the Caucus has left an indelible mark on 
Michigan and beyond.
  It is with great respect and gratitude that I ask that we recognize 
the Michigan Black Caucus of Local Elected Officials for its 
contributions and its role in shaping a more inclusive and prosperous 
future. May it continue to lead and inspire for many more years to 
come.
```
