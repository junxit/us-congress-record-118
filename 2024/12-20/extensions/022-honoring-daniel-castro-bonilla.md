# HONORING DANIEL CASTRO BONILLA

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                     HONORING DANIEL CASTRO BONILLA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the outstanding 
service of Daniel Castro Bonilla, my fellow who served with distinction 
in my office and dedicated his time to serving the people of Florida's 
24th Congressional District.
  Daniel, a proud Honduran American from Miami, FL, graduated from 
Bowdoin College with a degree in Asian Studies. His commitment to 
bridging cultural and linguistic divides is exemplified by his 
experiences studying in China under the Department of State's Critical 
Language Scholarship and conducting research with the Middlebury 
program. Furthermore, Daniel served four impactful years with the 
Fulbright Program in Taiwan, where he collaborated with schools 
nationwide to enhance educational opportunities.
  Daniel served as a research analyst for Miami-Dade County 
policymakers, whose work is instrumental in developing data-driven 
policy solutions. Daniel has demonstrated a profound commitment to 
public service in my office, showcasing his analytical skills and 
international perspective to benefit our constituents. His work has 
been particularly impactful in fostering meaningful conversations 
around current events in Haiti, using his international relations 
expertise to help me navigate these unprecedented times. His insights 
have been invaluable in supporting friends and families in foreign 
countries during these challenging circumstances.
  Daniel is a current Rangel fellow and will work for the State 
Department. I wish Daniel all the best as he transitions into his 
graduate studies in foreign service. I do not doubt that the next step 
in his journey will be filled with continued success.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Daniel Castro Bonilla for his 
commitment and service.
```
