# HONORING THE MEMORY OF OFFICER JACOB ANDREW CANDANOZA

**Speaking:** Gooden, Lance (G000589) [R-TX]

```
         HONORING THE MEMORY OF OFFICER JACOB ANDREW CANDANOZA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LANCE GOODEN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. GOODEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of 
Officer Jacob Andrew Candanoza of the Terrell Police Department, who 
lost his life in the line of duty on December 8, 2024.
  Officer Candanoza embodied the highest ideals of courage and 
commitment in his service to the Terrell community. On December 8, 
during a routine traffic stop, Officer Candanoza made the ultimate 
sacrifice to protect and serve.
  Officer Candanoza dedicated his life to the protection of our Nation 
as a sergeant in the United States Marine Corps between 2014 and 2019. 
Following his military service, he worked with the Dallas Sheriff's 
Office as a courtesy patrol technician. After being sworn in as an 
officer with the Terrell Police Department in July 2024, Officer 
Candanoza quickly earned the respect of his colleagues and community 
for his professionalism and compassion. His sacrifice is a solemn 
reminder of the debt of gratitude we owe to all who serve on the thin 
blue line.
  My deepest condolences go out to Officer Candanoza's family, friends, 
and fellow officers during this profoundly difficult time. May his 
memory serve as a beacon of courage and a reminder of the dedication of 
our law enforcement community.

                          ____________________
```
