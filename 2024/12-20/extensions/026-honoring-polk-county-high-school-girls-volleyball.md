# HONORING POLK COUNTY HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS VOLLEYBALL

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
           HONORING POLK COUNTY HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS VOLLEYBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Polk 
County High School girls volleyball team for winning the North Carolina 
1A volleyball championship game with a score of 3-0.
  This is the Wolverine girls' second consecutive 1A volleyball 
championship title.
  Making their championship win all the more impressive, the team did 
not have practices or games for a period of 19 days during the middle 
of their season due to Hurricane Helene.
  Congratulations to the Polk County High School girls volleyball team. 
I commend their work ethic, fortitude, and determination that resulted 
in an outstanding season.

                          ____________________
```
