# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF COMMISSIONER ANTONIO WILLIAMS OF WAYNE COUNTY, NORTH CAROLINA

**Speaking:** Nickel, Wiley (N000194) [D-NC]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF COMMISSIONER ANTONIO WILLIAMS OF WAYNE 
                         COUNTY, NORTH CAROLINA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. WILEY NICKEL

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. NICKEL. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
proclamation honoring the service of Commissioner Antonio Williams.
  Whereas Commissioner Antonio Williams continues to advocate for the 
creation of the Wayne County Homeless Committee to combat homelessness 
in Wayne County.
  Whereas Commissioner Williams accomplished the approval of digital 
libraries in District 2, which in turn brought more support to the 
Summer Youth Employment Program that he started.
  Whereas the commissioners approved resolutions authorizing the 
spending of national opioid settlement funds, which will bring needed 
relief to communities impacted by the opioid epidemic in Wayne County. 
These funds support every aspect of the effort to combat addiction, 
from early intervention to recovery support services and the treatment 
of incarcerated people.
  Whereas the commissioners' progress on public service infrastructure, 
from the Northern Wayne County Library, to Fremont Elementary school, 
adds to the quality of life in Wayne County and contributes to the 
delivery of essential support for all constituents.
  Whereas the increased allocation for public schools in this year's 
budget, including expansion of the School Resource Officer program is 
critical to ensuring safe and accessible public education.
  I, Wiley Nickel, Congressman of the 13th District of North Carolina, 
am honored to recognize the incredible service of Commissioner Antonio 
Williams of the Wayne County Board of Commissioners. Commissioner 
Williams' efforts to improve citizens' lives through public service is 
laudable, and his impact on community is immeasurable. I Thank 
Commissioner Williams for his devoted attention to Wayne County and 
contributions to the strength of our state.

                          ____________________
```
