# CELEBRATING DIWALI IN CARY, NORTH CAROLINA

**Speaking:** Nickel, Wiley (N000194) [D-NC]

```
               CELEBRATING DIWALI IN CARY, NORTH CAROLINA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. WILEY NICKEL

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. NICKEL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the celebration of 
the Diwali Festival in Cary, North Carolina, my home and part of North 
Carolina's 13th Congressional District.
  This year's Diwali Festival was made to happen thanks to the great 
work of the Cary Town Council. The event is being hosted in 
commemoration of Cary's Diwali Proclamation, which will be presented to 
Manoj Pandya, the President of the Hindu Society of North Carolina.
  It is also important that I recognize Councilwoman Sarika Bansal of 
the Town of Cary, for her efforts in organizing this event. 
Councilwoman Bansal is the first ever Indian-American woman to serve on 
the Cary Town Council. Councilwoman Bansal ran on the platform that 
including more voices and citizen engagement would help us to make 
investments in the town of Cary that will benefit many generations to 
come. She understands the importance of diversity and uplifting those 
voices.
  North Carolina is a place of abundance, where diverse communities and 
their traditions contribute to the prosperity of our great state. 
Specifically, the Indian-American and Hindu communities make great and 
important contributions to the economic, social, and cultural fabric of 
North Carolina.
  From business and technology to education and healthcare, our Hindu 
neighbors continue to make a lasting impact on our state. The tradition 
of Diwali is a testament to the strength that comes from our 
community's diversity.
  Diwali is a time that holds immense significance and marks the 
triumph of light over darkness, knowledge over ignorance, and good over 
evil. It is a time to come together with friends and family to enjoy 
dancing and delicious food. It is also a holiday of hope; an important 
opportunity to reflect on the importance of light, particularly in 
times of great darkness and social discord. Diwali stands as a 
testament to the enduring spirit of resilience and optimism that unites 
us as a community and a Nation.
  I ask my esteemed colleagues to join me in reminding our communities 
and ourselves to shine our light in darkness, to fight for peace, for 
justice, and for understanding, even as powerful forces work to sow 
hate and division. In North Carolina y'all means all.
  May we all dedicate ourselves to the beauty of diversity, and pursuit 
of a brighter future for everyone.
```
