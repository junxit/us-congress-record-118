# HONORING SWAIN COUNTY HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS CROSS COUNTRY

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
         HONORING SWAIN COUNTY HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS CROSS COUNTRY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Swain 
County High School girls cross country team on their fourth consecutive 
North Carolina 1A girls cross country state championship title.
  The Maroon Devils girls went above and beyond this season by posting 
their best score yet out of their four straight championships.
  Additionally, Swain County senior, Arizona Blankenship, won her 
second individual 1A cross country title.
  Once again, congratulations to the Swain County High School girls 
cross country team. Their streak of success is a testament to the 
dedication and passion of their runners and the exceptional culture of 
their program.

                          ____________________
```
