# CELEBRATING THE 80TH BIRTHDAY OF GWENDOLYN NAVY

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
            CELEBRATING THE 80TH BIRTHDAY OF GWENDOLYN NAVY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor and celebrate the 80th 
birthday of Mrs. Gwendolyn Navy. Mrs. Navy is a shining example of 
resilience, dedication, and love. A woman whose life story is a 
testament to her strength of character and deep commitment to family 
and community.
  Born on December 6, 1944 in Mobile, Alabama, Gwendolyn Navy grew up 
under the guidance and love of her parents, Melvin and Edna Wells. At 
eighteen, she moved north to Buffalo, New York, where she would build a 
life that exemplifies hard work and service.
  For ten years, Mrs. Navy worked at Fisher-Price, contributing to the 
joy of countless children through her role on the assembly line and toy 
making. She then devoted twenty-seven years of service to the State of 
New York as a Corrections Officer at the Attica Correctional Facility, 
where she upheld her duties with strength and integrity.
  Beyond her professional accomplishments, Mrs. Navy's greatest pride 
is her family. She is the beloved mother of two children, Gerard Navy 
and Felicia Williams. Her legacy extends to six grandchildren and one 
great-grandchild, who are undoubtedly inspired by her wisdom and love. 
Mrs. Navy was married eighteen years to her late husband, Walter Navy, 
whose memory she cherishes deeply.
  Mrs. Navy is known for her many talents including her culinary 
expertise. Her collard greens, okra, peach cobbler, and sweet potato 
pie are legendary within her family and community.
  Mr. Speaker, it is an honor to recognize Gwendolyn Navy, a woman with 
poise and resilience to her beloved family and community. On this 
milestone birthday, we celebrate her extraordinary life, her legacy, 
and the profound impact she has made on who knows her.

                          ____________________
```
