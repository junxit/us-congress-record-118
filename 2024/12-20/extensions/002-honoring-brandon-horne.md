# HONORING BRANDON HORNE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING BRANDON HORNE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the outstanding 
service of Brandon Horne, my intern who served with distinction in my 
office and dedicated his time to serving the people of Florida's 24th 
Congressional District.
  Brandon, a proud alumnus of Southern University, brought his 
background and passion for creating meaningful change to my office. He 
was a member of the Student Government Association serving as President 
and a member of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity Incorporated. His commitment 
to improving transportation and infrastructure across South Florida was 
evident in his diligent support of my work on the Transportation and 
Infrastructure Committee.
  One of Brandon's greatest strengths is building bridges and fostering 
collaboration. His dedication to empowering others and uplifting 
underrepresented voices has left a lasting impression on our team and 
the communities we serve.
  His next chapter will undoubtedly be marked by his continued success 
and impactful work in public service.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Brandon Horne for his drive and 
dedication to public service.

                          ____________________
```
