# HONORING WEST HENDERSON HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS VOLLEYBALL

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
          HONORING WEST HENDERSON HIGH SCHOOL GIRLS VOLLEYBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the West 
Henderson High School girls volleyball team for winning the North 
Carolina 3A volleyball championship game.
  This is the Falcons' sixth volleyball championship and first since 
2008.
  The West Henderson girls persevered through the challenges brought by 
Hurricane Helene, which interrupted their season for two weeks as many 
of the players were without power and water.
  On and off the court, the West Henderson girls battled all season 
long to finish with an impressive 26 and 2 record and the championship 
title.
  Congratulations to the West Henderson High School Girls Volleyball 
team, they have made all of Western North Carolina proud.

                          ____________________
```
