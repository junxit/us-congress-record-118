# HONORING PINYI WANG

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                          HONORING PINYI WANG

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the outstanding 
service of Pinyi Wang, my fellow who served with distinction in my 
office and dedicated her time to serving the people of Florida's 24th 
Congressional District.
  Pinyi is currently working on her Ph.D. in the Educational Leadership 
and Policy program at the University of Florida. I am proud to say that 
Pinyi has shown great dedication and passion for education and has 
devoted her time in my office to advance education policy forward.
  It was obvious that Pinyi was ready to meet the challenge of 
contributing to the field of education. Her commitment to closing the 
opportunity gap across race, ethnicity, and gender became apparent with 
her work in addressing the inequalities found in the use of Artificial 
Intelligence in educational spaces. Furthermore, she has contributed 
significantly to moving my One School, One Nurse Act along the 
legislative process.
  Pinyi's intellect, kindness, and hard work will always be remembered. 
I wish Pinyi all the best as she continues her doctoral studies in 
education equity and equality both domestically and internationally. I 
am certain that the next step in her journey will be filled with much 
success.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Pinyi Wang for her commitment and 
service.

                          ____________________
```
