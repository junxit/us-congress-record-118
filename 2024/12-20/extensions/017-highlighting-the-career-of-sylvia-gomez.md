# HIGHLIGHTING THE CAREER OF SYLVIA GOMEZ

**Speaking:** Gonzalez-Colon, Jenniffer (G000582) [R-PR]

```
                HIGHLIGHTING THE CAREER OF SYLVIA GOMEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. JENNIFFER GONZALEZ-COLON

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mrs. GONZALEZ-COLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
achievements and contributions of Puerto Rican broadcast journalist and 
news anchor Sylvia Gomez, after a career spanning over fifty years.
  Sylvia Gomez was a pioneer in broadcast journalism in Puerto Rico, 
starting in this field at a time when the opportunities for women had 
barely began to become available. She was able to find valuable mentors 
among seasoned figures and prove herself in the field and on the air, 
soon establishing herself in reporting, interviewing, and anchoring as 
part of the news teams in the San Juan media market.
  She has since been a constant presence in Puerto Rico TV screens, 
starting in public television and making her way through our major news 
outlets becoming an anchorwoman and a lead investigative reporter, 
eventually leading to a 44-year career at Telemundo WKAQ-TV.
  Her reporting on environmental issues helped set the standard for 
such coverage and earned her numerous recognitions inside and outside 
the industry, with over 15 awards from press organizations, including a 
Silver Circle Emmy awarded by the Suncoast Chapter of the National 
Academy of Television Arts and Sciences in 2022.
  I also want to highlight her principled fight against age 
discrimination by media employers, an issue that disproportionately 
impacts women in this field. Her stand on this subject bore witness to 
her integrity, strength and courage, and will certainly lead to a 
better workplace for many in the future. She has been an example to be 
followed, showing how one can and should continue fighting for your 
rights and the rights of others.
  Sylvia is now moving on to enjoy a different stage of her life. I 
know she will continue to be a valuable member of Puerto Rico's 
community, and today I applaud her outstanding work, thank her for her 
commitment to what she saw as right and true to keep us informed, and 
wish her all the best moving forward.
```
