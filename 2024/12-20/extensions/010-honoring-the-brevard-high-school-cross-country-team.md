# HONORING THE BREVARD HIGH SCHOOL CROSS-COUNTRY TEAM

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
          HONORING THE BREVARD HIGH SCHOOL CROSS-COUNTRY TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Brevard 
High School boys' cross-country team for winning the 2A state 
championship. This is the Brevard ``Blue Devils'' second 2A state 
championship in a row and third in the last four years, solidifying 
their dominant status as a dynasty.
  The Brevard High School girls' cross-country team placed 7th at the 
state cross country meet.
  Congratulations to the Blue Devils. Their continued success is a 
testament to countless hours of training, hard work, and a genuine 
passion for running.
  They have made Western North Carolina proud.
  May the future bring many more years of success as we continue to 
celebrate the legacy of your exceptional cross-country program.

                          ____________________
```
