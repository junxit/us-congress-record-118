# HONORING ROBERT SENA

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                          HONORING ROBERT SENA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the outstanding 
service of Robert Sena, my intern who served with distinction in my 
office and dedicated his time to serving the people of Florida's 24th 
Congressional District.
  Robert, a graduate of Rollins College, brought his background and 
enthusiasm for communications and civic engagement. His service in my 
office has been instrumental to the constituents of Florida's 24th 
Congressional District as well as the state of Florida.
  Robert's hard work and dedication were highlighted in his outstanding 
skills for sharing the functions of the federal government with a wide 
audience.
  I applaud Robert's passion for civic involvement and his plan to 
educate the youth on the importance of local-level government.
  Robert leaves a legacy of hard work and dedication. I do not doubt 
that the next step in his journey will be filled with continued 
success.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Robert Sena for his commitment and 
service.

                          ____________________
```
