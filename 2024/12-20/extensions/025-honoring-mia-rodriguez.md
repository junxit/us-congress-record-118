# HONORING MIA RODRIGUEZ

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING MIA RODRIGUEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the outstanding 
service of Mia Rodriguez, my intern who served with distinction in my 
office and dedicated her time to serving the people of Florida's 24th 
Congressional District.
  Mia is currently a student at Florida International University where 
her focus is on International Relations and Political Science. She also 
has served with distinction on FIU's Student Government Association as 
Chief Justice. I am proud to say that Mia has shown great dedication 
and has devoted her time in my office to diving deep into the 
legislative process, particularly when it comes to foreign affairs and 
trade policy.
  Mia's eagerness to learn and excel will always be remembered. I wish 
Mia all the best as she continues her academic journey of which I am 
certain will be filled with much success.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Mia Rodriguez for her commitment and 
service.

                          ____________________
```
