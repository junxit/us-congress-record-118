# HONORING DELANEY JANE CALLAHAN

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                     HONORING DELANEY JANE CALLAHAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the outstanding 
service of Delaney Jane Callahan, my intern who has served with

[[Page E1323]]

distinction in my office and dedicated their time to serving the people 
of Florida's 24th Congressional District.
  A Florida native, Delaney, arrived at my office determined to make a 
difference in the American legislative landscape. During her time here, 
she has advanced my agendas significantly, whether by drafting speeches 
and press releases, organizing press events, or conducting policy 
research. Her passion for public service and government is 
unparalleled, and she exemplifies the qualities of a dedicated public 
servant.
  Delaney is currently a student at the University of Colorado Boulder 
where she focuses on Journalism and Political Science. She also serves 
on CU Bold's Student Government Association as Strategic Communications 
and Public Relations Chair. In addition, Delaney is the Vice President 
of her Panhellenic sorority. Delaney has also followed her academic 
interests by studying abroad in Paris, France, where she served as a 
strategic communications intern for the U.S. Department of State's 
Diplomatic Security Service Bureau.
  Throughout Delaney's time in my office, I've observed that Delaney is 
deeply committed to journalism expertise to enhance the reach and 
influence of my office to as many constituents as possible. I am 
confident that she will continue to use her voice and talents to uplift 
underserved communities and champion important causes.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Delaney Jane Callahan for her 
commitment and service.

                          ____________________
```
