# CELEBRATING SHERIFF STEVE KEMPKER'S RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Huizenga, Bill (H001058) [R-MI]

```
             CELEBRATING SHERIFF STEVE KEMPKER'S RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BILL HUIZENGA

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. HUIZENGA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and congratulate 
Sheriff Steve Kempker on his retirement following 44 years of 
outstanding service to Ottawa County. Sheriff Kempker has been a sworn 
officer of the law for over four decades, protecting the citizens of 
West Michigan, and I thank him for his courageous service in the line 
of duty.
  Sheriff Kempker began his law enforcement career in 1980 with the 
City of Holland Police Department before joining the Ottawa County 
Sheriff's Office in 1989. Throughout his career, Sheriff Kempker served 
the county in a variety of roles, including Road Patrol Sergeant, 
Operations & Services Lieutenant, Captain, and Undersheriff. In 2016, 
Steve Kempker was elected as the 33rd Sheriff of Ottawa County.
  In addition to his storied career as an officer, Sheriff Kempker has 
been a part of several law enforcement organizations and was appointed 
by Governor Rick Snyder and reappointed by Governor Gretchen Whitmer to 
the State of Michigan Mental Health Diversion Council. He also served 
on the Michigan High Intensity Drug Trafficking Areas (HIDTA) Board 
under the President's Office of Drug Control and Policy.
  Sheriff Kempker has been an outstanding public servant for the people 
of Ottawa County for over 40 years. I am honored to call Steve a friend 
and wish him the best in his well-earned retirement. His impact on our 
community will never be forgotten.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in commending Sheriff Steve Kempker for 
his decades of exceptional service to Ottawa County. I wish him the 
best as he enjoys his well-deserved retrrement.
```
