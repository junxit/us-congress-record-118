# HONORING ANDRE PIERRE

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING ANDRE PIERRE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the outstanding 
service of Andre Pierre, my former intern who served with distinction 
and dedicated his time to serving the people of Florida's 24th 
Congressional District. Andre is currently a student at Florida 
International University, where he focuses on International Business 
and serves as the Vice-President of the Real Estate Club. I am proud to 
highlight Andre's remarkable contributions, particularly in foreign 
policy and Haiti. His work in this area has been instrumental in 
shaping policy discussions and providing valuable insights into the 
complex challenges facing Haiti. In my office, Andre has shown great 
dedication, immersing himself in the legislative process, especially in 
transportation and infrastructure. He has also worked closely with the 
Caucus on the Commission of the Social Status of Black Men and Boys, 
demonstrating his commitment to addressing critical issues impacting 
communities. Andre has emerged as a true leader on the Hill, uniting 
individuals from diverse backgrounds to foster a more inclusive and 
collaborative culture.
  I wish Andre all the best as he continues his academic journey, which 
I am certain will be filled with success.
  On behalf of Florida's 24th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in honoring Andre for his commitment and service.

                          ____________________
```
