# HONORING ASHEVILLE HIGH SCHOOL BOYS AND GIRLS INDIVIDUAL CROSS COUNTRY

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
 HONORING ASHEVILLE HIGH SCHOOL BOYS AND GIRLS INDIVIDUAL CROSS COUNTRY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 20, 2024

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Michael Haskin 
and Sofi Alexander, the boys and girls individual cross country winners 
of North Carolina's 4A state championship race, both belonging to 
Asheville High School.
  Asheville High senior Michael Haskin ran his second fasted time of 
the season in a neck and neck race to win the boys individual 4A 
championship.
  In the girls individual 4A race, junior Sofi Alexander took home 
first place for the Asheville High Cougars. Sofi is the first Cougar to 
win a girls cross country state championship.
  Both runners, Michael and Sofi, won their races by less than two 
seconds, a testament to the determination of both athletes and the 
highly competitive fields.
  On behalf of all of Western North Carolina, congratulations to 
Michael and Sofi.

                          ____________________
```
