# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9590

**Measures:** H.R. 9590

```
           By Mr. TONKO:
       H.R. 9590.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       ``The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces, as enumerated in 
     Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       A bill to regulate and establish public health guardrails 
     for states operating sports betting programs.
```
