# ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 12, 2024, TO MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 16, 2024

**Speaking:** Smith, Jason (S001195) [R-MO]

```
ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 12, 2024, TO MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 16, 
                                  2024

  Mr. SMITH of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 4 p.m. on Monday, 
September 16, 2024, and further, when the House adjourns on that day, 
it adjourn to meet at noon on Tuesday, September 17, 2024, for morning-
hour debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Missouri?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
