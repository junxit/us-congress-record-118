# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9565

**Measures:** H.R. 9565

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota:
       H.R. 9565.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Transportation from entering 
     into, extending, or renewing a contract wiht or awarding a 
     grant to an entity that uses or procures light detection and 
     ranging technology from certain foreign entities, and for 
     other purposes.
```
