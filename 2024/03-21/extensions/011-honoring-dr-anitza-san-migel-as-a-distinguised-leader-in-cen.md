# HONORING DR. ANITZA SAN MIGEL AS A DISTINGUISED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR WOMEN'S HISTORY MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING DR. ANITZA SAN MIGEL AS A DISTINGUISED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                   FLORIDA FOR WOMEN'S HISTORY MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 21, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Dr. Anitza San Miguel's mission is to spark 
the resting potential within leaders. She guides them to flourish and 
project their distinctive brilliance, thereby revolutionizing their 
mindset to realize their ultimate aspirations. She is a loving wife and 
mother, a scientist, an innovative educator, an author, and a visionary 
leader.
  Dr. San Miguel holds a bachelor's in biology, a master's in 
biotechnology, and a doctorate in higher education administration. She 
has served as a dean of Science at Valencia College, Osceola Campus, 
since moving to Orlando, Florida, in 2016. She is dedicated to the 
academic and professional success of individuals at all levels.
  She has served as a science professor and dean at esteemed 
institutions in Virginia and Florida. She currently leads a team in 
Florida's Ninth District with distinction. Her expertise has been 
recognized at the National Institutes of Health and the United States 
Patent and Trademark Office.
  Dr. San Miguel's entrepreneurial spirit led her to create ASM Mentors 
and the empowering podcast ``Sacudete y Toma Accion'' or ``Shake It Off 
and Take Action.'' She is the author of `Navegador,' a reflective 
journaling tool, as well as a collection of inspirational coffee table 
books, ``Reignite,'' ``Grow,'' and ``Shine.'' She has shared her 
guidance in the ``Today's Inspired Latina'' and ``Today's Inspired 
Leader'' book anthologies. Her eloquence and wisdom make her a coveted 
speaker, mentor, and coach.
  Dr. San Miguel's accomplishments are a testament to her optimism and 
tenacious spirit. Her dedication to shaping the academic and 
professional trajectories of emerging leaders encapsulates her passion 
and commitment to continual self-improvement and fostering limitless 
growth and excellence.
  Dr. San Miguel's pursuit of knowledge and development is persistent. 
Beyond the public eye, she savors moments with her family, explores new 
horizons, and engages in the reflective practice of journaling.
```
