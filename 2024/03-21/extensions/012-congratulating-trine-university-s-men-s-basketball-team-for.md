# CONGRATULATING TRINE UNIVERSITY'S MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM FOR WINNING THE NCAA DIVISION III CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Banks, Jim (B001299) [R-IN]

```
CONGRATULATING TRINE UNIVERSITY'S MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM FOR WINNING THE 
                     NCAA DIVISION III CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM BANKS

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 21, 2024

  Mr. BANKS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Trine 
University's men's basketball team for winning the 2024 NCAA Division 
III National Championship.
  Trine clinched their 69-61 victory over the top ranked Hampden-Sydney 
College in Fort Wayne, Indiana at the Allen County War Memorial 
Coliseum in front of a record crowd. 4,546 spectators witnessed the 
Thunder become national champions in their first ever NCAA Tournament 
appearance.
  This season, Trine has reminded Hoosiers why we love the game of 
basketball. They have exhibited all the traits that make the game so 
compelling. Not only have they excited and entertained their audiences 
in Northeast Indiana and beyond, but their dedication, hard work, and 
will to win have inspired them.
  I commend each player and coach for reaching the pinnacle of success 
in their division. I know they will continue to grow and achieve, both 
on and off the court, with the great support of their community behind 
them.
```
