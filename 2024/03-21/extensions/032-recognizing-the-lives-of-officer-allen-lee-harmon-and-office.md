# RECOGNIZING THE LIVES OF OFFICER ALLEN LEE HARMON AND OFFICER OTIS LEE ROBISON

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
RECOGNIZING THE LIVES OF OFFICER ALLEN LEE HARMON AND OFFICER OTIS LEE 
                                ROBISON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 21, 2024

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
lives of fallen officers Allen Lee Harmon and Otis Lee Robison of the 
Lincoln Police Department.
  November 16th will mark the 50th anniversary of Officer Harmon and 
Officer Robison's paying the ultimate sacrifice while in the line of 
duty. On November 16, 1974, the two men tragically lost their lives.
  On November 15, 2024, the City of Lincoln will hold a remembrance 
event at Lincoln City Hall to honor the lives of Officer Harmon and 
Officer Robison.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in remembering Officer Harmon and Officer 
Robison and honoring them for their brave service and dedication to 
their community.
```
