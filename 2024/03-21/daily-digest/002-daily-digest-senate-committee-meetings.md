# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Armed Services: Committee ordered favorably reported the 
nomination of Melissa Griffin Dalton, of Virginia, to be Under 
Secretary of the Air Force, Department of Defense, and 1,995 
nominations in the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Marine Corps.
USINDOPACOM AND USFK
Committee on Armed Services: Committee concluded open and closed 
hearings to examine the posture of United States Indo-Pacific Command 
and United States Forces Korea in review of the Defense Authorization 
Request for Fiscal Year 2025 and the Future Years Defense Program, 
after receiving testimony from Admiral John C. Aquilino, USN, 
Commander, United States Indo-Pacific Command, and General Paul J. 
LaCamera, USA, Commander, United Nations Command, Commander, United 
States-Republic of Korea Combined Forces Command, Commander, United 
States Forces Korea, both of the Department of Defense.
SPECTRUM AND NATIONAL SECURITY
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine spectrum and national security, after receiving 
testimony from Monisha Ghosh, University of Notre Dame Department of 
Electrical Engineering, South Bend, Indiana; Mary L. Brown, 
WifiForward, Annapolis, Maryland; Clete D. Johnson, Center for 
Strategic and International Studies, Rabun Gap, Georgia; Diane Rinaldo, 
Open RAN Policy Coalition, Edgewater, Maryland; and Harold Furchtgott-
Roth, Hudson Institute, Chevy Chase, Maryland.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources: Committee concluded a 
hearing to examine the nominations of David Rosner, of Massachusetts, 
Lindsay S. See, of West Virginia, and Judy W. Chang, of Massachusetts, 
each to be a Member of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, after 
the nominees testified and answered questions in their own behalf.

[[Page D284]]


PRESIDENT'S PROPOSED BUDGET
Committee on Finance: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
President's proposed budget request for fiscal year 2025, after 
receiving testimony from Janet L. Yellen, Secretary of the Treasury.
U.S. ANTI-CORRUPTION STRATEGY
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine implementation of the United States anti-corruption strategy, 
after receiving testimony from Richard M. Nephew, Coordinator on Global 
Anti-Corruption, Department of State; and Shannon Green, Assistant to 
the Administrator, Bureau for Democracy, Human Rights, and Governance, 
Agency for International Development.
```
