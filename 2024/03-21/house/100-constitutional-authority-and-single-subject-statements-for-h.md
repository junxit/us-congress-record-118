# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7788

**Measures:** H.R. 7788

```
           By Mr. SOTO:
       H.R. 7788.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would set up a Firefighter's Compensation fund for 
     firefighters or their families to claim compensation for 
     illness or death caused by their exposure to PFAS through 
     their profession.
```
