# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7770

**Measures:** H.R. 7770

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 7770.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 6 empowers Congress to coin 
     money.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in 
     recognition and celebration of the women who contributed to 
     the Home Front during World War II.
```
