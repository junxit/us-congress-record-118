# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7771

**Measures:** H.R. 7771

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 7771.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 36, United States Code, to designate 
     ``National Rosie the Riveter Day'' and request the President 
     to issue an annual proclamation.
```
