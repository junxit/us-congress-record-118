# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7773

**Measures:** H.R. 7773

```
           By Mr. HORSFORD:
       H.R. 7773.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to provide 
     additional entitlement to Post-9/11 Educational Assistance to 
     certain veterans and members of the Armed Forces who require 
     extra time to complete remedial and deficiency courses, and 
     for other purposes.
```
