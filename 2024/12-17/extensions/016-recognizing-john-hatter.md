# RECOGNIZING JOHN HATTER

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JOHN HATTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 17, 2024

  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and express 
gratitude to a terrific public servant, a trusted advisor to me and so 
many others, John Hatter. John Hatter, my District Director, has been 
serving the Hoosiers of Indiana's Sixth District for over a decade.
  John has consistently given my constituents the support they need 
when dealing with the Department of Veterans Affairs and Department of 
Defense. John is a humble man and a role model for public service.
  As this chapter in Congress comes to a close, I am beyond grateful 
for John, his service, and his friendship. Mr. Speaker, may God bless 
John.
```
