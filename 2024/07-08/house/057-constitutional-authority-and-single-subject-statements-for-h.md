# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8938

**Measures:** H.R. 8938

```
           By Mr. FULCHER:
       H.R. 8938.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 provides Congress with the 
     power to `lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and Excises' 
     in order to `provide for the general Welfare of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude 
     overtime compensation from gross income for purposes of 
     income tax.
```
