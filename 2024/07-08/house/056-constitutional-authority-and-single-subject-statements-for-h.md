# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8937

**Measures:** H.R. 8937

```
           By Mr. GRIJALVA:
       H.R. 8937.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:--
       U.S. Const. art. I, sec. 8, cl. 3
       U.S. Cont. art. IV, sec. 3, cl. 2, sen. a
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish subaccounts in the Indian Water Rights 
     Settlement Completion Fund to satisfy the obligations of the 
     United States with respect to certain Indian water rights 
     settlements.
```
