# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8949

**Measures:** H.R. 8949

```
           By Mr. SCHWEIKERT:
       H.R. 8949.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To approve the settlement of water rights claims of the 
     Yavapai-Apache Nation, to authorize construction of a water 
     project relating to those water rights claims, and for other 
     purposes.
```
