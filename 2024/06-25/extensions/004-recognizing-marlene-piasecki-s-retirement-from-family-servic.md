# RECOGNIZING MARLENE PIASECKI'S RETIREMENT FROM FAMILY SERVICE ASSOCIATION OF BUCKS COUNTY

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
     RECOGNIZING MARLENE PIASECKI'S RETIREMENT FROM FAMILY SERVICE 
                      ASSOCIATION OF BUCKS COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 25, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extremely 
notable constituent within my district, Marlene Piasecki of Washington 
Crossing, Pennsylvania who is retiring from Family Service Association 
of Bucks County, a non-profit agency located in Langhorne, Pennsylvania 
after 23 years of dedicated service.
  Marlene graduated from the University of Pennsylvania with a Masters 
in Social Work She has dedicated her life to social services. Holding 
several positions within Family Service from Director of Development to 
Chief Operating Officer to Interim Chief Executive Officer upon the 
sudden passing of their CEO, Audrey J Tucker, Marlene will be 
remembered for her mentorship, leadership and support of the staff 
members and the Bucks County community over these 23 years.
  Today, I am proud to recognize Marlene Piasecki as an individual who 
has devoted her career to helping others and helping to meet the social 
service needs of Bucks County's residents.
  We are all incredibly grateful for the positive impact Marlene has 
had through her long career, and we wish her much enjoyment in her 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
