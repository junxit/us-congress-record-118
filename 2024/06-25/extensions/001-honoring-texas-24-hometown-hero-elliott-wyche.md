# HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ELLIOTT WYCHE

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
             HONORING TEXAS-24 HOMETOWN HERO ELLIOTT WYCHE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 25, 2024

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor our Texas-24 Hometown 
Hero, Elliott Wyche. A native of Hurst, Elliott has shown us the 
boundless nature of a child's magical imagination. Born with half a 
heart and having undergone three open heart surgeries, Elliott has 
battled immense challenges with remarkable courage and resilience.
  Last month, Elliott not only inspired L.D. Bell High School's fine 
arts department through his creative vision, but he also played an 
integral role in their musical production. Elliott submitted his 
original story in the form of illustrations to the Sing Me a Story 
Foundation, which gave his drawings to Austin composer Mitch Gilly to 
transform into music.
  The school's orchestra, choir, and theater students rehearsed all 
spring to bring Elliott's creativity to life. The story, titled 
``Smashers Crashers,'' follows three superheroes who unite to defeat 
evil. Both his story and the school's heartwarming gesture are a 
testament to the broader sense of community and compassion that makes 
North Texas special.
  Elliott has inspired North Texans to overcome adversity with grace, 
joy, and extraordinary spirit. I look forward to seeing his ambition 
grow from here.

                          ____________________
```
