# HONORING MIKE HOWARD

**Speaking:** LaTurner, Jake (L000266) [R-KS]

```
                          HONORING MIKE HOWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JAKE LaTURNER

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 25, 2024

  Mr. LaTURNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in appreciation for a member 
of my staff, Mike Howard, who has recently embarked on a new job off 
the Hill after nearly three years of service as my communications 
director. Mike began in my office in August of 2021, and has served 
since then as a trusted advisor who has constantly sharpened our team 
and ensured that we are able to get out the message of our work here in 
Congress to my constituents from Galena to Atchison.
  Mike has spent countless early mornings and late nights preparing 
with me for interviews, town halls, committee hearings, and making sure 
that we are able to clearly explain the work that we do here. Mike will 
be sorely missed as he moves on to this new role, but I would like to 
thank him once again for his dedication and commitment to the people of 
the Second Congressional District of Kansas.

                          ____________________
```
