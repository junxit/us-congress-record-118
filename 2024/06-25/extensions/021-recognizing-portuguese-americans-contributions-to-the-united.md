# RECOGNIZING PORTUGUESE AMERICANS' CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Duarte, John S. (D000633) [R-CA]

```
  RECOGNIZING PORTUGUESE AMERICANS' CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE UNITED STATES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN S. DUARTE

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 25, 2024

  Mr. DUARTE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the month of June 
as National Portuguese Heritage Month, honoring the contributions 
Portuguese Americans make to our country.
  As the descendant of immigrants from the Azores, I admire the rich 
culture and history of Portuguese communities in the Central Valley, 
throughout California, and across the country. To me, Portuguese 
Heritage Month brings back memories of Festas--celebrations spent 
enjoying Portuguese culture, and food. I for one, have spent many days 
with friends and family enjoying sopas, sweet bread, and linguica. 
Bullfights, parades, and, of course, the bodo de leite are cherished 
traditions in our Valley. These events are made possible by the 
dedication of cultural organizations who commit themselves to keeping 
Portuguese heritage alive.
  Over 350,000 Portuguese Americans call California home--the largest 
community in the United States. This community shapes life in our 
State, and, especially, the Central Valley. The story of the Valley 
would be impossible without the stories of thousands of Azoreans who 
crossed an ocean and a continent determined to live the American Dream. 
Today, that dream is realized. Portuguese-Americans are leaders in 
dairy and farming, serve proudly in the U.S. military, and give back to 
our country in countless ways. Three Representatives of Portuguese 
heritage serve our Valley in Congress, and I am honored to be among 
them.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House to join me in proudly 
recognizing Portuguese Americans' invaluable contributions to our 
Nation's history, culture, and prosperity as we celebrate National 
Portuguese Heritage Month.
```
