# HONORING JACKIE HUNLEY

**Speaking:** LaTurner, Jake (L000266) [R-KS]

```
                         HONORING JACKIE HUNLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JAKE LaTURNER

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 25, 2024

  Mr. LaTURNER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in appreciation for a member 
of my staff for her dedicated service to the people of Kansas. Jackie 
Hunley began in my office in October of 2021 and since then has 
dedicated her time, energy, and expertise to improving the lives of the 
people of the Second Congressional District. From serving as a 
legislative correspondent and ensuring we provide thoughtful replies to 
each constituent letter to shaping our office's appropriations process 
as my legislative director, Jackie has made our office better with her 
quick wit, work ethic, and desire to serve others.
  Jackie has become an adopted Kansan during her time with our team, 
not least because she met her husband, Chance--a fellow Southeast 
Kansan, while serving on our staff. While we will miss her as she has 
moved on to serve her own home state of Iowa, I extend my deepest 
gratitude for her time of service to myself and my constituents and 
look forward to seeing her excel in her new role.
```
