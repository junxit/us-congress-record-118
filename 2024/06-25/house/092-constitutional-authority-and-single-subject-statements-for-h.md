# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8835

**Measures:** H.R. 8835

```
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 8835.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title II of the Social Security Act to exempt 
     widows and widowers of veterans from the government pension 
     offset.
```
