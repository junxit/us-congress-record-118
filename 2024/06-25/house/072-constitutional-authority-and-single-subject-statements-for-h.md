# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8815

**Measures:** H.R. 8815

```
           By Mr. SMITH of Nebraska:
       H.R. 8815.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct states to consult with youth and families with 
     lived experience when developing their state child welfare 
     plans under Title IV-B of the Social Security Act.
```
