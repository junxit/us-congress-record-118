# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8823

**Measures:** H.R. 8823

```
           By Mr. HUIZENGA:
       H.R. 8823.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 23, United States Code, to direct the 
     Secretary of Transportation to withhold from States certain 
     apportionments if the States do not make reasonable efforts 
     to prohibit certain roadway obstruction, and for other 
     purposes.
```
