# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8820

**Measures:** H.R. 8820

```
           By Mr. BURLISON:
       H.R. 8820.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for an above-the-line tax deduction for long-
     term care insurance premiums and require the IRS to redirect 
     funds from specified tax credits.
```
