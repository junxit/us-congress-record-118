# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7619

**Measures:** H.R. 7619

```
           By Mr. NEGUSE:
       H.R. 7619.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Health and Human Services to 
     issue guidance to States on strategies under Medicaid and 
     CHIP to increase mental health and substance use disorder 
     care provider education, training, recruitment, and 
     retention.
```
