# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7612

**Measures:** H.R. 7612

```
           By Mrs. HOUCHIN:
       H.R. 7612.

       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.

       To affirm and protect the First Amendment rights of
```
