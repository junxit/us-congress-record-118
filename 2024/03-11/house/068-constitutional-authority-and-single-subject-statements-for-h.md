# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7610

**Measures:** H.R. 7610

```
           By Mr. HIGGINS of Louisiana:
       H.R. 7610.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution, Congress 
     has the power ``to make all Laws which shall be necessary and 
     proper for carrying into Execution the foregoing Powers, and 
     all other Powers vested by this Constitution in the 
     Government of the United States, or any Department or Officer 
     thereof''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to clarify 
     utility line technicians qualify as emergency response 
     providers.
```
