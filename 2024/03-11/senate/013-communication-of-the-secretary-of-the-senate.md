# COMMUNICATION OF THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 4366

```
              COMMUNICATION OF THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair lays before the Senate a 
communication from the Secretary of the Senate regarding a message from 
the President received during the adjournment of the Senate.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       Dear Madam President, on Saturday, March 9, 2024, the 
     President of the United States sent by messenger the attached 
     sealed envelope addressed to the President of the Senate 
     dated March 9, 2024, said to contain a message regarding the 
     designation of funding as an emergency requirement in H.R. 
     4366, the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2024. The Senate 
     not being in session on the day which the President delivered 
     this message, I accepted the message at 12:05 p.m., and I now 
     present to you the President's message, with the accompanying 
     papers, for disposition by the Senate. Respectfully, Sonceria 
     A. Berry, Secretary of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
