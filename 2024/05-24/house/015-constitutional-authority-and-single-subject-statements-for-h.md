# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8569

**Measures:** H.R. 8569

```
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER:
       H.R. 8569.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The No Private Rides Act prohibits the use of federal funds 
     for a passenger motor vehicle or a personal driver for the 
     chief executive officer of the Metropolitan Transportation 
     Authority.
```
