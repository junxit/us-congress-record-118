# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9973

**Measures:** H.R. 9973

```
           By Mr. JACKSON of Illinois:
       H.R. 9973.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       In compliacne with Art 1 of the Consitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would compel government agencies to 
     release and require public disclosure of all records related 
     to the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)'s nefarious 
     COINTELPRO operation, a series of covert and illegal projects 
     aimed at surveilling, infiltrating, discrediting and 
     disrupting domestic political organizations.
```
