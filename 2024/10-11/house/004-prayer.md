# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Dr. Edrees Bridges, Chaplain, United States Capitol Police, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  O merciful benefactor, omnipotent source that redeems, as we open the 
Chamber of democracy, fill our hearts with Your presence.
  Protect, enlighten, and inspire our congressional leaders as they 
fulfill their role of representing the people and fostering unity 
across districts near and far to promote solidarity and progress for 
all.
  Grant them patience when discomfort and uncertainty threaten to cloud 
their judgment.
  Instill in them perseverance and the courage to follow Your guidance, 
even when it is unpopular or difficult.
  O hearer of our call, give them strength as they stand by their 
convictions while also being curious about the stance of others and 
open to the views of those whose convictions contrast or differ.
  Protect each Member and their staff. Exalt their efforts as they 
carry out their duties. Protect the men and women of the United States 
Capitol Police, who safeguard them, and those who visit these sacred 
grounds.
  For You are the exalter, and You are our protector.
  We ask this with Your mighty name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
