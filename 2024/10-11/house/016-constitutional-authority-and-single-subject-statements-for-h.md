# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9957

**Measures:** H.R. 9957

```
           By Mr. CURTIS:
       H.R. 9957.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Energy to conduct a study of the
```
