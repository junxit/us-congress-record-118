# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9980

**Measures:** H.R. 9980

```
           By Ms. MENG:
       H.R. 9980.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Improving access to kosher and halal foods across all USDA 
     feeding programs, including The Emergency Food Assistance 
     Program (TEFAP), by creating an office of kosher and halal 
     foods within the U.S. Department of Agriculture.
```
