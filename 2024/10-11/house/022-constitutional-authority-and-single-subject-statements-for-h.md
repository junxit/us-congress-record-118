# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9963

**Measures:** H.R. 9963

```
           By Mr. DeSAULNIER:
       H.R. 9963.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require employers who are ordering a plant closing or 
     mass layoff to cover the cost of an economic impact study in 
     each impacted unit of local government and to update the 
     Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act
```
