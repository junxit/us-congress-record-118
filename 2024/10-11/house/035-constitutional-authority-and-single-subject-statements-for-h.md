# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9976

**Measures:** H.R. 9976

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 9976.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Robert Levinson Hostage Recovery and Hostage-
```
