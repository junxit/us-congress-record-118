# HONORING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF FRESNO PACIFIC UNIVERSITY

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
       HONORING THE 80TH ANNIVERSARY OF FRESNO PACIFIC UNIVERSITY

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Mr. COSTA. Mr. Speaker, we gather to commemorate a remarkable 
milestone in the field of education--the 80th Anniversary of Fresno 
Pacific University. This historic occasion is a testament to the 
enduring legacy and unwavering commitment of Fresno Pacific University 
to improving education in the community.
  Fresno Pacific University encompasses a grand and diverse student 
body which entails many of the students being the first in their 
families to attend college. This has been possible partly due to the 
impressive campus locations from North and Southeast Fresno to Visalia, 
Bakersfield and Merced. These expansive locations have opened up many 
opportunities to those who believed that higher education was out of 
their reach. By implementing the proud Hispanic Serving Institution, 
Hispanic first-generation students graduate at the same rate as 
students overall.
  The implementation of this luminary program has provided countless 
educational opportunities for Hispanic Americans and underrepresented 
populations. It has also created a line of success for many career 
fields such as business, criminology, medicine, nursing, psychology and 
many other professional fields.
  The dedication and devotion to the success of their students is 
unparalleled with a faculty staff who are active in their professional 
field and dedicated to student success. Small class sizes encourage 
student/faculty relationships.
  With the School of Arts & Sciences and the School of Graduate and 
Professional Studies,

[[Page E1026]]

Fresno Pacific University has ranked in 78th place recently on the 2024 
Best United States Colleges list and ranked 41st overall in the 
Master's Universities--West category. Having such a glorious 
performance reputation has yielded an affluent impact on the community 
with over 66% of students completing their degrees within the four-year 
time while the national average is only 42%.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my esteemed colleagues to join me in honoring 
the 80th Anniversary milestone of Fresno Pacific University. Let us 
celebrate the diligence and devotion to the education of others and the 
community.
```
