# HONORING MRS. ALMA E. RANGEL

**Speaking:** Lee, Barbara (L000551) [D-CA]

```
                      HONORING MRS. ALMA E. RANGEL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BARBARA LEE

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Ms. LEE of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and 
legacy of a dear friend, dedicated wife and loving mother, Mrs Alma 
Rangel. She was an extraordinary woman who embodied strength, grace, 
and unwavering dedication to her family and community.
  I was fortunate enough to get to know Alma, laugh with Alma, and 
share many special moments with her. As a former staffer in the 1970s, 
I will always remember how Mrs. Rangel welcomed staff into her 
beautiful home for Sunday brunch after the Congressional Black Caucus 
Annual Legislative Conference. Her warmth and kindness left a lasting 
impression on everyone she met, and her commitment to public service 
was evident. She was a founding member and longtime chair of the 
Congressional Black Spouses, and led fundraisers to provide 
scholarships to students around the country. Mrs. Rangel worked 
tirelessly throughout her life to better the lives of those around her.
  As a member of Congress, I had the privilege to travel throughout the 
world with Mrs. Rangel and her loving husband, Congressman Charlie 
Rangel--and always enjoyed our private times together. Alma was a 
fierce advocate, and we bonded over our passion for human rights and 
women's rights efforts around the world. Her work with CBC Spouses, the 
Ralph J. Rangel Foundations and other organizations have had a 
significant impact on women and families both in the U.S. and abroad.
  Alma's legacy is one of service, resilience, and dedication. My 
heartfelt condolences go out to Congressman Rangel and all of Alma's 
family and loved ones. She will be deeply missed and fondly remembered. 
May she rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
