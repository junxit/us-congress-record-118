# COMMEMORATING NATIONAL BREAST CANCER AWARENESS MONTH

**Speaking:** Quigley, Mike (Q000023) [D-IL]

```
          COMMEMORATING NATIONAL BREAST CANCER AWARENESS MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE QUIGLEY

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Mr. QUIGLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate National Breast 
Cancer Awareness Month and join the Lynn Sage Breast Cancer Foundation 
in celebrating the unveiling of the BIG hope sculpture by Chicago 
artist Matthew Hoffman.
  Breast cancer is the second most common cancer present in women in 
the United States. Over the course of 2024, it is expected that about 
310,720 new cases of invasive breast cancer will be diagnosed in women, 
and about 42,250 women will die from breast cancer. These numbers are 
far too high.
  Since 1985, the Lynn Sage Breast Cancer Foundation has led the fight 
against breast cancer, raising funds for breast cancer research and 
doctors in the Chicagoland area. This organization is named for Lynn 
Sage who died at only 39 years old as a result of her breast cancer. 
The Lynn Sage Breast Cancer Foundation provides funding for research 
grants and fellowships. The organization also holds the annual Lynn 
Sage Breast Cancer Symposium which assembles breast cancer research 
leaders from across the world. The BIG hope sculpture is yet another 
example of their dedication to raising awareness of the fight against 
breast cancer. This sculpture serves as a symbol of hope for those 
impacted by the disease, and it will raise funds which will support the 
Chicago Breast Cancer Research Consortium.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me and all our colleagues in the House of 
Representatives in recognizing the work of the Lynn Sage Breast Cancer 
Foundation and congratulating them on the unveiling of this artwork.
```
