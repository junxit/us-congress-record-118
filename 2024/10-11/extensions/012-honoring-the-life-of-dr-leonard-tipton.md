# HONORING THE LIFE OF DR. LEONARD TIPTON

**Speaking:** Wasserman Schultz, Debbie (W000797) [D-FL]

```
                HONORING THE LIFE OF DR. LEONARD TIPTON

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, it is with a heavy heart that I 
rise to recognize the life of Dr. Leonard Tipton, who was a remarkably 
impactful individual until he sadly left us at 84 years old.
  Dr. Tipton was an American success story. The first in his family to 
graduate from high school, he would go on to earn a Ph.D. from the 
University of Wisconsin, ultimately bringing his talents to the 
University of Florida.
  Dr. Tipton made important contributions to Florida and beyond, 
serving as a Professor in the College of Journalism and Communications 
at the University of Florida from 1985 until his retirement in 2005.
  As a renowned scholar, Dr. Tipton pioneered work on survey research 
methodology and the role the media plays in setting, not just reporting 
on, a public agenda.
  Dr. Tipton was known to be an extraordinary teacher and mentor. Dr. 
Tipton's former students have an unprecedented track record of success, 
going on to become successful academics, Oscar-winning filmmakers, 
Pulitzer Prize-winning reporters, and this Member of Congress.
  I was fortunate enough to experience firsthand the teaching and 
mentorship of this creative enthusiastic educator. He always worked to 
make sure his students had the resources and tools to succeed, 
inspiring us to achieve greatness. His generosity and love of teaching 
were the hallmarks of his career as an educator and will be fondly 
remembered by all who met him.
  May his memory be a blessing.

                          ____________________
```
