# RECOGNIZING GRACE NORDHOFF'S LEGACY

**Speaking:** Jayapal, Pramila (J000298) [D-WA]

```
                  RECOGNIZING GRACE NORDHOFF'S LEGACY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. PRAMILA JAYAPAL

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Ms. JAYAPAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Grace Nordhoff 
and her service to

[[Page E1024]]

the writers, readers, and creative minds of the Pacific Northwest. 
Grace grew up in the Seattle area. She attended college and lived in 
North Carolina for 25 years. During her time in North Carolina, Grace 
worked on the publishing staff of an alternative newspaper, earned her 
Master of Social Work and founded a bakery cafe. Since moving back to 
Seattle, Grace has been involved in her family's legacy business, the 
Skinner Corporation. She has also been a dedicated volunteer with the 
Seattle Public Library where she worked at the Central Library's 
welcome desk.
  Grace has spent decades championing literary causes. She served on 
the boards of the Durham Library Foundation, The Seattle Public Library 
Foundation and Hedgebrook. Grace has also served on the board and been 
a critical leader in ensuring the success of Hedgebrook, a literary 
nonprofit working to support visionary women writers whose stories and 
ideas shape our culture now and for generations to come. Hedgebrook, 
founded by Nancy Nordhoff (Grace's mother), has been the home to a host 
of storied women writers, offering a space of solace, welcome and 
nurturing for women to make their voices heard. Over the years, 
Hedgebrook has welcome a range of women writers, from Gloria Steinem to 
V Ensler to Naomi Shihab Nye and Sarah Ladipo Manyika.
  Hedgebrook benefited immensely from Grace's dedication. Grace served 
on the Board for 15 years where she offered transformational guidance 
in organizational development. She played a pivotal role in the 
strategic planning process and helped to lead the Sustainability 
Campaign to renew the retreat. During her tenure. Hedgebrook's annual 
budget increased from $600,000 to $1.7 million, transitioned from a 
private foundation to a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, created its annual 
fundraiser ``Equivox'' and expanded its donor base across the country.
  Grace and her family have personified Seattle philanthropy. She has 
been a dedicated supporter of women's voices in the arts, literature, 
politics and education. Our region has greatly benefited from Grace's 
dedication to supporting her community.
  On behalf of Washington's 7th Congressional District, I would like to 
commend Grace Nordhoff's work and her many accomplishments in service 
to literary causes and the Pacific Northwest.
```
