# RECOGNIZING JOSE LUIS "JOE" LUCCIONI

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                 RECOGNIZING JOSE LUIS ``JOE'' LUCCIONI

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jose Luis 
``Joe'' Luccioni's dedication to our community and outstanding 
achievements. Originally from Puerto Rico, Joe eventually moved to 
North Texas, settling in Grapevine. Joe has become a pillar for our 
community, contributing significantly to the civic and cultural life 
for 31 years.
  In 2007, Joe joined the Grapevine Police Department's Volunteers in 
Police Service (VIPS) program, where he has served with distinction for 
the past 17 years. His dedication is reflected in an extraordinary 
8,697 lifetime volunteer hours, a testament to his unwavering 
commitment to the safety and welfare of Grapevine residents. From 2017 
to 2020, Joe served as the Director of VIPS, leading with passion and 
integrity. His exemplary service was recognized in 2018 when he was 
honored with the prestigious Presidential Achievement Award for 
volunteerism, in acknowledgment of his remarkable 4,000 hours of 
service. Joe's commitment to Grapevine also extends far beyond his work 
with the Volunteers in Police Service. He played a pivotal role in the 
construction of the 9/11 Flight Crew Memorial in Grapevine, a project 
completed in 2008 to honor the brave flight crew heroes of September 
11, 2001. Joe's leadership in this initiative was critical to its 
successful completion, creating a lasting tribute that continues to 
unite and inspire the community.
  In addition to his work on the memorial, Joe has served the community 
in various capacities. He was a member of the Grapevine Parks and 
Recreation Board from 2017 to 2021, where he contributed to the 
enhancement of local parks and community spaces. Since 2014, Joe has 
also been an active member of the Shield Foundation Board, reflecting 
his deep commitment to the well-being and development of his community. 
His involvement in the Grapevine Runners and Walkers since 1997 
emphasizes his passion for promoting health and fitness within the 
community. He has received numerous awards and recognitions for his 
relentless dedication, humility, and leadership. His selfless 
contributions have left an indelible impact on the City of Grapevine, 
the Latino community, and North Texas as a whole. Joe embodies the true 
spirit of community service and leadership, demonstrating what can be 
achieved when one devotes themselves to the greater good.
  It is my pleasure to recognize Jose Luis ``Joe'' Luccioni for his 
exemplary contributions and unwavering dedication to North Texas, as 
well as congratulate him on receiving the 2024 Latino Service Award.

                          ____________________
```
