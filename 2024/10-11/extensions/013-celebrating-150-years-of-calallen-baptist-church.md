# CELEBRATING 150 YEARS OF CALALLEN BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Cloud, Michael (C001115) [R-TX]

```
            CELEBRATING 150 YEARS OF CALALLEN BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL CLOUD

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Mr. CLOUD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Calallen Baptist Church 
in Corpus Christi, Texas, as it celebrates 150 years of faithful 
service to its community. This incredible milestone stands as a 
testament to the church's enduring commitment to faith, fellowship, and 
ministry, pointing generations of men and women toward the love of 
Jesus Christ.
  Founded on July 4, 1874, Calallen Baptist Church was established by a 
group of eleven dedicated believers on a donated parcel of land in 
Nuecestown. From the very beginning, it became a pillar of the 
community--a place where neighbors gathered to hear the Gospel and 
encourage each other in the faith.
  For a century and a half, the church has faithfully served Corpus 
Christi, persevering through adversity and disaster. After the 
devastation caused by Hurricane Celia, which left countless homes 
destroyed and damaged the church itself, Calallen Baptist continued to 
hold weekly services and became a refuge for those displaced by the 
storm. This is just one example of the church's dedication to serving 
its community even in the most trying of times.
  Calallen Baptist Church stands out with their commitment to ministry 
through service to the community. Whether through outreach programs, 
compassionate ministries, or disaster relief, the church has actively 
sought to care for its neighbors and to be a testimony for the Lord's 
goodness in trial. In recent years, they have expanded their facilities 
and ministries to include fellowships, mission trips, and a grief 
recovery group--furthering their outreach to those in need of spiritual 
and emotional support.
  This anniversary is truly a testament to God's sustaining grace and 
mercy. Paul's letter to the Philippians made note of how the Lord 
``shall supply every need of yours according to his riches in glory in 
Christ Jesus'' (Philippians 4:19). Through every trial victory in 
Christ, it is this principle that defines the story of Calallen 
Baptist.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Calallen Baptist Church on 150 years of 
remarkable ministry and service. May God continue to bless and sustain 
them as they press forward in His work, sharing the Good News of Jesus 
and serving the community for many years to come.
```
