# HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BOYS & GIRLS CLUB

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
         HONORING THE 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BOYS & GIRLS CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Mr. COSTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Boys & Girls Clubs 
of Fresno County as they celebrate 75 years of dedicated service to the 
youth and families of our community. Founded in early 1949, the Boys' 
Club in downtown Fresno, provided a safe haven for young boys. Over the 
decades, thanks to the generosity of donors and community members, more 
clubs have opened, expanding their reach.
  Through challenges and opportunities, the committed board of 
directors and caring staff have worked tirelessly to create 
environments that embody their core values: Respect, Integrity, 
Passion, Fun, and Commitment. Today, over 4,000 children and teens call 
the club their second home, with programs extending beyond Fresno to 
communities like Orange Cove, Huron, Firebaugh, and Reedley.
  Boys & Girls Clubs give kids opportunities to succeed through 
impactful programs. Serving 191,000 healthy meals annually, they ensure 
members receive the nutrition they need to thrive. Remarkably, 92% of 
Club members expect to graduate from high school, demonstrating the 
positive academic environment fostered by the Club. Additionally, 96% 
of members report that staff provide clear expectations and 
opportunities to better themselves, highlighting the supportive 
atmosphere that encourages personal growth. Formula for Impact ensures 
every child is welcomed and supported in areas of Academic Success, 
Healthy Lifestyles, and Good Character & Citizenship.
  Programs like Power Hour provide essential homework help, while 
Project Learn enhances educational performance through enrichment 
activities. The My Future initiative builds digital skills and fosters 
safe online interactions. Financial literacy is promoted through Money 
Matters, teaching young members about saving and investing.
  In health, Triple Play promotes nutrition and physical activity, and 
programs like SMART Moves and SMART Girls address vital prevention 
education. For character development, initiatives such as Youth of the 
Year and Keystone cultivate future leaders.
  As we celebrate this milestone, let us recognize the transformative 
impact the Boys & Girls Clubs have had on the lives of over 1 million 
young people. Their focus on academic success, healthy lifestyles, and 
character development is vital for shaping future leaders.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my esteemed colleagues to join me in 
celebrating the Boys and Girls Club Fresno County for their continuous 
commitment to our community.
```
