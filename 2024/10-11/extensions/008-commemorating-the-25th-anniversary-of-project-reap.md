# COMMEMORATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF PROJECT REAP

**Speaking:** Norton, Eleanor Holmes (N000147) [D-DC]

```
           COMMEMORATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF PROJECT REAP

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. ELEANOR HOLMES NORTON

                        of district of columbia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 11, 2024

  Ms. NORTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Project REAP (the Real 
Estate Associate Program).
  Project REAP, which was founded in the District of Columbia, is 
celebrating over 25 years of service to the commercial real estate 
industry and professionals of color. Its silver jubilee event will be 
held in D.C. on October 16, 2024.
  Project REAP was formed by commercial real estate executives who 
recognized that the industry's workforce was grossly unrepresentative 
of the demographics of the United States. Project REAP provides 
professional education, networking and employment opportunities for 
professionals of color in the commercial real estate industry.
  Project REAP's mission is ``to advance diversity, equity and 
inclusion in the commercial real estate industry through education, 
mentorship and partnerships.'' Through its work, Project REAP has 
helped address historic barriers to participation by professionals of 
color in the commercial real estate industry.
  Nearly 2,000 professionals of color have graduated from Project REAP 
and proudly identify themselves as Project REAP alumni. Project REAP 
alumni have obtained employment with Fortune 500 companies, as well as 
major commercial real estate firms. Throughout its history, Project 
REAP has been supported by or collaborated with some of the nation's 
leading commercial real estate industry associations.
  I ask the House of Representatives to join me in recognizing Project 
REAP.
```
