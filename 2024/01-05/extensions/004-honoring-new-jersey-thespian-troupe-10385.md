# HONORING NEW JERSEY THESPIAN TROUPE 10385

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
               HONORING NEW JERSEY THESPIAN TROUPE 10385

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 5, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Thespian 
Troupe 10385 at Hillsborough High School for their commitment to 
serving New Jersey and their community.
  This school raised 603 pounds of food for the Hillsborough Township 
Community Assistance Network Food Pantry as part of the Trick or Treat 
so Kids Can Eat program. With their dedication and persistence, New 
Jersey was able to collect 8,953.17 pounds of food with 11 schools and 
255 Thespians participating. It is students like Thespian Troupe 10385 
that exemplify the best of New Jersey, working towards a cause and 
giving back to the community.
  I am incredibly proud of the students in this troupe and thank them 
for their service.

                          ____________________
```
