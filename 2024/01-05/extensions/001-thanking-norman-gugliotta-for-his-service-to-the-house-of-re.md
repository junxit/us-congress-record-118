# THANKING NORMAN GUGLIOTTA FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Norton, Eleanor Holmes (N000147) [D-DC]

```
       THANKING NORMAN GUGLIOTTA FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. ELEANOR HOLMES NORTON

                      of the district of columbia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 5, 2024

  Ms. NORTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to thank Norman Gugliotta, a District 
of Columbia resident who is retiring today after 18 years of 
outstanding service to the House of Representatives in several 
administrative and support roles.
  Norman started as a contractor with the House Child Care Center in 
2005 and then worked for various offices of the Chief Administrative 
Officer, including Payroll & Benefits, Accounting, First Call and the 
Office of Financial Counseling, where he has been a Financial Counselor 
since 2007. He provided financial guidance and services to House 
offices, helping to ensure that their expenditures adhered to 
applicable House rules and accounting standards.
  With his knowledge, experience and courteousness, Norman was an 
invaluable asset to the House and respected by his coworkers and 
colleagues. Norman is a big fan of the ``Star Wars'' movie franchise. 
He has participated in conventions, Awesome & Comic Cons, parades and 
charitable endeavors, including visits to children's hospitals while in 
Star Wars costumes. In retirement, Norman plans to travel with his 
partner to help coordinate events for the 501st Legion Star Wars Group 
and Washington Nationals Star Wars Day.
  I ask the House to join me in thanking Norman for his distinguished 
service to the House and the nation it serves. I wish him all the best 
as he begins this new chapter in his life.

                          ____________________
```
