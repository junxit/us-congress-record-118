# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 3, 2024

```
              ADJOURNMENT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 3, 2024

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until 12 noon on Wednesday, January 3, 2024, 
and will reconvene for the second session of the 118th Congress.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:48 and 1 second a.m., adjourned until 
Wednesday, January 3, 2024, at 12 noon.
```
