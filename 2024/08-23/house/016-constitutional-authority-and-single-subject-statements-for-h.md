# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9397

**Measures:** H.R. 9397

```
           By Mrs. CAMMACK:
       H.R. 9397.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit Federal funds from being made available to any 
     law enforcement agency that employs an alien as a law 
     enforcement officer.
```
