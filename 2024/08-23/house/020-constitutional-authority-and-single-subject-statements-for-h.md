# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9401

**Measures:** H.R. 9401

```
           By Mr. DIAZ-BALART:
       H.R. 9401.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 4, United States Code, to ensure that a 
     funeral honors detail recites the 13 Folds of Honor when 
     presenting a flag of the United States in connection with the 
     death of a member of the Armed Forces or veteran.
```
