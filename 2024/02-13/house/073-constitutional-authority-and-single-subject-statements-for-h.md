# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7330

**Measures:** H.R. 7330

```
           By Mr. GAETZ:
       H.R. 7330.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Secure Fence Act of 2006 to authorize certain 
     States to erect temporary protective fencing within 25 miles 
     of the southwest border to deter unlawful immigra-tion, and 
     for other purposes.
```
