# HONORING THE LIVES OF FIVE U.S. MARINES

**Speaking:** Kiggans, Jennifer A (K000399) [R-VA]

```
                HONORING THE LIVES OF FIVE U.S. MARINES

  (Mrs. KIGGANS asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. KIGGANS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the lives of five 
United

[[Page H575]]

States Marines lost in an H-53 helicopter crash in San Diego, 
California, last week.
  These young men embodied what it means to be military pilots and U.S. 
Marines. As aviators, we know that risks, even during routine training 
missions, are part of the job. Our Nation will be forever indebted for 
their sacrifice.
  Captain Jack Casey was a 2019 graduate of the Virginia Military 
Institute from Dover, New Hampshire. He was 26.
  Captain Miguel Nava was a United States Naval Academy graduate from 
Traverse City, Michigan. He was 28.
  Captain Benjamin Moulton was from Emmett, Idaho. He was 27.
  Lance Corporal Donovan Davis was from Olathe, Kansas. He was 21.
  Sergeant Alec Langen was from Chandler, Arizona. He was 23.
  These five marines will forever be remembered in the military 
aviation community. Please keep their family and friends in your 
prayers. We are eternally grateful for their service and sacrifice to 
our great Nation.

                          ____________________
```
