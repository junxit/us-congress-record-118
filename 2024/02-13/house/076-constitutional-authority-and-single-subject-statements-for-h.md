# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7333

**Measures:** H.R. 7333

```
           By Mr. MAST:
       H.R. 7333.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18--To make all Laws which

     any Department or Officer thereof.

       Would rename the VA Medical Center located at 7305 N 
     Military Trail West Palm Beach, FL 33410, in honor of Mr.
```
