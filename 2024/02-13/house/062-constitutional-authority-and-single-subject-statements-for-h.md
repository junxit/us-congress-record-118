# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7319

**Measures:** H.R. 7319

```
           By Ms. TENNEY:
       H.R. 7319.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to prohibit 
     501(c)(3) organizations from providing direct funding to 
     official election organizations and to amend the Help America 
     Vote Act of 2002 to prohibit the District of Columbia from 
     receiving or using funds or certain donations from private 
     entities for the administration of DC elections.
```
