# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 7176

```
Committee Meetings
UNLOCKING OUR DOMESTIC LNG POTENTIAL ACT OF 2024
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H.R. 7176, the 
``Unlocking our Domestic LNG Potential Act of 2024''. The Committee 
granted, by a record vote of 9-4, a rule providing for consideration of 
H.R. 7176, the ``Unlocking our Domestic LNG Potential Act of 2024''. 
The rule provides for consideration of H.R. 7176, the ``Unlocking our 
Domestic LNG Potential Act of 2024'', under a closed rule. The rule 
waives all points of order against consideration of the bill. The rule 
provides that the bill shall be considered as read. The rule waives all 
points of order against provisions in the bill. The rule provides one 
hour of general debate equally divided and controlled by the chair and 
ranking minority member of the Committee on Energy and Commerce or 
their respective designees. Finally, the rule provides for one motion 
to recommit. Testimony was heard from Representatives Duncan and 
Pallone.
BUSINESS MEETING
Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: Full Committee held a 
business meeting on granting access to certain intelligence reporting 
to all Members of the House. This meeting was closed.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
