# BOYLE COUNTY FOOTBALL--STATE CHAMPIONS

**Speaking:** Comer, James (C001108) [R-KY]

```
                 BOYLE COUNTY FOOTBALL--STATE CHAMPIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAMES COMER

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 14, 2024

  Mr. COMER. Mr. Speaker, it gives me great pleasure today to rise and 
recognize the Boyle County Rebels, a football powerhouse, in the 1st 
Congressional District of Kentucky upon their recent winning of the 
state championship.
  I am honored to recognize the Boyle County Rebels on their fourth 
consecutive state championship. The hours of hard work that this team 
puts in both on and off the field does not go unnoticed as they ended 
their undefeated season as champions.
  I want to congratulate all the players, staff, faculty, and the 
entire community of Boyle County on this great achievement. This 
football program has had historic success, and I have no doubt they 
will continue their winning traditions in the future.
  I join with the community of Boyle County in celebrating the Rebels 
football team in their fourth consecutive 4A state championship.

                          ____________________
```
