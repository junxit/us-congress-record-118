# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7345

**Measures:** H.R. 7345

```
           By Mr. BRECHEEN:
       H.R. 7345.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation makes it out of order for the House to 
     adjourn for more than three calendar days during July or 
     August unless all appropriations bills are complete.
```
