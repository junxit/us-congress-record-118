# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7359

**Measures:** H.R. 7359

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 7359.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide a framework for the Bureau of Consumer Financial 
     Protection and the Department of Education to coordinate in 
     providing assistance to and serving borrowers seeking to 
     resolve complaints related to their private education or 
     Federal student loans, and for other purposes.
```
