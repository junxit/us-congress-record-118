# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7358

**Measures:** H.R. 7358

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 7358.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To ensure that the five generous general licenses issued by 
     the Office of Foreign Assets Control pursuant to the Houthis' 
     SDGT designation are removed, and that general licenses 
     related to the Houthis issued on or after January 17 must be 
     approved by an Act of Congress.
```
