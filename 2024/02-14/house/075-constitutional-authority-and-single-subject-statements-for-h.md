# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7352

**Measures:** H.R. 7352

```
           By Mr. FROST:
       H.R. 7352.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 and 18 of the U.S. 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit States from prohibiting or otherwise deterring 
     the usage of any merchant category code established by the 
     International Organization for Standardization, including 
     codes that identify firearm merchants and ammunition 
     merchants.
```
