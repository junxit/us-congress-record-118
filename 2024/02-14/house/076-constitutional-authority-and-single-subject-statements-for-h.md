# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7353

**Measures:** H.R. 7353

```
           By Mr. KEATING:
       H.R. 7353.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Legislation to provide temporary war risk insurance for 
     certain commercial vessels operating importing goods to, or 
     exporting goods from, Ukraine.
```
