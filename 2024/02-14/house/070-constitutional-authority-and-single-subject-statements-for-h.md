# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7347

**Measures:** H.R. 7347

```
           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.R. 7347.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To Amend Title 38, United States Code, to direct the 
     Secretary of Veterans Affairs to report on whether the 
     Secretary will include certain psychedelic drugs in the 
     formulary of the Department of Veterans Affairs
```
