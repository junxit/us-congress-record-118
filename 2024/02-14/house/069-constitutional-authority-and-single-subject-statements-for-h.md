# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7346

**Measures:** H.R. 7346

```
           By Ms. UNDERWOOD:
       H.R. 7346.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of congress to make rules for the common defense 
     and general welfare of the United States as enumerated in 
     Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Public Health Service Act to provide for the 
     continued implementation of the Climate and Health program by 
     the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
```
