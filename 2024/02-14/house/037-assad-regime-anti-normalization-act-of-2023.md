# ASSAD REGIME ANTI-NORMALIZATION ACT OF 2023

**Measures:** H.R. 3202

```
              ASSAD REGIME ANTI-NORMALIZATION ACT OF 2023

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 3202) to prohibit any official action to recognize 
or normalize relations with any Government of Syria that is led by 
Bashar al-Assad, and for other purposes, as amended, on which the yeas 
and nays were ordered.
```
