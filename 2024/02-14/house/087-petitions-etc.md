# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-10. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislature 
     of Guam, relative to Resolution No. 29-37, requesting for the 
     U.S. Congress to appropriate monies for Guam non-military 
     residents who were exposed to Agent Orange and who are 
     suffering from ailments caused by exposure to Agent Orange; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
```
