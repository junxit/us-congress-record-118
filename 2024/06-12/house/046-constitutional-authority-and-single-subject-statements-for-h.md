# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8709

**Measures:** H.R. 8709

```
           By Mr. GALLEGO:
       H.R. 8709.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To appropriate funds for the Federal Communications 
     Commissions' ``rip and replace'' program and Affordable 
     Connectivity Program, to improve the Affordable Connectivity 
     Program, to require a spectrum auction, and for other 
     purposes.
```
