# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8708

**Measures:** H.R. 8708

```
           By Mr. BILIRAKIS:
       H.R. 8708.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       a bill that would eliminate Diversity, Equity, and 
     Inclusion (DEI) programs at colleges and universities, if 
     those schools receive federal funds.
```
