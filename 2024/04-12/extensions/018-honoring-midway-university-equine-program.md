# HONORING MIDWAY UNIVERSITY EQUINE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Barr, Andy (B001282) [R-KY]

```
               HONORING MIDWAY UNIVERSITY EQUINE PROGRAM

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY BARR

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 12, 2024

  Mr. BARR. Mr Speaker, I rise today to honor the Equine Studies 
program at Midway University.
  Midway University is located in the heart of Kentucky's horse 
country. The equine industry is one of Kentucky's signature industries. 
Seeing an opportunity to train Midway students for support staff jobs, 
the University began their equine program in 1974. Stables and horses 
were leased, and in the first year 50 students were enrolled. The 
program grew, and in 1978 plans were underway for an indoor arena on 
campus.
  Over the years, the program has evolved from basic horsemanship and 
hands-on skills to much broader studies. Riding courses developed into 
competitive college-sponsored teams. Students now have the opportunity 
to concentrate in Equine Management, Equine Rehabilitation, or Equine 
Science. Students have access to a large equine herd of various breeds 
and disciplines. Hands-on learning experiences are available on 
Midway's working horse farm. In 2023, Midway University started an 
online program in Equine Business and Sales. They also offer an online 
MBA with Equine Management concentration.
  Graduates from Midway have entered every type of equine business 
throughout Kentucky, the United States, and internationally. Over the 
past 50 years Midway has provided high-quality equine educational 
opportunities for students from across the country.
  I appreciate the work of the dedicated staff at Midway University. 
They have many accomplishments to celebrate as they look back at 50 
years of equine education. The future for the Equine Studies program is 
bright and it is my honor to recognize Midway University before the 
United States Congress.
```
