# HONORING THE MEMORY OF NOBIE MAE HILL

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                 HONORING THE MEMORY OF NOBIE MAE HILL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 12, 2024

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize longtime Inkster 
and 12th District resident Nobie Mae Hill as we mourn her passing at 
the age of one hundred eleven.
  Born and raised in Alabama, Ms. Hill was no stranger to a life of 
hard work, having spent years working on a plantation. After marrying 
and starting a family, Ms. Hill and her family moved to Michigan, 
settling in Inkster in the 1940s. She lived a full and vibrant life and 
became an integral part of the community. A trailblazer, Ms. Hill owned 
and operated two beauty salons in Inkster. She was also an active 
member of Inkster's Springhill Baptist Church.
  Nobie Mae Hill was a beloved member of the community. Her kindness 
and warmth will be missed by all who knew her. Please join me in 
honoring her memory.

                          ____________________
```
