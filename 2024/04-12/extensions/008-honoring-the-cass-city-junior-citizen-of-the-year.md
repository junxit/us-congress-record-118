# HONORING THE CASS CITY JUNIOR CITIZEN OF THE YEAR

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
           HONORING THE CASS CITY JUNIOR CITIZEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 12, 2024

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Trysten Gruber, Jr., 
the Junior Cass City Citizen of the Year, whose exceptional academic 
record, dedication to community service, and outstanding leadership in 
athletics are complemented by his remarkable character and commitment 
to personal development.
  Trysten's academic journey is marked by a consistent record of high 
honors, demonstrating his commitment to excellence. His engagement in 
community initiatives, such as organizing blood drives and 
participating in environmental cleanups, showcases his strong sense of 
civic responsibility and desire to contribute positively to our 
community.
  In athletics, Trysten has proven himself as a natural leader, serving 
as the football team captain and earning accolades including the Golden 
Helmet Award and First Team All-State honors. His achievements in 
wrestling and track further underscore his athletic ability and 
dedication.
  Beyond the realm of sports, Trysten's involvement with the Boy Scouts 
of America, peer mentoring, and the National Honor Society highlight 
his well-rounded talents and his unwavering commitment to personal 
growth and community service. His attainment of the Eagle Scout rank 
and active participation in the National Honor Society are a testament 
to his exemplary character and leadership skills.
  Today, we celebrate not just Trysten Gruber, Jr.'s past 
accomplishments but also the potential he embodies for future success 
and positive impact. His journey serves as a powerful reminder of the 
values that strengthen and enrich our community and nation, and I am 
proud that he was selected as the Cass City Junior Citizen of the Year.

                          ____________________
```
