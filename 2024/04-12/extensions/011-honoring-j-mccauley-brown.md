# HONORING J. McCAULEY BROWN

**Speaking:** Barr, Andy (B001282) [R-KY]

```
                       HONORING J. McCAULEY BROWN

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. ANDY BARR

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 12, 2024

  Mr. BARR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a special Kentuckian, J. 
McCauley (Mac) Brown. Mac lives in Louisville, Kentucky.
  Mr. Brown earned a bachelor's degree from Georgetown University and a 
Master of Business Administration from George Washington University. 
While he was in college, he worked

[[Page E342]]

during the summers at Brown-Forman Corporation, which was founded by 
his great-grandfather George Garvin Brown. After graduation in 1977, he 
began his full-time career at Brown-Forman. He worked in sales and 
marketing. He served as a Brand Director and a Regional Director before 
becoming Vice President in 2002. After a long and distinguished career 
in one of Kentucky's signature industries, Mr. Brown retired in 2015.
  Mac Brown accepted the role as Chairman on the Republican Party of 
Kentucky following his retirement in 2015. Under his leadership, the 
state party made historic advances. After a very successful eight 
years, Mr. Brown resigned his position as Chairman in 2023. He lives in 
Louisville with his wife Eileen. They have four children and six 
grandchildren.
  Mac Brown has been a leader in the distillery industry and a leader 
in the political realm. He is a man who leads with dignity, character, 
and kindness. Mac Brown is widely respected, and I am honored to lift 
up his contributions to Kentucky before Congress.

                          ____________________
```
