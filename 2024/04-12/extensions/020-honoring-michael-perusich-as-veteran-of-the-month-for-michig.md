# HONORING MICHAEL PERUSICH AS VETERAN OF THE MONTH FOR MICHIGAN'S 9TH DISTRICT

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
 HONORING MICHAEL PERUSICH AS VETERAN OF THE MONTH FOR MICHIGAN'S 9TH 
                                DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 12, 2024

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor Michael Perusich, 
our Veteran of the Month for April 2024 from Michigan's 9th 
Congressional District. It is a privilege to acknowledge the service 
and dedication of a distinguished American veteran whose contributions 
and sacrifices have left a lasting impact on our Nation.
  Born and raised in Detroit, Michigan, in 1942, Michael graduated from 
Henry Ford High School in 1961. After a brief period working in 
California in the field of electronics and communications, Michael 
returned to Michigan and enlisted in the Air Force in November 1963, 
where he specialized in Top Secret Communications Maintenance.
  Michael's service took him to the historic island of Iwo Jima from 
September 1964 to 1965, where he played a crucial role in supporting 
communications and the Far East Network from this strategic location. 
His dedication and skill were further exemplified during his two tours 
in Vietnam from 1966 to 1967, marking a significant chapter in his 
military career.
  After honorably concluding his service, Michael embarked on a 
successful career with Control Data Corporation, yet his commitment to 
serving his fellow veterans never wavered. He joined the Veterans of 
Foreign Wars Post 4659 in 1992, where he has been an active and valued 
member for over 30 years. Rising through the ranks to Sr. Vice 
Commander, Michael has been instrumental in supporting the Post, the 
Honor Guard, and the Macomb County Honor Guard, demonstrating 
unwavering dedication to the veteran community.
  Michael's contributions extend beyond his military service; he is 
deeply involved in the local community, regularly assisting in 
ceremonies honoring veterans. His recognition with the ``White Hat'' at 
the 2016 State VFW Convention is a testament to his commitment to 
veteran support programs, community service, and hospital service.
  Mr. Speaker, Michael Perusich's life is a testament to the spirit of 
American courage, resilience, and unwavering dedication to service. His 
contributions both in uniform and as a veteran advocate have 
significantly impacted the lives of many and serve as a beacon of 
inspiration for future generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in offering our gratitude 
to Michael Perusich, not only as being named my district's Veteran of 
the Month but as a true American hero whose legacy of service and 
community continues to enrich our great Nation.
```
