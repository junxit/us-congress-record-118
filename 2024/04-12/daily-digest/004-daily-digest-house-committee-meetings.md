# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
FY25 BUDGET REQUEST FOR MISSILE DEFENSE AND MISSILE DEFEAT PROGRAMS
Committee on Armed Services: Subcommittee on Strategic Forces held a 
hearing entitled ``FY25 Budget Request for Missile Defense and Missile 
Defeat Programs''. Testimony was heard from the following Department of 
Defense officials: John D. Hill, Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense 
for Space and Missile Defense, Office of the Under Secretary of Defense 
for Policy; Lieutenant General Thomas L. James, Deputy Commander, U.S. 
Space Command; Lieutenant General Heath A. Collins, Director, Missile 
Defense Agency; and Lieutenant General Sean A. Gainey, Commanding 
General, U.S. Army and Missile Defense Command.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
