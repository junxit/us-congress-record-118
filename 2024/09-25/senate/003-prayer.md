# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, who rules the raging of the sea in a world facing 
hurt and pain, help us also to see the beauty of Your creative power. 
Enable the Members of this body to sense the transcendence in the 
beauty of the Earth and the glory of the skies.
  Lord, help our Senators to hear Your music in the symphony of the 
seasons, in the whispering of the wind, and in the constellations of 
the night. May the sounds of nature's music lead our lawmakers to place 
greater trust in the movement of Your prevailing providence.
  Lord, give them spiritual power so that they will do your will on 
Earth even as it is done in Heaven.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
