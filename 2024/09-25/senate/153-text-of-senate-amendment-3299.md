# Text of Senate Amendment 3299

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
  SA 3299. Mr. SCHUMER (for Mr. Hagerty) proposed an amendment to the 
bill S. 91, to award a Congressional Gold Medal collectively to 60 
diplomats, in recognition of their bravery and heroism during the 
Holocaust; as follows:

       Amend the title to read as follows: ``To award a 
     Congressional Gold Medal collectively to 60 diplomats, in 
     recognition of their bravery and heroism during the 
     Holocaust.''.
```
