# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
cloture motion with respect to the Perry nomination be withdrawn; 
further, that the Senate vote on confirmation of the Perry nomination 
at 5:30 p.m. on Tuesday, November 12.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I move to proceed to legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion.
  The motion was agreed to.
```
