# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9843

**Measures:** H.R. 9843

```
           By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
       H.R. 9843.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the Constitution of the United States of 
     America
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To tax capital gains and dividends made from investing in 
     the PRC at the same rate as ordinary income, following a 1-
     year divestment period.
```
