# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9823

**Measures:** H.R. 9823

```
           By Mr. FLOOD:
       H.R. 9823.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to waive 
     certain distance requirements for certain hospitals electing 
     to be designated as critical access hospitals.
```
