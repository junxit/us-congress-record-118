       CONFIRMATION OF CONGRESSIONAL OBSERVER ACCESS ACT OF 2023

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the vote on the 
motion to suspend the rules and concur in the Senate amendment to the 
bill (H.R. 6513) to amend the Help America Vote Act of 2002 to confirm 
the requirement that States allow access to designated congressional 
election observers to observe the election administration procedures in 
congressional elections, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Wisconsin (Mr. Steil) that the House suspend the rules 
and concur in the Senate amendment.
