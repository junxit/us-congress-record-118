# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9834

**Measures:** H.R. 9834

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota:
       H.R. 9834.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Interior to conduct a 
     feasibility study for constructing a project to supply water 
     from the Missouri River to the Western Dakota Regional Water 
     System.
```
