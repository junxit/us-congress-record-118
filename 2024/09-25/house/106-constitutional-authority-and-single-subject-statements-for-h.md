# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9797

**Measures:** H.R. 9797

```
           By Mr. ALFORD:
       H.R. 9797.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 ``The Congress shall have 
     power to . . . provide for the . . . general welfare of the 
     United States; . . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Administrator of the SBA shall include in any policy 
     directive of the SBIR/STTR programs that each participating 
     agency includes a special emphasis on rural communities when 
     agencies conduct outreach related to the SBIR/STTR program.
```
