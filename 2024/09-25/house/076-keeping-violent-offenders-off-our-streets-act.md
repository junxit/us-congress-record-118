# KEEPING VIOLENT OFFENDERS OFF OUR STREETS ACT

**Speaking:** Fitzgerald, Scott (F000471) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 8205

```
             KEEPING VIOLENT OFFENDERS OFF OUR STREETS ACT


                  Amendment Offered by Mr. Fitzgerald

  Mr. FITZGERALD. Mr. Speaker, I have an amendment to the title of H.R. 
8205 at the desk.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will designate the amendment.
  The text of the amendment is as follows:

  Amend the title so as to read: ``A bill to amend title 18, United 
    States Code, to prohibit fraud in connection with posting bail.''.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the amendment to the 
title is agreed to.
  There was no objection.
```
