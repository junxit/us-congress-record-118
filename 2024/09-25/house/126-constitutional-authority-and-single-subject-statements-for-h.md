# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9817

**Measures:** H.R. 9817

```
           By Ms. DELAURO:
       H.R. 9817.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the United States Constitution and its 
     subsequent amendments, and further clarified and interpreted 
     by the Supreme Court of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would overhaul the Food and Drug 
     Administration's (FDA) process for determining the safety of 
     chemicals used in the food supply by requiring the FDA to 
     close the so-called GRAS loophole and make the industry's 
     chemical food additives subject to FDA approval and 
     oversight.
```
