# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9815

**Measures:** H.R. 9815

```
           By Mr. CROW:
       H.R. 9815.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill directs the Attorney General, in consultation 
     with the Secretary of Veterans Affairs, to establish a pilot 
     program to help State prisons and local jails improve the 
     documentation of incarcerated veterans.
```
