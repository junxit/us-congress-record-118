# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9625

**Measures:** H.R. 9625

```
           By Mr. ISSA:
       H.R. 9625.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 18 allows Congress to make all 
     laws ``which shall be necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution'' any ``other'' powers vested by the Constitution 
     in the Government of the United States
       * Note According to the Supreme Court, the Necessary and 
     Proper Clause gives Congress the ``power to make laws for 
     carrying into execution all the Judgments which the Judicial 
     department has power to pronounce'' (Wayman v. Southard, 10 
     Wheat 1, 22 (1825)), and, thereby, Congress has ``undoubted 
     power to regulate the practice and procedure of federal 
     courts'' See Sibbach v. Wilson & Co., 312 U.S. 1, 9 (1941).
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize additional district judges for the district 
     courts and convert temporary judgeships.
```
