# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9617

**Measures:** H.R. 9617

```
           By Mr. BIGGS:
       H.R. 9617.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to prohibit grants under 
     Section 106 of the Housing and Community Development Act from 
     being used to assist persons who are neither a national of 
     the United States nor lawfully admitted for permanent 
     residence.
```
