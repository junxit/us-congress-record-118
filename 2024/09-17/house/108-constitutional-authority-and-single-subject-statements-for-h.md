# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 204

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 204

```
           By Mr. GUTHRIE:
       H.J. Res. 204.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Environmental Protection Agency relating to ``Review of Final 
     Rule Reclassification of Major Sources as Area Sources Under 
     Section 112 of the Clean Air Act''.
```
