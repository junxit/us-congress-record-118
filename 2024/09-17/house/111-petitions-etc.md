# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-53. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, relative to requesting enactment of Federal 
     legislation that would prohibit persons who are in the United 
     States, in violation of laws of the United States, from 
     contributing funds into ---- or from drawing disbursements of 
     funds out of ---- the Social Security program; which was 
     referred to the Committee on Ways and Means.
```
