# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9615

**Measures:** H.R. 9615

```
           By Mr. ALLRED:
       H.R. 9615.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 4, clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill establishes minimum requirements for notifying 
     individuals of a change in their polling place for a federal 
     election. Specifically, the bill requires a state that 
     changes an individual's polling place to notify the 
     individual at least seven days before the federal election
```
