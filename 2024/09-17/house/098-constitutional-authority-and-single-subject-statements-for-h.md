# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9634

**Measures:** H.R. 9634

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 9634.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to prohibit the National Institutes of Health from 
     conducting or supporting research that causes significant 
     pain or distress to a nonhuman primate.
```
