# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7403

**Measures:** H.R. 7403

```
            By Mr. BARR:
        H.R. 7403.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To specify when the record is complete on certain 
     acquisition applications related to depository institution
```
