# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9386

**Measures:** H.R. 9386

```
           By Mr. FLEISCHMANN:
       H.R. 9386.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will posthumously award the Congressional Gold 
     Medal, collectively, to James J. Andrews and William H. 
     Campbell in recognition of their extraordinary bravery and 
     steadfast devotion to the Nation during the Civil War as the 
     only civilian members of Andrews' Raiders, who launched a 
     daring military raid that became known as the ``Great 
     Locomotive Chase''.
```
