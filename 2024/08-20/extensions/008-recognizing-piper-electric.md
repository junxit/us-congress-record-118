# RECOGNIZING PIPER ELECTRIC

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING PIPER ELECTRIC

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 20, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Piper Electric 
of Arvada, Colorado for winning the Arvada Economic Development 
Association's 2024 Outstanding Large Business Award. Each year, this 
award honors a local company who brings job growth, economic 
investment, and innovation to the Arvada business community.
  Since 1983, Piper Electric has provided electrical services ranging 
from wiring updates and installation in residential homes to electrical 
construction and maintenance projects in commercial sites throughout 
Arvada and Jefferson County. As Piper Electric continues to expand, 
they remain committed to serving the Colorado community. Ongoing 
projects such as the expansion of their headquarters or their 
partnering with a national electrical group to increase their resources 
and job-capabilities, highlight the company's mission of providing 
high-quality jobs and services for families throughout our state.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Piper Electric for receiving the 
Outstanding Large Business Award and for their passionate work within 
Arvada, Colorado.

                          ____________________
```
