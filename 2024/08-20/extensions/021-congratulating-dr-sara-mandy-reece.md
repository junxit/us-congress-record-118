# CONGRATULATING DR. SARA "MANDY" REECE

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                CONGRATULATING DR. SARA ``MANDY'' REECE

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 20, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Dr. 
Sara ``Mandy'' Reece on her appointment as Interim dean of the School 
of Pharmacy at the Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine, known 
as PCOM.
  Dr. Reece has been a part of the Georgia PCOM community since 2010, 
having served as the Vice Chair of the Department of Pharmacy Practice 
and Director of Interprofessional Education.
  Before Joining the PCOM community, Dr. Reece served as a pharmacy 
director and diabetes educator at District 2 Public Health in 
Gainesville, Georgia.

[[Page E837]]

  Dr. Reece's appointment as interim dean is only one of her many 
accomplishments, among being named the PCOM School of Pharmacy Teacher 
of the Year for Pharmacy Practice in 2019 and Faculty Preceptor of the 
Year in 2022 and 2023.
  In Dr. Reece's new role as interim dean, she will continue to advance 
the great mission of PCOM's School of Pharmacy throughout the state of 
Georgia.
  I sincerely congratulate Dr. Reece on her accomplishments and share 
this excitement with the Georgia PCOM community.

                          ____________________
```
