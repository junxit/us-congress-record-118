# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9364

**Measures:** H.R. 9364

           By Mr. CROW:
       H.R. 9364
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 14
       The single subject of this legislation is:
