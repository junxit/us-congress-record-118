# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9362

**Measures:** H.R. 9362

```
           By Mr. CASTEN:
       H.R. 9362.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to 
     improve interregional electricity transfer capability between 
     immediately adjacent transmission planning regions.
```
