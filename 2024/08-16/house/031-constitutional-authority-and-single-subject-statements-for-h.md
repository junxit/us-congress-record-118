# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9374

**Measures:** H.R. 9374

       H.R. 9374.

     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution

       To amend the United Nations Participation Act of 1945 to
