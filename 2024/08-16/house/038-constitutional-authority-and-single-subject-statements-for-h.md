# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9381

**Measures:** H.R. 9381

```
           By Mrs. SYKES:
       H.R. 9381.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends title 51, United States Code, to promote 
     advancements and innovation in United States aeronautical 
     research and technology for enhanced safety, noise, 
     resiliency, and improved environmental impacts in United 
     States aviation systems.
```
