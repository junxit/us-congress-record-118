# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9376

**Measures:** H.R. 9376

```
           By Mr. PALMER:
       H.R. 9376.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires the Federal Communications Commission to amend the 
     rules of the Commission to include a shark attack as an event
```
