# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF FERNANDO RUELAS

**Speaking:** Ruiz, Raul (R000599) [D-CA]

```
             CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF FERNANDO RUELAS

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RAUL RUIZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 16, 2024

  Mr. RUIZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Fernando Ruelas and 
congratulate him on a remarkable 25-year career at the Coachella Valley 
Water District. As he prepares to retire, it is my honor to recognize 
Fernando's dedication to his work and thank him for his service to the 
entire Coachella Valley community.
  A proud Mexican American, Fernando graduated from Indio High School 
in 1990. It was during these formative years that Fernando worked as a 
palm tree worker and dishwasher, where he developed his admirable work 
ethic.
  In 1995, Fernando began walking as a service worker for the Indio 
Water Authority. Through his hard work and dedication, along with the 
encouragement from his community, he sought out to further his career 
by acquiring six certifications to ensure a better future for himself 
and his family.
  He began working for the Coachella Valley Water District in 1999. It 
was here that he climbed the ladder from meter repair trainee to 
Domestic Operations Manager. His leadership ensured our desert 
community received sufficient and treated water for residents and 
agricultural sites.
  Fernando's journey of perseverance led him to a fulfilling chapter at 
the Coachella Valley Water District, where he founded and managed IRC 
Backflow, a thriving water backflow business. He balanced serving his 
community during the week and being an entrepreneur on the weekends, 
all while raising his three children--Fernando III, Silvia, and Maria--
with his wife Giselle.
  Mr Speaker, in celebrating Fernando Ruelas, we celebrate the 
unwavering spirit of a true role model. He has set an example for young 
people aspiring for a brighter future within our community as 
entrepreneur, public servant, and father. His dedication and impact 
have paved a legacy that will continue to motivate and uplift for years 
to come.
  On behalf of California's 25th District, I extend my gratitude to 
Fernando and his family for his service as we join in congratulating 
him on his retirement.
```
