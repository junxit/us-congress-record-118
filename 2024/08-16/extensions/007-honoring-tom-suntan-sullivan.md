# HONORING TOM "SUNTAN" SULLIVAN

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
                    HONORING TOM ``SUNTAN'' SULLIVAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 16, 2024

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the 
extraordinary career of Tom ``SunTan'' Sullivan, from his service to 
our Nation to his hard work at the Barnes & Noble Bookstore at 
Northeastern University.
  Tom has been called the ``backbone'' of the operations at the Barnes 
& Noble--as a warehouse manager he assured smooth facilitation of 
operations and inventory, holding a vital role which contributed 
greatly to the University throughout his tenure.
  Tom served his country honorably as well, aboard the Navy's USS 
Brownson DD 868 from 1970 to 1975. Tom demonstrated great humility, 
honor, and determination in that role.
  August 3rd is now known as ``National Suntan Day,'' honoring a man 
who is beloved, admired and respected by all.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the extraordinary career of 
Tom ``SunTan'' Sullivan and I wish him the very best in his future 
endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
