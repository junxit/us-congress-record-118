# HONORING LARRY STRAUS ON HIS 95TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
               HONORING LARRY STRAUS ON HIS 95TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 16, 2024

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to honor Mr. Larry 
Straus for his 74 years of dedicated service to the United States 
Postal Service.
  A Drummond Island native, Mr. Straus has been a mail carrier of the 
USPS since 1950 and has served many Michigan communities over the past 
seven decades, including Mt. Pleasant and Kingsley. Larry has served 
the Drummond Island community since 2000 and recently just celebrated 
his 95 birthday.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to honor Mr. Larry Straus for his 74 
years of dedicated service to the United States Postal Service and wish 
him an incredibly happy 95th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
