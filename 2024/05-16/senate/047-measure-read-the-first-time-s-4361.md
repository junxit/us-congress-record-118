**Speaking:** Schatz, Brian (S001194) [D-HI]

**Measures:** S. 4361

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 4361

  Mr. SCHATZ. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk, 
and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.
```
