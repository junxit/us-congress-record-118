# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8404

**Measures:** H.R. 8404

```
           By Mr. FITZPATRICK:
       H.R. 8404.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Creates a Radiation Oncology Case Rate Value Based Payment 
     Program exempt from budget neutrality adjustment 
     requirements, and to amend section 1128A of title XI of the 
     Social Security Act to create a new statutory exception for 
     the provision of free or

[[Page H3274]]

     discounted transportation for radiation oncology patients to 
     receive radiation therapy services.
```
