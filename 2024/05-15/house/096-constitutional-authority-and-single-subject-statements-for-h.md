# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 140

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 140

```
           By Mr. ALLEN:
       H.J. Res. 140.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, section 8, clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Disapproves the rule submitted by the Department of Labor 
     relating to ``Amendment to Prohibited Transaction Exemption 
     2020-02'' (89 Fed. Reg. 32260 (April 25, 2024)), and such 
     rule shall have no force or effect.
```
