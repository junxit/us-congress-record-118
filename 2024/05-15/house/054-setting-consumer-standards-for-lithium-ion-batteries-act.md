  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 1797) to require the Consumer Product Safety 
Commission to promulgate a consumer product safety standard with 
respect to rechargeable lithium-ion batteries used in micromobility 
devices, and for other purposes, as amended, on which the yeas and nays 
were ordered.

gentleman from Florida (Mr. Bilirakis) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  This will be a 2-minute vote.
