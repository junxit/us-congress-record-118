# HONORING LOUISIANA OFFICERS KILLED IN THE LINE OF DUTY

**Speaking:** Letlow, Julia (L000595) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1226

```
         HONORING LOUISIANA OFFICERS KILLED IN THE LINE OF DUTY

  (Ms. LETLOW asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. LETLOW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in full support of H. Res. 
1226, memorializing law enforcement officers killed in the line of 
duty, that I am proudly co-leading with Representatives   Michael 
Guest, Chris Pappas, and Abigail Spanberger.
  Today, I would like to take a moment to honor the courageous heroes 
from my home State of Louisiana who have been added to the Fallen Law 
Enforcement Officers Memorial:
  Officer Carl Douglas Kimball; Deputy Sheriff Marylin Anjanette Mayo; 
Officer Trevor Aron Abney; Corporal Scotty Wayne Canezaro; Sergeant 
Nicholas Neal Pepper; Sergeant Charles Fitzgerald Dotson; Lieutenant 
Barry Paul Giglio; Corporal Robert William McKinney; Deputy First Class 
Kyle Michael Melancon; Sergeant David Jacob Poirrier; Corporal Shawn 
Kevin Kelly; and Lieutenant Michael Stephen Godawa.
  I ask everyone to join me in praying for these officers and their 
families who put their lives on the line each day for every American.
  May we continue to teach future generations the critical mission that 
law enforcement personnel undertake in service to our communities.

                          ____________________
```
