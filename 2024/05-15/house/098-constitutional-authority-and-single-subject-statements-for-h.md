# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 142

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 142

```
           By Mr. ALLEN:
       H.J. Res. 142.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following
       Article 1, section 8, clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Disapprove of the rule submitted by the Department of Labor 
     relating to `Retirement Security Rule:Definition of an 
     Investment Advice Fiduciary' (89 Fed.Reg. 32122 (April 25, 
     2024)), and such rule shall have no force or effect.
```
