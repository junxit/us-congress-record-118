# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 106, H.R. 4814, H.R. 5390, H.R. 6132, H.R. 7659

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 4814. An act to direct the Consumer Product Safety 
     Commission to establish a pilot program to explore the use of 
     artificial intelligence in support of the mission of the 
     Commission and to direct the Secretary of Commerce and the 
     Federal Trade Commission to study and report on the use of 
     blockchain technology and tokens, respectively; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
       H.R. 5390. An act to direct the Secretary of Commerce to 
     conduct a study on the feasibility of manufacturing in the 
     United States products for critical infrastructure sectors, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, 
     Science, and Transportation.
       H.R. 6132. An act to require the Consumer Product Safety 
     Commission to promulgate a mandatory consumer product safety 
     standard with respect to retractable awnings; to the 
     Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
       H.R. 7659. An act to authorize and amend authorities, 
     programs, and statutes administered by the Coast Guard; to 
     the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation.

  The following concurrent resolution was read, and referred as 
indicated:

       H. Con. Res. 106. Concurrent resolution expressing support 
     for local law enforcement officers and condemning efforts to 
     defund local law enforcement agencies; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
