# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
following nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of 
Courtney Diesel O'Donnell, of California, to be United States Permanent 
Representative to the United Nations Educational, Scientific, and 
Cultural Organization, with the rank of Ambassador.
```
