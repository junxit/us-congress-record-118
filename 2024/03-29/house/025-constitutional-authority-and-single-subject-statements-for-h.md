# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7844

**Measures:** H.R. 7844

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 7844.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would create a $30 million grant program for 
     schools to train teachers, principals and other personnel on 
     implicit bias with respect to actual or perceived race, 
     religion, sex (including sexual orientation and gender 
     identity), disability, ethnicity and socioeconomic status.
```
