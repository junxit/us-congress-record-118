# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 122

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 122

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.J. Res. 122.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Resolution under the Congressional Review Act to nullify 
     the Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection Rule ``Credit 
     Card Penalty Fees (Regulation Z)''.
```
