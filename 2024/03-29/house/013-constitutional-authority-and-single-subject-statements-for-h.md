# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7832

**Measures:** H.R. 7832

```
           By Mr. CORREA:
       H.R. 7832.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Address capability gaps in border security operations by 
     requiring the Secretary of Homeland Security to submit a plan 
     to Congress to identify, integrate and deploy innovative 
     technologies like artificial intelligence (AI), machine 
     learning, and nanotechnology.
```
