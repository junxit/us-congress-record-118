# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7851

**Measures:** H.R. 7851

```
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 7851.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require that large, federally funded bridges include 
     protective features designed to avert allisions along 
     critical bridge supports.
```
