# RECOGNIZING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF SERVICES PLUS

**Speaking:** Gallagher, Mike (G000579) [R-WI]

```
           RECOGNIZING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF SERVICES PLUS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MIKE GALLAGHER

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 29, 2024

  Mr. GALLAGHER. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize Services Plus 
for celebrating its 40th anniversary. The Green Bay company is a 
shining example of hard work, dedication, and community leadership.
  Services Plus was founded, and continues today, as a contract 
manufacturer and packager of consumer tissue products. Their current 
partners are some of the largest consumer products goods companies and 
distributors in the world, most of whom have been longterm partners. 
Through their ideation, quick execution, flexibility, and ability to 
create value for their customers, the company has flourished into a 
nationally renowned and respected industry leader.
  Marvin Schumacher left retirement in 1984 to establish Services Plus 
after a long career as President and founding member of Warehouse 
Specialists. Marvin's vision for Services Plus prioritized 
collaboration and customer service with a focus on close relationships 
with customers and the Green Bay community. Rejecting the idea of 
developing their own brand, they instead focus on maintaining customer-
owned assets and solving a customer's problems. Marvin's values have 
guided the company's journey to 40 years of sustained success and 
growth.
  Marvin's two oldest sons, Mike and Tom, joined his new mission, 
learning the ins and outs of the industry from their father. In the 
1990s, Marvin retired, this time for good, selling his shares to Mike 
and Tom. The two brothers carried on their father's legacy at Services 
Plus until they too retired in 2015; developing a new leadership team 
led by Services Plus President, Tad Campana. Though no longer at the 
center of daily operations, the Schumacher family's values and 
dedication to customer service continue to guide the company forward. 
Presently, under Tad Campana's leadership, the company plans to be the 
preferred choice of Outsourced Contract Manufacturing and Outsourced 
Innovation for consumer tissue products. The continued success of 
Services Plus would not be possible without talented leadership and 
hard work from all team members.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Services Plus as they celebrate their 40th anniversary. Through 
Marvin's vision, his sons' persistence, and Tad's leadership today, the 
company has found remarkable success and become a cornerstone of our 
community. It's an honor to witness their journey and to see the impact 
they've made on Green Bay, Northeast Wisconsin, and our country. I wish 
Services Plus continued prosperity and success in the next 40 years.

                          ____________________
```
