# RECOGNIZING NICK WALTON

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                        RECOGNIZING NICK WALTON

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 29, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions made in our community by one of my constituents, 
Nicholas Walton. Nick's unwavering dedication and tireless efforts have 
significantly impacted the lives of children in the East Pasco area, 
making him a true hero among us. Nick has gone above and beyond in his 
role at the Sarah Vande Berg Tennis Foundation, demonstrating an 
exceptional commitment to the betterment of the community. Through his 
leadership, the foundation has played a vital role in enhancing the 
lives of children in the East Pasco area, particularly through the 
promotion of tennis as a means to foster physical health, teamwork, and 
personal development. His vision and passion for creating positive 
change have resulted in numerous initiatives that directly benefit the 
youth in our community. Nick's ability to inspire and mobilize others 
has led to the successful implementation of programs that provide 
access to tennis facilities, coaching, and educational opportunities 
for children who may not have otherwise had such access. Nick's impact 
extends beyond the tennis court, as he consistently strives to instill 
values of leadership, discipline, and community engagement in the young 
minds he mentors. His commitment to fostering a supportive environment 
for children to thrive in is truly commendable. Part of what makes our 
country so great is the willingness of ordinary Americans to go above 
and beyond to selflessly give back to others. Volunteerism is one of 
the cornerstones of our society and part of the fabric that unites us.
  It is clear that Mr. Nicholas Walton has made a profoundly positive 
impact on our community, and I am grateful for his service.

                          ____________________
```
