# RECOGNIZING TODD ALBRECHT

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING TODD ALBRECHT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 29, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Todd Albrecht 
of Canon City for being inducted into the Fremont Hall of Fame.
  Todd is being honored for his dedication and service to the Canon 
City community through education and arts. His 31-year career as a 
music educator has left an enormous mark on Canon City High School 
students, parents, educators, and the surrounding community. He has 
developed show choirs, supported the musical theater program, and 
taught countless young people the importance of music and the arts.
  Todd's commitment to the community is also demonstrated through his 
work with the Canon City School Board and the Colorado Association of 
School Boards. He is dedicated to the success of our local education 
system and has worked towards these goals his entire career.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, it 
is my honor to congratulate Todd Albrecht for being inducted into the 
Fremont Hall of Fame and thank him for his contribution to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
