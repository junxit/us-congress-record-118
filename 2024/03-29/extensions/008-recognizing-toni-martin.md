# RECOGNIZING TONI MARTIN

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                        RECOGNIZING TONI MARTIN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 29, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions of one of my constituents, Toni Martin, who 
volunteers countless hours each week helping colleagues, businesses, 
and children navigate the tumultuous world in which we live, and she 
does it will a golden heart. Toni is a leader in educating the 
community on the issue of human trafficking and its prevalence in the 
Tampa Bay area. She also dedicates her time to the New Beginnings Youth 
Shelter in Brooksville, serving as a mentor to young boys and girls who 
have been left or abandoned by their parents. Well-known for her 
innovative problem solving skills, local leaders at all levels rely 
upon Toni to help strategize on how to address unmet needs in the 
community, and she always delivers positive results. Part of what makes 
our country so great is the willingness of ordinary Americans to go 
above and beyond to selflessly give back to others. Volunteerism is one 
of the cornerstones of our society and part of the fabric that unites 
us.
  It is clear that Ms. Toni Martin has made a profoundly positive 
impact on our community, and I am grateful for her service.

                          ____________________
```
