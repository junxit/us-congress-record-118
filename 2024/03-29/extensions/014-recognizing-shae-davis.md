# RECOGNIZING SHAE DAVIS

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                         RECOGNIZING SHAE DAVIS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 29, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions made in our community by one of my constituents, 
Shae Davis. Shae's unwavering dedication and tireless efforts have 
significantly impacted the lives of children in the East Pasco area, 
making her a true hero among us. As a volunteer at the Sarah Vande Berg 
Tennis Foundation, Shae has played a vital role in enhancing the lives 
of children in the East Pasco area, particularly through the promotion 
of tennis as a means to foster physical health, teamwork, and personal 
development. Her vision and passion for creating positive change have 
resulted in numerous initiatives that directly benefit the youth in our 
community.
  Part of what makes our country so great is the willingness of 
ordinary Americans to go above and beyond to selflessly give back to 
others. Volunteerism is one of the cornerstones of our society and part 
of the fabric that unites us.
  It is clear that Ms. Shae Davis has made a profoundly positive impact 
on our community, and I am grateful for her service.

                          ____________________
```
