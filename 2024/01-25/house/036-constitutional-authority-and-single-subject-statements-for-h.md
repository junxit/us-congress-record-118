# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7094

**Measures:** H.R. 7094

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
        H.R. 7094.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would would permit the use of marijuana in 
     federally assisted housing, in compliance with the marijuana 
     laws of the state where the property is located.
```
