# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7099

**Measures:** H.R. 7099

```
            By Mr. WALBERG:
        H.R. 7099.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the Constitution of the 
     United States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To preserve the companionship services exemption for 
     minimum wage and overtime pay, and the live-in domestic 
     services exemption for overtime pay, under the Fair Labor 
     Standards Act of 1938.
```
