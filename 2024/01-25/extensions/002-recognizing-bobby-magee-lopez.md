# RECOGNIZING BOBBY MAGEE LOPEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING BOBBY MAGEE LOPEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Mr. Bobby 
Magee Lopez, the winner of the Arvada Olde Town Shindig Mural Contest.
  This competition showcased the work of local artists competing for 
the next Olde Town Mural commission. Mr. Lopez was selected by a panel 
of art experts to complete the city's next Olde Town mural project.
  It is my honor to recognize and congratulate Mr. Lopez on his 
achievement and thank him for his dedication to public arts. Colorado's 
7th Congressional District looks forward to enjoying his mural upon its 
completion.

                          ____________________
```
