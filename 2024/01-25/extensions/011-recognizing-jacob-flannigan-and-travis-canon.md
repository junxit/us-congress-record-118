# RECOGNIZING JACOB FLANNIGAN AND TRAVIS CANON

```
              RECOGNIZING JACOB FLANNIGAN AND TRAVIS CANON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Ms. Pettersen. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Jacob Flannigan and Travis Canon for earning an Honorable Mention Award 
in the 2023 Congressional App Challenge.
  Jacob and Travis built an interactive app entitled Study Buddy which 
allows users to dress up a virtual animal avatar after completing tasks 
such as homework.
  Jacob and Travis, students from Golden High School, spent countless 
hours and used numerous tools to learn about coding and app development 
while developing new skills and further advancing their STEM education. 
Their perseverance and dedication to this challenge exemplifies the 
character and determination the faculty instills in the students at 
Golden High.
  Congratulations to Jacob Flannigan and Travis Canon for their hard 
work on this project. I am certain they will exhibit the same 
dedication and character in their future accomplishments, and I wish 
them the best of luck going forward.

                          ____________________
```
