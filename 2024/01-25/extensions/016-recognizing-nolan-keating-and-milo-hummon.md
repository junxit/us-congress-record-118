# RECOGNIZING NOLAN KEATING AND MILO HUMMON

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
               RECOGNIZING NOLAN KEATING AND MILO HUMMON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Nolan Keating and Milo Hummon for earning the Best in Show Award in the 
2023 Congressional App Challenge.
  Nolan and Milo built an app entitled Carbon Chronicle, which features 
a technically sophisticated interactive platforming game of an animal 
escaping rising ocean levels as well as informational slides to teach 
users about climate change.
  Nolan and Milo, students from Golden High School, spent countless 
hours and used numerous tools to learn about coding and app development 
while developing new skills and further advancing their STEM education. 
Their perseverance and dedication to this challenge exemplifies the 
character and determination the faculty instills in the students at 
Golden High.
  Congratulations to Nolan Keating and Milo Hummon for their hard work 
on this project. I am certain they will exhibit the same dedication and 
character in their future accomplishments, and I wish them the best of 
luck going forward.

                          ____________________
```
