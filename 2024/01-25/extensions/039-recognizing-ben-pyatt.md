# RECOGNIZING BEN PYATT

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING BEN PYATT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ben Pyatt's 
dedication as a lifelong educator in our community. Mr. Pyatt has 
served generations of Arvada students and is retiring after thirty 
years in public education.
  In addition to his job as a math teacher, Mr. Pyatt helped teach 
student driving when Arvada High School was short on driving 
instructors. After the school disbanded the driver's ed program, Mr. 
Pyatt joined the Arvada Driving School, where he served for nearly 
three decades.
  Mr. Pyatt has played a key part in driver's education for Arvadans, 
teaching an estimated twenty-thousand people in his community how to 
drive over his career. It is my honor to recognize Mr. Ben Pyatt for 
his dedication to his community.

                          ____________________
```
