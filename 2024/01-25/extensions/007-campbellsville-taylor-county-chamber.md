# CAMPBELLSVILLE-TAYLOR COUNTY CHAMBER

**Speaking:** Comer, James (C001108) [R-KY]

```
                  CAMPBELLSVILLE-TAYLOR COUNTY CHAMBER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JAMES COMER

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Mr. COMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 75th anniversary of 
the Campbellsville-Taylor County Chamber of Commerce of Kentucky's 1st 
Congressional District.
  Through the efforts of the Rotary Club, Lions Club, and local 
businesses, the Campbellsville-Taylor County Chamber of Commerce was 
first organized in May of 1949. Membership was small at first but has 
grown over the decades to include over 325 investors. I commend the 
bridge that has been created between local businesses, education, 
government, civic, and religious organizations.
  The Chamber has been able to boost tourism efforts in Taylor County 
and has organized many popular events that has brought the community 
together, including the annual 4th of July Celebration which has become 
the largest Independence Day Celebration in Kentucky. I am honored to 
represent them in Congress.
  I join with the community of Taylor County in celebrating the 75th 
anniversary of the Campbellsville-Taylor County Chamber of Commerce and 
look forward to many more years of services.

                          ____________________
```
