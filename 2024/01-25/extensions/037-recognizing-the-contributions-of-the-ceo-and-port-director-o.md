# RECOGNIZING THE CONTRIBUTIONS OF THE CEO AND PORT DIRECTOR OF PORT EVERGLADES, JONATHAN DANIELS

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE CONTRIBUTIONS OF THE CEO AND PORT DIRECTOR OF PORT 
                      EVERGLADES, JONATHAN DANIELS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. Jonathan 
Daniels, the CEO and Port Director of Port Everglades, for his service 
and leadership to the industry.
  Jonathan Daniels has been the CEO and Port Director of Port 
Everglades since the summer of 2020. Located in South Florida, Port 
Everglades is one of the busiest ports in the world, welcoming cruises 
and container and cargo traffic. Assuming the role at the height of the 
COVID-19 pandemic, Mr. Daniels successfully led the port through 
tumultuous times and helped build a future of sustained growth. Through 
his leadership and the efforts of his staff, the port persevered and is 
experiencing a revival with the return of cruises and upgrades to 
infrastructure.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that you join me in recognizing Mr. Jonathan 
Daniels for his outstanding leadership and contributions to Port 
Everglades and the constituents of Florida's 23rd Congressional 
District.
```
