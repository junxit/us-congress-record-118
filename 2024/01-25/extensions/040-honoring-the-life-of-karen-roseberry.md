# HONORING THE LIFE OF KAREN ROSEBERRY

**Speaking:** Issa, Darrell (I000056) [R-CA]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF KAREN ROSEBERRY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARRELL ISSA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Mr. ISSA. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize and celebrate the 
life and accomplishments of Karen Roseberry, who passed away on January 
12, 2024.
  Mrs. Roseberry devoted her life to public service: in the classroom, 
health clinics, and political arenas. She was a highly-regarded 
educator, teaching hundreds of students over a nearly ten-year span. 
Mrs. Roseberry also served as an advisor to several candidates running 
for public office.
  Mrs. Roseberry's passion for helping others was demonstrated through 
her time as Executive Director of the Women's Resource Center in 
Lancaster, California, which offered counseling, testing, and spiritual 
support for expectant mothers and fathers. Her tireless dedication to 
protecting the health and welfare of the unborn was illuminated through 
her work on the California Prolife Council, most recently serving as 
Vice President.
  Mrs. Roseberry's years of service left a lasting impact on Southern 
California--and the entire state--that will be admired and modeled for 
future leaders to reproduce. While we mourn the loss of an 
extraordinary leader in our community, we can find joy in the fact that 
Mrs. Roseberry's legacy will continue to enrich the lives of 
Californians to come.

                          ____________________
```
