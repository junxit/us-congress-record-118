# RECOGNIZING PARKER BROWN AND THE CARRABBA'S TEAM

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
            RECOGNIZING PARKER BROWN AND THE CARRABBA'S TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Parker Brown 
and his team at Carrabba's Italian Grill on winning Westminster's 2023 
Large Business Community Support Award.
  Receiving four nominations for the award, constituents in the 
community thanked Carrabba's owner, Parker Brown, for donating much of 
his resources and time to support local school events and students. 
Carraba's and Mr. Brown have donated food and raised funds for the 
community as a whole as well, earning them this Large Business 
Community Support Award.
  Mr. Brown has been a managing partner for Carrabba's since 2013, and 
also serves as the Chairman of the Board of Directors at the 
Westminster Chamber of Commerce.
  On behalf of the residents of Colorado's 7th Congressional District, 
I congratulate Mr. Brown and his team for their hard work and thank 
them for their dedication to our community.

                          ____________________
```
