# HONORING MS. AMY FALK SHELDON

**Speaking:** Beyer, Donald S. (B001292) [D-VA]

```
                     HONORING MS. AMY FALK SHELDON

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. DONALD S. BEYER, JR.

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 25, 2024

  Mr. BEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize my constituent Amy Falk 
Sheldon for her outstanding work at the National PTA. As Director of 
Resource Development Amy made an immeasurable difference in the lives 
of PTA leaders across the county. She tirelessly dedicated her time to 
PTA's mission to make every child's potential a reality by engaging and 
empowering families and communities to advocate for all children. 
Beginning in 2015, Amy collaborated with local PTA leaders to develop 
corporate alliances and create partnerships which delivered engaging 
and educational opportunities for members. Amy is a dedicated 
humanitarian having worked at the Northern Virginia AIDS Ministry and 
American Association of University Women. She is also a board member of 
the Washington Area Women's Foundation.
  I am proud the National PTA is headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia 
and congratulate Amy for her service to the organization, its staff, 
partners, and affiliates.

                          ____________________
```
