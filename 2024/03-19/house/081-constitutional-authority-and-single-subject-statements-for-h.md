# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7724

**Measures:** H.R. 7724

```
            By Ms. LOFGREN:
        H.R. 7724.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 4 provides Congress with the 
     power to establish a ``uniform rule of Naturalization.''
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       To establish under article I of the Constitution of the 
     United States, a court of record to be known as the United 
     States Immigration Courts.
```
