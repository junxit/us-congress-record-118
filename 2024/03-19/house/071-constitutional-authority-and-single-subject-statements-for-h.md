# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7714

**Measures:** H.R. 7714

```
            By Ms. CARAVEO:
        H.R. 7714.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Constitutional Authority--Necessary and Proper Clause (Art. 
     I, Sec. 8, Clause 18)
       THE U.S. CONSTITUTION
       ARTICLE I, SECTION 8: POWERS OF CONGRESS
       CLAUSE 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the Secretary of Health and Human Services, 
     acting through the Director of the Centers for Disease 
     Control and Prevention, to make grants to States to increase 
     awareness and education for colorectal cancer and improve 
     early detection of colorectal cancer in young individuals, 
     and for other purposes.
```
