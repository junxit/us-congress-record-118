# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7713

**Measures:** H.R. 7713

```
            By Mr. BERGMAN:
        H.R. 7713.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires the Secretary of VA to submit to Congress the 
     annual performance plans for VA political appointees no more 
     than 30 days after completion.
```
