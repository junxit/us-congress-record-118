# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7710

**Measures:** H.R. 7710

```
           By Ms. JACOBS:
        H.R. 7710.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To encourage and enable the United States Agency for 
     International Development to pursue a model of locally led 
     development and humanitarian response and expand engagement 
     and partners with local entities.
```
