# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, the Heavens proclaim Your glory, and the skies display 
Your craftsmanship. Shine Your light on our path as we work today.
  Lord, You have led America through troubled times in the past. Be now 
for us a source of life, light, and liberty.
  Give wisdom to our Senators. May they follow Your light, trust in 
Your might, and find strength for life's fights. Teach them what they 
should think, say, and do, so they will not stumble along the way. 
Replace doubt with faith, fear with courage, and despair with hope.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
