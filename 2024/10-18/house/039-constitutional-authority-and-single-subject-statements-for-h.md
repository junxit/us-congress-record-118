# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 215

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 215

```
           By Mr. PALMER:
       H.J. Res. 215.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To disapprove of the rule submitted by the United States 
     Fish and Wildlife Service relating to ``Migratory Bird 
     Hunting; 2024-25 Seasons for Certain Migratory Game Birds''.
```
