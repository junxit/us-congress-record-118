# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10012

**Measures:** H.R. 10012

```
           By Ms. MALOY:
       H.R. 10012.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To Amend title 38, United States Code, to include eyeglass 
     lens fittings in the category of medical services authorized 
     to be furnished to veterans under the Veterans Community Care 
     Program, and for other purposes.
```
