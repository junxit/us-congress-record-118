# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF LIEUTENANT COLONEL COLBY K. KRUG

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
     CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF LIEUTENANT COLONEL COLBY K. KRUG

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the retirement of 
Lieutenant Colonel Colby K. Krug and recognize his professional 
leadership skills in the Armed Forces of the United States. He is a 
Buffalo native and leaves active service with numerous decorations and 
accolades.
  Lieutenant Colonel Colby K. Krug received his commission as an 
engineering officer from St. Bonaventure University Reserve Officer's 
Training Corps in 2004, where he also earned his bachelor of business 
administration degree in accounting and a master of arts degree in 
engineering management from Missouri University School of Science and 
Technology. Lieutenant Colonel Krug also holds a master of arts degree 
in operational art from the Army School of Advanced Military Studies 
and is a certified project manager and associate emergency manager.
  Showing his loyalty and dedication to his Nation, Lieutenant Colonel 
Krug has received several awards, including the Bronze Star Medal, 
Meritorious Service Medal, Army Commendation Medal, Joint Service 
Achievement Medal, Army Achievement Medal, Ranger Tab, Sapper Tab, 
Combat Action Badge, Air Assault Badge, and Parachutist Badge. Under 
his leadership, his regiment received accolades for ``Most Outstanding 
Engineer Company'' in the U.S. Forces Command in 2011. He was awarded 
the Army's Best Sapper Competition in 2007 with a Bronze DeFluery 
Medal. He has been deployed multiple times in support of Operation 
Iraqi Freedom and Operation Enduring Freedom.
  At his side throughout his military career has been his beloved wife, 
Erin, and daughter, Chloe, and son, Jack. Today, we thank Lieutenant 
Colonel Colby K. Krug for his outstanding service accolades and duty to 
serve

[[Page E1041]]

the country. Please join me in celebrating Lieutenant Colonel Colby K. 
Krug upon the occasion of his retirement from active service.
```
