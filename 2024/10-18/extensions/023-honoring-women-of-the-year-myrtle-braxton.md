# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, MYRTLE BRAXTON

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, MYRTLE BRAXTON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Myrtle Braxton, honored as one of California's 8th 
Congressional District's Women of the Year.

[[Page E1040]]

Ms. Braxton is a dedicated community leader and advocate, renowned for 
her tireless work in social justice and public service. As a long-
standing member of Easter Hill United Methodist Church.
  Ms. Braxton has worn many hats, championing justice and environmental 
causes. Following her 35-year career with the Social Security 
Administration, she continued her public service by becoming the first 
Black female president of a national citizens branch of the National 
Parks Association.
  Ms. Braxton remains actively involved in numerous community 
organizations, including the Richmond Commission on Aging and the West 
Contra Costa County League of Women Voters. A staunch advocate for 
women's rights and police accountability, she inspires others with her 
compassion and unwavering dedication to justice. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor Ms. Braxton for her invaluable contributions to our community.
```
