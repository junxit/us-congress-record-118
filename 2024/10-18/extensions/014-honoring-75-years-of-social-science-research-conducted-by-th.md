# HONORING 75 YEARS OF SOCIAL SCIENCE RESEARCH CONDUCTED BY THE INSTITUTE FOR SOCIAL RESEARCH AT THE UNIVERSITY OF MICHIGAN

**Speaking:** Dingell, Debbie (D000624) [D-MI]

```
HONORING 75 YEARS OF SOCIAL SCIENCE RESEARCH CONDUCTED BY THE INSTITUTE 
           FOR SOCIAL RESEARCH AT THE UNIVERSITY OF MICHIGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DEBBIE DINGELL

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend the 
historic work conducted by the Institute of Social Research, or ISR, at 
the University of Michigan in the social sciences. As one of the 
world's largest and oldest academic survey research organizations, the 
ISR has paved the way for modern social science methods across the 
globe. Their work is worthy of recognition.
  The ISR's founding director, Rensis Likert, creator of the Likert 
scale, along with six of his colleagues from the Bureau of Agricultural 
Economics established the institute in 1948. One of the center's first 
major projects was changing the way they sampled people to conduct 
polling for the 1948 U.S. presidential election between Harry Truman 
and Thomas Dewey. While most national polls, using biased methods, 
predicted a Dewey landslide, the ISR accurately concluded that Harry 
Truman had a popular advantage.
  Currently, the ISR consists of five separate, but interdependent 
centers, including the Center for Political Studies, Inter-university 
Consortium for Political and Social Research, Population Studies 
Center, Research Center for Group Dynamics, and the Survey Research 
Center. These five centers are home to over 300 professionally 
acclaimed research scientists ranging from over 20 academic disciplines 
that focus on the fields of governance, economics, demographics, 
inequality, health, and sustainability. Some of the institute's major 
projects include the University of Michigan Consumer Sentiment Index, 
which has become an industry standard in indicating how American 
consumers feel about the general economy. The American National 
Election Studies, founded in 1948 by the ISR, has been the oldest 
continuous research study

[[Page E1038]]

producing high-quality voting and public opinion data in the country. 
Methods developed at ISR have helped academics, government agencies, 
and corporations for 75 years learn about how people think and act.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me today in commending the 
incredible work conducted by the ISR at the University of Michigan for 
the past 75 years. Their dedication to continually improving the rigor 
of the social sciences has made an everlasting impact across Ann Arbor, 
Michigan, the United States, and the world. Their educational and 
training programs have influenced the social sciences everywhere. 
Thousands of researchers who work around the country have had training 
ISR, and they produce high-quality studies on the full spectrum of 
human behavior. ISRs impact cannot be overstated, and their work should 
be celebrated.
```
