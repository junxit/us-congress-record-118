# HONORING EDITH MORGAN AND CELEBRATING HER 94TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
        HONORING EDITH MORGAN AND CELEBRATING HER 94TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JAMES P. McGOVERN

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Edith Morgan, to recognize 
her 94th birthday, and to celebrate her remarkable life and legacy of 
service.
  Edith's story is one of resilience, courage, and dedication. Fleeing 
Nazi persecution during one of the darkest periods of our world's 
history, she eventually found a home in the U.S. Midwest. After earning 
her bachelor of arts from Macalester College, she set out to make a 
lasting impact in the world.
  Edith later made Worcester, Massachusetts, her permanent home, where 
she earned her masters in education from Worcester State University. 
She went on to dedicate over 40 years of her life to teaching 
elementary school students. Her exceptional contributions to education 
were recognized by the late Senator Ted Kennedy when she was awarded 
the ``Master Educator in Massachusetts'' award.
  Edith's commitment to serving others extended beyond the classroom. 
She played a key role in refugee resettlement, fostering three children 
from Vietnam, all of whom remain close to her and still reside in 
Worcester today. This speaks to her deep compassion and the bonds she 
has formed with those she has helped.
  In addition to her work as an educator, Edith has been a tireless 
advocate for the Worcester community. She helped revitalize Green Hill 
Park, served as an active member of the Worcester School Committee, and 
has long been involved in local politics as a committed member of the 
Worcester Democrats and a dedicated neighborhood activist.
  Edith's contributions to our community are immeasurable, and her 
lifetime of achievement, service, and leadership continues to inspire 
all of us. She has been a steadfast advocate for immigrant youth, for 
our local education system, and for those in need.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in wishing Edith a very 
happy 94th birthday and to extend our deepest gratitude for her many 
years of service to our community and our country. We honor Edith today 
for the countless lives she has touched and the lasting legacy she has 
created. I thank her for her dedication and for continuing to inspire 
future generations.
```
