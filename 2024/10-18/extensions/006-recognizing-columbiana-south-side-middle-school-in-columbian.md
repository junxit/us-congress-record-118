# RECOGNIZING COLUMBIANA SOUTH SIDE MIDDLE SCHOOL IN COLUMBIANA, OHIO, AS A 2024 BLUE RIBBON SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Rulli, Michael A. (R000619) [R-OH]

```
RECOGNIZING COLUMBIANA SOUTH SIDE MIDDLE SCHOOL IN COLUMBIANA, OHIO, AS 
                       A 2024 BLUE RIBBON SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL A. RULLI

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. RULLI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the accomplishments 
of South Side Middle School in Columbiana, Ohio, as they were 
recognized as a National Blue Ribbon School for 2024. I thank the 
Columbiana Exempted School District and their supportive educators and 
staff for leading students on the path to success.
  As a resident of Columbiana County, I am proud to be able to honor 
South Side Middle School for their exceptional work as upstanding 
academic leaders in the community.
  The teachers and faculty not only strive for students to achieve 
their academic goals but are also invested in the well-being of each 
student. They go above and beyond, focusing on academic success but 
also the students' growth as an individual.
  We are grateful for the gifted teachers and staff of the Columbiana 
Exempted School District for the countless hours of time and energy 
they invest into youth education, as it is having a profoundly positive 
impact on our children.
  I ask my colleagues in the United States House of Representatives to 
join me in recognizing South Side Middle School in Columbiana, Ohio, as 
we congratulate its students, teachers, staff, and families on their 
accomplishments and their contributions to the community. These 
admirable citizens illustrate the preeminent standard of the Ohio 
educators and community leaders I am proud to represent. May their work 
continue to be a blessing to our Nation.

                          ____________________
```
