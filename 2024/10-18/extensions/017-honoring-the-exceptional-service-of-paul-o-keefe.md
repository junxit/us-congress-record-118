# HONORING THE EXCEPTIONAL SERVICE OF PAUL O'KEEFE

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
            HONORING THE EXCEPTIONAL SERVICE OF PAUL O'KEEFE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the dedicated 
service of United States Army veteran and hometown hero Paul O'Keefe.
  Paul O'Keefe, an esteemed member of the Mechanicville community, 
graduated from Mechanicville High School in 1950. Two years after his 
graduation, he was drafted into the army and served bravely in the 
Korean War. Following his service to his country, Paul returned home to 
Mechanicville where he married his high school sweetheart, Marie 
Cavotta, and together they raised a wonderful family with four 
children.
  Throughout his life, Paul demonstrated an unwavering dedication to 
his community. While balancing his professional commitments at West 
Virginia Pulp and Paper and General Electric, he served as a volunteer 
firefighter for an astounding 65 years. His service extended far beyond 
the fire department, acting as a chaplain for various organizations, 
including the Hemstreet Park Fire Department, American Legion Post 91, 
Saratoga County Deceased Veterans Monthly Ceremony, and the Department 
of New York State, a testament to his remarkable 56 years of dedicated 
service.
  Throughout his life, Paul demonstrated an unwavering dedication to 
his community and his commitment to others knew no bounds. Notably, in 
1965, during his daily commute, Paul observed two children suffering 
from muscular dystrophy and became aware of their family's financial 
struggles. Determined to make a difference, Paul conceived a unique 
idea. In an effort to raise money and awareness, Paul pushed a 
wheelbarrow twenty-two miles from Mechanicville to City Hall in Albany 
asking travelers for donations to help the family. Paul's initial 
fundraiser was a resounding success raising $16,000 dollars for the 
family on his first walk alone. Inspired by the impact he had made on 
the children's futures, he embarked on two more walks to City Hall, 
wheelbarrow in tow.
  Paul's service to those around him is truly inspiring and we are 
immensely grateful to have him as our Grand Marshall for the parade. On 
behalf of New York's 21st District, I am honored to recognize the 
incredible service and commitment to his community of Paul O'Keefe for 
his decades of service. I congratulate him on his incredible 
achievements and look forward to witnessing his future endeavors in the 
community. Today, I stand before you to honor the exemplary service of 
a distinguished United States Army Veteran and hometown hero, Mr. Paul 
O'Keefe, a respected member of the Mechanicville community.

                          ____________________
```
