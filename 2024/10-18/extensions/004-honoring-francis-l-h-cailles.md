# HONORING FRANCIS L.H. CAILLES

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                     HONORING FRANCIS L.H. CAILLES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Francis L.H. Cailles, an esteemed member of our community. Mr. Cailles 
is being honored today for his outstanding career in service to the 
California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation.
  Mr. Cailles dedicated 29 years of public service to the State of 
California. On June 14, 1994, he began his career in public service as 
a Staff Services Management Auditor with the California Department of 
Corrections and Rehabilitation's Audits Services Section, which is now 
known as the Office of Audits and Court Compliance.
  He worked in this department until his retirement, holding many 
positions. He was first promoted to Associate Management Auditor in 
1997. Then, he was appointed to Staff Management Auditor in 2006, and 
Senior Management Auditor in 2008.
  After nearly three decades in public service, he retired from state 
service in 2023.
  Mr. Cailles's colleagues describe him as a true leader and mentor. He 
is known to be unfailingly thorough in his work, diligent, 
conscientious, and a person his peers could always depend upon. Mr. 
Cailles is also a deeply caring and humble man, known to all around him 
as a patient person, with a good sense.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my esteemed privilege to acknowledge Mr. Cailles 
for his dedication to public service, and the state of California. His 
many years of service deserve recognition. Therefore, it is fitting and 
proper that we honor him here today.

                          ____________________
```
