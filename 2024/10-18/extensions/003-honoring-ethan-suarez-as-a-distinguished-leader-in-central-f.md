# HONORING ETHAN SUAREZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING ETHAN SUAREZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                              PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Ethan Suarez, the dynamic CEO of Pineapple 
Healthcare, has steered the institution towards becoming a cornerstone 
of support and care for the LGBTQ+ community in Central Florida.
  With a visionary approach and a genuine spirit of philanthropy, 
Suarez has transformed Pineapple Healthcare into more than just a 
healthcare provider; it is now a trusted ally and advocate for LGBTQ+ 
individuals. Under Suarez's leadership, Pineapple Healthcare has seen 
unprecedented growth, both in its scope of services and its impact on 
the community. His strategic initiatives have expanded the 
organization's reach, ensuring that comprehensive and inclusive 
healthcare services are accessible to all members of the LGBTQ+ 
community. Suarez's dedication to inclusivity is evident in the 
welcoming environment Pineapple Healthcare offers, where every 
individual feels respected and valued.
  Philanthropy is at the heart of Suarez's leadership philosophy. He 
firmly believes in the importance of investing back into the community. 
This belief is reflected in Pineapple Healthcare's numerous community 
outreach programs, educational initiatives, and support services. From 
sponsoring local LGBTQ+ events to providing free health screenings and 
educational workshops, the institution plays a pivotal role in 
fostering a healthier, more informed community. Suarez's efforts have 
cemented Pineapple Healthcare as a vital player in Central Florida's 
LGBTQ+ ecosystem. By addressing the unique healthcare needs of LGBTQ+ 
individuals and advocating for their rights, Pineapple Healthcare not 
only enhances the well-being of its patients but also contributes to 
the broader fight for equality and acceptance. Under Ethan Suarez's 
visionary leadership, Pineapple Healthcare continues to thrive as a 
beacon of hope, support, and empowerment for the LGBTQ+ community.

                          ____________________
```
