# RECOGNIZING STARLINK'S ROLE IN HURRICANE RECOVERY EFFORTS

**Speaking:** Cloud, Michael (C001115) [R-TX]

```
       RECOGNIZING STARLINK'S ROLE IN HURRICANE RECOVERY EFFORTS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL CLOUD

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. CLOUD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend the vital work of 
Starlink in the recovery efforts following Hurricane Helene and 
Hurricane Milton. With one of its key facilities in Bastrop, Texas, 
located in the 27th District, Starlink has made a significant impact by 
providing internet access to areas where traditional communication 
infrastructure has been severely damaged or destroyed completely. Our 
South Texas community is honored to play a small role in driving 
technological innovation that is directly benefiting Americans in times 
of crisis.
  In response to the devastation, Starlink quickly deployed over 20,000 
of its satellite kits--many of which were made in Bastrop--reconnecting 
families and facilitating emergency services in some of the hardest-hit 
areas. Starlink has extended free satellite internet service through 
the end of the year, ensuring that Americans maintain access to 
essential communications as they rebuild and support relief efforts.
  In addition, this initiative exemplifies the strength of the free 
market in delivering rapid, lifesaving solutions. The ability of 
private-sector companies like SpaceX to innovate and respond to crises 
faster than traditional channels is a testament to the importance of 
entrepreneurial spirit in serving the public good. I would also like to 
personally thank Elon Musk for his leadership and commitment to 
providing this invaluable resource to disaster-stricken communities.
  Mr. Speaker, may God bless the recovery efforts, the victims 
affected, and those tirelessly working to provide them with relief and 
support.
```
