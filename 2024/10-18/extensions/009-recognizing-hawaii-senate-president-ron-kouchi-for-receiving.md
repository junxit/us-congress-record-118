# RECOGNIZING HAWAII SENATE PRESIDENT RON KOUCHI FOR RECEIVING THE LEGISLATOR OF THE YEAR AWARD FROM THE HEALTHCARE ASSOCIATION OF HAWAII

**Speaking:** Tokuda, Jill N. (T000487) [D-HI]

```
   RECOGNIZING HAWAII SENATE PRESIDENT RON KOUCHI FOR RECEIVING THE 
 LEGISLATOR OF THE YEAR AWARD FROM THE HEALTHCARE ASSOCIATION OF HAWAII

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JILL N. TOKUDA

                               of hawaii

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Ms. TOKUDA. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor and privilege to recognize 
Senate President Ron Kouchi of the Hawaii State Legislature, a life-
long and distinguished legislator and this year's recipient of the 
Legislator of the Year award from the Healthcare Association of Hawaii.
  Hawaii Senate President Ron Kouchi has spent 14 years in the Hawaii 
State Senate, with nearly a decade as Senate President. During that 
time, he has championed significant increases in funding for rural 
hospitals, providing care for some of our more remote communities in 
Hawaii and truly making a difference in the lives and wellbeing of our 
residents. His support for growing our local healthcare workforce and 
prioritizing access to care to encourage regular school attendance are 
further examples of his commitment to a healthy Hawaii.
  As Senate President, Ron Kouchi also helped lead Hawaii through and 
out of a global pandemic. His leadership helped our state successfully 
navigate an unprecedented worldwide public health emergency.
  President Kouchi's steadfast commitment and lifelong achievements are 
an inspiration to many, and I am pleased to join the Healthcare 
Association of Hawaii in celebrating his many accomplishments. 
Congratulations and mahalo to Senate President Kouchi for all his 
contributions to the people of Hawaii.

                          ____________________
```
