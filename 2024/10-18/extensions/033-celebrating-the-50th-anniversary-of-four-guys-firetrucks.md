# CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF FOUR GUYS FIRETRUCKS

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
        CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF FOUR GUYS FIRETRUCKS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the 50th 
anniversary of Four Guys Firetrucks.
  Four Guys Firetrucks started in October 1974, when four friends 
worked to repair stainless steel milk tankers in Meyersdale, 
Pennsylvania. These individuals capitalized on the similarities between 
milk tankers and firetrucks, bought a small factory, and started Four 
Guys Firetrucks.
  Four Guys has adapted and expanded over the last 50 years. The 
original factory went through multiple renovations but is still used by 
the company today. Over the past five decades, the company has made a 
significant impact on the people of southwestern Pennsylvania. They 
provide jobs to over 50 residents, fueling our local small business 
economy. Four Guys also serves the greater Pennsylvania area, 
fulfilling orders throughout the Commonwealth.
  Today, Four Guys prides themselves on their top-quality products that 
offer a high degree of customization. They value personable service and 
believe that customer satisfaction is the key to success. These values 
are what sets this company apart from other firetruck manufacturers.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Pennsylvania's 14th 
Congressional District, I congratulate Four Guys Firetrucks on their 
50th anniversary and wish them all the best in the future.
```
