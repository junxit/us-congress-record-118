# RECOGNIZING JANUARY 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
           RECOGNIZING JANUARY 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize health care 
advocates and Encinitas residents, Ted and Carla DeBont, as my January 
2024 Constituents of the Month.
  In fall of 2021, Ted aged into Medicare while Carla stayed on her 
Affordable Care Act approved medical plan. However, due to a loophole 
in the ACA's premium tax credit formula, their premiums increased from 
$300 to $600 a month. Ted reached out to our office to see what he 
could do.
  In response, I introduced the Health Insurance Premium Fairness Act. 
This bill would close the loophole that penalizes married couples 
whenever one spouse ages into Medicare while the other remains on the 
ACA marketplace plan. Thanks to Ted and Carla's advocacy, this bill 
will save them--and countless other Americans like them--thousands of 
dollars annually on healthcare premiums costs and bills.
  This year a record 20 million Americans signed up for an ACA plan, 
and because of Ted and Carla's advocacy, we're fighting to ensure that 
all Americans can access the quality, affordable health care they 
deserve. Ted and Carla's advocacy is admirable and I'm proud to honor 
them as my January 2024 Constituent of the Month.

                          ____________________
```
