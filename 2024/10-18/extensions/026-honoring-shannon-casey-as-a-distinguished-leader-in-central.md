# HONORING SHANNON CASEY AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING SHANNON CASEY AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                              PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Shannon Casey is a proud firefighter and 
paramedic, currently a distinguished member of Osceola County Fire 
Rescue. She graduated from Coral Springs Regional Institute of Public 
Safety with class 17-05, receiving the student achievement award for 
her class. She has since served the communities of Coral Springs and 
Osceola County for the last five years.
  Working as a firefighter/paramedic is not for the faint of heart. 
They work long hours, receiving call after call, going from one trauma 
to the next, many times never receiving closure or recognition for 
their efforts. When everyone else is running away from danger, they are 
the ones running towards it, bravely fighting to keep the community 
safe. In a male-dominated and heteronormative field, Shannon has not 
only survived, but she has thrived.
  With over 15 years of experience in the field, she has a wealth of 
knowledge and experience that makes her a natural leader and role model 
for her peers. She has gained the respect of a diverse group of 
individuals while working as a float firefighter/paramedic across 
Osceola County. She responds to a multitude of emergencies, both fire 
and medical, often encountering adversity and exposing herself to a 
great deal of trauma. She continues to carry herself with honor and 
dedication with each and every call and willingly makes many personal 
sacrifices in order to be there for people in need on their worst days.
  Her ability to remain calm in the face of chaos is unmatched, making 
life-or-death decisions in a matter of seconds. Her positive outcomes 
speak for themselves, as her accomplishments have deeply benefited her 
patients and community. She always strives to do and learn more, and 
her strength and resilience are inspiring, to say the least.

                          ____________________
```
