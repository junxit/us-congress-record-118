# RECOGNIZING FEBRUARY 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
           RECOGNIZING FEBRUARY 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize a leading 
figure in the Oceanside community, Robbie Hass, as my Constituent of 
the Month for February 2024.
  For more than 20 years, Robbie has been an active member of our 
community, donating her time and energy, as well as being active in 
numerous clubs and organizations. In 2000 she joined the North County 
African American Women's Association, which provides scholarships to 
young women in our district to fulfill their academic ambitions.
  As a lifetime member of the NAACP, Robbie was named Woman of the Year 
in 2017 for all the work she's done in our community as a local 
business owner. She now serves as the first Black President of the 
Oceanside Chamber of Commerce and most recently joined the Board of 
Directors of Community Housing works, an affordable housing non-profit 
dedicated to making sure working families have housing opportunities in 
an expensive housing market.
  Robbie's work as a public servant is inspirational and I'm proud to 
honor her as my February 2024 Constituent of the Month.
```
