# CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF LANSING CHURCH OF GOD IN CHRIST

**Speaking:** Slotkin, Elissa (S001208) [D-MI]

```
  CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF LANSING CHURCH OF GOD IN CHRIST

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELISSA SLOTKIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Ms. SLOTKIN. Mr. Speaker, today I honor and celebrate the 100th 
anniversary of Lansing Church of God in Christ, a cherished institution 
that has provided spiritual guidance to mid-Michigan and beyond for the 
past century. Since its founding on August 6, 1924, by Elder Richard 
Wilson, this church has been a beacon of faith, service, and unity in 
Lansing, Michigan, making a profound impact on its congregation and the 
broader community.
  Lansing Church of God in Christ held its first official Bible study 
in 1924 in a small edifice on Williams Street. As its membership grew, 
so did the need for a larger facility. In 1926, the church relocated to 
Birch Street, where it became known as the ``Birch Street Church of God 
in Christ.'' There, Elder Wilson, with the help of Elder Perry 
Robinson, Sister Dunkin, and Missionary Carrie Williams, laid a strong 
foundation for the church's growth. In the early 1970s, the church 
moved to its current location on Wise Road, where it has continued to 
serve the city ever since.
  Over the years, Lansing Church of God in Christ has remained 
steadfast in its mission to enlighten and uplift the community. Through 
its many programs and partnerships with local nonprofit organizations, 
the church has offered spiritual support while addressing the practical 
needs of Lansing residents. The church's food pantry, computer learning 
lab, and youth mentoring programs exemplify its dedication to 
supporting individuals and families. Additionally, through its service 
groups, grief ministry, men's and women's ministries, and 412 Youth 
Ministry, the church helps members find freedom, purpose, and the 
ability to make a positive impact in Michigan and beyond.
  Under the leadership of Bishop Samuel Duncan, Jr., who has served as 
pastor since 1992, the church continues to be a place of hope, healing, 
and inspiration. Alongside his wife, Evangelist Dorothy Duncan; their 
son, co-pastor Samuel ``Cisco'' Duncan IV; and daughter-in-law, 
Evangelist Benita Duncan; the Duncan family's dedication to the church 
and its members is a testament to their deep roots in Lansing and 
commitment to serving the needs of their community.
  Lansing Church of God in Christ is more than a place; it is a 
fellowship of people devoted to living out their faith and 
strengthening relationships. The church's vision of empowering and 
developing individuals has been realized through generations of leaders 
and worshipers who have passed through its doors. With its strong focus 
on youth programming, the church has demonstrated its commitment to 
raising the next generation of compassionate, visionary leaders.
  As we celebrate the church's 100th anniversary, it is with great 
appreciation that I recognize Lansing Church of God in Christ's century 
of service and guidance. May it continue to inspire and uplift for many 
more years to come.
```
