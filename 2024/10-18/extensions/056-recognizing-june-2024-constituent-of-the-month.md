# RECOGNIZING JUNE 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
             RECOGNIZING JUNE 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, during Gun Violence Prevention Month, it is 
my great honor to recognize Nikki Faddick, a leading gun violence 
prevention advocate from Carlsbad, as my Constituent of the Month for 
June 2024.
  Ms. Faddick has spearheaded gun safety efforts in our community. She 
serves as a volunteer for the California chapter of Moms Demand Action 
and is a senior organizing manager at Everytown for Gun Safety.
  Through her efforts, Ms. Faddick advocates for commonsense gun reform 
and fights to protect our community members. She has participated in 
the Moms Demand Action for Gun Sense in America's advocacy days and has 
fought for increased funding for the state's most vulnerable cities 
through the California Violence Intervention and Prevention grant 
program.
  As part of her work, Ms. Faddick has met with leaders across all 
levels of government to push for gun violence reduction measures, and 
she helped lead local volunteers in a phone bank effort placing over a 
million calls to make communities across the country safer.
  Ms. Faddick's work to protect the residents in my district from gun 
violence is inspirational, and I am proud to honor her as my June 2024 
Constituent of the Month.

                          ____________________
```
