# HONORING BRANDON RAGAN AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING BRANDON RAGAN AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                              PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Captain Brandon Ragan is a trailblazing law 
enforcement leader who has proudly served his hometown of Orlando, 
Florida, as a longtime member of the Orange County Sheriffs Office 
(OCSO). Knowing in high school that he wanted to be a deputy sheriff, 
Captain Ragan joined OCSO's Explorer program as a teenager in the late 
1990s. As an Explorer, he gained invaluable experience that amplified 
his motivation to pursue a career in public service.
  In 2003, Captain Ragan took his oath to become an Orange County 
Deputy, hitting the ground running in a variety of roles. He has worked 
as a patrol deputy, a sergeant for school resource officers, a 
detective in Criminal Investigations, a night watch commander, and a 
lieutenant in both Criminal Investigations and Strategic 
Communications.
  A tireless force for change, Captain Ragan has spearheaded multiple 
LGBTQ+ advocacy efforts fostering unity, understanding, and inclusion 
in Orange County. As a founding member of the first nationally 
accredited LGBTQ+ liaison program launched in 2016 at OCSO, Captain 
Ragan has strengthened the relationship between deputies and LGBTQ+ 
residents while educating the agency about LGBTQ+ topics and policy 
updates. He also spent five years as vice president, then president of 
the Gay Officers Action League of Central Florida (GOALcfl) and 
continues to advise the board of directors. In addition, he helped 
create the OCSO Safe Place program, which invites businesses to display 
a decal identifying them as safe environments for

[[Page E1037]]

LGBTQ+ community members to report crimes or harassment.
  In 2020, the International Association of Chiefs of Police (IACP) 
named Captain Ragan one of the ``40 Under 40'' law enforcement 
professionals worldwide.
  Captain Ragan earned a Bachelor of Science in Criminal Justice 
Administration and a Master of Arts in Management and Leadership. He 
also holds a graduate certificate in cybersecurity threat detection.
```
