# HONORING JAMIE PERDUE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
HONORING JAMIE PERDUE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                              PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Jamie Perdue is a dedicated and invaluable 
volunteer at The Center Kissimmee. He plays a crucial role in 
maintaining the daily operations of the center, particularly through 
his management of the front desk. In this capacity, Jamie welcomes 
visitors, answers inquiries, and provides detailed information on the 
wide range of services available, ensuring that everyone who enters the 
center feels comfortable and supported.
  In addition to his front desk duties, Jamie is also a certified 
volunteer HIV tester. He performs rapid HIV testing, offering both 
compassionate care and essential information to individuals seeking to 
know their HIV status. His contributions in this area help bridge the 
gap to critical health services and raise awareness about sexual health 
in the community.
  Through his volunteer work, Jamie has become a valued member of The 
Center Kissimmee team consistently demonstrating a commitment to 
supporting the LGBTQ+ community and fostering a safe, inclusive, and 
educational environment for all.
```
