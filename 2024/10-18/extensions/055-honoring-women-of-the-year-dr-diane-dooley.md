# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, DR. DIANE DOOLEY

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, DR. DIANE DOOLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. Diane Dooley, 
recognized as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of 
the Year. Dr. Dooley is a dedicated pediatrician, public health 
advocate, community leader, former school board trustee, and proud 
Benicia native. For 41 years, Dr. Dooley served as a pediatrician at 
Contra Costa Medical Center, providing compassionate care to countless 
families and ensuring that children received the medical attention they 
deserve.
  In 2004, Dr. Dooley became a Pediatric Quality Physician, working 
tirelessly for 14 years to improve the quality of care and expand 
healthcare access for low-income youth. Beyond her clinical work, Dr. 
Dooley founded the Healthy and Active Before 5 initiative, which 
advances health equity for children by collaborating with community 
organizations to advocate for sustainable practices through local 
policy. Since her retirement from clinical roles, she has taken on the 
role of Chairperson for the California American Academy of Pediatrics 
Mental Health Legislative Team, where she has been instrumental in 
advancing legislation that supports the mental well-being of children 
across our state.
  In addition to her healthcare work, Dr. Dooley served on the Board of 
Trustees for the Benicia Unified School District for eight years, 
influencing policies that support student success. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor Dr. Diane Dooley for her invaluable contributions to our 
community.
```
