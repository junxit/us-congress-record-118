# HONORING KATIE KLUTZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING KATIE KLUTZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                              PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Katie Klutz, MSW, LCSW, ASDCS, is a licensed 
clinical social worker with nearly 20 years of experience in Florida's 
non-profit sector. She earned a bachelor's degree in social work with a 
double major in psychology in 2005 and a master's degree in social work 
in 2006, both from Florida State University.
  Throughout her career, Katie has worked in various pediatric 
specialties, including foster care, adoption, juvenile justice, and 
medical social work. She has led initiatives in child welfare by 
utilizing solution-focused, multisystemic, and trauma-informed 
approaches with at-risk youth and families.
  Since 2017, Katie has specialized in childhood cancer and blood 
disorders, focusing on support for patients and their family members by 
providing mental health interventions, support, education, and 
addressing any barriers to their care. Katie's strong belief in the 
empowerment of others inspired her to organize a community program for 
children and young adults living with chronic medical conditions to 
promote a successful healthcare transition from pediatric to adult 
care.
  Her commitment to advocacy and the empowerment of others is evident 
in her work and achievements. Her recent notable achievements include 
being the 2022 recipient of the National Bleeding Disorders 
Foundation's Social Work Excellence Fellowship and the 2023 Orlando 
Health Support Services Excellence Award. Additionally, she has co-
authored healthcare transition curriculum workbooks, which were created 
to help patients navigate the transition from pediatric to adult 
healthcare, emphasizing a focus on empowering young patients and their 
families.
  Lastly, she served as a sub-investigator on a study in 2024 that 
aimed to improve clinical practices and direct patient education. These 
achievements demonstrate her leadership in social work and her ongoing 
commitment to enhancing the quality of care for patients and families 
within this community.
```
