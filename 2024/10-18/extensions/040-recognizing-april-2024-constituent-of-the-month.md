# RECOGNIZING APRIL 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
            RECOGNIZING APRIL 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize a leading 
figure in our local health care community, Deysi Merino-Gonzalez, as my 
Constituent of the Month for April 2024.
  A Vista resident, Deysi immigrated to the United States from Mexico 
almost 20 years ago. After joining the Vista Community Clinic as a 
community health specialist in 2020 during the peak of the COVID-19 
pandemic, Deysi played an important role in bringing vaccines and 
health care to agricultural workers in my district.
  Thanks to her efforts, Deysi was awarded the 2020 Jugel & Zuroweste 
Health Justice Award by the Migrant Clinicians Network. This nationwide 
nonprofit addresses the health needs of migrants, immigrants, and other 
underserved communities. Deysi's previous experiences have given her 
valuable insight to understand how to improve our health care system 
for everyone.
  Deysi's work to provide countless migrant and immigrant farmers in my 
district and region with vital access to health care is inspirational, 
and I'm proud to honor her as my April 2024 Constituent of the Month.

                          ____________________
```
