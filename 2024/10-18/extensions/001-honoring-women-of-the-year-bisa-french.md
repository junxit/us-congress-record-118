# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, BISA FRENCH

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, BISA FRENCH

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Bisa French as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Ms. French is a 
dedicated and compassionate leader of the Richmond Police Department. 
Beginning her career with the Richmond Police Department in 1998, Ms. 
French has worked in various roles throughout the department, including 
Community Policing Officer and Detective for Family Services.
  Through her work, Ms. French developed a deep passion for youth 
advocacy. She rose through the ranks, becoming a Sergeant in 2006, 
Lieutenant in 2011, and Captain in 2013, where she became the first 
African American and Latina to hold the position. She also worked with 
numerous stakeholders in the community, including Contra Costa County, 
STAND! For Families Free of Violence, and Community Violence Solutions, 
to establish the Family Justice Center in West Contra Costa County, 
which opened in 2015.
  In 2019, Ms. French was appointed Interim Chief and became the first 
woman to serve as Chief of Police in the 110-year history of the 
department. In 2020, she was hired as the permanent Chief of Police. 
Ms. French's leadership and dedication to the community have impacted 
numerous lives. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Bisa 
French for her invaluable contributions to our community.
```
