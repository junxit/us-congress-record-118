# RECOGNIZING DECEMBER 2023 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
           RECOGNIZING DECEMBER 2023 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize public 
servant and Vista resident Jeffrey Olsen as my December 2023 
Constituent of the Month.
  Mr. Olsen is a generous local hero, helping over 57,000 people in his 
35 years of service to the San Diego region. He largely uses his own 
money to buy the goods he donates, filling his shed with toys and 
clothes throughout the year. This past February, he also donated tens 
of thousands of dollars' worth of sports memorabilia to help military 
families and veterans.
  During the holiday season, I am honored to recognize leaders, like 
Jeffrey, whose selfless dedication to giving back to others is truly 
admirable. He is a compassionate and inspiring member of our community, 
and I'm proud to honor Jeffrey Olsen as my December 2023 Constituent of 
the Month.

                          ____________________
```
