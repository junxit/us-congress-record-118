# HONORING SCOTT ARELLANO-ZANE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING SCOTT ARELLANO-ZANE AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                        FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Scott Arellano-Zane began his public service 
career in Illinois in 1988. He moved to Orlando in 1996 and worked at 
the Orlando Police Department. In November 1999, he became a police 
officer at the Edgewood Police Department, where he has served for 25 
years.
  Throughout his career, Scott has also held roles such as city 
information technology manager, LGBTQ+ liaison officer, and field 
training officer. In 2019, Scott was selected as a founding board 
member of the Gay Officers Action League Central Florida (GOALcfl), an 
official chapter of GOAL New York. Alongside his fellow board members, 
they brought about a new era in Central Florida law enforcement. Their 
focus shifted from being recognized solely for LGBTQ+ efforts within 
their agencies to actively engaging with the surrounding communities. 
GOALcfl now proudly includes several LGBTQ+ liaisons and allies serving 
in various Central Florida law enforcement agencies. He is equally as 
proud of his LGBTQ+ community work as he is of his other work for the 
City of Edgewood. This past year, he wrote grants awarding the agency 
two new patrol vehicles, all-new ruggedized laptops for officers, and 
more technology upgrades. The value of these grants alone exceeded 
$300,000.
  Scott's commitment to promoting transparency, equality, and trust in 
the community is evident in his work. In partnership with agency 
leadership, he secured a grant in 2016 to equip all officers with body-
worn cameras. This initiative was a testament to Scott's values and his 
dedication to serving the community.
  With over 35 years in public service, Scott still wakes up each day 
excited to serve. He looks forward to serving the Central Florida 
community for many years to come. Scott would like to thank his mother, 
Janine; father, Jack; sister, Dianne; and spouse of 11 years, Alex, for 
their unconditional love, support, and sacrifices.
```
