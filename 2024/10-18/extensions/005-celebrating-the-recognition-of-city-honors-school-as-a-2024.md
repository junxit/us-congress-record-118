# CELEBRATING THE RECOGNITION OF CITY HONORS SCHOOL AS A 2024 NATIONAL BLUE RIBBON SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
 CELEBRATING THE RECOGNITION OF CITY HONORS SCHOOL AS A 2024 NATIONAL 
                           BLUE RIBBON SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate a 
remarkable achievement for City Honors School. On September 23, 2024, 
City Honors was named a 2024 National Blue Ribbon School by the United 
States Department of Education. This prestigious designation recognizes 
the school for its culture of effective teaching, collaboration, a 
caring environment, and, above all, academic excellence.
  In the winter of 2023, the New York State Department of Education 
recognized City Honors as a Blue Ribbon candidate, highlighting their 
success with a diverse range of students. The comprehensive application 
submitted this spring showcased the school's vibrant culture, 
innovative programs, robust assessments, exemplary instructional 
practices, and strong leadership. This achievement speaks volumes about 
the dedication, commitment, and talent that permeate City Honors' 
halls.
  We must also take a moment to express our gratitude to the Buffalo 
Board of Education and Superintendent Dr. Tonja Williams Knight

[[Page E1036]]

for their steadfast support of the Blue Ribbon application. Dr. 
Williams' belief in City Honors' mission and unwavering support have 
been invaluable to the journey toward this recognition.
  As we celebrate this significant milestone, let us remember that this 
award is not merely a recognition of their past achievements, but a 
promise for the future to the community of Buffalo. It is a call to 
continue fostering an inclusive, supportive, and academically enriching 
environment for all students.
  We celebrate and congratulate City Honors School, Principal William 
Kresse, Dr. Tonja Williams Knight, and the entire school community. 
Together, we will continue to inspire and empower all students to reach 
new heights.

                          ____________________
```
