# CELEBRATING THE ONE HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY OF SAINT GEORGE GREEK ORTHODOX CHURCH IN BAKERSFIELD

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
    CELEBRATING THE ONE HUNDREDTH ANNIVERSARY OF SAINT GEORGE GREEK 
                     ORTHODOX CHURCH IN BAKERSFIELD

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 100th 
Anniversary of Saint George Greek Orthodox Church in Bakersfield, 
California. As St. George Greek Orthodox Church celebrates a century of 
faith, heritage, and hope this year, I urge my colleagues to join me in 
recognizing the history and roots this Church has upheld since its 
founding.
  Bakersfield's Greek Orthodox organized formally after a time when the 
Greek community experienced ethnic and religious persecution in their 
homeland and were forced to look for a new home overseas to practice 
their culture and faith. Many found work building the transcontinental 
railroad, venturing further and further west until some reached Kern 
County in the early 20th century. St. George Greek Orthodox Church's 
precursor community was founded in 1916 under the name Uplift Society 
Socrates. According to the narrative history of St. George Greek 
Orthodox Church in these early years, Greek Americans worked to balance 
two values so characteristic of the American immigrant experience--
upholding the faith and customs of their homeland while also adapting 
to a new life in the United States.
  In 1924, Uplift Society Socrates accomplished the former by 
constructing a modest hall for religious and social gatherings at 401 
Truxtun Avenue. This hall became the foundation upon and around where 
the Bakersfield Greek Orthodox Church community has since grown. By the 
early 1930s, the Bakersfield Greek Orthodox community had grown to over 
200 families, who soon called for the construction of a Church, which 
was completed in 1932. Also in 1932, the community changed its name to 
The Hellenic Community of St. George Greek Orthodox Church. Father 
Vasilios Markopoulos became the community's first official priest in 
1944, though four ``Pioneer Priests'' had been active in the community 
in the preceding few decades. Unfortunately, the 1952 Kern County 
earthquake precipitated the demolition of the original hall. As a mark 
of the faith of the Greek Orthodox community in Bakersfield, the new 
Church was blessed by the Bishop of the Fourth Diocese of Los Angeles 
in 1956. St. George Greek Orthodox Church continues to thrive. And it 
continues to exemplify the role of faith as the heart of such a vibrant 
community, uniting individuals in love, compassion, and shared purpose.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate St. George Greek Orthodox Church as it 
marks its 100th Anniversary, standing as an example of the incredible 
growth and faith of the Bakersfield community. Generations of 
Bakersfield Greek Orthodox have worked to ensure that their traditions 
and sense of community remain strong in the heart of Bakersfield.
```
