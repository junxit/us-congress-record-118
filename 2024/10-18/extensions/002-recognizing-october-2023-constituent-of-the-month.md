# RECOGNIZING OCTOBER 2023 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
           RECOGNIZING OCTOBER 2023 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize community 
advocate and Del Mar resident Bonnie Grossman as my October 2023 
Constituent of the Month.
  Bonnie is a fierce champion for children facing abuse and neglect. As 
a Court Appointed Special Advocate (CASA), she advocates for foster 
care youth in court, school. medical settings, and more to ensure their 
needs are met. Over the past seven years she has supported numerous 
foster children, including her current case youth who was 15 years old 
when he first met Grossman at the height of the pandemic in 2020.
  I am thankful for Bonnie's work and the work of CASA leaders who 
dedicate themselves to uplifting and protecting our most vulnerable 
communities. She is an incredible example of a compassionate and hard-
working public servant, and I'm proud to honor Bonnie Grossman as my 
October 2023 Constituent of the Month.

                          ____________________
```
