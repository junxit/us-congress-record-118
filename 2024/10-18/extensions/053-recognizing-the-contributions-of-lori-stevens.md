# RECOGNIZING THE CONTRIBUTIONS OF LORI STEVENS

**Speaking:** Fallon, Pat (F000246) [R-TX]

```
             RECOGNIZING THE CONTRIBUTIONS OF LORI STEVENS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PAT FALLON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. FALLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ms. Lori Stevens 
of Rockwall, Texas. Lori has served our community through her work as 
the Founder and Executive Director of Patriot PAWS Service Dogs.
  For over four decades, Lori has specialized in dog training and 
sought to make a difference in the lives of those in need. Prior to

[[Page E1047]]

founding Patriot PAWS, she became certified in professional dog 
training at the Karen Pryor Academy and later owned Country K9 Academy. 
In 2005, Lori was approached by disabled veterans at the Dallas VA 
hospital, who requested her assistance with training service animals. 
In the following year, she established Patriot PAWS, a nonprofit 
organization that trains and provides highly skilled service dogs at no 
cost to veterans with physical disabilities, traumatic brain injuries, 
and post-traumatic stress. Due to Lori's passion and visionary 
leadership, Patriot PAWS has provided over 600 service dogs to wounded 
veterans nationwide. These incredible service dogs have helped veterans 
meet their needs, improve their quality of life, and spend time with a 
faithful companion.
  As a result of her outstanding contributions to the service dog 
industry, Lori has received numerous awards, including the Champion of 
Freedom Award by the Texas Federation of Republican Women, the Texas 
Animal Hall of Fame Award, and the Governor's Criminal Justice 
Volunteer Service Award. In November 2023, Lori was recognized by 
Governor Greg Abbott and presented with the prestigious Yellow Rose of 
Texas Commission award, the highest award bestowed on women by the 
Texas governor. I am proud of the work that Patriot PAWS has 
accomplished for our veterans, and I know Lori will continue to make a 
positive impact on the world around us for many years to come.
  I have requested the United States flag to be flown over our nation's 
Capitol in recognition of Lori's exceptional contributions to the 
people of North Texas. I thank her for all she has done.

                          ____________________
```
