# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, BARBARA AKORO

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, BARBARA AKORO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Barbara Akoro as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Ms. Akoro leads her 
life with resilience and compassion. After the tragic loss of her 
children's father, she raised her two children with remarkable strength 
and determination.
  In addition to her family responsibilities, Ms. Akoro has achieved 
significant academic milestones, earning degrees in chemistry and 
forensic chemistry, and she will soon complete her doctorate in public 
health. She has worked as a laboratory scientist and caregiver and has 
interned with the International Federation of Red Cross and Red 
Crescent, focusing on climate change as a public health environmental 
specialist.
  Driven by her passion for helping others, Ms. Akoro co-founded 
TishZeeLCC, a home care agency dedicated to ensuring individuals can 
live healthy lives. As a member of Rivertown Community Church in 
Antioch, her faith and values guide her purpose. Ms. Akoro's commitment 
to making a meaningful difference is truly an inspiration to our 
community. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, I am proud to recognize Barbara Akoro for her invaluable 
contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
