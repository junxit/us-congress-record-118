# CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF UNILAND DEVELOPMENT COMPANY

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
    CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF UNILAND DEVELOPMENT COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
50th anniversary of Uniland Development Company. Uniland Development 
was founded in a single warehouse in Tonawanda, New York, by Carl J. 
Montante and Nancy R. Dobson in 1974. Since then Uniland has grown into 
the largest commercial real estate developer in western New York, 
changing the face of Buffalo and western New York through passion and 
dedication.
  Uniland's success stems from a steadfast focus on improving the 
community. With a talented team of over 200 associates, the company has 
consistently delivered solutions for businesses throughout the region, 
helping to spur economic development and growth. Their guiding 
principles of integrity, professionalism, excellence, innovation, 
customer focus, and teamwork are not just words; they are the 
foundation upon which Uniland has built its legacy.
  Over the past five decades, Uniland has transformed the landscape of 
western New York with remarkable projects that have enhanced our 
communities. From the early days at 260 Wales Avenue to the expansive 
CrossPoint Business Park, Uniland has continually set the standard for 
commercial real estate. Their developments have created thousands of 
jobs and revitalized neighborhoods, ensuring that both businesses and 
communities thrive.
  Notable milestones, such as the transformation of the Elm-Oak 
corridor in Buffalo and the iconic Avant mixed-use redevelopment, 
showcase Uniland's commitment to innovation and community improvement. 
Their ongoing work in residential rentals and their

[[Page E1050]]

dedication to socially responsible initiatives speak volumes about 
their mission to build better communities.
  Congratulations to Carl Montante, Nancy Dobson, and the entire 
Uniland team on this extraordinary 50-year journey. Your unwavering 
commitment to excellence and community enhancement has made a lasting 
impact on western New York.

                          ____________________
```
