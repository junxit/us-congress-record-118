# HONORING THE LIFE OF JON WEST

**Speaking:** Hinson, Ashley (H001091) [R-IA]

```
                     HONORING THE LIFE OF JON WEST

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ASHLEY HINSON

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mrs. HINSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of a fellow Iowan, Jon West.
  Mr. West was born in 1971 in Mason City, Iowa, to Terry and Linda 
West. He grew up in Forest City and graduated from Forest City High 
School in 1989.
  In 1990, Jon married the love of his life, Tammy Schoolcraft, in 
Forest City, Iowa. Their love story is one of true partnership and 
devotion, standing as a testament to the power of enduring love and 
commitment.
  Jon dedicated 30 years of his professional life to Winnebago 
Industries. He started in an entry-level position and worked his way up 
to become the lead manufacturing engineer in the cabinet shop. His 
unwavering dedication and innovative spirit led to numerous 
improvements within the company, benefiting his community.
  Beyond his professional achievements, Jon was deeply committed to his 
community. He served as a two-term city council member in Forest City, 
always striving to give back and make a positive impact. His love for 
farming, alongside his dear friends Jay and Kevin, further demonstrated 
his desire to contribute to the local community.
  Jon was a fourth-generation Iowan, with his family history in the 
State dating back to the late 1800s. His roots ran deep in Iowa and 
pride in his heritage was evident in everything he did. Jon's greatest 
joy, however, came from being with his family. Whether it was spending 
time with his children TJ and Ashley or his granddaughter, Rhea Lou, 
Jon found happiness in the warmth and love of those around him. He 
continues to live on through his family, especially his second 
grandchild and namesake, Lyra Jon, whom he never met.
  Jon West was a proud Iowan, a dedicated member of the Forest City 
community, and a devoted husband, father, and friend. I am honored to 
recognize his life, and I invite my esteemed colleagues to join me in 
paying tribute to the memory of Jon West.

                          ____________________
```
