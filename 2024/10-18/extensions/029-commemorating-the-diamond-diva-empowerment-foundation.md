# COMMEMORATING THE DIAMOND DIVA EMPOWERMENT FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Bush, Cori (B001224) [D-MO]

```
         COMMEMORATING THE DIAMOND DIVA EMPOWERMENT FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. CORI BUSH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Ms. BUSH. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, along with the people of St. 
Louis, to honor and recognize the Diamond Diva Empowerment Foundation 
for their unwavering commitment to empower victims and survivors of 
domestic violence through their Fourth Annual Domestic Violence 
Awareness Rally & Walk.
  This event is a testament to the foundation's vital role in raising 
awareness, providing resources, and standing in solidarity with victims 
and survivors, not just in our community but across the Nation. Their 
dedication has helped countless women, men, and families to find 
safety, healing, and hope in the face of unimaginable adversity.
  As a survivor of domestic violence myself, this issue is deeply 
personal. I know firsthand the scars--both seen and unseen--that 
domestic violence leaves behind, and the importance of having a 
community that empowers your resilience and worth. Organizations like 
the Diamond Diva Empowerment Foundation remind survivors that we are 
not alone and that our stories matter.
  We must continue to support organizations like Diamond Diva that are 
ensuring survivors have access to the support networks and resources 
they need to reclaim their lives. As they host their annual rally and 
walk, the people of Missouri's First District stand with them in the 
push to end domestic violence.
  I commend the Diamond Diva Empowerment Foundation for its leadership, 
advocacy, and dedication to creating a safer, more supportive world for 
survivors. Let this event be a powerful reminder of the work we still 
must do, and of the strength that comes from standing together.

                          ____________________
```
