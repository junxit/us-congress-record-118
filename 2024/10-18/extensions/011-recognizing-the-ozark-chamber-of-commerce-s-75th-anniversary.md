# RECOGNIZING THE OZARK CHAMBER OF COMMERCE'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Burlison, Eric (B001316) [R-MO]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE OZARK CHAMBER OF COMMERCE'S 75TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIC BURLISON

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. BURLISON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Ozark, 
Missouri, Chamber of Commerce on its 75th anniversary.
  The chamber was formed on Monday, October 14, 1949, when 
approximately 90 visionary business leaders from Ozark gathered to 
establish the chamber. Joe Majors, who was owner of Major Cheese Co., 
was elected as the first president while Dr. Stanley Roper was named 
vice president and Herbert Taylor was elected treasurer.
  Recognizing the need to grow, the chamber's first committee was 
focused on gaining membership. They started with 49 members who paid an 
annual fee of $15 for men and $7.50 for women. Later that month, the 
membership had already grown to 58 business leaders. The following 
month, the chamber hired a part-time secretary and joined the National 
Chamber of Commerce. These efforts were just the start of three-
quarters of a century of growth and success. Today, nearly 400 
businesses and individuals make up the organization that is dedicated 
to supporting local businesses, fostering economic growth, and 
enhancing the quality of life in the community.
  While a lot has changed over the past 75 years, the chambers 
dedication to the Ozark community has not wavered. Members continue to 
work tirelessly to support local businesses, engage with citizens, and 
collaborate with organizations that make Ozark a vibrant place to live 
and to work. Events such as the annual community awards banquet, the 
free business and community expo, the Welcome Home Event for new 
teachers, the widely anticipated annual Christmas parade, and 
partnerships with city, school and county entities all serve to enhance 
local businesses, the local economy and the local community.
  I would like to congratulate Chamber President and Chief Executive 
Officer Chris Rusell on this landmark occasion, and I wish the chamber 
many, many more years of growth and success.
```
