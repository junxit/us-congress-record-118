# HONORING THE LIFE OF ROSE MARIE HANSEN

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                 HONORING THE LIFE OF ROSE MARIE HANSEN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of 
longtime Concord resident Rose Marie Hansen.
  Rose Marie was born in Kansas, where she spent many of her formative 
years working on the family farm with her father and brother. She 
eventually moved to Oakland, California, where she worked as a nanny. 
Shortly after, Rose Marie met her husband, Alfred ``Bud'' Hansen, at a 
dance in Alameda. The two married in 1941 and were together for 52 
years until Bud's passing in 1993.
  She and her husband owned a contracting business and later partnered 
with a local businessman to open a bowling alley and retail complex, 
known today as Clayton Fair Properties. When Bud passed away in the 
early 1990s, Rose Marie took over as manager, a position she held until 
2023, helping the successful business for more than 60 years.
  Rose Marie was also very active in her community, volunteering at 
Concord's First Presbyterian Church where she served as an accountant, 
deacon, and elder. She also volunteered at the American Cancer Society 
as their coordinator of transportation for over 21 years, served as 
president of the Concord Diablo Historical Society for over 7 years, 
and traveled the world as international president of the of the USA 
Inner Wheel Association. On top of it all, she was also vice president 
of the Concord Homes Board of Directors, overseeing the development of 
two multi-story residential buildings for seniors.
  Rose Marie will be greatly missed, but I am confident her impact will 
be felt in the community for generations to come. Please join me in 
recognizing Rose Marie for her many contributions to our community and 
devoted service to the city of Concord.
```
