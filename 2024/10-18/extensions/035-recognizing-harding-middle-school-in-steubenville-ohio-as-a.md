# RECOGNIZING HARDING MIDDLE SCHOOL IN STEUBENVILLE, OHIO, AS A 2024 BLUE RIBBON SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Rulli, Michael A. (R000619) [R-OH]

```
RECOGNIZING HARDING MIDDLE SCHOOL IN STEUBENVILLE, OHIO, AS A 2024 BLUE 
                             RIBBON SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL A. RULLI

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. RULLI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the success of 
Harding Middle School in Steubenville, Ohio, as they were recognized as 
a National Blue Ribbon School for 2024. I thank the Steubenville City 
School District and their determined, compassionate, and caring staff 
for guiding students to success.
  I am proud to be able to honor Harding Middle School for their 
exceptional work as upstanding academic leaders in the community. The 
teachers and faculty not only strive for students to achieve their 
academic goals but are also invested in the well-being of each student. 
The educators of this district devote excessive amounts of their time 
and energy to the success of their learners because they understand the 
great impact it will have on the students' lives and the advancement of 
their region.
  I want to acknowledge and thank the Steubenville City School 
District's gifted teachers and staff for their dedication to the 
prosperity of Ohio's youth.
  I ask my colleagues in the United States House of Representatives to 
join me in recognizing these honorable leaders, pillars of Harding 
Middle School in Steubenville, Ohio, as we congratulate them on their 
accomplishments and their contributions to the community. These 
admirable citizens illustrate the preeminent standard of the Ohio 
educators I am proud to represent. May their work continue to be a 
blessing to our industrious Nation.

                          ____________________
```
