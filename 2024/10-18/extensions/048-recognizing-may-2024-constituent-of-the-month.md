# RECOGNIZING MAY 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
             RECOGNIZING MAY 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, during Asian American and Pacific Islander 
Heritage Month, it is my great honor to recognize Shannalei Seau, a 
leading figure in our Oceanside community, as my Constituent of the 
Month for May 2024.
  Ms. Seau serves on the Oceanside Parks and Recreation Commission. She 
is dedicated to fostering inclusive ways for residents and visitors to 
engage in our beautiful community.
  Through her efforts on the Oceanside Parks and Recreation 
Commissions, she works to ensure that there are programs and activities 
for everyone in our community. She is not afraid to ask questions to 
ensure that Oceanside parks and facilities are not only safe, but also 
sanitary.
  Ms. Seau collaborates with multiple key stakeholders, including 
community leaders and the Oceanside Police Department, and proactively 
engages with other agencies to seek where community members can take 
proactive action. She exemplifies what it means to be a community 
leader, and I especially commend her efforts within the AAPI community 
in Oceanside.
  Ms. Seau's work to improve the lives of residents in my district is 
inspirational, and I am proud to honor her as my May 2024 Constituent 
of the Month.
```
