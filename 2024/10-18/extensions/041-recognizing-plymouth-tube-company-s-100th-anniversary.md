# RECOGNIZING PLYMOUTH TUBE COMPANY'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
         RECOGNIZING PLYMOUTH TUBE COMPANY'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the remarkable 
journey of Plymouth

[[Page E1044]]

Tube Company as it marks its 100th anniversary. Since its establishment 
in 1924 by Herbert and Donald Van Pelt in Plymouth, Michigan, this 
fifth-generation family-owned specialty metals manufacturer has been 
guided by its core values--people, customer focus, community and 
environment, and long-term orientation.
  Plymouth Tube Company is a global specialty manufacturer of carbon 
alloy, nickel alloy, and stainless precision steel tubing, comprised of 
eight manufacturing plants and nine business units. It has long been at 
the forefront of innovation in the specialty metals industry. Plymouth 
Tube has consistently demonstrated a commitment to excellence through 
its production of tubular products in industries including aerospace, 
life sciences, fluid power, nuclear energy, performance racing, and 
industrial markets.
  Plymouth Tube Company is committed to its people and the communities 
it serves. Over the last century, this company has fostered a workplace 
culture that prioritizes safety, diversity, and professional growth. 
The employees of Plymouth Tube are not just workers; they are artisans 
of their craft, dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what is 
possible. In doing so, they have created a legacy of craftsmanship and 
integrity that has earned the trust of customers.
  Plymouth Tube Company's dedication to sustainability and responsible 
manufacturing practices reflects the company's understanding of its 
role in building a better world. Facing the challenges of climate 
change and environmental stewardship, Plymouth Tube has taken a 
proactive stance by investing in technologies and practices that not 
only benefit their business but also contribute to a healthier planet.
  The success of Plymouth Tube Company is not just a testament to its 
innovation but also to its dedication to building strong relationships 
with its customers and partners. By engaging deeply with those they 
serve, Plymouth has fostered an environment of continuous improvement, 
making it a trusted global source for steel tubing and titanium and 
steel extrusions.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to have Plymouth Tube Companies corporate 
headquarters in Warrenville, Illinois, part of the Illinois 11th 
District. I invite my colleagues to join me in celebrating this 
centennial achievement of excellence in manufacturing and innovation. 
We look forward to witnessing their continued success in the years to 
come.
```
