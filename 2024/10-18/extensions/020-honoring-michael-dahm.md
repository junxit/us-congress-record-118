# HONORING MICHAEL DAHM

**Speaking:** Banks, Jim (B001299) [R-IN]

```
                         HONORING MICHAEL DAHM

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM BANKS

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. BANKS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the distinguished 
career of business and community leader Michael Dahm of Fort Wayne, 
Indiana. His contributions have greatly improved the lives of those in 
our community and helped make the region a wonderful place to live and 
do business.
  A native Hoosier, Michael has been deeply committed to the growth and 
prosperity of northeast Indiana. After graduating from Central Catholic 
High School in Fort Wayne, he earned a bachelor of business 
administration with a minor in real estate from Xavier University. Upon 
returning to Fort Wayne, he co-founded NAI Harding Dahm, now Bradley 
Company, where I had the privilege of working alongside him as a 
commercial real estate broker. More than five decades later, Michael 
continues to share his expertise in office, industrial, and investment 
sales and leasing.
  Throughout his career, Michael has given back to the community in 
numerous ways. Among his many service contributions and accolades, he 
was named ``Realtor of the Year'' by the Indiana Commercial Board of 
Realtors (ICBR). He has also served as president of the Society of 
Industrial and Office Realtors (SIOR) and the Fort Wayne Board of 
Realtors. His volunteer work includes serving on the boards of the 
Better Business Bureau and Big Brothers Big Sisters of America. 
Additionally, he is a past member of the Anthony Wayne Rotary Club and 
a current member of the Rotary Club of Fort Wayne.
  Recently, Michael celebrated both his 90th birthday and his 61st 
wedding anniversary with his wife, Dee Dee. As he prepares to retire 
after 66 years in real estate, I want to express my gratitude for his 
decades of service to our region. People like Michael are the reason 
the Midwest remains the backbone of America. I wish him continued 
success in this next great chapter of life.

                          ____________________
```
