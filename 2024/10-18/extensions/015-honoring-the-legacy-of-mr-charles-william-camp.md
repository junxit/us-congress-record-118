# HONORING THE LEGACY OF MR. CHARLES WILLIAM CAMP

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
            HONORING THE LEGACY OF MR. CHARLES WILLIAM CAMP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Mr. Charles William Camp. Mr. Camp, who passed away at the age of 
80, leaves behind an indelible legacy of advocacy for social and 
economic justice for all.
  Born in Anderson, South Carolina, in 1944, Mr. Camp dedicated his 
life to championing the rights of workers and marginalized communities. 
His journey began at Millsaps College, where he first engaged in the 
civil rights work and organizing efforts that shaped his lifelong 
commitment to equity and justice. As a student, Mr. Camp faced threats 
to his safety because of his activism, yet he never wavered in his 
fight for racial equity and workers rights.
  Throughout his career, Mr. Camp was a tireless advocate for the fair 
treatment of workers, serving in various influential roles including as 
Executive Secretary of the Sacramento Central Labor Council. He 
skillfully combined his passion for economic justice with a commitment 
to inclusivity, ensuring that the voices of all community members were 
heard and valued.
  Mr. Camp was also a devoted family man, actively supporting the 
artistic and personal endeavors of his sons and grandchildren. His 
generosity extended beyond his family, and he was known for mentoring 
young activists and always encouraging others to engage in the 
political process and work towards a more just society.
  Mr. Camp's legacy is one of compassion, leadership, and unwavering 
dedication to advancing a progressive vision of social justice for all. 
His impact will be felt for generations to come, not only through the 
organizations he strengthened but also through the countless lives he 
touched.
  Mr. Camp's enduring spirit will live on in his beloved wife, two 
sons, and five grandchildren, who will carry on his legacy of service 
and commitment to justice. I want to extend my deepest sympathies to 
Mr. Camp's family and all who were fortunate to know him. Together, let 
us celebrate his life and the enduring impact he made on our community.
```
