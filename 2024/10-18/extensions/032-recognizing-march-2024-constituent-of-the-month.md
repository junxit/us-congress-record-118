# RECOGNIZING MARCH 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
            RECOGNIZING MARCH 2024 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, during Women's History Month, it is my great 
honor to recognize a leading figure in the Oceanside community, 
Gabriela Hooshmand, as my Constituent of the Month for March 2024.
  Gabriela is the current President and Co-founder of MANA de North 
County San Diego

[[Page E1042]]

and has contributed over fifteen years to the organization, which aims 
to empower Latinas to be involved in community service, develop their 
leadership skills, and advocate for themselves.
  MANA de North County San Diego's Hermanitas Program matches students' 
skills and passions to a career path. Gabriella's introduction of this 
program has been instrumental in the betterment of my district's youth, 
especially for young Latinas. Carlsbad Unified School District, 
Oceanside Unified School District, Vista Unified School District, and 
Vista Charter Public Schools all participate in this unique national 
youth mentoring program for Latinas.
  Gabriela's work to improve the lives of countless Latinas in my 
district is inspirational and I'm proud to honor her as my March 2024 
Constituent of the Month.

                          ____________________
```
