# CELEBRATING THE 10TH ANNIVERSARY OF PASTOR JEFFREY C. CHAMBLESS OF MOUNT MORIAH MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
  CELEBRATING THE 10TH ANNIVERSARY OF PASTOR JEFFREY C. CHAMBLESS OF 
                 MOUNT MORIAH MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
10th pastoral anniversary of Pastor Jeffrey C. Chambless of Mount 
Moriah Missionary Baptist Church. Pastor Chambless' story began in 
Rockville Centre, Long Island, where he was nurtured in the faith as a 
Baptist believer at the Shiloh Baptist Church, becoming an active 
member at the age of 10. Pastor Chambless received his education at 
Canisius College, now Canisius University, in the city of Buffalo, 
later attending Erie Community College in pursuit of a major in police 
services.
  Under the spiritual leadership of Reverend Dr. Robert E. Baines, Sr., 
in 2014, Pastor Chambless was sent to serve at Mount Moriah Missionary 
Baptist Church. Mount Moriah was founded in 1960 by the late Rev. 
Anthony Benson, Sr. Since assuming the pastorship of the church, Mount 
Moriah has grown due to Pastor Chambless' lifelong commitment to 
serving Christ and his people, guided by the teachings of the late Rev. 
Dr. Morgan M. Days, Jr.
  His move to Buffalo to attend Canisius was not just an academic 
pursuit; it was a divine appointment. It was there that he met his 
beloved wife, Clarisse Y. Williams, and together they formed a family 
grounded in faith, welcoming their daughter, Camille. Their marriage 
exemplifies the strength found in a family that prays and worships 
together.
  As a licensed and ordained minister of the Gospel, Pastor Chambless 
embodies the belief that we are all called to be servants of the Lord. 
His passion for service resonates throughout our church, and his 
leadership inspires us to grow in faith and unity.
  Today, as we celebrate a decade of Pastor Chambless' unwavering 
dedication, let us recognize the countless lives he has touched and the 
profound impact he has made at Mount Moriah and beyond. 
Congratulations, Pastor Jeffrey C. Chambless, on this significant 
milestone. Your service is a blessing, and we look forward to many more 
years of your inspired leadership.
```
