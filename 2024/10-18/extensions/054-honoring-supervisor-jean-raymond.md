# HONORING SUPERVISOR JEAN RAYMOND

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
                    HONORING SUPERVISOR JEAN RAYMOND

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Town Supervisor Jean 
Raymond for her thirty-six years of public service to the Town of 
Edinburg, New York.
  As the longest-serving member of the Saratoga County Board of 
Supervisors, Jean Raymond's legacy is one of commitment, leadership, 
and unwavering dedication to her community. After spending her 
formative years in her hometown of Stamford, Connecticut, Jean and her 
family relocated to their new home in Edinburg in the 1970s. Jean owned 
and operated a local general store while also managing responsibilities 
as a single mother before selling the business in 1987.
  When the position of Town Supervisor opened, the Edinburg community 
encouraged Jean to run. Jean stepped into the role in 1988 and brought 
her strong convictions and work ethic to serve the town and give her 
time back to the community, working through evenings, weekends, and 
holidays.
  Jean has taken great strides to improve the Town of Edinburg. Early 
in her tenure, she secured a dedicated space for Edinburg's government 
offices and oversaw the construction of a new Town Hall, which opened 
in July 2005. Jean helped to close the Town's landfill and played a 
pivotal role in securing state funding that covered ninety percent of 
the associated costs. One of her most significant achievements was 
securing $46 million to repair the Batchellerville Bridge, a piece of 
critical infrastructure and the linchpin of a central Town route. 
Complete repairs to the 3,000-foot bridge took eighteen years and were 
successfully completed under her supervision.
  Jean continues to embark on new projects, including the development 
of a local park and nature trail. Today, Jean Raymond serves the Town 
with the same passion that has guided her for more than three decades. 
On behalf of New York's 21st Congressional District, I am honored to 
recognize her record of extraordinary public service.
```
