# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, GENOVEVA GARCIA CALLOWAY

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
          HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, GENOVEVA GARCIA CALLOWAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Genoveva Garcia Calloway as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. After 
completing high school, she enrolled at Contra Costa Community College 
in San Pablo, California. During the Civil Rights Movement of the late 
1960s and early 1970s, she was a founding member of the Chicano Studies 
Department, now known as La Raza Studies Department, at Contra Costa 
College. She also worked with Cesar Chavez's United Farm Workers Union, 
supporting local strikes and encouraging high school graduates to 
pursue higher education.
  After receiving a master's degree in social work from UC Berkeley, 
Ms. Calloway began working at La Clinica de La Raza in Oakland, 
dedicating five years to improving community health. Her career later 
led her to Contra Costa County Behavioral Health Services, where she 
served for 35 years as a Program Manager, working with individuals and 
families facing severe mental illness. In 2004, Ms. Calloway was 
elected to the San Pablo City Council, where she served four terms, 
including two terms as Mayor. Following her retirement four years ago, 
she began mentoring individuals interested in political office or 
nonprofit board appointments.
  As a co-founder of Familias Unidas Counseling Center in Richmond, Ms. 
Calloway has played a vital role in supporting families throughout West 
Contra Costa County. She continues to serve her community as a Board 
Member of the San Pablo Economic Development Corporation and a 
representative on the Contra Costa Community College District's Equal 
Employment Advisory Committee. During the COVID-19 pandemic, Ms. 
Calloway became an active member of United Latino Voices of Contra 
Costa County, working to provide critical information on testing and 
vaccination to Spanish-speaking communities. In light of the upcoming 
general elections, she has taken on the role of Elections Ambassador, 
ensuring voters understand the safety and accuracy of the voting 
process. I commend Ms. Calloway for her extraordinary contributions to 
her community and her dedication to public service. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor Ms. Calloway for her positive contributions to our community.
```
