# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, DR. MAYA ALTMAN

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
              HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, DR. MAYA ALTMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Dr. Myra Altman as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Dr. Altman 
has dedicated her career to advancing mental health care, particularly 
for children. Through her leadership, she helped develop and implement 
the Brightline Kids program, which provides free mental health coaching 
to parents and children in California. This innovative program is 
available to all California residents with children aged 0-12, offering 
crucial support in a system often hindered by financial and 
administrative barriers.
  Dr. Altman's commitment to improving mental health care is evident in 
her tireless work leading a national team of clinicians, conducting 
research to enhance care quality, and developing sustainable models for 
affordable mental health services. Her vision to expand access to care 
through both government and private insurance reflects her dedication 
to ensuring individuals receive the care they need, regardless of 
financial circumstances. She is a role model whose work is transforming 
mental health care for children and families across our state and 
nation. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my pleasure to honor Dr. Myra Altman for her invaluable 
contributions to our community.
```
