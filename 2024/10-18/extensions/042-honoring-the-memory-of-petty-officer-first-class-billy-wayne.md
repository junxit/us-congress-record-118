# HONORING THE MEMORY OF PETTY OFFICER FIRST CLASS BILLY WAYNE MACHEN AND DEDICATING CAMP BILLY MACHEN MEMORIAL PARK

**Speaking:** Moran, Nathaniel (M001224) [R-TX]

```
HONORING THE MEMORY OF PETTY OFFICER FIRST CLASS BILLY WAYNE MACHEN AND 
               DEDICATING CAMP BILLY MACHEN MEMORIAL PARK

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to express my gratitude and 
admiration as we honor the service and valor of someone who truly 
embodies Texas spirit, Petty Officer First Class Billy Wayne Machen. 
Today, we honor his legacy and dedicate a Texas Historical Commission 
Marker at the Camp Billy Machen Memorial Park.
  East Texans are tough-minded, kind-hearted, hard-working, selfless, 
and service-oriented individuals. Petty Officer Machen personified each 
of these characteristics, as evidenced by his service and sacrifice--
not just for our country, but also for his fellow service members in 
combat.
  During the Vietnam war, Petty Officer Machen and his Underwater 
Demolition Team--a unit that pioneered the creation of the Navy SEALs--
were assigned to the Rung Sat Special Zone, also known as the ``Forest 
of Assassins.''
  Despite the imminent danger he faced as the team's point man, he made 
the courageous decision to confront enemy forces alone, allowing his 
comrades to escape safely and unharmed. In spite of being mortally 
wounded, Petty Officer Machen was rescued by his comrades, Roger 
Moscone and Nick Benzschawel, and carried more than 500 yards to 
safety.
  Through his swift actions and sacrifice, Petty Officer Machen laid 
the foundation for our Nation's most elite force of fighters by never 
leaving a teammate behind--a motto that holds as much weight across our 
military today as it did nearly 60 years ago when he perished in the 
line of duty.
  Today, generations after the Founding Fathers sacrificed to preserve 
our freedom, the spirit and essence of that freedom are defended and 
embodied by the acts of heroism of our service members. Petty Officer 
Machen's story is a testament to the enduring spirit of our sovereign 
Nation.
  I want to commend all who have played a role in honoring the life and 
service of Petty Officer First Class Billy Wayne Machen. This 
commitment to an American hero reflects our shared values in promoting 
and preserving freedom for generations past, present, and future.
```
