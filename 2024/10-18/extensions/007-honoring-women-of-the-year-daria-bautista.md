# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, DARIA BAUTISTA

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, DARIA BAUTISTA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Daria Bautista, 
recognized as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of 
the Year. Ms. Bautista is a dedicated public servant, exceptional 
leader, and vital asset to the United States Air Force. With over 32 
years of administrative experience, Ms. Bautista has served at three 
Air Force Bases throughout her career. She is currently the Executive 
Assistant to the Commander of the 60th Air Mobility Wing at Travis Air 
Force Base. In this role, she has executed nearly 3,000 wing-level 
meetings, managed 1,100 personal records, and coordinated the Wing's 
awards program and official correspondence with the 18th Air Force, Air 
Mobility Command, Headquarters Air Force, and local community 
leadership.
  Her efforts ensure the seamless coordination of critical initiatives 
that support one of our Nation's most essential Air Mobility Wings. 
Beyond her official duties, Ms. Bautista mentors a team of 33 active-
duty and civilian personnel, overseeing a $216,000 Wing Staff Agency 
budget to facilitate 11 senior leader travel events and ensure 
efficient resource utilization supporting the base's mission. Ms. 
Bautista also initiated the first-ever Base Defense Council, with 
lessons learned that have informed installation protection procedures 
throughout the 110,000-member command. Her extraordinary service, 
leadership, and commitment to the United States Air Force's mission are 
truly commendable. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor Ms. Bautista for her 
invaluable contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
