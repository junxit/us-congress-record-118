# RECOGNIZING MIRACLE FLIGHTS 100-MILLIONTH MILE FLOWN

**Speaking:** Titus, Dina (T000468) [D-NV]

```
          RECOGNIZING MIRACLE FLIGHTS 100-MILLIONTH MILE FLOWN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DINA TITUS

                               of nevada

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Ms. TITUS. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Miracle Flights as they 
prepare to celebrate their 100-millionth mile flown on October 24th. 
Miracle Flights was founded in Clark County, Nevada, in 1985, and has 
remained dedicated to its mission of providing free flights to children 
and families in need of specialized medical care across the United 
States.
  Their long-standing partnership with Southwest Airlines has been 
instrumental in facilitating these flights, providing essential support 
to families who would otherwise be unable to access the specialized 
medical care they require.
  On Thursday, October 24. Miracle Flights will reach an extraordinary 
milestone of 100 million miles flown, symbolizing the organization's 
unwavering commitment to ensuring no distance is too great for children 
to receive life-saving treatment, regardless of their financial means.
  The flight will benefit 8-year-old Luna D. of Las Vegas, who was 
diagnosed with an optic glioma tumor in May 2017. Luna travels to 
Primary Children's Hospital in Salt Lake City, Utah, every three months 
for specialized oncology care, testing, and an MRI. Before boarding the 
flight, Luna and her family will get a big sendoff from the Miracle 
Flights team at Harry Reid International Airport.
  This monumental achievement is not only a testament to Miracle 
Flights' enduring dedication to families in Clark County and Nevada's 
First Congressional District, but it also reflects its nationwide 
impact on reducing barriers to essential healthcare.
```
