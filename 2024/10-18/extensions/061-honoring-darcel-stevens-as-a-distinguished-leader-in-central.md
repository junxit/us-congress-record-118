# HONORING DARCEL STEVENS AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR PRIDE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING DARCEL STEVENS AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                            FOR PRIDE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Darcel Stevens is an individual with a diverse 
range of accomplishments and experiences. Not only is he a graduate of 
the esteemed University of Florida and a proud U.S. Army veteran, but 
he/she is also a talented comedian, a dedicated political-social 
activist, and the recent organizer of the impactful ``THE DRAG QUEEN'S 
MARCH 2023,'' a statewide mobilization of over 540 drag queens and 
their allies from across Florida to the state's Capitol, protesting 
against discriminatory anti-drag queen legislation, FLSB-1438.
  Recognized for outstanding contributions to the community, Darcel has 
received numerous prestigious certificates and community service 
awards, including the Orlando Come Out With Pride Award, the Chuck 
Hummer Award, the Orlando Social Justice Award, Watermark Orlando's 
Most Outstanding People Honors, the 2023 Naples Pride Award, and the 
2023 Rainbow Awards for Outstanding Community Service.
  In the competitive realm of drag, Darcel has proven herself. She has 
emerged victorious in several regional, state, and national 
competitions, including the titles of Miss National at Large, Miss 
Continental Plus, and Miss FL Universo Latina. She is also the reigning 
Miss FL Babalu International 2024.
  Professionally, Darcel excels as Harmony Healthcare's marketing-
outreach coordinator, with a passion for providing valuable health 
services to marginalized communities. Darcel showcases his exceptional 
skills utilizing diverse marketing strategies while building valuable 
community partnerships.
  However, it is during the weekends that her unique talents shine 
through as she takes the

[[Page E1049]]

stage across the nation and Florida, captivating audiences with 
mesmerizing performances and quick-witted improvisational repertoire.
  Beyond the glitz and glamour, Darcel Stevens is a remarkable 
individual with a heart of gold known for her brassy and complicated 
persona, but behind the scenes, a true gem. Always ready to lend a 
helping hand to those in need, generously giving time and resources to 
support numerous charitable causes. Her unique combination of humor, 
complexity, and unwavering kindness leaves a lasting impression on all 
who cross his or her fabulous path. ``Let us be vigilant but not 
afraid!''
```
