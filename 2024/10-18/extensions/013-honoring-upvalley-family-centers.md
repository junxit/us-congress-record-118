# HONORING UPVALLEY FAMILY CENTERS

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                    HONORING UPVALLEY FAMILY CENTERS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
UpValley Family Centers, an esteemed group in our community. This 
organization is being honored today for their 25th anniversary and 
outstanding service in promoting equity and access to social resources 
throughout Napa County.
  Since its founding in 1999, UpValley Family Centers has worked to 
support working families in the greater Calistoga and St. Helena area. 
UpValley Family Centers is an organization committed to offering 
prevention and intervention services at a local level. It generously 
serves our community with time and the money it raises.
  UpValley Family Centers' 25 years of service have been commendable. 
They serve over 4,000 people annually. Last year, 94 percent of those 
served fell below the median income for Napa County. Sixty percent were 
women, and 56 percent were Spanish speakers. The family center itself 
provides a variety of programs: from assisting residents with accessing 
health insurance and housing resources to playgroups and family 
support. UpValley also collaborates with over 40 partner organizations 
to help engage residents and community members as volunteers.
  Over their 25 years of service, UpValley Family Centers' programs 
have made significant longterm impacts. As a Department of Justice 
recognized organization, UpValley has helped over 2,000 people become 
U.S. citizens in tandem with the Napa Citizenship Legal Service. They 
have increased early literacy, socialization, and school readiness 
skills in over 1,000 children 0-5 through weekly bilingual playgroups. 
Children participating in their youth mentoring services are more 
likely to meet requirements needed to attend a 4-year college. And in 
2020, they distributed over $2.1 million in pandemic and wildfire-
related emergency funds, which reached over 300 households.
  UpValley Family Centers also participates in broader civic 
initiatives throughout Napa County. As a part of the Drug-Free 
Communities Initiative, they partnered with local officials and helped 
pass social host ordinances in Calistoga and St. Helena. And in 2020, 
they assisted local messengers and other Napa County-based community 
organizations to increase participation in the 2020 Census. Napa County 
saw a 2 percent increase in census participation as a result of their 
efforts.
  Mr. Speaker, we thank UpValley Family Centers for their dedicated 
service to the Napa County community. Therefore, it is fitting and 
proper that we honor them here today.
```
