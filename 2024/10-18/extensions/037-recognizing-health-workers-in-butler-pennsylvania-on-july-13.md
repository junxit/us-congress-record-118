# RECOGNIZING HEALTH WORKERS IN BUTLER, PENNSYLVANIA, ON JULY 13, 2024

**Speaking:** Kelly, Mike (K000376) [R-PA]

```
  RECOGNIZING HEALTH WORKERS IN BUTLER, PENNSYLVANIA, ON JULY 13, 2024

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE KELLY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 
outstanding care and emergency response from Independence Health System 
Butler Memorial Hospital and Allegheny Health Network, Allegheny 
General Hospital following the July 13 shooting at former President 
Trump's campaign rally in Butler, Pennsylvania.
  These tragedies reveal the enduring strength within our communities 
and the caregivers who are always there when we need them. The 
emergency response from our local hospitals included emergency medical 
service providers, trauma specialists, emergency management leaders, 
nurses and clinical staff, and countless others who worked together to 
care for and support the victims at the scene and at their respective 
facilities.
  I thank the medical teams and leaders at Independence Health System 
Butler Memorial Hospital and Allegheny Health Network, Allegheny 
General Hospital for their care for the victims and for their 
unwavering commitment to their communities.

                          ____________________
```
