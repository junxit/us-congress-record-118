# RECOGNIZING BRACKEN AMERICAN LEGION CADETS 100TH ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATION

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
     RECOGNIZING BRACKEN AMERICAN LEGION CADETS 100TH ANNIVERSARY 
                              CELEBRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
outstanding organization from my district, Bristol's Bracken American 
Legion Cadet Junior Drum and Bugle Corps. In 1922, a Boy Scout troop in 
Bristol, PA, introduced drums and military-style bugles for parades, 
adopting the name ``Cadets of Bristol.'' This initiative quickly gained 
popularity among Bristol's youth, and, by 1924, the Cadets of Bristol 
sought sponsorship from the Robert W. Bracken American Legion Post 382. 
The Bracken Post graciously agreed, leading to the establishment of the 
Robert W. Bracken Cadets Junior Drum and Bugle Corps. Now, a century 
later, Bracken Post 382 remains the proud sponsor of our corps.
  Bristol's Bracken American Legion Cadet Junior Drum and Bugle Corps 
promotes an appreciation of music through a historical looking glass, 
and embraces the musical talents of its members, while cultivating 
confidence and self-development among its participants.
  Bristol's Bracken Cadets have flourished over the last century and 
have brought joy and entertainment through hosting concerts and 
festivals and even achieved numerous State Championships and Eastern 
States Titles.
  The renowned Bracken Cadets were in high demand for parades and 
exhibitions and performed in prestigious venues, including Madison 
Square Garden in 1970. They charmed audiences across the Philadelphia 
region with their performances from the 1950s to the 1970s in parades 
and have since transitioned from marching to performing on a flatbed 
float in a concert-style format.
  In 1981, during Bristol's 300th anniversary, corps alumni proudly 
paraded through the streets to the delight of the townspeople. In 2004, 
a grand 80th Anniversary Celebration reunited former members in 
Bristol. They performed the National Anthem to open Historic Bristol 
Day and enjoyed a memorable reunion

[[Page E1045]]

dinner and other corps activities. In 2005, an Alumni corps was 
established and remains active to this day. However, it is set to 
conclude after their final performance on Saturday, October 19th, 2024, 
at the All-Fresh Market on Pond St., Bristol (Historic Bristol Day).
  A mural commemorating their legacy will be dedicated during the 
event. The day will culminate with a celebration dinner at Georgine's 
and a farewell breakfast at the Golden Eagle Diner on Sunday morning at 
11 a.m.
  We are incredibly grateful for all that Bristol's Bracken American 
Legion Cadet Junior Drum and Bugle Corps has done for our community, 
and we proudly pay tribute to an amazing 100 years.
```
