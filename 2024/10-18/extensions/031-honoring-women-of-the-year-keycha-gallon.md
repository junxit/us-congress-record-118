# HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, KEYCHA GALLON

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
               HONORING WOMEN OF THE YEAR, KEYCHA GALLON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Keycha Gallon as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. As a devoted mother, 
entrepreneur, community leader, and author, Ms. Gallon has made a 
lasting impact on her community. Serving as Treasurer for Motivated 2 
Help Others, Ms. Gallon provides critical resources to underserved 
populations. Under her leadership, six major community resource events 
are held annually across Richmond and Vallejo, addressing urgent needs 
such as housing, food security, and health services.
  Ms. Gallon's passion for raising awareness aboul vital social issues 
is evident in her coordination of the annual Cancer and Domestic 
Violence Awareness Walk, which educates and advocates for health and 
justice in these crucial areas. In addition to her extensive nonprofit 
work, Ms. Gallon is the Founder and CEO of Keyz 2 The Future, a 
grassroots organization focused on supporting Black and Brown youth in 
the Greater Bay Area who have been affected by gun violence. Through 
this nonprofit, Ms. Gallon has established a safe environment that 
fosters healing, leadership development, and entrepreneurial skills for 
young people facing the challenges of grief and loss.
  Her advocacy extends to her volunteer leadership with Moms Demand 
Action, where she works diligently to end gun violence in our 
communities. Ms. Gallon is also a talented author, using her platform 
to elevate important societal conversations. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor Ms. 
Gallon for her invaluable contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
