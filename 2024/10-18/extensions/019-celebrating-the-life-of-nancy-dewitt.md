# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF NANCY DeWITT

**Speaking:** Fallon, Pat (F000246) [R-TX]

```
                  CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF NANCY DeWITT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PAT FALLON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 18, 2024

  Mr. FALLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of Mrs. 
Nancy Elizabeth

[[Page E1039]]

DeWitt of Frisco, Texas, who peacefully passed away on June 26, 2024.
  Mrs. DeWitt was born on April 11, 1936, in Evergreen Park, Illinois 
to Thomas and Verena Colvin. She grew up with five other siblings in 
Downers Grove, where she enjoyed bike riding, ice skating, and staying 
at her family's lake cottage in Wisconsin. In 1953, Mrs. DeWitt 
graduated from Downers Grove North High School and began working as a 
secretary for Swift & Co. in Chicago. This is where she met the love of 
her life, Dale, and the two married just four years later. Together, 
they traveled across the country for work and raised six amazing 
children. During this time, Mrs. DeWitt worked at a bowling league and 
bridge club while volunteering at her children's Catholic schools. 
Along with her best friend Robyn, she co-founded the Bent Tree Travel 
agency and took advantage of travel perks to take her family on 
fantastic vacations around the world.
  After retirement, the DeWitts settled in Frisco Lakes. Mrs. DeWitt 
was an active member of our community, and she spent many hours 
volunteering with the Republican Conservative Coalition to advocate for 
her values and support conservative candidates for office. Throughout 
her life, Mrs. DeWitt was most proud of having raised a wonderful 
family and spending time with loved ones. In her free time, she enjoyed 
singing along to Elvis Presley and Engelbert Humperdinck, her two 
favorite artists. Moreover, Mrs. DeWitt had a great sense of humor and 
always had a clever joke ready to bring laughs to those around her. I 
am saddened to hear of her passing, as her presence brought joy and 
warmth wherever she went.
  I have requested the United States flag to be flown over our nation's 
Capitol to honor Mrs. DeWitt's wonderful life and contributions to our 
community. She will be dearly missed by her friends, family, and all 
who knew her.

                          ____________________
```
