# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Listen to our prayer, dear Lord, for You are our mighty fortress. 
When we feel overwhelmed, we place our hope in You. When we feel doubt, 
You fill us with Your faith. When we feel afraid, You continue to be 
our light and salvation. Thank You for keeping us safe.
  Lord, guide our lawmakers. May they give You their burdens as they 
seek to do Your will and live for Your glory. Use them to help heal our 
hurting nation and world. Let faith, hope, and love abound in their 
lives.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
