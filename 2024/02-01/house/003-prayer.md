# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Dr. Michael Cramer, New Life Church, Osceola, Indiana, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy, Heavenly Father, creator and sustainer of this universe, You 
have given us the greatest Nation in the world to care for as stewards 
of Your people, Your resources, and Your land. Forgive us of our 
shortcomings.
  You have assembled these specific Representatives here today to 
fulfill a precise purpose in Your sovereign, eternal plan.
  You tell us that blessed is the Nation whose God is You. You tell us 
You give wisdom freely to all who ask.
  So we call on You today for Your wisdom in the matters to be 
discussed and determined. We look to You today for unity. We draw on 
Your strength. We ask for Your grace and mercy to accomplish all things 
in a manner that is pleasing to You.
  We yield all things to Your complete and sovereign will and pray this 
in the name of Your eternal Son, Jesus Christ.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
