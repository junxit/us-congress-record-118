# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8955

**Measures:** H.R. 8955

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota:
       H.R. 8955.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Indian Health Care Improvement Act to ensure 
     that, whenever the Indian Health Service undertakes an 
     investigation into the professional conduct of a licensee of 
     a State, the Service notifies the relevant State medical 
     board, and for other purposes.
```
