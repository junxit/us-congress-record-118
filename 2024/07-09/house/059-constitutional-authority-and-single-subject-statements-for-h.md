# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8957

**Measures:** H.R. 8957

```
           By Mr. CURTIS:
       H.R. 8957.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Energy to conduct a study and 
     submit a report on the greenhouse gas emissions intensity of 
     certain products produced in the United States and in certain 
     foreign countries, and for other purposes.
```
