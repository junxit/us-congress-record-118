                      RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the en bloc consideration of the following Senate 
resolutions: S. Res. 755, S. Res. 756, and S. Res. 757.
