# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
following nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Dorothy Camille Shea, of 
North Carolina, a Career Member of the Senior Foreign Service, Class of 
Minister-Counselor, to be Deputy Representative of the United States of 
America to the United Nations, with the rank and status of Ambassador 
Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary and the Deputy Representative of the 
United States of America in the Security Council of the United Nations.
```
