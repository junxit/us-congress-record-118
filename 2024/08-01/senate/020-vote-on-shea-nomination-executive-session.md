# Vote on Shea nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Shea nomination

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, The 
question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Shea nomination?
  Mr. DURBIN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant executive clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. 
Fetterman), the Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Menendez), and the Senator 
from Virginia (Mr. Warner) are necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from North Dakota (Mr. Hoeven), the Senator from Utah (Mr. Romney), the 
Senator from South Carolina (Mr. Scott), and the Senator from Ohio (Mr. 
Vance).
  Further, if present and voting: the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. 
Hoeven) would have voted ``nay.''
```
