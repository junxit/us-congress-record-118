# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9477

**Measures:** H.R. 9477

```
            By Mr. MEUSER:
        H.R. 9477.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 with respect 
     to reviewable actions taken pursuant to delegated functions 
     of the Securities and Exchange Commission, and for other 
     purposes.
```
