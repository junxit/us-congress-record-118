# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9485

**Measures:** H.R. 9485

```
            By Mr. SELF:
        H.R. 9485.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would permit the voluntary transmission of 
     patient contact information to certain religious or faith-
     based organizations by chaplains employed by the Department 
     of Veterans Affairs.
```
