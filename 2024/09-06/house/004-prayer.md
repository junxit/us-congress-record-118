# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Gil Stoy, St. Joseph's on Capitol Hill Catholic Church, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Heavenly Father, help us to be generous today. Give us the desire to 
greet those we disagree with, to offer a hand to the stranger who needs 
us, to forgive those who have wronged us, and to be grateful to those 
who have helped us. Bless the Members of this Congress. May they humbly 
serve their country well.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
