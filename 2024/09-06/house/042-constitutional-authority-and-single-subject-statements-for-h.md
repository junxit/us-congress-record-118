# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9486

**Measures:** H.R. 9486

```
            By Ms. STANSBURY:
        H.R. 9486.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a grant program within the Small Business 
     Administration to provide funds for eligible accelerators or 
     eligible incubators that provide services to small business 
     concerns owned and controlled by women and rural small 
     business concerns, and for other purposes.
```
