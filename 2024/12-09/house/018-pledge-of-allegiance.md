# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentleman from Washington (Mr. 
Larsen) come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mr. LARSEN of Washington led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On December 9, 2024, page H6450, in the third column, the 
following appeared: gentleman from Pennsylvania (Mr. THOMPSON) 
come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance. Mr. 
THOMPSON of Pennsylvania led
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: gentleman from 
Washington (Mr. LARSEN) come forward and lead the House in the 
Pledge of Allegiance. Mr. LARSEN of Washington led


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 



       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.

                          ____________________
```
