# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9447

**Measures:** H.R. 9447

```
           By Mr. DUNN of Florida:
       H.R. 9447.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 1 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will prohibit funding for the Montreal Protocol 
     on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer and the United 
     Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change until China is 
     no longer defined as a developing country.
```
