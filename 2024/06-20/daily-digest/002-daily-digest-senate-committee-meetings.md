# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
CHALLENGES FACING PUBLIC SCHOOL TEACHERS
Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions: Committee 
concluded a hearing to examine the immediate and long-term challenges 
facing public school teachers, focusing on low pay, teacher shortages, 
and underfunded public schools, after receiving testimony from John 
Arthur, Meadowlark Elementary, Holladay, Utah; Gemayel Keyes, Gilbert 
Spruance Elementary School, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania; William E. 
Kirwan, Maryland's Accountability and Implementation Board, Rockville; 
Robert Pondiscio, American Enterprise Institute, Medusa, New York; and 
Nicole Neily, Parents Defending Education, Arlington, Virginia.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
the nominations of Karla M. Campbell, of Tennessee, to be United States 
Circuit Judge for the Sixth Circuit, Julia M. Lipez, of Maine, to be 
United States Circuit Judge for the

[[Page D649]]

First Circuit, who was introduced by Senator King, Catherine Henry, to 
be United States District Judge for the Eastern District of 
Pennsylvania, who was introduced by Senator Casey, and Mary Kay 
Lanthier, to be United States District Judge for the District of 
Vermont, who was introduced by Senator Sanders, after the nominees 
testified and answered questions in their own behalf.
```
