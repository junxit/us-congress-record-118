# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the following nomination, which the clerk will 
report.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Stephanie Sanders 
Sullivan, of Maryland, a Career Member of the Senior Foreign Service, 
Class of Career Minister, to be Representative of the United States of 
America to the African Union, with the rank and status of Ambassador 
Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary.
```
