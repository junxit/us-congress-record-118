# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant executive clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 536, Robin Michelle Meriweather, of 
     Virginia, to be a Judge of the United States Court of Federal 
     Claims for a term of fifteen years.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Peter Welch, 
           Laphonza R. Butler, Richard Blumenthal, Alex Padilla, 
           Tim Kaine, Christopher A. Coons, Robert P. Casey, Jr., 
           Margaret Wood Hassan, Sheldon Whitehouse, Gary C. 
           Peters, Catherine Cortez Masto, Jeanne Shaheen, Tammy 
           Duckworth, Tina Smith, Chris Van Hollen.

                          ____________________
```
