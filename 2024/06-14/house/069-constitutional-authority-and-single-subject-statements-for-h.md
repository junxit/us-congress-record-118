# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8770

**Measures:** H.R. 8770

```
            By Mr. VEASEY:
        H.R. 8770.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a grant program carried out by the Department 
     of Homeland Security to fund university-based cybersecurity 
     clinics at junior or community colleges, historically Black 
     colleges and universities (HBCU's), Hispanic-serving 
     institutions (HSIs), and other minority-serving institutions, 
     and for other purposes.
```
