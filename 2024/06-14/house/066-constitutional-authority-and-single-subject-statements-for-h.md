# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8767

**Measures:** H.R. 8767

```
            By Mr. THANEDAR:
        H.R. 8767.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress shall have . . . power to make all laws. Article 1 
     Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XIX of the Social Security Act to remove the 
     exclusion from medical assistance under the Medicaid program 
     of items and services for patients in an institution for 
     mental diseases.
```
