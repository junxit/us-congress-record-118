# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8762

**Measures:** H.R. 8762

```
            By Mrs. PELTOLA:
        H.R. 8762.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 10, United States Code, to provide for the 
     membership of the Commandant of the Coast Guard on the Joint 
     Chiefs of Staff.
```
