# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8761

**Measures:** H.R. 8761

```
            By Mr. OWENS:
        H.R. 8761.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to provide that 
     non-Federal revenue generated through certain distance 
     education programs may be counted purposes of the non-Federal 
     revenue requirements applicable to proprietary institutions 
     of higher education (commonly known as the ``90/10 rule'').
```
