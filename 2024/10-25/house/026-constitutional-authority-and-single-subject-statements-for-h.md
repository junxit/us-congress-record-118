# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10047

**Measures:** H.R. 10047

```
           By Mr. LARSEN of Washington:
       H.R. 10047.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Public Health Service Act to direct the 
     Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the 
     Assistant Secretary for Mental Health and Substance Use, to 
     award grants to support community-based programs for harm 
     reduction services for individuals who use substances.
```
