# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10046

**Measures:** H.R. 10046

```
           By Mr. LANDSMAN:
       H.R. 10046.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase the new Clean Vehicle Tax Credit, the 
     previously-owned Clean Vehicle Tax Credit, and the 
     Alternative Fuel Refueling Property Tax Credit; to require 
     the submission of the Vehicle Choice Report by the Secretary 
     of Commerce; and for other purposes.
```
