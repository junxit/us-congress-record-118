# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10041

**Measures:** H.R. 10041

```
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER:
       H.R. 10041.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to establish the 
     childcare provider startup credit, and to increase the amount 
     of and make refundable the expenses for household and 
     dependent care credit.
```
