# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10053

**Measures:** H.R. 10053

```
            By Mr. PFLUGER:
        H.R. 10053.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Office of Personnel Management to implement 
     federal workforce training on prohibited activities which 
     obstruct, impede or oppose lawful directives from the 
     Administration, establishes penalties and confidential 
     reporting processes.
```
