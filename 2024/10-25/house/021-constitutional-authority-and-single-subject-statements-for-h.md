# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10042

**Measures:** H.R. 10042

```
           By Ms. HAGEMAN:
       H.R. 10042.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To limit the ability of the Secretary of Agriculture and 
     the Secretary of the Interior to penalize an owner of 
     livestock for grazing by such livestock on certain Federal 
     lands on which grazing is permitted on or after the date of 
     enactment of this Act and subsequently prohibited, and for 
     other purposes.
```
