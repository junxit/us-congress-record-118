# COMMEMORATING PRIDE MONTH AND HONORING ROSY MOTA

**Speaking:** Goldman, Daniel S. (G000599) [D-NY]

```
            COMMEMORATING PRIDE MONTH AND HONORING ROSY MOTA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DANIEL S. GOLDMAN

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 28, 2024

  Mr. GOLDMAN of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate 
Pride Month and honor an LGBQTIA+ community leader in New York's Tenth 
District, Rosy Mota.
  Rosy Mota is a public servant, activist, and advocate for her 
community in Sunset Park and around the country. Rosy is the Director 
of Policy for the Latino Commission on AIDS, a nationwide group 
dedicated to addressing the unique needs of her community.
  At the commission, she advocates for health policy that prioritizes 
the needs of Latino Americans across the country. In the past 18 
months, Rosy led a team of twelve community health workers in the North 
Bronx dedicated to improving health outcomes in this underserved 
community.
  Currently, Rosy is helping newly arrived migrants in our City 
navigate our health system and receive the care that they need. Rosy is 
also the treasurer for Sunset Park's industrious Community Board 7, 
giving her community a voice in City government and ensuring its needs 
are met. It is a great honor to recognize Rosy's tireless advocacy on 
behalf of her community and our great City.

                          ____________________
```
