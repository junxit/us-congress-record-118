# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8903

**Measures:** H.R. 8903

```
           By Mr. PFLUGER:
       H.R. 8903.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is,
       To direct the Secretary of State to revoke the visa of any 
     alien admitted to the United States under section 
     101(a)(15)(F) of the Immigration and Nationality Act who has 
     been convicted of assault in a police officer related to 
     rioting.
```
