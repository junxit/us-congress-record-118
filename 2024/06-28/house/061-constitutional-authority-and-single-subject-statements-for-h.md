# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8883

**Measures:** H.R. 8883

```
           By Mr. BANKS:
       H.R. 8883.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to require 
     Institutions of higher education to disclose campus policies 
     relating to responding to certain incidents of civil
```
