# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8892

**Measures:** H.R. 8892

```
           By Mr. HUIZENGA:
       H.R. 8892.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution; The 
     Congress shall have the Power to lay and collect Taxes, 
     Duties, Imposts and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for 
     the common Defense and general Welfare of the United States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To modify certain provisions relating to bilateral 
     agreements and AUKUS defense trade cooperation under the Arms 
     Export Control Act, and for other purposes.
```
