# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8899

**Measures:** H.R. 8899

```
           By Mr. MOYLAN:
       H.R. 8899.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to Article one of the United States Constitution 
     Congress has the power to enact this legislation.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill provides for the issuance of a Ko'ko' Bird 
     Semipostal Stamp to support Guam's native wildlife 
     conservation efforts, programs for native bird rehabilitation 
     and breeding efforts, and brown tree snake eradication.
```
