# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8910

**Measures:** H.R. 8910

```
           By Mr. TURNER:
       H.R. 8910.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs 
     to enter into an agreement with the Montgomery County Land 
     Bank for the transfer of certain land near Dayton National 
     Cemetery to the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other 
     purposes.
```
