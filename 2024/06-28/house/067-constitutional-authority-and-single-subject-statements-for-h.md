# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8889

**Measures:** H.R. 8889

```
           By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
       H.R. 8889.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 1: All legislative Powers herein granted 
     shall be vested in a Congress of the United States, which 
     shall consist of a Senate and House of Representatives.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for the sunset and disapproval of rules upheld 
     based on Chevron deference.
```
