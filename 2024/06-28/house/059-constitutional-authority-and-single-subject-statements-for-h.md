# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8881

**Measures:** H.R. 8881

```
           By Mr. CISCOMANI:
       H.R. 8881.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To make permanent the license portability authority for 
     contract health care professionals conducting C&P 
     examinations for the Department of Veterans Affairs and 
     expand the categories for who can perform them.
```
