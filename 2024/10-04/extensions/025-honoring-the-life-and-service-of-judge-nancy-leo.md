# HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF JUDGE NANCY LEO

**Speaking:** McClintock, Tom (M001177) [R-CA]

```
            HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF JUDGE NANCY LEO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TOM McCLINTOCK

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. McCLINTOCK. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life and service of 
The Honorable Nancy Leo, who passed away on July 29, 2024, at the age 
of 64.
  Judge Leo was born and raised in California, where she attended El 
Camino Real High School. After graduation, Judge Leo attended 
California State University, Northridge, where she earned a bachelor's 
degree in marketing in 1981. Afterwards, her love of the law led her to 
Santa Barbara College of Law to earn her Juris Doctorate before passing 
the California Bar in 1990.
  Judge Leo began her distinguished legal career at the Stanislaus 
County District Attorney's office. In 1996, she was elected to the 
Stanislaus County Municipal Court and two years later, she was elevated 
to the Stanislaus Superior Court, where she primarily presided over 
criminal cases. Despite the challenging nature of these cases, Judge 
Leo was known for her thoughtful, kind, and deliberate approach, 
treating everyone in her courtroom with the utmost respect. Her 
commitment to justice in Stanislaus County earned her the respect of 
her colleagues on the bench, court staff, and community justice 
partners.
  Judge Leo's passions extended beyond the courtroom, as she was also 
an avid music lover, particularly enjoying The Beatles, Frank Sinatra, 
and Dean Martin. Additionally, she loved baking her famous Christmas 
cookies

[[Page E1015]]

for her friends and family. Throughout her life, Judge Leo developed a 
love for Hawaii, which she considered a second home. She travelled 
frequently to visit the island of Oahu, where she married her husband, 
Douglas Leo.
  Judge Leo is survived by her husband, stepdaughters, and beloved 
grandchildren.
  Mr. Speaker, Judge Nancy Leo spent her life honorably serving the 
community in her judicial capacity for over 27 years. It is with a 
heavy heart that I rise today to honor her memory.

                          ____________________
```
