# HONORING COLONEL TIMOTHY EARL BOOK

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
                   HONORING COLONEL TIMOTHY EARL BOOK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I'm honored and humbled to recognize the 
distinguished career and tireless service of Colonel (Col.) Timothy 
Earl Book, United States Air Force, upon the auspicious occasion of his 
retirement after 30 years of selfless and loyal service to our Nation.
  Col. Book's legacy--most recently as Assistant Professor and Director 
of Strategic Management Systems at the United States Army War College--
is a testament to his unwavering commitment and dedication in myriad 
ways.
  Col. Book's service began in 1989, when he enlisted in the U.S. Air 
Force. He initially served as an F-16 crew chief in Misawa, Japan and 
Spangdahlem, Germany. He separated briefly to pursue higher education, 
while working in aviation maintenance, and was commissioned in 2000 
through Officer Training School at Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama. On 
the horrific day of September 11, 2001, then-Second Lieutenant Book was 
in pilot training. He served in Operations Iraqi Freedom, Noble Eagle, 
Inherent Resolve, and Enduring Freedom--where his exceptional 
performance earned him numerous awards and commendations, to include 
the Defense Meritorious Service Medal, four Meritorious Service Medals, 
six Air Medals, and an Aerial Achievement Award. His talents were 
further used as Branch Chief at United States Strategic Command, where 
he successfully integrated the Joint All-Domain Command and Control 
with the Nuclear Command, Control, and Communications Branch, 
streamlining operations and eliminating redundancies.
  Col. Books military career concludes as a command pilot with 4,152 
flight hours--1,376 in combat--a flight instructor and evaluator pilot 
in the RC-135 and E-3 AWAVS aircrafts, and having served in theaters 
around the globe. He has served in key positions across Squadron Wing, 
Headquarters Air Staff, and Combatant command levels, and notably 
deployed to U.S. Central Command as Director of Operations for the 
763rd Expeditionary Reconnaissance Squadron--leading over 1,000 Airmen 
in the operation and maintenance of a $712 million aircraft fleet, 
finally executing 645 critical missions. He went on to lead the 55th 
Operations Support Squadron at Offutt Air Force Base, Nebraska, 
commanding 234 personnel while providing operational support to twelve 
active component and two Air National Guard squadrons across five 
worldwide locations--further demonstrating his leadership and 
excellence at all levels.
  In addition to his exceptional service, Colonel Book is fiercely 
dedicated to education; he's earned three Masters Degrees, and 
currently is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Command, 
Leadership, and Management at the United States Army War College. His 
instruction of graduate-level Joint Professional Military Education for 
Joint and Coalition Officers and select civilian members of the U.S. 
Government, and his contributions to the design, execution, and 
evaluation of care and elective curricula and research projects, 
underscore his commitment to our Nation and his critical role in 
shaping future leaders of America.
  Colonel Book is fiercely dedicated to his wife, Julia, with him 
tirelessly on their lifetime of service, and his sons, John and Colin. 
Their sacrifices and unwavering support were pivotal to Colonel Book's 
successes and legacy, as were the sacrifices of the Veterans upon whose 
shoulders he stood throughout his career.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of a grateful Nation, I commend and render 
heartfelt thanks to Colonel Timothy Book for his unwavering dedication, 
tireless service, and faithful duty to the United States of America.
```
