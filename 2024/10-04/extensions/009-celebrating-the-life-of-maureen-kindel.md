# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF MAUREEN KINDEL

**Speaking:** Gomez, Jimmy (G000585) [D-CA]

```
                 CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF MAUREEN KINDEL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIMMY GOMEZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. GOMEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to mourn the loss of Maureen 
Kindel, who passed away on September 26, 2024 at the age of 86.
  Maureen was a longtime civic leader who mentored and advised dozens 
of elected and appointed officials in Los Angeles and was a special 
contributor to the political, educational, and business life of our 
city. As an adviser to Mayor Tom Bradley, she was appointed as the 
first woman in the history of the city to serve as President of the Los 
Angeles Board of Public Works. In that role, she initiated the city's 
first program to increase the participation of minority and women-owned 
businesses in public contracting. Maureen was a leader in business: she 
co-founded two prominent public affairs and lobbying firms, and was a 
founding director of the Los Angeles Business Federation, the Los 
Angeles Business Council, and the Pacific Council on Foreign Relations.
  Maureen served as a member of the Organizing Committee for the 1984 
Olympics in Los Angeles, and founded the LA84 Foundation alongside 
Rafer Johnson, Yvonne Brathwaite Burke, Gil Vasquez, and Peter 
Ueberroth. She also helped organize the hugely successful Los Angeles 
Summer Arts Festival preceding the games.
  In addition, Maureen contributed her personal and professional 
passion to public education. She helped design, organize, and lead 
three major education summits in Los Angeles, integrating participation 
by business and civic leaders, educators, and public officials in 
examining the status and acute needs of the public education system. 
For her years of service, Maureen received many honors and awards, 
including the Los Angeles League of Women Voters Founder's Award. Among 
her proudest achievements was working with President Nelson Mandela 
when he was released from prison and after he was elected President of 
South Africa.
  Maureen is survived by sons Tom and Gerry Flintoft, daughters-in-law 
Ingrid and Janette, and grandchildren Chelsea, Stefan, Collin, 
Caroline, and Aidan.
  Mr. Speaker, I hope everyone will join me in mourning the loss of 
Maureen Kindel and celebrating her dedication to a life of service.
```
