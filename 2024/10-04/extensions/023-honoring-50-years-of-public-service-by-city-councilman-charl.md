# HONORING 50 YEARS OF PUBLIC SERVICE BY CITY COUNCILMAN CHARLIE MIRANDA

**Speaking:** Castor, Kathy (C001066) [D-FL]

```
 HONORING 50 YEARS OF PUBLIC SERVICE BY CITY COUNCILMAN CHARLIE MIRANDA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. KATHY CASTOR

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Ms. CASTOR of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the career 
and contributions of Tampa City Councilman Charlie Miranda who is a 
fierce leader and public servant. We celebrate the 50th anniversary of 
Charlie being elected to Tampa City Council. Few public servants have 
served the City of Tampa with such steadfast and personal devotion. As 
he continues his ninth term. I congratulate and thank Charlie for the 
incredible impact he has had on our Tampa Bay Community.
  Charlie is a quintessential Tampeno-born and raised in Ybor City, the 
son of a Cuban father and a mother born in Ybor City to Spanish 
parents. He grew up speaking both Spanish and English in public 
housing, surrounded by a community of working-class families. Back in 
the day, Ybor City was a cigar capital of the world. People in 
Charlie's Ponce De Leon Court neighborhood spent their evenings playing 
dominoes, catching up with neighbors on front porches and talking 
baseball. This community shaped Charlie's devotion to Tampa. Seeing the 
way people in Ybor took care of each other inspired his work on behalf 
of Tampa neighbors, such as proposing three healthcare clinics for 
10,000 city workers, their dependents, and retirees to provide low-cost 
treatment alternatives.
  Charlie attended Thomas Jefferson High School and graduated in 1959. 
After high school, he began working, usually multiple jobs at a time. 
At one of those jobs, he met Shirley--his true partner in all he has 
accomplished and the love of his life. At age 21, Charlie married 
Shirley. Shirley was a true public servant herself who I was blessed to 
work with as she tirelessly gave back to her neighbors. I miss Shirley 
every day.
  As they did all things, they raised their three children together: 
Yvette, Danette and Frank. Before the advent of computers, Charlie 
supported his family by calculating payouts at Tampa's Jai-Alai 
fronton. In addition to serving as a City Councilman, Charlie still 
holds a job he has worked since 1988, serving as a steward for the 
State of Florida Horse Racing at Tampa Bay Downs.
  When Charlie first ran for City Council in 1974, his campaign slogan 
was: ``Who cares? Miranda Cares.'' Currently, in his ninth term of 
serving on the City Council, Charlie Miranda has not stopped caring 
about the people of Tampa. Charlie is a levelheaded, practical leader 
with a focus on the issues that impact people's quality of life, like 
public safety, mass transportation and affordable housing. His work on 
water issues has been invaluable to City residents and businesses, and 
to every mayor during his service. His institutional knowledge and 
commitment to improving the city's basic infrastructure have been 
critical to Tampa's growth and the quality of life for all of us. The 
Tampa Bay Times said. ``Miranda is the council member residents turn to 
when they have a problem. He is open, accessible and accountable, a 
strong advocate for open government and a voice for the little guy.''
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of my neighbors across the Tampa Bay area and 
the citizens of Florida, I am proud to honor Charlie Miranda for his 
service to the City of Tampa. Hardworking and selfless public servants 
like Charlie make Tampa the welcoming and generous place it is today. 
Our community is better for Charlie's immense contributions.
```
