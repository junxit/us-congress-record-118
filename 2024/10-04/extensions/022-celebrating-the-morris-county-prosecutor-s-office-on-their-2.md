# CELEBRATING THE MORRIS COUNTY PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE ON THEIR 200TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
   CELEBRATING THE MORRIS COUNTY PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE ON THEIR 200TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate and 
congratulate the Morris County Prosecutor's Office on its 200th 
anniversary. I am extremely grateful to be able to celebrate this 
incredible milestone with the Prosecutor's Office and the Morris County 
community. As an office of integrity, the Morris County Prosecutor's 
Office stands on the principles of justice, fairness, and a commitment 
to attentive response to the public. This truly selfless office is 
dedicated to ensuring the safety of those in Morris County. By 
cooperating with law enforcement, the members of the office work to 
reduce crime and protect the families of Morris County. As we reflect 
on the past 200 years of the Morris County Prosecutor's Office, it is 
clear they have exceeded the expectations of their community, and I 
know they will continue to do so for the next 200 years. I am honored 
to represent such an amazing community in the 7th Congressional 
District, and I look forward to what the future holds.

                          ____________________
```
