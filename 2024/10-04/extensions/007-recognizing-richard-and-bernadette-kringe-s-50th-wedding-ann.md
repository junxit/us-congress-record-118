# RECOGNIZING RICHARD AND BERNADETTE KRINGE'S 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY-

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
 RECOGNIZING RICHARD AND BERNADETTE KRINGE'S 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY-

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize outstanding 
constituents from my district, Richard and Bernadette Kringe, as we 
celebrate their golden wedding anniversary.
  Richard Kringe and Bernadette Kringe were married on September 14, 
1974 in Philadelphia, PA and soon moved to Croydon, PA where they have 
lived ever since. Rich recently retired from a life-long career as a 
plumber, and Bern was a school bus driver for many years: gaining the 
moniker ``Driver.''
  They raised two sons, Rich and Bob, and instilled in them the 
importance of family. They are also the proud grandparents of RJ, 
Dennis, and Jack.
  Congratulations to Rich and Bern on their golden milestone 
anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
