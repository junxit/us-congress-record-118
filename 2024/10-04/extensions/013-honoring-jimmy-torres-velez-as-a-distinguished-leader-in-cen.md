# HONORING JIMMY TORRES-VELEZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
   HONORING JIMMY TORRES-VELEZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                  FLORIDA FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Jimmy Torres is the executive director of APM/
Iniciativa, Florida, that advocates for the improvements and advances 
of the Puerto Rican and Latino communities in our communities in 
Orlando, Kissimmee, and other geographical areas that are part of 
District Nine.
  With the support and collaboration of many, Iniciativa has built 
opportunities for students, teachers, working people, and new residents 
of our greater Orlando/Kissimmee community.
  As part of his employment responsibilities, Jimmy coordinated the 
1199 SEIU Retirees political program in Florida in support of the 
thousands of healthcare workers represented by the union in the state
  He holds a master's from the University of Massachusetts, Amherst, 
and has been a professor at the University of Puerto Rico.
  Jimmy has been a community activist for the last 40 years and has 
worked in many Puerto Rican and Latino communities throughout the 
United States.
  As part of his work, he has created communication and educational 
spaces on radio, TV, and social media, and has published opinion 
columns in newspapers.
  Jimmy has experience in political-legislative work, adult education, 
and the development of mobilization projects, and he is a founder of 
the Boricua Vota Movement.
  Currently, he is the vice president of the National Puerto Rican 
Agenda and chair of the Civic Engagement and Action Communities that 
strategize ways of mobilizing and representing 5.9 million Boricuas in 
the United States.
```
