# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF DAISY BARNES

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
             RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF DAISY BARNES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I ask for the House's attention 
today to recognize the 100th birthday of Daisy Barnes.
  Daisy was born to Willie Andrew and Sally Ann Hooks Barnes on October 
11, 1924, in Lapine, Alabama. On May 16, 1942, she married the love of 
her life, Charlie W. Sankey. They were married 68 years before his 
death in 2010 and were blessed with nine children: Theodias Sankey 
(deceased), Charlie Lee Sankey, Willie Sankey, Daisy Sankey Henderson, 
Dorothy Sankey Brown, Doris Sankey, Diana Sankey Motley, David Sankey 
and Danny Sankey.
  Daisy attends Rockdale Baptist Church under the leadership of Pastor 
Samuel Robinson and has served as an usher and deaconess. She is also a 
founding member of The Sunrising Five gospel group and sang with them 
for over 50 years.
  Daisy is full of laughter and is a fabulous dresser. She loves 
gardening, cooking and caring for her family. She is also a member of 
Eastern Star.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Daisy on this milestone 
and wishing her a very happy 100th birthday.

                          ____________________
```
