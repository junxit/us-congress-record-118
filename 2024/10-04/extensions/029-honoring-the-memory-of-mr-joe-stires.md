# HONORING THE MEMORY OF MR. JOE STIRES

**Speaking:** Vasquez, Gabe (V000136) [D-NM]

```
                 HONORING THE MEMORY OF MR. JOE STIRES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GABE VASQUEZ

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. VASQUEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the memory of Joe 
Stires and to celebrate his legacy of service.
  Joe Stires began his lifetime of service to our community when he 
joined the United States Air Force, immediately after finishing high 
school. He served our community honorably in the Air Force for nearly a 
decade. It was during this time that he met his life partner, Matilde.
  After leaving the military, Joe took on challenge after challenge. 
After working as an insurance agent in Chaparral, Stires eventually 
opened his own grocery store. This store became a huge success, leading 
him to open many more stores. Throughout his career, Joe never lost his 
heart for service and giving back to his community. In his spare time, 
he became a licensed EMT and eventually a paramedic. He used these 
certifications to serve as a firefighter for the Chaparral Volunteer 
Fire Department for nearly 25 years. His exposure to loud sirens during 
this period led to

[[Page E1016]]

significant hearing loss, but Joe was willing to sacrifice his own 
health for those around him.
  Joe was a well-known pillar of his community and was there to lend a 
hand to anyone who was in need. He would help people that did not have 
food and people who could not pay their bills. Joe never hesitated to 
support his community with time, money or labor. His loved ones say 
that he viewed his community as an extension of his family, fondly 
sharing stories about the time he helped deliver a baby at his grocery 
store. Joe wanted nothing more than to share love and family, and if we 
all cared about out communities the way that he did, the world would be 
a much brighter place.
  On this day, I would like to celebrate Joe Stires as a veteran, a 
volunteer firefighter and as a pillar in our community who dedicated 
himself to a life of giving. His service has made his community and New 
Mexico a better place.
  On behalf of the great people of New Mexico's 2nd Congressional 
District, I thank Joe Stires, for all he has done for us.

                          ____________________
```
