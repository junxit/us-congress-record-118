# CELEBRATING FLY CREEK CIDER MILL & ORCHARD

**Speaking:** Molinaro, Marcus J. (M001221) [R-NY]

```
               CELEBRATING FLY CREEK CIDER MILL & ORCHARD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MARCUS J. MOLINARO

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. MOLINARO. Mr. Speaker, today, I stand before you to recognize the 
remarkable community destination, Fly Creek Cider Mill & Orchard. A 
pillar of upstate New York and a landmark in the Cooperstown area, Fly 
Creek Cider Mill has been providing incredible, one-of-a-kind touring 
and tasting experiences since 1856.
  Small family-owned businesses like this make upstate New York the 
heart of family traditions. The mill creates apple wines and hard 
ciders from 100 percent New York state grown apples, and visitors can 
experience the cider making process through the mill's most recent 
Learning Center. A testament to local cuisine, Mill Store Marketplace 
is the heart of

[[Page E1013]]

activity offering more than 150 specialty food products made on site. 
As a result of years of hard work and dedication, USA Today recognized 
Fly Creek Cider Mill & Orchard as the No. 1 best cidery in the U.S. for 
2024. Passed down for three generations in the Michaels family, this 
honor is well deserved and a testament to the orchard's historic 
legacy.
  Visitors experience the delicious food and drinks, the welcoming 
hospitality, and the family-oriented culture in every trip. Fly Creek 
Cider Mill is truly special to the community, and it is important we 
honor and recognize their legacy and continued growth.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues in the House join me in 
celebrating Fly Creek Cider Mill & Orchard as a pillar of Cooperstown 
that deserves to continue to be enjoyed and cherished by families and 
generations to come.
```
