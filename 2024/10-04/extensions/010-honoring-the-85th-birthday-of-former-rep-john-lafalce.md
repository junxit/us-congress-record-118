# HONORING THE 85TH BIRTHDAY OF FORMER REP. JOHN LaFALCE

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
         HONORING THE 85TH BIRTHDAY OF FORMER REP. JOHN LaFALCE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor former Rep. John J. 
LaFalce on his 85th birthday.
  Born on October 6, 1939, in Buffalo, New York, John LaFalce has 
served, throughout his entire life and career, as a public servant to 
the American people. He undoubtedly gave his very best to representing 
the needs of every American, and Western New York will never forget the 
care, attention, and dedication that Rep. LaFalce provided for our 
community.
  Rep. LaFalce began his career of service in Vietnam, where he 
committed himself to fighting to protect American values and democracy. 
After rising to the rank of Captain in the United States Army, LaFalce 
returned home to Western New York. Continuing his journey and 
commitment to service, John LaFalce decided to run for the New York 
State Senate and later for the New York State Assembly. While in the 
Legislature, LaFalce worked tirelessly for his constituents.
  After a successful few years in New York State politics, at the age 
of 35, LaFalce ran to represent the people of New York's 36th 
congressional district. Serving in Congress for almost three decades, 
Congressman LaFalce achieved an incredible list of legislative and 
personal accomplishments. LaFalce worked hard to provide Western New 
York with quality representation, whether through his advocacy for 
residents in his district affected by the Love Canal disaster, or 
through Renewal Community legislation that greatly benefitted Buffalo, 
Rochester, and Niagara Falls.
  While achieving so much for Western New Yorkers, LaFalce also pushed 
forward legislation that has had a monumental impact on all Americans. 
Specifically, during his time on the Committee on Small Business and 
the then-Committee on Banking, Finance, and Urban Affairs, Rep. LaFalce 
crafted consequential legislation, including the Financial Services 
Modernization Act and the Sarbanes-Oxley Act, which both revolutionized 
the United States' banking and financial sector.
  Today, we honor John J. LaFalce on his 85th birthday. We also 
celebrate his ongoing commitment to serving Western New York, and his 
inextinguishable desire for seeking to better his community. I, and 
many Western New Yorkers thank him tremendously for his service to our 
region, and to our country.
```
