# PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 100TH YEAR ANNIVERSARY OF ABRAMS MEMORIAL CHURCH OF DENTON, MARYLAND

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
  PROCLAMATION HONORING THE 100TH YEAR ANNIVERSARY OF ABRAMS MEMORIAL 
                       CHURCH OF DENTON, MARYLAND

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. HARRIS. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
Proclamation in honor of the 100th year anniversary of Abrams Memorial 
Church in Denton, Maryland.

       Whereas, the Abrams Memorial Church was established in 1924 
     as the Kings Apostle Holy Temple Holiness Church of Good by 
     Mother Carrie Virginia Curry and;
       Whereas, it has served God with passion and commitment 
     throughout its existence and;
       Whereas, it has served the local community in Denton and 
     the surrounding area and;
       Whereas, on October 19, 2024 the church is commemorating 
     its 100th year anniversary;
       Now, Therefore, I, Andy Harris, Member of Congress, do 
     hereby proclaim and celebrate Abrams Memorial Church in 
     Denton, Maryland on its 100th anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
