# CELEBRATING MS. LILLIE CUNNINGHAM'S 110TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Aderholt, Robert B. (A000055) [R-AL]

```
           CELEBRATING MS. LILLIE CUNNINGHAM'S 110TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. ADERHOLT. Mr. Speaker, Lillie Cunningham, of Florence, Alabama, 
has hit a milestone in her life that few will ever reach. Born in 1914, 
she celebrated her 110th birthday on September 26, 2024.
  It's difficult to imagine how different times were when Lillie was 
born. President Woodrow Wilson was in his first term as President, the 
United States had not yet entered World War I, and movies still had no 
sound.
  Lillie has lived a life of service to her community, volunteering 
with the kitchen ministry at her church, and exemplifying a true 
servant heart in all she does.
  When Lillie turned 108, she joked that she had found a secret diet 
that was responsible for allowing her to live to that age. Now, when 
asked how she has made it to 110 years old, Lillie says that there is 
no secret. Instead, she contributes her long, healthy life to the grace 
of God. Even at 110 years old, Lillie continues to do some of her 
favorite activities, such as cooking and finishing puzzles.
  Despite all the festivities surrounding her birthday, which included: 
receiving a golden key to the city, a proclamation designating 
September 26, 2024, as Lillie Cunningham Day, and being sent over two 
hundred cards from people across the nation, Lillie never neglects 
reading her Bible.
  I want to personally wish Lillie a Happy 110th Birthday. I pray that 
she will enjoy many more birthdays surrounded by those who love her and 
care for her. I know that her servant heart and love for her community 
will continue to inspire all those who have had the privilege of 
knowing her for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I hope the entire United States House of Representatives 
will join me in sending our best wishes to Lillie as she continues to 
celebrate this remarkable milestone in her life.

[[Page E1011]]

  Happy Birthday, Ms. Lillie.

                          ____________________
```
