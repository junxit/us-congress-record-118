# HONORING TAMMIE HOLLAND

**Speaking:** Bush, Cori (B001224) [D-MO]

```
                        HONORING TAMMIE HOLLAND

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. CORI BUSH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Ms. BUSH. Mr. Speaker, I, along with the people of St. Louis, rise 
today to honor and commemorate the exceptional life and legacy of Ms. 
Tammie Holland, a beloved on-air media personality and a voice for the 
community. Ms. Holland used her platform to connect with and inspire 
her listeners, engage with community issues and promote positive 
change. Her voice served as a guiding light to the St. Louis region for 
over three decades and will remain forever imprinted on our hearts.
  Tammie Holland, a St. Louis native, broke barriers for Black women 
throughout her work at ``Majic 108'' KMJM, KDHX radio, Channel 5 KSDK-
TV and Channel 46 WRBU-TV. We can all remember her as our morning 
motivator on the Breakfast Crew with co-host Tony Scott. She continued 
to break barriers becoming the leading voice on FOXY 106.9 FM's first 
all-Black female radio show.
  Throughout her life, Tammie always centered love for her community. 
Her career reflected her unwavering commitment to her audience. She 
gave back in countless ways, always showed up, and used her voice to 
help whenever she could. Her warmth and authenticity and sweetness 
brought comfort to many.
  On behalf of Missouri's First District, our prayers are with her 
beautiful daughter, Meadow, her family, her friends, and all who loved 
her. Tammie Holland has built a remarkable legacy, and she will 
continue to inspire future generations.

                          ____________________
```
