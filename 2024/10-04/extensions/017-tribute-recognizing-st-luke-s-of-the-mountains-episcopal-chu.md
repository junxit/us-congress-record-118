# TRIBUTE RECOGNIZING ST. LUKE'S OF THE MOUNTAINS EPISCOPAL CHURCH'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
  TRIBUTE RECOGNIZING ST. LUKE'S OF THE MOUNTAINS EPISCOPAL CHURCH'S 
                           100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ADAM B. SCHIFF

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. SCHIFF. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the centennial 
milestone of St. Luke's of the Mountains Episcopal Church, a cherished 
institution in La Crescenta, California.
  St. Luke's traces its roots back to the early 1920's when Canon 
Burson Renison, an Episcopal missionary and founder of St. Barnabas 
Episcopal Church in Los Angeles, began visiting the Crescenta Valley 
area to establish a new church. After meeting with renowned American 
portrait artist S. Seymour Thomas and his wife, Helen, who had fled 
their home in Paris, France during World War I to relocate in La 
Canada, the Thomases and Canon Renison began planning for the new 
church. The inaugural service was held in a school auditorium on 
November 25, 1923, and on the following Easter Sunday, April 20, 1924, 
179 people gathered to lay the church's cornerstone on land donated by 
Pasadena philanthropist Louise Janvier. The church was believed to have 
been named by S. Seymour Thomas after St. Luke, the biblical saint who 
was an artist and medical practitioner.
  Nestled in the beautiful foothills of La Crescenta, the new church, 
as a result of Mr. Thomas' artistic vision, was reminiscent of the 
California missions and chapels of the French countryside. The church's 
look is distinctive because it was constructed from rocks and stones 
that Mr. Thomas chose on his hikes in the local mountains and that the 
new parishioners gathered. On Christmas Day in 1925, the church opened 
its doors for its first service and in 1931, St. Luke's was admitted as 
a parish church of the Episcopal Diocese of Los Angeles.
  From its beginning, St, Luke's of the Mountains Episcopal Church 
thrived and grew, becoming the center of, and the spiritual home for, 
the greater La Crescenta community. The church has steadfastly followed 
its mission of service for one hundred years, meeting the spiritual 
needs of the community, continuing to positively impact the Crescenta 
Valley area. Some of St. Luke's programs include a community garden to 
support its Food Justice program, the Fire House youth center which is 
open to all youth, free breakfasts for middle school children, and 
participation in local civic organizations that host food drives and 
programs for foster children.
  I am proud to recognize St. Luke's of the Mountains Episcopal Church 
upon its 100th anniversary, and I invite all Members to join me in 
honoring this remarkable institution.
```
