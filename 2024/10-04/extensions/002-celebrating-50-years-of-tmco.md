# CELEBRATING 50 YEARS OF TMCO

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
                      CELEBRATING 50 YEARS OF TMCO

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE FLOOD

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate five decades of 
innovation, excellence, and leadership by one of Nebraska's foremost 
industrial leaders and economic drivers: TMCO.
  Over the last half century, TMCO has earned a reputation as a 
cornerstone of Nebraska's growing economy. What began with just eight 
machines in a small shop--smaller in square footage than most 
apartments--has grown into a symbol of economic opportunity, proving 
that hard work and vision are still rewarded in our country.
  TMCO'S impact extends far beyond creating jobs and fueling Nebraska's 
economy. Its commitment to community engagement has built a legacy of 
uplifting the very communities that it serves. From mentoring the next 
generation to supporting local events and art installations, TMCO and 
its employees have always put Lincoln and Nebraska first.
  Given all of this and more, it's no surprise that TMCO has thrived 
for five decades. From its leadership to its employees, the company's 
reputation for quality manufacturing, coupled with its deep community 
involvement, has made it a pillar of the Lincoln economy.
  On behalf of the people of the first district, I want to extend our 
best wishes for TMCO's continued success.
  I look forward to another five decades of remarkable leadership and 
community engagement.

                          ____________________
```
