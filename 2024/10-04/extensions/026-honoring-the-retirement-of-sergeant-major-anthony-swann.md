# HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF SERGEANT MAJOR ANTHONY SWANN

**Speaking:** Spanberger, Abigail Davis (S001209) [D-VA]

```
        HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF SERGEANT MAJOR ANTHONY SWANN

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ABIGAIL DAVIS SPANBERGER

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Ms. SPANBERGER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Retired 
U.S. Marine Corps Sergeant Major Anthony Swann for his leadership and 
countless contributions over the 14 years that he served as the Peer 
Services Director at the Virginia Department of Veterans Services.
  Sgt. Maj. Swann's career began with 27 years of dedicated military 
service. Throughout his time in the U.S. Marine Corps, he demonstrated 
his commitment to duty while gaining valuable leadership skills and the 
respect of his peers. Following his time in the Marines, Sgt. Maj. 
Swann transitioned to the Virginia Department of Veterans Services. At 
DVS, he worked to develop peer support services and has continued to 
serve his fellow Veterans and their families as Peer Services Director. 
His vision and leadership in addressing the diverse needs of our 
Commonwealth's Veterans--particularly as it relates to mental health, 
housing, and reintegration into civilian life--have served as a 
blueprint that will continue to provide guidance for years to come.
  I want to especially thank Sgt. Maj. Swann for his advocacy and work 
on behalf of Veterans contending with post-traumatic stress disorder. 
His dedication to service is matched only by his compassion for those 
he serves--making him a trusted leader and advocate for Virginia's 
Veterans.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that you and our colleagues join me in honoring 
Sgt. Maj. Anthony Swann. His commitment to public service and 
dedication to our country has been immeasurable. I would like to 
congratulate him on an exceptional career and wish him the best on his 
well-earned retirement.
```
