# CONGRATULATING OAK KNOLL SCHOOL OF THE HOLY CHILD'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
 CONGRATULATING OAK KNOLL SCHOOL OF THE HOLY CHILD'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to extend my 
warmest congratulations to the Oak Knoll School of the Holy Child on 
their 100th anniversary. I am truly grateful to be celebrating this 
monumental milestone with you all. The Oak Knoll School of the Holy 
Child was founded by the Society of the Holy

[[Page E1018]]

Child Jesus and has been at the forefront of empowering students not 
only academically but also in personal growth and social 
responsibility. Over the past 100 years, Oak Knoll has fostered a 
welcoming diverse student body of inquisitive students who think 
critically, embrace knowledge, respond with moral and ethical 
integrity, and make responsible choices that enrich their own lives and 
contribute to the lives of others. The Oak Knoll school has maintained 
a steadfast commitment to integrating faith with education, promoting a 
community of compassionate and informed citizens who are ready to lead 
and serve in a global society. The past 100 years have served as a 
reflection of the vibrant legacy that will continue to inspire and 
shape future generations. The Oak Knoll School of the Holy Child has 
stood as a testament to a century's worth of educational innovation, 
resilience, and dedication. May the schools next 100 years be filled 
with as much success as the past 100 years.

                          ____________________
```
