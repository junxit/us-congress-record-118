# RECOGNIZING ABRAHAM WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
                      RECOGNIZING ABRAHAM WILLIAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor Dr. Abraham Williams. I am 
proud to join the Bowling Green community in celebrating his retirement 
as Executive Director of the Housing Authority of Bowling Green.
  Dr. Williams began his career as Executive Director of the Housing 
Authority of Bowling Green in September of 1995. Since then, he has 
been an inspiration to the community working to make everyone around 
him better. Aside from overseeing 600 Units of public housing, 36 
Section 202 units, and 20 elderly housing units, Dr. Williams served on 
the Kentucky Housing Board of Directors and numerous other local boards 
dedicated to community service. Dr. Williams' career was devoted to 
empowering people to be self-sufficient and creating six non-profits 
under the Housing Authority and seeing great success with eighty 
percent of the Reach Higher's program graduates achieving full-time 
employment. For his dedication, he and the Bowling Green Housing 
Authority have been the recipient of many honors including HUD's 
Housing Authority of the Year and NAHRO's National Award of Excellence. 
In 2022, he received an Honorary Doctorate of Public Service from 
Western Kentucky University.
  A proud husband, father, and grandfather, Dr. Abraham Williams is 
looking forward to spending more time with his wife, Mildred, and his 
family. I thank him for his career of service and dedication to the 
people of Bowling Green.

                          ____________________
```
