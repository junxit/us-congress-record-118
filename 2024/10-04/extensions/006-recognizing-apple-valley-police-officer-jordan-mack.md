# RECOGNIZING APPLE VALLEY POLICE OFFICER JORDAN MACK

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
          RECOGNIZING APPLE VALLEY POLICE OFFICER JORDAN MACK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and honor Apple Valley 
Police Officer Jordan Mack for his courageous service during the tragic 
February 18th incident in Burnsville, MN which lost officers Matthew 
Ruge and Paul Elmstrand and firefighter/paramedic Adam Finseth.
  Officer Mack provided mutual aid support to Burnsville and was in the 
Bearcat and helped extract the mortally wounded officers and the rest 
of the Burnsville staff that were on scene.
  Officer Mack has been with his hometown Apple Valley Police 
Department since 2019. During high school he was part of the local 
Police Explorer Post and earned a bachelor's in law enforcement from 
Winona State University. Prior to joining the Apple Valley Police 
Department, he worked as a park ranger for the Dakota County Sheriff's 
Office.
  I'm grateful for Officer Mack's service to Apple Valley, Burnsville 
and Minnesota. He represents the best of law enforcement and it is a 
privilege to represent him in Congress.

                          ____________________
```
