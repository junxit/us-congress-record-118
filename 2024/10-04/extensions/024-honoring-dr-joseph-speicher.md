# HONORING DR. JOSEPH SPEICHER

**Speaking:** Frankel, Lois (F000462) [D-FL]

```
                      HONORING DR. JOSEPH SPEICHER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOIS FRANKEL

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Ms. LOIS FRANKEL of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. 
Joseph Speicher, a mental health professional and Chief Executive 
Officer of South County Mental Health Center. Dr. Speicher earned the 
rank of corporal when he served in the United States Army. He served as 
a marine engineer working on supply boats originating from Cam Ranh Bay 
and continued through the waters of South Vietnam during the 1968 Tet 
Offensive, one of the largest military campaigns of the Vietnam War.
  Dr. Speicher earned an undergraduate degree in Sociology and a 
master's in social work. He obtained a Doctoral degree in Public 
Administration from Nova Southeastern University. Dr. Speicher has been 
with the South County Mental Health Center (SCMHC) for 48 years, 
working as an Intake Evaluator, Case Manager, Team Leader, and Vietnam 
Veteran Outreach Therapist. He has served as Chief Executive Officer 
for 29 years with exceptional leadership.
  As CEO, Dr. Speicher has ensured that adults and children of Palm 
Beach County have access to quality behavioral health and substance 
abuse care. He oversees a wide range of health care professionals, and 
SCMHC's outpatient programs serve over 600,000 residents in Palm Beach 
County. Dr. Speicher also serves our community as a member of the Board 
of Directors for the Southeast Florida Behavioral Health Network.
  I admire Dr. Speicher's dedication to health care and his service to 
the people of South Florida. I am proud to recognize him today and 
celebrate his contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
