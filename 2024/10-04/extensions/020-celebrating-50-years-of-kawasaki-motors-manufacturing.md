# CELEBRATING 50 YEARS OF KAWASAKI MOTORS MANUFACTURING

**Speaking:** Flood, Mike (F000474) [R-NE]

```
         CELEBRATING 50 YEARS OF KAWASAKI MOTORS MANUFACTURING

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE FLOOD

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate fifty years of one 
of Nebraska's crucial industrial leaders and centers of innovation: 
Kawasaki Motors Manufacturing.
  Over its five decades of growth in Lincoln, Kawasaki Motors 
Manufacturing has demonstrated great stability and leadership. Through 
it all, Kawasaki has remained a key driver of Nebraska's economic 
growth, burnishing its reputation as a globally competitive 
manufacturing company.
  Starting as a fledgling motorcycle assembly plant in 1974, Kawasaki 
Motors Manufacturing has expanded to produce ATVs, Utility Vehicles, 
and even passenger railcars.
  To this day, Kawasaki Motors Manufacturing holds to the highest 
standards, maintaining a commitment to manufacturing innovative 
products of the highest quality.
  With its strong leadership and dedicated workforce, it is clear why 
Kawasaki Motors Manufacturing has flourished for the past fifty years.
  On behalf of the people of the first district, I want to extend our 
best wishes for Kawasaki Motors Manufacturing's continued success and 
growth.
  May Kawasaki Motors Manufacturing continue to flourish over the next 
fifty years and serve as a global beacon of Nebraska's innovation.

                          ____________________
```
