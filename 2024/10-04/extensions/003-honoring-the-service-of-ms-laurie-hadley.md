# HONORING THE SERVICE OF MS. LAURIE HADLEY

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
               HONORING THE SERVICE OF MS. LAURIE HADLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize the 
career of Ms. Laurie Hadley as she retires after nearly 14 years of 
service as the City Manager of Round Rock, Texas. With over 37 years 
working in local government, Ms. Hadley has been an integral part of 
Round Rock's transformation into one of the most dynamic and well-
managed municipalities in Texas.
  During her tenure, Round Rock grew to 140,000 residents and became a 
major hub for economic development, attracting international industries 
and serving as the world headquarters for Dell Technologies. Under her 
leadership, the city expanded its medical facilities, welcomed two 
university campuses, and bolstered its transportation infrastructure.
  Notably, Ms. Hadley spearheaded the largest bond election in the 
city's history, securing $274 million to enhance public safety, parks, 
and infrastructure. Her focus on cementing Round Rock's reputation as a 
destination for business and tourism is further exemplified by her role 
in bringing Kalahari Resorts & Conventions to the city--one of her many 
achievements that will leave a lasting legacy.
  Beyond infrastructure and economic development, Ms. Hadley's 
commitment to the people of Round Rock has fostered a stronger, more 
connected community. Ms. Hadley announced her plans to retire in 
December 2024, marking the end of a remarkable era of leadership. Her 
pride in Round Rock's transformation is evident, and her visionary 
leadership will continue to benefit the community for years to come.
  On behalf of the United States House of Representatives, I extend my 
heartfelt gratitude to Ms. Hadley for her dedicated service and wish 
her all the best in her well-deserved retirement.

                          ____________________
```
