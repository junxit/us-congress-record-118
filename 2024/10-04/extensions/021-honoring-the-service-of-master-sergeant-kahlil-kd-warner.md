# HONORING THE SERVICE OF MASTER SERGEANT KAHLIL "KD" WARNER

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
      HONORING THE SERVICE OF MASTER SERGEANT KAHLIL ``KD'' WARNER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I proudly salute Master Sergeant 
Kahlil ``KD'' Warner as he retires after 22 years of honorable service 
in the United States Army. Throughout his extraordinary military 
career, MSG Warner embodied the duty, honor, and loyalty that makes the 
U.S. Army the world's premier fighting force.
  Kahlil served in various logistics positions during his unique and 
distinguished career. He held numerous tactical and organizational 
leadership roles within Brigade Combat Teams and served in the elite 
75th Ranger Regiment. In addition to leading our Nation's sons and 
daughters, Kahlil helped train the next generation of Soldiers as an 
Advanced Individual Training Instructor and advised leaders at the 
Defense Logistics Agency Distribution Headquarters.
  I had the privilege of meeting Kahlil when he was selected to 
participate in the prestigious Army Congressional Fellowship Program 
and became my Defense Fellow. After his tenure as my Defense Fellow, 
MSG Warner went on to serve as the Military Construction Legislative 
Liaison for the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Financial 
Management

[[Page E1014]]

and Comptroller. In this role, he maintained a close relationship with 
my office and assisted other Members and staff in understanding the 
Army's policies, requirements, and actions.
  Kahlil's accolades include the designation of ``Demonstrated Senior 
Logistician'' from the Society of Logistics Engineers, FY 2019 Office 
of the Secretary of Defense Enlisted Member of the Year and is a member 
of the U.S. Army Forces Command Sergeant Audie Murphy Club. With six 
combat deployments across Iraq and Afghanistan and receiving numerous 
awards and commendations, including a Purple Heart, MSG Warner has 
dedicated his life to serving our nation.
  On behalf of the United States House of Representatives, I extend my 
heartfelt thanks to Master Sergeant Kahlil Warner for his 22 years of 
distinguished service. It is a privilege and an honor to recognize his 
selfless service to our Nation. I wish him, his wife Anna, and their 
five boys all the best in the years to come as he embarks on a well-
earned retirement.

                          ____________________
```
