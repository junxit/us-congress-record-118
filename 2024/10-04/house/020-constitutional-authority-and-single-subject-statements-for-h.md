# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9920

**Measures:** H.R. 9920

```
            By Mr. TONY GONZALES of Texas:
        H.R. 9920.

[[Page H5854]]

        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To include sexual assault and aggravated sexual violence in 
     the definition of aggravated felonies under the Immigration 
     and Nationality Act in order to expedite the removal of 
     aliens convicted of such crimes.
```
