# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9919

**Measures:** H.R. 9919

```
            By Mr. GOLDEN of Maine:
        H.R. 9919.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, clause 18
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 39 of the United States Code to require the 
     Postmaster General to be appointed by the President, subject 
     to Senate confirmation, and for other purposes.
```
