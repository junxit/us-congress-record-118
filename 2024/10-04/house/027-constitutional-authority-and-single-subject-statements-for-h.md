# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9927

**Measures:** H.R. 9927

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 9927.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To terminate the Shelter and Services Program of the 
     Federal Emergency Management Agency and transfer unobligated 
     balances to be made available for necessary expenses to carry 
     out the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and Emergency 
     Assistance Act
```
