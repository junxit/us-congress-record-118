# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9923

**Measures:** H.R. 9923

```
           By Mr. JACKSON of Texas:
       H.R. 9923.

       Article 1 Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
```
