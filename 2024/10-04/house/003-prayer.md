# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Spirit of the living God, make Your presence known to us this day. 
For it is Your spirit that guides and directs us throughout the course 
of our comings and goings. It is Your spirit that counsels and advises 
us when we are faced with challenges and dilemmas. It is Your spirit 
that comforts us in the uncertainty and anxieties of our lives.
  Knowing that You are in our midst in all that we do and endure, may 
we reflect Your spirit of gentleness when we encounter those who are, 
as we often are, ensnared by their own bad decisions, trapped in their 
own bad attitudes.
  Without ourselves falling into our natural inclinations to shun and 
deride and having been ministered to by the power of Your spirit and 
our wrongdoing and set right by Your own endless mercy, may we serve as 
Your agents of grace to those who have strayed from the right.
  Spirit of the living God, fall afresh on us, break our willfulness, 
melt our coldness, mold our kindliness, and fill us with Your peace.
  In Your eternal name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
