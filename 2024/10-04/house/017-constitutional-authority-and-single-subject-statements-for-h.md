# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9917

**Measures:** H.R. 9917

```
            By Mr. FINSTAD:
        H.R. 9917.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       Modifying procurement requirements relating to rare earth 
     elements and strategic and critical materials by contractors 
     of the Department of Defense.
```
