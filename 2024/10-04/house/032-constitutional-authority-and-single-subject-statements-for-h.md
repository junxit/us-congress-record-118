# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9932

**Measures:** H.R. 9932

```
           By Mr. NEWHOUSE:
       H.R. 9932.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the United States Postal Service from using 
     funds to close small post offices or perform mail processing 
     facility reviews in election years, and for other purposes.
```
