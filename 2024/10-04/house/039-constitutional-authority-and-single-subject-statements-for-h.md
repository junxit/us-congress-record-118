# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9939

**Measures:** H.R. 9939

```
           By Mr. WITTMAN:
       H.R. 9939.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Assistant Secretary of Health and Human 
     Services for Planning and Evaluation to conduct a study on 
     electronic automated deceased donor referrals. The study and 
     accompanying report shall include recomendations on how to 
     remove barriers to implementing such referrals, identify 
     costs of installing the system, savings in staff time, the 
     impact on organ donation volumes, best practices, and organ 
     donor referral criteria that can be standardized across 
     technologies to promote its broader adoption.
```
