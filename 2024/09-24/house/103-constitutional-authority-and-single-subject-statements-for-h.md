# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9789

**Measures:** H.R. 9789

```
           By Ms. STANSBURY:
       H.R. 9789.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the Interior to take into trust 
     for the benefit of the San Felipe Pueblo certain Federal land 
     in Sandoval County, New Mexico, and for other purposes.
```
