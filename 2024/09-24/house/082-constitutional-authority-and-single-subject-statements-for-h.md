# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9768

**Measures:** H.R. 9768

```
           By Mr. SWALWELL:
       H.R. 9768.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution, 
     specifically Clause 18 (relating to the power to make all 
     laws necessary and proper for carrying out the powers vested 
     in congress).
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to establish 
     within the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency a 
     Joint Cyber Defense Collaborative, and for other purposes.
```
