# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9785

**Measures:** H.R. 9785

```
           By Mr. LANGWORTHY:
       H.R. 9785.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Unites States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of the Office of Management and 
     Budget to submit to Congress an annual report on biomedical 
     research funded by the United States and performed in China.
```
