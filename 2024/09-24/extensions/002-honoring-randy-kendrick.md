# HONORING RANDY KENDRICK

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                        HONORING RANDY KENDRICK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize Randy Kendrick 
for her many years of support and commitment to Arizona and our Nation.
  For as long as I have known her, Randy has been a strong advocate and 
supporter of candidates and organizations that promote the values and 
principles that have made our Nation great.
  In the early 2000s, I met Randy while I served as a Member-at-Large 
on the Board of Directors of the Arizona Federation of Taxpayers, an 
organization that fought for low-taxes, opposed wasteful government 
spending, and supported free-market principles.
  Randy not only advocates for common-sense policies like these in 
Arizona, but she supports conservative candidates, especially women 
candidates, from across the country. Before I ran for Congress, I 
attended events at Randy and Ken Kendrick's home, where Randy hosted 
conservative women candidates running for federal office.
  When I ran for Congress, Randy was there to support and encourage me.
  As I end my time in Congress, I want to thank Randy Kendrick for her 
passion, encouragement and support for not only me, but for the 
countless number of people and projects she has had a positive impact 
on throughout Arizona and the United States.
  I know Randy cares about the future of our state and Nation. I can 
hear it when I talk to her. She is a true American patriot and for that 
I am truly grateful.
  May God continue to bless Randy and her family now and for evermore.

                          ____________________
```
