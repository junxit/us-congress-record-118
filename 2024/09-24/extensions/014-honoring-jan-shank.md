# HONORING JAN SHANK

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                           HONORING JAN SHANK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my honor to recognize Jan Shank, 1st Vice President of 
the Sun City Grand Republican Club.
  Jan has distinguished herself through tireless advocacy and 
dedication to the residents of the West Valley. Jan has been a driving 
force for educating members on the civic engagement process and 
bringing programming events to the community that educate the 
electorate on critical issues.
  The efforts of Jan and the Sun City Grand Republican Club have been 
pivotal to the success of elected officials and candidates in the 
Phoenix West Valley.
  I am thankful for Jan's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate her service to the 8th Congressional 
District and the State of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
