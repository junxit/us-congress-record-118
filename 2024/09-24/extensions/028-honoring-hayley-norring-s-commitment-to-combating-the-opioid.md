# HONORING HAYLEY NORRING'S COMMITMENT TO COMBATING THE OPIOID CRISIS

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
  HONORING HAYLEY NORRING'S COMMITMENT TO COMBATING THE OPIOID CRISIS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor my constituent, Hayley 
Norring, who worked in partnership with her peers to put together a 
Fight Back Against Fentanyl PSA.
  Hayley is from Hastings, Minnesota. Her brother, Devin, was an honors 
student who loved playing football, making music and skateboarding.
  On April 4, 2020, Hayley and her family lost Devin to fentanyl 
poisoning. He was seeking relief from blackout migraines and dental 
pain. He bought what he thought was Percocet marketed by a drug dealer 
on Snapchat. That pill turned out to be 100 percent fentanyl.
  Devin was 19 years old.
  The Devin Norring Foundation, in partnership with Hayley, produced an 
impactful and heartfelt PSA to fight back against fentanyl. In turning 
her grief into activism, Hayley has shown strength and courage beyond 
her years. I am proud to have her as a constituent and an ally in this 
fight against the fentanyl crisis.
  I thank Hayley for sharing Devin's story and demonstrating a 
commitment to combating the opioid crisis.

                          ____________________
```
