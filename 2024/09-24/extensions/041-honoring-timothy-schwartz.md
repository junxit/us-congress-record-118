# HONORING TIMOTHY SCHWARTZ

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                       HONORING TIMOTHY SCHWARTZ

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my honor to recognize Timothy Schwartz, Chairman of 
Republican Legislative District 26. This district includes the Central 
West Phoenix area of Maricopa County.
  As Chairman, Timothy has worked tirelessly to educate, train, and 
inspire the next generation of citizens through civic engagement. His 
efforts to ensure Republicans are elected have been instrumental to the 
success of Legislative District 26 and beyond.
  I am thankful for Timothy's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate his service to the legislative 
district and to the state of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
