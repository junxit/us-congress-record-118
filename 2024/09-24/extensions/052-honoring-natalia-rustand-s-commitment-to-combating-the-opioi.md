# HONORING NATALIA RUSTAND'S COMMITMENT TO COMBATING THE OPIOID CRISIS

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
  HONORING NATALIA RUSTAND'S COMMITMENT TO COMBATING THE OPIOID CRISIS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor my constituent, Natalia 
Rustand, who

[[Page E965]]

worked in partnership with her peers to put together a Fight Back 
Against Fentanyl PSA.
  Natalia is from St. Paul Park, Minnesota. She has proven her 
commitment to combating the opioid crisis through her advocacy and work 
with the Devin Norring Foundation. The Devin Norring Foundation was 
established to honor Devin's legacy and raise awareness about the 
dangers of illicit drugs and fentanyl.
  On April 4, 2020, Devin passed away from fentanyl poisoning. He was 
seeking relief from blackout migraines and dental pain. He bought what 
he thought was Percocet marketed by a drug dealer on Snapchat. That 
pill turned out to be 100 percent fentanyl.
  Devin was 19 years old.
  The Devin Norring Foundation, in partnership with Natalia, produced 
an impactful and heartfelt PSA to fight back against fentanyl. In 
turning grief into activism, Natalia has shown strength and courage 
beyond her years. I am proud to have her as a constituent and an ally 
in this fight against the fentanyl crisis.
  I thank Natalia for demonstrating a commitment to combating the 
opioid crisis.

                          ____________________
```
