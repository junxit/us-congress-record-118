# CONGRATULATING JUDGE DON DAVIS

**Speaking:** Carl, Jerry L. (C001054) [R-AL]

```
                     CONGRATULATING JUDGE DON DAVIS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JERRY L. CARL

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mr. CARL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and congratulate Judge 
Don Davis on his upcoming retirement after 24 years of service.
  Upon graduation from the University of South Alabama and Cumberland 
School of Law, Judge Don Davis began his legal career at McDermott, 
Slepian, Windom, & Reed in 1981. The firm later merged with Sirote & 
Permutt, and Judge Davis remained with the firm until 2001, when he 
became the judge of probate of Mobile County, Alabama. He was elected 
to serve as the judge of probate in November 2000 and was reelected in 
2006, 2012, and 2018 without opposition.
  His numerous accomplishments include implementing a new election 
system for Mobile County, receiving the More Than A Lawyer Award, 
formulating the Mobile Volunteer Guardian Program, and chairing the 
triathlon host committee for the Tri the Gulf Triathlon.
  I am honored to congratulate Judge Don Davis on his career 
accomplishments, and I wish him the best of luck in his retirement.

                          ____________________
```
