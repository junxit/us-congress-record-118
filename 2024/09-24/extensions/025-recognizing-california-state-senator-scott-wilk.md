# RECOGNIZING CALIFORNIA STATE SENATOR SCOTT WILK

**Speaking:** Garcia, Mike (G000061) [R-CA]

```
            RECOGNIZING CALIFORNIA STATE SENATOR SCOTT WILK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mr. MIKE GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
a member of our community who has proudly served the people of Santa 
Clarita for more than 12 years, State Senator Scott Wilk. As Senator 
for the 21st Senate District, Scott Wilk has dedicated his life to the 
betterment of his residents and to solving California's problems.
  His leadership in the Problem-Solvers Caucus as well as his 
membership in the Little Hoover Commission and the State Allocation 
Board has provided invaluable contributions to California residents and 
small businesses who needed a helping hand. Within Sacramento, Senator 
Wilk has worked tirelessly to enact change and deepen the bonds between 
his constituents.
  As a member of the Valley Industry Association, Senator Wilk has 
helped the Santa Clarita Valley business industry thrive and acted as 
an indispensable mentor to young entrepreneurs. His voice and actions 
have been paramount to the continuous growth of the Valley as the 
network he has built supports both businesses and individuals in need.
  A champion of change and a loving family man, I want to sincerely 
thank State Senator Scott Wilk for his years of service. The 21st 
Senate District and the state of California are better because of his 
voice and actions, and I look forward to seeing how he continues making 
a positive impact to those around him.

                          ____________________
```
