# RECOGNIZING KEIR SCOUBES

**Speaking:** Curtis, John R. (C001114) [R-UT]

```
                        RECOGNIZING KEIR SCOUBES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CURTIS

                                of utah

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mr. CURTIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Spanish Fork's 
Keir A. Scoubes, who was recently promoted to Land Component Commander 
with the Utah Army National Guard. Scoubes served in Iraq and 
Afghanistan and has been serving as a Lieutenant Colonel with the 
National Guard. In addition to his military career, Scoubes has also 
been a City Councilman and a teacher at Spanish Fork's Larsen 
Elementary. He is a perfect example of someone bringing their talents 
to the community and finding a way to contribute.
  I congratulate Colonel Scoubes on his tremendous accomplishment.

                          ____________________
```
