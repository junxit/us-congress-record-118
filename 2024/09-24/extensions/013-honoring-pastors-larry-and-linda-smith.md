# HONORING PASTORS LARRY AND LINDA SMITH

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                 HONORING PASTORS LARRY AND LINDA SMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize Pastors Larry and 
Linda Smith, of New St. Mark Baptist Church, who has announced their 
plans to retire later this year.
  For the past forty-four years, they led New St. Mark Baptist Church 
with integrity and devotion to the congregation and the greater Detroit 
community. Their unwavering commitment and exemplary leadership have 
undoubtedly been a beacon of hope and inspiration for countless 
individuals.
  Throughout these decades, your steadfast guidance has nurtured a 
thriving and vibrant congregation. From your work with Sunrise Sister's 
and your leadership in the Michigan District Association and the B.M.E. 
State Convention and Congress has not only shaped the spiritual lives 
of many but has also made a lasting impact on the broader community. 
Your dedication to spreading faith, love, and compassion is a testament 
to your profound commitment to your callings.
  Please join me in thanking Pastors Larry and Linda Smith for their 
outstanding service to our residents in Southeast Michigan.

                          ____________________
```
