# HONORING MANNION'S IRISH PUB AND RESTAURANT'S 25TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
     HONORING MANNION'S IRISH PUB AND RESTAURANT'S 25TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Mannion's Irish Pub and Restaurant on their 25th 
anniversary. I am extremely honored to be able to celebrate this 
impressive milestone with Mannion's and the Somerville Community. 
Mannion's Irish Pub and Restaurant harbors a warm environment where 
food, music, conversation, and laughter all find a home.
  For the past 25 years, Mannion's has showed its commitment by serving 
authentic Irish specialties and fostering a ``proper Irish pub'' by 
ensuring friendly service, making it feel as if you are entering not 
just a restaurant, but also a home.
  As we look back on the past 25 years of Mannion's, they have exceeded 
the expectation of what an Irish pub can provide for a community. I am 
honored to have such an amazing establishment in New Jersey's 7th 
Congressional District, and I look forward to what is to come.

                          ____________________
```
