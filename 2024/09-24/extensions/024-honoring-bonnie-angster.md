# HONORING BONNIE ANGSTER

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                        HONORING BONNIE ANGSTER

                                 arizona
                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my honor to recognize Bonnie Angster of the Sun City 
Republican Club.
  As Programs Chair, Bonnie has distinguished herself through tireless 
advocacy and dedication to the residents of the West Valley. Bonnie has 
been a driving force for educating members on the civic engagement 
process and bringing programming events that educate the electorate on 
critical issues.
  The efforts of Bonnie and the Sun City Republican Club have been 
pivotal to the success of elected officials and candidates in the 
Phoenix West Valley.
  I am thankful for Bonnie's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate her service to the 8th Congressional 
District and the State of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
