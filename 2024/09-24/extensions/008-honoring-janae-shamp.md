# HONORING JANAE SHAMP

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                          HONORING JANAE SHAMP

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, I want to recognize Arizona State Senator Janae Shamp, whose 
commitment to service throughout her life has impacted many lives.
  Shamp has called Arizona her home for over 30 years, earning degrees 
from Arizona State University and Grand Canyon University to become a 
registered nurse.
  In 2022, she began her public service career in the State 
Legislature, where she has served as Vice Chairman of the Health and 
Human Services Committee and as a member of the Finance and Commerce 
Committee, Government Committee, Military Affairs, Public Safety, and 
Border Security Committee, Natural Resources, Energy, and Water 
Committee, and the Senate Ethics Committee.
  Senator Shamp has been a champion for strong border security, 
conservative values, and free-market health care. In all aspects of 
life, Senator Shamp has proven herself as a committed, hardworking, and 
outspoken leader, who will always look out for those in need.
  I thank Senator Shamp for her service and dedication to the state of 
Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
