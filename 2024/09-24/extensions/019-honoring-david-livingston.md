# HONORING DAVID LIVINGSTON

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                       HONORING DAVID LIVINGSTON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, I would like to recognize State Representative David 
Livingston for his dedication to our community and the people of 
Arizona.
  Representative Livingston began to call Arizona his home in 1983 and 
graduated from Arizona State University in 1988. He then built a 
successful financial consulting business, where he proudly served the 
Peoria community for over two decades. In 2012, he started in the State 
Legislature, representing the northwest valley--first in the House, and 
then in the Senate, where he was selected as Majority Whip for the 
Republican Caucus. He later returned to the House where he currently 
serves.
  Throughout his tenure, he has played a critical role in passing many 
important pieces of legislation, including a balanced budget for the 
state. He also chaired several committees, including the Finance and 
Transportation Committees in the Senate, and the Appropriations and 
Finance Insurance and Banking Committees in the House.
  I thank Representative Livingston for his service to Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
