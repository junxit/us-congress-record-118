# HONORING KEVIN PAYNE

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                          HONORING KEVIN PAYNE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 24, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my honor to recognize Arizona State Representative 
Kevin Payne from Legislative District 27, covering the northwest valley 
in the Phoenix-metro area.
  Kevin served our nation's military in the U.S. Navy, where he was 
honorably discharged after eight years. He has an impressive resume in 
the private sector, working as an Electrical Engineer, Aerospace 
Technical Consultant, and in Information Technology. Kevin has also 
been a small business' owner and operator, which gives him unique 
insight into how government should work for all Arizonans attempting to 
achieve the American Dream for themselves.
  Representative Payne is the Chairman of the Military Affairs and 
Public Safety Committee, and he is a member of the Government and 
Regulatory Affairs Committees. He has worked to defend Arizona's 
standing as one of the top conservative states in the nation.
  Arizona's 8th Congressional District is thankful for Representative 
Payne's endeavors on behalf of his district and our state.

                          ____________________
```
