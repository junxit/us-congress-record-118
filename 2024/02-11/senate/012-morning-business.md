# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Cruz, Ted (C001098) [R-TX]

**Measures:** S. 3796

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

                                 ______
                                 

              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. CRUZ (for himself, Mr. Ricketts, Mr. Scott of 
             Florida, Mr. Lee, and Mrs. Blackburn):
       S. 3796. A bill to prohibit the transfer of Department of 
     Homeland Security staff from an international land port of 
     entry along southwest border unless such transfer would not 
     impact the processing of trade through such port of entry, 
     such staff would be immediately replaced, or such staff are 
     needed to actively engage in physical detentions to secure 
     such border, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs.
```
