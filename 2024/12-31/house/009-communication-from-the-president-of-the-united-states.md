# COMMUNICATION FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
         COMMUNICATION FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the President of the United States:

                                              The White House,

                                Washington, DC, December 30, 2024.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Attached is a message informing the 
     Congress of the death of James Earl Carter, Jr., the thirty-
     ninth President of the United States.
           Sincerely,
     Joseph R. Biden, Jr.

                          ____________________
```
