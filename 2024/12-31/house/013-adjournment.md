# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 5, H.Res. 1626

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 3(z) of House Resolution 
5 and pursuant to H. Res. 1626, the House stands adjourned until 11 
a.m. on Friday, January 3, 2025, as a further mark of respect to the 
memory of the late Honorable James Earl Carter, Jr., the 39th President 
of the United States.
  Thereupon (at 10 o'clock and 11 minutes a.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until Friday, January 3, 2025, at 11 a.m., 
as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable James 
Earl Carter, Jr.
```
