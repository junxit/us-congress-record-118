                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Ms. LEE of Pennsylvania:
       H.R. 10563. A bill to ensure efficiency and fairness in

     States of America; considered and agreed to.

       H. Res. 1627. A resolution honoring the life, achievements, 
     legacy, and public service of former President James Earl 
     ``Jimmy'' Carter, Jr; to the Committee on Oversight and 
     Accountability.
