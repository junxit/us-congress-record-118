  Dr. Khalilah Campbell-Rhone, Deputy Superintendent of Transformation,

leaving a lasting legacy.
  She has also turned around campuses in Houston ISD including Sam 
Houston High School, Thomas Middle School, Worthing High School, and 
Wheatley High School.
  These educators remind us that teaching is not just a profession, but 
a calling to shape future generations.

continued support.
  I thank these educators for their tireless dedication to our children
