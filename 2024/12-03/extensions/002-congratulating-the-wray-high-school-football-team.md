# CONGRATULATING THE WRAY HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Lopez, Greg (L000604) [R-CO]

```
           CONGRATULATING THE WRAY HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG LOPEZ

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 3, 2024

  Mr. LOPEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Wray High 
School Football team for winning the 1A State Championship with an 
undefeated record.
  Their overwhelming victory over Forge Christian, another undefeated 
team heading into the championship, is a testament to the teamwork and 
determination of each and every member of the team. I say thank you to 
the players for setting an example that will serve as an inspiration 
for future generations of high school athletes. They should all be 
extremely proud of what they have accomplished and know that this is 
just a precursor for the many great achievements that each of them will 
have in their lifetimes.
  I am honored to celebrate with the team and wish them all the best in 
their future seasons and beyond.

                          ____________________
```
