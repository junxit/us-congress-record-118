# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
the Chair to execute the order of November 20 with respect to the 
Weilheimer nomination.

  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Gail A. Weilheimer, of 
Pennsylvania, to be United States District Judge for the Eastern 
District of Pennsylvania.
```
