# DESIGNATION OF INDIVIDUAL AS CO-CHAIR TO THE NATIONAL COMMISSION ON THE FUTURE OF THE NAVY

```
DESIGNATION OF INDIVIDUAL AS CO-CHAIR TO THE NATIONAL COMMISSION ON THE 
                           FUTURE OF THE NAVY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces, on behalf of the 
Speaker and the Republican Leader of the Senate, their joint 
designation, pursuant to 1092(b)(2) of the James M. Inhofe National 
Defense Authorization Act for fiscal year 2023 (Public Law 117-263), 
and the order of the House of January 9, 2023, of the following 
individual to serve as Co-Chair of the Commission on the Future of the 
Navy:
  Ms. Mackenzie Eaglen, Arlington, Virginia
```
