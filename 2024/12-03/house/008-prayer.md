# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Emmanuel, God with us, come and ransom us from all that seeks to hold 
us captive. Release us from our fears, attend to us in our loneliness, 
bring us back from our exile, and receive us once again into the 
certainty of Your favor.
  You who are wisdom, who has ordered all things, show us the path of 
Your knowledge, and teach us the ways we should go.
  Bright morning star, bring us Your comfort. Divine dayspring, shine 
down on us the promise of Your kingdom and bring us Your consolation. 
Light of the world, dispel the shadows of our night and turn our 
darkness into day.
  O come, O King of kings, come and bind our hearts to Your own. Bid 
cease all our sad divisions. Shake all the nations so that our deepest 
desires for Your justice and mercy would be revealed. Fill this House 
and our lives with Your glory. King of peace, reign in our lives this 
day.
  For it is in Your sovereign name we pray.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
