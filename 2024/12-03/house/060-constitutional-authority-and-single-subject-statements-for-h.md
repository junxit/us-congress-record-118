# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10267

**Measures:** H.R. 10267

```
           By Mr. BOST:
       H.R. 10267.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution, 
     which states ``[t]he Congress shall have power to lay and 
     collect taxes, duties, imposts and excises, to pay the debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States; but all duties, imposts and excises shall be 
     uniform throughout the United States''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would make improvements to the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs delivery of community care and increase 
     access to care for veterans.
```
