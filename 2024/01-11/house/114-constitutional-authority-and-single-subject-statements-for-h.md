# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6974

**Measures:** H.R. 6974

```
           By Ms. MALOY:
       H.R. 6974.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Administrator of the Western Area Power 
     Administration to reduce rates for firm electric service 
     customers due to shortfalls in generation from certain Bureau 
     of Reclamation hydroelectric facilities, and for other 
     purposes.
```
