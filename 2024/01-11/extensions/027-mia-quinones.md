**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mia Quinones 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassador for Youth Award.

challenges along the way. Students like Mia, who strive to make the

will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a testament 
to Mia's hard work, determination, and perseverance at Arvada High 
School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and promising 
future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Mia Quinones on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
