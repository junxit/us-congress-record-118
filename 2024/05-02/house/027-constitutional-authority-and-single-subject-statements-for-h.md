# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8239

**Measures:** H.R. 8239

           By Mrs. WAGNER:
       H.R. 8239.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution

       This bill improves the consideration by the Securities and
