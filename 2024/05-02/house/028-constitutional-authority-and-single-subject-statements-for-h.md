# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8240

**Measures:** H.R. 8240

           By Mrs. WAGNER:
       H.R. 8240.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S, Constitution

       This bill requires a GAO audit of the information
