# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8231

**Measures:** H.R. 8231

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 8231.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 7
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To award a congressional gold medal to James Earl Jones, an 
     American icon, in recognition of a remarkable life in 
     reshaping perceptions, dismantling racial barriers, and 
     advocating for equal opportunities for people of all 
     backgrounds in film and theatre.
```
