# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6931

**Measures:** H.R. 6931

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 6931.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 9, Clause 7-``No money shall be drawn 
     from the Treasury, but in consequence of Appropriations made 
     by Law;''
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3-``Regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations, and among the several states, and with the 
     Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Reauthorizes funding for the National Dam Safety Program 
     through FY2028 and makes improvements to the National Dam 
     Safety Act.
```
