# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6930

**Measures:** H.R. 6930

```
           By Mr. CROW:
       H.R. 6930.

[[Page H52]]

       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require political committees to file separate reports 
     for contributions of $1,000 or more which are received fewer 
     than 20 days before the date of any election in which the 
     committee makes a contribution to, or an expenditure or 
     electioneering communication on behalf of or in opposition 
     to, a candidate or political party in the election.
```
