# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6932

**Measures:** H.R. 6932

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 6932.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``Regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the National Dam Safety Act to require the inclusion 
     of low-head dams in the National Dam Inventory.
```
