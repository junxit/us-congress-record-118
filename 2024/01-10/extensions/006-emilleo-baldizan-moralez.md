Baldizan-Moralez for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors

  Emilleo has achieved great things, all while overcoming adversity and

will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award is a testament 
to Emilleo's hard work, determination, and perseverance at Arvada K-8 
School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and promising 
future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Emilleo Baldizan-Moralez on achieving 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
