# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Sovereign God, You uphold the universe by Your powerful word. Sustain 
this Nation with Your righteous promises.
  Eternal Lord, the times and seasons are subject to Your control. 
Kings and empires are established and removed at Your direction. Direct 
this Republic and its leaders on paths of mercy and truth.
  In these days of our country's decisions, give wisdom to the wise and 
knowledge to those who yield themselves to Your authority, for You 
alone rule our days. You alone have established our paths. Into the 
care of Your righteous right hand, we offer our future.
  Silence the debates which have drowned out Your divine assurance that 
You alone know what we need. Forgive our paralyzing anxiety, our 
overwhelming uncertainty. Pardon us for failing to heed Your clear 
reassurance that we need only seek Your kingdom and Your righteousness.
  Show us what mercy looks like, that we would share it with one 
another when the fervor of this contest subsides.
  Grant us Your grace in these days and Your peace in the living of 
them.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
