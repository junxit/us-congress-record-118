# PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 1563, H.R. 10100, H.R. 10101

```
                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
       H.R. 10100. A bill to amend the State Department Basic 
     Authorities Act to establish a Deputy Secretary of State for 
     Economic Security, redesignate and relocate other offices of 
     the Department of State, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Foreign Affairs.
           By Mr. STRONG:
       H.R. 10101. A bill to authorize the conveyance of a certain 
     Federal courthouse to the City of Huntsville, Alabama; to the 
     Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
           By Mr. THOMPSON of California (for himself, Ms. Eshoo, 
             Mr. Carbajal, Mr. Thanedar, and Mr. Morelle):
       H. Res. 1563. A resolution expressing the sense of Congress 
     that the United States is committed to ensuring a safe and 
     healthy climate for future generations, and thus to restoring 
     the climate; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce, and in 
     addition to the Committee on Foreign Affairs, for a period to 
     be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
```
