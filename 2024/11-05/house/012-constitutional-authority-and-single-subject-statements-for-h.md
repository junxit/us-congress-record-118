# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10100

**Measures:** H.R. 10100

```
           By Mr. MOOLENAAR:
        H.R. 10100.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the Constitution of the United States of 
     America
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To reform the State Department to focus on economic 
     security issues, create a new Deputy Secretary for Economic 
     Security, and for other purposes.
```
