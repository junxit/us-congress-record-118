# HONORING VICTOR BENCOMO

**Speaking:** Crow, Jason (C001121) [D-CO]

```
                        HONORING VICTOR BENCOMO

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JASON CROW

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 5, 2024

  Mr. CROW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of an 
extraordinary leader from Colorado, Victor Bencomo.
  When Victor passed away on October 1, 2024. we lost a loving father 
and husband, a fierce advocate, and a support system for many. Victor 
is survived by his wife Sara Reins Bencomo and three sons Kyle, Alden, 
and Beck.
  Victor dedicated more than 30 years of his life to public service, 
including a 22-year career in the Navy as a combat medic. He was a 
leading advocate for gun violence prevention policy as President of the 
Colorado Chapter of Giffords Gun Owners for Safety. He testified for 
sensible gun safety laws at both the Colorado State Capitol and the 
U.S. Congress. He was also a consistent advocate for veterans' suicide 
prevention resources, and even in the last few months of his life, 
Victor was advocating for glioblastoma research.
  Victor dedicated his life in service to others and he exuded love and 
kindness. He lit up every room and those of us who were lucky enough to 
see him speak or spend time in his company were better for it. I am 
proud to have known Victor and to have been part of his journey.
  Victor once described his legacy as a ``testament to the enduring 
pursuit of a brighter, fairer, and safer world for all.'' Our community 
and our country are grateful for this enduring pursuit.
  I honor the life and service of Victor Bencomo and I extend my 
gratitude to his family for sharing such a bright light with us.

                          ____________________
```
