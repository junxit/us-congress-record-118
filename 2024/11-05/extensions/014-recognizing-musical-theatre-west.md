# RECOGNIZING MUSICAL THEATRE WEST

**Speaking:** Garcia, Robert (G000598) [D-CA]

```
                    RECOGNIZING MUSICAL THEATRE WEST

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ROBERT GARCIA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 5, 2024

  Mr. ROBERT GARCIA of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor an 
extraordinary institution in my district, Musical Theatre West, a 
premier musical theater company celebrating 72 years in business and 27 
years since they moved to my hometown of Long Beach.
  What began in 1952 as the Whittier Civic Light Opera has blossomed 
into one of Southern California's most respected theatrical production 
companies. From humble beginnings performing in a high school 
auditorium, they have grown to produce a full season of shows at the 
Richard and Karen Carpenter Performing Arts Center, showcasing both 
classic staples and contemporary favorites.
  Their commitment to artistic excellence has not only enriched our 
cultural landscape but has also nurtured countless homegrown talents 
who have gone on to shine on Broadway and beyond.
  Musical Theatre West's commitment to accessibility has introduced 
audiences of all ages and classes to beloved productions, reaching 
demographics who may have not been otherwise exposed to the art of 
musical theater.
  But most importantly their dedication to education reaches over 
17,000 children yearly through outreach programs, sharing the magic of 
musical theater with the next generation.
  Let us celebrate Musical Theatre West for its remarkable journey, its 
lasting impact on our community, and for showcasing the power of the 
performing arts to inspire and uplift.

                          ____________________
```
