# CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF ELIM CHRISTIAN FELLOWSHIP

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
     CELEBRATING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF ELIM CHRISTIAN FELLOWSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 5, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate ELIM Christian 
Fellowship on its 25th anniversary. Established November 7, 1999, under 
the inspirational leadership of Bishop Troy Anthony Bronner, ELIM is 
known for its joyous worship services, a source of support and 
encouragement for many, and more importantly, being a cornerstone in 
the Buffalo community for a quarter of a century.
  Over the last 25 years, ELIM Christian Fellowship has been deeply 
committed to serving the spiritual, social, and cultural needs of the 
Buffalo community. Through their dynamic ministries and dedicated 
outreach, they have witnessed the powerful impact of their mission. 
Each prayer, each act of service, and each moment of fellowship has 
contributed to a legacy that continues to uplift those around us.
  Founded on a vision of love, unity, and service, ELIM Christian 
Fellowship has been a beacon of hope in Buffalo, New York. Over the 
past 25 years, countless lives have been transformed, hearts healed, 
and families strengthened. Their congregation has been a spiritual home 
to many and a source of support and encouragement.
  Today, as we celebrate the next chapter of ELIM Christian Fellowship, 
we pray that they continue to serve with purpose and continue to grow 
and make a difference in our community and beyond. Here's to the next 
25 years of ELIM Christian Fellowship--may their service continue to be 
a blessing as we look forward to many more years of their inspired 
leadership.

                          ____________________
```
