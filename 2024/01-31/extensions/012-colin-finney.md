# COLIN FINNEY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                              COLIN FINNEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, this past December, we gathered to 
celebrate Colin Finney, a bright young man who worked his way up to the 
rank of Eagle Scout. To achieve this rank, Colin undertook an Eagle 
Scout Project involving the restoration of generators and fans at Fort 
Mott State Park in Pennsville, South Jersey. Fort Mott holds a 
significant historical importance to our nation, serving as an Army 
Base during World War II. In 1942, three Diesel Generators were 
installed to provide essential electric services. Colin dedicated his 
time to the meticulous restoration work of these generators that 
involved cleaning, priming, and painting, as well as the restoration of 
the three fans and the gate door, ensuring their preservation for 
future generations. Looking ahead, Colin has ambitious plans. He plans 
to graduate from Salem Vo-Tech's Electrical program and continue his 
journey in the Electrical Field as a Lineman. Beyond scouting and his 
career aspirations, Colin enjoys hunting, spending time with friends, 
and has a commendable interest in firefighting. This remarkable young 
man has a bright future ahead of him and I can't wait to see what he 
accomplishes. God Bless Colin and God Bless the United States of 
America.

                          ____________________
```
