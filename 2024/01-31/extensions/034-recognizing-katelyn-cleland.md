# RECOGNIZING KATELYN CLELAND

**Speaking:** Strickland, Marilyn (S001159) [D-WA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING KATELYN CLELAND

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MARILYN STRICKLAND

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Ms. STRICKLAND. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Katelyn 
Cleland, a South Sound resident who has shown exemplary commitment to 
community service and self-improvement, and congratulate her on being 
awarded the Bronze Medal Congressional Award for such service.
  I commend her desire to better both herself and the community around 
her and know that her contributions to our South Sound community merit 
Congress's highest form of recognition that can be granted to America's 
youth.
  I hope that Katelyn's service has paved the path for more 
achievements in the next chapters of her life, as well as inspired 
others in our community to prioritize service.

                          ____________________
```
