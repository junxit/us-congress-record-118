# RECOGNIZING CHRIS ADAMS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING CHRIS ADAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Chris Adams of 
Arvada, Colorado

[[Page E98]]

for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's Chamber Champion Award.
  Every year, the Arvada Chamber of Commerce honors outstanding 
individuals and organizations that have left a lasting legacy on the 
Arvada area and its residents. Mr. Adams was a treasured member of the 
community, serving on the Chamber's Board of Directors, as well as at 
Thrive Workplace and Revive Church.
  Mr. Adams was constantly asking tough questions and pushing those 
around him to achieve greatness. This drive created an environment of 
continuous improvement, propelling the Arvada Chamber and many local 
businesses toward success. His leadership, grace, and kindness will 
long be remembered in this area and beyond.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Mr. Adams for this award and thank him 
for his contributions to our community.

                          ____________________
```
