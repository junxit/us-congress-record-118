# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Trone, David J. (T000483) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 1435

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DAVID J. TRONE

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Mr. TRONE. Mr. Speaker, on September 14, 2023, I was unable to cast 
my vote. Had I been present, I would have voted ``NAY'' on Roll Call 
No. 391.
  I strongly oppose H.R. 1435, the Preserving Choice in Vehicle 
Purchases Act. This bill modifies the waiver process under the Clean 
Air Act. In doing so it would limit vehicle choices for consumers, 
hinder climate progress, and put public health at risk.
```
