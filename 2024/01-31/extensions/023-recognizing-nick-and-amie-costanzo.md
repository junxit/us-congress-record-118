# RECOGNIZING NICK AND AMIE COSTANZO

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                   RECOGNIZING NICK AND AMIE COSTANZO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Nick and Amie 
Costanzo of Arvada, Colorado for receiving the Arvada Chamber of 
Commerce's 2023 Entrepreneurs of the Year Award.
  Every year, the Entrepreneur of the Year award recognizes an 
individual or team's commitment, investment, and innovation as a 
business owner in the Arvada community.
  The Costanzos have owned and operated the Marco's Pizza franchise in 
Arvada and surrounding communities for over 10 years. Recently, they 
had a vision of opening a space in northwest Arvada that would provide 
a community gathering space. That vision became a reality in 2022 when 
the Costanzos, along with three business partners, opened up Freedom 
Street Social, which has become a place many residents frequent to have 
fun and build community.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Nick and Amie Costanzo for this award 
and thank them for their commitment to our community.
```
