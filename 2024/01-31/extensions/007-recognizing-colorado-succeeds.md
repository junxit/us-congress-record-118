# RECOGNIZING COLORADO SUCCEEDS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING COLORADO SUCCEEDS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Colorado 
Succeeds for receiving the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 2023 Behind the 
Scenes Award.
  Every year, the Chamber of Commerce awards one Behind the Scenes 
honor to an organization or individual who has served as an unsung hero 
in the Arvada community.
  Colorado Succeeds is a coalition of business leaders with a vision to 
radically improve education and training outcomes for learners across 
backgrounds. Through their invaluable partnership with the Arvada 
Chamber of Commerce, they support workforce development projects, 
recruit talent for local businesses and nonprofits, advocate for 
positive change in government, and fight to break down barriers in our 
state's workforce system.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Colorado Succeeds for this award and 
thank them for their commitment to our community.

                          ____________________
```
