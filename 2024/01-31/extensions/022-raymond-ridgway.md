# RAYMOND RIDGWAY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                            RAYMOND RIDGWAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, this past December, it was my pleasure to 
attend Raymond Ridgway's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Throughout his 
scouting experience, Raymond has gained invaluable life skills, 
particularly in survival skills and how to be a great leader. To 
achieve the rank of eagle scout, he chose to freshen up the Education 
Wing at Quinton Baptist Church in Quinton, South Jersey. Recognizing 
the importance of their church in the community, he took on the task of 
painting the walls and installing storage cubbies in the Education 
Wing. His efforts have not only improved the physical environment, but 
he has also created a more welcoming and functional space for education 
and community gatherings. Beyond scouting and his commitment to 
service, Raymond enjoys bowling, music, and working at his job. It was 
a pleasure to attend his Eagle Scout Court of Honor and I wish this 
young man all the best. God Bless Raymond and God Bless the United 
States of America.

                          ____________________
```
