# HONORING THE SERVICE OF ANDY FLICK

**Speaking:** Kuster, Ann M. (K000382) [D-NH]

```
                   HONORING THE SERVICE OF ANDY FLICK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ANN M. KUSTER

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Ms. KUSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and express my 
thanks to Andy Flick, the Executive Director of the New Democrat 
Coalition for the past two years.
  Throughout his 15-year career in the House of Representatives, Andy 
has supported many New Dem Members and the Coalition as a whole in 
their work to break through gridlock and deliver for the American 
people.
  As Chair of the New Democrat Coalition, Andy and I worked hand-in-
hand to find new solutions to the persistent challenges facing our 
country, Congress, and Coalition. From standing up our policy task 
forces and working groups to ensuring each and every New Dem had the 
resources to succeed, he has made an incredible and long-lasting impact 
on our Members.
  Under Andy's guidance, he has grown the Coalition's staff, tackled 
issues from inflation to infrastructure, and has ultimately helped turn 
the organization into one of the most powerful forces in Congress. His 
work to see old problems through a new lens has been at the center of 
our policy program and we will carry on his vision in the years to 
come. He truly embodies the spirit of the `Can Do Caucus.'
  As a result of his impressive work ethic, extensive knowledge of 
Capitol Hill dynamics, and policy prowess, he caught the eye of the 
Vice President's office, where he now serves as the Director of 
Legislative Affairs.
  As we look toward the future, we know Andy's influence will leave a 
mark on the NDC and he will always be known for his exemplary service 
to the House, multiple Member offices, and the Coalition.
  New Dems will be forever grateful to Andy for his astute insights, 
friendly demeanor, and political savvy, and there is no doubt he will 
bring this same positive attitude to his new role.
  I wish Andy all the best in his future endeavors and look forward to 
continuing our shared work to move our country forward and ensure 
everyone has the chance to thrive.

                          ____________________
```
