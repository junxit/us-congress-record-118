# CLAYTON SKROBANEK

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                           CLAYTON SKROBANEK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 31, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, this past November, I had the pleasure of 
attending Clayton Skrobanek's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Clayton has 
been involved in the Eagle Scouts for over a decade, starting in first 
grade as a Tiger Scout. Over the years, he has been very involved in 
his troop by holding four different leadership positions, contributing 
over 100 hours to community service, and participating in countless 
outdoor adventures all while earning 37 merit badges. Outside of the 
scouts, he dedicates his time to helping those in need by volunteering 
at the ``Your Place at the Table'' Food Pantry. This upcoming year, he 
is expected to graduate from Clearview Regional High School and hopes 
to pursue a career in nursing. I am honored to congratulate this young 
man on his remarkable achievements, and I wish him the best. God Bless 
Clayton and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
