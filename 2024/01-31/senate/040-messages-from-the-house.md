# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 3427, H.R. 5585

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered by 
Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 5585. An act to impose criminal and immigration 
     penalties for intentionally fleeing a pursuing Federal 
     officer while operating a motor vehicle.


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 2:16 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 3427. An act to extend the authority to provide 
     employees of the United States Secret Service with overtime 
     pay beyond other statutory limitations, and for other 
     purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mrs. Murray).

                          ____________________
```
