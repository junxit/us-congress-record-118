# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7174

**Measures:** H.R. 7174

```
 By Mr. MURPHY: 
H.R. 7174. Congress has the power to enact this legislation 
pursuant to the following: Article I, Section 8 The single subject 
of this legislation is: To amend title XI of the Social Security 
Act to equalize the negotiation period between small-molecule and 
biologic candidates under the Drug Price Negotiation Program.
```
