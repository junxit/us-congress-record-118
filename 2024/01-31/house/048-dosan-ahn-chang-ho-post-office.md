# DOSAN AHN CHANG HO POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 599

```
                     DOSAN AHN CHANG HO POST OFFICE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the question on suspending the rules and passing 
the bill (H.R. 599) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 3500 West 6th Street, Suite 103 in Los 
Angeles, California, as the ``Dosan Ahn Chang Ho Post Office,'' as 
amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by

[[Page H359]]

the gentleman from Florida (Mr. Donalds) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
