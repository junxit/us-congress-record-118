# DR. MARGARET B. HILL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1060

```
               DR. MARGARET B. HILL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the question on suspending the rules and passing 
the bill (H.R. 1060) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1663 East Date Place in San Bernardino, 
California, as the ``Dr. Margaret B. Hill Post Office Building.''
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Florida (Mr. Donalds) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On January 31, 2024, page H359, in the first column, the 
following appeared: Margaret B. Hill Post Office Building,'' as 
amended. The Clerk read the title of the bill. The SPEAKER pro 
tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the gentleman 
from Florida (Mr. Donalds) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, as amended.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: Margaret B. Hill 
Post Office Building.'' The Clerk read the title of the bill. The 
SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Florida (Mr. Donalds) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
