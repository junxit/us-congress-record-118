# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 9468

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          enrolled bill signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on September 19, 2024, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives announcing that the Speaker has signed the following 
enrolled bill:

       H.R. 9468. An act making supplemental appropriations for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, and for other 
     purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the enrolled bill was subsequently signed on September 19, 2024, during 
the adjournment of the Senate, by the President pro tempore (Mrs. 
Murray).

                          ____________________
```
