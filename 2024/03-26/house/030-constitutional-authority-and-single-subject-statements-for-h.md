# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7827

**Measures:** H.R. 7827

```
            By Mr. SCHWEIKERT:
        H.R. 7827.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to 
     encourage the development of vaccines to prevent, treat, or 
     mitigate opioid, cocaine, methamphetamine, or alcohol use 
     disorder, to establish an x-prize for the development of such 
     a vaccine, and for other purposes.
```
