# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7820

**Measures:** H.R. 7820

```
            By Ms. CROCKETT:
        H.R. 7820.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Public Health Service Act to direct the 
     Secretary of Health and Human Services to conduct a public 
     health education, awareness, and outreach campaign to enhance 
     access to abortion and related health services.
```
