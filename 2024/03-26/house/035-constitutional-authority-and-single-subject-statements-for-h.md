# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 121

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 121

```
            By Mr. OGLES:
        H.J. Res. 121.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to ``Credit 
     Card Penalty Fees (Regulation Z)''.
```
