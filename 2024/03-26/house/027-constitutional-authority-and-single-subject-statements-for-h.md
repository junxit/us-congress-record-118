# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7824

**Measures:** H.R. 7824

```
            By Mr. OWENS:
        H.R. 7824.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I or the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Office for Victims of Crime of the Department 
     of Justice to implement anti-trafficking recommendations of 
     the Government Accountability Office.
```
