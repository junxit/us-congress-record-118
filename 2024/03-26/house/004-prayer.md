# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, we are entering upon a most sacred time of the 
year. May we contemplate Your word as it speaks to us and follow Your 
holy will in all that we do. As the season of spring witnesses to Your 
resurrected life, may we too rise with You to eternal life.
  We pray all this in Thy holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
