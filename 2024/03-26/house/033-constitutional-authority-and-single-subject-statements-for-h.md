# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7830

**Measures:** H.R. 7830

```
            By Mr. VAN DREW:
        H.R. 7830.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to expand the 
     surviving spouse filing status to Individuals whose spouses 
     have died within the last five taxable years.
```
