# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8725

**Measures:** H.R. 8725

```
           By Ms. CARAVEO:
       H.R. 8725.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Constitutional Authority--Necessary and Proper Clause (Art. 
     I, Sec. 8, Clause 18)
       THE U.S. CONSTITUTION
       ARTICLE I, SECTION 8 POWERS OF CONGRESS
       CLAUSE 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Health and Human Services to 
     submit to Congress a report on the option to elect to pay 
     cost-sharing under a prescription drug plan or MA-PD plan in 
     monthly capped amounts.
```
