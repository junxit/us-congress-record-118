# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8737

**Measures:** H.R. 8737

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 8737.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Infrastructure and Investment and Jobs Act to 
     repeal the authority of the Secretary of Energy and the 
     Secretary of Transportation to maintain an electric vehicle 
     working group, and for other purposes.
```
