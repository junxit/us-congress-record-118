# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8745

**Measures:** H.R. 8745

```
           By Mr. SMUCKER:
       H.R. 8745.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend part B of title IV of the Social Security Act to 
     improve the monthly caseworker visits program so states can 
     invest in the child welfare workforce ensuring that children 
     in care have access to quality and timely care.
```
