# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8100

**Measures:** H.R. 8100

```
           By Mr. WALBERG:
       H.R. 8100.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill directs the U.S. Postal Service to issue a 
     semipostal stamp to contribute to funding operations 
     supported by the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative.
```
