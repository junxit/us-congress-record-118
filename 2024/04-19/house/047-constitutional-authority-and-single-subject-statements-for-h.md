# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8093

**Measures:** H.R. 8093

```
           By Ms. JACKSON LEE:
       H.R. 8093.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article 1, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the 
     United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill will establish a State Judicial Threat 
     Intelligence and Resource Center to provide technical 
     assistance, training, and monitoring of threats for state and 
     local judges and court personnel .
```
