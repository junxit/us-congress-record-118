# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 130

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 130

```
           By Mr. BURLISON:
       H.J. Res. 130.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This is a Congressional Review Act resolution that 
     disapproves of the rule submitted by the Federal Railroad 
     Administration relating to ``Train Crew Size Safety 
     Requirements'' and states such rule shall have no force or 
     effect.
```
