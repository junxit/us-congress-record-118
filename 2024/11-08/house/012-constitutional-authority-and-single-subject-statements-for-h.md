# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10102

**Measures:** H.R. 10102

```
           By Mr. CROW:
       H.R. 10102.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To sunset the Office of the Special Inspector General for 
     Afghanistan Reconstruction, to provide that employees of the 
     Office are eligible for the Interagency Career Transition 
     Assistance Program, and for other purposes.
```
