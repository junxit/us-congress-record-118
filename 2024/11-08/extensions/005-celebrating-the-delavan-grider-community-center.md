# CELEBRATING THE DELAVAN GRIDER COMMUNITY CENTER

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
            CELEBRATING THE DELAVAN GRIDER COMMUNITY CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 8, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the remarkable 
history and accomplishments of the Delavan Grider Community Center. 
Established in 2001, under the bold vision of passionate block club 
leaders, the Delavan Grider Community Center has evolved into a space 
where neighbors of all ages come together to share experiences and 
build lasting bonds that strengthen our community.
  The Delavan Grider Community Center is a resource in our community--
offering diverse programming for all ages, including youth and seniors, 
Girl Scouts, after-school programs, and summer activities. The 
programming offered by the Delavan Grider Community Center has 
empowered countless individuals, providing a safe environment where 
they can discover new skills, forge lifelong friendships, and grow in 
ways they never imagined.
  The Delavan Grider Community Center is a beautiful reminder of what 
we can achieve when we come together with a shared purpose, as well as 
a reminder that there is always something and somewhere for everyone. 
This Center is not just a thriving hub in the heart of our community, 
but also a vital part of the fabric that makes the East Side of Buffalo 
strong, connected, and resilient.
  Today, as we look back at all that the Delavan Grider Community 
Center has achieved, it is important to reflect on the many ways this 
center has enriched the lives of so many through its past 
accomplishments and the exciting future that lies ahead. Please join me 
in celebrating the Delavan Grider Community Center--may it continue to 
grow, inspire, and strengthen the power of community.

                          ____________________
```
