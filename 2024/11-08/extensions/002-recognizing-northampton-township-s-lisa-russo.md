# RECOGNIZING NORTHAMPTON TOWNSHIP'S LISA RUSSO

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
             RECOGNIZING NORTHAMPTON TOWNSHIP'S LISA RUSSO

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 8, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
exceptional constituent from my district, Lisa Russo. Since she was a 
child, she was driven to serve her country in the military. And she did 
just that and joined the United States Navy from 1986 through 1992 as a 
Communications and Electronics Technician.
  Lisa continued her civic service as the Administration Executive 
Assistant to the Northampton Township Manager since 2007. Lisa played 
an integral role in implementing and maintaining the Patriots Flag 
Program. She ensured the respectful transfer of 92 flags, representing 
92 residents honored under the Patriots Flag Program during the Global 
War on Terrorism from 2003 to 2021, to the display's new home in 
Northampton Township Library.
  In addition, her substantial contributions were instrumental in 
creating awareness of the issues faced by Veterans, Active-Duty Service 
Members and their families, providing support to countless lives in our 
community. I am beyond proud of and exceptionally grateful for Lisa's 
service to our great nation and community. Her dedication to this 
country as a Navy Veteran demonstrates the highest commitment to the 
values that make America the beacon of liberty to the world. I am truly 
impressed by her determination to achieve so much and employ all her 
talents in service of others.

                          ____________________
```
