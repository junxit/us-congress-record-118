# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10121

**Measures:** H.R. 10121

```
           By Mr. FROST:
       H.R. 10121.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 and 18 of the U.S. 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish youth advisory councils for the purpose of 
     providing recommendations to the Environmental Protection 
     Agency, Department of the Interior, Department of Energy, 
     Department of Agriculture, and Department of Commerce with 
     respect to environmental issues as those issues relate to 
     youth communities, and for other purposes.
```
