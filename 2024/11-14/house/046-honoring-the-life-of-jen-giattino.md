# HONORING THE LIFE OF JEN GIATTINO

**Speaking:** Menendez, Robert (M001226) [D-NJ]

```
                   HONORING THE LIFE OF JEN GIATTINO

  (Mr. MENENDEZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. MENENDEZ. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of a 
dedicated public servant and friend, Hoboken Council President Jen 
Giattino, who passed away last week.
  Jen was more than an elected official. She was someone who cared 
deeply for Hoboken and was a pillar for her community.
  She was a champion for the unhoused, a compassionate advocate for 
small business owners, and a warmhearted, generous presence throughout 
the city that she served and loved.
  Her legacy will live on in Hoboken and the hearts of all those who 
had the privilege of knowing her.

[[Page H6003]]

  In this difficult time, my thoughts are with her husband, Joe; her 
sons, Joe, Jack, and Alex; and her family, friends, and the people of 
Hoboken who adored her.
  In her memory, let us live by the example that she set: compassion, 
generosity, and care for others.

                          ____________________
```
