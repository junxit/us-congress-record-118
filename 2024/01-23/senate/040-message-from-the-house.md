```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered by 
Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that pursuant to 22 
U.S.C. 2761, and the order of the House of January 9, 2023, the Speaker 
appoints the following Members on the part of the House of 
Representatives to the British-American Interparliamentary Group: Mr. 
Crow of Colorado, Mr. Meeks of New York, Ms. DelBene of Washington, Mr. 
Gomez of California, and Ms. Matsui of California.
```
