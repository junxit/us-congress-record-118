  Committee on Rules and Administration: to hold hearings to examine 
the use of Artificial Intelligence at the Library of Congress, 
Government Publishing Office, and Smithsonian Institution, 2:15 
p.m., SR-301.
  Committee on Small Business and Entrepreneurship: to hold hearings 
to examine succession planning, focusing on opportunities to build 
wealth and keep jobs in local communities, 2:15 p.m., SR-428A.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: to receive a closed briefing on 
certain intelligence matters, 2:30 p.m., SH-219.


                                 House
