# SEPTEMBER 2023 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
                SEPTEMBER 2023 CONSTITUENT OF THE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 11, 2024

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize community 
leader and Solana Beach resident Lisa Montes as my September 2023 
Constituent of the Month.
  Lisa is a descendent of the original Mexican families of La Colonia 
de Eden Gardens, and she has a passion for giving back to her community 
that was instilled in her by her ancestors. I know she is making them 
proud today. Lisa devotes her time and effort to numerous

[[Page E885]]

local organizations dedicated to serving and empowering Latino and 
Latina students and the community at-large.
  During Hispanic Heritage Month, I am thrilled to recognize the 
efforts of leaders, like Lisa, whose service to others strengthens our 
unique and diverse community. I'm so grateful for residents of 
California's 49th Congressional District who dedicate their lives to 
the betterment of our community, and I'm proud to honor Lisa Montes as 
my Constituent of the Month.

                          ____________________
```
