**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                  TRIBUTE TO ANTHONY ADRIAN CHERVENKOV

Chervenkov, a summer 2024 intern with my Orlando office, for the good 
work he did for my office and the people of Florida.
```
