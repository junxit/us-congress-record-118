# TRIBUTE TO TOMAS IGNACIO USECHE-CANCRO

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                 TRIBUTE TO TOMAS IGNACIO USECHE-CANCRO

● Mr. RUBIO. Madam President, I recognize Tomas Ignacio Useche-
Cancro, a summer 2024 intern with my Orlando office, for the good work 
he did for my office and the people of Florida.
  Tomas is currently a student at St. Olaf College, pursuing a major in 
political science with a minor degree in international relations. He is 
a dedicated and diligent worker who was devoted to getting the most out 
of his internship experience.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Tomas, and I look forward to hearing 
of his good work in the years to come.●
```
