# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9538

**Measures:** H.R. 9538

```
           By Ms. DeLAURO:
       H.R. 9538.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the United States Constitution and its 
     subsequent amendments, and further clarified and interpreted 
     by the Supreme Court of the United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would amend the Agricultural Marketing Act of 
     1946 to ensure that USDA uses its immense purchasing power to 
     support local and regional food systems and to create market 
     opportunities for small and mid-sized farms.
```
