# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9539

**Measures:** H.R. 9539

```
           By Mr. GARBARINO:
       H.R. 9539.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would allow FEMA to reimburse the State and City 
     pension systems for the loss in pension payments due to the 
     unanticipated deaths of those who responded the terrorist 
     attacks and who participated in the clean-up in the weeks 
     following the attacks.
```
