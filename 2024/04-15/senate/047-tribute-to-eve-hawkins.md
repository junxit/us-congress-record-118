# TRIBUTE TO EVE HAWKINS

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO EVE HAWKINS

  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the opportunity 
to express my appreciation to Eve for her hard work as an intern in the 
Senate Republican Conference. I recognize her efforts and contributions 
to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Eve is a native of Virginia. She is currently a junior at the Madeira 
School in McLean, VA. She has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which 
has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of her work 
is reflected in her great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Eve for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.

                          ____________________
```
