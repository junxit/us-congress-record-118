# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  God of our destinies, guide our Senators this day by Your higher 
wisdom. Watch over them and use their work for Your glory. Lord, 
replace fear with faith, pessimism with hope, and error with truth. May 
these lawmakers become Your instruments for enabling justice to thrive. 
Give them the serenity to accept what they can't change, the courage to 
change what they can, and the wisdom to know the difference.
  Lord, we also ask You to comfort the many who mourn the loss of loved 
ones because of the horrors of war.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
