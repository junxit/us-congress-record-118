# TRIBUTE TO JASMINE BROTHERS

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO JASMINE BROTHERS

  Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the opportunity 
to express my appreciation to Jasmine for her hard work as an intern in 
the Senate Republican Conference. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Jasmine is a native of Virginia. She is currently a senior at the 
Madeira School in McLean, VA. She has demonstrated a strong work ethic, 
which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of 
her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last several 
months.
  I want to thank Jasmine for the dedication she has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have her as part of 
our team. I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her journey.

                          ____________________
```
