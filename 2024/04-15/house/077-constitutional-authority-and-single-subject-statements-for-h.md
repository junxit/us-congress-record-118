# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8003

**Measures:** H.R. 8003

```
            By Mrs. HINSON:
        H.R. 8003.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Agriculture to conduct an 
     annual assessment of dependencies on the People's Republic of 
     China in the supply chains of critical agricultural inputs.
```
