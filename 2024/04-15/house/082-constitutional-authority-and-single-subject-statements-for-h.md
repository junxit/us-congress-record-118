# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8008

**Measures:** H.R. 8008

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 8008.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would award a posthumous Congressional Gold Medal 
     to the 13 African Americans who became the first African 
     American commissioned and warrant officers in the United 
     States Navy.
```
