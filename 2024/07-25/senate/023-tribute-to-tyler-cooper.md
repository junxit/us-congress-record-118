# TRIBUTE TO TYLER COOPER

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO TYLER COOPER

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Tyler for his hard work as an intern in my 
office in Casper, WY. I recognize his efforts and contributions to my 
office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Tyler is a native of Casper. He attends Casper College, where he 
studies political science. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, 
which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of 
his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last several 
months.
  I want to thank Tyler for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
