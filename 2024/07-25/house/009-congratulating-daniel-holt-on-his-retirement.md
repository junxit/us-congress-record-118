# CONGRATULATING DANIEL HOLT ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA]

```
              CONGRATULATING DANIEL HOLT ON HIS RETIREMENT

  (Mr. McGOVERN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank an incredible member 
of my team, my director of operations, Daniel Holt, who is retiring 
later this summer after 25 years of public service.
  Daniel's title doesn't even begin to reflect all that he does. Daniel 
is a rock star. He always knows who to call, what to do, and how to get 
it done. He makes sure that everyone in our district has an open door 
to my office. He has served this institution, this country, and the 
people of Massachusetts with incredible integrity and dedication, and, 
in the process, he has made the world a better place.
  On a personal level, Daniel is like family to me and my wife, Lisa, 
and my kids, Molly and Patrick. I also want to recognize his family: 
Denise, Eleanor, Isaac, and Ava.
  I know Daniel will continue to do great things in the future. On 
behalf of team McGovern, we are full of gratitude to Daniel, and we 
will forever be grateful to call him a mentor, colleague, and friend.

                          ____________________
```
