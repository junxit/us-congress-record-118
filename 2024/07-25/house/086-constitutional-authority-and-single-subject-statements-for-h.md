# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9160

**Measures:** H.R. 9160

```
           By Mr. LAWLER:
       H.R. 9160.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To impose sanctions with respect to the system of 
     compensation of the Palestine Liberation Organization and the 
     Palestinian Authority that supports acts of terrorism.
```
