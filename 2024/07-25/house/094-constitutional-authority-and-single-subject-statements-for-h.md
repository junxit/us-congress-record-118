# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9168

**Measures:** H.R. 9168

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 9168.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Interior to annually submit 
     to Congress, and make publicly available on a website, a 
     report on decommissioning offshore oil and gas wells, 
     platforms, and pipelines.
```
