# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9174

**Measures:** H.R. 9174

```
           By Mr. STAUBER:
       H.R. 9174.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Designates the facility of the United States Postal Service 
     located at 31143 State Highway 65 in Pengilly, Minnesota, as
```
