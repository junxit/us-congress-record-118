# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9138

**Measures:** H.R. 9138

```
           By Mr. BALDERSON:
       H.R. 9138.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Expands psychological mental and behavioral health services 
     to Medicare, Medicaid, and CHIP beneficiaries by permitting 
     reimbursement of psychological services provided by certain 
     supervised psychology trainees, and facilitating the 
     reimbursement of those services.
```
