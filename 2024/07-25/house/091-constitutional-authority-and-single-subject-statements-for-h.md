# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9165

**Measures:** H.R. 9165

```
           By Ms. MALOY:
       H.R. 9165.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of the Interior to establish a 
     grant program to allocate resources for remote search and 
     rescue activities conducted on Federal land under the 
     jurisdiction of the Secretary of the Interior or the 
     Secretary of Agriculture, and for other purposes.
```
