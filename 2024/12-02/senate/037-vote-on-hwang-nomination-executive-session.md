# Vote on Hwang Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Kelly, Mark (K000377) [D-AZ]

```
                        Vote on Hwang Nomination

  Under the previous order, the question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Hwang nomination?
  Mr. MURPHY. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. 
Fetterman), the Senator from Maine (Mr. King), the Senator from

Hoeven), the Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch), the Senator from Florida 
(Mr. Rubio), and the Senator from Ohio (Mr. Vance).
  Further, if present and voting: the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. 
Hoeven) would have voted ``nay''.
```
