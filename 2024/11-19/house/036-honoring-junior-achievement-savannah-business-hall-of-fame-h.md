# HONORING JUNIOR ACHIEVEMENT SAVANNAH BUSINESS HALL OF FAME HONOREES JEFF KOLE AND DIANA MORRISON

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
  HONORING JUNIOR ACHIEVEMENT SAVANNAH BUSINESS HALL OF FAME HONOREES 
                      JEFF KOLE AND DIANA MORRISON

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jeff 
Kole and Diana Morrison for their achievements as the 2024 honorees of 
the Junior Achievement Savannah Business Hall of Fame.
  Jeff Kole is the president of Kole Investment Co. and is actively 
involved in improving the Savannah community through numerous boards 
and charity organizations. In addition, he is the founding facilitator 
and board chair of East Savannah United, a nonprofit that will open a 
new library and children's center next year.
  Diana Morrison is the founder and CEO of Ad Specialty. She built her 
successful business up from just the trunk of her car.
  Diana contributes her time to many organizations in the Savannah area 
and has been honored by the Savannah

[[Page H6098]]

Area Chamber of Commerce twice for her accomplishments.
  I am proud to have leaders like Jeff and Diana in our district. I 
wish them both the very best.

                          ____________________
```
