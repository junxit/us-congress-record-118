# DR. WILLIAM I. `BILL' KOTT POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 7192

```
            DR. WILLIAM I. `BILL' KOTT POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 7192) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 333 West Broadway in Anaheim, California, as 
the ``Dr. William I. `Bill' Kott Post Office Building'' was ordered to 
a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
