# SERGEANT KENNETH E. MURPHY POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6651

```
            SERGEANT KENNETH E. MURPHY POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6651) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 603 West 3rd Street in Necedah, Wisconsin, as 
the ``Sergeant Kenneth E. Murphy Post Office Building'' was ordered to 
a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
