# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 872

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the previous 
order.
  There being no objection, under the previous order and pursuant to S. 
Res. 872, as a further mark of respect to the late Daniel J. Evans, 
former Senator from Washington, the Senate, at 7:40 p.m., adjourned 
until Wednesday, November 20, 2024, at 10 a.m.
```
