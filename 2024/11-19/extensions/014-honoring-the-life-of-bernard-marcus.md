# HONORING THE LIFE OF BERNARD MARCUS

**Speaking:** Scalise, Steve (S001176) [R-LA]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF BERNARD MARCUS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE SCALISE

                              of louisiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 19, 2024

  Mr. SCALISE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of 
Bernard `Bernie' Marcus and his many contributions to our great nation.
  Bernie was not only the co-founder of The Home Depot; he was also a 
self-made man, born to an immigrant family, who on many occasions 
turned adversity into opportunity.
  He earned his degree from Rutgers University where he enrolled in 
pharmacy school. He went on to work in both the manufacturing and 
retail industries before becoming Chairman of the Board and President 
of Handy Dan Improvement Centers in 1972.
  At age 49, Bernie, alongside his friend Arthur Blank, sought to bring 
to life a ``vision of a one-stop shop for do-it-yourselfers''--The Home 
Depot. The pair opened the first two Home Depot stores in Atlanta, 
Georgia in 1979. Today, there are over 2,300 stores worldwide.
  Despite all of his success. Bernie remained humble and always sought 
to live up to his mother's approach of ``the more you give, the more 
you get.''
  His generosity to numerous charities undoubtedly helped so many 
throughout his lifetime. He supported causes like helping Jewish 
communities, our military veterans, and children with autism, just to 
name a few. He also started the Homer Fund to help The Home Depot 
associates who fell on hard times.
  He built The Home Depot with the core values of giving back, 
excellent customer service, entrepreneurial spirit, respect for all, 
and doing the right thing according to their website. These values hold 
extra meaning for me as well.
  When I was working my way through college at LSU to become the first 
in my family to earn a degree, I worked as a Home Depot associate and 
experienced firsthand the embodiment of these values. They still hold 
true for me today as they did back then.
  Bernie truly believed in giving back, and his legacy of generosity 
and philanthropy will outlive him in the lives of people he has 
touched.
  Bernie helped create a ``nation of doers'' and our world is certainly 
a better place because of him.
```
