# RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF CORAL SPRINGS, FLORIDA CITY MANAGER, FRANK BABINEC

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF CORAL SPRINGS, FLORIDA CITY MANAGER, 
                             FRANK BABINEC

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 19, 2024

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend the 
30 years of dedicated public service of Mr. Frank Babinec, the City 
Manager for Coral Springs, Florida. Over the years, Frank has displayed 
an unwavering commitment to the community's safety, well-being, and 
prosperity.
  Frank's journey in public service began as a volunteer in 1993 when 
he joined what is now known as the Coral Springs-Parkland Fire 
Department, putting his life on the line to protect others. His hard 
work and dedication led him to be appointed to Fire Chief in 2014. His 
commitment to public safety went beyond firefighting. He championed 
emergency medical services and advanced training programs and 
strengthened partnerships with other public safety organizations, 
raising the department's standards and response capabilities.
  In his role as Fire Chief, Frank supported the community through a 
tragic shooting that took place at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School 
in 2018. His leadership in the aftermath demonstrated his ability to 
provide calm and competent direction in times of crisis, encouraging a 
sense of resilience and unity within the community.
  In 2019, Frank was appointed City Manager of Coral Springs. He has 
guided the city through unprecedented challenges, including the COVID-
19 pandemic, and focused his commitment to the city's health and 
economic stability. Over the years, Frank has worked tirelessly to 
promote sustainable growth, improve city services, and address the 
needs of residents while fostering transparency and accountability in 
local government. During his distinguished career, he has earned 
numerous accolades and awards through his service over the last 30 
years.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Mr. Frank 
Babinec's invaluable contributions to the City of Coral Springs and his 
exemplary public service career. Frank's legacy in Coral Springs is one 
of compassion, strength, and deep commitment to serving others. I wish 
him all the best in his retirement.

                          ____________________
```
