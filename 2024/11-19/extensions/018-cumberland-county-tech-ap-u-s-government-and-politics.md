# CUMBERLAND COUNTY TECH AP U.S. GOVERNMENT AND POLITICS

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
         CUMBERLAND COUNTY TECH AP U.S. GOVERNMENT AND POLITICS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 19, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, I am proud to address the next generation 
of American leaders from the AP United States Government and Politics 
class at Cumberland County Tech. Today, I stand before them as their 
Representative in the United States Congress, and I want to thank them 
for their decision to take a college-level course that deepens their 
understanding of American government and politics. Learning civics is 
an important step toward becoming informed citizens and future leaders 
of our nation. It is essential for people to understand how laws are 
made, to participate in the political process, and to know their rights 
in order to make a positive impact on their community and country. The 
House of Representatives, where I serve, is one of the two chambers of 
Congress that form the federal government's legislative branch. This 
branch plays a crucial role in creating laws, declaring war, and 
overseeing the Executive Branch. By understanding how this process 
works, they enhance their ability to become our country's next leaders 
and to make meaningful contributions as outstanding Americans. I thank 
them for taking this important first step toward making a significant 
difference. God bless Cumberland County Tech, and God bless our United 
States of America.

                          ____________________
```
