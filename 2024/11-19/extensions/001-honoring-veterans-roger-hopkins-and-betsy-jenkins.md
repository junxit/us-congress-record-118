# HONORING VETERANS ROGER HOPKINS AND BETSY JENKINS

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
           HONORING VETERANS ROGER HOPKINS AND BETSY JENKINS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 19, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, today, I want to celebrate the story of 
two American heroes from the Air Force and Navy who not only served the 
country in Afghanistan but also started a beautiful relationship. Roger 
Hopkins and Betsy Jenkins, two individuals who had never crossed paths, 
found their destinies intertwined in a remarkable twist of fate when 
they heard each other's voices on the radio in the heart of 
Afghanistan. They were both on deployment, with Betsy working for the 
Combined Joint Task Force, while Roger was embedded with the primary 
assault team for the Kunar-Nuristan when the gunfire started. Roger was 
on a no-fail mission, a critical operation considered highly dangerous 
due to the hostile environment in Afghanistan. This mission was of 
utmost importance, as its success was crucial for the safety and 
security of the region. After a successful completion, Betsy was the 
much needed escort out of the city, which was a daunting task given the 
ongoing conflict, marked by frequent enemy attacks and the constant 
threat of ambush. During this intense situation, Betsy's calm and 
composed voice, a rare sound for Roger and the Airmen, became their 
beacon of hope. Using the aerial views from the standard operating 
picture, she skillfully and bravely guided the convoy out of the city, 
demonstrating her exceptional leadership and tactical skills that 
earned the entire team's respect. Betsy's female voice of calm was not 
something that Roger and the Airmen were used to, and Roger became 
determined to figure out who this voice was. In a display of true 
warrior spirit, Roger, determined to find the hero who led them out, 
used every means at his disposal, including an Afghan translator, to 
track down Master Chief Betsy Jenkins of the United States Navy, a 
testament to his unwavering commitment and deep admiration for her. 
Roger used his charm to ask Betsy what she missed most other than her 
family, which she responded with, ``mangos and avocados.'' In almost 
unheard-of fashion, Roger delivered her mangos and avocados in less 
than a week. This was the beginning of their relationship and as Betsy 
would say, showed her ``without a doubt, Roger saw no obstacles, only 
opportunities.'' Roger and Betsy have dedicated much of their lives to 
serving their country, often at great personal sacrifice, and have 
enjoyed outstanding military careers. Roger retired as Master Sergeant, 
having served as the Senior Enlisted Advisor to the Special Operations 
Command of the Pacific's Medical Directorate, providing unconventional 
Special Forces mission support. His career was marked by numerous 
commendations and successful missions, a testament to his dedication 
and bravery. He also served at the Pentagon and, after September 11th, 
re-trained as a part of special operations command as a Para-rescue man 
and subsequently did a total of nine combat deployments across three 
countries in 23 years of service. Betsy has been in the United States 
Navy for 23 years, with her first 12 serving U.S. Naval forces in 
Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Greece, New Caledonia, and other 
countries before deploying to Afghanistan in 2014. She is currently 
supporting NAVIFORES as the Information Systems Technician Rating 
Advisor. Her awards include the Defense Meritorious Service Medal, 
three Navy and Marine Corps Commendation Medals, and three Navy and 
Marine Corps Achievement Medals, as well as many others. She and Roger 
have seven beautiful children they are raising in our great Nation, and 
I am sure they will learn from their parents' excellent leadership and 
service. God bless Roger and Betsy, and God bless our United States of 
America.
```
