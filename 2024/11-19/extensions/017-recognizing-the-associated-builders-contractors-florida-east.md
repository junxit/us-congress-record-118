# RECOGNIZING THE ASSOCIATED BUILDERS & CONTRACTORS FLORIDA EAST COAST AND THE ASSOCIATED BUILDERS & CONTRACTORS INSTITUTE (ABCI) DURING NATIONAL APPRENTICESHIP WEEK

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE ASSOCIATED BUILDERS & CONTRACTORS FLORIDA EAST COAST 
   AND THE ASSOCIATED BUILDERS & CONTRACTORS INSTITUTE (ABCI) DURING 
                      NATIONAL APPRENTICESHIP WEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 19, 2024

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Associated 
Builders & Contractors Florida East Coast and the Associated Builders & 
Contractors Institute (ABCI), which are both based in my District and 
provide not only my constituents but all of South Florida, the Treasure 
Coast, and some of the Space Coast with construction workforce. This 
week is National Apprenticeship Week, and recently, I had the 
opportunity to visit ABC Florida East Coast and ABCI to see first-hand 
the apprenticeship education program provided to my constituents in 
seven high-demand fields in construction.
  ABCI is Florida's single largest apprenticeship provider out of 
hundreds of apprenticeship programs. More than 800 students are 
enrolled at ABCI, benefiting from state laws that keep education 
tuition-free and guarantee progressive wages. ABCI provides robust job 
placement services, helping students secure employment before 
completion and during their education journeys.
  ABCI programs are accredited by the National Center for Construction 
Education and Research, ensuring apprentices graduate with credentials 
recognized nationwide. Today in South Florida, there is much growth in 
population and commercial construction; the industry-driven curriculum 
is critical and is designed in collaboration with top industry leaders, 
so therefore, ABCI's programs meet the ever-evolving needs of the 
construction sector, producing highly skilled, growth-ready graduates.
  As we commemorate National Apprenticeship Week, I'd like to once 
again recognize the Associated Builders & Contractors and ABCI for the 
positive impact apprentices have on the South Florida and American 
economies. ABCI's role in preparing the next generation of skilled 
workers is a testament to that success.
```
