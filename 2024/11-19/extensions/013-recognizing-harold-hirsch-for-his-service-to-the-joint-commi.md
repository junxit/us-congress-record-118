# RECOGNIZING HAROLD HIRSCH FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON TAXATION OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Smith, Jason (S001195) [R-MO]

```
  RECOGNIZING HAROLD HIRSCH FOR HIS SERVICE TO THE JOINT COMMITTEE ON 
                     TAXATION OF THE UNITED STATES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JASON SMITH

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 19, 2024

  Mr. SMITH of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, with Ranking Member 
Neal, to commend Harold Hirsch on his service to the United States 
Congress for over 47 years. Mr. Hirsch served as a lawyer and tax 
expert on the staff of the nonpartisan Joint Committee on Taxation of 
the United States Congress from the beginning of 1977 to October, 2024, 
when he retired. Prior to 1977, Mr. Hirsch had worked as a lawyer for 
the office of the Judge Advocate General and for the Legislation and 
Regulations section at the Internal Revenue Service, government service 
for which we also thank him.
  The staff of the Joint Committee on Taxation performs useful or at 
times indispensable functions to Members of Congress as the Legislative 
Branch develops tax policy. Mr. Hirsch's legal acumen, drafting 
brilliance, and deep understanding of tax issues contributed to the 
quality of tax legislation in numerous tax bills over the period he 
served on the JCT staff. Though reserved, even self-effacing, Mr. 
Hirsch brought thoughtful commentary to the development of proposed tax 
legislation. He gave an extremely careful reading of proposed 
legislative text, and his keen eye for possible statutory errors 
averted problems that would otherwise have plagued taxpayers, 
practitioners, and government administrators alike. Mr. Hirsch's 
technical skills and expertise in the craft of helping Congress produce 
the best statutory representation of Members' intent redounded to the 
benefit of the American taxpayer.
  This dedicated work should not go unknown and unrecognized. I thank 
Mr. Hirsch for his service in the field of tax legislation.

                          ____________________
```
