# NORTHFIELD FIRE COMPANY'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
              NORTHFIELD FIRE COMPANY'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 19, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to congratulate the 
Northfield Volunteer Fire Company on its 100th anniversary, marking a 
century of exceptional service. Over the years, many dedicated 
firefighters have been part of this outstanding department, with 
several serving for over 50 years. A few of their veterans recall their 
involvement in negotiating a deal in 1968 for land on Burton Avenue, 
where the current firehouse and training area are located. Times were 
different back then, as firefighters responded to calls in the 1960s 
and `70s with gear that was assigned on a ``first

[[Page E1167]]

come, first served'' basis. Despite these challenges, these brave men 
always answered the call and served their community valiantly. Today, 
the fire department has expanded and ensures that their firefighters 
have all the necessary gear, enhancing their reliability in Northfield. 
Recently, the department dedicated its new fire truck, Ladder 63, in 
honor of Jake Eggie and Robert Leeds, both of whom dedicated 50 years 
of service to the community. Fire Captain Flaherty and Chief Cummings 
have emphasized that you cannot celebrate 100 years without remembering 
those who made this achievement possible. Congratulations on 100 years 
of exemplary service. God bless the Northfield Volunteer Fire Company, 
and God bless our United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
