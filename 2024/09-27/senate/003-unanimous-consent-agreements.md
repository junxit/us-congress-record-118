# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENTS

**Speaking:** Booker, Cory A. (B001288) [D-NJ]

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENTS

  Mr. BOOKER. In my capacity as the Senator from New Jersey, I ask 
unanimous consent that, notwithstanding the order of September 25, 
2024, I be recognized to ask consent to modify that order.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. BOOKER. In my capacity as the Senator from New Jersey, I ask 
unanimous consent that, notwithstanding the order of September 25, 
2024, that when the Senate adjourns today, it next convene at 10 a.m. 
on Tuesday, October 1, and not convene at 1 p.m. on Tuesday as 
previously ordered, and that all other provisions of the September 25 
order remain in effect.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
