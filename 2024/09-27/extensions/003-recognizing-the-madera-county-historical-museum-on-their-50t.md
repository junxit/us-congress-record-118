# RECOGNIZING THE MADERA COUNTY HISTORICAL MUSEUM ON THEIR 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Duarte, John S. (D000633) [R-CA]

```
     RECOGNIZING THE MADERA COUNTY HISTORICAL MUSEUM ON THEIR 50TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN S. DUARTE

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 27, 2024

  Mr. DUARTE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Madera County 
Historical Museum's 50th Anniversary on September 28, 2024.
  The Madera County Museum was opened to the public on September 28, 
1974. For fifty years, the Madera County Historical Society, 
volunteers, and local organizations have made the Madera County Museum 
a historic and informative destination in the Valley. I want to also 
recognize the efforts of the community for their donations and efforts 
to provide exciting events and learning experiences showcasing Madera 
County's rich history.
  Through the efforts of the community, the Madera County Museum 
provides Madera County with educational school tours, holiday events, 
and weekly educational public visitation options. Madera County Museum 
gives back to the community by hosting mock trial and 4-H educational 
opportunities for students, holiday events to bring the community 
together, and supporting local law enforcement.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives to join me in recognizing the Madera County Museum's 
role in preserving the rich history of Madera County. I would also like 
to encourage every Valley family to visit the Madera County Museum and 
learn about our Valley's rich history.

                          ____________________
```
