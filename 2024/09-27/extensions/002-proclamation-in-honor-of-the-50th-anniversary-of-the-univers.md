# PROCLAMATION IN HONOR OF THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE UNIVERSITY OF MARYLAND EASTERN SHORE CLASS OF 1974

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
  PROCLAMATION IN HONOR OF THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE UNIVERSITY OF 
                  MARYLAND EASTERN SHORE CLASS OF 1974

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 27, 2024

  Mr. HARRIS. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
Proclamation in honor of the 50th anniversary of the University of 
Maryland Eastern Shore class of 1974.
  Whereas, the University of Maryland Eastern Shore was established in 
1886 as the Delaware Conference Academy and renamed several times 
since; and
  Whereas, between 1948 and 1970 it was known as the Maryland State 
College; and
  Whereas, in July of 1970 it was renamed to its current name as the 
University of Maryland Eastern Shore; and
  Whereas, the class of 1974 was and is the first class to graduate 
under its new name; and
  Whereas, on its 30th anniversary class reunion, the class of 1974 
resolved to raise $1,000,000 to be donated and contributed to the 
University of Maryland Eastern Shore and has since raised and donated 
$650,000 which constitutes three-fourths of the resolved amount.
  Now, therefore, I, Andy Harris, Member of Congress, do hereby 
proclaim and celebrate the class of 1974 of the University of Maryland 
Eastern Shore on its 50th reunion anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
