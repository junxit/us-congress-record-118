# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9880

**Measures:** H.R. 9880

```
            By Mr. PHILLIPS:
        H.R. 9880.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec. 8, cl. 3 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill creates a national service program within the 
     Small Business Administration to place fellows at startup 
     companies in economically distressed areas of the country.
```
