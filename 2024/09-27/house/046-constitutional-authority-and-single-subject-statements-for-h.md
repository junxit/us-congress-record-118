# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9888

**Measures:** H.R. 9888

```
            By Mr. TRONE:
        H.R. 9888.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provides States the flexibility within the NEVI one mile 
     requirement and allow States to propose a small town near the 
     highway system as a recipient of Nevi Formula funding.
```
