# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9869

**Measures:** H.R. 9869

```
           By Mr. LaLOTA:
       H.R. 9869.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To make dependents of members of the Armed Forces who died 
     while serving on active duty eligible for enrollment in 
     Department of Defense Education Activity schools on a 
     tuition-free, space-available basis.
```
