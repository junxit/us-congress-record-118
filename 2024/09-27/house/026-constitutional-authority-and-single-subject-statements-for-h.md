# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9868

**Measures:** H.R. 9868

```
           By Mr. JOHNSON of Georgia:
       H.R. 9868.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 1.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This is a bill to amend title 28, United States Code, to 
     clarify the availability of Federal habeas corpus relief for 
     a person who is sentenced to death though actually innocent.
```
