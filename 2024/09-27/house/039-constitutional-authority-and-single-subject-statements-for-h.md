# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9881

**Measures:** H.R. 9881

```
            By Mr. PHILLIPS:
        H.R. 9881.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. I, Sec. 8, cl. 3 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill establishes the American Dream Accounts for every 
     American child to have access to an investment account and 
     disbursement at age of 18 years old.
```
