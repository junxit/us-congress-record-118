# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9864

**Measures:** H.R. 9864

```
           By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 9864.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to 
     prohibit the introduction or delivery for introduction into 
     interstate commerce of food packaging containing 
     intentionally added PFAS, and for other purposes.
```
