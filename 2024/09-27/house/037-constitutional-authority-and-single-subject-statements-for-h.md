# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9879

**Measures:** H.R. 9879

```
           By Mr. PFLUGER:
       H.R. 9879.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) with 
     the authority to temporarily and permanently schedule 
     synthetic analogues of controlled substances as Schedule A 
     drugs, in order to prevent their unlawful importation, 
     manufacture, and abuse.
```
