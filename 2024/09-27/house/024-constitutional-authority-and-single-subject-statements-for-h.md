# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9866

**Measures:** H.R. 9866

```
           By Mrs. HINSON:
       H.R. 9866.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Directs the Secretary of HHS to conduct a study and submit 
     to Congress a report on contraceptive access at community 
     health centers in health care deserts.
```
