# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7880

**Measures:** H.R. 7880

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 7880.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to require that 
     institutions of higher education maintain certain adjusted 
     cohort default rates to participate in programs under title 
     IV of such Act, and for other purposes.
```
