# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 125

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 125

```
           By Mr. FITZGERALD:
       H.J. Res. 125.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation Is:
       Resolution under the Congressional Review Act to nullify 
     the rule submitted by the Board of Governors of the Federal 
     Reserve System relating to ``Principles for Climate-Related 
     Financial Risk Management for Large Financial Institutions''
```
