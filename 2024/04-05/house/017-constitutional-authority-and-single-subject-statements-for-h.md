# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7870

**Measures:** H.R. 7870

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 7870.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     provide that certain tips shall not be subject to income or 
     employment taxes for a period of 5 years.
```
