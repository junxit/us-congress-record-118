# COMMEMORATING B&B THEATRES

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                       COMMEMORATING B&B THEATRES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 5, 2024

  Mr. GRAVES of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the 
remarkable journey of B&B Theatres as they mark their centennial 
milestone. As we celebrate the 100th anniversary of B&B Theatres, we 
reflect upon a century of enriching experiences, cherished memories, 
and cultural contributions in my home state of Missouri, as well as 
communities across the U.S.
  Beyond its cinematic offerings, B&B Theatres has been a beacon of 
community engagement, fostering connections and bringing people 
together. Through partnerships with local organizations, schools, and 
charities, B&B Theatres has demonstrated a profound dedication to 
giving back to the communities it serves.
  Moreover, B&B Theatres has continuously embraced innovation, adapting 
to the evolving landscape of the entertainment industry while staying 
true to its core values. From the introduction of state-of-the-art 
technology to the enhancement of amenities and services, B&B Theatres 
has consistently elevated the moviegoing experience, ensuring that 
patrons receive nothing short of excellence.
  As we honor the legacy of B&B Theatres on this momentous occasion, 
let us extend our heartfelt congratulations to the founders, employees, 
and patrons who have contributed to its enduring success. May the next 
century be filled with continued prosperity, innovation, and memorable 
experiences for all who walk through the doors of B&B Theatres.

[[Page E304]]

  In recognition of its 100 years of unparalleled entertainment and 
community service, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating the 
remarkable legacy of B&B Theatres and the profound impact it has had on 
our Nation's cultural landscape.

                          ____________________
```
