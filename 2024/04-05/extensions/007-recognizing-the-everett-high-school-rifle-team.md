# RECOGNIZING THE EVERETT HIGH SCHOOL RIFLE TEAM

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
             RECOGNIZING THE EVERETT HIGH SCHOOL RIFLE TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 5, 2024

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Everett High School Rifle Team on winning the 2024 Pennsylvania 
Interscholastic Rifle Championships held in Meyersdale, Pennsylvania.
  The discipline, focus, and sportsmanship demonstrated by each member 
of the Everett High School Rifle Team are a testament to

[[Page E305]]

their character and the exceptional coaching they have received. 
Through countless hours of practice and unwavering determination, they 
have honed their skills to perfection.
  Under the leadership of Coach Coltin Rice, the team has gone 
undefeated since 2017. Their dedication, perseverance, and unwavering 
commitment to excellence have propelled them to new heights, setting 
the bar even higher for future teams.
  On behalf of the people of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional 
District, I congratulate the Everett High School Rifle Team, and wish 
them every continued success in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
