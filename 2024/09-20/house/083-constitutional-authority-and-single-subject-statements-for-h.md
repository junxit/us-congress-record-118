# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9741

**Measures:** H.R. 9741

```
           By Ms. SHERRILL:
       H.R. 9741.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution of 
     the United States of America.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to ensure that 
     beneficiaries and health care providers receive certain 
     information regarding reproductive health care under the 
     TRICARE program.
```
