# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9721

**Measures:** H.R. 9721

```
            By Ms. De La CRUZ:
        H.R. 9721.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would make clear what contract activities mandate 
     the Department of Housing and Urban Development to make best 
     efforts to include in any modification made to a contract 
     into which it has previously entered a contract clause that 
     provides for the same whistleblower protections that are 
     contained in Section 4712 of title 41, United States Code.
```
