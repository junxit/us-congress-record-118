# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9724

**Measures:** H.R. 9724

```
            By Mr. ARRINGTON:
        H.R. 9724.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would amend Section 301 of the Trade Act 
     of 1974 to allow the U.S. International Trade Commission 
     (USITC) to investigate tariff evasion from non-market 
     entities into third countries, and advise USTR or Congress to 
     enact a trade remedy against the third country investment if 
     tariff evasion is determined.
```
