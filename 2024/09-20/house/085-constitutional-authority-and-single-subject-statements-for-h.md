# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9743

**Measures:** H.R. 9743

```
           By Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi:
       H.R. 9743.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow a 
     deduction for traveling expenses for Federal disaster relief 
     workers away from home for more than 1 year.
```
