# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9718

**Measures:** H.R. 9718

```
            By Mr. ZINKE:
        H.R. 9718.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for the approval of the Amendment to the 
     Agreement Between the Government of the United States of 
     America and the Government of the United Kingdom of Great 
     Britain and Northern Ireland for Cooperation on the Uses of 
     Atomic Energy for Mutual Defense Purposes
```
