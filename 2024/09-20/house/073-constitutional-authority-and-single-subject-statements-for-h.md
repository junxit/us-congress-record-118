# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9731

**Measures:** H.R. 9731

```
           By Ms. GREENE of Georgia:
       H.R. 9731.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Homeland Security to publish on 
     a monthly basis the number of special interest aliens 
     encountered attempting to unlawfully enter the United States, 
     and for other purposes.
```
