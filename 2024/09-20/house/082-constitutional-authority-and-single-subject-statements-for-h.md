# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9740

**Measures:** H.R. 9740

```
           By Ms. SHERRILL:
       H.R. 9740.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution of 
     the United States of America.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Defense to increase the maximum 
     amount of contraceptive supplies provided to a beneficiary 
     through the TRICARE program.
```
