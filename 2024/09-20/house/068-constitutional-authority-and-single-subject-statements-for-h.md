# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9726

**Measures:** H.R. 9726

```
            By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
        H.R. 9726.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The NFIP Premium Transparency Act would require FEMA and 
     Insurance Companies to provide NFIP policyholders with data 
     used to calculate their NFIP premiums. The NFIP Premium 
     Transparency Act would also direct GAO to conduct a study 
     examining additional data that could be shared with NFIP 
     Policyholders and potential uses of that data. (FINANCIAL 
     SERVICES)
```
