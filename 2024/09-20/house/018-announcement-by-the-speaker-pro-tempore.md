# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 136, H.R. 5717, H.R. 9106

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  The motion to recommit H.R. 5717;
  Passage of H.R. 5717, if ordered;
  Passage of H.J. Res. 136; and
  The motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 9106.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Pursuant to clause 9 of rule XX, remaining electronic votes will be 
conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
