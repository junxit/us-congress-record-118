# RECOGNIZING TOM DeBAUN

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                         RECOGNIZING TOM DeBAUN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 20, 2024

  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize former mayor of 
Shelbyville, Tom DeBaun.
  First elected in 2011, Tom served his community as mayor for three 
terms.
  As mayor, he prioritized economic development and residents, 
establishing an adult high school, creating Advantage Shelby County, 
and investing in trails and sidewalks to increase accessibility.
  Before serving as mayor, Tom was a probation officer and head of the 
city's building and planning commission.
  Today, I thank Tom for his service and support for the Hoosiers of 
Shelbyville.

                          ____________________
```
