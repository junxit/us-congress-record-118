# RECOGNIZING UPLAND COLUMBUS PUMP HOUSE

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                 RECOGNIZING UPLAND COLUMBUS PUMP HOUSE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 20, 2024

  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Upland Columbus 
Pump House in my hometown.
  The Columbus Pump House was designed by Harrison Albright and 
constructed in 1903.
  Over the years, the historic building has had many incarnations, and 
now houses Upland Brewing Company, which has been brewing quality beer 
for 25 years.
  Even as it changes, the Pump House has never lost its intended 
architectural quality.
  Today I thank Upland for their dedication to keeping that history.
```
