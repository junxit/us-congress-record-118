# RECOGNIZING ANGOTTI'S ITALIAN RESTAURANT

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                RECOGNIZING ANGOTTI'S ITALIAN RESTAURANT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 20, 2024

  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Angotti's Italian 
Restaurant in Columbus, Indiana.
  Serving the community since 2019, Angotti's moved to its current 
location downtown last year.
  Denise and I are frequent customers, and their gift is that they 
always make us feel like family.
  Angotti's is a blessing for the Columbus area, and I'm grateful for 
the opportunity to recognize them today.

                          ____________________
```
