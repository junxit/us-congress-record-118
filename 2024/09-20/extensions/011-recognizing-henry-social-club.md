# RECOGNIZING HENRY SOCIAL CLUB

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                     RECOGNIZING HENRY SOCIAL CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 20, 2024

  Mr. PENCE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Henry Social Club, 
a restaurant in my hometown of Columbus, Indiana.
  Opening in 2014, Henry Social Club is helmed by Gethin Thomas, former 
executive chef at Columbus-based Cummins.
  With an impressive resume including work in New York, Paris, and 
London, Chef Thomas now calls Columbus home, a testament to the 
community we have in my district.
  Today, I recognize Henry Social Club and Chef Thomas' efforts as he 
continues to bless the Sixth District.

                          ____________________
```
