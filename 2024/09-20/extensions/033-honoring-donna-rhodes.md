# HONORING DONNA RHODES

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                         HONORING DONNA RHODES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 20, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, I would like to recognize Donna Rhodes, president of the Sun 
City Grand Republican Club.
  Donna has distinguished herself by providing exceptional service to 
the residents of Sun City Grand, Arizona. As president of the club, 
Donna has been an advocate for educating members on the civic 
engagement process and how to interact with elected officials. The 
efforts of Donna and the Sun City Grand Republican Club have been 
pivotal to the success of elected officials and candidates in the 
Phoenix West Valley.
  I am thankful for Donna's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate her service to the 8th Congressional 
District and the State of Arizona.
```
