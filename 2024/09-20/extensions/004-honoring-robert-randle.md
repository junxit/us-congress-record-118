# HONORING ROBERT RANDLE

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                         HONORING ROBERT RANDLE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 20, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, I would like to recognize Robert Randle, president of the Sun 
City West Republican Club.

[[Page E934]]

  Throughout his tenure as president, Robert has distinguished himself 
by providing exceptional service to the residents of Sun City West, 
Arizona. As president of the club, Robert has been an advocate for 
educating members on the civic engagement process and how to interact 
with elected officials. The efforts of Robert and the Sun City West 
Republican Club have been pivotal to the success of elected officials 
and candidates in the Phoenix West Valley.
  I am thankful for Robert's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate his service to the 8th Congressional 
District and the State of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
