# HONORING CATHY SCHWANKE

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                        HONORING CATHY SCHWANKE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 20, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, I would like to recognize Cathy Schwanke for her work with 
the Anthem, New River, Desert Hills, and Phoenix Republican Club.
  Cathy saw a need to educate Republicans in the northern most areas of 
our Congressional District and was a driving force in creating this 
group. She works tirelessly to keep members engaged in the political 
process, promote civic engagement, and educate citizens on Republican 
principles.
  I am thankful for Cathy's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate her service to Congressional 
District 8 and the State of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
