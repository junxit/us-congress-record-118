# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
MADURO STOLE THE ELECTIONS AGAIN: THE RESPONSE TO FRAUD IN VENEZUELA
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on the Western Hemisphere 
held a hearing entitled ``Maduro Stole the Elections Again: The 
Response to Fraud in Venezuela''. Testimony was heard from Kevin 
Sullivan, Deputy Assistant Secretary for Brazil Southern Cone and 
Andean Affairs, Bureau of Western Hemisphere Affairs, Department of 
State; Enrique Roig, Deputy Assistant Secretary, Bureau of Democracy, 
Human Rights, and Labor, Department of State; and Greg Howell, Senior 
Deputy Assistant Administrator, Bureau for Latin America and the 
Caribbean, U.S. Agency for International Development.
PROTECTING AMERICAN INNOVATION BY ESTABLISHING AND ENFORCING STRONG 
DIGITAL TRADE RULES
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Trade held a hearing 
entitled ``Protecting American Innovation by Establishing and Enforcing 
Strong Digital Trade Rules''. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.

  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
