# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Praise the Lord, my soul, and forget not all His benefits. May we, in 
our innermost being, praise God's holy name.
  For You, O Lord, have graced us with the richness of our lives and 
the bounty of friendships. You have presented us both opportunities and 
challenges, providing for us the privilege to serve one another and the 
honor to enrich the lives around us. In all these things, we give 
thanks.
  Grant us the patience to appreciate the passage of time, the humility 
not to see ourselves as greater than we are, but to find the grace 
within us to receive the mentorship of those who have gone before, the 
counsel of friends and colleagues who see things that we can't see, 
that we may demure to the wisdom You reveal to us every day of our 
lives.
  Continue to bless those whose lives exemplify the ultimate service to 
You and to this Nation, the men and women who stand the watch on the 
days we rest, that we may enjoy the liberties You have afforded us and 
the freedoms You have allowed this country.
  We pray to You, standing on the noble traditions this body seeks to 
uphold, sure of Your abiding protection.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
