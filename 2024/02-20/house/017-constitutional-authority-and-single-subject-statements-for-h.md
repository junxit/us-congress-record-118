# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7413

**Measures:** H.R. 7413

```
           By Mr. D'ESPOSITO:
       H.R. 7413.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Help America Vote Act of 2002 to direct the 
     Election Assistance Commission to conduct ongoing studies of 
     State programs for recruiting poll workers and providing poll 
     workers with safety training in order to identify voluntary 
     considerations for carrying out such programs, and for other 
     purposes.
```
