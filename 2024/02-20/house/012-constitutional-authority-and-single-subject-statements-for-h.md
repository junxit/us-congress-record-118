# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7408

**Measures:** H.R. 7408

```
           By Mr. WESTERMAN:
       H.R. 7408.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To recover species currently listed as threatened or 
     endangered under the Endangered Species Act (16 U.S.C. 1531 
     et seq.) or under state law and to prevent the need to list 
     species under the Endangered Species Act (16 U.S.C. 1531 et 
     seq.) or under state law.
```
