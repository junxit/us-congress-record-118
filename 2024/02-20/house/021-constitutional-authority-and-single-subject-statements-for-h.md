# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7417

**Measures:** H.R. 7417

```
           By Mr. KELLY of Pennsylvania:
       H.R. 7417.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United States 
     Constitution: ``Congress shall have the power . . . to 
     establish post offices and post roads.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Redesignates the Titusville, Pennsylvania Post Office.
```
