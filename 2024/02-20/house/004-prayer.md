# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Washington Parish, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  O Lord, our Governor, whose glory is in all the world: We commend 
this Nation to Your merciful care, that being guided by Your 
providence, we may dwell secure in Your peace. Grant to the Congress of 
the United States and to all in authority, wisdom and strength to know 
and to do Your will. Fill our Representatives with the love of truth 
and righteousness and make them ever mindful of their calling to serve 
this people.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
