# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7425

**Measures:** H.R. 7425

```
           By Mr. SCHWEIKERT:
       H.R. 7425.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Sixteenth Amendment provides Congress with the power to 
     ``lay and collect taxes on incomes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide taxpayers with a tax deduction for certain 
     newborn expenses.
```
