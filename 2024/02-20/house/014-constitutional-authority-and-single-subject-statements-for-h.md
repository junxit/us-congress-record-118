# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7410

**Measures:** H.R. 7410

```
           By Mr. CAREY:
       H.R. 7410.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend part A of title IV of the Social Security Act to 
     establish deadlines for the obligation and expenditure of 
     funds and allow States to establish rainy day funds under the 
     program of block grants to States for temporary assistance 
     for needy families.
```
