# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7414

**Measures:** H.R. 7414

```
           By Mr. TONY GONZALES of Texas:
       H.R. 7414.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the development of a national strategy to secure 
     schools against terrorism nationwide, and ensure domestic 
     preparedness for and the response to terrorism, and for other 
     purposes.
```
