# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7421

**Measures:** H.R. 7421

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 7421.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to include the District of Columbia for eligibility for 
     funding under the McIntire-Stennis Act.
```
