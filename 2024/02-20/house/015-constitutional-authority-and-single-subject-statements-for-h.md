# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7411

**Measures:** H.R. 7411

```
           By Mr. CHAVEZ-DeREMER:
       H.R. 7411.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal 
     Year 2020 to improve reviews of characterizations of 
     discharges of members of the Armed Forces on the basis of 
     sexual orientation.
```
