         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to

     Services in consultations regarding designations of critical 
     minerals, elements, substances, and materials (Rept. 118-
     710). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 3675. A 
     bill to amend the

       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 1314. A 
     bill to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to enter into
