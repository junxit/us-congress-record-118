# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10029

**Measures:** H.R. 10029

```
           By Mr. KILEY:
       H.R. 10029.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       To make all laws which shall be necessary and proper for 
     carrying into execution the foregoing powers, and all other 
     powers vested by this Constitution in the government of the 
     United States,
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation offers incentives to states to report data 
     on human trafficking for the creation of a national human 
     trafficking database.
```
