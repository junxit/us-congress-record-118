# HONORING SUE DONAHUE

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                          HONORING SUE DONAHUE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, I would like to recognize the president of the Arrowhead 
Republican Women, Sue Donahue.
  Sue has distinguished herself by providing exceptional service to the 
members of this organization, with roots in the West Valley dating back 
to 1993. As president, Sue has been an advocate for educating members 
on philosophies of smaller government, reduced taxes, and personal 
freedoms. The efforts of Sue and the Arrowhead Republican Women have 
been pivotal to the success of elected officials and candidates in the 
State of Arizona.
  As a member of the Arrowhead Republican Women, I am thankful for 
Sue's leadership and dedication to conservative principles. I sincerely 
appreciate her service to Congressional District 8 and the State of 
Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
