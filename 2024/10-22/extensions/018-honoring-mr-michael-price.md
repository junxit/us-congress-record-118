# HONORING MR. MICHAEL PRICE

**Speaking:** Vargas, Juan (V000130) [D-CA]

```
                       HONORING MR. MICHAEL PRICE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JUAN VARGAS

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. VARGAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. Michael Price, an 
outstanding citizen who serves the south San Diego community as a chef 
and small business owner. Mr.

[[Page E1056]]

Price is the proud owner of Chef Budda Blasian Soul Food, a food truck 
that incorporates the traditions of Soul Food alongside a creative 
Asian flare to provide a unique culinary experience for its consumers.
  During the pandemic, Mr. Price and his wife, Lily Nachampasak, turned 
their focus to their passion for food and brought their dreams of 
cooking professionally to life when they started a pop-up kitchen in 
City Heights. They succeeded not only in supporting their family 
through this new business venture, but also in enriching our community 
by providing joy and a flavorful dining experience for all.
  Their pop-up kitchen quickly found success, leading to invitations to 
serve food at music festivals, night markets, and more. Through hard 
work and determination, they expanded their business and purchased a 
food truck. Now, Chef Budda Blasian Soul Food is a cherished part of 
the local food scene and can be found all throughout our communities.
  It was great to have been able to meet and speak with Mr. Price about 
how he built this thriving small business, recognize him for his 
contributions to the community, and get some delicious fried chicken, 
shrimp, and catfish and fixings.
  Mr. Price's dedication has made our community a better place, and I 
can't think of a better person to have recognized during National Black 
Business Month.

                          ____________________
```
