# RECOGNIZING BRIAN WINKLER

**Speaking:** McMorris Rodgers, Cathy (M001159) [R-WA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BRIAN WINKLER

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. CATHY McMORRIS RODGERS

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mrs. RODGERS of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Brian Winkler for his 40 years of dedicated service to our community in 
Eastern Washington and to the United States of America. Following in 
the footsteps of his father, a decorated World War II veteran, Brian 
served as a U.S. Navy Seabee for 15 years and dedicated his life to 
upholding their motto of ``We Build, We Fight, Can Do.''
  Brian's ``Can Do'' spirit and commitment to service did not stop 
there. For the past 25 years, he has tirelessly devoted himself to 
helping veterans, their families, and his community. Among his many 
remarkable achievements, Mr. Winkler spearheaded the construction and 
donation of a water park that has provided joy to generations of young 
children in our region. Moreover, he has generously shared his time, 
expertise, and resources with the Salvation Army, City Gate, and 
numerous other locaI charities.
  To this day, Mr. Winkler maintains a ``Seabee'' float that he built 
which he showcases at

[[Page E1052]]

parades and veterans events across Eastern Washington. He has also used 
his business to create employment opportunities for more than 200 
individuals in Washington's Fifth Congressional District. significantly 
improving the lives of families in his community.
  In addition to his extensive community work, Mr. Winkler has been an 
unwavering advocate for veterans from every branch of the military. He 
has extended his support to organizations such as the Seabee Veterans 
of America, Spokane Veterans Home, Navy League of the United States, 
Honor Point Military Aerospace Museum, Veterans of Foreign Wars, the 
Spokane chapter of the Vietnam Veterans of America, the United States 
Marine Corps League, and many more.
  Mr. Winkler is a selfless individual who represents everything that 
is good in the United States of America. His unyielding desire to give 
back to his community, as well as his enduring commitment to our 
country and veterans who selflessly served it, are a testament to his 
profound dedication to helping others. His continued contributions will 
undoubtedly have a lasting impact on the lives of so many, and I know I 
speak for everyone in Eastern Washington when I say our community is a 
better place due to his ``Can Do'' efforts to help others.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge my colleagues to join me in recognizing Brian 
Winkler for his tremendous contributions to Eastern Washington and 
thanking him for his dedicated service to our Nation.
```
