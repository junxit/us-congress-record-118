# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. FRANCINE L. BROADOUS OPUTA

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
     HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DR. FRANCINE L. BROADOUS OPUTA

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. COSTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the extraordinary 
life and enduring legacy of Dr. Francine L. Broadous Oputa. Her 
journey, characterized by unwavering faith, an insatiable thirst for 
knowledge, and an unyielding dedication to her community, stands as an 
inspirational testament to the profound impact of a life devoted to 
service.
  Born on May 31, 1953, in San Fernando, California, Dr. Francine's 
life unfolded as a tapestry interwoven with threads of faith, 
education, and community service. She was the youngest among eleven 
siblings, born to Pastor/Dr. Hillery Talbert and Mother Rosa Lee (Cox-
Thompson) Broadous. Her spiritual roots were firmly planted at Calvary 
Missionary Baptist Church, founded by her own father, where her passion 
for performance first took root. Francine's thirst for knowledge knew 
no bounds. Her academic journey led her through several distinguished 
institutions, including Long Beach State, Stanislaus State, UC Davis, 
and Fresno State. Her unwavering pursuit of excellence culminated in a 
doctoral degree in Educational Leadership. Throughout her academic 
odyssey, she not only enriched her own mind but also ignited the flames 
of aspiration in countless young hearts within her community.
  In the autumn of 1976, fate brought Francine and Henry Ifeanyi-Chukwu 
Oputa together at Stanislaus State in Turlock, California. Their love 
story blossomed, and on August 20, 1977, they embarked on a 46-year-
long adventure as husband and wife. Their journey led them to Fresno, 
where they would establish their family and leave a lasting mark on the 
community. Faith played a pivotal role in their lives, inspiring the 
founding of The Way Ministries John . . . 14:6 in November 1989. Their 
dedication to the community materialized in the establishment of the 
Saturday Cultural School and the creation of a lasting tribute, the 
``When I think of Home'' mural, which stands as a testament to their 
unwavering commitment to West Fresno.
  Francine's career spanned 30 impactful years at Fresno State, where 
she held the esteemed position of Director of the Cross Cultural and 
Gender Center (CCGC). Her dedication to diversity, cultural competency, 
and support for students, faculty, and staff was evident through her 
tireless efforts and the programs she championed, including the Central 
Valley Cultural Heritage Institute and the Women's Resource Center. 
Upon retiring from the university in 2020, Francine founded Oputa & 
Associates, a successful consulting firm that drew from her decades of 
experience in performing, inspirational speaking, and diversity, 
equity, and inclusion training. As CEO, she continued her mission of 
making a positive impact on a nationwide scale.
  Dr. Francine L. Broadous Oputa's life exemplified the values of 
faith, education, and community service. Her legacy will continue to 
inspire generations, a testament to the transformative power of a life 
well-lived. Her contributions to her community, her unwavering 
commitment to education, and her boundless faith will forever be etched 
in our hearts.
  Francine was preceded in death by her parents, Pastor/Dr. Hillery 
Talbert and Mother Rosa Lee (Cox-Thompson) Broadous, as well as six 
siblings. She is survived by her husband, Rev./Dr. Henry Ifeanyi-Chukwu 
Oputa, her three children--Chunyendu ``CeCe'' N. Oputa, I Adeficha 
Oputa, and Nwachukwu N. Oputa, and several siblings. Her memory will 
forever be cherished by her family, her community, and all those whose 
lives she touched.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my esteemed colleagues to join me in 
commemorating this significant moment in the life of Dr. Francine L. 
Broadous Oputa, a woman whose contributions have left an indelible mark 
on the hearts and souls of many.
```
