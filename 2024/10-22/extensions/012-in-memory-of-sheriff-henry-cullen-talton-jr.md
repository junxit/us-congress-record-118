# IN MEMORY OF SHERIFF HENRY CULLEN TALTON, JR.

**Speaking:** Bishop, Sanford D. (B000490) [D-GA]

```
             IN MEMORY OF SHERIFF HENRY CULLEN TALTON, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. SANFORD D. BISHOP, JR.

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. BISHOP of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart 
to honor and remember a loving husband and father, doting grandfather 
and great-grandfather, lifelong public servant, the longest tenured 
sheriff in the United States of America, and my friend of long 
standing, the Honorable Henry Cullen Talton, Jr. Sadly, he transitioned 
from labor to reward on Saturday, October 19, 2024. The funeral service 
honoring and celebrating Sheriff Talton's life will be held on 
Wednesday, October 23, 2024, at Southside Baptist Church in Warner 
Robins, Georgia.
  Sheriff Talton was born to the union of Henry Cullen Talton, Sr. and 
Lois Ammons Talton on October 16, 1932, in Bonaire, Georgia. Early on 
in his adult life Sheriff Talton began his career as a middle Georgia 
dairy farmer before making a successful run for the Houston County 
Commission. In 1972, he won his first term as Sheriff of Houston 
County, and he would go on to win 12 additional successive terms in his 
lifetime. For nearly 52 years, Sheriff Talton served as Houston's top 
law enforcement officer, and he became nationally known as America's 
longest-tenured sheriff.
  Rarely seen wearing his sheriff's badge or his firearm, Sheriff 
Talton was truly a man of the people. His commitment to fostering 
public safety and building relationships with the people he served 
earned him the respect and admiration of both the people of Houston 
County and the Georgia law enforcement community. Sheriff Talton 
presided over the Houston County Sheriff's Department during a period 
of substantial population growth in the county. Throughout his tenure, 
Sheriff Talton made sure his department was responsive, effective, and 
on the cutting edge of law enforcement. At the time of his election as 
sheriff in 1972, Houston County was home to about 63,000 people; today, 
the county is home to nearly 170,000 people.
  As Sheriff, Talton made a point to engage with and collaborate with 
leaders from across the state in order to improve public safety for 
all. He actively contributed to various organizations, including 
serving on the Board of Directors for the Georgia Sheriffs' Association 
and the Georgia Sheriffs' Youth Homes where he received special 
recognitions multiple times for his fundraising initiatives. Throughout 
his career, Sheriff Talton was honored with numerous awards, including 
the Golden Eagle Award (2010); the Meritorious Service Master Award 
(2022); the President's Award from the Georgia Sheriffs Association; 
the Warner Robins/Houston County Chamber of Commerce Good Government 
Service Award; the Liberty Bell Award; and was named Sheriff of the 
Year in 2019.
  Sheriff Talton contributed so much to his community and accomplished 
so much in his life, but none of it would have been possible without 
the Grace of God, and the love and support of his family, to include 
his late wife of 68 years, Peggy; his devoted children, Carlene Talton, 
Patty Redmond (Ed), Cully Talton, and Neal Talton (Ginger); 
grandchildren, Kitt Orr (Brandon), Lindsey Farrar, Tal Talton (Amanda), 
Sydney Morstad; along with 10 great-grandchildren.
  Sir Winston Churchill often said: ``You make your living by what you 
get; you make your life by what you give.'' Sheriff Talton gave so much 
to so many for so long. His over 50 years of servant leadership on 
behalf of the people of Houston County has left his community stronger, 
more prosperous, and more secure. Truly, the world is a better place 
because Sheriff Henry Cullen Talton, Jr. passed this way.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives to join my wife, Vivian; and me, along with the more 
than 765,000 people of Georgia's Second District. in celebrating the 
life of Sheriff Henry Cullen Talton, Jr. We extend our deepest 
condolences to his family, friends, loved ones and all who mourn his 
loss. We pray that they all will be consoled and comforted by an 
abiding faith and the Holy Spirit in the days, weeks, and months ahead.
```
