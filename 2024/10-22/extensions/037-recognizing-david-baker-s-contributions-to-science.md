# RECOGNIZING DAVID BAKER'S CONTRIBUTIONS TO SCIENCE

**Speaking:** Jayapal, Pramila (J000298) [D-WA]

```
           RECOGNIZING DAVID BAKER'S CONTRIBUTIONS TO SCIENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. PRAMILA JAYAPAL

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Ms. JAYAPAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Dr. David 
Baker on winning the Nobel Prize in Chemistry. The Nobel Prize award 
recognizes Dr. Baker's tremendous achievement in utilizing 
computational protein design to turn amino acids into proteins. He has 
made significant strides in the field of biochemistry through his 
groundbreaking research and has trailblazed various methods to build 
new proteins.
  Dr. Baker is currently a Professor of Biochemistry at the University 
of Washington in Seattle. He is the eighth University of Washington 
faculty member to receive a Nobel Prize. Dr. Baker's research has been 
described as visionary. His lab pioneered the field by developing much 
of the world's most popular and effective software for computational 
protein design. Dr. Baker's research has helped analyze database 
information about existing proteins to develop new proteins that do not 
exist in nature.
  His success has allowed for the creation of novel proteins to tackle 
some of the greatest challenges in medicine, technology, and 
sustainability. He has deepened scientific understanding of protein 
prediction and made immense contributions to biochemistry research. Dr. 
Baker's career has been remarkable--to date, he has published more than 
640 peer-reviewed research papers, been awarded more than 100 patents, 
and co-founded 21 biotechnology companies.
  I was proud to secure $550,000 for the Institute for Protein Design 
at the University of Washington where Dr. Baker's lab is housed. I am 
committed to ensuring that Washington's 7th Congressional District 
remains a hub for science and innovation. Scientists like Dr. Baker are 
at the forefront of innovation and must have the resources to continue 
to change our understanding of the world.
  On behalf of Washington's 7th Congressional District, I would like to 
congratulate Dr. David Baker on receiving the Nobel Prize in Chemistry. 
I am so proud to recognize his tremendous contributions to science and 
innovation.

                          ____________________
```
