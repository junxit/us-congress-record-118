# HONORING THE INVESTITURE OF DR. STANFORD E. ANGION AS THE 25TH PRESIDENT OF SELMA UNIVERSITY

**Speaking:** Sewell, Terri A. (S001185) [D-AL]

```
    HONORING THE INVESTITURE OF DR. STANFORD E. ANGION AS THE 25TH 
                     PRESIDENT OF SELMA UNIVERSITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. TERRI A. SEWELL

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Ms. SEWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the Inaugural 
Convocation and Investiture of Dr. Stanford E. Angion as the 25th 
President of Selma University.
  A lifelong learner, Dr. Angion obtained his Bachelor of Soil Science 
at Alabama A&M University. He went on to earn multiple postgraduate 
degrees, including a Master's of Journalism and Mass Communication from 
Marshall University in Huntington, WV, a Master's of Education from the 
University of West Alabama, a Master's of Arts in Bible and Pastoral 
Ministry from Selma University, and a Doctor of Education from Alabama 
State University.
  An esteemed educator and administrator, Dr. Angion began his tenure 
at Selma University in 2016 as the Vice President for Academic Affairs. 
During this period, he oversaw the responsibilities of administering 
the development, implementation, and evaluation of course materials and 
methods, along with recruiting faculty and supervising the Chairs of 
all academic divisions. Moreover, he played a critical role in 
decision-making regarding the testing placement of new students, 
admissions, student-faculty relations, academic counseling, college 
catalogs, and student academic records. His diligence and hard work 
were recognized in June 2020 when he was assigned to the position of 
Provost/Vice President, a capacity in which he served until he was 
appointed as Interim President in 2021.
  Dr. Angion began his professional life in 1987 when, upon graduating 
from Alabama A&M University, he received an active-duty commission into 
the United States Army as a Second Lieutenant. During his thirteen 
years of honorable service, he attained the rank of Major. In October 
2000, Dr. Angion accepted his call into the gospel ministry to preach 
and was honorably discharged from the Army.
  After accepting his anointed call, Dr. Angion was licensed as a 
Baptist preacher in March 2000 and ordained later that year. He served 
for two years at Mt. Zion Baptist Church in Hinesville, GA, as a Youth 
Pastor before being installed in 2003 as Pastor of Mt. Gilead 
Missionary Baptist Church in Coy, AL, where he still serves today.
  As pastor of Mt. Gilead Missionary Baptist Church, Dr. Angion has 
implemented programs and completed projects to uplift his congregation 
and the greater community. Under his leadership, the congregation built 
a Family Recreational Park, renovated the sanctuary, and started the 
MGS&A Institute, which offers a plethora of courses designed to train 
students in the performing arts and enhance elementary and secondary 
student learning.
  Dr. Angion is a proud member of the Phi Beta Sigma Fraternity 
incorporated and is an active member of the Alpha Eta Sigma Chapter in 
Montgomery. He joined the fraternity during his sophomore year at 
Alabama A&M University as a member of the Gamma Epsilon Chapter.
  Under his strong and level-headed leadership, Selma University is 
planning for both today and tomorrow. Dr. Angion's tranformational 
vision is building a framework for the university that will create 
lasting change and growth. Continuing the core mission of Selma 
University, Dr. Angion is dedicated to the quality of education the 
university provides to its students. Through his insightful planning 
and forward-thinking, Dr. Angion is taking strides to create a better 
and brighter future for Selma University.
  On a personal note, I have long admired President Angion's steadfast 
leadership at Selma University. He has demonstrated a strong commitment 
to upholding the standard of excellence for its students, faculty and 
administration, even in the face of major adversity. I congratulate 
him. I look forward to our continued partnership in uplifting this 
historic institution and extend my best wishes for his long and 
successful stewardship of Selma University.
  On behalf of Alabama's 7th Congressional District, I ask my 
colleagues to join me in celebrating the investiture of Dr. Stanford E. 
Angion as the 25th President of Selma University.

                          ____________________
```
