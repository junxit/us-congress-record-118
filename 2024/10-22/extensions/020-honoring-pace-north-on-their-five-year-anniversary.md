# HONORING PACE NORTH ON THEIR FIVE YEAR ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
           HONORING PACE NORTH ON THEIR FIVE YEAR ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate PACE North 
on their five years of dedicated service to the Northern Michigan 
Community.
  Program of All Inclusive Care for the Elderly--North is an all-
inclusive healthcare plan, which serves primarily as an alternative 
tailored to individuals' and families' specific needs. Once admitted, 
an interdisciplinary team works with families to design a plan that 
works for each person. Once this is done, PACE replaces traditional 
health coverage such as Medicare and Medicaid to become both provider 
and caregiver.
  Under the leadership of Executive Director Nicole Farkas, PACE North 
has helped hundreds of seniors navigate the uncertain terrain of living 
co-dependently, providing top of the line medical care as well as kind 
and compassionate assistance with day-to-day life.
  PACE North has certainly lived up to their core values of Quality 
Safety, Compassion, Integrity, Collaboration and Community.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate PACE North on their five 
years of dedicated service to the Northern Michigan Community. I wish 
Director Farkas, Dr. Wilborn and the rest of the team the very best 
going forward.
```
