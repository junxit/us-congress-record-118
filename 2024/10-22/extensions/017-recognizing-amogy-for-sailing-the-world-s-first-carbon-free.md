# RECOGNIZING AMOGY FOR SAILING THE WORLD'S FIRST CARBON-FREE AMMONIA- POWERED MARITIME VESSEL

**Speaking:** Ryan, Patrick (R000579) [D-NY]

```
  RECOGNIZING AMOGY FOR SAILING THE WORLD'S FIRST CARBON-FREE AMMONIA-
                        POWERED MARITIME VESSEL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PATRICK RYAN

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. RYAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extraordinary 
milestone in clean energy innovation achieved by Amogy, a trailblazing 
company in the sustainable energy sector. On September 22nd, Amogy 
successfully demonstrated the world's first carbon-free, ammonia-
powered maritime vessel on Rondout Creek and the Hudson River, located 
in my district in New York.
  This achievement reflects Amogy's commitment to addressing one of the 
most pressing challenges of our time--reducing greenhouse gas emissions 
in the maritime industry. The successful voyage of this vessel marks a 
historic step forward in the global push to transition to cleaner 
energy solutions, and it serves as a beacon of what can be achieved 
when we leverage groundbreaking technology for the good of our planet.
  Amogy's innovation lies in its advanced ammonia-to-power platform, 
which enables the use of ammonia as a renewable, carbon-free fuel. This 
vessel's demonstration is a tangible example of how we can reimagine 
existing industries--such as shipping--and move toward a more 
sustainable future.
  Not only is this accomplishment a testament to the technical prowess 
of the engineers, scientists, and visionaries at Amogy, but it also 
represents a significant moment for environmental policy and industrial 
decarbonization. By replacing fossil fuels with alternative, cleaner 
energy sources like ammonia, we can dramatically reduce emissions in 
sectors that have historically been difficult to decarbonize.
  I would like to commend the entire team at Amogy for their pursuit of 
innovation and sustainability. Their dedication to pushing the 
boundaries of clean energy is a vital contribution to the fight against 
climate change. I would also like to recognize that this historic 
demonstration took place at Feeney Shipyard, in Kingston, New York, 
which has long been a hub of maritime activity in the Hudson Valley.
  I congratulate Amogy on this monumental achievement and look forward 
to witnessing the continued advancements the company will bring to the 
clean energy landscape. It is through the efforts of companies like 
Amogy that we will continue to make strides toward a more sustainable 
and resilient future.
```
