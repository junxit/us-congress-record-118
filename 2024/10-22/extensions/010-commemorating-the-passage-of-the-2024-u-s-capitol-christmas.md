# COMMEMORATING THE PASSAGE OF THE 2024 U.S. CAPITOL CHRISTMAS TREE THROUGH FORT WAYNE, INDIANA

**Speaking:** Banks, Jim (B001299) [R-IN]

```
   COMMEMORATING THE PASSAGE OF THE 2024 U.S. CAPITOL CHRISTMAS TREE 
                      THROUGH FORT WAYNE, INDIANA

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM BANKS

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. BANKS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the passage of 
the 2024 U.S. Capitol Christmas tree through Fort Wayne, Indiana.
  From its origins in Alaska's National Forests to its final 
destination in Washington D.C., this distinguished tree has traveled 
through many of North America's beautiful landscapes. It has visited 
small-town main streets, grand state capitals, elementary schools, and 
local businesses. And now, it has passed through Fort Wayne, Indiana.
  Situated in the heart and crossroads of America, Fort Wayne 
introduces the tree to a vibrant community that exemplifies Midwestern 
values, patriotism, and the true essence of Christmas. Each Christmas 
season, this city's festive spirit is reborn through longstanding 
traditions that celebrate togetherness, generosity, and faith.
  The Capitol Christmas Tree has been affectionately dubbed ``The 
People's Tree,'' and this journey it undertakes truly cements it as 
such. While the tree may not see, hear, or remember the communities it 
encounters, the people of Fort Wayne will long recall the honor of 
hosting a symbol that will stand proudly before the U.S. Capitol, 
embodying all that makes Christmas in America so special.
  I thank Mike Nutter and the TinCaps organization for hosting the tree 
at Parkview Field, allowing Northeast Indiana to enjoy the occasion.

                          ____________________
```
