# RECOGNIZING MARTIN COUNTY HOSPITAL DISTRICT IN STANTON, TEXAS

**Speaking:** Arrington, Jodey C. (A000375) [R-TX]

```
     RECOGNIZING MARTIN COUNTY HOSPITAL DISTRICT IN STANTON, TEXAS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JODEY C. ARRINGTON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. ARRINGTON. Mr. Speaker, I often refer to West Texas as the Food, 
Fuel, and Fiber Capital of the World--where our hardworking cowboys and 
plowboys feed, power, and clothe this great country and the entire 
world. However, the greatest contribution we make as a community is not 
our ag and energy product. It's our people and their values of hard 
work, faith, and love of neighbor--exemplified in Stanton, Texas, by 
Martin County Hospital District.
  Despite being a small community of about 3,000 residents, Stanton 
boasts an award-winning hospital recently recognized as one of the Top 
20 Critical Access Hospitals (CAHs) in 2023 and 2024. With over 1,300 
critical access hospitals nationwide, we take great pride in having one 
of the best for back-to-back years.
  I would like to commend Martin County Hospital District CEO Nancy 
Cooke who said, ``Our results as a top patient care hospital means our 
community can count on us to deliver the services they need now and in 
the future.''
  Our great country does not reap the benefits of energy independence 
and food security without excellent rural health care keeping our hard-
working, freedom-loving workers in Rural America healthy and taken care 
of. Nobody understands this better than Martin County Medical Hospital 
District.
  On behalf of the 19th Congressional District of Texas and untold 
grateful patients and families, I thank them, God bless them, and go 
West Texas.

                          ____________________
```
