# RECOGNIZING NATIONAL EDUCATION WEEK OF 2024

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
              RECOGNIZING NATIONAL EDUCATION WEEK OF 2024

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. HARRIS. Mr. Speaker, it is my privilege to recognize all who 
contribute to Education in anticipation of National Education Week 
2024.
  American Education Week started as an effort to better generate 
public support for education as a joint effort between the National 
Education Association (NEA) and the American Legion. Concerned with as 
much as 25 percent of World War I draftees being illiterate, the two 
organizations sought to shine a national light on the need to increase 
support for education. 103 years later, educators around the country 
continue this advocacy which I am proud to support.
  Maryland's First Congressional district has over 200 schools at all 
levels of our education system, all of which with a dedicated team of 
teachers and support staff committed to bringing out the best in our 
next generation. As we celebrate the upcoming 103rd National Education 
week between November 18th through November 22nd, let us all take a 
moment to express our gratitude for their service to our communities.

                          ____________________
```
