# HONORING PAUL CARVER

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                          HONORING PAUL CARVER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my honor to recognize Paul Carver, the Chairman of 
Republican Legislative District 2, which is in the north central area 
of Phoenix and has a population of over 246,000.
  As chairman, Paul has worked tirelessly to educate, train, and 
inspire the next generation of citizens through civic engagement. His 
efforts to ensure Republicans are elected in Maricopa County have been 
instrumental to the success of Legislative District 2 and beyond.
  I am thankful for Paul's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate his service to the legislative 
district and to the state of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
