# HONORING NORTHERN MICHIGAN CHILDREN'S ASSESSMENT CENTER ON ITS 10TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Bergman, Jack (B001301) [R-MI]

```
  HONORING NORTHERN MICHIGAN CHILDREN'S ASSESSMENT CENTER ON ITS 10TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate Northern 
Michigan Children's Assessment Center on 10 years of dedicated service 
to the community. Northern Michigan Children's Assessment Center 
(NMCAC) provides services for abused children in areas all across the 
region, including Crawford, Iosco, Ogemaw, Oscoda, Otsego, and 
Roscommon counties. Since 2014, NMCAC has been a compassionate voice 
for those most vulnerable in our society. Under the leadership of 
Executive Director and Founder Rebecca Yuncker, NMCAC has transformed 
from a one-person operation into one of the greatest advocacy 
organizations in the state, offering counseling, advocacy, forensic 
interviewing and medical services for both children and adults. With 
locations in both Tawas City and Roscommon, this diverse group of 
professionals have dedicated their lives to the noble undertaking of 
improving the lives of those who desperately need it most. They are a 
great example of servant leadership and I'm grateful for their selfless 
devotion to this cause. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate 
Northern Michigan Children's Assessment Center on 10 years of dedicated 
service to our communities and state.
```
