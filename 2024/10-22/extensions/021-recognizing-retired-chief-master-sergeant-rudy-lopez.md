# RECOGNIZING RETIRED CHIEF MASTER SERGEANT RUDY LOPEZ

**Speaking:** McMorris Rodgers, Cathy (M001159) [R-WA]

```
          RECOGNIZING RETIRED CHIEF MASTER SERGEANT RUDY LOPEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. CATHY McMORRIS RODGERS

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mrs. RODGERS of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
retired Chief Master Sergeant Rudy Lopez for his distinguished military 
service and commitment to serving Washington's veterans and military 
families as the Director of the Washington State Veterans Cemetery in 
Medical Lake, Wash.
  Retired CMSgt. Rudy Lopez first entered the Air Force as a pharmacy 
technician in 1983 where he worked in a variety of settings--from small 
clinic pharmacies to 50-bed hospital pharmacies--at McClellan Air Force 
Base and Eglin Air Force Base. During this time in the field, he 
notably led an Air Force beta test on pharmacy automation that was 
successfully implemented throughout the Department of Defense within 3 
years of proof of concept.
  In 2001, Lopez was selected for promotion to Chief Master Sergeant 
and transitioned out of pharmacy to serve as a Medical Group 
Superintendent--a role in which he would later deploy with the 332 
Expeditionary Medical Group to Balad Air Base in 2007. CMSgt. Lopez 
also spent five years serving in a dual capacity as the Air Force 
Pharmacy Career Field Manager, advising the Air Force Surgeon General 
on the training, utilization, and career progression for more than 
1,300 pharmacy technicians around the world.
  In 2013, CMSgt. Lopez ended his illustrious three-decades-long career 
in Washington's Fifth Congressional District after serving three years 
as the 92nd Air Refueling Wing Command Chief Master Sergeant at 
Fairchild Air Force Base. Throughout his decorated career, Lopez earned 
the Meritorious Service Medal with six oak leaf clusters, Air Force 
Commendation Medal with an oak leaf cluster, an Iraq Campaign Medal 
with one service star, and many others.
  Shortly after retiring from the Air Force, Rudy joined the Washington 
Department of Veterans Affairs as Director of the Washington State 
Veterans Cemetery in Medical Lake. In this role, he is responsible for 
the strategic planning, coordination, and oversight of administrative 
and operational functions of the Washington State Veterans Cemeteries 
Program, which includes cemetery locations across the state. He and his 
team diligently serve the state's 554,000 veterans by providing more 
than 900 dignified and honorable interment services to our nation's 
heroes each year.
  He and his team are responsible for playing an active role in the 
research and recovery of well over 800 unclaimed or abandoned veterans' 
remains, all of whom receive a long overdue and proper final resting 
place in recognition of their service to our Nation. As if that weren't 
enough, retired CMSgt. Lopez also serves as an advisor on veteran's 
burial benefits state-wide, provides much-needed outreach to 
communities across Eastern Washington and Northern Idaho, and serves on 
a number of civic non-profit advisory boards and committees.
  Mr. Speaker, I am grateful for retired CMSgt. Rudy Lopez's continued 
commitment to serving his country and community here in Eastern 
Washington. His dedication to honoring our nation's heroes is second to 
none, so I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing him for his 
tremendous efforts and celebrating the lasting legacy they will have on 
the lives he has touched.
```
