# HONORING THE USDA'S CENTENNIAL GRAPEVINE BREEDING PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
       HONORING THE USDA'S CENTENNIAL GRAPEVINE BREEDING PROGRAM

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. COSTA. Mr. Speaker, today we gather to celebrate an extraordinary 
milestone in American agricultural history, an occasion of profound 
significance--the 100th anniversary of the USDA Grapevine Breeding 
Research Program. This monumental event offers a unique opportunity to 
reflect upon a century of innovation, dedication, and visionary 
research that has shaped the grape industry in the United States.
  The story of the USDA Grapevine Breeding Research Program commenced 
in 1923 within the fertile soils of Fresno, California. Founded

[[Page E1054]]

at the USDA Experiment Vineyard, it became an integral part of the USDA 
Bureau of Plant Industry. Its mission was clear--to redefine the grape 
industry through groundbreaking research.
  In 1972, the program found a new home within the Agricultural 
Research Service (ARS), the USDA's premier in-house research agency. 
Throughout its remarkable journey, the program remained unwavering in 
its commitment to developing high-quality, disease-resistant cultivars 
of Vitis, spanning table grapes and raisins.
  The San Joaquin Valley Agricultural Sciences Center (SJVASC) has been 
the hub of innovation for this program. Its dedicated researchers have 
consistently led the charge in scientific advancements within the field 
of grape breeding. Their efforts have not only met the demands of U.S. 
agriculture but have also supplied top-tier, nutritious fruits to both 
national and international markets.
  As we convene to mark this momentous centenary, we acknowledge the 
program's profound influence. For one hundred years, it has been a 
pillar of grape production and innovation, perfectly situated in the 
heart of American grape cultivation, the Central Valley of California.
  The 100th anniversary ceremony is not merely retrospective; it is a 
glimpse into the future. Their collective wisdom and vision will chart 
the course of grapevine breeding in the years to come. This anniversary 
of the USDA Grapevine Breeding Research Program is a momentous occasion 
that deserves our highest regard. It celebrates a century of scientific 
excellence, unwavering dedication, and the pursuit of a resilient and 
prosperous grape industry.
  Mr. Speaker, I respectfully invite my esteemed colleagues to join me 
in commemorating the 100th anniversary of the USDA's grapevine breeding 
research program. Let us come together to honor a century of dedication 
to the growth and prosperity of American agriculture.
```
