# CELEBRATING 75 YEARS WITH SANDIA NATIONAL LABORATORIES

**Speaking:** Swalwell, Eric (S001193) [D-CA]

```
         CELEBRATING 75 YEARS WITH SANDIA NATIONAL LABORATORIES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIC SWALWELL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 22, 2024

  Mr. SWALWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Sandia National 
Laboratories (Sandia) which will celebrate 75 years of outstanding 
innovation and dedication to advancing the frontiers of science and 
technology today, October 22, 2024.
  Sandia was established in 1949 in Albuquerque, New Mexico as a key 
component of the United States' commitment to developing and 
maintaining a safe and secure nuclear deterrent. Sandia has since 
evolved into a premier multidisciplinary research institution dedicated 
to solving the nation's most challenging problems. The dedicated 
scientists, engineers and staff at Sandia have exemplified the highest 
standards of professionalism and innovation, contributing to the 
security and well-being of our nation and enhancing the quality of life 
for people around the world.
  In 1956, Sandia opened its second site in California's Livermore 
Valley, growing from a small group of scientists to what is now over 
1,600 staff, along with 300 contractors, postdoctoral fellows, and 
students, broadening the scope of the work and playing a vital role in 
Sandia's overall mission. Sandia has fostered a culture of 
collaboration and excellence, working closely with neighboring Lawrence 
Livermore National Lab, government agencies, industry partners, and 
academic institutions to address complex technical challenges and 
deliver groundbreaking solutions.
  Sandia's impact extends beyond their immediate research endeavors, 
inspiring future generations of scientists and engineers, and playing a 
pivotal role in shaping the technological landscape of our nation.
  For the past 75 years, Sandia has demonstrated unparalleled 
excellence in engineering, research and development, driving 
advancements in areas such as renewable energy, cybersecurity, and 
advanced manufacturing. I join the past and present teams of scientists 
and supporting staff at Sandia National Laboratories in celebrating the 
legacy they've created and wish them many more productive years to 
come.
```
