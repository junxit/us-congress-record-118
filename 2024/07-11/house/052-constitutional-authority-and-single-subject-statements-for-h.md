# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9000

**Measures:** H.R. 9000

```
           By Mr. BEYER:
       H.R. 9000.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide tax incentives for building owners to perform 
     indoor air quality inspections and upgrades, which would make 
     workplaces cleaner, more comfortable, and healthier for 
     workers.
```
