# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9017

**Measures:** H.R. 9017

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 9017.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Mineral Leasing Act to make certain 
     adjustments to the royalty rates for leases for oil and gas 
     extraction on Federal land, and for other purposes.
```
