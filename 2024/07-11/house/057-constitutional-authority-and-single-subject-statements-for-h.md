# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9005

**Measures:** H.R. 9005

```
           By Mr. CROW:
       H.R. 9005.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       To ensure that Federal work-study funding is available for 
     students enrolled in residency programs for teachers, 
     principals, or school leaders, and for other purposes.
```
