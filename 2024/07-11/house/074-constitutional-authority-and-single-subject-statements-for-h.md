# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9022

**Measures:** H.R. 9022

```
           By Ms. STEFANIK:
       H.R. 9022.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the US Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To include pregnancy and loss of pregnancy as qualifying 
     life events under the TRICARE program and to require a study 
     on maternal health in the military health system.
```
