# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9008

**Measures:** H.R. 9008

```
           By Mr. ROBERT GARCIA of California:
       H.R. 9008.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 1; which provides Congress 
     with the power to ``lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts 
     and Excises'' in order to ``provide for the . . . general 
     Welfare of the United States.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Public health surveillance of infectious diseases in 
     wastewater
```
