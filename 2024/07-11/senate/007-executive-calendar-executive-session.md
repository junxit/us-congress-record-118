# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Kennedy, John (K000393) [R-LA]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. KENNEDY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session and that at 1:45 p.m. today the Chair 
execute the order of July 9, 2024, with respect to the Meriweather 
nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the Meriweather nomination.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of Robin 
Michelle Meriweather, of Virginia, to be a Judge of the United States 
Court of Federal Claims for a term of fifteen years.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Kansas.
```
