# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8848

**Measures:** H.R. 8848

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 8848.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power . . . To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to allow 
     for the importation of affordable and safe drugs by wholesale 
     distributors, pharmacies, and individuals.
```
