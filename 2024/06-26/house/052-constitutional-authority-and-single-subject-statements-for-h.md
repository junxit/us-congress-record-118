# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8851

**Measures:** H.R. 8851

```
           By Mr. VARGAS:
       H.R. 8851.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services to 
     facilitate naturalization services for noncitizen veterans 
     who have been removed from the United States or are 
     inadmissible.
```
