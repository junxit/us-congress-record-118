# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8847

**Measures:** H.R. 8847

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 8847.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the inclusion of the Ministry of Public 
     Security's Institute of Forensic Science of China on the 
     entity list maintained by the Bureau of Industry and Security 
     of the Department of Commerce.
```
