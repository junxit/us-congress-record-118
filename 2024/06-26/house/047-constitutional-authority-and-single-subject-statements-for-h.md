# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8846

**Measures:** H.R. 8846

```
           By Mr. NUNN of Iowa:
       H.R. 8846.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill excludes from taxpayer gross income, for income 
     tax purposes, any amount received by an individual taxpayer 
     as compensation for expenses or losses incurred from severe 
     storms, straight-line winds, and tornadoes.
```
