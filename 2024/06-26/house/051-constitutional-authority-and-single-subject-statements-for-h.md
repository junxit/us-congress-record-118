# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8850

**Measures:** H.R. 8850

```
           By Ms. STEFANIK:
       H.R. 8850.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To return the maximum monthly allowance of milk available 
     for families utilizing the Special Supplemental Nutrition 
     Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC) to previous 
     levels.
```
