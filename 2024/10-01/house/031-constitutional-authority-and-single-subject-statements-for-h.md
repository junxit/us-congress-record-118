# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9908

**Measures:** H.R. 9908

```
           By Ms. PETTERSEN:
       H.R. 9908.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Creates a three-year pilot program under USGS for states to 
     purchase and integrate wildfire satellite monitoring for 
     early detection and post-disaster recovery through public-
     private partnerships.
```
