# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9895

**Measures:** H.R. 9895

```
            By Mr. GAETZ:
        H.R. 9895.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 9 Clause 7, Article 1 Section 8 Clause 4, 
     and Article 1 Section 8 Clause 18
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To limit the availability of Federal financial assistance 
     for entities that aid or facilitate illegal immigration, and 
     for other purposes.
```
