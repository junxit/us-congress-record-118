# HONORING TAYLOR BRISTOW

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                        HONORING TAYLOR BRISTOW

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 1, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to mourn the death 
of Investigator Taylor Bristow of Carrolton, Georgia.
  On Friday, August 23, 2024, Taylor Bristow heroically laid down his 
life in the line of duty at only 30 years old.
  Taylor Bristow was born on July 2, 1994, in Fayetteville, North 
Carolina. Investigator Bristow was a dedicated family man and is 
survived by his wife and two children.
  When he wasn't out protecting and serving his community, Taylor 
enjoyed playing with his children and two dogs and cheering on the 
Georgia Bulldogs and Atlanta Braves.
  As an investigator, he was passionate about working with children and 
animal abuse cases. He wanted to help people, and was successful in 
that life goal. Our state has lost a hero, and we send our condolences 
to his family and community.

                          ____________________
```
