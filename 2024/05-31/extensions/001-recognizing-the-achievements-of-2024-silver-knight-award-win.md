# RECOGNIZING THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF 2024 SILVER KNIGHT AWARD WINNER ARIZEL CORNIEL IN THE ATHLETIC DISCIPLINE

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
RECOGNIZING THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF 2024 SILVER KNIGHT AWARD WINNER ARIZEL 
                   CORNIEL IN THE ATHLETIC DISCIPLINE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 31, 2024

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. Arizel Corniel, 
a truly exceptional individual who has been awarded the prestigious 
Silver Knight Award in athletics. This esteemed accolade is bestowed 
upon high school students in Broward and Miami-Dade County who have not 
only demonstrated outstanding academic achievement but have also made 
noteworthy contributions to their community.
  Arizel assumed the position of goalkeeper on the varsity soccer team 
at Marjory Stoneman Douglas (MSD) High School and played an integral 
role in leading her team to victory at the district championship three 
times and once at the regional championship. Due to her outstanding 
display of leadership on her team, she was given the MSD Lady Eagles 
Alhadeff Coaches Award. Alongside her dedication to athletics, she also 
devoted time to serving her community as a co-founder of Career Closet. 
She founded this organization with the help of her school's DECA 
chapter to help students obtain lightly used business attire for 
various professional endeavors. She was able to collect more than 2,000 
articles of clothing in the non-profit's first year of operations.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in recognizing Ms. Arizel Corniel 
as a remarkable student and exceptional contributor to the discipline 
of athletics.
```
