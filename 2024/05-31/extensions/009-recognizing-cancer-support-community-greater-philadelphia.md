# RECOGNIZING CANCER SUPPORT COMMUNITY GREATER PHILADELPHIA

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
       RECOGNIZING CANCER SUPPORT COMMUNITY GREATER PHILADELPHIA

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 31, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I use today to recognize an 
organization from my District and celebrate the profound impact of 
Cancer Support Community Greater Philadelphia and to advocate for the 
designation of ``Community Is Stronger Than Cancer Day.''
  Cancer Support Community Greater Philadelphia is an organization that 
uplifts and strengthens individuals impacted by cancer. With a mission 
to provide support, foster compassionate communities, and break down 
barriers to care, they have been a beacon of hope for countless 
individuals in the Greater Philadelphia Area.
  Over the past 30 years, Cancer Support Community Greater Philadelphia 
has served over 500,000 people in Eastern Pennsylvania, offering a 
comprehensive range of free programs and services. From groups and 
evidence-based counseling to educational events, health and wellness 
classes, and social gatherings, their tireless efforts have provided 
solace and support to those navigating the challenges of a cancer 
diagnosis.
  The statistics are staggering, each year, roughly 9,000 people in the 
Greater Philadelphia Area receive a new cancer diagnosis. It is 
imperative that no one facing this journey feels alone. Cancer Support 
Community Greater Philadelphia ensures that individuals and their 
families have access to the support and resources they need to navigate 
this difficult journey with dignity and resilience.
  In recognition of their unwavering dedication and the resilience of 
those impacted by cancer, I am proud to introduce this resolution. I 
call upon all members of our community to join me in celebrating the 
strength and courage of cancer survivors, patients, caregivers, and 
their loved ones. Let us honor those who have fought valiantly and 
remember those we have lost, while reaffirming our commitment to 
supporting one another through every step of the journey.
  I believe that it is important for Congress to support the 
designation of June 28, 2024, as ``Community Is Stronger Than Cancer 
Day.'' Together, let us acknowledge the power of community in 
overcoming adversity and supporting those in need.
  I encourage all individuals to learn more about Cancer Support 
Community Greater Philadelphia and the invaluable work they do.
```
