# HONORING SOUTH REDFORD SCHOOL DISTRICT'S INNOVATION CENTER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
       HONORING SOUTH REDFORD SCHOOL DISTRICT'S INNOVATION CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 31, 2024

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize the South Redford 
School District as they celebrate the grand opening of their Innovation 
Center.
  The Innovation Center proudly offers new programming for building 
trades, culinary paths, welding, health professions, and hospitality. 
This new programming will open innumerable doors to so many students 
within the district, ensuring that they find what fits their passions.
  The Center is also equipped to ensure that its students have the 
needed career preparatory skills to succeed after graduation. These 
skills include the creation of an Educational Development Plan, 
apprenticeship programs, and paid or non-paid internships. 
Additionally, the Center works to guide its students into any path they 
choose to embark on. This means that they put an important focus on 
aiding their employability or assisting them with navigating post-
secondary institutions.
  Please join me and the 12th Congressional District in celebrating the 
opening of Redford School District's Innovation Center and the 
subsequent launch of their newest programming.

                          ____________________
```
