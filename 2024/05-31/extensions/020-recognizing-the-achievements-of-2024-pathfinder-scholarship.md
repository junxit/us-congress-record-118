# RECOGNIZING THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF 2024 PATHFINDER SCHOLARSHIP FIRST PLACE WINNER EMILIA McGOVERN IN THE BUSINESS CATEGORY

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
RECOGNIZING THE ACHIEVEMENTS OF 2024 PATHFINDER SCHOLARSHIP FIRST PLACE 
            WINNER EMILIA McGOVERN IN THE BUSINESS CATEGORY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 31, 2024

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ms. Emilia 
McGovern, the first-place winner of the prestigious Pathfinder 
Scholarship in the Business category. This scholarship is awarded to 
Palm Beach and Martin County students who have displayed excellence in 
their academic and communal endeavors in a multitude of categories.
  Emilia's academic journey is a testament to her dedication and 
passion. She earned an AICE Diploma with Distinction from Cambridge 
University and College Board Hispanic Recognition, showcasing her 
academic prowess. Her leadership role in her high school's Marketing 
Academy and her commendable performance in DECA competitions, where she 
secured the 5th position in the state for Marketing Communications, 
demonstrate her commitment to her community. Her aspiration to pursue a 
degree in public relations to become a social media manager is a 
reflection of her forward-thinking mindset and her desire to make a 
positive impact.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today to recognize Ms. Emilia McGovern as 
an excellent student and notable contributor to the field of business.
```
