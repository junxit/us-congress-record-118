# HONORING SURVIVORS OF THE HOLOCAUST ON HOLOCAUST SURVIVOR DAY

**Speaking:** Moskowitz, Jared (M001217) [D-FL]

```
     HONORING SURVIVORS OF THE HOLOCAUST ON HOLOCAUST SURVIVOR DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JARED MOSKOWITZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 31, 2024

  Mr. MOSKOWITZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Holocaust 
Survivor Day. On June 4th, we come together to celebrate the survivors 
for their bravery, sacrifices, and lives.
  Holocaust survivors are our teachers, and it is our collective 
responsibility to pass on the legacy of their testimonies.
  I would like to recognize and honor the following survivors Frances 
Gaspar, 103 years old; Chaim Greenberg, 102 years old; Lucy Blicker, 
102 years old; Elaine Lefkowitz, 101 years old; Hana Altarac, 104 years 
old; Allan Feld, 105 years old; Rena Reiter, 101 years old; Rosa 
Lindenberg, 100 years old; Rose Boyarsky, 100 years old; Herman Haller, 
100 years old; Betty Eisdorfer, 100 years old; Estelle Dreese, 100 
years old; David Elefant, 100 years old; Miriam Rozenbaum, 100 years 
old; and Lena Porter, 100 years old.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Florida's 23rd Congressional District, 
please Join me in commemorating Holocaust Survivor Day. May their 
stories be forever remembered.
```
