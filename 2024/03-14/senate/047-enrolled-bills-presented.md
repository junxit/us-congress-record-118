# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 206, S. 1858

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, March 14, 2024, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 206. An act to require the Commissioner of U.S. Customs 
     and Border Protection to regularly review and update policies 
     and manuals related to inspections at ports of entry.
       S. 1858. An act to amend the Robert T. Stafford Disaster 
     Relief and Emergency Assistance Act to establish a deadline 
     for applying for disaster unemployment assistance.

                          ____________________
```
