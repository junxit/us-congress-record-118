# MEASURES REFERRED

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 6276. An act to authorize the Administrator of General 
     Services and the Director of the Office of Management and 
     Budget to identify the utilization rate of certain public 
     buildings and federally-leased space, and for other purposes;

     application or service and any other application or service 
     developed or provided by ByteDance Ltd. or an entity under 
     the control of ByteDance Ltd; to the Committee on Commerce, 
     Science, and Transportation.
```
