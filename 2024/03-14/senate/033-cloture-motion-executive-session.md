# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 463, Eumi K. Lee, of California, to 
     be United States District Judge for the Northern District of 
     California.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, Mazie K. Hirono, Alex Padilla, Margaret 
           Wood Hassan, Tim Kaine, Tammy Duckworth, Thomas R. 
           Carper, Tina Smith, Jeff Merkley, Catherine Cortez 
           Masto, Martin Heinrich, Christopher Murphy, Debbie 
           Stabenow, Brian Schatz, Chris Van Hollen.

  Mr. SCHUMER. I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory quorum calls 
for the cloture motions filed today, March 14, be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Maryland.
```
