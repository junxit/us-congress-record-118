# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, Architect and Creator of our destinies, we marvel at 
Your power, majesty, and might. From the beginning, Your grace has 
underlain the foundations of our lives, so we ask that You would lead 
us in the paths of Your purposes.
  Today, awaken in our lawmakers the ability to see the opportunities 
that exist in the challenges they face. May this knowledge motivate 
them to move forward with faith, optimism, and peace.
  Lord, show them unused resources that can be mobilized to solve 
problems and to make dreams become reality. When they experience doubts 
and uncertainties, give them the wisdom to ask for Your Guidance.
  We pray in Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
