# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8679

**Measures:** H.R. 8679

```
           By Mr. COHEN:
       H.R. 8679.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To include the Czech Republic in the list of foreign states 
     whose nationals are eligible for admission into the United 
     States as E-1 nonimmigrants if United States nationals are 
     treated similarly by the Government of the Czech Republic.
```
