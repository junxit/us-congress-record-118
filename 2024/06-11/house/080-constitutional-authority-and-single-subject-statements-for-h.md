# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8686

**Measures:** H.R. 8686

```
           By Mr. LOUDERMILK:
       H.R. 8686.

     to the following:
       Section 1 of Article I of the Constitution, Clause 18 of

       To require the Secretary of Treasury to update thresholds
```
