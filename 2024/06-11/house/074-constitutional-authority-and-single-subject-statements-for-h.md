# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8680

**Measures:** H.R. 8680

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 8680.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1
       The Congress shall have Power to . . . provide for the . . 
     . general Welfare of the United States; . . .

       This would create a grant program that would allow 
     hospitals and other medical clinics to train community mental 
     health wellness workers.
```
