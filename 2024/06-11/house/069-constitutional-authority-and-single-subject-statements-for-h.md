# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8675

**Measures:** H.R. 8675

```
           By Mr. GARBARINO:
       H.R. 8675.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires TSA, through the Federal Air Marshal 
     Service (FAMS), to conduct a study on the digital data 
     collection process for law enforcement officers who are 
     flying armed on commercial flights. The study would include 
     projected timelines for when this digital data collection 
     could begin and estimates of what would be required to 
     digitize the current system.
```
