# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8683

**Measures:** H.R. 8683

```
           By Mr. HUIZENGA:
       H.R. 8683.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, the Necessary and Proper Clause
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Defense and the Secretary of 
     State to monitor efforts by the People's Republic of China to 
     build or buy strategic foreign ports, and for other purposes.
```
