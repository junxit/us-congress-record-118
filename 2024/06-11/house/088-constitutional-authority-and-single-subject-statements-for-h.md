# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8694

**Measures:** H.R. 8694

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 8694.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would permit the flag of the United States to be 
     flown at half-staff in the event of the death of the Mayor of
```
