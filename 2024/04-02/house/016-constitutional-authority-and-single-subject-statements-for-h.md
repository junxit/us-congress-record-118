# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7857

**Measures:** H.R. 7857

```
           By Mr. FITZGERALD:
       H.R. 7857.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would codify the long-standing requirement that 
     Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac (the Enterprises) obtain a credit 
     report and each credit score on a borrower from each of the 
     three national credit bureaus when they purchase a 
     residential mortgage loan (the tri-merge requirement).
```
