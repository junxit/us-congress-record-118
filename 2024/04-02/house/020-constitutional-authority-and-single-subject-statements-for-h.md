# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7861

**Measures:** H.R. 7861

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 7861.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 5 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to 
     mint coins recognizing Paul Laurence Dunbar, with a surcharge 
     on each coin to benefit the scholarships and similar 
     activities of the Dunbar Alumni Federation.
```
