# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Majestic Father, Your glory fills the Earth. In this first month of a 
new year, manifest Your glory and might in our Nation and world.
  Arise, O Lord. Stand up against the evil that seeks to destroy 
freedom, to steal, kill, and desecrate. Rule over our world with Your 
marvelous might, using our lawmakers to do Your will on Earth even as 
it is done in Heaven.
  Eternal God, we praise You with all our hearts.
  We pray in Your wonderful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
