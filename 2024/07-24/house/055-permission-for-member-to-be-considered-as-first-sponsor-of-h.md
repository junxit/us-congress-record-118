# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 5617

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 5617

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 5617

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 5617, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Mr. McCarthy of California, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
