# Authorizing Appointment of Escort Committee (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
              Authorizing Appointment of Escort Committee

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Acting 
President pro tempore of the Senate be authorized to appoint a 
committee on the part of the Senate to join with a like committee on 
the part of the House of Representatives to escort His Excellency 
Binyamin Netanyahu, Prime Minister of Israel, into the House Chamber 
for the joint meeting on Wednesday, July 24, 2024.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
