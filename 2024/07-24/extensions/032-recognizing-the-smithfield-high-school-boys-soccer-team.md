# RECOGNIZING THE SMITHFIELD HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' SOCCER TEAM

**Speaking:** Kiggans, Jennifer A. (K000399) [R-VA]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE SMITHFIELD HIGH SCHOOL BOYS' SOCCER TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JENNIFER A. KIGGANS

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 24, 2024

  Mrs. KIGGANS of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
congratulate the Smithfield High School Boys' Soccer team on winning 
the Class 4 Region A Championship.
  As their Congresswoman, I am proud of these young athletes and their 
recent achievement. Located in Smithfield, Virginia, the Smithfield 
High School Packers were able to bring the Class 4 Region A 
Championship back home to their fellow students, faculty, families, and 
friends through their hard work and dedication to their team. The 
Smithfield Boys' Soccer team won the game two to zero and advanced to 
the state tournament.
  As a former athlete myself, I want each of these young men to know 
Virginia's 2nd District is extremely proud of their accomplishment and 
applauds them on their well-earned victory. Again, congratulations to 
the Smithfield High School Boys' Soccer team on winning the Class 4 
Region A Championship.

                          ____________________
```
