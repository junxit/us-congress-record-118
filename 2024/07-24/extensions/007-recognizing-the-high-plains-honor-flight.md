# RECOGNIZING THE HIGH PLAINS HONOR FLIGHT

**Speaking:** Lopez, Greg (L000604) [R-CO]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE HIGH PLAINS HONOR FLIGHT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG LOPEZ

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 24, 2024

  Mr. LOPEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of America's heroic 
veterans and to recognize the Honor Flight Network, which conducts 
annual trips for veterans to Washington, D.C., to tour the memorials 
built in celebration of their bravery. On April 28, 2024, High Plains 
Honor Flight gave many of our courageous veterans this extraordinary 
opportunity, flying them to our nation's capital. I am pleased to 
congratulate the April 28, 2024, High Plains Honor Flight and to 
acknowledge the Korean and Vietnam War veterans of Colorado who took 
part in it.
```
