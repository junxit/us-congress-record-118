# RECOGNIZING THE 100th BIRTHDAY OF AURELIA GONZALEZ LOPEZ

**Speaking:** Torres, Norma J. (T000474) [D-CA]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE 100th BIRTHDAY OF AURELIA GONZALEZ LOPEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. NORMA J. TORRES

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 24, 2024

  Mrs. TORRES of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
celebrate the 100th birthday of Aurelia Gonzalez Lopez.
  Aurelia was born July 25, 1924, in Buenavista, Mexico. She migrated 
to the United States in 1990, establishing a new life in the City of 
Pomona for the last 34 years. Raising her family is one of her proudest 
accomplishments. She had 12 children with her husband, and now her 
family has expanded to 43 grandchildren and 29 great-grandchildren.
  On behalf of the 35th Congressional District, I congratulate Aurelia 
on this incredible milestone. It is my honor to represent people like 
her who bring so much to family and neighbors. I am grateful for her 34 
years of residency in my district I hope that she enjoys much happiness 
in the coming year.

                          ____________________
```
