# Remembering Dexter Scott King (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Butler, Laphonza R. (B001320) [D-CA]

```
                     Remembering Dexter Scott King

  Ms. BUTLER. Madam President, just last week, we honored the legacy of 
Reverend Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. on what would have been his 95th 
birthday. Today, misfortune touches the King family again with the 
passing of Reverend King's youngest son, Dexter Scott King. America 
will forever be indebted to the sacrifices of the King family, and our 
prayers remain with them in this challenging time of loss.
```
