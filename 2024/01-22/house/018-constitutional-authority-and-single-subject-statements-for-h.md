# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7063

**Measures:** H.R. 7063

```
            By Mr. LANDSMAN:
        H.R. 7063.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit federal funds from being made available to any 
     pregnancy center that diverts people from accessing 
     comprehensive and timely medical care from licnesed medical 
     professionals.
```
