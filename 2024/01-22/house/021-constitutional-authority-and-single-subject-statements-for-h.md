# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7066

**Measures:** H.R. 7066

```
            By Mr. NEWHOUSE:
        H.R. 7066.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to prohibit the use of federal funds to allow or study the 
     breach or alteration of the Lower Snake River dams or 
     implement the Columbia Basin Restoration Initiative
```
