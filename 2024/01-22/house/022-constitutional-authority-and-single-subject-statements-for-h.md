# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7067

**Measures:** H.R. 7067

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
        H.R. 7067.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would amend the Coastal Zone Management Act of 
     1972 to allow the District of Columbia to receive federal 
     funding under such Act.
```
