# CONGRATULATIONS TO ALEDO BEARCATS FOR THEIR WIN

**Speaking:** Williams, Roger (W000816) [R-TX]

```
            CONGRATULATIONS TO ALEDO BEARCATS FOR THEIR WIN

  (Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the 
Aledo Bearcats for their win over Smithson Valley Rangers with a score 
of 51-8 to become the UIL class 5A State football champions.
  The Aledo Bearcats finished the season with a record of 16 wins and 
zero losses.
  After a season of early mornings, long hours on the field, and 
staying focused in school, these young men showed dedication, strength, 
and an unwavering commitment to victory. I am proud that all their hard 
work and dedication throughout the year has paid off.
  As these bright and talented men and women prepare for their next 
endeavors, I wish them the best of luck and blessings for an even 
brighter and more victorious future.
  Go Bearcats.
  In God we trust.

                          ____________________
```
