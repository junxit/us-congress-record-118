# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7237

**Measures:** H.R. 7237

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 7237.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would establish a grant program for the design 
     and construction of barriers to mitigate rail activity that 
     may harm homes and their occupants, including derailments, 
     noise and vibrations.
```
