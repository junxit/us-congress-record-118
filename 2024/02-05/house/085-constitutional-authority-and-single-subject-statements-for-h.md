# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7230

**Measures:** H.R. 7230

```
           By Mrs. GONZALEZ-COLON:
       H.R. 7230.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section I of the U.S. Constitution
       ``All legislative Powers herein granted shall be vested in 
     a Congress of the United States, which shall consist of a 
     Senate and a House of Representatives.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the definition of extremely low-income families 
     under the United States Housing Act of 1937.
```
