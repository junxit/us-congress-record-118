# TRIBUTE TO JUDY MURPHY

**Speaking:** Ossoff, Jon (O000174) [D-GA]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JUDY MURPHY

  Mr. OSSOFF. Mr. President, I rise to commend Judy Murphy for her 
commitment to the well-being of residents across Augusta.
● For nearly two decades, Ms. Murphy has been volunteering at 
nursing and retirement communities across the Augusta area. Her weekly 
craft class at Thrive Senior Living in Augusta is frequently attended 
because of her passion and commitment to serving her community. Ms. 
Murphy recently received WJBF Augusta's Giving Your Best award after 
being nominated by Thrive's Social Network director, Robbie Harrell. As 
a retired nurse, Ms. Murphy remains committed to a life of service and 
finds joy in the life lessons and friendships she has earned because of 
her commitment to doing good.
  As Georgia's U.S. Senator, I commend Judy Murphy for her service and 
commitment to her community.●

                          ____________________
```
