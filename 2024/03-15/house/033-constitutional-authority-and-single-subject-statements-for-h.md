# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7701

**Measures:** H.R. 7701

```
            By Mr. MEEKS:
        H.R. 7701.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation imposes sanctions on any foreign person 
     that contributes to the construction of a tunnel from the 
     Russian mainland to Crimea.
```
