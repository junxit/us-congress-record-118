# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7699

**Measures:** H.R. 7699

```
           By Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI:
       H.R. 7699.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the Constitution of the United States of 
     America
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of National Intelligence to develop 
     a strategy to improve the sharing of information and 
     intelligence on foreign adversary tactics and illicit 
     activities affecting the ability of United States persons to 
     compete in foreign jurisdictions on projects relating to 
     energy generation and storage, and for other purposes.
```
