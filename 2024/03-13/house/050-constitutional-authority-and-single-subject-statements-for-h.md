# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7649

**Measures:** H.R. 7649

```
            By Mr. BUCHANAN:
        H.R. 7649.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Immigration and Nationality to provide for the 
     detention, inadmissibility, and removal of aliens who commit 
     sexual assault.
```
