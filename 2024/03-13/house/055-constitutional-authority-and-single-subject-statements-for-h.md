# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7654

**Measures:** H.R. 7654

```
            By Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina:
        H.R. 7654.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To expand and modify the grant program of the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs to provide innovative transportation options 
     to veterans in highly rural areas.
```
