# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7659

**Measures:** H.R. 7659

```
            By Mr. GRAVES of Missouri:
        H.R. 7659.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1, Clause 3, and Clause 18 of 
     the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize and amend authorities, programs, and statutes
```
