# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7672

**Measures:** H.R. 7672

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 7672.

       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would amend title 40 of the United States Code to 
     permit commercial filmmaking and photography on the United 
     States Capitol grounds.
```
