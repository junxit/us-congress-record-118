# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7647

**Measures:** H.R. 7647

```
            By Ms. BARRAGAN:
        H.R. 7647.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill directs the Secretary of Health and Human 
     Services to provide guidance to State Medicaid agencies, 
     public housing agencies, Continuums of Care, and housing 
     finance agencies on connecting Medicaid beneficiaries with 
     housing-related services and supports under Medicaid.
```
