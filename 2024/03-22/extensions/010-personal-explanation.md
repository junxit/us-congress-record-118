**Speaking:** Trone, David J. (T000483) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 1121, H.R. 6009

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DAVID J. TRONE

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 22, 2024

  Mr. TRONE. Mr. Speaker, on March 20, 2024, I was ill and unable to

No 89 and No. 90; Yea on Roll Call No. 91 and No. 92; Nay on Roll Call

strong proponent of clean energy and the environment, I oppose H.R.

Protecting American Energy Production Act.
```
