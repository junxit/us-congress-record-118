# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7810

**Measures:** H.R. 7810

```
           By Mr. SMUCKER:
       H.R. 7810.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section VIII of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 to clarify the 
     clock hour requirements for certain eligible programs under 
     title IV of such Act.
```
