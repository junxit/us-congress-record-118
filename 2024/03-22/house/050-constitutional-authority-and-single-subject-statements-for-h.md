# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7797

**Measures:** H.R. 7797

```
           By Mr. CARTER of Georgia:
       H.R. 7797.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the Constitution of the 
     United States which gives Congress the power to regulate 
     interstate commerce.

     restoration research and development.
```
