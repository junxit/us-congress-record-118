# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7803

**Measures:** H.R. 7803

```
           By Mr. ISSA:
       H.R. 7803.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill includes a technical correction for certain 
     penalty provisions contained in Title 35. The amendments 
     provide the Director of the United States Patent and 
     Trademark Office with authorities necessary not to impose 
     certain punitive fines where an entity can prove it acted in 
     good faith when it falsely asserted or certified that it 
     qualified for certain reduced fee programs.
```
