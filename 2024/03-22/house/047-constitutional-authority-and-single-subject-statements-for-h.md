# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7794

**Measures:** H.R. 7794

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 7794.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Public Safety Officers' Benefits Program to 
     include certain retired law enforcement officers in the 
     public safety officers' death benefits program.
```
