# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7801

**Measures:** H.R. 7801

```
           By Mr. CRAWFORD:
       H.R. 7801.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:

[[Page H1499]]

       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins to 
     honor and memorialize the tragedy of the Sultana steamboat 
     explosion of 1865.
```
