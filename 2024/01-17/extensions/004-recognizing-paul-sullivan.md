# RECOGNIZING PAUL SULLIVAN

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING PAUL SULLIVAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Paul Sullivan, 
owner of Esters Pub for receiving the Wheat Ridge Business 
Association's 2023 Reinvestment Award.
  The Reinvestment Award recognizes businesses that have demonstrated 
their commitment and investment in making Wheat Ridge a wonderful place 
to live, work, and play.
  Congratulations to Paul Sullivan for his contribution to the Wheat 
Ridge community and for receiving this award.
```
