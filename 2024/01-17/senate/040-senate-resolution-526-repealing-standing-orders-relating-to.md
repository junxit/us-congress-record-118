# SENATE RESOLUTION 526--REPEALING STANDING ORDERS RELATING TO FLOWERS IN THE SENATE CHAMBER

**Measures:** S.Res. 221, S.Res. 526

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 526--REPEALING STANDING ORDERS RELATING TO FLOWERS IN 
                           THE SENATE CHAMBER

  Mrs. FISCHER (for herself and Ms. Klobuchar) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 526

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. REPEAL OF RESTRICTION ON FLOWERS.

       (a) In General.--Senate Resolution 284 (58th Congress), 
     agreed to February 24, 1905, is repealed.
       (b) Conforming Repeal.--Senate Resolution 221 (98th 
     Congress), agreed to September 15, 1983, is repealed.

                          ____________________
```
