# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10470

**Measures:** H.R. 10470

```
           By Mr. BEYER:
       H.R. 10470.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit certain former Federal officials from investing 
     in or serving in a managerial role in an investment fund in 
     which a foreign principal owns shares within a certain time 
     period if such investment or managerial role is based on 
     conversations between such former officials and such foreign 
     principal while such former official was employed by the 
     Federal Government
```
