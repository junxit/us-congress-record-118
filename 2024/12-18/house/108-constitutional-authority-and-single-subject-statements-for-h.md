# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10484

**Measures:** H.R. 10484

```
            By Mr. GOTTHEIMER:
        H.R. 10484.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The SEARCH Act directs the Secretary of Health and Human 
     Services to carry out a public awareness campaign to increase 
     participation by women in clinical trials that are conducted 
     or supported by the National Institutes of Health.
```
