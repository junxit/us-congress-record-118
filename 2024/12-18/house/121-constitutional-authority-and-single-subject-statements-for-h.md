# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10497

**Measures:** H.R. 10497

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 10497.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for certain rules for housing or transportation 
     of prisoners in the Federal Bureau of Prisons based on sex 
     and to provide for a limitation on gender-related medical 
     treatment.
```
