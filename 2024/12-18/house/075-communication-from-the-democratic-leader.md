# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                December 18, 2024.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: Pursuant to section 1092(b)(2) of the 
     James M. Inhofe National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal 
     Year 2023 (Public Law 117-263), I am pleased to announce the 
     joint appointment, together with the Majority Leader of the 
     Senate, of the Honorable Filemon Vela of Houston, Texas to 
     serve as a Co-Chairperson of the Commission on the Future of 
     the Navy.

[[Page H7349]]

       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
