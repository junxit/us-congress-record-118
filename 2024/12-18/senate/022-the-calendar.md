**Speaking:** Wicker, Roger F. (W000437) [R-MS]

  Mr. WICKER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of the following bills en bloc: 
Calendar No. 588, S. 2620, Chesapeake National Recreation Area Act, and 
Calendar No. 636, S. 4994, Vicksburg National Military Park Boundary 
Modification.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bills 
en bloc.
