# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9354

**Measures:** H.R. 9354

```
            By Ms. PORTER:
        H.R. 9354.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make certain 
     temporary provisions relating to the child tax credit 
     permanent, to make such credit fully refundable, and to 
     increase the corporate tax rate.
```
