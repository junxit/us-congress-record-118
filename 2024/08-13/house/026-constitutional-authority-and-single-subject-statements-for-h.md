# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9359

**Measures:** H.R. 9359

```
            By Mr. VAN DREW:
        H.R. 9359.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the United States 
     Constitution, which provides Congress the power to lay and 
     collect taxes, duties, imposts, and excises, to pay the debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Repeals the gross inclusion of social security benefits in 
     adjusted gross income.
```
