# HONORING CITY OF MISSION VIEJO CITY MANAGER DENNIS WILBERG

**Speaking:** Kim, Young (K000397) [R-CA]

```
       HONORING CITY OF MISSION VIEJO CITY MANAGER DENNIS WILBERG

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. YOUNG KIM

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 26, 2024

  Mrs. KIM of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise to thank Mission Viejo 
City Manager Dennis Wilberg for his decades of service to our Mission 
Viejo community and to Orange County.
  His work in the public and private sectors of civil and 
transportation engineering, including 20 years as City Manager, has 
left an enormous impact across Mission Viejo and the County.
  From helping create the second-largest animal shelter in Orange 
County and building a water treatment facility, to increasing public 
safety and maintaining the City's transportation network, there's no 
question how he has become the longest-tenured city manager in Orange 
County.
  I thank him for his dedication to keeping our communities safe and 
connected.
  We are grateful for his leadership, and I wish him a wonderful 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
