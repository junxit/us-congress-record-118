# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10523

**Measures:** H.R. 10523

```
           By Mr. CURTIS:
       H.R. 10523.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The President, through the Federal Emergency Management 
     Agency (FEMA), shall be required to declare a wildfire as a 
     catastrophic wildfire for relief to be available under this 
     Act.
```
