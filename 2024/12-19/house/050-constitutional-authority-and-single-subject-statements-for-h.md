# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10528

**Measures:** H.R. 10528

```
            By Ms. HAGEMAN:
        H.R. 10528.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Atricle I Section VIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Exempts Federal actions related to energy and mineral 
     activities on certain Federal lands from the requirements of 
     the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969.
```
