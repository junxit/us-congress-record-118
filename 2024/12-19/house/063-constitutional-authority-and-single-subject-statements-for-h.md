# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10541

**Measures:** H.R. 10541

```
            By Mr. SWALWELL:
        H.R. 10541.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution, 
     specifically Clause 18 (relating to the power to make all 
     laws necessary and proper for carrying out the powers vested 
     in congress).
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to limit 
     the deferral of gain for officers of the executive branch of 
     the Federal Government in the case of the sale of property to 
     comply with conflict-of-interest requirements.
```
