# GEORGE HENRY WHITE POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 7607

```
                GEORGE HENRY WHITE POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 7607) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at Block 1025, Lots 18 & 19, Northeast Corner of 
US Route 9 South and Main Street in the Township of Middle, County of 
Cape May, New Jersey, as the ``George Henry White Post Office 
Building'' was ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and 
passed.
```
