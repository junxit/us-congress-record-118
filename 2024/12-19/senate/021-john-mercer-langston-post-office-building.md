# JOHN MERCER LANGSTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 7385

```
               JOHN MERCER LANGSTON POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 7385) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 29 Franklin Street in Petersburg, Virginia, 
as the ``John Mercer Langston Post Office Building'' was ordered to a
```
