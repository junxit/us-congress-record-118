# LABRUCE `BRUCE' TIDWELL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 6162

```
              LABRUCE `BRUCE' TIDWELL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 6162) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 379 North Oates Street in Dothan, Alabama, as 
the ``LaBruce `Bruce' Tidwell Post Office Building'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
