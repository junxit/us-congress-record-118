                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. BENNET. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
consider the following nominations en bloc: Calendar Nos. 859, 860, 
861, 862, 863, 864, 865, 866, 867, 868, 869, 871, 873, 874, 530, and 
all nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Coast 
Guard, Navy, and Space Force; that the nominations be confirmed en 
bloc; that the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions 
be in order to any of the nominations; and that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action.
