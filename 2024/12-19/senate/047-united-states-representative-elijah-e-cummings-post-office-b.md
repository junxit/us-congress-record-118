# UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE ELIJAH E. CUMMINGS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 9544

```
  UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE ELIJAH E. CUMMINGS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 9544) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 340 South Loudon Avenue in Baltimore, 
Maryland, as the ``United States Representative Elijah E. Cummings Post 
Office Building'' was ordered to a third reading, was read the third
```
