# COLONEL JOSEPH WILLIAM KITTINGER II POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 5867

```
        COLONEL JOSEPH WILLIAM KITTINGER II POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 5867) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 109 Live Oaks Boulevard in Casselberry, 
Florida, as the ``Colonel Joseph William Kittinger II Post Office 
Building'' was ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and
```
