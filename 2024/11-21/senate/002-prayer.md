# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, our Father, thank You for the gifts You have given to us 
and to all humanity. Thank You for the loveliness of earth and sea and 
sky. Thank You for the beauty You have enabled the minds and hands of 
people to conceive. Thank You for a nation conceived in liberty and 
dedicated to freedom. Thank You for enabling us to harness the forces 
of nature to find ways of defeating disease and easing pain. Thank You 
for lawmakers who seek to enable us to live in peace and quietness, in 
godliness and dignity. Lord of us all, to You we raise these our 
prayers of grateful praise.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
