# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10215

**Measures:** H.R. 10215

```
           By Mr. GARCIA of Illinois:
       H.R. 10215.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill facilitates voter registration for eligible and 
     interested tenants of certain federally assisted housing for 
     the purpose of federal elections.
```
