# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10246

**Measures:** H.R. 10246

```
           By Ms. VELAZQUEZ:
       H.R. 10246.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress shall have Power . . . To regulate Commerce 
     with Foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill improves SBA's loan guaranty program for small 
     manufacturers in order to make it easier for them to access 
     capital.
```
