# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10237

**Measures:** H.R. 10237

```
           By Mrs. RAMIREZ:
       H.R. 10237.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would require the Commissioner of U.S. 
     Customs and Border Protection to develop and disseminate 
     guidance for the handling of personal property of individuals 
     who are under arrest, restrained, or confined by U.S. Customs 
     and Border Protection, and for other purposes.
```
