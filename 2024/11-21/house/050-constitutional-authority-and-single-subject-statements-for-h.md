# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10195

**Measures:** H.R. 10195

```
           By Mr. GOOD of Virginia:
       H.R. 10195.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section VIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the low-income housing tax credit from being
```
