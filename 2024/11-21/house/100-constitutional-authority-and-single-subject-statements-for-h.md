# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10245

**Measures:** H.R. 10245

```
           By Ms. VELAZQUEZ:
       H.R. 10245.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress shall have Power . . . To regulate Commerce 
     with Foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill amends the Small Business Investment Act of 1958 
     to allow the SBA Administrator to increase the amount of 
     commitments of guaranteed loans to qualified State or local 
     development companies.
```
