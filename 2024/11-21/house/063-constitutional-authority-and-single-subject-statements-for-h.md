# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10208

**Measures:** H.R. 10208

```
           By Ms. CHU:
       H.R. 10208.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill authorizes the Department of Education to award 
     grants to states and, through them, subgrants to consortia of 
     local educational agencies and community partners to address 
     school readiness and achievement. Subgrants may be used for 
     activities including early learning, academic support 
     services, parent-education programs, and teacher training.
```
