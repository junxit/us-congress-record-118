# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10217

**Measures:** H.R. 10217

```
           By Ms. HAGEMAN:
       H.R. 10217.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to require all aliens applying for a nonimmigrant visa who 
     are unlawfully present in the United States to submit to an 
     in person interview with a consular officer
```
