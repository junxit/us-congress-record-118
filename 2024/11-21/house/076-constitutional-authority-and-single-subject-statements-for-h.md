# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10221

**Measures:** H.R. 10221

```
           By Mr. HUIZENGA:
       H.R. 10221.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Public Works and Economic Development Act of 
     1965 with respect to the eligibility of youth sports 
     facilities for certain grants, and for other purposes.
```
