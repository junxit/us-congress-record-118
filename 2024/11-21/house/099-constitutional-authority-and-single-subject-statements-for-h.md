# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10244

**Measures:** H.R. 10244

```
           By Ms. VELAZQUEZ:
       H.R. 10244.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress shall have Power . . . To regulate Commerce 
     with Foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires the SBA to improve outreach and education on 
     cooperatives and employee-owned businesses through Federal 
     agencies and offices.
```
