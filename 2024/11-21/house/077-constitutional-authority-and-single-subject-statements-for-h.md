# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10222

**Measures:** H.R. 10222

```
           By Mr. ISSA:
       H.R. 10222.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 with respect to 
     the application of the excise tax on tobacco to pipe and 
     waterpipe tobacco.
```
