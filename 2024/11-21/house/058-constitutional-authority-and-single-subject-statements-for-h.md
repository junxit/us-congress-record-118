# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10203

**Measures:** H.R. 10203

```
           By Mr. BUCHANAN:
       H.R. 10203.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 7, Clause 1, also known as the 
     Origination Clause. It states:
       ``All Bills for raising Revenue shall originate in the 
     House of Representatives; but the Senate may propose or 
     concur with Amendments as on other Bills,''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Hurricane Helene and Milton Tax Relief Act of 2024
```
