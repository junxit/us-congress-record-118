# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9517

**Measures:** H.R. 9517

```
           By Mr. COURTNEY:
       H.R. 9517.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Education to deem each month for 
     which certain Federal student loans are in deferment during a 
     period of active duty service as months counted toward public 
     service loan forgiveness, and for other purposes.
```
