# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9533

**Measures:** H.R. 9533

```
           By Mr. WESTERMAN:
       H.R. 9533.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Endangered Species Act of 1973 to optimize 
     conservation through resource prioritization, incentivize 
     wildlife conservation on private lands, provide for greater 
     incentives to recover listed species, create greater 
     transparency and accountability in recovering listed species, 
     and limit reasonable and prudent measures.
```
