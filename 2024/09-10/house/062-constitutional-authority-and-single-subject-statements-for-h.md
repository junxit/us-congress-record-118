# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9515

**Measures:** H.R. 9515

```
           By Mr. CALVERT:
       H.R. 9515.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States grants Congress the authority to enact this bill.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish an interest-bearing account for the non-
     Federal contributions to the Lower Colorado River Multi-
     Species Conservation Program, and for other purposes
```
