# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9520

**Measures:** H.R. 9520

```
           By Mr. FALLON:
       H.R. 9520.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the National Cyber Director to submit to 
     Congress a plan to establish an institute within the Federal 
     Government to serve as a centralized resource and training 
     center for Federal cyber workforce development.
```
