# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8445

**Measures:** H.R. 8445

```
           By Mr. RESCHENTHALER:
       H.R. 8445.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, and the 
     Servicemembers Civil Relief Act to provide for the eligbility 
     of United States citizens who serve in the Israeli Defense 
     Forces for certain protections relating to such service.
```
