# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 150

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 150

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
       H.J. Res. 150.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Environmental Protection Agency relating to ``National 
     Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: Coal- and 
     Oil-Fired Electric Utility Steam Generating Units Review of 
     the Residual Risk and Technology Review''.
```
