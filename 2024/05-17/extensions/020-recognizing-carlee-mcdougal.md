# RECOGNIZING CARLEE McDOUGAL

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING CARLEE McDOUGAL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Carlee McDougal 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Carlee has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Carlee, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Carlee's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Ralston Valley High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Carlee McDougal on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
