# RECOGNIZING ANNETTE ALAZAE REYES

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                    RECOGNIZING ANNETTE ALAZAE REYES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Annette Alazae 
Reyes for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Annette has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Annette, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Annette's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Standley Lake High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Annette Alazae Reyes on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
