# COMMEMORATING ARMED FORCES DAY AND THE MEN AND WOMEN WHO SERVE IN THE UNITED STATES MILITARY

**Speaking:** Langworthy, Nicholas A. (L000600) [R-NY]

```
 COMMEMORATING ARMED FORCES DAY AND THE MEN AND WOMEN WHO SERVE IN THE 
                         UNITED STATES MILITARY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 17, 2024

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
service of our brave men and women for Armed Forces Day, May 18, 2024. 
Armed Forces Day is a seventy-four-year tradition to thank all of those 
who are currently serving, as well as those who have served and 
sacrificed to defend our freedom. It is because of their duty, honor, 
and courage that the United States of America remains free today.
  This Saturday, the Western New York Armed Forces Committee will honor 
twelve service men and women at its 61st Military Ball, and it is my 
honor to include in the Record their names as a tribute to their 
service, and the service of their brothers and sisters in arms:
  Staff Sergeant Martin Gutierrez--U.S. ARMY
  Staff Sergeant Richard Hy--U.S. ARMY RESERVE
  Technical Sergeant Andrew D. Lindaman--U.S. AIR FORCE
  Technical Sergeant Brian P. Nosbisch--U.S. AIR FORCE RESERVE
  Sergeant Ruby D. Wingo--NEW YORK ARMY NATIONAL GUARD
  Master Sergeant Seth D. Sampson--NEW YORK NATIONAL GUARD
  Specialist Jazon Krauze--NEW YORK GUARD
  Sergeant Tiffany Nowicki--U.S. MARINE CORPS
  Staff Sergeant Landon Suber--U.S. MARINE CORPS RESERVE
  Petty Officer First Class Pierce Schrecengast--U.S. COAST GUARD
  Petty Officer First Class Nolan M. Kinne--U.S. COAST GUARD RESERVES
  Lance Corporal James J. Ward--NEW YORK NAVAL MILITIA
  Western New York and the Southern Tier are home to many proud service 
members and veterans, and our community has a long and proud tradition 
of military service. It is my honor to recognize their bravery. On 
behalf of the United States Congress and the constituents of New York's 
23rd Congressional District, I thank these men and women for their 
service and all those who continue to serve with them.

                          ____________________
```
