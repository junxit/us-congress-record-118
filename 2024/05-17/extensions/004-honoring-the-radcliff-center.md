# HONORING THE RADCLIFF CENTER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                      HONORING THE RADCLIFF CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 17, 2024

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize the Radcliff 
Center, Garden City's recreation hub, as they celebrate the grand 
opening of their newly renovated space and newest facilities, the Teen 
Tech Room and the E-Sports Arena.
  The Radcliff Center is a place where a community can thrive with no 
bounds The extensive resources the Center offers, along with the events 
it hosts, cater to all ages and numerous interests, making for an 
incredible place where residents can come together socially. 
Additionally, the center houses community support resources such as the 
Food Pantry, Tax Assistance, and Veteran Services. These services are 
the backbone of the community. But, most importantly, they ensure that 
our residents feel comfortable and cared for.
  The Radcliff Center is proud to debut these new spaces to the public 
to increase community engagement and social reach. The Teen Tech Room 
serves as a gateway for teens to come together and establish peer-to-
peer connections. Likewise, the E-Sports Arena strives to accomplish 
the same goals within the bounds of electronic sports.
  Please join me and the 12th Congressional District in celebrating the 
opening of Garden City's revamped Radcliff Center and its two newest 
resources.

                          ____________________
```
