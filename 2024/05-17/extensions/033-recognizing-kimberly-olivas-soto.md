# RECOGNIZING KIMBERLY OLIVAS SOTO

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                    RECOGNIZING KIMBERLY OLIVAS SOTO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kimberly Olivas 
Soto for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth 
Award.
  Kimberly has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Kimberly, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Kimberly's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Standley Lake High School and is clearly just the 
beginning of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Kimberly Olivas Soto on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
