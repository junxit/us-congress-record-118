# RECOGNIZING CAMI WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING CAMI WILLIAMS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 17, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Cami Williams 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Cami has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Cami, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Cami's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Ralston Valley High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Cami Williams on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
