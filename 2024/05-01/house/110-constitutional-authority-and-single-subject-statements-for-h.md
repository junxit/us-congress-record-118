# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements, for H.R. 8219

**Measures:** H.R. 8219

```
           By Ms. TOKUDA:
       H.R. 8219.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States

       Authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to conduct a
```
