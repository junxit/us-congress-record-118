# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements, for H.R. 8195

**Measures:** H.R. 8195

```
           By Mr. BERGMAN:
       H.R. 8195.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a budget neutral requirement for discretionary 
     administrative actions of the executive branch that affect 
     direct spending.
```
