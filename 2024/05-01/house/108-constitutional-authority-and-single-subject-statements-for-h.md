# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements, for H.R. 8217

**Measures:** H.R. 8217

```
           By Mr. SMUCKER:
       H.R. 8217.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section VIII of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill provides for a special enrollment period for 
     Medicare medical benefits for individuals who are enrolled in 
     COBRA continuation coverage at the time they qualify for 
     Medicare.
```
