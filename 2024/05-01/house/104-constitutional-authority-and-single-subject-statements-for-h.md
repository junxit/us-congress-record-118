# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements, for H.R. 8213

**Measures:** H.R. 8213

```
           By Mr. PAPPAS:
       H.R. 8213.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 23, United States Code, to provide for a 
     national standard to prevent driving while intoxicated by 
     requiring ignition interlocks for DWI offenders.
```
