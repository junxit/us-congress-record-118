# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements, for H.R. 8209

**Measures:** H.R. 8209

```
           By Mr. HUIZENGA:
       H.R. 8209.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, the Necessary and Proper Clause
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Homeland Security to notify 
     Members of Congress and United States Governors each time a 
     migrant flight lands in such official's area of jurisdiction.
```
