# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements, for H.R. 8215

**Measures:** H.R. 8215

```
           By Mr. PERRY:
       H.R. 8215.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To exempt certain vessels transporting liquefied natural 
     gas from certain coastwise endorsement requirements.
```
