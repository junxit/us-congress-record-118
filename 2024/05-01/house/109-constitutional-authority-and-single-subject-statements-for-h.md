# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements, for H.R. 8218

**Measures:** H.R. 8218

```
           By Mr. TIMMONS:
       H.R. 8218.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the District of Columbia Home Rule Act to require 
     any individual who votes in a municipal election of the 
     District of Columbia to be a United States citizen and to 
     provide proof of citizenship.
```
