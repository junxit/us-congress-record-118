# CELEBRATING NATIONAL SMALL BUSINESS WEEK

**Speaking:** Lee, Susie (L000590) [D-NV]

```
                CELEBRATING NATIONAL SMALL BUSINESS WEEK

  (Ms. LEE of Nevada asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute.)
  Ms. LEE of Nevada. Mr. Speaker, I rise to extend my personal 
congratulations to all the local entrepreneurs, the communities they 
support, and the Nevada Small Business Administration at their annual 
awards luncheon celebrating National Small Business Week.
  From restaurants to wrestling gyms, these small businesses are the 
life of our local economy. As our community grows beyond the reputation 
as the entertainment capital of the world, one

[[Page H2784]]

thing will always remain true: We are a people-driven economy.
  Investing in our economy to make it more resilient and diverse 
requires investing in the people who keep it running. I thank all the 
small business owners, their employees, and hardworking families who 
make our community strong. Happy Small Business Week.

                          ____________________
```
