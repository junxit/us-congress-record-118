# TRIBUTE TO ANNE SHADDOCK

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ANNE SHADDOCK

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Anne Shaddock, a spring 
2024 intern with my Jacksonville office, for the hard work she has done 
for my office and the people of Florida.
  Anne is pursuing a bachelor of science in communications with a 
public relations emphasis alongside a minor in nutrition at the 
University of North Florida. She is a dedicated and diligent worker who 
was devoted to getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Anne, and I look forward to hearing 
of her successes in the years to come.●
```
