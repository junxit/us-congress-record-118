# TRIBUTE TO SAMUEL JOSEPH COLLINS

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO SAMUEL JOSEPH COLLINS

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I recognize Samuel Joseph Collins, a 
spring 2024 intern with my Orlando office, for the hard work he has 
done for my office and the people of Florida.
  Samuel is currently studying at the University of Central Florida, 
where he is pursuing an degree in both political science and 
communication and conflict. He is a dedicated and diligent worker who 
was devoted to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my deepest gratitude to Samuel, and I look forward to 
hearing of his successes in the years to come.●
```
