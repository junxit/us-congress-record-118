# RECOGNIZING KELLY ALBRECHT

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING KELLY ALBRECHT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 20, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kelly Albrecht 
of Canon City for being inducted into the Fremont Hall of Fame.
  Throughout her distinguished 32-year career, Kelly has exemplified 
innovation in education. Over her tenure, Kelly taught fifth grade and 
elementary music and took on leadership roles, including serving as 
Canon Exploratory School middle school principal. After retiring, Kelly 
continued to shape the future of education in Fremont County as the 
Induction Coordinator and Teacher Development Coach for the Canon City 
School District. Her creative and innovative style of leadership has 
touched the lives of countless students and new teachers starting on 
their professional journeys.
  Kelly's legacy is a testament to her passion for education and her 
impact on creating a culture of learning and excellence within the 
community.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Kelly Albrecht for her accomplishment 
and thank her for her contribution to our community.

                          ____________________
```
