# RECOGNIZING DELORES COMSTOCK

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING DELORES COMSTOCK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 20, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
service of Delores Comstock and congratulate her for being inducted 
into the Fremont Hall of Fame.
  Delores' commitment to education and volunteerism has made her a 
beloved figure in the community, particularly her involvement with the 
Fremont County 4-H programs. With over 60 years of service, her work as 
an educator, Special Education volunteer, and Four Mile 4-H Club 
leader, has enriched the lives of countless individuals and exemplifies 
the spirit of community service. Delores' ongoing altruism has made a 
significant difference across Fremont County and makes her a fitting 
recipient for a spot in their Hall of Fame.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Delores Comstock for her Fremont Hall of 
Fame induction and thank her for her service to our community.

                          ____________________
```
