# RECOGNIZING ALYSSA QUINN FOR TWO YEARS OF OUTSTANDING SERVICE

**Speaking:** DelBene, Suzan K. (D000617) [D-WA]

```
     RECOGNIZING ALYSSA QUINN FOR TWO YEARS OF OUTSTANDING SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SUZAN K. DelBENE

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 20, 2024

  Ms. DelBENE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank Alyssa Quinn for her 
two years of invaluable service to my office and the constituents in 
Washington's 1st Congressional District. Alyssa joined my office as an 
intern in January 2022 and then became our Legislative Correspondent in 
July 2022. She has worked tirelessly to ensure our constituents have 
received timely and thoughtful responses to their correspondence with 
my office. Alyssa is a very strong and reliable writer who excels at 
communicating complex and sensitive issues. She has also run our 
internship program in the D.C. office and has been a dedicated mentor 
to all those she has managed, helping countless interns find full-time 
employment. Alyssa has brought to our office the spirit of the Pacific 
Northwest and a deep love for all things Washington. Her enthusiasm for 
farm animals, dairy, and staying hydrated has been infectious to all in 
our office. Alyssa has been an incredible staffer and a wonderful 
friend to all in the office, and we will sorely miss her positive 
spirit.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Alyssa Quinn 
and wishing her the best in all of her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
