# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7734

**Measures:** H.R. 7734

```
           By Mr. SCOTT FRANKLIN of Florida:
       H.R. 7734.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to require a 
     notation in the personnel record file of certain employees of 
     the Department of Veterans Affairs who resign from Government 
     employment under certain circumstances.
```
