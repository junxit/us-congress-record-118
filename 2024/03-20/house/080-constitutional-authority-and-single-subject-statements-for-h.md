# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7756

**Measures:** H.R. 7756

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 7756.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power . . . To regulate Commerce

[[Page H1296]]

     with foreign Nations, and among the several States, and with 
     the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To expand the scope of the Do Not Call rules under the 
     Telephone Consumer Protection Act to include all telephone 
     subscribers, and to expand the private right of action for 
     calls in violation of those rules.
```
