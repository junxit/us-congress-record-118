# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10118

**Measures:** H.R. 10118

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 10118.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power . . . To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Protects American consumers by providing protections and 
     incentives for whistleblowers who disclose information about 
     an activity they reasonably believe is a potential violation 
     of any law, rule, or regulation enforced by the Federal Trade 
     Commission.
```
