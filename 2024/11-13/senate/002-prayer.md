# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You have given us the great hope that Your Kingdom shall 
come on Earth. Use us to make this dream a reality. Help us to trust 
You without wavering, fervently believing that our times are in Your 
hands.
  Today, empower our lawmakers to be aware of Your unfailing love and 
to live according to Your truth. May they examine the motives of their 
hearts as You lead them along the path that will glorify You. Empower 
them to live with such integrity that they will always stand on solid 
ground.
  Lord, ransom them from all troubles as they put their trust in You.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
