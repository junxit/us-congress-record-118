                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 399

Pennsylvania (Mr. Fetterman) was added as a cosponsor of S. 399, a bill 
to place limitations on excepting positions from the competitive 
service, and for other purposes.

Hampshire (Ms. Hassan) and the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) were
