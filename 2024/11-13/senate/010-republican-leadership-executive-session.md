# Republican Leadership (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                         Republican Leadership

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I have just been told that Senator John 
Thune will be the next Republican leader in the Senate. I congratulate 
Senator Thune on being chosen by his colleagues as the next Republican 
leader. I look forward to working with him. We have done many 
bipartisan things here in the Senate together, and I hope that 
continues. As you know, I strongly believe that bipartisanship is the 
best and often the only way to get things done in the Senate.
```
