# BUILDING ON REEMPLOYMENT IMPROVEMENTS TO DELIVER GOOD EMPLOYMENT FOR WORKERS ACT

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 5861

```
 BUILDING ON REEMPLOYMENT IMPROVEMENTS TO DELIVER GOOD EMPLOYMENT FOR 
                              WORKERS ACT

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Finance be discharged from further consideration of H.R. 
5861, and the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5861) to extend reemployment services and 
     eligibility assessments to all claimants for unemployment 
     benefits, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the committee was discharged, and the 
Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I further ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
considered read a third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table, with no intervening action or 
debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 5861) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
