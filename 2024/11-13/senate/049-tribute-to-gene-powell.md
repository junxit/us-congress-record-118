# TRIBUTE TO GENE POWELL

**Speaking:** Cassidy, Bill (C001075) [R-LA]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO GENE POWELL

● Mr. CASSIDY. Mr. President, I rise to pay tribute to Gene 
Powell, who has served the Louisiana constituents of Shreveport, LA. 
Since September of 1976, Gene Powell has donated over 1,900 blood 
platelet products in over 700 donation visits. As a U.S. Air Force 
veteran and 90 years of age, I raise the actions of Gene Powell as 
selflessness.
  Today, Gene Powell carries forward his Lifetime Achievement Award by 
LifeShare Blood Center. His actions ensure improving the lives of 
countless individuals and service to his community, by publicly 
honoring him.
  Thank you to Gene Powell, a veteran and community serviceman; his 
service will never be forgotten.●

                          ____________________
```
