# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
UNIDENTIFIED ANOMALOUS PHENOMENA: EXPOSING THE TRUTH
Committee on Oversight and Accountability: Subcommittee on 
Cybersecurity, Information Technology, and Government Innovation; and 
Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs held 
a joint hearing entitled ``Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Exposing 
the Truth''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
OUR NATION'S HALLOWED GROUNDS: REVIEW OF AMERICA'S NATIONAL CEMETERY 
FUNERAL OPERATION FOR VETERANS AND SERVICEMEMBERS
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Disability Assistance 
and Memorial Affairs held a hearing entitled ``Our Nation's Hallowed 
Grounds: Review of America's National Cemetery Funeral Operation for 
Veterans and Servicemembers''. Testimony was heard from Major General 
Trevor J. Bredenkamp, Commanding General, Joint Task Force--National 
Capital Region, U.S. Army Military District of Washington; Ronald E. 
Walters, Acting Under Secretary for Memorial Affairs, National Cemetery 
Administration, Department of Veterans Affairs; Shawn P. Buck, 
Director, State Veterans Cemetery, New Hampshire; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
