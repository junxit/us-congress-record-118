# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
GUN VIOLENCE EPIDEMIC
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
the gun violence epidemic, focusing on bump stocks and other conversion 
devices, after receiving testimony from Jens Ludwig, University of 
Chicago Crime Lab, and Laura O'Donnell, both of Chicago, Illinois; 
Esther Sanchez-Gomez, GIFFORDS Law Center To Prevent Gun Violence, New 
York, New York; Ryan M. Cleckner, FFLSafe, Flagstaff, Arizona; and Zack 
Smith, The Heritage Foundation, Washington, D.C.
COPYRIGHT OFFICE OVERSIGHT
Committee on the Judiciary: Subcommittee on Intellectual Property 
concluded an oversight hearing to examine the United States Copyright 
Office, after receiving testimony from Shira Perlmutter, Register of 
Copyrights and Director, United States Copyright Office.
INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
