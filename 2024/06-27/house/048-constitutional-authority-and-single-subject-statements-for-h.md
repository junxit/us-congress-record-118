# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8856

**Measures:** H.R. 8856

```
           By Mr. ARMSTRONG:
       H.R. 8856.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To protect the constitutional right to trial and discourage 
     imposition of extended sentences for defendants who elect to 
     go to trial instead of accepting a plea offer, and for other 
     purposes.
```
