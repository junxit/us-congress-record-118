# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8859

**Measures:** H.R. 8859

```
           By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
       H.R. 8859.

[[Page H4411]]

       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would require the U.S. Bureau of the Census to 
     use their existing data on individual disabilities from the 
     American Community Survey to create and publish an 
     approximation of the U.S. deafblind population, and to report 
     to Congress on the feasibility of expanding data collection 
     on the deafblind population.
```
