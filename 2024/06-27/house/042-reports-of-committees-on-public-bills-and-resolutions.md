     gas leases, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 
     118-562). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.

     the Union.

     to approve amendments to the territorial constitution based 
     on majority rule in a democratic act of self-determination,

     administration of Federal territorial

       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 5443. A
