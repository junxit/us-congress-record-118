# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8863

**Measures:** H.R. 8863

```
           By Mr. HUIZENGA:
       H.R. 8863.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution; The 
     Congress shall have the Power to lay and collect Taxes, 
     Duties, Imposts and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for 
     the common Defense and general Welfare of the United States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Burma Unified through Rigorous Military 
     Accountability Act of 2022 to extend the sunset, to require a 
     determination with respect to the imposition of sanctions on 
     certain persons of Burma, and for other purposes.
```
