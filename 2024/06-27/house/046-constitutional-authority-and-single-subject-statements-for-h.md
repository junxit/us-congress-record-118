# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8854

**Measures:** H.R. 8854

```
           By Mr. YAKYM:
       H.R. 8854.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would allow a veteran's survivor who chose to 
     receive an urn or plaque in lieu of their veteran loved one's 
     interred at a VA National Cemetery, to subsequently have the 
     veteran interred at a VA National Cemetery if they pay the 
     expense of that urn or plaque.
```
