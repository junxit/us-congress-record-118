# HONORING NATE WORLEY

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
                          HONORING NATE WORLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 27, 2024

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. Nate Worley, for 
his work as an intern serving in my D.C. office. I would like to take 
this opportunity to thank him for his hard work and recognize the 
meaningful contributions he has made to the office this summer.
  Nate is from Leicester, North Carolina and is a recent graduate of 
the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill where he studied 
economics. Nate is active in local politics and is a dedicated member 
of the Western North Carolina Community.
  Interns work with staff to serve constituents in North Carolina's 
11th Congressional District. Nate was chosen from a competitive pool of 
applicants and exceeded every expectation with his positive attitude 
and impressive work ethic.
  It was a pleasure to work with Nate, and I am so proud to have such 
an intelligent and ambitious intern representing my office. Nate 
exemplified the spirit of public service and represents the best that 
our Nation has to offer. I congratulate Nate on this successful 
completion of his internship, and on behalf of the people of Western 
North Carolina, I thank him.

                          ____________________
```
