# CELEBRATING THE NEW IMAGE YOUTH CENTER 20TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Frost, Maxwell (F000476) [D-FL]

```
        CELEBRATING THE NEW IMAGE YOUTH CENTER 20TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MAXWELL FROST

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 27, 2024

  Mr. FROST. Mr. Speaker, today, we celebrate the 20th anniversary of 
New Image Youth Center located in the Parramore neighborhood of 
Orlando. New Image Youth Center's contributions over the past 20 years 
cannot be measured. With just $5 and a game of Monopoly, the mustard 
seed was sown, and NIYC was born. Founded by licensed therapist, 
entrepreneur, and author Dr. Shanta Barton Stubbs, her goal was to 
transform the narrative of the Parramore community and shift the 
mindset from surviving to thriving.
  New Image Youth Center prides itself on being a safe haven for K-12 
Orlando youth. For the past 20 years, their dedicated staff and 
volunteers have equipped their youth with the tools and space to pursue 
their wildest dreams. New Image provides mentorship and programming 
rooted in mental health counseling, academic support, social 
development, and more. Their love for their kids goes beyond the call 
of duty by serving and supporting them till their adult years as they 
seek employment and post-secondary education.
  I thank New Image Youth Center for 20 years of dedication and 
service. Their contributions continue to make a profound difference in 
our community, and we are grateful for their support and commitment to 
make Central Florida a better place for all.

                          ____________________
```
