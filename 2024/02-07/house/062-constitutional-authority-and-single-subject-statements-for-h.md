# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7279

**Measures:** H.R. 7279

```
            By Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina:
        H.R. 7279.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to reduce 
     the administrative burden on physicians and physical 
     therapists with respect to plan of care certification and 
     recertification requirements.
```
