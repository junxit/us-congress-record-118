# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7274

**Measures:** H.R. 7274

```
            By Mr. CAREY:
        H.R. 7274.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to provide 
     for outreach and education to Medicare beneficiaries to 
     simplify access to information for family caregivers through 
     1-800-MEDICARE, and for other purposes.
```
