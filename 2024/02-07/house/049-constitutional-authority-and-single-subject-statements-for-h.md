# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7265

**Measures:** H.R. 7265

```
           By Ms. UNDERWOOD:
       H.R. 7265.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the of Health and Human Services to award 
     grants to establish or expand programs to implement evidence-
     aligned practices in health care settings for the purpose of 
     reducing the suicide rates of covered individuals, and for 
     other purposes.
```
