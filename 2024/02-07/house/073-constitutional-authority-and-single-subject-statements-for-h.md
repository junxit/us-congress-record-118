# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7290

**Measures:** H.R. 7290

```
            By Mr. MILLS:
        H.R. 7290.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit corruption in the office of any State or local 
     chief prosecutor.
```
