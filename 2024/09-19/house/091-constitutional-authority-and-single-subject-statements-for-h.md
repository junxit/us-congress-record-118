# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9692

**Measures:** H.R. 9692

```
           By Mr. DIAZ-BALART:
       H.R. 9692.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To increase the maximum reward amount for information 
     leading to the arrest and conviction of Nicolas Maduro Moros 
     to $100,000,000, which shall be paid out by the Federal 
     Government from all assets being withheld from Nicolas Maduro 
     Moros, officials of the Maduro regime and their co-
     conspirators.
```
