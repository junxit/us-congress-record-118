# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9700

**Measures:** H.R. 9700

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 9700.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit bilateral economic assistance to foreign 
     governments that abridge the right to free speech that would 
     be speech protected by the Constitution of the United States
```
