# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9710

**Measures:** H.R. 9710

```
           By Mr. STRONG:
       H.R. 9710.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Energy Policy Act of 2005 to support a program 
     to advance the research, development, demonstration, and 
     commercial application of small modular reactors and micro-
     reactors in order to accelerate the availability of United 
     States-based technologies, and for other purposes.
```
