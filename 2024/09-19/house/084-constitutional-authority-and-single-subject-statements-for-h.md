# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9685

**Measures:** H.R. 9685

```
           By Mr. BOST:
       H.R. 9685.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The USPS Act will establish timelines and procedures for 
     post office emergency suspensions, require community input, 
     and mandate annual reports to Congress on postal suspensions.
```
