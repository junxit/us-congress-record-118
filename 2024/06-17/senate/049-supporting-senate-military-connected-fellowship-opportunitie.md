```
     SUPPORTING SENATE MILITARY-CONNECTED FELLOWSHIP OPPORTUNITIES

  Ms. SMITH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 737, which is at the desk.
```
