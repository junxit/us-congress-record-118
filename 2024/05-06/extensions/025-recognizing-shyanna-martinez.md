# RECOGNIZING SHYANNA MARTINEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING SHYANNA MARTINEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 6, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Shyanna 
Martinez for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Shyanna has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Shyanna, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Shyanna's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Jefferson Jr./Sr. High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Shyanna Martinez on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
