# HONORING THE KOREAN BAPTIST CHURCH OF GAINESVILLE'S 2024 KOREAN WAR VETERANS EVENT

**Speaking:** Cammack, Kat (C001039) [R-FL]

```
  HONORING THE KOREAN BAPTIST CHURCH OF GAINESVILLE'S 2024 KOREAN WAR 
                             VETERANS EVENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. KAT CAMMACK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 6, 2024

  Mrs. CAMMACK. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Florida's Third Congressional 
District, we commend the Korean Baptist Church of Gainesville, Florida 
for its 2024 Korean War Veterans Event on Saturday, June 1, 2024.
  Following the Korean War, the American people and Korean people have 
formed a strong, admirable friendship that has stood the test of time 
for decades. It is important to continue this relationship for years to 
come and I am grateful for the efforts of the Korean Baptist Church of 
Gainesville to foster these bonds.
  I congratulate them on another great event. We look forward to seeing 
the fruits of this labor in our North Central Florida community.

                          ____________________
```
