# RECOGNIZING AGEWAYS

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                          RECOGNIZING AGEWAYS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 6, 2024

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize AgeWays, a human 
services agency, as they celebrate fifty years of service to our 
seasoned residents of Southeast Michigan.
  Formerly known as Area Agency on Aging 1-B, AgeWays has been a 
resource for seniors and their caregivers since 1974. They take an 
active role in helping older adults access services and assistance they 
need to live independently. They serve over 700,000 people in their 
six-county service areas, providing comprehensive services such as 
direct care, meals on wheels, transportation, and health and wellness 
programs. They have been a steadfast partner for the communities of the 
12th Congressional District who rely on their work.
  The 12th District is proud to recognize their incredible and 
dedicated leadership to serving our seasoned residents and we offer our 
congratulations as they celebrate their 50th anniversary. Please join 
me in recognizing AgeWays in celebration of fifty years of commitment 
to our community.
```
