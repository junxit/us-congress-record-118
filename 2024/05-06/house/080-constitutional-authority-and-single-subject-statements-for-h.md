# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8257

**Measures:** H.R. 8257

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 8257.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would prohibit the Secretary of the Army from 
     implementing a rule that restricts public access to the 
     Washington Channel in the District of Columbia.
```
