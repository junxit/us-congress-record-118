# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 195

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-100. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of Tennessee, relative to Senate Resolution No. 
     195, urging the President of the United States and the U.S. 
     Congress to refrain from reducing appropriations to the 
     Victims of Crime Act (VOCA) Victims Fund for Fiscal Year 2024 
     and instead restore full funding to Fiscal Year 2021 levels; 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       ML-101. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Maine, relative to Joint Resolution S.P. 998, requesting the 
     United States Department of Veterans Affairs to provide 
     access to medical care and assistance to members of the Maine 
     National Guard who trained at the military support base in 
     Gagetown, New Brunswick, Canada; to the Committee on 
     Veterans' Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
