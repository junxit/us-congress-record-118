# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7926

**Measures:** H.R. 7926

```
           By Mr. ESPAILLAT:
       H.R. 7926.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       section 5 of Amendment XIV to the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 49, United States Code, with respect to 
     requirements for air carriers to disclose certain exposures 
     to toxic fumes, and for other purposes.
```
