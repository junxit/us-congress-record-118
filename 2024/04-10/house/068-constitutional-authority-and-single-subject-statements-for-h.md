# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7930

**Measures:** H.R. 7930

```
           By Ms. KUSTER:
       H.R. 7930.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution, Congress 
     has the power ``to make all Laws which shall be necesary and 
     proper for carrying into Execution the forgoing Powers, and 
     all other Powers vested by this Constitution in the 
     Government of the United States or any Department or Officer 
     thereof.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Health/Teen Dating Violence
```
