# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7459

**Measures:** H.R. 7459

```
            By Ms. NORTON:
        H.R. 7459.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to make the District of Columbia eligible for the same 
     federal funding as states under the Pittman-Robertson 
     Wildlife Restoration Act and the Dingell-Johnson Sport Fish 
     Restoration Act.
```
