# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7457

**Measures:** H.R. 7457

```
            By Mr. GARAMENDI:
        H.R. 7457.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Education to count months during 
     which Peace Corps volunteers and AmeriCorps members have 
     eligible Federal Direct Loans in deferment or forbearance 
     toward eligibility for public service loan forgiveness.
```
