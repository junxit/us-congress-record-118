# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Cardin, Benjamin L. (C000141) [D-MD], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
following nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Jacqueline Becerra, of 
Florida, to be United States District Judge for the Southern District 
of Florida.
  Mr. CARDIN. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
