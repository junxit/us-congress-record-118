# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 507, Tanya Monique Jones Bosier, of 
     the District of Columbia, to be an Associate Judge of the 
     Superior Court of the District of Columbia for the term of 
     fifteen years.
         Charles E. Schumer, Gary C. Peters, Jeanne Shaheen, John 
           W. Hickenlooper, Alex Padilla, Richard J. Durbin, Amy 
           Klobuchar, Jack Reed, Tina Smith, Richard Blumenthal, 
           Tammy Duckworth, Robert P. Casey, Jr., Catherine Cortez 
           Masto, Margaret Wood Hassan, Peter Welch, Sheldon 
           Whitehouse, Raphael G. Warnock.

                          ____________________
```
