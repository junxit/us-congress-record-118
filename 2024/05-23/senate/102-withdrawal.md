# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on May 
23, 2024 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       NELSON W. CUNNINGHAM, OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, TO BE A 
     DEPUTY UNITED STATES TRADE REPRESENTATIVE (WESTERN 
     HEMISPHERE, EUROPE, THE MIDDLE EAST, LABOR, AND ENVIRONMENT), 
     WITH THE RANK OF AMBASSADOR, VICE JAYME RAY WHITE, WHICH WAS 
     SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 11, 2024.
```
