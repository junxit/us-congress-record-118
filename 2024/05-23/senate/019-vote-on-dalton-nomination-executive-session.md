# Vote on Dalton Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reed, Jack (R000122) [D-RI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       Vote on Dalton Nomination

  The ACTING PRESIDENT Pro Tempore. The question is, Will the Senate 
advise and consent to the Dalton nomination?
  Mr. REED. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays on the pending 
nomination.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from West Virginia (Mr. 
Manchin), the Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Menendez), and the Senator 
from Massachusetts (Ms. Warren) are necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Tennessee (Mr. Hagerty) and the Senator from Oklahoma (Mr. 
Mullin).
  The result was announced--yeas 56, nays 39, as follows:
```
