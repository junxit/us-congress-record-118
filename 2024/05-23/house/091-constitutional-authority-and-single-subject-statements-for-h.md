# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8551

**Measures:** H.R. 8551

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 8551.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of National Intelligence to prepare 
     and make available a report on the wealth and corrupt 
     activities of the leadership of the Chinese Communist Party.
```
