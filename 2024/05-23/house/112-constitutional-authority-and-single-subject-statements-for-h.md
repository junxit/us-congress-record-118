# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 158

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 158

```
           By Ms. HAGEMAN:
       H.J. Res. 158.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation provides Congressional disaproval of the 
     rule submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service 
     relating to ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife Plants; 
     Listing Endangered and Threatened Species and Designating 
     Critical Habitat'', and states that such rule shall have no 
     force or effect.
```
