# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 155

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 155

```
           By Mr. DUARTE:
       H.J. Res. 155.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     National Marine Fisheries Service relating to ``Endangered 
     and Threatened Wildlife and Plants; Regulations for 
     Interagency Cooperation''.
```
