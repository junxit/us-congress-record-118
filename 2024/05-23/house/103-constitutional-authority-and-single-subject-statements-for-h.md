# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8563

**Measures:** H.R. 8563

```
           By Ms. TOKUDA:
       H.R. 8563.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.

       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to
```
