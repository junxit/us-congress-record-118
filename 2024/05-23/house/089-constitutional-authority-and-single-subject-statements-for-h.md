# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8549

**Measures:** H.R. 8549

```
            By Mr. OGLES:
        H.R. 8549.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit any person convicted of an unlawful activity on 
     or after October 7, 2023, on a college campus from being 
     eligible for public service loan forgiveness.
```
