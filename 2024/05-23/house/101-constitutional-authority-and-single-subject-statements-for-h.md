# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8561

**Measures:** H.R. 8561

```
           By Ms. TENNEY:
       H.R. 8561.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires skilled nursing facilities, nursing 
     facilities, intermediate care facilities for the 
     intellectually disabled, and nearby inpatient rehabilitation 
     facilities to establish an essential caregivers program 
     during a public health emergency.
```
