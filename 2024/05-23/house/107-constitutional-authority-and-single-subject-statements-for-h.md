# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 153

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 153

```

       H.J. Res. 153.

     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Provide for congressional disapproval of the rule submitted
```
