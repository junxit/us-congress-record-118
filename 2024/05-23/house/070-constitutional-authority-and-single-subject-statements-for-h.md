# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8530

**Measures:** H.R. 8530

```
           By Mr. EZELL:
       H.R. 8530.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require Facility Security Committees to respond to 
     security recommendations issued by the Federal Protective 
     Service relating to facility security, and for other 
     purposes.
```
