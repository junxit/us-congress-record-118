# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8550

**Measures:** H.R. 8550

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 8550.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for the prohibition on the use of United States 
     passports for travel to, in, or through the Turks and Caicos 
     Islands.
```
