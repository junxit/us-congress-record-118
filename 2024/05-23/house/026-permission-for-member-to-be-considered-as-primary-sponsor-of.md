# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 4105

**Speaking:** Joyce, David P. (J000295) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4105

```
 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 4105

  Mr. JOYCE of Ohio. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the primary sponsor of H.R. 4105, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Buck of Colorado, for the 
purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to 
clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  There was no objection.
```
