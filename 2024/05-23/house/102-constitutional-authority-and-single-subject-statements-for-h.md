# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8562

**Measures:** H.R. 8562

```
           By Ms. TOKUDA:
       H.R. 8562.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to improve the 
     provision of direct housing loans and medical care from the 
     Department of Veterans Affairs for Native Hawaiians.
```
