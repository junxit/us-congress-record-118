# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8564

**Measures:** H.R. 8564

```
           By Mr. WENSTRUP:
       H.R. 8564.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require certain elements of the intelligence community 
     to submit to the congressional intelligence committees a 
     report with respect to biotechnology threats, and for other 
     purposes.
```
