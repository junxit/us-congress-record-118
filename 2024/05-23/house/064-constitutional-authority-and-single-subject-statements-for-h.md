# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8523

**Measures:** H.R. 8523

```
           By Mr. CASTEN:
       H.R. 8523.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require Electricity Transmission Organizations to allow
```
