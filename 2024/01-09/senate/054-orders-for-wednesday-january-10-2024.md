# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 10, 2024

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 10, 2024

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, finally, I ask unanimous consent that 
when the Senate completes its business today, it stand adjourned until 
10 a.m. on Wednesday, January 10; that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; and

[[Page S50]]

that upon the conclusion of morning business, the Senate proceed to 
executive session to resume consideration of the Mehalchick nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
