# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6914

**Measures:** H.R. 6914

```
            By Mrs. HINSON:
        H.R. 6914.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Aricle 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires a public institution of higher education (IHE) 
     that participates in federal student-aid programs to provide 
     information to admitted and enrolled students on the rights 
     and resources for students who are pregnant or may become 
     pregnant, excluding abortion services.
```
