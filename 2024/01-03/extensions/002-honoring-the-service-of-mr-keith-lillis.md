# HONORING THE SERVICE OF MR. KEITH LILLIS

**Speaking:** Guest, Michael (G000591) [R-MS]

```
                HONORING THE SERVICE OF MR. KEITH LILLIS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL GUEST

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 3, 2024

  Mr. GUEST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Keith Lillis who 
has served six terms as the District One Supervisor on the Board of 
Supervisors for Neshoba County. Keith Lillis previously served as 
president of the Board and will retire as vice president of the Board.
  Mississippi born and raised, Mr. Lillis graduated from Neshoba County 
High School and attended East Central Junior College where he played 
football for the East Central Warriors. Mr. Lillis met and married 
Union, Mississippi native Marcha Howle, and together they have two sons 
and nine grandchildren.
  Mr. Lillis has committed his time and expertise to the supervisor 
community in Mississippi, serving on the Board of Directors for the 
Mississippi Association of Supervisors since 2012. He also attended and 
graduated as a member of the inaugural class of the Mississippi 
Association of Supervisors Institute for Excellence in County 
Government. Since 2020, Mr. Lillis has also served on the Board of 
Directors of the East Central Planning and Development District.
  Mr. Lillis's career of service is appreciated throughout Neshoba 
County, and I am thankful to honor his service here today. I wish Mr. 
Lillis well in his retirement.
```
