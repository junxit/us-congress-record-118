# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 6912

**Measures:** H.R. 6912

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 6912.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would direct the Librarian of Congress to obtain a 
     stained glass panel depicting the seal of the District of 
     Columbia and install the panel among the stained glass panels 
     depicting the seals of States.
```
