# CELEBRATING GABRIEL BURNS

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                       CELEBRATING GABRIEL BURNS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, this February, we gathered to celebrate 
Gabriel Burns, a bright young man who worked his way up to the rank of 
Eagle Scout. To achieve this rank, Gabriel undertook an Eagle Scout 
Project involving the renovation of his local youth baseball field. 
This long process involved getting permission from the City of 
Millville, meeting with the president of the local baseball 
association, and analyzing the state of the fields. Since he joined 
years ago, Gabriel has really enjoyed his time with the Scouts, and he 
is grateful for all the opportunities they have given him. He will 
always cherish making memories at Camp Roosevelt, hanging out with his 
friends, and earning merit badges. This young man has a bright future 
ahead of him and I can't wait to see what he accomplishes. God Bless 
Gabriel and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
