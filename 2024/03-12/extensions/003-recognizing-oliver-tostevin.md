# RECOGNIZING OLIVER TOSTEVIN

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                      RECOGNIZING OLIVER TOSTEVIN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, last month, I had the pleasure of 
attending Oliver Tostevin's Eagle Scout Court of Honor. Oliver has been 
involved in the Eagle Scouts since the fifth grade when he joined as a 
cub scout. Over the years, he has been very involved in his troop by 
contributing countless hours to community service, participating in 
many outdoor adventures, and leading his fellow scouts on their path to 
Eagle Scout. To achieve the rank of Eagle Scout, Oliver decided to 
renovate his local baseball field. His project involved putting 
together new bleachers, painting the dugouts, and cleaning out any 
trash from the area. Thanks to him and his efforts, our South Jersey 
community has a beautiful baseball field for everyone to enjoy. I am 
honored to congratulate this young man on his remarkable achievements, 
and I wish him all the best. God Bless Oliver and God Bless the United 
States of America.

                          ____________________
```
