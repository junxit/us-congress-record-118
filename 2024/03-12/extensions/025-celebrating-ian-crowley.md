# CELEBRATING IAN CROWLEY

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                        CELEBRATING IAN CROWLEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, this February, I had the pleasure of 
attending the Eagle Scout Court of Honor for Ian Crowley, a young man 
who is a model of dedication, perseverance, and commitment to community 
service. During his time in the Scouts, Ian has learned the importance 
of preserving and celebrating our history, a lesson which inspired his

[[Page E245]]

Eagle Scout project. He undertook a major task and installed QR codes 
at the Lifesaving Station, a local historical site in South Jersey. 
These codes provide access to videos that detail each of the artifacts' 
histories, preserving the knowledge for generations to come. Beyond his 
time spent with the Scouts, Ian excels academically and demonstrates a 
remarkable work ethic. He always keeps busy, whether that's his pursuit 
of his pilot's license or his participation in the Ocean City High 
School golf team. In recognition of his outstanding achievements, Ian 
has been honored with the Order of the Arrow, an award which celebrates 
his dedication to community service, leadership, and the preservation 
of history. I congratulate Ian on all accomplishments. He makes us all 
proud. God Bless Ian and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
