# CELEBRATING COSTA MARINE

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                        CELEBRATING COSTA MARINE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, I want to highlight the remarkable journey 
of Costa Marine Canvas and Enclosures, a true American success story, 
founded by Joe and Donna Costa in 1971. Starting with humble beginnings 
in the basement of their apartment, Costa Marine has grown into a 
leading fabricator of custom yacht enclosures, upholstery, covers, and 
interiors. Over the years, Costa Marine's commitment to excellence has 
earned them exclusive partnerships with renowned boatbuilders like 
Viking Yachts and Ocean Yachts. Despite the passing of Joe in 2011, 
Donna continues to play a vital role in the company's operations 
alongside the second-generation leadership of Chris Costa, Jennifer 
Smith, and Kevin Jelinski. I had the privilege of touring their 
facility this February, and I can attest to the impressive operation of 
Costa Marine Canvas and Enclosures. From their dedication to quality 
craftsmanship to their focus on community and employees, Costa Marine 
exemplifies the very best. Their story serves as an inspiration to us 
all. I thank Costa Marine for their outstanding contributions and for 
representing the best of American entrepreneurship. God Bless Costa 
Marine and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
