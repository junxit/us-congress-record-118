# CELEBRATING JAYSON CAGLE

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                        CELEBRATING JAYSON CAGLE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, last month, I had the honor of celebrating 
Jayson Cagle, a remarkable young man from Pittsgrove, South Jersey, who 
has achieved the rank of Eagle Scout with Boy Scouts of America Troop 
1058. Jayson's journey to Eagle Scout is not just a personal triumph 
but a testament to his unwavering commitment to making a positive 
impact in his surroundings. His Eagle Scout project, improving his high 
school's music room, reflects his dedication to both his community and 
his passion for music. As a graduating high school senior this year, 
Jayson has been accepted into Seton Hall University's School of 
Diplomacy and International Relations, where he hopes to learn how to 
serve his country on the global stage. Jayson's commitment to service 
and leadership is an inspiration to us all. I'm proud to congratulate 
him for his achievements and I wish him all the best. God Bless Jayson 
and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
