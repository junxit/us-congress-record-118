# CELEBRATING FLANAGAN'S

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                         CELEBRATING FLANAGAN'S

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, I have the privilege of sharing the 
inspiring story of Tommy Flanagan and his family. In 1995, Tommy 
Flanagan bought a bar in Manhattan called Flanagan's at 119 Fulton St. 
Despite their efforts, the aftermath of 9/11 impacted their business 
beyond repair, ultimately leading to its closure in 2004. Seeking a 
fresh start, Tommy, along with his wife Eve and their children, Tommy 
Jr. and Shannon, made the decision to relocate to South Jersey in 2018. 
It was here that they discovered the old Buck Tavern, a place that they 
knew would be their next adventure. This month, I had the honor of 
attending the ribbon cutting ceremony for the newly renovated and 
refurbished Flanagan's, a bar that I know will quickly become a 
cornerstone of the community. Tommy and his family have embraced their 
new community wholeheartedly and are excited for the opportunities that 
lie ahead in South Jersey. As they embark on this new chapter, I want 
to extend my support and well wishes to the Flanagan family. I thank 
Tommy and Eve for choosing South Jersey as their home. May their new 
beginnings be filled with prosperity and happiness. God Bless Tommy and 
Eve and God Bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
