# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7640

**Measures:** H.R. 7640

```
           By Mr. PFLUGER:
       H.R. 7640.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the hiring of individuals who are not citizens 
     of the United States to administer an election for Federal 
     office in a State or local jurisdiction, and for other 
     purposes.
```
