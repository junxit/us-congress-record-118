# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7631

**Measures:** H.R. 7631

```
           By Mr. GRAVES of Missouri:
       H.R. 7631.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the United States Postal Service to reimburse 
     fees charged for the late payment of bills that were delayed 
     in the mail, and for other purposes.
```
