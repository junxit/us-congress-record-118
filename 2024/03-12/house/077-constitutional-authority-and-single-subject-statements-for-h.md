# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7626

**Measures:** H.R. 7626

```
           By Mr. CRENSHAW:
       H.R. 7626.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The power granted to Congress under Article I, Section 8 of 
     the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Energy from prescribing or 
     enforcing energy conservation standards for room air 
     conditioners that are not cost-effective or technologically 
     feasible.
```
