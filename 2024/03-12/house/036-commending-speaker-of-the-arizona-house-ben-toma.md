# COMMENDING SPEAKER OF THE ARIZONA HOUSE BEN TOMA

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                              {time}  1215
            COMMENDING SPEAKER OF THE ARIZONA HOUSE BEN TOMA

  (Mrs. LESKO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commend an accomplished and 
dedicated leader who has served Arizona greatly: the speaker of the 
Arizona House, Ben Toma.
  During his tenure in public service, Speaker Toma has achieved many 
great things for Arizona.
  He advanced universal school choice, a ballot measure to require 
voter ID, a border security ballot measure to require E-Verify, and he 
instituted the largest tax cut in Arizona history.
  He also signed on to an amicus brief to support the State of Texas as 
they fight in court for their right to defend their State from 
invasion.
  Speaker Toma's career is a testament to his dedication to Arizona. 
His legacy of leadership will last for years to come.

                          ____________________
```
