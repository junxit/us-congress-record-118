# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7637

**Measures:** H.R. 7637

```
           By Mrs. MILLER-MEEKS:
       H.R. 7637.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Secretary of Energy from prescribing or 
     enforcing energy conservation standards for refrigerators, 
     refrigerator-freezers, and freezers that are not cost-
     effective or technologically feasible, and for other 
     purposes.
```
