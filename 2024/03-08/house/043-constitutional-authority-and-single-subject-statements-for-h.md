# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7591

**Measures:** H.R. 7591

```
           By Mr. BARRAGAN:
       H.R. 7591.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill establishes the National Patient Safety Board for 
     the purposes of preventing and reducing patient safety events 
     (i.e., an action or inaction that led to, or could have led 
     to, patient injury, harm, or death in a health care setting).
```
