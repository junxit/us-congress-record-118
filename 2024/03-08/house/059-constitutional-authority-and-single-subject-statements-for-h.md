# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7607

**Measures:** H.R. 7607

```
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 7607.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United States 
     Constitution:
       ``To establish Post Offices and post Roads;''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To designate the facility of the United States Postal 
     Service located at Block 1025, Lots 18 & 19, Northeast Corner 
     of US Route 9 South and Main Street in the Township of 
     Middle, County of Cape May, New Jersey, as the ``George Henry 
     White Post Office Building''.

                          ____________________
```
