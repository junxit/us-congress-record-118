# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9649

**Measures:** H.R. 9649

```
           By Mr. CARSON:
       H.R. 9649.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The UNRWA Funding Emergency Restoration Act restores 
     funding to the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for 
     Palestine Refugees in the Near East (UNRWA).
```
