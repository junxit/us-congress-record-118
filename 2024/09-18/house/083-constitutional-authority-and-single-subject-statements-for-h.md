# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9665

**Measures:** H.R. 9665

```
            By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
        H.R. 9665.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3: The Congress shall have 
     Power . . . To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and 
     among the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Federal Trade Commission Act to affirmatively 
     confirm the authority of the Federal Trade Commission to seek 
     permanent injunctions and other equitable relief for 
     violations of any provision of law enforced by the 
     Commission.
```
