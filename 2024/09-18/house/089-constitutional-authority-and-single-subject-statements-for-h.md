# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9671

**Measures:** H.R. 9671

```
            By Mr. WILLIAMS of New York:
        H.R. 9671.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Section 8 of Article I of the U.S. Constitution.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide guidance for and investment in the research and 
     development activities of artificial intelligence at the 
     Department of Energy, and for other purposes.
```
