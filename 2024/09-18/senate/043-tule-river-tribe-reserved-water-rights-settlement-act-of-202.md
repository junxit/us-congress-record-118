# TULE RIVER TRIBE RESERVED WATER RIGHTS SETTLEMENT ACT OF 2023

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S. 306

```
     TULE RIVER TRIBE RESERVED WATER RIGHTS SETTLEMENT ACT OF 2023

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, today, as ranking member of the Budget 
Committee, I placed a hold on S. 306, the Tule River Tribe Reserved 
Water Rights Settlement Act of 2023.
  Although I don't find fault with the substance of the bill, the 
legislation is not paid for and would violate multiple budget 
enforcement rules. According to the Congressional Budget Office, the 
bill would increase the deficit by $804 million.

                          ____________________
```
