# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4973

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 4973

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4973

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 4973) to reassert the constitutional authority 
     of Congress to determine the general applicability of the 
     criminal laws of the United States, and for other purposes.

  Mr. SCHUMER. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceeding.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
