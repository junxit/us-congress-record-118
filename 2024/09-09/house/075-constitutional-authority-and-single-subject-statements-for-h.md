# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9508

**Measures:** H.R. 9508

```
           By Ms. TENNEY:
       H.R. 9508.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow certain 
     veterinary expenses for pets and service animals to be 
     treated as amounts paid for medical care for purposes of a 
     health savings account or flexible savings account.
```
