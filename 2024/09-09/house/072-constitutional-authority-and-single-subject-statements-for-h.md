# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9505

**Measures:** H.R. 9505

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 9505.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 17 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would give the District of Columbia Courts and 
     the Public Defender Service for the District of Columbia the 
     authority to offer voluntary separation incentive payments to 
     their employees.
```
