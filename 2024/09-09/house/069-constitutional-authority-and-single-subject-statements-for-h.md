# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9502

**Measures:** H.R. 9502

```
           By Mr. FROST:
       H.R. 9502.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 and 18 of the U.S. 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for automatic extension of employment 
     authorizations, to require earlier determinations on 
     temporary protected status designations, and for other 
     purposes.
```
