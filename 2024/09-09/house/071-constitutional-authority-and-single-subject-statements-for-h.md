# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9504

**Measures:** H.R. 9504

```

       H.R. 9504.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1. Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
```
