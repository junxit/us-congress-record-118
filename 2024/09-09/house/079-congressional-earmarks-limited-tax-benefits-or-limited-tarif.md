# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 9456

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                         Offered By Mr. McCaul

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Foreign Affairs in H.R. 9456, the Protecting American 
     Agriculture from Foreign Adversaries Act of 2024, do not 
     contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or 
     limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.

                        Offered By Mrs. Rodgers

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Energy and Commerce in H.R. 9456, Protecting American 
     Agriculture from Foreign Adversaries Act of 2024, do not 
     contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or 
     limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.

                         Offered By Mr. McHenry

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Financial Services in H.R. 9456, the Protecting American 
     Agriculture from Foreign Adversaries Act, do not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.
```
