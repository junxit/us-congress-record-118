# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1361

**Speaking:** McIver, LaMonica (M001229) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 1361

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 1361

  Mrs. McIVER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 1361, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative  Donald Payne, Jr., of New 
Jersey, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings 
pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
