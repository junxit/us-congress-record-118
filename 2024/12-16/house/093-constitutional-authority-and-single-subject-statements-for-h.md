# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10424

**Measures:** H.R. 10424

```
           By Mrs. DINGELL:
       H.R. 10424.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill amends part E of the Title IX of the Social 
     Security Act to requrie States to follow certain procedures 
     in placing a child who has been removed from the custody of 
     his or her parents.
```
