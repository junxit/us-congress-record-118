# CELEBRATING BANGLADESH VICTORY DAY

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Timothy M. (K000402) [D-NY]

```
                   CELEBRATING BANGLADESH VICTORY DAY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. Speaker, today we join the people of Bangladesh and 
Bangladeshi communities around the world in celebrating Victory Day on 
December 16, 2024. It acknowledges a day of triumph, remembrance, and 
persistence through the courage and unity of the Bangladeshi people.
  As we honor this significant moment in history, we must remember the 
sacrifices made by the patriotic individuals who fought for freedom, 
peace, and justice. Victory Day serves not only as a celebration but as 
a demonstration of the steep costs of liberty. This inspiring 
commemoration exemplifies the values of democracy, human rights, and 
self-determination.
  In a short period of time, Bangladesh has shown power of 
determination and innovation. On December 16, 1971, after a 9-months-
long liberation war, Bangladesh claimed victory. With a skilled 
workforce, technological advancement, strong healthcare, and a vibrant

[[Page E1272]]

youth population, Bangladesh is emerging as a leader, building on its 
rich history. With more transformative innovation, the country is 
developing adaptation strategies to combat the climate crisis with 
leadership in protecting the environment and strengthening its climate 
resilience.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in celebrating Bangladesh Victory Day as 
we acknowledge and celebrate the sacrifices made by countless brave 
souls and citizens who fought for a future of justice and sovereignty. 
Let us also take this moment to recognize the vibrant Bangladeshi-
American community throughout our country. Their dedication to cultural 
contributions enriches our Nation, and we are all the better for it.

                          ____________________
```
