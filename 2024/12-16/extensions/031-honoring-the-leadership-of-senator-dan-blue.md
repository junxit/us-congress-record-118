# HONORING THE LEADERSHIP OF SENATOR DAN BLUE

**Speaking:** Nickel, Wiley (N000194) [D-NC]

```
              HONORING THE LEADERSHIP OF SENATOR DAN BLUE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. WILEY NICKEL

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. NICKEL. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor Senator Dan Blue, a 
dedicated public servant and lifelong North Carolinian, for his 
remarkable leadership as Senate Minority Leader and his tireless 
commitment to the people of our state.
  From his early days growing up in Robeson County to becoming the 
first African American Speaker of the North Carolina House of 
Representatives, Senator Blue has been a trailblazer and an advocate 
for working families. His leadership has focused on investing in public 
education, creating jobs, protecting the environment, and ensuring 
access to affordable healthcare.
  Throughout his distinguished career, Senator Blue has consistently 
been one of the most effective legislators in North Carolina. He has 
shaped significant reforms in workplace safety, public schools, and the 
criminal justice system, leaving a lasting impact on the state. As 
Senate Minority Leader, his steady and principled leadership has been a 
guiding force for his caucus during some of the most challenging 
political times.
  As he steps down from his leadership role, I honor Senator Blue's 
decades of public service and his unwavering dedication to the people 
of North Carolina. His legacy as a leader, advocate, and mentor will 
continue to inspire future generations of public servants.
  Please join me in recognizing Senator Dan Blue for his extraordinary 
service to North Carolina.

                          ____________________
```
