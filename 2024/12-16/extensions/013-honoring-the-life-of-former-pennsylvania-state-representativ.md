# HONORING THE LIFE OF FORMER PENNSYLVANIA STATE REPRESENTATIVE ISABELLA V. FITZGERALD

**Speaking:** Evans, Dwight (E000296) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 579

```
HONORING THE LIFE OF FORMER PENNSYLVANIA STATE REPRESENTATIVE ISABELLA 
                             V. FITZGERALD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DWIGHT EVANS

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. EVANS. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor the extraordinary life 
and enduring legacy of Former Pennsylvania State Representative 
Isabella V. Fitzgerald, a beloved leader, trailblazer, and pillar of 
the Northwest Philadelphia community. Affectionately known as ``Izzy,'' 
Representative Fitzgerald departed this earthly life peacefully on 
December 6, 2024, leaving a legacy of service, compassion, and 
transformative impact.
  In 2017, Representative Fitzgerald made history as the first African 
American woman elected to serve as State Representative for the 203rd 
Legislative District, a role she held with distinction for three terms. 
During her tenure, she championed many issues, including public safety, 
small business development, community revitalization, and healthcare 
access. Her dedication to her community is exemplified by her efforts 
to modernize public spaces, her creation of the Community Angel Awards 
to honor unsung heroes, and her revival of Super Saturday, a 
celebration of Northwest Philadelphia neighborhoods.
  Representative Fitzgerald's legislative accomplishments included 
sponsoring House Bill 579, which improved insurance coverage for breast 
imaging and was signed into law by Governor Tom Wolf in 2020. Her 
advocacy for affordable housing, health care, and education reflected 
her deep commitment to addressing the challenges faced by families in 
her community. A staunch supporter of public engagement, she fostered 
partnerships with local law enforcement and organized events such as 
National Night Out to promote community safety.
  Even in retirement, Representative Fitzgerald's dedication to her 
neighbors and community never waned. She continued to organize 
grassroots efforts, advocate for breast cancer awareness, and provide 
mentorship to countless individuals.
  Beyond her professional achievements, Isabella Fitzgerald was a 
devoted mother, grandmother, great-grandmother, and friend.
  In recognition of her incredible contributions, the intersection of 
Stenton Avenue and Washington Lane in Philadelphia was renamed 
``Isabella Fitzgerald Way'' on December 15, 2024--a fitting tribute to 
a woman who dedicated her life to uplifting her community.
```
