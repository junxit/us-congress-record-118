# RECOGNIZING THE COLORADO SCHOOL OF MINES CROSS COUNTRY TEAM

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE COLORADO SCHOOL OF MINES CROSS COUNTRY TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Colorado 
School of Mines Men's Cross Country Team for winning their fourth NCAA 
Division II National Championship.
  Over the past ten years, the Orediggers have dominated Division II 
Athletics. This season, they won the team title in every race and six 
out of seven runners earned All-American honors. The championship race 
was especially impressive, with three runners finishing in the top ten. 
This team, made up of Logan Bocovich, Loic Scomparin, Ethan Grolnic, 
Paul Knight, Dawson Gunn, Jeremiah Vaille, and Max Bonenger, showed the 
very best in competitiveness that the Colorado School of Mines Athletic 
Department has to offer.
  This achievement was earned by the commitment of the runners, support 
from students, guidance from Athletic Director David Hansburg, and the 
coaching of Chris Siemers. Their Division II championship title, 
cements Colorado School of Mines's reputation as an academic and 
athletic powerhouse in Colorado and across the country.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate the Orediggers on their successful 
season.

                          ____________________
```
