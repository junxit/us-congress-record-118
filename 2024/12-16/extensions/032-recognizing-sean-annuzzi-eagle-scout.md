# RECOGNIZING SEAN ANNUZZI--EAGLE SCOUT

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                 RECOGNIZING SEAN ANNUZZI--EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, it is my great pleasure to congratulate 
Sean Annuzzi on his exemplary achievement of attaining the rank of 
Eagle Scout. This distinguished honor represents the culmination of his 
dedication, having earned nearly 40 merit badges across a wide array of 
skill sets. From Automotive Maintenance to American Business to 
Archaeology, Sean has shown a deep commitment to becoming a well-
rounded young man, always eager to learn. To achieve the rank of Eagle 
Scout, he completed 14 additional merit badges, including Citizenship 
in the Nation, Personal Fitness, and Lifesaving. These accomplishments 
not only signify his dedication to personal growth but also his 
unwavering commitment to serving his community. Outside of his studies, 
he is already making a meaningful impact by mentoring children and 
teenagers with disabilities, exemplifying the spirit of compassion and 
service that the Scouting program seeks to instill. In his free time, 
Sean trains youth and teenagers in karate, equipping them with skills 
that foster both confidence and resilience. His efforts in this regard 
are highly commendable and are a testament to his character and 
leadership. Sean's family takes immense pride in his accomplishments, 
and I am honored to share in this sentiment. I have no doubt he will 
continue to do amazing things in the future. May God bless Sean and God 
bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
