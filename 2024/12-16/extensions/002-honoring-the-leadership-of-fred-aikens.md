# HONORING THE LEADERSHIP OF FRED AIKENS

**Speaking:** Nickel, Wiley (N000194) [D-NC]

```
                 HONORING THE LEADERSHIP OF FRED AIKENS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. WILEY NICKEL

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. NICKEL. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor Frederick Aikens, a 
dedicated public servant and Chief of Staff to Senate Minority Leader 
Dan Blue, for his outstanding leadership and service to the people of 
North Carolina.
  Fred Aikens has spent over three decades serving our state with 
distinction, from his early work as a fiscal analyst in the General 
Assembly to his role at the North Carolina Department of 
Transportation, where he served as Deputy Secretary and Acting 
Commissioner of the Division of Motor Vehicles.
  As Chief of Staff, Fred Aikens has been a crucial leader, helping 
Senator Blue guide the Democratic caucus through some of the most 
challenging legislative sessions in our state's history.
  A native of Wilmington, Fred's long career in public service is 
marked by his strategic vision and his ability to navigate complex 
challenges with integrity.
  In addition to his contributions to the public sector, Fred Aikens 
has served our country as a Lt. Colonel in the North Carolina Army 
National Guard. His commitment to public service, whether in state 
government or in uniform, has been nothing short of exemplary.
  Today, I commend Fred Aikens for his remarkable career and thank him 
for his enduring service to the people of North Carolina. His 
leadership, wisdom, and dedication have made our state stronger, and we 
are grateful for all he has done.

                          ____________________
```
