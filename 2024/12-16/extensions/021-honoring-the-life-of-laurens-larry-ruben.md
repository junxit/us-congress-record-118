# HONORING THE LIFE OF LAURENS "LARRY" RUBEN

**Speaking:** DelBene, Suzan K. (D000617) [D-WA]

```
              HONORING THE LIFE OF LAURENS ``LARRY'' RUBEN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SUZAN K. DelBENE

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Ms. DelBENE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the remarkable life 
and legacy of Laurens ``Larry'' Ruben, a beloved professor, whose 
influence not only shaped my life but also the lives of countless 
students, colleagues, family, and friends. A source of light and a 
friend to all, we recognize the extraordinary impact Larry had on the 
many lives he touched.
  Larry Ruben graduated from the University of Michigan with Bachelor's 
and Master's degrees in Biology. After moving to New York with his wife 
and longtime lab partner, Judith, he earned his Ph.D. from Columbia 
University and completed post-doctoral work at Princeton University 
before joining Reed College in 1955. At Reed, Larry was instrumental in 
developing the Biology Department's research-rich curriculum, improving 
student research opportunities, and emphasizing the importance of 
outside funding to support the program. In his research lab, which 
remained open until he was 86 years old, I had the privilege to conduct 
research during my time at Reed. Larry contributed significant insights 
into immunology and cancer biology.
  Larry was not only an outstanding researcher but also an inspiring 
teacher and mentor. Known for his kindness and generosity, he was 
deeply committed to his students and their success. As my thesis 
advisor, he inspired my early career in immunology. Beyond his work in 
biology, Larry helped establish the Arts Division at Reed College and 
often sang with a Reed-based rock and roll band. He was also known for 
his exceptional dance moves, which left a lasting impression on 
everyone fortunate enough to witness them.
  Larry Ruben was a friend, a mentor, and a man whose immense kindness 
and contributions will never be forgotten. We extend our deepest 
condolences to Larry's family, friends, students, and colleagues. 
Larry's impact will live on in all those he touched.

                          ____________________
```
