# RECOGNIZING WES RHODES

**Speaking:** Van Duyne, Beth (V000134) [R-TX]

```
                         RECOGNIZING WES RHODES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Wes Rhodes of 
Euless, who is retiring after 38 years of service to North Texas. After 
graduating from Arlington High School, Wes received his associate's 
degree in Fire Protection Technology from Tarrant County College in 
Fort Worth. He began his career with the Haltom City Fire Department 
and spent 25 years as a Firefighter and EMT before quickly moving up 
the ranks to Fire Chief. In 2011, Wes accepted the position of Fire 
Chief for the City of Euless. During his time with the Euless Fire 
Department, Wes focused on community involvement, regional training, 
empowering the ranks, and maintaining open lines of communication with 
his team.
  Wes' commitment to North Texas has been acknowledged through numerous 
accolades, including the Outstanding Leadership Award from Tarrant 
County Fire Service Training Center, Distinguished Alumni Award from 
Tarrant County Fire Service Training Center, Texas Association of Fire 
Educators' Instructor of the Year, and Fire Officer of the Year in 
1996, 1998, and 1999 from Haltom City Fire/Rescue. His contributions 
extend beyond fire service, being named the 2024 Hurst-Euless-Bedford 
Rotarian of the Year for his work as president of the Hurst-Euless-
Bedford Rotary Club.
  Wes holds an array of certifications and licenses, including 
Certified Public Manager through Texas State University, Fire Service 
Chief Executive Officer through Texas A&M University, EMT, Master 
Firefighter, Master Fire Inspector, Fire Officer IV, HazMat Technician, 
Fire Investigator, Fire Instructor, Certified Head of Department, Fire 
Safety Officer, Swift Water Technician, and graduate of the Texas Fire 
Chiefs Academy. In 2021, he was promoted to Assistant City Manager of 
Euless. Wes and his wife Mary have been married for 34 years and are 
proud parents of two grown children, one of whom has followed in his 
father's footsteps as a Driver and Engineer with Grapevine Fire 
Department.
  I thank Mr. Rhodes, for his unwavering dedication to protecting and 
serving our community. I congratulate him on his well-deserved 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
