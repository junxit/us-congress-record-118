# HONORING FLORENCE DUNLEVY'S 105TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

```
               HONORING FLORENCE DUNLEVY'S 105TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to Florence 
Dunlevy, a constituent from West St. Paul, who will be celebrating her 
105th birthday on December 21st.
  Florence was born in Armour, South Dakota, in 1919 into a farm 
family, and raised a farming family of her own with her husband John. 
Throughout her life, Florence has been a loving mother at home and avid 
volunteer at church and in her community. These days, she has many 
hobbies to stay busy, including knitting blankets, hats, mittens, and 
tablecloths, many of which she has given away to U.S. servicemembers.
  Her advice for a good life? Be happy, smile, enjoy your friends and 
family, and always try to live your best life.
  We can all take inspiration from Florence's long life of humility and 
service. Happy birthday to Florence.

                          ____________________
```
