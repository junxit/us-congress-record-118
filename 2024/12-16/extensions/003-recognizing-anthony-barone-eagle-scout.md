# RECOGNIZING ANTHONY BARONE--EAGLE SCOUT

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                RECOGNIZING ANTHONY BARONE--EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, today, I celebrate Anthony Barone for 
reaching the coveted rank of Eagle Scout after many outstanding years 
of achievement. Anthony's journey began six years ago, a testament to 
his unwavering dedication to the Boy Scouts. He earned his way through 
each of the respected positions, demonstrating his commitment and 
earning 21 merit badges in the areas of citizenship, personal fitness, 
and family life. While working on his photography merit badge, he 
discovered a passion for photography--a hobby which has only added to 
his well-roundedness. Anthony's love for the community shone through as 
he acquired over 250 volunteer hours, feeding people experiencing 
homelessness, and mentoring the younger Cub Scouts in town. For his 
Eagle Scout project, he honored our men and women in uniform by 
installing six flagpoles to the town Veteran Memorial, to house each of 
America's branches of service, complete with a granite marker to honor 
our troops. Anthony is currently a senior at Kingsway Regional High 
School in the Woolwich Township of South Jersey, where he is taking 
three college-level courses, which led to his induction into the 
National Honor Society. He has many plans for his future and greatly 
enjoys public speaking and speechwriting. He plans to utilize these 
skills by writing a book that will inspire others to follow in his 
footsteps. First, he plans to attend Rowan University and pursue a 
degree in Business Administration. I have complete faith that whatever 
Anthony puts his mind to, he will accomplish, and I look forward to 
seeing the great things he will achieve in the future. God Bless 
Anthony and God Bless our United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
