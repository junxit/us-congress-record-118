# CONGRATULATING THE TIA MARIA FOUNDATION'S 1ST ANNUAL SCHOLARSHIP GALA

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
 CONGRATULATING THE TIA MARIA FOUNDATION'S 1ST ANNUAL SCHOLARSHIP GALA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to offer my heartfelt 
congratulations to The Tia Maria Foundation on the momentous occasion 
of its First Annual Scholarship Gala. Tonight is a special moment--not 
only because it marks a significant milestone for the foundation but 
because it celebrates the presentation of its inaugural scholarship to 
a well-deserving student. This organization has been built on the 
outstanding legacy Maria M. Luis left behind after a remarkable life 
that began in Terceira, Portugal, over 100 years ago. Maria endured 
countless hardships that began at the beginning of her life when she 
lost her mother during childbirth, causing her to step into the role of 
caregiver to her eight younger brothers and sisters. She valiantly took 
on this job and ensured that her siblings received the care and love 
that every child deserves. Maria didn't get a chance to pursue higher 
education due to her responsibilities tending to her family and other 
obstacles from her health, but this did not stop her from chasing a 
better life for herself and family. She migrated to the United States 
in 1970 and ensured that her children and grandchildren could have an 
excellent education. Now, The Tia Maria Foundation, under the 
leadership of Chairman Gavin Szilvasi, is continuing Maria's mission by 
empowering the youth of Elizabeth, New Jersey. By awarding 
scholarships, this foundation is making a tangible difference in the 
lives of students, ensuring they have the resources they need to pursue 
the higher education that Maria so greatly valued. Your foundation is 
not just awarding scholarships, but it is also about making a way for 
students to reach their highest academic potential and achieve their 
greatest dreams. This mission is something we can all be proud of, and 
I want to take a moment to thank them for the difference they are 
making in so many lives. Congratulations on a great year. I understand 
that the journey to this point has not been easy, and I commend their 
resilience and dedication. I hope they have a great time tonight and 
take tonight to celebrate the real difference their work in the 
community has had. God Bless The Tia Maria Foundation and God Bless our 
United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
