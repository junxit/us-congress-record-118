# HONORING MARK ROOT'S SERVICE TO ST. JOSEPH COUNTY, INDIANA

**Speaking:** Yakym, Rudy (Y000067) [R-IN]

```
       HONORING MARK ROOT'S SERVICE TO ST. JOSEPH COUNTY, INDIANA

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RUDY YAKYM III

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. YAKYM. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate my friend and devoted 
public servant Mark Root as he prepares to step down from the St. 
Joseph County Council after nearly two decades of service in elected 
office.
  As Councilman, Mark's commitment to meeting the needs of St. Joseph 
County Hoosiers has been deep and unwavering. Quite simply, Mark is the 
kind of person that you want in your corner as well as serving in 
government and making decisions that affect peoples' lives.
  Mark's leadership abilities have brought important projects and 
investments to St. Joseph's County over the course of his twenty years 
serving on the Council, and he has helped keep St. Joseph County on the 
move through both good economic times and bad. Mark's focus has always 
been on making his home county an even better place to live, work, and 
raise a family, something he has done through bringing to bear his 
expertise on fiscal and budgetary matters, and his commitment to being 
a responsible steward of taxpayers' dollars.
  Through his time on the Council, Mark has developed a well-deserved 
reputation for being a thoughtful leader who is easy to work with. 
Regardless of their political beliefs, so many people have testified to 
Mark's good nature, his personal loyalty, and his Hoosier common sense.
  In addition to his service to the people of St. Joseph County in 
elected office, Mark has also been a mentor to me and a close personal 
friend. In fact, Mark is the one who taught me how to comb through 
election results on a precinct-by-precinct basis, a lesson that has 
been invaluable to me in my political career.
  As Mark's time and career in public service comes to a close, I have 
absolutely no doubt that St. Joseph County and our communities are 
better off thanks to this good man with a servant's heart. I also know 
that Mark's wife Sara and his whole family are very proud of him, a 
sentiment I share wholeheartedly.
  I join Hoosiers throughout St. Joseph County and Indiana's Second 
Congressional District in expressing our gratitude to Mark Root for his 
service and in wishing him a very happy retirement and all the best 
going forward.

                          ____________________
```
