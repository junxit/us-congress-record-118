# RECOGNIZING DEVIN SCILLIAN'S SERVICE TO THE NATION AND HIS COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Dingell, Debbie (D000624) [D-MI]

```
  RECOGNIZING DEVIN SCILLIAN'S SERVICE TO THE NATION AND HIS COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DEBBIE DINGELL

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Devin Scillian 
of Grosse Pointe, Michigan, on the occasion of his retirement from WDIV 
Local 4 News in Detroit. As a local news anchor, his steady presence 
and informative programs have been a stalwart part of life in 
Southeastern Michigan for the last twenty-eight years, and his service 
to his community is worthy of commendation.
  Devin was born in Kansas in 1963 to Billie Hugh and Betti Jo 
Scillian. His late father earned four bronze stars and a Meritorious 
Service Medal over two tours in Vietnam. His mother, a writer with a 
history in community theater, he credits with being his creative spark. 
Devin started dating his wife, Corey, while they were in high school 
after his family moved off base at Fort Riley, Kansas.
  After graduating from the School of Journalism at the University of 
Kansas in 1985, Devin began his career in local news at WIBW in Topeka, 
Kansas. This was followed by stops at WAND in Decatur, Illinois, KLTV 
in Tyler, Texas, and KFOR in Oklahoma City. It was at KFOR in 1995 
where his coverage of the bombing at the Murrah Federal Building helped 
the station win a Peabody Award,
  Devin joined WDIV in Detroit in 1995 as a reporter before moving to 
the anchor position in 1996. In this position he has traveled the 
world, including stops in Beijing, the Vatican, Jerusalem, and Moscow. 
He has covered six Olympiads and has three times won the Edward R. 
Murrow Award for excellence in broadcast journalism. His weekly 
program, Flashpoint, has become almost mandatory viewing for those who 
wish to be in the know for the most pressing issues in Southeastern 
Michigan. He has used his position to champion charities, whether that 
is hosting a dinner for the Capuchin Soup Kitchen, collecting shoes for 
Lace Up Detroit, or recording a song for ``A Michigan Christmas of 
Hope''--a compilation of Christmas songs brought to you by some of 
Detroit & Michigan's Best artists and bands to benefit Holy Cross 
Children's Services.
  Outside of his career in local news, Devin is also an accomplished 
musician. He won the Detroit Music Award for Best Country Vocalist in 
2018 and Best Country Performer in 2001. He and his band ``Arizona 
Son'' have opened for artists such as Toby Kieth, Reba McEntire, and 
LeAnn Rimes. He is also the author of 22 children's books, including S 
is For Sunflower, which won the Bill Martin, Jr. Picture Book Award in 
2007.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me today in recognizing 
Devin Scillian for the lifetime of dedication to his community. From 
Kansas to Michigan, his consistent and reliable reporting has a boon 
for the community which he is serving. Though we are sad to see him 
leave, he is certainly going to continue to be a presence in our lives 
through his music and his writing. We thank him for his service and 
wish him all the happiness as he starts this next chapter in his life. 
I thank Devin.
```
