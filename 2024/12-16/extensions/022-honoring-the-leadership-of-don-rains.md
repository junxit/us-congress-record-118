# HONORING THE LEADERSHIP OF DON RAINS

**Speaking:** Nickel, Wiley (N000194) [D-NC]

```
                  HONORING THE LEADERSHIP OF DON RAINS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. WILEY NICKEL

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. NICKEL. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor Don Rains, a 
dedicated public servant and lifelong resident of Princeton, North 
Carolina, who has made significant contributions to his community 
through more than two decades of service as Mayor.
  For 22 years, Don Rains led Princeton with vision, determination, and 
an unwavering commitment to the town's future. Under his leadership, 
the town saw critical infrastructure improvements, including 
advancements in water, sewer, and stormwater management, all of which 
helped prepare Princeton for future growth.
  His leadership in key projects like the Princeton Community Center 
and the work surrounding the future Interstate 42 will have a lasting 
impact on the town and its residents for years to come.
  In addition to his work as Mayor, Don Rains has served his community 
through his involvement in Princeton United Methodist Church, the 
Princeton Lions Club, and St. Patrick's Masonic Lodge. His leadership 
extended beyond the town, serving as Chair of the Triangle J Council of 
Governments and the US 70/1-42 Commission, where he advocated for 
regional and statewide development.
  Don and his family believe deeply in giving back, and through their 
efforts, they have bolstered initiatives to enhance the quality of life 
for all Princeton residents, including support for the Princeton Public 
Library and the new Community Center.
  Please join me in recognizing Don Rains for his exemplary leadership, 
dedication, and service to Princeton, North Carolina. His contributions 
will continue to shape the community he loves for generations to come.

                          ____________________
```
