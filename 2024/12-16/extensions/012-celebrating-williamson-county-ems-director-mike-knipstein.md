# CELEBRATING WILLIAMSON COUNTY EMS DIRECTOR MIKE KNIPSTEIN

**Speaking:** Carter, John R. (C001051) [R-TX]

```
       CELEBRATING WILLIAMSON COUNTY EMS DIRECTOR MIKE KNIPSTEIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, it is my distinct honor to 
recognize Williamson County EMS Director Mike Knipstein for his 
exemplary 30-year career of service to residents. A career of such 
longevity and impact is truly commendable and deserving of recognition. 
Throughout his tenure, Director Knipstein has demonstrated unwavering 
dedication to public safety and healthcare, leaving a lasting legacy of 
excellence and compassionate service to the community.
  Mike began his career as a paramedic in 1994 and has earned the 
Williamson County EMS Paramedic of the Year award twice for his 
exceptional care. Following his promotion to Lieutenant, he pioneered 
the creation of a medical continuing education program that remains 
instrumental in training medics and first responders today.
  From 2003 to 2014, as a Commander, Mike shaped the department's 
future through professional development initiatives, infection control 
management, and strategic growth planning. As Division Commander of EMS 
Operations, he introduced budgetary tracking measures that improved 
overtime cost management.
  In 2015, Mike became Deputy Director, leading Williamson County EMS 
to achieve and maintain accreditation from the Commission on 
Accreditation of Ambulance Services, the EMS industry's ``gold 
standard.'' Since 2016, as Director, he has enhanced services by 
securing stable funding for the Community Health Paramedic Program, 
implementing innovative safety policies, and ensuring resource 
readiness during the COVID-19 pandemic.
  Director Knipstein's leadership has profoundly elevated EMS services 
and improved countless lives in Williamson County. On behalf of the 
United States House of Representatives, I extend my deepest gratitude 
for his remarkable service and wish him continued success.

                          ____________________
```
