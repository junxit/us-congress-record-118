# RECOGNIZING MATTHEW GLEASON--EAGLE SCOUT

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                RECOGNIZING MATTHEW GLEASON--EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, this afternoon, I have the privilege of 
recognizing and celebrating Matthew Gleason for achieving the great 
rank of Eagle Scout after 13 years of steadfast commitment to the Scout 
Oath and Law. Matthew's scouting career began as a Cub Scout in the 
fall of 2011, where he rapidly ascended to leadership roles, including 
Assistant Patrol Leader and Patrol Leader. Early on, he earned the 
prestigious Arrow of Light award, a testament to his dedication and 
hard work. Matthew's commitment to community service has been nothing 
short of exemplary. He logged nearly 200 hours helping neighbors, 
friends, and strangers alike, demonstrating an inspiring dedication to 
those around him. In addition to his service, he has shown impressive 
physical fitness by completing a challenging 33-mile hike through 
rigorous terrain, successfully reaching every mission-required 
destination. For his Eagle Scout project, he dedicated over 160 hours 
to the Aura Volunteer Fire Company, where he built a shed for their 
antique fire truck, preserving

[[Page E1281]]

and honoring the department's rich history. Beyond his scouting 
achievements, Matthew is a remarkable student. He has consistently made 
the honor roll ten times and has been recognized on the 
Superintendent's List five times. As a standout soccer player, he has 
lettered three times at Delsea Regional High School and has earned a 
spot on the All-County team for all three years. Looking ahead, Matthew 
has clear and ambitious goals: he plans to pursue a degree in Civil 
Engineering and eventually establish his own engineering firm. In a 
beautiful full circle moment, inspired by the tremendous support he 
received from firefighters during his project, Matthew is determined to 
serve his community as a volunteer firefighter. I am confident that 
Matthew will excel in any path he chooses, and I eagerly anticipate the 
significant impact he will make in our world. May God bless Matthew and 
God bless our United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
