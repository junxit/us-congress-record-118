# RECOGNIZING DICK LUNDY'S SERVICE TO HIS COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Dingell, Debbie (D000624) [D-MI]

```
           RECOGNIZING DICK LUNDY'S SERVICE TO HIS COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DEBBIE DINGELL

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dick Lundy, a 
resident of Dexter, Michigan, who is retiring after 45 years on the 
Dexter School Board. His decades of service to his community and its 
children are worthy of commendation.
  Dick earned a degree in electrical engineering from the University of 
Michigan and started his own business, Control-O-Matic, later COM Inc., 
in 1972. He served as its President and CEO until it was sold in 1999.
  Dick was first elected to the Dexter School Board in 1979, the first 
of eleven elections that he would win. He also spent 40 years as 
Treasurer and another four as Vice President. During his time on the 
board, he has seen a plethora of changes, from Michigan's shift to per-
student state funding in 1994 or the introduction of computers and 
every new technology since. This also includes the successful passage 
of half a dozen school bonds, the construction of multiple new 
buildings and development of the school campus, and the hiring of seven 
superintendents and two interim superintendents, in addition to the 
vast financial oversight of the district. Both of his children as well 
as three grand-children have graduated from Dexter Schools, and two 
great-grandchildren are currently enrolled.
  Outside of his time on the Dexter School Board, Dick has served on 
the board of many local nonprofits, including the Washtenaw United Way, 
the Ann Arbor Hands-On Museum, the Dexter United Methodist Church, and 
Emergent Health Partners. In addition, Dick has served on the Dexter 
Downtown Development Authority and the Local Development Finance 
Authority which created the Dexter Business and Research Park. He also 
worked with the nonprofit Michigan From the Heart, where he's assisted 
in connecting University of Michigan student athletes with patients at 
C.S. Mott Children's Hospital.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me today in celebrating Dick 
Lundy and his 45 years of service to the Dexter community. The future 
of Dexter is much brighter because of Dick's hard work. We wish him the 
best in retirement and thank him for all he has done.

                          ____________________
```
