# CELEBRATING JOHN M. BARR'S CAREER AND SERVICE, YPSILANTI, MICHIGAN

**Speaking:** Dingell, Debbie (D000624) [D-MI]

```
   CELEBRATING JOHN M. BARR'S CAREER AND SERVICE, YPSILANTI, MICHIGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DEBBIE DINGELL

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the retirement 
of John M. Barr and his dedication to the legal profession. As City 
Attorney of Ypsilanti, Michigan for decades and founder of Barr, Anhut 
and Associates, P.C., John's commitment to serving his community is 
worthy of commendation.
  John attended Michigan State University where he earned a Bachelor of 
Art in Social Science. He then earned his J.D. from the University of 
Michigan Law School. Throughout his successful legal career, John has 
been admitted into the State Bar of Michigan, the U.S. Federal District 
Court, the U.S. Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals, and the U.S. Supreme 
Court. Outside of the law, John heroically served his country as member 
of the United States Army.
  John has served as City Attorney for Ypsilanti since 1981. For over 
43 years, he has served as the chief legal advisor to the City Council, 
Manager, and various city departments. As a testament to his dedication 
to the community, John founded Barr, Anhut and Associates, P.C., which 
focuses on municipal law, estate planning/probate, real estate, and 
corporate law. His firm often provides bar recommended pro-bono 
services and John has always expected his staff to be active members of 
their community through volunteering and other service.
  Outside of his career, John has personally served in numerous 
community organizations such as the Boy Scouts of America, Emmanuel 
Lutheran Church, and Washtenaw 100. He currently serves as board member 
of HighScope Educational Research Foundation, which aims to provide 
high-quality, equitable early childhood education to every child. He 
also serves as a board member of the Michigan Municipal League 
Foundation, which aims to create financial security among local 
communities to address individual social and economic needs across a 
wide set of industries.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me today in celebrating the 
magnificent career of John M. Barr and congratulating him on his 
retirement. His dedication to the city of Ypsilanti and the broader 
Michigan community has created a more just and equitable community for 
all. I thank John for his decades of service to our community, and we 
wish him all the best as he moves on to the next chapter of his life.

                          ____________________
```
