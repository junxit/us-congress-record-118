# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF CHAIRMAN JEFFREY BOHM

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
            RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF CHAIRMAN JEFFREY BOHM

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize St. Clair County 
Commissioner Jeffrey ``Jeff'' Bohm, who will be chairing his last Board 
of Commissioners meeting this evening.
  Throughout Jeff's career as a county commissioner, Jeff has 
demonstrated an unwavering commitment to his community. Thanks largely 
to his leadership, St. Clair County has remained a great place to live 
and raise a family.
  Jeff has been known by many to start conversations and ``brag'' about 
St. Clair County--and rightfully so. Jeff has a passion for learning, 
is known to bring people together, and will always take time to listen. 
These are values reflective of any good leader.
  Jeff has always put his community first. After his service, I am 
confident that he will continue to inspire and serve his neighbors--
even without a title.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Chairman Bohm 
for over 20 years of service to his community. I am proud to

[[Page E1270]]

represent Jeff in Congress, and I wish him, his wife Carrie, and their 
two daughters, Jessica and Christina, the best of luck in the years to 
come.

                          ____________________
```
