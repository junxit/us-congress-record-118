# RECOGNIZING REVEREND KEVIN MOUANOUTOUA

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
                 RECOGNIZING REVEREND KEVIN MOUANOUTOUA

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the pastoral life of 
Reverend Kevin Mouanoutoua of the Lao Evangelical Church of Fresno.
  Reverend Kevin dedicated his life to serving the Central Valley of 
California, serving as Pastor of the Lao Evangelical Churches of 
Tulare, Porterville, and Fresno, where he taught about God's love and 
grace to many faithful congregations. He also spent decades as a social 
worker for the Fresno County Department of Social Services' Child 
Protective Services. Reverend Kevin has served as a strong leader to 
his congregations and someone who represents the compassion of Christ 
in both his words and actions.
  On behalf of my constituents in the Central Valley of California, I 
would like to congratulate Reverend Kevin Mouanoutoua on his decades of 
devoted service to faith and to others. As he enters this exciting new 
chapter in his life, I thank him for his many contributions to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
