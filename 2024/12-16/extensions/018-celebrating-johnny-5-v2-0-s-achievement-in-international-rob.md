# CELEBRATING JOHNNY 5 V2.0'S ACHIEVEMENT IN INTERNATIONAL ROBOTICS COMPETITIONS

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
   CELEBRATING JOHNNY 5 V2.0'S ACHIEVEMENT IN INTERNATIONAL ROBOTICS 
                              COMPETITIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sawyer Rinn and 
Tristan Souza, of Johnny 5 v2.0, a robotics team from the 11th District 
of Illinois, on their recent achievements in representing the United 
States at two international competitions.
  In November, Sawyer and Tristan earned 5th place at the World Robot 
Olympiad (WRO) International Finals, competing against 100 teams from 
90 countries. Their success was the result of their exceptional 
engineering, problem-solving, and innovation skills, which enabled them 
to design and program a robot capable of completing complex tasks. 
Additionally, in October, the team placed 1st at the World Robotics 
Olympiad Americas Open in Puerto Rico, where they competed against 25 
teams from 17 countries.
  Coach William Wong of Naperville leads the team. He is the founder of 
Chasewood Learning, which has produced multiple national robotics 
champions. His programs inspire children to develop STEM skills and 
pursue related careers. Sawyer Rinn, 12, from Geneva, Illinois, in his 
third year of robotics, won the 2022 WRO National Championship and 
placed 27th at the WRO International Finals. Tristan Souza, 12, from 
Hinsdale, Illinois, in his second year, reached the FIRST LEGO League 
state finals in 2022. Both aspire to study engineering at top 
universities.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to have outstanding students and mentors in 
my district who drive innovation and promote STEM career pathways. 
Please join me in congratulating Sawyer Rinn, Tristan Souza, and Coach 
William Wong on their impressive achievements.
```
