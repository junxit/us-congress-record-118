# TRIBUTE TO FRANCISCO IGNACIO FREDRICKSON

**Speaking:** Connolly, Gerald E. (C001078) [D-VA]

```
                TRIBUTE TO FRANCISCO IGNACIO FREDRICKSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. GERALD E. CONNOLLY

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 16, 2024

  Mr. CONNOLLY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of the Nativity 
Catholic School Third Grade Class, and the entire Nativity Community, 
to pay tribute to Francisco Ignacio Fredrickson. Francisco was born on 
November 27, 2015, and passed away at the age of nine on December 5, 
2024, in the loving arms of his parents. The Nativity Parish, Catholic 
School and especially Ms. Fetzer's third grade class will miss his 
ever-smiling face and positive attitude.
  In June of 2023 Francisco was diagnosed with DIPG, an aggressive form 
of childhood brain cancer that is terminal. The doctors gave him nine 
months to live, and yet Francisco persevered and lived 18 months. DIPG 
is especially devastating because the cancer is located on the brain 
stem which makes it inoperable and while it leaves their cognitive 
skills intact, it slowly takes away a person's motor skills. This 
leaves patients unable to walk, talk, move, and eventually unable to 
eat, drink or breathe, all while their minds are fully aware of what is 
happening to their bodies. While DIPG is rare--only 150 to 300 
diagnosis per year--it is devastating for the children and their 
families.
  However, much like Francisco, I do not want to focus on the illness 
but on a life lived well. Francisco loved everything to do with space 
and NASA. He was able to visit Kennedy Space Center and watch a launch 
at Cape Canaveral in Florida in the fall of 2023. Francisco was a boy 
of deep faith and was fond of joking with Nativity Catholic School's 
Priests, Monsignor Bob Cilinski, Father Bresnahan, and Father Bennett. 
He also loved a good party and often hosted wonderful gatherings at his 
grandparent's house where he and his friends could run and play. 
Francisco is survived by his mother and father, Laura and Ryan; his 
sister Mariana; his twin brothers, Gabriel and Mateo; his grandparents, 
John and Carole Fredrickson, Teresa Hernandez and Wilfredo Lizarzaburu; 
as well as his aunts and uncles, Carlos Lizarzaburu, Diana Lopez, and 
Brenna and Jamie Fredrickson.
  In the words of his mother, ``We know beyond a shadow of a doubt that 
Francisco is now where he so intensely longed to be: at Home with 
Jesus. Now that his body rests, his soul is free. And with that freedom 
he is doing all the things he was so looking forward to doing . . . 
Playing soccer, playing piano (``with both hands, like a pro!''), 
partying it up with the Saints, and finally making those Nutella 
pancakes that he said he'd make for Jesus as soon as he got to Heaven. 
Many hearts are grieving our boy's death, ours included. But know as 
well, how elated we are. Francisco didn't get to marry the love of his 
life, or to have children, or do any of those big life milestones . . . 
But that's because he was meant to skip all those and go straight to 
the absolute biggest milestone there will ever be: to go straight to 
Jesus, to hug him, to sit next to him, and to crack him up with his 
absolutely silly, lovable nature.''
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues join me in recognizing the life 
of Francisco Ignacio Fredrickson and in sending his family our sincere 
condolences. Though Francisco only lived nine short years, he brought a 
lifetime of joy to his family, friends and school community. The light 
and love he brought to the world remains and is carried forth in the 
hearts of many.

                          ____________________
```
