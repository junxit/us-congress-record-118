# GSA TECHNOLOGY ACCOUNTABILITY ACT

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 7524

```
                   GSA TECHNOLOGY ACCOUNTABILITY ACT

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar No. 688, H.R. 7524.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 7524) to amend title 40, United States Code, 
     to require the submission of reports on certain information 
     technology services funds to Congress before expenditures may 
     be made, and for other purposes.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill, 
which had been reported from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
Governmental Affairs.
  Mr. SCHUMER. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered read 
a third time and passed and that the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 7524) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
