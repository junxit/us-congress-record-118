# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been 
presented under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the 
motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 849, Benjamin J. Cheeks, of 
     California, to be United States District Judge for the 
     Southern District of California.
         Charles E. Schumer, Richard J. Durbin, Tammy Duckworth, 
           Robert P. Casey, Jr., Tammy Baldwin, Catherine Cortez 
           Masto, Debbie Stabenow, Patty Murray, Amy Klobuchar, 
           Chris Van Hollen, Jack Reed, Jeanne Shaheen, Andy Kim, 
           Margaret Wood Hassan, Elizabeth Warren, Christopher A. 
           Coons, Adam B. Schiff, Jeff Merkley.
```
