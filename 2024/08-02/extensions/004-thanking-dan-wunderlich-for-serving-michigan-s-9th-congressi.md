# THANKING DAN WUNDERLICH FOR SERVING MICHIGAN'S 9TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

**Speaking:** McClain, Lisa C. (M001136) [R-MI]

```
   THANKING DAN WUNDERLICH FOR SERVING MICHIGAN'S 9TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                                DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 2, 2024

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I wanted to take a brief opportunity to 
recognize Dan Wunderlich, who for over two years has served the People 
of Michigan's 9th District as my director of communications.
  In 2017, Dan began his career with Representative Dave Trott as an 
intern. Since then, he has served a United States Senator and three 
House Representatives including myself.
  At every opportunity, Dan has proven his devotion to his community, 
his family, his friends, and his team. He has been an integral part of 
my office, has always brightened everyone's day, and will forever be 
someone I can rely on for advice.
  We will all miss Dan's role in my office, but I am heartened that he 
will have the opportunity to spend more time with his family and find 
success in other ventures. I sincerely hope that he will come visit us 
again sometime soon.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Dan 
Wunderlich for his years of service to the United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
