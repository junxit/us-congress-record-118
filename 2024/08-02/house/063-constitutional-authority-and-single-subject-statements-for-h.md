# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9284

**Measures:** H.R. 9284

```
           By Mrs. RAMIREZ:
        H.R. 9284.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would amend the Servicemembers Civil 
     Relief Act (SCRA) to allow service members to consolidate or 
     refinance federal or private student loan debt they or they 
     and their spouse jointly incurred before they entered the 
     military and continue to benefit from the six percent student 
     loan interest rate cap to which they are entitled through the 
     SCRA.
```
