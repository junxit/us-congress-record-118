# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 199

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 199

```
            By Mr. WALBERG:
        H.J. Res. 199.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Artile 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Consitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     National Highway Traffic Safety Administration relating to 
     ``Corporate Average Fuel Economy Standards for Passenger Cars 
     and Light Trucks for Model Years 2027 and Beyond and Fuel 
     Efficiency Standards for Heavy-Duty Pickup Trucks and Vans 
     for Model Years 2030 and Beyond''
```
