# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9300

**Measures:** H.R. 9300

```
            By Ms. TOKUDA:
        H.R. 9300.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title II of the Social Security Act and the 
     Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to make improvements in the 
     old-age, survivors, and disability insurance program.
```
