# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 195

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 195

```
            By Mr. DONALDS:
        H.J. Res. 195.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art 1, Sec 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 
     of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to ``Truth 
     in Lending (Regulation Z); Use of Digital User Accounts to 
     Access Buy Now, Pay Later Loans''
```
