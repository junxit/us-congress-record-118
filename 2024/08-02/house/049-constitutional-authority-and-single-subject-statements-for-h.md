# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9270

**Measures:** H.R. 9270

```
           By Ms. LEE of Nevada:
       H.R. 9270.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 provides Congress with the 
     power to ``lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts and 
     Excises'' in order to ``provide for the . . . general Welfare 
     of the United States.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a grant program within the Department of Labor 
     to support the creation, implementation, and expansion of 
     registered apprenticeship programs in cybersecurity.
```
