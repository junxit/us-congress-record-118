# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9303

**Measures:** H.R. 9303

```
            By Ms. WATERS:
        H.R. 9303.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 3 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution, 
     which provides Congress with the power to ``regulate commerce 
     with foreign nations, and among the several states, and with 
     the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill modifies the Electronic Fund Transfer Act to 
     improve consumer protections relating to payments.
```
