# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9251

**Measures:** H.R. 9251

```
           By Mr. FEENSTRA:
       H.R. 9251.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Poultry Products Inspection Act to allow 
     poultry processing facilities to perform regular slaughter 
     and custom exempt at the same facility.
```
