# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9241

**Measures:** H.R. 9241

```
           By Mr. CARDENAS:
       H.R. 9241.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, section 1.
       All legislative powers herein granted shall be vested in a 
     Congress of the United States, which shall consist of a 
     Senate and House of Representatives.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide that weighted sleep products for infants shall 
     be considered banned hazardous products under section 8 of 
     the Consumer Product Safety Act, and for other purposes.
```
