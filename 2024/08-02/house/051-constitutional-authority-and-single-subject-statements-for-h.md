# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9272

**Measures:** H.R. 9272

```
           By Mr. LOUDERMILK:
       H.R. 9272.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 1 of Article I of the Constitution, Clause 18 of 
     Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to 
     establish new payment rules for certain catastrophic 
     specialty hospitals under the Medicare program.
```
