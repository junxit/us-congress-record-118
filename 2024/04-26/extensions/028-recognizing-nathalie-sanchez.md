# RECOGNIZING NATHALIE SANCHEZ

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING NATHALIE SANCHEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 26, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Nathalie 
Sanchez for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Nathalie has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Nathalie, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Nathalie's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Arvada K-8 and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Nathalie Sanchez on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
