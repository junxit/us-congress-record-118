# RECOGNIZING AVA CONLEY-WILLOUGHBY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                   RECOGNIZING AVA CONLEY-WILLOUGHBY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 26, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ava Conley-
Willoughby for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Ava has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Ava, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Ava's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Arvada West High School and is clearly just the beginning of a bright 
and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Ava Conley-Willoughby on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
