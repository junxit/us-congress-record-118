# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR MARC WEISS

**Speaking:** Smucker, Lloyd (S001199) [R-PA]

```
      RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR MARC WEISS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 26, 2024

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Command Sgt. Maj. Marc 
Weiss of Red Lion, Pennsylvania, for his 35 years of service in the 
Pennsylvania Army National Guard.
  Marc enlisted in the Pennsylvania National Guard in 1988. He began 
his career of military leadership at Foxtrot Battery, 109th Field 
Artillery Regiment, where he eventually became First Sergeant.
  For the last two and a half years, Marc led the 213th Regional 
Support Group. Marc's colleagues speak highly of his dedication to 
understanding units within his command, which he did to better prepare 
and lead the formations.
  Throughout his career, Marc was involved in overseas missions to 
Bosnia, Kosovo, England, and Iraq, as well as multiple state and drug 
interdiction missions.
  Upon his retirement, Marc was presented with the Legion of Merit 
award for his devoted service and career.
  Mr. Speaker, I want to thank Sgt. Maj. Weiss for his commitment to 
our Nation and his selfless service. We are indebted to the 35 years of 
sacrifice he has given. I wish him all the best in his well-earned 
retirement.
```
