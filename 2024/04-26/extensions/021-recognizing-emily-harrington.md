# RECOGNIZING EMILY HARRINGTON

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING EMILY HARRINGTON

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 26, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions of one of my constituents, Emily Harrington. She 
joined the Gulf Coast Manatees Homeschool Group in 2023 and has been an 
incredible member and volunteer that goes above and beyond 
expectations. Although she is somewhat new, Emily jumped right in with 
facilitating classes, becoming the Volunteer Coordinator and helps 
assist with anything needed in the group. Emily spearheads the Nature 
Journaling class and has a passion for teaching children. She is a 
natural leader. She loves to volunteer and offers ideas for the co-op. 
She always gets involved in each community service projects and helps 
with community outreach. Emily teaches the younger age group and they 
love her. It is easy to see the joy she feels as they learn and grow. 
She is truly an unsung hero, as it is important to have a great role 
model, friend and fellow teacher by one's side.
  Part of what makes our country so great is the willingness of 
ordinary Americans to go above and beyond to selflessly give back to 
others. Volunteerism is one of the cornerstones of our society and part 
of the fabric that unites us.
  It is clear that Ms. Harrington has made a profoundly positive impact 
on our community, and I am grateful for her service.

                          ____________________
```
