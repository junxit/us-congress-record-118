# DESIGNATION OF MR. MOOLENAAR AS CHAIR OF SELECT COMMITTEE ON THE STRATEGIC COMPETITION BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND THE CHINESE COMMUNIST PARTY

**Measures:** H.Res. 11, H.Res. 78

```
   DESIGNATION OF MR. MOOLENAAR AS CHAIR OF SELECT COMMITTEE ON THE 
    STRATEGIC COMPETITION BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND THE CHINESE 
                            COMMUNIST PARTY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces the Speaker's 
designation, pursuant to section 1(a)(2)(A) of House Resolution 11, 
118th Congress, as amended by section 1(b) of House Resolution 78, 
118th Congress, and the order of the House of January 9, 2023, of the 
following member of the Select Committee on the Strategic Competition 
Between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party as its chair:
  Mr. Moolenaar, Michigan.

                          ____________________
```
