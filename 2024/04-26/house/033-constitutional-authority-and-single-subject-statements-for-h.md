# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8138

**Measures:** H.R. 8138

```
           By Mr. SOTO:
       H.R. 8138.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Agriculture to establish a 
     disaster response training program to train certain Farm 
     Service Agency employees to provide outreach and technical 
     assistance to farmers and ranchers affected by a natural 
     disaster, and for other purposes.
```
