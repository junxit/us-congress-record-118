# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8657

**Measures:** H.R. 8657

```
            By Mr. HIGGINS of Louisiana:
        H.R. 8657.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art. 1, Sec. 8, Clause 3--The Congress shall have Power. . 
     . To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the 
     several States, and with the Indian Tribes.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Commerce to produce a report 
     that provides recommendations to improve the effectiveness, 
     efficiency, and impact of Department of Commerce programs 
     related to supply chain resilience and manufacturing and 
     industrial innovation.
```
