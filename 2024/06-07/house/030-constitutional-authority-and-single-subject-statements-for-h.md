# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8663

**Measures:** H.R. 8663

```
            By Mr. LALOTA:
        H.R. 8663.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is;
       To require the Science and Technology Directorate in the 
     Department of Homeland Security to develop greater capacity 
     to detect, identify, and disrupt illicit substances in very 
     law concentrations.
```
