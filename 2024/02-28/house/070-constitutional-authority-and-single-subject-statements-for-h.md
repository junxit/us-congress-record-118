# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7464

**Measures:** H.R. 7464

```
           By Mr. YAKYM:
       H.R. 7464.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would decrease the VA community care 
     access standards for mental healthcare to five days for 
     veterans that are over 50 percent disabled for a mental 
     health disorder.
```
