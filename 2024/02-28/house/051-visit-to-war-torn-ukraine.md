# VISIT TO WAR-TORN UKRAINE

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                       VISIT TO WAR-TORN UKRAINE

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, as a result of intense 
fighting on the front line, a Ukrainian soldier's leg was blown off. 
However, he lived to share his story with me. He now uses a prosthetic 
leg to stand.
  February 24 marked 2 years that Ukraine has successfully resisted 
Russia's unprovoked invasion.
  During my recent visit to war-torn Ukraine, I witnessed the 
undeniable resilience of its people. While Ukrainian soldiers have 
given so much, including life and limbs, much like this soldier, we 
must not ignore their urgent plea for assistance to finish the fight.
  Democracies around the world look to us, and the eyes of the world 
are on

[[Page H735]]

us, including Putin's. Ukrainians are fighting tirelessly on the front 
lines, not just for their nation, but for democracy.
  Mr. Speaker, in the face of these threats, the time to act is now.

                          ____________________
```
