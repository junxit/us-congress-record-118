            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

             Bennet, Mrs. Murray, Mr. Welch, Mr. Sanders, Mr.

             Hirono, Mr. Durbin, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Kaine, Mrs. 
             Capito, and Mr. Braun):
       S. Res. 568. A resolution designating the week of February 
     26 through March 1, 2024, as ``Public Schools Week''; 
     considered and agreed to.
