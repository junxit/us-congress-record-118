# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church, Washington Parish, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Loving God, You have made every single one of us in Your image, to 
serve You in freedom and in peace. Give to the people of our country a 
zeal for justice and the strength of forbearance, that we may use our 
liberty in accordance with Your gracious will.
  Amen.
```
