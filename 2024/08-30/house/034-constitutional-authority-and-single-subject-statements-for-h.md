# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 201

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 201

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.J. Res. 201.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To provide for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Bureau of Consumer Financial Protection relating to 
     ``Registry of Nonbank Covered Persons Subject to Certain 
     Agency and Court Orders''.
```
