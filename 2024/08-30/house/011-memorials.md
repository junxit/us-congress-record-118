# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 155

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       ML-148. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of 
     the State of Ohio, relative to Senate Resolution 155, to 
     express support for a consumer choice in the automotive 
     marketplace and against government-preferred vehicle 
     technology mandates that will harm Ohio's consumers by 
     prematurely requiring the transaction of the automotive fleet 
     from internal combustion powered vehicles to all-electric 
     vehicles; which was referred to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.
```
