# CONGRATULATING SHANNON W. SWING ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE FEDERAL AVIATION ADMINISTRATION

**Speaking:** Allen, Rick W. (A000372) [R-GA]

```
  CONGRATULATING SHANNON W. SWING ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE FEDERAL 
                        AVIATION ADMINISTRATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RICK W. ALLEN

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 30, 2024

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and congratulate 
Shannon W. Swing on his retirement from the Federal Aviation 
Administration.
  Shannon faithfully served this institution for 32 years, first in 
Anchorage, Alaska, then in Salt Lake City, Utah and finally in Boise, 
Idaho. With his calm demeanor, Shannon quickly became a leader in the 
organization, training new employees and being a steadfast presence in 
the tower.
  His contributions to ensuring a safe airspace were invaluable to 
providing efficient and secure travel for passengers across the country 
and world. We thank Shannon for his dedication and congratulate him on 
his well-earned retirement.

                          ____________________
```
