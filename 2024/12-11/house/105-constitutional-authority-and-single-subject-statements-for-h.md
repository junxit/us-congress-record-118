# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10375

**Measures:** H.R. 10375

```
           By Mr. TORRES of New York:
       H.R. 10375.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act and the Mutual 
     Education and Cultural Exchange Act of 1961 to strengthen the 
     critical minerals workforce in the United States.
```
