# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10372

**Measures:** H.R. 10372

```
           By Mr. SCHNEIDER:
       H.R. 10372.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit schools and public libraries that receive 
     universal service support from blocking internet access to 
     lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, and queer resources.
```
