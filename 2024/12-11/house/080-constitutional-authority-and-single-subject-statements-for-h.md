# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10350

**Measures:** H.R. 10350

```
           By Mr. STEUBE:
       H.R. 10350.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act to designate the 
     portion of the Myakka River lying within Sarasota County, 
     Florida as a component of the National Wild and Scenic Rivers 
     System, and for other purposes.
```
