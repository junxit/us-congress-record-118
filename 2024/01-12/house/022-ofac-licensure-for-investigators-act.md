# OFAC LICENSURE FOR INVESTIGATORS ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 6370

```
                  OFAC LICENSURE FOR INVESTIGATORS ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 6370) to require the 
Office of Foreign Assets Control to develop a program under which 
private sector firms may receive a license to conduct nominal financial 
transactions in furtherance of the firms' investigations, and for other 
purposes, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from North Carolina (Mr. McHenry) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
