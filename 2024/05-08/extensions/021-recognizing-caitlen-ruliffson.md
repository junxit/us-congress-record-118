                    in the house of representatives

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Caitlen 
Ruliffson for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for

demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make

work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives.

perseverance at Jefferson Jr./Sr. High School and is clearly just the 
beginning of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Caitlen Ruliffson on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
