**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                      BIDENFLATION HURTS FAMILIES

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)

and destroy jobs.

24 percent, and coffee is up 20 percent.
  Corrupt Judge Merchan has unintentionally confirmed the deranged Big 
Government corruption to defame Donald Trump. This helps Donald Trump. 
This corrupt judge now will be my guest. I invite him to come in 
January

causing.

dictators as more 9/11 attacks across America are imminent, as 
repeatedly warned by the FBI.
```
