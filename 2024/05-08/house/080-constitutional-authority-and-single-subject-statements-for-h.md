# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8293

**Measures:** H.R. 8293

```
            By Mr. SCHWEIKERT:
        H.R. 8293.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 1 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution 
     (Taxing and Spending Clause)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide for 
     the public reporting of data on certain contributions 
     received by tax-exempt organizations from foreign sources, 
     and for other purposes.
```
