# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8321

**Measures:** H.R. 8321

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 8321.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       To send any person convicted of unlawful activity on a 
     college campus on or since October 7, 2023 to Gaza for the 
     purpose of providing community service.
```
