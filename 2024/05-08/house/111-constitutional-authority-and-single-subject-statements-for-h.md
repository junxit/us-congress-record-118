# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8324

**Measures:** H.R. 8324

```
           By Mr. ROGERS of Kentucky:
       H.R. 8324.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To designate the United States courthouse annex located at 
     310 South Main Street in London, Kentucky as the ``Eugene E. 
     Siler, Jr. United States Courthouse Annex''.
```
