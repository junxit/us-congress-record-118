# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8302

**Measures:** H.R. 8302

```
           By Mr. DAVIDSON:
       H.R. 8302.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a commission to review the programs of the 
     Department of Housing and Urban Development and make 
     recommendations for legislative reforms
```
