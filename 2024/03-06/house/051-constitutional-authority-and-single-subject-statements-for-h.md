# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7562

**Measures:** H.R. 7562

```
            By Mr. DAVIDSON:
        H.R. 7562.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Reserve Act to prohibit Federal 
     reserve banks from paying interest on excess reserves.
```
