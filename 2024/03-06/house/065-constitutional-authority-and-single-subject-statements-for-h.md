# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 116

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 116

```
            By Mr. KILEY:
        H.J. Res. 116.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Department of the Labor relating to ``Employee or Independent 
     Contractor Classification Under the Fair Labor Standards 
     Act''
```
