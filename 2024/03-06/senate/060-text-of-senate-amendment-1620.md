# Text of Senate Amendment 1620

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
  SA 1620. Mr. SCHUMER submitted an amendment intended to be proposed 
by him to the bill H.R. 4366, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, and for other purposes; 
as follows:

       At the end add the following:

     SEC. EFFECTIVE DATE.

       This Act shall take effect on the date that is 3 days after 
     the date of enactment of this Act.
                                 ______
```
