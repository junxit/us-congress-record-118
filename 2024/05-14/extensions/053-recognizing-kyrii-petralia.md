# RECOGNIZING KYRII PETRALIA

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING KYRII PETRALIA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 14, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kyrii Petralia 
for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Kyrii has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Kyrii, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Kyrii's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
The Manning School of Academics and Arts and is clearly just the 
beginning of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Kyrii Petralia on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
