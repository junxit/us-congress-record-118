# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8391

**Measures:** H.R. 8391

```
           By Mr. MOYLAN:
       H.R. 8391.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 1156 of the Water Resource Development Act of 1986 
     (33 U.S.C. 2310).
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Water Resource Development Act of 1986 with 
     respect to cost sharing provisions for the territories and 
     Indian Tribes, and for other purposes.
```
