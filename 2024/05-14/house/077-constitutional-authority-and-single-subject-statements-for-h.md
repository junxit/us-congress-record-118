# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8382

**Measures:** H.R. 8382

```
            By Mr. HORSFORD:
        H.R. 8382.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article 1 of the U.S. Constitution.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        This bill extends the tax exclusion of the combat zone 
     compensation of enlisted personnel and commissioned offices 
     to include individuals operating a remotely piloted aircraft 
     in a combat zone or providing certain intelligence with 
     respect to such aircraft.
```
