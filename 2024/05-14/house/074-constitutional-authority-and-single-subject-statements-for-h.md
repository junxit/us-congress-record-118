# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8379

**Measures:** H.R. 8379

```
            By Mr. GOTTHEIMER:
        H.R. 8379.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I Section 8
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        The ETA Act codifies important recent rule changes to 
     promote airline transparency and protect passengers from the 
     Department of Transportation, so they can't be changed on a 
     whim by a future Administration.
```
