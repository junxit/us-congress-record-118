# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8385

**Measures:** H.R. 8385

```
            By Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI:
        H.R. 8385.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, Clause I
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to 
     ensure the safety of food and limit the presence of 
     contaminants in infant and toddler food, and for other 
     purposes.
```
