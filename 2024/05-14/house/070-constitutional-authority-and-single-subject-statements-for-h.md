# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8375

**Measures:** H.R. 8375

```
            By Ms. CARAVEO:
        H.R. 8375.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Constitutional Authority--Necessary and Proper Clause 
     (Art. I, Sec. 8, Clause 18)
        THE U.S. CONSTITUTION
        ARTICLE I, SECTION 8: POWERS OF CONGRESS
        CLAUSE 18
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        To require the Secretary of Health and Human Services to 
     issue guidance to States on strategies under Medicaid and 
     CHIP to increase pediatric mental and behavioral health 
     provider education, training, recruitment, retention, and 
     support, and for other purposes.
```
