# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray. Eternal Father, thank You for this day, for a land of 
spacious skies, bountiful grain, majestic mountains, and fruited 
plains. We accept this day as a gift from Your bounty, and we will use 
it for the glory of Your Name.
  As our lawmakers strive to do what is best for this great land, lead 
them with Your might. Guide them by Your higher wisdom and help them 
know the constancy of Your presence. Lord, give them the greatness of 
being on Your side and the delight of knowing they are doing Your will. 
As they seek to be responsive to Your leading, keep our Senators' 
hearts riveted on You. Make them stewards of the blessings You daily 
provide.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
