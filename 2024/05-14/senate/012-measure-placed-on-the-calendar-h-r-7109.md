# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 7109

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 7109

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 7109

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at the 
desk due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill by 
title for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:


[[Page S3673]]


  

       A bill (H.R. 7109) to require a citizenship question on the 
     decennial census, to require reporting on certain census 
     statistics, and to modify apportionment of Representatives to 
     be based on United States citizens instead of all 
     individuals.

  Mr. SCHUMER. In order to place the bill on the Calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceeding.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the 
bill will be placed on the Calendar.
  I yield the floor.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Republican leader.

                          ____________________
```
