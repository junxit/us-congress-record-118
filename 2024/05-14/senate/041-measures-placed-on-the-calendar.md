# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 7109

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bill was read the second time, and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 7109. An act to require a citizenship question on the 
     decennial census, to require reporting on certain census 
     statistics, and to modify apportionment of Representatives to 
     be based on United States citizens instead of all 
     individuals.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S3683, May 14, 2024, in the third column, the following 
appears: H.R. 7109. An act to require . . . United States citizens 
instead of all persons.
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: H.R. 7109. An act 
to require . . . United States citizens instead of all 
individuals.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 




                          ____________________
```
