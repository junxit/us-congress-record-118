# TRIBUTE TO SAM NINER

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                          TRIBUTE TO SAM NINER

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Sam for his hard work as an intern in the 
Energy and Natural Resources Committee. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Sam is a native of Virginia. He is currently a sophomore at George 
Mason University. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has 
made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is 
reflected in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Sam for the dedication he has shown while working for 
me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. I 
know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. I 
wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
