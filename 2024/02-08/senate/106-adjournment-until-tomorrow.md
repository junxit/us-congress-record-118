# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 553

```
                       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the previous 
order.
  There being no objection, under the previous order and pursuant to S. 
Res. 553, as a further mark of respect to the late Jean A. Carnahan, 
former Senator from Missouri, the Senate, at 6:18 p.m., adjourned until 
Friday, February 9, 2024, at 12 noon.

                          ____________________
```
