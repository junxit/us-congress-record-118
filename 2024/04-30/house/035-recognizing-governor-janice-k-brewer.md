# RECOGNIZING GOVERNOR JANICE K. BREWER

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                 RECOGNIZING GOVERNOR JANICE K. BREWER

  (Mrs. LESKO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize someone who is 
undoubtedly one of the greatest public servants my State has ever 
known, Janice K. Brewer.
  Jan Brewer served Arizona for decades, starting in the State House of 
Representatives and then in the State Senate where she was majority 
whip. She also served on the Maricopa County Board of Supervisors and 
then as Arizona's Secretary of State. After a gubernatorial vacancy, 
Jan became Arizona's 22nd Governor and was overwhelmingly reelected for 
a second term.
  Governor Brewer is someone I have admired for her independence and 
convictions. She dedicated her time in office to leave our State better 
than she found it, and her efforts helped lay the foundation for 
Arizona's current success and prosperity.
  Arizona's Eighth Congressional District thanks Governor Jan Brewer 
for her amazing legacy of service to the Grand Canyon State.

                          ____________________
```
