# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8175

**Measures:** H.R. 8175

```
           By Mr. GOLDEN of Maine:
       H.R. 8175.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to prohibit 
     501(c)(4) entities from using more than 10 percent of total 
     expenditures on certain political expenditures.
```
