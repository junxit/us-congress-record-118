# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8170

**Measures:** H.R. 8170

```
           By Mr. EDWARDS:
       H.R. 8170.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       ``Regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among the 
     several states, and with the Indian tribes.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establishes an exemption application for states to apply 
     for a waiver of the route's mileage limitation if the final 
     alignment, as determined through the NEPA documentation 
     process, exceeds the current Appalachian Development Highway 
     System mileage limitation.
```
