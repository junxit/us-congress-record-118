# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8167

**Measures:** H.R. 8167

```
           By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
       H.R. 8167.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would authorize grant funding for research in the 
     orthotic and prosthetic (O&P) medical field and would 
     establish a centralized collection of O&P outcomes-based 
     research.
```
