# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 815

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on April 23, 2024, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker pro tempore (Mrs. Kiggans) has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 815. An act making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, 
     and for other purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the enrolled bill was signed on April 23, 2024, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, by the President pro tempore (Mrs. Murray).

                          ____________________
```
