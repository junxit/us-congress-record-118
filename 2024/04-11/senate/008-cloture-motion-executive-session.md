# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the

     close debate on the nomination of Executive Calendar No. 478, 
     Ramona Villagomez Manglona, of the Northern Mariana Islands, 
     to be Judge for the District Court for the Northern Mariana 
     Islands for a term of ten years. (Reappointment)
```
