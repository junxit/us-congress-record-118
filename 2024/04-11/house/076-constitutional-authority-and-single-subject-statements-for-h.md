# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7958

**Measures:** H.R. 7958

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 7958.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Director of the National Institutes of 
     Health from conducting or funding research that causes 
     significant pain or distress to a dog or cat.
```
