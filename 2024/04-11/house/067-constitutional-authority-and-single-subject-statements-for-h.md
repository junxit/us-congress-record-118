# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7948

**Measures:** H.R. 7948

```
           By Mr. FOSTER:
       H.R. 7948.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to the power granted to 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the 
     United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Adds an allowance for broadband internet to the utility 
     allowance provided to families with children in HUD housing 
     programs.
```
