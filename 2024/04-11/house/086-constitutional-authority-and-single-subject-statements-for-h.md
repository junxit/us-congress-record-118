# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7968

**Measures:** H.R. 7968

```
           By Mr. SORENSEN:
       H.R. 7968.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Public Works and Economic Development Act of 
     1965 to provide for the establishment of a Critical Supply 
     Chain Site Development grant program, and for other purposes.
```
