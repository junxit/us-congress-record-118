# SUPPORTING ISRAEL ASSISTANCE

**Speaking:** Davis, Donald G. (D000230) [D-NC]

```
                      SUPPORTING ISRAEL ASSISTANCE

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I wholeheartedly stand in 
full support of the $14.3 billion aid package to our ally, Israel.
  Let me be clear. I stand firmly with Israel, and we must take swift 
and decisive action against Hamas.
  It is heartbreaking that innocent people are caught in the crossfire, 
but we must honor the laws of armed conflict. We must never waver.
  It is essential that we continue our efforts to defeat Hamas 
extremists and get all hostages back with their loved ones, hostages 
like Keith Siegel from North Carolina, who was known as a quiet and 
kind man. We must bring him home to his wife, Aviva, and his family.
  Bring them home now. We must never ever lose focus here.

                          ____________________
```
