# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7959

**Measures:** H.R. 7959

```
           By Mr. MEUSER:
       H.R. 7959.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Make an illegal alien deportable and inadmissible to the 
     United States upon admission or conviction of trespassing.
```
