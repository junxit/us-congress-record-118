# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7970

**Measures:** H.R. 7970

```
           By Ms. WILLIAMS of Georgia:
       H.R. 7970.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, section 8, clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid 
     Services to issue a rule requiring direct-to-consumer 
     television advertisements for prescription drugs that are 
     covered by Medicare or Medicaid to include a textual 
     statement of the drug's list price.
```
