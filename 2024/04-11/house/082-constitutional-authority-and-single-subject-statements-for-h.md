# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7964

**Measures:** H.R. 7964

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 7964.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Prohibits federal funds be used to establish, implement, or 
     enforce any COVID-19 mask mandate.
```
