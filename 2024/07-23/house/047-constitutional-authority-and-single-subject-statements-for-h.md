# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9100

**Measures:** H.R. 9100

```
           By Mr. GAETZ:
       H.R. 9100.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to direct the court 
     to deduct the portion of a sentence imposed in violation of 
     law from the upper limit of the applicable sentencing 
     guidelines on resentencing.
```
