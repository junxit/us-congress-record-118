# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9105

**Measures:** H.R. 9105

```
           By Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI:
       H.R. 9105.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause I
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to ensure 
     the safety of food and limit the presence of contaminants in 
     infant and toddler food, and for other purposes.
```
