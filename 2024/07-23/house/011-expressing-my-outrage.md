# EXPRESSING MY OUTRAGE

**Speaking:** Ramirez, Delia C. (R000617) [D-IL]

```
                         EXPRESSING MY OUTRAGE

  (Mrs. RAMIREZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. RAMIREZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise to express my outrage on the eve of 
Prime Minister Netanyahu's joint address.
  His address is a slap in the face to the thousands and thousands of 
Americans who have been demanding a cease-fire, the people of 
conscience opposed to U.S.-supplied bombs killing women and children, 
and the 12 U.S. Government officials who have resigned protesting the 
national security and moral crisis our policies are creating.
  We cannot work toward peace while giving a war criminal the floor or 
exert moral authority while supporting the killing of children and 
civilians nor defend national interests while eroding our relationships 
across the world.
  We must listen to the American people and our officials and 
diplomats, like Major Harrison Mann, who will be standing with me 
tomorrow to demand the administration and our leadership change course.
  I hear them. What they say is: Not one more bomb, not one more 
dollar, not one more excuse for Netanyahu's atrocities.

                          ____________________
```
