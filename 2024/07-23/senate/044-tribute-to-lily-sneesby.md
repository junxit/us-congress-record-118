# TRIBUTE TO LILY SNEESBY

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO LILY SNEESBY

● Mr. ROUNDS. Madam President, today I recognize Lily Sneesby, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she has 
done on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Ms. Sneesby 
is a graduate of Spearfish High School in Spearfish, SD. Currently, she 
is attending Pace University NYC, where she studies business management 
and finance. Ms. Sneesby is a dedicated and diligent individual who has 
been devoted to getting the most out of her internship experience. She 
has been a true asset to my office. I extend my sincere thanks and 
appreciation to Ms. Sneesby for all of the work she has done and wish 
her continued success in the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
