# Vote on Kiko Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Kiko Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the question is, 
Will the Senate advise and consent to the Kiko nomination?
  Mrs. BLACKBURN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.

(Mr. Vance).
  Further, if present and voting: the Senator from North Dakota (Mr. 
Hoeven) would have voted ``yea'' and the Senator from Kansas (Mr. 
Marshall) would have voted ``yea.''
```
