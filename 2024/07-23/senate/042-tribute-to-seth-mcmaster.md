# TRIBUTE TO SETH McMASTER

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO SETH McMASTER

● Mr. ROUNDS. Madam President, today I recognize Seth McMaster, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work he has 
done on behalf of my office and the State of South Dakota. Mr. McMaster 
is a graduate of O'Gorman High School in Sioux Falls, SD. Currently, he 
is attending Arizona State University, where he studies business law 
and business public policy and public service. Mr. McMaster is a 
dedicated and diligent individual who has been devoted to getting the 
most out of his internship experience. He has been a true asset to my 
office. I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Mr. McMaster for 
all of the work he has done and wish him continued success in the years 
to come.●

                          ____________________
```
