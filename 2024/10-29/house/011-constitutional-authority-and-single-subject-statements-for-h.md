# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10061

**Measures:** H.R. 10061

```
           By Mr. BANKS:
       H.R. 10061.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To restore Congress's authority over immigration by 
     tightening the requirements and restrictions for the use of 
     Temporary Protected Status (TPS), immigration parole and the 
     handling of unaccompanied minors.
```
