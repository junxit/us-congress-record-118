# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10075

**Measures:** H.R. 10075

```
           By Mr. OGLES:
       H.R. 10075.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit federal funds going to entities which provide 
     any medical or surgical intervention for the purpose of 
     assisting an individual's disassociation from his or her sex.
```
