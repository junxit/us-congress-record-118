# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10066

**Measures:** H.R. 10066

```
           By Mr. GAETZ:
       H.R. 10066.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 Clause 18, Article 1 Section 8 Clause 4
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To transfer the criminal offense of voting by aliens from 
     title 18, United States Code, to the Voting Rights Act of 
     1965.
```
