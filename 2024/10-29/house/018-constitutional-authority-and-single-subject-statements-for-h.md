# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10068

**Measures:** H.R. 10068

```
           By Mr. GRIFFITH:
       H.R. 10068.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require power producers to announce plant retirements 
     and allow for the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission to 
     issue reliability orders.
```
