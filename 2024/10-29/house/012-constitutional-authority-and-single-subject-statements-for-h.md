# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10062

**Measures:** H.R. 10062

```
           By Mr. BIGGS:
       H.R. 10062.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The single subject of this bill is to revise DC Code to 
     clarify that meetings held with federal government officials 
     in the District of Columbia do not constitute doing business 
     in the District of Columbia for purposes of determining 
     whether organizations are required to register with the 
     District of Columbia.
```
