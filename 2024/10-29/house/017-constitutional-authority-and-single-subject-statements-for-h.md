# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10067

**Measures:** H.R. 10067

```
           By Mr. GAETZ:
       H.R. 10067.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to exclude from 
     tax-exempt status entities which provide goods or services to 
     individuals who are not citizens or nationals of the United 
     States.
```
