# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, APRIL 11, 2024, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY FUMIO KISHIDA, PRIME MINISTER OF JAPAN

**Speaking:** Pfluger, August (P000048) [R-TX]

```
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, APRIL 11, 
  2024, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY 
                 FUMIO KISHIDA, PRIME MINISTER OF JAPAN

  Mr. PFLUGER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Thursday, April 11, 2024, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Fumio Kishida, Prime Minister 
of Japan.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
