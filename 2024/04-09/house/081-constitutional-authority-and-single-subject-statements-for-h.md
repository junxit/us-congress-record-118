# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7899

**Measures:** H.R. 7899

```
           By Mr. EZELL:
       H.R. 7899.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Attorney General, in consulation with the 
     Secretary of Health and Human Services to promulgate the 
     final regulations required under section 311 (h)(2) of the 
     Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 831(h)(2))
```
