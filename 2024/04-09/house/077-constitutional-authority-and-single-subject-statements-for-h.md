# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7895

**Measures:** H.R. 7895

```
           By Mr. BOST:
       H.R. 7895.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution, 
     which states ``[t]he Congress shall have power to lay and 
     collect taxes, duties, imposts and excises, to pay the debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States; but all duties, imposed and excises shall be 
     uniform throughout the United States''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Restoration of entitlement for educational assistance and 
     disapproval of educational institutions for violating certain 
     provisions
```
