# HONORING SOUTHERN OPPORTUNITY FOUNDATION

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                HONORING SOUTHERN OPPORTUNITY FOUNDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 9, 2024

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
remarkable organization, Southern Opportunity Foundation.
  The Southern Opportunity Foundation is a non-profit dedicated to 
positive change in the South. The Southern Opportunity Foundation 
organization offers a range of impactful programs designed to address 
social and economic disparities in the Southern region. From education 
and workforce development to healthcare access and community 
empowerment, we strive to make a lasting difference.
  The Southern Opportunity Foundation healthcare programs are designed 
to break down barriers to access, providing essential services and 
support to underserved communities across the Southern region. From 
preventive care and health education to facilitating access to medical 
resources, we are dedicated to promoting a culture to wellness and 
resilience.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Southern 
Opportunity Foundation for their dedication and tenacity to serving 
their community and desire to be an example for all.
```
