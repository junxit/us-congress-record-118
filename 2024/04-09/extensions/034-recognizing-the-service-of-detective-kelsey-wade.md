# RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF DETECTIVE KELSEY WADE

**Speaking:** Norman, Ralph (N000190) [R-SC]

```
            RECOGNIZING THE SERVICE OF DETECTIVE KELSEY WADE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RALPH NORMAN

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 9, 2024

  Mr. NORMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the exemplary 
actions of Detective Kelsey Wade of the Sumter Police Department, whose 
quick thinking and bravery undoubtedly saved a life on the night of 
February 9, 2024.
  On that fateful evening, officers were dispatched to respond to a 
shooting incident on Thomas Drive. Among them was Detective Kelsey 
Wade, who arrived at the scene promptly and without hesitation.
  Upon her arrival, she encountered a male victim with a serious 
gunshot wound to his neck. Undeterred by the severity of the situation, 
Detective Wade immediately sprang into action, identifying the severed 
artery and pinching it shut while providing first aid to the victim.
  Detective Wade's decisive actions undoubtedly played a pivotal role 
in stabilizing the victim's condition and ensuring that he received the 
necessary medical attention in a timely manner. Her selfless dedication 
to duty and unwavering commitment to protecting and serving her 
community exemplify the highest ideals of law enforcement.
  In recognition of her heroic efforts and exemplary service, I commend 
Detective Kelsey Wade for her bravery and professionalism. Her actions 
on the night of February 9th are a testament to her exceptional 
character and the values she upholds as a member of the Sumter Police 
Department's Investigations Division.
  Please join me in expressing our heartfelt gratitude and appreciation 
to Detective Kelsey Wade for her selfless service and dedication to the 
safety and well-being of our community.

                          ____________________
```
