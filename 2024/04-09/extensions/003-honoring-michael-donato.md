# HONORING MICHAEL DONATO

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                        HONORING MICHAEL DONATO

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 9, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate 
Michael Donato for his service at Fort Pulaski National Monument.
  Michael began working for the National Park Service in Washington, 
D.C. as a park guide. In 2021, Michael moved to Savannah, Georgia to 
become an Administrative Officer at Ft. Pulaski.
  He was promoted to the Chief of Business Management and Operations in 
2023.
  In just a few years, Michael has contributed greatly to the park's 
organization and development while coordinating beneficial partnerships 
for the park.
  His partnership with the Friends of the Cockspur Island Lighthouse 
helped relight the historic lighthouse. He also worked with Chatham 
County to enhance the facilities at the Lazaretto Creek Boat Ramp.
  Michael has taken a new position back in D.C. as Chief of Staff for 
National Capital Parks East, but his contributions will serve as a 
reminder of his public service for generations to come.
```
