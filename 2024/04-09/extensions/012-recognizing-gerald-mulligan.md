# RECOGNIZING GERALD MULLIGAN

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING GERALD MULLIGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 9, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions made in our community by one of my constituents, 
Gerald Mulligan. ``Gerry'' as he is lovingly referred to, is a 
prominent figure in Citrus County, known for his dedication to 
community development and service. As the former Publisher of the 
Citrus County Chronicle, Gerry has used his platform to advocate for 
various causes over the years, notably the establishment of a YMCA in 
the area. His commitment to this project spans back to the 1990s, and 
he played a pivotal role in its development. His tireless efforts as a 
volunteer, advocate, and community leader are highlighted by his co-
chairing the capital campaign committee to build a YMCA in Citrus 
County, which successfully raised over $8 million in four years, 
allowing the YMCA to be built without incurring any debt. Being so 
engaged and associated with so many in the community, Gerry Mulligan 
played a role as a connector in nearly every relationship the YMCA 
established during the capital campaign. His leadership roles, 
including serving as the first Advisory Council Chair of the Citrus 
Memorial Health Foundation YMCA (where he continues to serve as a 
Council member today) and serving on the YMCA of the Suncoast's Board 
of Directors, highlight his commitment to the YMCA and the community. 
You can often find Gerry at any of the special events at the Y, as he 
continues to be an active YMCA member and volunteer. Gerry's 
involvement in the community extends beyond his role at the YMCA. He 
actively engages in numerous volunteer efforts and civic organizations, 
including the Rotary Club of Crystal River and a founder of the United 
Way in Citrus County. Gerry's civic contributions have been widely 
recognized, as evidenced by his induction into the Florida Newspaper 
Hall of Fame and his recognition as Citizen of the Year for Citrus 
County in 2021. Gerry Mulligan's leadership, support, and advocacy 
create a lasting legacy in Citrus County. In addition to the work he 
continues to do with the

[[Page E312]]

YMCA in Citrus County, his multifaceted involvement across various 
sectors demonstrates his dedication to making his community a better 
place for its residents. Gerry's work as a community champion continues 
to earn him the admiration of his fellow citizens and the well-deserved 
recognition.
  It is clear that Mr. Mulligan has made a profoundly positive impact 
on our community, and I am grateful for his service.

                          ____________________
```
