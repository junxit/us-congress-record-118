# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mrs. Murray).
  The legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                Washington, DC, November 12, 2024.
     To the Senate:
  Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the Standing Rules of 
the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable George S. Helmy, a Senator 
from the State of New Jersey, to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                     Patty Murray,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. HELMY thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________
```
