# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, we have humbled ourselves, prayed, and sought You. We 
have turned from evil, believing that You have heard and forgiven us 
and will bring healing to our land. You have spoken, so let Your will 
be done.
  In this season of transition, bless President Joseph Biden and 
President-elect Donald Trump. Lord, use them to enable our Nation and 
world to lead peaceful and quiet lives that are godly and dignified in 
every way.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.
```
