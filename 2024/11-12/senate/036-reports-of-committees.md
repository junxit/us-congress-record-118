                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

  The following reports of committees were submitted:

       By Mr. PETERS, from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs, with an amendment in the nature of a 
     substitute:
       S. 3635. A bill to improve the President's Cup 
     Cybersecurity Competitions (Rept. No. 118-234).

     reform the management of Federal records, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. No. 118-235).
       S. 4181. A bill to require the development of a workforce 
     plan for the Federal Emergency Management Agency (Rept. No.

     governments to directly request fire management assistance 
     declarations and grants, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 
     118-237).
       S. 4715. A bill to require the National Cyber Director to 
     submit to Congress a plan to establish an institute within 
     the Federal Government to serve as a centralized resource and 
     training center for Federal cyber workforce development 
     (Rept. No. 118-238).
       By Mr. PETERS, from the Committee on Homeland Security and 
     Governmental Affairs, without amendment:

     the Department of Homeland Security, and for other purposes

       By Mr. SCHATZ, from the Committee on Indian Affairs, with 
     an amendment in the nature of a substitute:
       S. 3022. A bill to amend the Indian Health Care Improvement 
     Act to allow Indian Health Service scholarship and loan 
     recipients to fulfill service obligations through half-time 
     clinical practice, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 118-
     240).
       By Mr. SCHATZ, from the Committee on Indian Affairs, 
     without amendment:
       S. 3406. A bill to amend the Omnibus Public Land Management 
     Act of 2009 to make a technical correction to the Navajo 
     Nation Water Resources Development Trust Fund, to amend the 
     Claims Resolution Act of 2010 to make technical corrections 
     to the Taos Pueblo Water Development Fund and Aamodt 
     Settlement Pueblos' Fund, and for other purposes (Rept. No. 
     118-241).
