# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10114

**Measures:** H.R. 10114

```
            By Mr. FULCHER:
        H.R. 10114.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 allows Congress to make all 
     laws ``which shall be necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution'' any of Congress' enumerated powers, including 
     Congress's power over appropriations.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill to amends the Aquifer Recharge Flexibility Act to 
     clarify a provision relating to conveyances for aquifer 
     recharge purposes.
```
