# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10115

**Measures:** H.R. 10115

```
            By Mr. LANDSMAN:
        H.R. 10115.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is;
       To amend the Small Business Act and the Small Business 
     Investment Act of 1958 to provide resources, counseling, and 
     access to capital for child care providers, and for other 
     purposes.
```
