# RECOGNIZING JACI BOBBITT

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                        RECOGNIZING JACI BOBBITT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, I want to remember Jaci Bobbitt, an 
incredible resident of South Jersey and a great friend of mine. Jaci 
lived a remarkable life, starting in Portsmouth, Ohio before attending 
West Virginia University, where she met her husband, John. Her vibrant 
personality kept her social calendar full, allowing her to enjoy 
quality time with family members and beloved friends. Her and John both 
shared a passion for music; she was a fantastic dancer, specializing in 
the jitterbug, fox trot and the waltz. Jaci was a tremendously involved 
member of her community, volunteering and running numerous 
organizations throughout the years. Some of those include Chairperson 
of the Candy Striper Committee at the Memorial Hospital of Salem 
County, Charter member and Secretary of Boots and Saddles Riding Club, 
and Leader of two winning State Horse Bowl Championships. She received 
multiple awards for her dedication including the Woman of Achievement 
Award and the prestigious Crystal Rose Award from the Salem Chamber of 
Commerce. As a long-time member of Trinity United Methodist Church in 
Pennsville, South Jersey, she continually nurtured, studied and grew 
her faith. I will miss seeing Jaci at the Salem County Fair, but I take 
comfort knowing she is with our Lord in Heaven.
  God bless Jaci and God bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
