# HONORING KYLER CAMPSMITH

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                        HONORING KYLER CAMPSMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, I had the great honor of attending the 
Eagle Scout ceremony for an extraordinary young man in Kyler Campsmith. 
Kyler comes from a legacy of Eagle Scouts in his family and has been 
involved in scouts since the first grade. Since that time, he has been 
involved in a variety of leadership roles to include patrol leader, 
senior patrol, and troop guide where he displayed his gift for teaching 
by being exceptionally patient with the younger scouts. For his eagle 
scout project, Kyler demonstrated his love for God by building a Gaga 
ball pit for the youth ministry for Wellspring Church of which he is a 
member and plays guitar for the worship team. Kyler is also a 
tremendous athlete playing both varsity basketball and baseball at the 
Pilgrim Academy in Mays Landing, South Jersey where he will graduate 
from in June. He works exceptionally hard outside of boy scouts working 
as a lifeguard and will continue to work at the Ocean City Waterpark 
this summer until he begins pursuing his dream. He has always dreamed 
of becoming an electrical line worker and once he graduates from the 
Pilgrim Academy, he will begin the path toward achieving it. I have no 
doubt that Kyler will be successful in whatever endeavor he decides to 
pursue and will continue to be an invaluable member of his community. 
God bless Kyler Campsmith and God bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
