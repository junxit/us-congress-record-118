# KNIGHTS OF COLUMBUS FLAG REDEDICATION

**Speaking:** Van Drew, Jefferson (V000133) [R-NJ]

```
                 KNIGHTS OF COLUMBUS FLAG REDEDICATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 12, 2024

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, I recently had the honor of having a flag 
flown over the United States Capitol for the re-dedication of the 
Fourth Degree St. Vincent Pallotti, Assembly 680 of the Knights of 
Columbus for their new location in Hammonton, South Jersey. For nearly 
150 years, the Knights of Columbus have been a beacon of true brotherly 
love by showing God's power in men of the Catholic faith. St. Vincent 
boasts of having the fourth degree, the most prestigious members of the 
order, all of them obtaining the title of ``Sir Knight.'' It is the 
highest degree and extension of the third with the primary objective 
being to ``foster the spirit of patriotism and to encourage active 
Catholic Citizenship''. I salute the men of the Fourth Degree at St. 
Vincent Pallotti, Assembly 680 on doing an exemplary job of serving God 
through the Catholic faith and being outstanding patriots to our great 
Nation. May God bless Fourth Degree St. Vincent Pallotti Assembly 680 
and God bless the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
