# CONGRATULATING PUSCH RIDGE CHRISTIAN ACADEMY

**Speaking:** Ciscomani, Juan (C001133) [R-AZ]

```
              CONGRATULATING PUSCH RIDGE CHRISTIAN ACADEMY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair recognizes the gentleman from 
Arizona (Mr. Ciscomani) for 5 minutes.
  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Pusch 
Ridge Christian Academy boys' soccer team for winning the 3A State 
championship.
  By beating the number one ranked Gilbert Christian School, they 
secured the title with a score of 2-1.
  Pusch Ridge Christian Academy is known for its sports and does not 
fail to disappoint when it comes to State championships.
  Head Coach Geoff Neale led the team to the championship alongside 
Assistant Coaches Adam Odell and Taric Doyle.
  This team has a great future ahead of them. I am excited to see what 
the future holds for all of them.
  As Congressman of Arizona's Sixth Congressional District, I take 
pride in the accomplishments of these talented young athletes.
  Keep up the great work. Congratulations.


      Recognizing Salpointe Catholic High School Boys' Soccer Team

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Salpointe 
Catholic High School boys' soccer team. The Lancers won their fourth 
straight 4A State championship, defeating the Mica Mountain High School 
Thunderbolts 4-1.
  Salpointe's fourth straight State title ties the school with Lakeside 
Blue Ridge, Chino Valley, and Yuma Catholic for the most consecutive 
State championships in Arizona's history.
  Wolfgang Weber, head coach of the boys' soccer program and a 
legendary local youth soccer coach, led the team to their 11th State 
championship.
  I am so proud of these boys, and I am excited to see where their 
soccer careers take them.
  Go Lancers. Congratulations.


     Recognizing the Salpointe Catholic High School Wrestling Team

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Salpointe 
Catholic High School wrestling team. Nate Higgins and Che Jenkins, 
class of 2025, secured the State championship for the Salpointe 
Catholic Lancers this year.
  Their dedication, skill, and perseverance have brought immense pride 
to their school. Salpointe Catholic High School is a hub for athletes 
in our district, offering 26 sports with 52 teams. With those, the 
Lancers have won 78 State championships.
  Mr. Speaker, I am excited to see the bright and promising futures of 
these exceptional athletes as they excel both on and off the mat.
  Congratulations.


       Congratulating Walden Grove High School Girls' Soccer Team

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Walden 
Grove High School girls' soccer team for winning the Arizona 4A State 
championship.
  In an exciting match, Coach Troy Bosley and his team secured victory 
over Salpointe by two goals with a final score of 3-1.
  This was Walden Grove's first State championship since 2014. Their 
dedication and perseverance throughout the past year has been truly 
commendable. It is no surprise that they emerged with the victory this 
season.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to represent such talented student athletes, 
determined coaches, and supportive parents.
  Keep up the great work, and congratulations.
```
