# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7478

**Measures:** H.R. 7478

```
           By Mr. YAKYM:
       H.R. 7478.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would condition Title IV financial aid 
     funds, authorized under the Higher Education Act, on the 
     adoption of the International Holocaust Remembrance 
     Alliance's working definition of antisemitism in university 
     student and employee codes of conduct.
```
