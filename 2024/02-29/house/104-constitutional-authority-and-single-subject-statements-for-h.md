# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7494

**Measures:** H.R. 7494

```
            By Mr. GREEN of Tennessee:
        H.R. 7494.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill will stop the SEC from implementing the proposed 
     rule or future similar rules that would allow Natural Asset 
     Companies to list their assets on the NYSE or any other 
     securities exchange.
```
