# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7481

**Measures:** H.R. 7481

```
           By Ms. BARRAGAN:
       H.R. 7481.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill requires the Centers for Disease Control and 
     Prevention (CDC) to establish the Social Determinants of 
     Health Program to improve health outcomes and reduce health 
     inequities by, for example, coordinating activities across 
     the CDC.
```
