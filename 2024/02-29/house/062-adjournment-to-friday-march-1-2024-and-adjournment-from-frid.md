# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, MARCH 1, 2024; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY MARCH 1, 2024, TO TUESDAY, MARCH 5, 2024

**Speaking:** Bean, Aaron (B001314) [R-FL]

```
ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, MARCH 1, 2024; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY MARCH 
                   1, 2024, TO TUESDAY, MARCH 5, 2024

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. tomorrow, and 
further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet on 
Tuesday, March 5, 2024, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour 
debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
