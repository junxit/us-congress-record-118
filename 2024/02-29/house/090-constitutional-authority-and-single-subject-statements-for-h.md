# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7480

**Measures:** H.R. 7480

```
           By Ms. De La CRUZ:
       H.R. 7480.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the Housing and Community Development Act of 1974 to 
     require the exclusion of service-connected disability 
     compensation when determining whether a person is a person of 
     low and moderate income, a person of low income, or a person 
     of moderate income.
```
