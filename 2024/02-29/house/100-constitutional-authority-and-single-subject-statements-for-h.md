# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7490

**Measures:** H.R. 7490

```
            By Mr. CRANE:
        H.R. 7490.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of the Office of Personnel 
     Management to establish a pilot program to identify and refer 
     veterans for potential employment with Federal land 
     management agencies, and for other purposes.
```
