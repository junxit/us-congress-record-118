# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7477

**Measures:** H.R. 7477

```
           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.R. 7477.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3
       Article I, Section 8, clause 14
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Defense to complete a data-
     matching agreement with the Secretary of Education in order 
     to ensure individuals who are current or former active-duty 
     military service members or civilian employees and are 
     otherwise eligible for assistance under the public service 
     loan forgiveness program have their periods of employment 
     automatically certified and counted towards the public 
     service loan forgiveness program.
```
