# STUDENT ATHLETE CELEBRATION

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                      STUDENT ATHLETE CELEBRATION

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize Western High 
School's football team in my district and to congratulate 11 seniors 
going on to play college football.
  Eleven is a 10-year record for the program under Coach Dan Davidson 
who pushed these athletes to compete at the next level. These players, 
coaches, and parents put in countless hours and accomplished something 
very special.
  Let me say, Mr. Speaker, many professional football players have come 
and have started in my district, including Heisman Trophy winners John 
Huarte, Matt Leinart, and Bryce Young.
  Now, these 11 student athletes will have the opportunity to blaze 
their own paths.
  Western High School and Coach Dan Davidson, again, have made us 
proud, and I congratulate them.

                          ____________________
```
