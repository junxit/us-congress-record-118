# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7507

**Measures:** H.R. 7507

```
           By Mr. SMITH of Nebraska:
       H.R. 7507.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United States 
     Constitution: ``Congress shall have the power . . . to 
     establish post offices and post roads.''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To designate the Post Office located at 203 East 6th Street 
     in Lexington, Nebraska, as the ``Bill Barrett Post Office 
     Building''
```
