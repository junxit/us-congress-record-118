# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7487

**Measures:** H.R. 7487

```
            By Ms. CARAVEO:
        H.R. 7487.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Constitutional Authority--Necessary and Proper Clause (Art. 
     I, Sec. 8, Clause 18)
       THE U.S. CONSTITUTION
       ARTICLE I, SECTION 8: POWERS OF CONGRESS
       CLAUSE 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To statutorily establish Operation Stonegarden, through 
     which eligible law enforcement agencies shall be awarded 
     grants for border security enhancement. Additonally this bill 
     provide enhanced resources for local law enforcement agencies 
     across the country.
```
