# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7497

**Measures:** H.R. 7497

```
            By Mr. KILMER:
        H.R. 7497.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill amends the Federal Election Campaign Act of 1971 
     to make reforms to the composition and functioning of the
```
