# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Executive Calendar No. 456, Cathy Ann Harris, of Maryland, 
     to be Chairman of the Merit Systems Protection Board.
         Charles E. Schumer, Gary C. Peters, Tim Kaine, Robert P. 
           Casey, Jr., Catherine Cortez Masto, Margaret Wood 
           Hassan, Jeanne Shaheen, Tammy Duckworth, Tina Smith, 
           Christopher A. Coons, Chris Van Hollen, Mark R. Warner, 
           Amy Klobuchar, Elizabeth Warren, Alex Padilla, Brian 
           Schatz, Mark Kelly.

  Mr. SCHUMER. I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory quorum calls 
for the cloture motions filed today, February 29, be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
