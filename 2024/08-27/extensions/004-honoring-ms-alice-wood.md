# HONORING MS. ALICE WOOD

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chuck (E000246) [R-NC]

```
                        HONORING MS. ALICE WOOD

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 27, 2024

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. Alice Wood, for 
her work as an intern serving in my D.C. office. I would like to take 
this opportunity to thank her for her hard work and recognize the 
meaningful contributions she has made to the office this summer.
  Alice is a student at Wake Forest University, where she is majoring 
in political science and double minoring in Spanish and English 
studies. After graduation, Alice wants to continue her education by 
attending law school.
  Interns work with staff to serve constituents in North Carolina's 
11th Congressional District. Alice was chosen from a competitive pool 
of applicants and exceeded every expectation with her positive attitude 
and impressive work ethic.
  It was a pleasure to work with Alice, and I am so proud to have such 
an intelligent and ambitious intern representing my office. She 
exemplified the spirit of public service and represents the best that 
our Nation has to offer. Congratulations to Alice on this successful 
completion of her internship, and on behalf of the people of Western 
North Carolina, I thank her.

                          ____________________
```
