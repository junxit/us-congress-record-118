# RECOGNIZING JEFF KOLE AND DIANA MORRISON

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                RECOGNIZING JEFF KOLE AND DIANA MORRISON

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 27, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jeff 
Kole and Diana Morrison for their achievements as the 2024 honorees of 
the Junior Achievement Savannah Business Hall of Fame.
  Jeff Kole is the President of Kole Investment Company and has been 
actively involved in improving the Savannah community through numerous 
boards and charity organizations.
  In addition, he is the founding facilitator and Board Chair of East 
Savannah United, a nonprofit that will be opening a new library and 
children's center next year.
  Diana Morrison is the Founder and CEO of Ad Specialty. She built her 
successful business up from just the trunk of her car.
  Diana contributes her time to many organizations in the Savannah area 
and has been honored by the Savannah Chamber of Commerce twice for her 
accomplishments. We are proud to have leaders like Jeff and Diana in 
our district and wish them both the very best.

                          ____________________
```
