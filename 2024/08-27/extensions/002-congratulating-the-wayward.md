# CONGRATULATING THE WAYWARD

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                       CONGRATULATING THE WAYWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 27, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate The 
Wayward in Savannah, Georgia for ranking number 6 on USA Today's top 10 
list for best hotel bars.
  The Wayward lies in the heart of Savannah's Historic District, housed 
in the new and charming Perry Lane Hotel. The Wayward is not your 
typical Savannah bar. It selves up a unique alternative rock feel, a 
refreshing change of pace that perfectly contrasts the coastal city.
  This accomplishment is no small feat, as an expert panel chose 20 
widely recognized bars known for their great atmosphere and mixology, 
and let USA Today readers vote on the top 10.
  There are no limits to creativity at The Wayward. Patrons may enjoy 
items from the menu or rely on the skills of the mixologists to prepare 
unique flavor profiles. I am very proud to share this excitement with 
The Wayward and the City of Savannah.

                          ____________________
```
