# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7515

**Measures:** H.R. 7515

```
            By Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota:
        H.R. 7515.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Fair Credit Reporting Act to delay the 
     inclusion in consumer credit reports and to establish 
     requirements for debt collectors with respect to medical debt 
     information of Native Americans due to inappropriate or 
     delayed billing payments or reimbursements from the Indian 
     Health Service, and for other purposes.
```
