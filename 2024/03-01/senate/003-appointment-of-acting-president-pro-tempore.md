# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Parliamentarian will please read a 
communication to the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mrs. 
Murray).
  The Senior Assistant Parliamentarian read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                    Washington, DC, March 1, 2024.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Christopher Murphy, a Senator from the State of Connecticut, 
     to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                     Patty Murray,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. MURPHY thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________
```
