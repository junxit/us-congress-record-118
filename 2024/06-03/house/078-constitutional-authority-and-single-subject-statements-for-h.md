# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8595

**Measures:** H.R. 8595

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 8595.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       clause 18 of section 8 of article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would add 500,000 new AmeriCorps members and 
     establish that the minimum wage for any AmeriCorps State & 
     National member would be 200 percent of the federal poverty 
     line.
```
