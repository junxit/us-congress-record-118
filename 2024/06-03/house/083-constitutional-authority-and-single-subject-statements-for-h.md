# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8600

**Measures:** H.R. 8600

```
           By Mrs. McBATH:
       H.R. 8600.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Article I, Section 8 of 
     the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 18, United States Code, to restrict the 
     possession of certain firearms, and for other purposes.
```
