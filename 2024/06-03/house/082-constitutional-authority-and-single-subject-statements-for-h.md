# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8599

**Measures:** H.R. 8599

```
           By Mr. VAN DREW:
       H.R. 8599.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to include over-
     the-counter oral healthcare products as qualified medical 
     expenses which can be purchased with HSA and FSA funds.
```
