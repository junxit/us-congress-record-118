# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, before You, nations rise and fall. They grow strong or 
wither by Your design. Help our Nation to embrace righteousness and to 
strive for unity and renewal. May America set an example of right 
living that will inspire other nations to follow You. Teach all nations 
the way of peace so we may plow up battlefields and pound weapons into 
liberation tools. Teach us to talk across boundaries as brothers and 
sisters united by Your love.
  Today, help our Senators to work with a renewed sense of their 
accountability to You.
  We pray in your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
