# RECOGNIZING FIRE CAPTAIN JON DRENNON

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
                  RECOGNIZING FIRE CAPTAIN JON DRENNON

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 21, 2024

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today I want to recognize Fire Captain Jon 
Drennon for his twenty-five years of service with the Dearborn Fire 
Department in Michigan's 12th District Strong.
  Fire Captain Drennon got his start in public service in 1999 as a 
Firefighter. After only one year of service, he received the first of 
many Meritorious Company Commendations. In addition, he has been 
recognized numerous times for valor and lifesaving. Over the course of 
his public service career, Fire Captain Drennon has committed himself 
to continuous professional development, completing numerous 
certification and educational programs in various rescue operations 
techniques and other technical programming.
  Fire Captain Drennon has been an outstanding public servant to the 
communities served by the Dearborn Fire Department. Please join me in 
recognizing his twenty-five years of commitment to protecting our 
families' safety across Michigan's 12th District Strong as we wish him 
well in his retirement.
```
