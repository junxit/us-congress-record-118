# RECOGNIZING MIDDLETOWN TOWNSHIP POLICE OFFICE NELSON WEBB'S RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
 RECOGNIZING MIDDLETOWN TOWNSHIP POLICE OFFICE NELSON WEBB'S RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 21, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
outstanding constituent of my district, Middletown Township Police 
Officer Nelson Webb, who is retiring on June 28, 2024, after 36 years 
of dedicated and faithful service to the citizens of Southeastern 
Pennsylvania.
  Nelson began his career in policing on June 24, 1990, as a Penndel 
Borough Police Officer. After six years with the Penndel Borough Police 
Department, he joined the Middletown Township Police Department on June 
24, 1996. During his distinguished career, he has received several 
department commendations for outstanding work. In addition, Nelson has 
served as a field training officer, a motorcycle patrol officer, and 
served for 20 years on the Bucks County South Central Special Emergency 
Response (SWAT) team.
  Beyond his accomplishments, Officer Webb embodies the essence of a 
devoted public servant. Modest and humble, he avoids the spotlight, 
preferring to lead through actions and example rather than words. His 
exemplary conduct serves as an inspiration to his colleagues, earning 
him respect as a true leader and mentor among his peers. In every 
sense, Officer Nelson Webb epitomizes the true law enforcement 
professional--a person of integrity, dedication, and great work ethic.
  I wish to congratulate Nelson Webb on his 36 years of service to the 
citizens of Bucks County and on his well-deserved retirement from the 
Middletown Township Police Department.

                          ____________________
```
