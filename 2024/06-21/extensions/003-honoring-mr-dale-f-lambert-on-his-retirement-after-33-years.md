# HONORING MR. DALE F. LAMBERT ON HIS RETIREMENT AFTER 33 YEARS IN THE ELECTRIC UTILITY INDUSTRY

**Speaking:** Hudson, Richard (H001067) [R-NC]

```
 HONORING MR. DALE F. LAMBERT ON HIS RETIREMENT AFTER 33 YEARS IN THE 
                       ELECTRIC UTILITY INDUSTRY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 21, 2024

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Mr. Dale F. Lambert on his retirement after 33 years of working in the 
electric utility industry.
  Dale is the embodiment of the American Dream. He started off with the 
Randolph Electric Membership Corporation as an Apprentice Lineman in 
1984. He then served as a District Manager before working his way up to 
CEO, a position he has held for the last 17 years. Thanks to his hard 
work and dedication we've been able to keep the lights on.
  Dale is not only committed to making sure folks in Randolph County 
have reliable access to power, but he is also deeply involved with the 
community. Dale frequently volunteers with his church, the Beulah 
Baptist Church, as well as the Asheboro Rotary Club and the NC Jaycee 
Burn Center Advisory Committee. Dale is dedicated to improving the 
lives of the folks around him and is truly a steward of our region. 
Dale has given 33 great years to Randolph County, and while we will 
miss him dearly, I'm sure he is looking forward to spending more time 
with his wife Regena and their wonderful children and granddaughter.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in congratulating Mr. Dale F. 
Lambert on his well-earned retirement after a 33-year career in the 
electric utility industry.
```
