# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8795

**Measures:** H.R. 8795

```
           By Mr. AMODEI:
       H.R. 8795.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to establish Post Offices and post 
     Roads under Article I, Section 8, Clause 7 of the United 
     States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill would limit the closure or consolidation of any 
     United States Postal Service processing and distribution 
     center if the United States Postal Service has failed to meet 
     certain conditions, and for other purposes.
```
