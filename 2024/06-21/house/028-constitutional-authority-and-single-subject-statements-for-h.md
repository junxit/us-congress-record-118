# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8806

**Measures:** H.R. 8806

```
           By Mr. NUNN of Iowa:
       H.R. 8806.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       Requires a study on the resiliency of Social Security and 
     Medicare. Specifically, the bill would direct the Comptroller 
     General to conduct and submit a report to Congress on how 
     inflation and cost-of-living increases affect seniors and 
     provide legislative recommendations to improve the ability of 
     the programs to provide full benefits.
```
