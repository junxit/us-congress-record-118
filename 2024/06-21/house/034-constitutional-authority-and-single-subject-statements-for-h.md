# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 169

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 169

```
           By Mr. ARRINGTON:
       H.J. Res. 169.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Congressional disapproval of the United States Fish and 
     Wildlife Service's ``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and 
     Plants; Endangered Species Status With Critical Habitat for 
     Guadalupe Fatmucket, Texas Fatmucket, Guadalupe Orb, Texas 
     Pimpleback, Balcones Spike, and False Spike, and Threatened 
     Species Status With Section 4(d) Rule and Critical Habitat 
     for Texas Fawnsfoot'' rule.
```
