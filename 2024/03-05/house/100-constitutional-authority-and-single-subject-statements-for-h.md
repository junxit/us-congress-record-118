# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7554

**Measures:** H.R. 7554

```
           By Ms. TENNEY:
       H.R. 7554.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend part A of title IV of the Social Security Act to 
     ensure that Federal funds provided under the program of block 
     grants to States for temporary assistance for needy families 
     are used to supplement State spending, and for other 
     purposes.
```
