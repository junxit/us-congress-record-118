# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7535

**Measures:** H.R. 7535

```
           By Ms. CARAVEO:
       H.R. 7535.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Constitutional Authority--Necessary and Proper Clause (Art. 
     I, Sec. 8, Clause 18)
       THE U.S. CONSTITUTION
       ARTICLE I. SECTION 8: POWERS OF CONGRESS
       CLAUSE 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Comptroller General of the United States to 
     conduct a study and submit a report on price-related 
     compensation and payment structures in the prescription drug 
     supply chain.
```
