# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7524

**Measures:** H.R. 7524

```
           By Mr. SESSIONS:
       H.R. 7524.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the General Services Administration to provide 
     an annual report to Congress regarding each project funded by 
     the Citizen Services Fund (CSF) and some projects funded by 
     the Acquisition Services Fund (ASF) by the end of that fiscal 
     year. The bill requires that this report include information 
     about when the funded project was initiated, an explanation 
     of the project, the project's total cost, and the amount of 
     reimbursements associated with the project.
```
