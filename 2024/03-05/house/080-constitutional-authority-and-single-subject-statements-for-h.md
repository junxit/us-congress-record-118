# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7534

**Measures:** H.R. 7534

```
           By Mr. AUCHINCLOSS:
       H.R. 7534.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act 
     (COPPA) to raise the age of data privacy protections and 
     require privacy-protective methods of age verification.
```
