# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7531

**Measures:** H.R. 7531

```
           By Mr. LUETKEMEYER:
       H.R. 7531.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve 
     System to study the impacts of the Board's Reg II proposed 
     rule, to complete a quantitative impact analysis of such 
     rule, and to consider the results of such study and analysis 
     before finalizing such rule, and for other purposes.
```
