# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7525

**Measures:** H.R. 7525

```
           By Mr. FALLON:
       H.R. 7525.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Unites States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of the Office of Management and 
     Budget to issue guidance to agencies requiring special 
     districts to be recognized as local government for the 
     purpose of Federal financial assistance determinations.
```
