# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7544

**Measures:** H.R. 7544

```
           By Ms. MALOY:
       H.R. 7544.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section I
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the conditioning of any permit, lease, or other 
     use agreement on the transfer of any water right to the 
     United States by the Secretary of the Interior and the 
     Secretary of Agriculture, and for other purposes.
```
