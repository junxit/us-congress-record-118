# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7520

**Measures:** H.R. 7520

```
           By Mr. PALLONE:
       H.R. 7520.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3: [The Congress shall 
     have Power] To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, sand 
     among the several states, and with the Indian Tribes;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit data brokers from transferring sensitive data 
     of United States individuals to foreign adversaries, and for 
     other purposes.
```
