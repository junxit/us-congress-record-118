# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7311

**Measures:** H.R. 7311

```
           By Mr. GUEST:
       H.R. 7311.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to enhance the 
     operations of the U.S. Customs and Border Protection, Air and 
     Marine Operations, in foreign countries.
```
