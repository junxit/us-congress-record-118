# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7309

**Measures:** H.R. 7309

```
           By Mr. BURCHETT:
       H.R. 7309.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill repeals certain formula grants under the 
     Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 and uses the 
     funds to award block grants to states.
```
