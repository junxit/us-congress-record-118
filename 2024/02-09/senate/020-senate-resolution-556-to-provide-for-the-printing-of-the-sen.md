# SENATE RESOLUTION 556--TO PROVIDE FOR THE PRINTING OF THE SENATE MANUAL FOR THE ONE HUNDRED EIGHTEENTH CONGRESS

**Measures:** S.Res. 556

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 556--TO PROVIDE FOR THE PRINTING OF THE SENATE MANUAL 
                FOR THE ONE HUNDRED EIGHTEENTH CONGRESS

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR (for herself and Mrs. Fischer) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 556

       Resolved, That a revised edition of the Senate Manual for 
     the One Hundred Eighteenth Congress be prepared by the 
     Committee on Rules and Administration and printed as a Senate 
     document, and that 1,500 additional copies shall be printed 
     and bound for the use of the Senate, bound and delivered as 
     may be directed by the Committee on Rules and Administration.
```
