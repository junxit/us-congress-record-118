# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O Lord, may our hearts be open to receive Your guidance. Help us to 
bow to Your will and live lives devoted to Your providential leading.
  Bless our Senators. Let faith, hope, and love abound in their lives. 
Lord, use them to heal our hurting Nation and world and to be forces 
for harmony, goodness, and peace. May they hunger for Your wisdom and 
make decisions that will honor You. Open their minds, and give them a 
vision of the unlimited possibilities available to those who trust You 
as their guide.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
