# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 2950, H.R. 5302, H.R. 5536, H.R. 5799, H.R. 7218, H.R. 7438, H.R. 7764, H.R. 8932

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

     (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D1082)
  H.R. 2950, to authorize the Secretary of the Interior, through the 
Coastal Program of the United States Fish and Wildlife Service, to work 
with willing partners and provide support to efforts to assess, 
protect, restore, and enhance important coastal landscapes that provide 
fish and wildlife habitat on which certain Federal trust species 
depend. Signed on December 11, 2024. (Public Law 118-138)

[[Page D1139]]


  H.R. 5302, to designate the Air and Marine Operations Marine Unit of 
the U.S. Customs and Border Protection located at 101 Km 18.5 in Cabo 
Rojo, Puerto Rico, as the ``Michel O. Maceda Marine Unit''. Signed on 
December 11, 2024. (Public Law 118-139)
  H.R. 5536, to require transparency in notices of funding opportunity. 
Signed on December 11, 2024. (Public Law 118-140)
  H.R. 5799, to designate the checkpoint of the United States Border 
Patrol located on United States Highway 90 West in Uvalde County, 
Texas, as the ``James R. Dominguez Border Patrol Checkpoint''. Signed 
on December 11, 2024. (Public Law 118-141)
  H.R. 7218, to amend title III of the Public Health Service Act to 
extend the program for promotion of public health knowledge and 
awareness of Alzheimer's disease and related dementias. Signed on 
December 11, 2024. (Public Law 118-142)
  H.R. 7438, to require the Secretary of the Treasury to mint coins in 
commemoration of the FIFA World Cup 2026. Signed on December 11, 2024. 
(Public Law 118-143)
  H.R. 7764, to establish a commission to study the potential transfer 
of the Weitzman National Museum of American Jewish History to the 
Smithsonian Institution. Signed on December 11, 2024. (Public Law 118-
144)
  H.R. 8932, to establish an earlier application processing cycle for 
the FAFSA. Signed on December 11, 2024. (Public Law 118-145)
```
