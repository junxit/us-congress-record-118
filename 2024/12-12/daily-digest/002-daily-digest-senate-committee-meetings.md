# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee ordered 
favorably reported the nominations of David Michael Capozzi, of 
Maryland, Ronald L. Batory, of New Mexico, Elaine Marie Clegg, of 
Idaho, and Lanhee J. Chen, of California, each to be a Director of the 
Amtrak Board of Directors, Lisa T. Ballance, of Oregon, to be a Member 
of the Marine Mammal Commission, Felix R. Sanchez, of the District of 
Columbia, and Adam Jeffrey White, of Virginia, both to be a Member of 
the Board of Directors of the Corporation for Public Broadcasting, L. 
E. Sola, of Florida, to be a Federal Maritime Commissioner, and routine 
lists in the Coast Guard.
U.S. AIR TRAFFIC CONTROL SYSTEMS
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Subcommittee on 
Aviation Safety, Operations, and Innovation concluded a hearing to 
examine U.S. Air Traffic Control systems, personnel, and safety, 
including urgent FAA actions needed to modernize aging systems, after 
receiving testimony from Kevin Walsh, Director, Information Technology 
and Cybersecurity, Government Accountability Office; Dean Iacopelli, 
National Air Traffic Controllers Association, David Spero, Professional 
Aviation Safety Specialists, and Marc Scribner, Reason Foundation, all 
of Washington, D.C.; and Jason Ambrosi, Air Line Pilots Association, 
International, Atlanta, Georgia.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee ordered favorably reported the 
nominations of Benjamin J. Cheeks, to be United States District Judge 
for the Southern District of California, and Serena Raquel Murillo, to 
be United States District Judge for the Central District of California.
EMPOWERING PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES
Special Committee on Aging: Committee concluded a hearing to examine 
empowering people with disabilities to live, work, learn, and thrive, 
after receiving testimony from Mayor Neil McDevitt, North Wales, 
Pennsylvania; Brent Orrell, American Enterprise Institute, and Lydia 
Xinzhen Zhangsun Brown, National Disability Institute, both of 
Washington, D.C.; Erin Willman, White Cane Coffee Company, Warren, 
Pennsylvania; and Ai-jen Poo, Domestic Workers Alliance and Caring 
Across Generations, Chicago, Illinois.
```
