# RECOGNIZING BOB WEST FOR HIS CONTRIBUTIONS TO SPORTS AND SPORTSMANSHIP

**Speaking:** McMorris Rodgers, Cathy (M001159) [R-WA]

```
 RECOGNIZING BOB WEST FOR HIS CONTRIBUTIONS TO SPORTS AND SPORTSMANSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. CATHY McMORRIS RODGERS

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 12, 2024

  Mrs. RODGERS of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
recognize Mr. Robert ``Bob'' West, a distinguished veteran, sports 
official, advocate, and community leader whose life and career 
exemplify a commitment to restoring the joy of sports through a renewal 
of good sportsmanship. His tireless efforts have impacted not only his 
hometown of Spokane Valley, Washington, but also the broader sports 
community across the Nation.
  From the moment his eighth-grade teacher handed him a whistle, Bob 
West discovered a lifelong passion for officiating at sporting events. 
For more than thirty-five years, he officiated at national 
championships in the United States and abroad, earning respect for his 
fairness, professionalism, and trademark enthusiasm on the field. His 
animated calls and unwavering integrity became a hallmark of his 
officiating style, cherished by athletes and fans alike.
  Tragically, Mr. West's officiating career was cut short in 1996 when 
he was assaulted by an athlete while officiating a high school 
wrestling match, an incident that underscored the growing issue of 
violence against sports officials. Despite enduring several surgeries 
and a long recovery, Mr. West transformed his pain into purpose. He 
became a relentless advocate for referee safety, launching legislative 
efforts to increase penalties for assaults on sports officials, which 
culminated in bipartisan support for bills aimed at creating safer 
environments for referees and athletes alike.
  Mr. West also established the website UR-SAFE.org, a vital resource 
dedicated to educating the public about the importance of sportsmanship 
and advocating for the protection of sports officials. Through this 
platform, he has championed respect for those who ensure fair play, 
reminding us all of the critical role officials play in preserving the 
integrity of sports.
  Beyond his contributions to sports, Mr. West is a proud veteran, 
having spent twenty years in the military, during which he officiated 
sports even while deployed overseas. Following his military service, he 
has continued to dedicate himself to public service in the Spokane 
area.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Bob West 
for his extraordinary dedication to restoring the joy of sports and 
promoting good sportsmanship. His life's work reminds us of the power 
of respect and integrity, both on the field and in our communltles.
```
