# RECOGNIZING MY LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT, RUBY ZIMMERMAN, FOR HER EXCELLENT WORK ON BEHALF OF MINNESOTA'S THIRD CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Phillips, Dean (P000616) [D-MN]

```
RECOGNIZING MY LEGISLATIVE ASSISTANT, RUBY ZIMMERMAN, FOR HER EXCELLENT 
       WORK ON BEHALF OF MINNESOTA'S THIRD CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEAN PHILLIPS

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 12, 2024

  Mr. PHILLIPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize my Legislative 
Assistant, Ruby Zimmerman, for her work on behalf of every constituent 
in Minnesota's Third Congressional District.
  Ruby is a Georgia native who moved to Washington D.C. after 
graduating from Georgia College and State University with a double 
bachelor's degree in political science and government and rhetoric. She 
started her career on the Hill as a Staff Assistant in Congressman 
David Scott's office, working her way up to being his Legislative Aide 
before joining my team as my Legislative Assistant. Ruby was essential 
in introducing my American Dream Accounts Act, a legacy bill that would 
help kickstart the lives and careers of the next generation.
  Ruby is full of life and puts a smile on everyone's faces with her 
energy, optimism, and dog Tillie. Despite being the most recent member 
of our team, she has made a large and lasting impression on the work 
we've been able to accomplish in the final months of my term. Ruby has 
been a steady hand during a time of major transition. On her first day, 
Ruby was ready with her sleeves rolled up and hit the ground running. 
She's been the epitome of a team player, and we're grateful for her 
tireless service in advocating for the people of our district.
  The people of Minnesota's Third Congressional District were lucky to 
have her dedication and leadership, and she will be dearly missed. I 
wish her all the best in her future endeavors and thank her faithful 
service to this Nation.

                          ____________________
```
