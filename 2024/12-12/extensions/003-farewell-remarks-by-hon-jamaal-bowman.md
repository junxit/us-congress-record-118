# FAREWELL REMARKS BY HON. JAMAAL BOWMAN

**Speaking:** Lee, Barbara (L000551) [D-CA]

```
                 FAREWELL REMARKS BY HON. JAMAAL BOWMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BARBARA LEE

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 12, 2024

  Ms. LEE of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the impactful 
Congressional service of Congressman Jamaal Bowman.
  Congressman Bowman came to Congress as a progressive activist--a 
father, a teacher, a principal, and an organizer.
  He came to Congress ready to serve, fighting for a brighter future 
for his constituents in New York's 16th district.
  With a career in education spanning two decades, Rep. Bowman has been 
a tireless advocate for the rights of teachers, students, and families. 
He has organized and been an integral part of crucial movements that 
bring together communities from all different backgrounds fighting for 
equity in our education system.
  While in Congress, he fought for hundreds of millions in Community 
Project Funding for his district, he stood with workers fighting for 
labor rights, advocated for the health and well-being of Black men and 
boys, and so much more.
  He was a friend to all in our caucus, bringing a warm energy and a 
smile to every member he came in touch with.
  Congressman Bowman's dedication to service, advocacy, community, and 
meaningful change has made him a powerful force for justice and equity 
in Congress and I wish him well in his next chapter.

                          ____________________
```
