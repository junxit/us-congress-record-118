# CELEBRATING OUR 2024 FALL INTERN CLASS FOR THEIR SERVICE TO CALIFORNIA'S 20TH DISTRICT

**Speaking:** Fong, Vince (F000480) [R-CA]

```
      CELEBRATING OUR 2024 FALL INTERN CLASS FOR THEIR SERVICE TO 
                       CALIFORNIA'S 20TH DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VINCE FONG

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 12, 2024

  Mr. FONG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
outstanding interns who have dedicated their time and talents to 
serving the 20th District of California this fall. These bright and 
committed young people, working from both our Washington, D.C., and 
Clovis, California offices, have brought fresh perspectives, unwavering 
enthusiasm, and a shared passion for making a difference. At a time 
when many young people are questioning the role of public service, 
their dedication inspires confidence in the leaders of tomorrow.
  As their fall internships come to a close, I want to express my 
deepest gratitude to Grace Lee Delgado, Elijah Lidgett, and Tristan 
Wertanzl for their contributions to our Washington, D.C., office and to 
Jake Dowell for his service in our Clovis, California office. Their 
hard work has been instrumental to our efforts, and I trust that the 
skills and educational experiences they have gained will guide them 
toward continued success in their future endeavors in public service.

                          ____________________
```
