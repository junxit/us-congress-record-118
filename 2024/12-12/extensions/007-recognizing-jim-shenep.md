# RECOGNIZING JIM SHENEP

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                         RECOGNIZING JIM SHENEP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 12, 2024

  Mr. HILL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extraordinary 
Arkansan, my friend, Jim Shenep.
  It is unbelievable that three decades after we started working 
together, it is time for him to retire.
  I want to say how thankful I am for our three decades of friendship, 
and Martha and I treasure our relationship with him and his family.
  It has been such a privilege to watch his continued success, starting 
when we were at First Commercial, then Regions, then Delta Trust, and 
now at Simmons.
  He has always put his customers first, and that was at the heart of 
what we tried to build at Delta Trust, and I know it is at the heart of 
Simmons Trust Company.
  I wish him well in his retirement and I wish I could be there with 
him in person to celebrate, but I wanted to thank him for his 
dedication to his customers and our friendship.

                          ____________________
```
