# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 227

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 227

```
           By Mr. COHEN:
       H.J. Res. 227.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article V
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Proposing an amendment to the Constitution of the United 
     States to abolish the electoral college and to provide for 
     the direct election of the President and Vice President of 
     the United States.
```
