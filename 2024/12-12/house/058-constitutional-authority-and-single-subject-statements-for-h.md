# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10395

**Measures:** H.R. 10395

```
           By Mr. KENNEDY:
       H.R. 10395.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 3, Section 8, Article 1 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to establish 
     and carry out a pilot program to furnish a headstone or 
     burial marker to veterans who died on or before November 1, 
     1990, and for other purposes.
```
