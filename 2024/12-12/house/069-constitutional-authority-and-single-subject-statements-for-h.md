# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10406

**Measures:** H.R. 10406

```
           By Ms. SHERRILL:
       H.R. 10406.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the Constitution of 
     the United States of America.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to 
     authorize requiring the manufacturers of a covered device to 
     disclose to a patient all patient-specific data that is 
     recorded or transmitted by the device and accessible to the 
     manufacturer, and for other purposes.
```
