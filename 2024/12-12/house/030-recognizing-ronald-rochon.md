# RECOGNIZING RONALD ROCHON

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING RONALD ROCHON

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize California State 
University at Fullerton's new president, Dr. Ronald Rochon.
  Dr. Rochon was born on the south side of Chicago where he founded and 
nurtured his passion for education and community service. He stepped 
into the top role at Cal State University at Fullerton, my alma mater, 
this past July.
  Dr. Rochon brings 14 years of experience to southern California, 
having led the University of Southern Indiana through COVID-19 and a 
new athletic division.
  As the proud Titan that I am, I am very excited to see Dr. Ronald 
Rochon at the helm. We are happy to see Ron at Cal State, Fullerton, 
and we look forward to working with him. Let's get busy.

                          ____________________
```
