# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10391

**Measures:** H.R. 10391

```
            By Mr. FLOOD:
        H.R. 10391.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the continuing availability of any portion of a 
     Federal payment to the District of Columbia for a program of 
     District of Columbia resident tuition support for a fiscal 
     year which remains unobligated as of the end of the fiscal 
     year, and for other purposes.
```
