# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10380

**Measures:** H.R. 10380

```
            By Mr. STEUBE:
        H.R. 10380.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
        The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to allow a 
     portion of general business credit carryforwards to be 
     transferred by certain taxpayers affected by Federally 
     declared disasters.
```
