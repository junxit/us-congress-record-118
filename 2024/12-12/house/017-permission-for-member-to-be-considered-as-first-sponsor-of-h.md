# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 7840

**Speaking:** Lieu, Ted (L000582) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 7840

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 7840

  Mr. LIEU. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 7840, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Jackson Lee of Texas, for the purpose of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
