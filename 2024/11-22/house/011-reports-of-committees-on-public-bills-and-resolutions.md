# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 133, H.R. 3284, H.R. 5389, H.R. 5396, H.R. 7188, H.R. 7198, H.R. 7227

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Ms. FOXX: Committee on Education and the Workforce. H.R. 
     7227. A bill to establish the Truth and Healing Commission on 
     Indian Boarding School Policies in the United States, and for 
     other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 118-760, Pt. 1). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. JORDAN: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 7198. A bill 
     to amend title 5, United States Code, to require greater 
     transparency for Federal regulatory decisions that impact 
     small businesses, and for other purposes; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 118-761, Pt. 1). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mrs. RODGERS of Washington: Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce. H.R. 5396. A bill to amend title XVIII of the 
     Social Security Act to prohibit Medicare local coverage 
     determinations from restricting access to care, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 118-762, Pt. 1). Ordered 
     to be printed.
       Mrs. RODGERS of Washington: Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce. H.R. 5389. A bill to amend title XVIII of the 
     Social Security Act to ensure transparency in the national 
     coverage determination process under the Medicare program; 
     with an amendment (Rept. 118-763, Pt. 1). Ordered to be 
     printed.
       Mrs. RODGERS of Washington: Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce. H.R. 133. A bill to amend title XVIII of the Social 
     Security Act to clarify the use of national coverage 
     determination process under the Medicare program; with an 
     amendment (Rept. 118-764, Pt. 1). Ordered to be printed.
       Mrs. RODGERS of Washington: Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce. H.R. 3284. A bill to require the Secretary of 
     Health and Human Services to submit an annual report on the 
     impact of certain Medicare regulations on provider and payer 
     consolidation; with an amendment (Rept. 118-765, Pt. 1). 
     Ordered to be printed.
       Mrs. RODGERS of Washington: Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce. H.R. 7188. A bill to require the Secretary of 
     Health and Human Services to conduct a national, evidence-
     based education campaign to increase public and health care 
     provider awareness regarding the potential risks and benefits 
     of human cell and tissue products transplants, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 118-766). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.


                         discharge of committee

  Pursuant to clause 2 of rule XIII, Committees on Natural Resources
```
