# RECOGNIZING STEVE STIRLING

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING STEVE STIRLING

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 9, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge and 
thank Steve Stirling, who is retiring as CEO of Medicine for All People 
(MAP) International.
  MAP International is a Christian, non-profit, global health 
organization that provides medicine and health supplies to those in 
need.
  This organization is headquartered in Atlanta and does amazing work 
worldwide. Steve has successfully led this organization since 2014.
  Steve has an impressive leadership background in both the non-profit 
and corporate spaces, and he brought that valuable experience to MAP 
for over a decade of loyal service.

[[Page E817]]

  He has worked tirelessly to bring health and hope to millions of 
people around the globe.
  In addition to his strong leadership, he has led MAP International 
into entering its 70th year of service with a promising future that 
includes extending its impact across the globe.
  I want to recognize and congratulate Steve on his outstanding career 
with MAP International and wish him a happy and healthy retirement. I 
also want to recognize Chris Palombo as the new CEO and congratulate 
him on his achievement.

                          ____________________
```
