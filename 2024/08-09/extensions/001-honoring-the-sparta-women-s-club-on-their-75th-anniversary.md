# HONORING THE SPARTA WOMEN'S CLUB ON THEIR 75TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
       HONORING THE SPARTA WOMEN'S CLUB ON THEIR 75TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 9, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the Sparta, New 
Jersey Women's club as they celebrate their 75th anniversary. Their 
selfless commitment to community service, volunteerism, and engagement 
makes an invaluable contribution to the betterment of the Sparta 
community and Sussex County. Through their variety of programs, they 
foster a welcoming environment for all NJ women, not just residents of 
Sparta, to learn about and advocate for the issues that pertain to 
them. I am proud to see them pass this milestone and look forward to 
seeing the continued success of their work.

                          ____________________
```
