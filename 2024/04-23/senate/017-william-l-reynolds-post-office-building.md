# WILLIAM L. REYNOLDS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 292

```
                WILLIAM L. REYNOLDS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 292) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 24355 Creekside Road in Santa Clarita, 
California, as the ``William L. Reynolds Post Office Building'' was 
ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
