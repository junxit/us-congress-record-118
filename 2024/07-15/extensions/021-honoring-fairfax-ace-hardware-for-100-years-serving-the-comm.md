# HONORING FAIRFAX ACE HARDWARE FOR 100 YEARS SERVING THE COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Connolly, Gerald E. (C001078) [D-VA]

```
   HONORING FAIRFAX ACE HARDWARE FOR 100 YEARS SERVING THE COMMUNITY

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. GERALD E. CONNOLLY

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 15, 2024

  Mr. CONNOLLY. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Fairfax Ace Hardware 
as a cornerstone of the Fairfax neighborhood and an exemplary example 
of commitment to service, innovation, and community spirit.
  From humble beginnings to becoming a cornerstone of neighborhoods all 
over the country, Ace Hardware has been a hub for DIY enthusiasts, 
builders, and neighbors alike and since its opening in 2011, Fairfax 
Ace Hardware has become a helpful place, in more ways than one, for 
their Fairfax neighbors.
  For 13 years, Fairfax Ace Hardware has been more than just a place to 
purchase tools and grilling supplies. It has been a gathering place 
where neighbors connect, share stories, and support one another. The 
employee-owned store's knowledgeable team, led by CEO Craig Smith, has 
always gone above and beyond to ensure the consistent delivery of 
outstanding customer service, a dedication to customer service and a 
no-hassle return policy.
  The dedication of the Fairfax Ace Hardware team has led to multiple 
instances of being noticed by Ace Hardware's national offices, most 
recently and most monumentally being chosen as one out of ten stores to 
host a celebration commemorating 100 years of operation of Ace 
Hardware. This celebration, aptly named ``The Biggest Block Party'' and 
hosted on June 29, 2024, serves as a glowing manifestation of years of 
superb customer service and community engagement.
  In addition to retail success, Fairfax Ace Hardware has been an 
outstanding corporate citizen sponsoring countless Fairfax events, 
fairs, and initiatives, supporting youth sports teams, and contributing 
to numerous charitable causes including raising over $20,000 over the 
last two years for Children's National Hospital through a national 
partnership with Ace Hardware. Their generosity and community spirit 
have left an indelible mark on our town, fostering a sense of unity and 
pride among its residents.
  As we celebrate this achievement, I would like to extend my heartfelt 
congratulations and deepest gratitude to the Fairfax Ace Hardware team. 
Your hard work, dedication, and love for our community have made a 
lasting impact.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in honoring Fairfax Ace Hardware for its 
outstanding contributions to Fairfax and in being an example of Ace 
Hardware's century long commitment to fostering community spirit. We 
look forward to many more years of success and service.

                          ____________________
```
