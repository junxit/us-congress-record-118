# HONORING THE LIFE OF JAMES HATCH

**Speaking:** DeSaulnier, Mark (D000623) [D-CA]

```
                    HONORING THE LIFE OF JAMES HATCH

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 15, 2024

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the life of 
James ``Jim'' Hatch.
  Jim was dedicated to serving our community and was always kind and 
helpful. Included in his long career, he assisted with former 
Congressman George Miller's campaign and the Youth Fund. Additionally, 
Jim was incredibly hardworking and was a skilled handyman. He also 
enjoyed traveling the world and was a proud dog parent to Minnie the 
Pooch. Jim will be remembered for his optimism, positivity, and the 
impact he's had on countless lives.
  Jim is survived by Carol, his loving wife of 60 years, and his 
children Suzanne (Rob) and Jill (Steve), grandchildren Sophie (Joshua), 
Sarah (Rick), Matthew, Lauren, Kristi (Ben) and Sarah (Jason Henry), 
great grandchildren Olivia and Cameron, his sister Sandra, brother 
Robert, and bonus daughter and grandsons Tomoko, Hiro, and Akira 
Tamura.
  Please join me in honoring my friend Jim Hatch. He will be remembered 
for his outstanding character and his commitment to his community.

                          ____________________
```
