# RECOGNIZING THE CAPE HENRY COLLEGIATE GIRLS LACROSSE TEAM

**Speaking:** Kiggans, Jennifer A. (K000399) [R-VA]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE CAPE HENRY COLLEGIATE GIRLS LACROSSE TEAM

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. JENNIFER A. KIGGANS

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 15, 2024

  Mrs. KIGGANS of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and 
congratulate the Cape Henry Collegiate Girls Lacrosse team on winning 
the 2024 Tidewater Conference of Independent Schools (TCIS) 
Championship.
  As their Congresswoman, I am proud of these young athletes and their 
recent achievement.
  Located in Virginia Beach, Virginia, the Cape Henry Collegiate 
Dolphins were able to bring the TCIS Championship back home to their 
fellow students, faculty, families, and friends through their hard work 
and dedication to their team. The Cape Henry Collegiate Girls Lacrosse 
Team defeated their opponents in overtime, finishing the game eight to 
seven. Coach Ashmore Standing received the TCIS co-coach of the year 
award, and her student, Madeleine Keogh, won TCIS's Player of the Year.
  As a former athlete myself, I want each of these young women to know 
Virginia's 2nd District is extremely proud of their accomplishment and 
applauds them on their well-earned victory. Again, congratulations to 
the Cape Henry Collegiate Girls Lacrosse team on the 2024 TCIS 
Championship.

                          ____________________
```
