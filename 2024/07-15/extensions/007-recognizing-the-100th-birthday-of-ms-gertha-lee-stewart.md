# RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MS. GERTHA LEE STEWART

**Speaking:** Rulli, Michael A. (R000619) [R-OH]

```
        RECOGNIZING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF MS. GERTHA LEE STEWART

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL A. RULLI

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 15, 2024

  Mr. RULLI. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Ms. Gertha Lee Stewart on 
her 100th birthday.
  Gertha Lee Stewart is a venerable matriarch who has become the heart 
and soul of her large family, consisting of 250 members. Her wisdom and 
love have prospered generations, making her a cherished figure in their 
lives. Known for her incredible life story, Gertha Lee has been a 
beacon of strength and resilience.
  A devout Christian, Gertha Lee's faith has been the cornerstone of 
her existence. She has spent countless hours in prayer and service, 
always ready to lend a helping hand or a listening ear. Her deep 
spirituality and unwavering commitment to her beliefs have inspired 
many in her community to find their own paths of faith and devotion.
  Beyond her family, Gertha Lee is recognized as a pillar of her 
community. Her charitable acts, wisdom, and leadership have earned her 
the respect and admiration of all who know her. Whether organizing 
community events, supporting local charities, or simply offering sage 
advice, Gertha Lee Stewart embodies the spirit of kindness and 
community service, leaving an indelible mark in her wake.
  I ask my colleagues in the United States House of Representatives to 
join me in recognizing Ms. Gertha Lee Stewart and her family as we 
congratulate her on 100 years of life. May she continually be a 
blessing and light to all who know her.

                          ____________________
```
