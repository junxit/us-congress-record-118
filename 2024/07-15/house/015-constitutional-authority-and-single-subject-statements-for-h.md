# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9031

**Measures:** H.R. 9031

```
           By Mr. STAUBER:
       H.R. 9031.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 ``The Congress shall have 
     power to . . . provide for the . . . general welfare of the 
     United States; . . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requiring agencies to report to Congress how they determine 
     a rule's impact on businesses.
```
