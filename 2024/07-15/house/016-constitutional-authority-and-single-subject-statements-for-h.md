# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9032

**Measures:** H.R. 9032

```
           By Ms. MALOY:
       H.R. 9032.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 ``The Congress shall have 
     power to . . . provide for the . . . general welfare of the 
     United States; . . .''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires agencies to include the full impact of their rules 
     in agency analyses of such rules.
```
