# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9048

**Measures:** H.R. 9048

```
           By Ms. NORTON:
       H.R. 9048.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clauses 16 and 17 of Section 8 of Article I of the 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:

[[Page H4634]]

       This bill would permanently authorize funding for the Major 
     General David F. Wherley, Jr., District of Columbia National 
     Guard Retention and College Access Program, which provides 
     financial assistance for higher education to members of the 
     D.C. National Guard.
```
