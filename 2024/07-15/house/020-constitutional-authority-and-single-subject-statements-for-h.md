# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9036

**Measures:** H.R. 9036

```
           By Mr. CARTER of Louisiana:
       H.R. 9036.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 (Spending Clause) and 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 (Necessary and Proper Clause)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Small Business Act to provide for cancellation 
     or repayment for Small Business Administration disaster 
     loans, and for other purposes.
```
