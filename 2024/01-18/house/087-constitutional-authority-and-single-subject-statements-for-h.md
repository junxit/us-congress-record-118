# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7036

**Measures:** H.R. 7036

```
            By Mr. FOSTER:
        H.R. 7036.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the United States 
     Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation provides NCUA and FHFA with the authority 
     to regulate third-party vendors that provide services to 
     their regulated entities.
```
