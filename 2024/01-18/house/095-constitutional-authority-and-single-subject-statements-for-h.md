# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7044

**Measures:** H.R. 7044

```
            By Mrs. McCLAIN:
        H.R. 7044.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To ensure that women seeking an abortion are notified, 
     before giving informed consent to receive an abortion, of the 
     medical risks associated with the abortion procedure and the 
     major developmental characteristics of the unborn child.
```
