# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7030

**Measures:** H.R. 7030

```
            By Mrs. KIM of California:
        H.R. 7030.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill directs the Securities and Exchange Commission to 
     establish a rule review process and consider the cummulative 
     costs of rules and regulations in rulemaking.
```
