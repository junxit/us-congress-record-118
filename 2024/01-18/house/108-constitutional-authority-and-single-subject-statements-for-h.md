# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7057

**Measures:** H.R. 7057

```
            By Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas:
        H.R. 7057.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Requires the Financial Stability Oversight Council to 
     include illegal immigration risks as an emerging threat in 
     their annual U.S.

[[Page H242]]

     financial stability report and provide recommendations to 
     Congress on how to mitigate these risks.
```
