# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7033

**Measures:** H.R. 7033

```
            By Mr. CALVERT:
        H.R. 7033.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8 of the United 
     States Constitution, specifically clause 1 and clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to apply a 6 
     percent excise tax on large endowments of certain private 
     colleges and universities.
```
