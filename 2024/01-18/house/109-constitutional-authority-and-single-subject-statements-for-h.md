# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7058

**Measures:** H.R. 7058

```
            By Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas:
        H.R. 7058.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Establishes a reserve fund at the Department of the 
     Treasury to be used by border states and the U.S. Border 
     Patrol to invest in border security. Account is funded by 
     confiscated unlawful narcotics seized by U.S. Custom and 
     Border Protections. Prohibits funds to be used by sanctuary 
     cities.

                          ____________________
```
