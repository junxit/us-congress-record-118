# HONORING THE WASHINGTON HUSKIES' 2023 FOOTBALL SEASON

**Speaking:** Jayapal, Pramila (J000298) [D-WA]

```
         HONORING THE WASHINGTON HUSKIES' 2023 FOOTBALL SEASON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. PRAMILA JAYAPAL

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 18, 2024

  Ms. JAYAPAL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the Washington 
Huskies' remarkable 2023 football season. The Huskies reached the 
College Football Playoff, secured the greatest number of wins in the 
football program's history, and won the Pac-12 Championship. Under the 
leadership of head coach Kalen DeBoer, the Huskies put a well-deserved 
national spotlight on the university's football program and concluded 
the regular season with a perfect 13-0 record.
  The University of Washington continues to bring immense pride to 
Seattle and Washington State. It is a leader in both academic and 
athletic excellence in the Pacific Northwest and the United States. I 
would like to commemorate the amazing work of the coaching staff and 
the student-athletes as they prepare for another excellent season. 
Congratulations on a phenomenal season and go Dawgs.

                          ____________________
```
