# RECOGNIZING CANTON MAYOR THOMAS BERNABEI

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
                RECOGNIZING CANTON MAYOR THOMAS BERNABEI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 18, 2024

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to 
Thomas Bernabei on his service as the Mayor of Canton, and my sincere 
gratitude for all that he has done for the people of Ohio's 13th 
Congressional District, the ``Birthplace of Champions.'' I have 
appreciated his partnership as we worked together to expand opportunity 
and deliver results for our shared constituents.
  Mr. Bernabei's drive to lead is commendable, and I have no doubt that 
he has bettered countless lives as a result of his steadfast service. 
Throughout his time in office, he proved to be a true public servant 
and leader. He leads by example through his perseverance, compassion, 
and dedication to his role in the community.
  As Mr. Bernabei goes on to continue serving our community in new 
ways, I wish him all the best in his future endeavors. On behalf of 
Ohio's 13th Congressional District, I thank Mr. Bernabei for his 
commitment to the people of Canton and to Northeast Ohio.

                          ____________________
```
