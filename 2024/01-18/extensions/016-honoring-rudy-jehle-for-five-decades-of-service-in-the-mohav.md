# HONORING RUDY JEHLE FOR FIVE DECADES OF SERVICE IN THE MOHAVE COUNTY SHERIFF'S SEARCH AND RESCUE

**Speaking:** Gosar, Paul A. (G000565) [R-AZ]

```
 HONORING RUDY JEHLE FOR FIVE DECADES OF SERVICE IN THE MOHAVE COUNTY 
                      SHERIFF'S SEARCH AND RESCUE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PAUL A. GOSAR

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 18, 2024

  Mr. GOSAR. Mr. Speaker, I honor Rudy Jehle who will retire from the 
Mohave County Sheriff's Search and Rescue after five decades of 
exemplary dedication to his community. Rudy truly personifies the 
patriotic virtue of service. From 1962 to 1964, he served his country 
in the Army and was honorably discharged. After moving to Lake Havasu 
City, Arizona in 1973, Rudy served as a Mohave County Sheriff's deputy 
and then as a reserve officer in the Lake Havasu Police Department.
  Rudy also served in the Mohave County Search and Rescue program. 
Anyone who has traversed the sweeping wilderness of western Arizona can 
appreciate the lifesaving importance of this work. Through his time 
with the Search and Rescue division, of which he was a founding member, 
Rudy has been recognized with greatly deserved accreditations and 
awards. His work and service for the Mohave County community merits the 
utmost commendation.
  On behalf of myself and the great people of Arizona's ninth district, 
I extend my sincere gratitude to Rudy for his service, and I wish him 
the best as he moves onto the next chapter of his life.

                          ____________________
```
