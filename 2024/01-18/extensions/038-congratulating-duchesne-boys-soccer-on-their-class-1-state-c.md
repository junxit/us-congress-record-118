# CONGRATULATING DUCHESNE BOYS' SOCCER ON THEIR CLASS 1 STATE CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
      CONGRATULATING DUCHESNE BOYS' SOCCER ON THEIR CLASS 1 STATE 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 18, 2024

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Duchesne High School on their victory in 
Missouri's Class 1 Boys' Soccer State Championship.
  The Pioneers claimed the Class 1 title for the first time in 20 years 
with a narrow 1-0 victory over Dubourg. Junior Zach Hubert came off the 
bench to net the game's only goal, as goalie Ben Burke led the defense 
in recording its ninth shutout of the season. The team should be 
commended for all their hard work throughout the past year and for 
bringing home a state championship to their school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Duchesne Boys' Soccer 
Team for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
