# RECOGNIZING THE WHEAT RIDGE THEATRE COMPANY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
              RECOGNIZING THE WHEAT RIDGE THEATRE COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 18, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Wheat Ridge 
Theatre Company for receiving the Wheat Ridge Business Association's 
2023 Cultural Commission Award.
  The Cultural Commission Award recognizes a local business or 
organization that helps enrich art and culture in Wheat Ridge. Wheat 
Ridge Theatre Company strives to promote an artistic community where 
participants can express their diverse points of view through 
performative arts with the focus of promoting equality, diversity, and 
inclusion. Their hard work and dedication to the Wheat Ridge Community 
is tremendous.
  Congratulations to the Wheat Ridge Theatre Company on the 
accomplishments that led to this prestigious award. We are grateful for 
their continued contribution to the Wheat Ridge community.
```
