# RECOGNIZING MASSILLON MAYOR KATHY CATAZARO-PERRY

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
            RECOGNIZING MASSILLON MAYOR KATHY CATAZARO-PERRY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 18, 2024

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to 
Kathy Catazaro-Perry on her service as the Mayor of Massillon, and my 
sincere gratitude for all that she has done for the people of Ohio's 
13th Congressional District, the ``Birthplace of Champions.'' I have 
appreciated her partnership as we worked together to expand opportunity 
and deliver results for our shared constituents.
  Ms. Catazaro-Perry's drive to lead is commendable, and I have no 
doubt that she has bettered countless lives as a result of her 
steadfast service. Throughout her time in office, she proved to be a 
true public servant and leader. She leads by example through her 
perseverance, compassion, and dedication to her role in the community.
  As Ms. Catazaro-Perry goes on to continue serving our community in 
new ways, I wish her all the best in her future endeavors. On behalf of 
Ohio's 13th Congressional District, I thank Ms. Catazaro-Perry for her 
commitment to the people of Massillon and to Northeast Ohio.

                          ____________________
```
