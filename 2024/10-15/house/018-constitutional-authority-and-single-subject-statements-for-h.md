# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9996

**Measures:** H.R. 9996

```
           By Mr. YAKYM:
       H.R. 9996.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This legislation would create an independent office under 
     the Office of the VA Secretary that would provide casework 
     services to veterans and provide Congress with quarterly 
     recommendations to improve the provision of VA healthcare, 
     benefits, and cost efficiency.
```
