# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

**Measures:** H.Res. 917

```
         COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the chair of the Committee on the Judiciary:

                                         House of Representatives,


                                   Committee on the Judiciary,

                                     Washington DC, July 30, 2024.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to Section 4(b) of House 
     Resolution 917, I write to inform you and the House of 
     Representatives that the Committee on the Judiciary has 
     commenced litigation against Merrick Garland, in his official 
     capacity as Attorney General of the United States, to enforce 
     a duly authorized, issued, and served congressional subpoena. 
     The Committee filed the civil action, Committee on the 
     Judiciary, U.S. House of Representatives v. Garland, No. 
     1:24-cv-01911, in the U.S. District Court for the District of 
     Columbia on July 1, 2024.
           Sincerely,
                                                       Jim Jordan,
     Chairman.

                          ____________________
```
