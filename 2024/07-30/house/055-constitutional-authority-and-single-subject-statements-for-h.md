# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9221

**Measures:** H.R. 9221

```
           By Mr. MORAN:
       H.R. 9221.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, clause 8,
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       ``To amend title 35, United States Code, to establish a
```
