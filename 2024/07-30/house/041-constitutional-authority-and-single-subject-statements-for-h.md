# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9207

**Measures:** H.R. 9207

```
           By Ms. HAGEMAN:
       H.R. 9207.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is.
       To prohibit Federal employees and contractors from 
     directing online platforms to censor any speech that is 
     protected by the First Amendment to the Constitution of the 
     United States
```
