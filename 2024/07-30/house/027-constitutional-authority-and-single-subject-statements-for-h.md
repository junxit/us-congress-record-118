# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9193

**Measures:** H.R. 9193

```
           By Ms. BUDZINSKI:
       H.R. 9193.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8 Clause 18 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To appropriate funds for the Federal Communications 
     Commission's ``rip and replace'' program and Affordable 
     Connectivity Program, to improve the Affordable Connectivity 
     Program, to require a spectrum auction, and for other 
     purposes.
```
