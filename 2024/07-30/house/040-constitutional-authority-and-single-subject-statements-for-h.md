# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9206

**Measures:** H.R. 9206

```
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER:
       H.R. 9206.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To authorize the President to take actions to ensure Israel 
     is prepared for all contingencies if Iran seeks to develop a 
     nuclear weapon, and for other purposes.
```
