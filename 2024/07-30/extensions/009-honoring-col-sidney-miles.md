# HONORING COL. SIDNEY MILES

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                       HONORING COL. SIDNEY MILES

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 30, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to mourn the loss of 
Colonel Sidney ``Sid'' Miles. Sid was the Colonel and Commissioner of 
the Georgia Department of Public Safety.
  Recognized for his commitment to public education and enforcement of 
the Georgia safety belt use law, Mr. Miles was presented with a public 
service award from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration 
in 1998.
  Prior to his appointment at the Department of Public Safety, Mr. 
Miles spent several decades serving at the Georgia State Patrol. He 
retired from his career in law enforcement in 1999. Mr. Miles was a 
dedicated public servant to the state of Georgia and will be greatly 
missed by those he served with.
  I send my deepest condolences to Sid's wife and two children. My 
thoughts and prayers are with all of his family and friends at this 
moment.

                          ____________________
```
