# CONGRATULATING STEVIE CANNADY

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                     CONGRATULATING STEVIE CANNADY

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. EARL L. ``BUDDY'' CARTER

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 30, 2024

  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Golfweek Super Senior National Championship Winner Stevie Cannady. Mr. 
Cannady turned 65 in early January, making 2024 his inaugural season in 
the Super Senior division.
  Prior to his National Championship victory, he had his best finish of 
2024 in early May at the Lowcountry Senior invitational, placing in the 
top 5. He hit 86 percent of fairways and 89 percent of greens with a 
final-round score of 70, jumping up to the lead and placing first in 
the National Championship.
  Aside from his many golf achievements, Mr. Cannady owns a logistics 
company specializing in trucking, warehousing, and commercial real 
estate.
  Cannady is a valued member of our community and a force on the golf 
course. Congratulations to Mr. Cannady on an amazing accomplishment, 
and I can't wait to see what he achieves next.

                          ____________________
```
