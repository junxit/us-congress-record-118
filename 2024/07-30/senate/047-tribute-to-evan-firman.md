# TRIBUTE TO EVAN FIRMAN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO EVAN FIRMAN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Evan Firman, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Evan is a graduate of the U.S. Department of Defense Education 
Activity- Ramstein High School in Ramstein, Germany. Currently, he is 
attending the University of South Dakota in Vermillion, SD, where he is 
pursing degrees in economics and political science. He is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Evan for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
