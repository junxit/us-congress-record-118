# TRIBUTE TO GRAYSON HOWES

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO GRAYSON HOWES

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Grayson Howes, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Grayson is a graduate of O'Gorman High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, he is attending Fordham University in New York, NY, where he 
is pursuing a degree in global business. He is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Grayson for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
