# TRIBUTE TO GRIFFIN PETERSEN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO GRIFFIN PETERSEN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Griffin Petersen, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Griffin is a graduate of Sully Buttes High School in Onida, SD. 
Currently, he is attending the University of South Dakota in 
Vermillion, SD, where he is pursuing a degree in political science. He 
is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of his 
internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Griffin for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
