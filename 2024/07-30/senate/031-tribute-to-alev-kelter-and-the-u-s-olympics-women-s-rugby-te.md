# Tribute to Alev Kelter and the U.S. Olympics Women's Rugby Team (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Sullivan, Dan (S001198) [R-AK]

```
    Tribute to Alev Kelter and the U.S. Olympics Women's Rugby Team

  Mr. SULLIVAN. Mr. President, I have a resolution that I am looking 
forward to passing here on the Senate floor. I am pretty sure no one is 
going to object to it, but we will see in a minute.
  Before I begin, I want to do a huge shout-out and congratulations to 
Alaska's own Alev Kelter and the U.S. women's rugby team. Thanks to 
Alev's score late in the first half, the Americans beat the vaunted 
Australians in rugby to deliver the women's rugby team's first Olympic 
medal in rugby sevens.
  So great job, Alev. Great job to the American women's rugby team in 
getting some medals there for our great Nation.

                          ____________________
```
