# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8145

**Measures:** H.R. 8145

```
           By Mr. BURLISON:
       H.R. 8145.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section VIII of the United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Army to convey or lease 
     certain land acquired for the Table Rock Lake project to 
     owners of property located adjacent to such project, and for 
     other purposes.
```
