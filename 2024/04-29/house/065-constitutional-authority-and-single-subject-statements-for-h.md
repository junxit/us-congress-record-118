# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8159

**Measures:** H.R. 8159

```
           By Mr. NEWHOUSE:
       H.R. 8159.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       to prohibit the breaching of federally operated dams if
```
