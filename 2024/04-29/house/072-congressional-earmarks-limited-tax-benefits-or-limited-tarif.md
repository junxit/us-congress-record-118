# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 6090, H.R. 6285

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                         Offered By Mr. Jordan

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary in H.R. 6090, the Antisemitism Awareness Act 
     of 2023, do not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9 of rule XXI.

                         Offered By Mr. Stauber

       The amendment to be offered by myself, or my designee, to 
     H.R. 6285, the Alaska's Right to Produce Act of 2023, does 
     not contain any congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, 
     or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule 
     XXI.
```
