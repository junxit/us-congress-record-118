# TRIBUTE TO GLEN SAYLER

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO GLEN SAYLER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 29, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to recognize the president of 
the Sun City Republican Club, Glen Sayler. Glen Sayler has 
distinguished himself by providing exceptional service to the residents 
of Sun City, Arizona.
  As president, Glen has been an advocate for educating members on the 
civic engagement process and how to interact with elected officials. 
The efforts of Glen and the Sun City Republican Club have been pivotal 
to the success of elected officials and candidates in the Phoenix West 
Valley.
  I am thankful for Glen's leadership and dedication to conservative 
principles. I sincerely appreciate his service to Congressional 
District 8 and the State of Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
