# TRIBUTE TO CAROL AYOTTE

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO CAROL AYOTTE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 29, 2024

  Mrs. LESKO. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Arizona's 8th Congressional 
District and the State of Arizona's 27th Legislative District, it is my 
honor to recognize Carol Ayotte. Carol Ayotte is the Chairwoman of the 
Republican Legislative District 27 which spans portions of the Cities 
of Glendale, Peoria, and Phoenix with a population of over 240,000.
  As Chairwoman, Carol has worked tirelessly to educate, train, and 
inspire the next generation of citizens through civic engagement. Her 
efforts to work collaboratively with other leaders in the Phoenix West 
Valley have been instrumental to the success of Republican elected 
officials and candidates in the state legislature and beyond.
  As a resident of Legislative District 27, I am thankful for her 
leadership and dedication to conservative principles. I sincerely 
appreciate her service to the legislative district and to the State of 
Arizona.

                          ____________________
```
