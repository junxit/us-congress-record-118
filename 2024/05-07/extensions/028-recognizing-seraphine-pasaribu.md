# RECOGNIZING SERAPHINE PASARIBU

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING SERAPHINE PASARIBU

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 7, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Seraphine 
Pasaribu for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Seraphine has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Seraphine, develop crucial skills and 
a work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This 
award is a testament to Seraphine's hard work, determination, and 
perseverance at Jefferson Jr./Sr. High School and is clearly just the 
beginning of a bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Seraphine Pasaribu on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.

                          ____________________
```
