# RECOGNIZING THE 86TH BIRTHDAY OF DOROTHY SANDERS KENDRICK

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE 86TH BIRTHDAY OF DOROTHY SANDERS KENDRICK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 7, 2024

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
86th birthday of Dorothy Sanders Kendrick.
  Dorothy was born on April 24, 1938, and was the second child of 10 
children born to George and Eugenia Sanders.
  Dorothy was married to the late William Earl Kendrick. They were 
blessed with six children: Leonard Kendrick, Barbara Scott, Valerie 
Cousin, Charles Kendrick, Anthony Kendrick and Sonya Kirk.
  Dorothy has 11 grandchildren and 22 great-grandchildren. Dorothy 
loves the Lord and is a member of Greenbrier Baptist Church in 
Anniston, Alabama.
  She will be celebrated by family and friends on July 20, 2024.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Dorothy Sanders Kendrick 
and wishing her a very happy birthday.

                          ____________________
```
