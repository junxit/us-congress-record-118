# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8269

**Measures:** H.R. 8269

```
            By Mr. FALLON:
        H.R. 8269.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        To amend the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 
     1965 to require local educational agencies to allow 
     recruiters to access the secondary schools served by the 
     local educational agency for recruiting activities, and for 
     other purposes.
```
