# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8285

**Measures:** H.R. 8285

```
           By Mr. SWALWELL:
       H.R. 8285.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, of the United States Constitution, 
     specifically Clause 18 (relating to the power to make all 
     laws necessary and proper for carrying out the powers vested 
     in congress).
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       A bill to direct the Secretary of Housing and Urban 
     Development to award grants to provide financial assistance 
     to certain educators to make down payments on certain homes, 
     and for other purposes.
```
