# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8276

**Measures:** H.R. 8276

```
           By Mrs. McCLAIN:
       H.R. 8276.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the General Services Administration (GSA) to 
     make excess personal property information public, so decision 
     makers and taxpayers understand how agencies are working to 
     cut wasteful spending.
```
