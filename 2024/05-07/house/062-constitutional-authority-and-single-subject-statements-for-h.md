# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8264

**Measures:** H.R. 8264

```
            By Mr. BARR:
        H.R. 8264.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8 of the United States Constitution.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        To amend the Federal Deposit Insurance Act and the Federal 
     Credit Union Act to improve the timeliness of examination 
     reports and other guidance and to establish panels to oversee 
     appeals from insured depository institutions and insured 
     credit unions of material supervisory determinations.
```
