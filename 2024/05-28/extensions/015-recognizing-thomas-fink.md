# RECOGNIZING THOMAS FINK

**Speaking:** Morelle, Joseph D. (M001206) [D-NY]

```
                        RECOGNIZING THOMAS FINK

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 28, 2024

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in honor of Jewish American 
Heritage Month and to recognize an extraordinary individual from 
Rochester, NY, who is making a profound impact on our Jewish community: 
Thomas Fink.
  Tom Fink is the embodiment of service in my hometown. Tom is a past 
president of Temple B'rith Kodesh and a former National Board Member of 
the Union of Reform Jewish Congregations. He has been active in 
volunteer work and social activism for decades, particularly on the 
issue of homelessness and affordable housing. He has dedicated himself 
time and time again to bettering our region, going above and beyond to 
serve his neighbors in ways both big and small.
  During Jewish American Heritage Month, it is my privilege to 
recognize Thomas Fink for his selflessness, generosity, and dedication 
to uplifting our Jewish community. Rochester is a better and brighter 
place because of his leadership. His legacy will leave a lasting mark 
on all those whose lives he has touched.
```
