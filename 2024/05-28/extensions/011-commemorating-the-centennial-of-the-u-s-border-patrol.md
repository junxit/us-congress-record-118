# COMMEMORATING THE CENTENNIAL OF THE U.S. BORDER PATROL

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1261

```
         COMMEMORATING THE CENTENNIAL OF THE U.S. BORDER PATROL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 28, 2024

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, with the introduction of H. 
Res. 1261, I join my colleagues on the House Committee on Homeland 
Security in commemorating the 100th anniversary of the creation of the 
United States Border Patrol. It is important that we honor the service 
and sacrifices of the men and women of the U.S. Border Patrol and their 
families, past and present.
  Since the U.S. Border Patrol's creation on May 28, 1924, Border 
Patrol agents have shown commitment and dedication to safeguarding our 
Nation by securing and protecting our borders.
  The U.S. Border Patrol has seen its mission expand greatly since its 
first stations opened in Detroit, Michigan, and El Paso, Texas, in 
1924. Congress expanded its duties to patrolling the seacoast in 1925. 
U.S. Border Patrol's Air Operations officially commenced in 1941.
  The tragic events of September 11, 2001, underscored the importance 
of homeland security and led to significant enhancements in U.S. Border 
Patrol operations.
  With each new mission set, the men and women of the U.S. Border 
Patrol have consistently risen to the challenge. As we mark the 
agency's centennial, we particularly remember the 156 Border Patrol 
agents who made the ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty.
  The United States Border Patrol has overcome significant challenges 
while protecting Americans and our national security. I take this 
opportunity to thank the nearly 20,000 agents currently protecting the 
6,000 miles of international land borders and 2,000 miles of coastal 
waters. I also celebrate the agents who came before them and the 
families who endure great sacrifices and provide unwavering support to 
those who serve in the United States Border Patrol.

                          ____________________
```
