# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 161

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 161

```
            By Mr. HIGGINS of Louisiana:
        H.J. Res. 161.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution, Congress 
     has the power ``to make all Laws which shall be necessary and 
     proper for carrying into Execution the foregoing Powers, and 
     all other Powers vested by this Constitution in the 
     Government of the United States, or any Department or Officer 
     thereof''
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
     Environmental Protection Agency relating to ``New Source 
     Performance Standards for the Synthetic Organic Chemical 
     Manufacturing Industry and National Emission Standards for 
     Hazardous Air Pollutants for the Synthetic Organic Chemical 
     Manufacturing Industry and Group I & II Polymers and Resins 
     Industry''.
```
