# CONGRATULATING DR. BRITTNEY F. MOBLEY

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
                 CONGRATULATING DR. BRITTNEY F. MOBLEY

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Dr. 
Brittney F. Mobley, who was named the 2024 National Distinguished 
Principal of Georgia.
  This award was administered by the National Association of Elementary 
School Principals National Distinguished Principals program, an annual 
award that honors the exemplary leadership of principals across each 
State.
  In 2020, while educators across the country were facing challenges 
with remote teaching due to the pandemic, Ms. Mobley was working to 
open her county's newest elementary school, Frances Meeks Elementary 
School.
  Frances Meeks resides in Richmond Hill, Georgia, and serves its 
students with unique learning experiences such as the elementary 
agricultural education program that provides students with hands-on 
learning experiences pertaining to agriculture and creates longstanding 
community partnerships.
  Thanks to Dr. Mobley, the school boasts a 95 percent curriculum 
mastery rate and a growing number of students qualifying for its gifted 
programs.
  Congratulations to Dr. Mobley on this award. We greatly appreciate 
the work she has dedicated to the students of Georgia's First 
Congressional District.

                          ____________________
```
