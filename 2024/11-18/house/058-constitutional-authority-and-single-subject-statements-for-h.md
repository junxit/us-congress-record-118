# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10162

**Measures:** H.R. 10162

```
           By Ms. PORTER:
       H.R. 10162.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of the Interior to enter into an 
     agreement with the National Academy of Sciences to carry out 
     a study on reservation systems for Federal land.
```
