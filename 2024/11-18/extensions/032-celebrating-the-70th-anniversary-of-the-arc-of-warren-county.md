# CELEBRATING THE 70TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ARC OF WARREN COUNTY

**Speaking:** Kean, Thomas H. (K000398) [R-NJ]

```
      CELEBRATING THE 70TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ARC OF WARREN COUNTY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 18, 2024

  Mr. KEAN of New Jersey. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognized The 
Arc of Warren County for its 70 years of service to the 7th 
Congressional District of New Jersey. By assisting families in 
addressing the challenges associated with the lifelong care of 
relatives with intellectual and developmental disabilities. The Arc of 
Warren County has created an environment where those they serve are 
accepted and respected in the community. Currently serving over 1,100 
individuals annually, The Arc strives every day to ensure that all 
those associated with the program are able to achieve independence, 
make friends, and contribute to society. I am honored to have such an 
amazing organization working to strengthen the lives of New Jersey 
residents, and I look forward to seeing all that they will achieve in 
the future.

                          ____________________
```
