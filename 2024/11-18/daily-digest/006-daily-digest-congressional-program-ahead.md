                             Senate Chamber

 On Tuesday, Senate will resume consideration of the nomination of 
Mustafa Taher Kasubhai, of Oregon, to be District Judge for the 
District of Oregon, and vote on the motion to invoke cloture thereon at 
11:30 a.m.
