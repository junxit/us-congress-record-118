# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8922

**Measures:** H.R. 8922

```
           By Mr. HIGGINS of Louisiana:
       H.R. 8922.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Artl.S8.C3--The Congress shall have Power To . . . regulate 
     Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the several States, 
     and with the Indian Tribes;
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Director of the Bureau of Consumer Financial 
     Protection to issue a final rule requiring any card issuer 
     that issues a pre-approved credit card to a senior citizen to 
     provide fraud alerts
```
