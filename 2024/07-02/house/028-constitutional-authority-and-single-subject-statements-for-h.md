# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8929

**Measures:** H.R. 8929

```
           By Mr. SCHWEIKERT:
       H.R. 8929.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1 Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit digital platforms from using information about 
     a user unless the user consents to such use, to ensure 
     personal information is considered a property right, and for 
     other purposes.
```
