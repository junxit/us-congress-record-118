# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 179

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 179

```
           By Mr. PALMER:
       H.J. Res. 179.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To disapprove of the rule submitted by the Department of 
     the Treasury relating to ``Clean Vehicle Credits Under 
     Sections 25E and 30D; Tansfer of Credits; Critical Minerals 
     and Battery Components; Foreign Entities of Concern''.
```
