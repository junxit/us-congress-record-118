# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8914

**Measures:** H.R. 8914

```
            By Ms. MALLIOTAKIS:
       H.R. 8914.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1: The Congress shall have 
     Power To lay and collect Taxes,
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to impose 
     penalties with respect to civil rights violations by certain 
     tax-exempt educational institutions.
```
