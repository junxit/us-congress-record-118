# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 172

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 172

```
           By Mr. PALMER:
       H.J. Res. 172.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To disapprove of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
     Protection Agency relating to ``National Emission Standards 
     for Hazardous Air Pollutants: Coal- and Oil-Fired Electric 
     Utility Steam Generating Units Review of the Residual Risk 
     and Technology Review''.
```
