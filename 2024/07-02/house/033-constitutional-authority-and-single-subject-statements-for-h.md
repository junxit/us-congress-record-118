# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 174

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 174

```
           By Mr. PALMER:
       H.J. Res. 174.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To disapprove of the rule submitted by the Environmental 
     Protection Agency relating to ``Hazardous and Solid Waste 
     Management System. Disposal of Coal Combustion Residuals From 
     Electric Utilities; Legacy CCR Surface Impoundments''.
```
