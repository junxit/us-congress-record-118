# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 28, S.Con.Res. 29, S.Res. 559, S.Res. 560, S.Res. 561, S.Res. 562, S.Res. 563, S.Res. 564, H.R. 815

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were 
read, and referred (or acted upon), as indicated:

           By Mr. RISCH (for himself, Mr. Cardin, Mr. Scott of 
             South Carolina, and Mr. Booker):
       S. Res. 559. A resolution recognizing the actions of the 
     Rapid Support Forces and allied militia in the Darfur region 
     of Sudan against non-Arab ethnic communities as acts of 
     genocide; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
           By Mr. DURBIN (for himself and Mr. Crapo):
       S. Res. 560. A resolution designating February 2024 as 
     ``American Heart Month''; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. SULLIVAN (for himself and Ms. Murkowski):
       S. Res. 561. A resolution designating February 16, 2024, as 
     ``National Elizabeth Peratrovich Day''; considered and agreed 
     to.
           By Mr. BRAUN (for himself and Mr. Young):
       S. Res. 562. A resolution honoring the life and legacy of 
     George F. McGinnis; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. BRAUN (for himself and Mr. Young):
       S. Res. 563. A resolution recognizing the 50th anniversary 
     of the Hulman Center; considered and agreed to.

[[Page S955]]

  

           By Mr. SCOTT of Florida (for himself and Mr. Rubio):
       S. Res. 564. A resolution honoring the memories of the 
     victims of the senseless attack at Marjory Stoneman Douglas 
     High School on February 14, 2018; considered and agreed to.
           By Mr. SCOTT of South Carolina (for himself and Mr. 
             Wicker):
       S. Con. Res. 28. A concurrent resolution expressing the 
     sense of Congress regarding the authority of the President to 
     use appropriate and necessary force to liberate United States 
     citizens being held by Hamas; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations.
           By Mrs. MURRAY:
       S. Con. Res. 29. A concurrent resolution providing for a 
     correction in the enrollment of H.R. 815; considered and 
     agreed to.
```
