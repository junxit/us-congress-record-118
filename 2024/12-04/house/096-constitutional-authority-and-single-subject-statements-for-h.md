# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10294

**Measures:** H.R. 10294

```
           By Mr. MEEKS:
       H.R. 10294.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To establish a pilot program for the Secretary of Housing 
     and Urban Development to evaluate the effectiveness of 
     insuring mortgages made to finance improvements to convert 
     basement spaces into safely habitable dwelling units, and for 
     other purposes.
```
