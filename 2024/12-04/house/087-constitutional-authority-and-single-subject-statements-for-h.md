# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10285

**Measures:** H.R. 10285

```
           By Mr. CONNOLLY:
       H.R. 10285.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       To amend Chapter 63 of title 5, United States Code, to 
     modify the accumulation of annual leave for use in succeeding 
     years for administrative law judges.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       This bill aims to modify the limits on accumulation of 
     annual leave for administratve law judges by adding an 
     administrative law judge position under section 3105.
```
