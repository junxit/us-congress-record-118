# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10290

**Measures:** H.R. 10290

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 10290.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit an entity from receiving Federal funds if such 
     entity permits an individual to access or use a single-sex 
     facility on the property of such entity that does not 
     correspond to the biological sex of such person
```
