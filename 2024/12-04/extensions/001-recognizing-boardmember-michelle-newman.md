# RECOGNIZING BOARDMEMBER MICHELLE NEWMAN

**Speaking:** Sykes, Emilia Strong (S001223) [D-OH]

```
                RECOGNIZING BOARDMEMBER MICHELLE NEWMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 4, 2024

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations to 
Boardmember Michelle Newman for her service on the Ohio State Board of 
Education, and my sincere gratitude for all that she has done for the 
people of Ohio's 13th Congressional District, the ``Birthplace of 
Champions.'' I have appreciated her partnership as we worked together 
to expand opportunity and deliver results for our shared constituents.
  Her drive to lead is commendable, and I have no doubt that she has 
bettered countless lives as a result of her steadfast service. 
Throughout her time in office, she proved to be a true public servant 
and leader. She has led by example through her perseverance, 
compassion, and dedication to her role in the community.
  As she goes on to continue serving our community in new ways, I wish 
her all the best in her future endeavors. On behalf of Ohio's 13th 
Congressional District, I thank her for her commitment to the people of 
Northeast Ohio and our entire state.

                          ____________________
```
