# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, You inhabit all ages and all worlds. Dwell among 
our Senators today. Tune their hearts to Your purposes, and open their 
lips to speak Your wisdom.
  Lord, infuse them with Your Spirit so that their work will make a 
positive impact on our Nation and world. Banish their anxieties as You 
provide them with a faith strong enough to face whatever challenges 
they must confront. Give them openness of mind in order that they might 
perceive Your will more clearly, sincerity of heart that they might 
love You more profoundly, and clarity of purpose that they might serve 
You more devotedly.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
