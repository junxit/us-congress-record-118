# Tribute to Laphonza R. Butler (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                     Tribute to Laphonza R. Butler

  Finally, I want to acknowledge Senator Laphonza Butler. She was 
appointed just over a year ago to finish the term of the late Senator 
Dianne Feinstein. She took Senator Feinstein's seat on the Senate 
Judiciary Committee. I was honored to chair that committee and work 
with her every time we met.
  Thoughtful, collegial, hard-working--you could always find her ready 
to proceed forward for as much time as it may take. She sought to 
advance the ideals of our Nation and give voice to Americans facing 
critical challenges, from access to reproductive health care to voter 
suppression.
  She is one of the youngest Senators, the third Black woman and 12th 
Black person to serve in this Chamber and the only Black person ever to 
chair the Constitution subcommittee. She has brought an important new 
voice to our debates. We are grateful for her new perspectives and her 
distinguished service.
  As I said, Mr. President, I will have more to say about other 
departing Senators. For now, I want to say to these Senators: It has 
been an honor to serve with you, to work with you for the good of our 
Nation and the good of the world.
  I yield the floor.
```
