```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR TUESDAY,

                             APRIL 9, 2024

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Defense, to hold 
hearings to examine proposed budget estimates and justification for 
fiscal year 2025 for the Air Force and Space Force, 10 a.m., SD-192.
  Subcommittee on State, Foreign Operations, and Related Programs, 
to hold hearings to examine proposed budget estimates and 
justification for fiscal year 2025 for United States Agency for 
International Development, 2:30 p.m., SD-138.

SH-216.
  Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: to hold hearings 
to examine an update from the Department of Treasury, focusing on 
countering illicit finance, terrorism, and sanctions evasion, 10 
a.m., SD-538.
  Committee on Finance: Subcommittee on Health Care, to hold 
hearings to examine opportunities to improve substance use disorder 
care in the Federal health programs, focusing on closing gaps in the 
care continuum, 2:30 p.m., SD-215.
  Committee on the Judiciary: to hold hearings to examine the 
effects of forced arbitration, 10 a.m., SD-G50.
  Committee on Rules and Administration: to hold an oversight 
hearing to examine the Smithsonian Institution, 2:45 p.m., SR-301.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: to receive a closed briefing on

  United States Senate Caucus on International Narcotics Control: to 
hold hearings to examine shell companies, focusing on a risk to 
national security, public health, and rule of law, 2 p.m., SD-608.


                                 House

  Committee on Rules, Full Committee, hearing on legislation on the 
Reforming Intelligence and Securing America Act; H.R. 529, the 
``Extending Limits of U.S. Customs Waters Act''; H. Res. 1112, 
denouncing the Biden administration's immigration policies; and 
legislation opposing efforts to place one-sided pressure on Israel 
with respect to Gaza, 4 p.m., H-313.
  Committee on Veterans' Affairs, Subcommittee on Technology 
Modernization, hearing entitled ``VA Supply Chain Modernization: 
Ready for Prime Time'', 3 p.m., 360 Cannon.
```
