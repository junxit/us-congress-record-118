# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Savior, we need You every hour of every day. We not only need 
You during crisis moments but also in the solitary moments of daily 
living.
  Our lawmakers need You. As they open their hearts to You, fill them 
with power for today's tasks. Lord, show them Your will for our times 
and give them the wisdom to say: Speak Lord, for we are listening.
  May the inspiration they receive from You keep their hearts pure, 
their minds clear, their words true, and their deeds compassionate.
  And Lord, we pray for the Iranian people, who mourn the death of 
their President.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
