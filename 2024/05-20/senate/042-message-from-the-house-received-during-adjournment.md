# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 546, H.R. 3935

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                         Enrolled Bills Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on May 16, 2024, during the adjournment of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bills:

       S. 546. An act to amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe 
     Streets Act of 1968 to authorize law enforcement agencies to 
     use COPS grants for recruitment activities, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 3935. An act to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     reauthorize and improve the Federal Aviation Administration 
     and other civil aviation programs, and for other purposes.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2023, 
the enrolled bills were subsequently signed on May 16, 2024, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, by the President pro tempore (Mrs. Murray).

                          ____________________
```
