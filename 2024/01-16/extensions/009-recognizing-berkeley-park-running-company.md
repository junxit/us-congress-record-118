# RECOGNIZING BERKELEY PARK RUNNING COMPANY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
               RECOGNIZING BERKELEY PARK RUNNING COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 16, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Berkeley Park 
Running Company for receiving the Wheat Ridge Business Association's 
2023 Reinvestment Award.
  The Reinvestment Award recognizes businesses that have demonstrated 
their commitment and investment in making Wheat Ridge a wonderful place 
to live, work, and play.
  Congratulations to Berkeley Park Running Company for their 
contribution to the Wheat Ridge community and for receiving this award.
```
