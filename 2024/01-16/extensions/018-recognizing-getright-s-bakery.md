# RECOGNIZING GETRIGHT'S BAKERY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                     RECOGNIZING GETRIGHT'S BAKERY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 16, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize GetRight's 
Bakery for receiving the Wheat Ridge Business Association's 2023 
Reinvestment Award.
  The Reinvestment Award recognizes businesses that have demonstrated 
their commitment and investment in making Wheat Ridge a wonderful place 
to live, work, and play.
  Congratulations to GetRight's Bakery for their contribution to the 
Wheat Ridge community and for receiving this award.
```
