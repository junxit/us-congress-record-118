# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 793

**Speaking:** Blunt Rochester, Lisa (B001303) [D-DE]

**Measures:** H.R. 793

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 793

  Ms. BLUNT ROCHESTER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 793, a bill 
originally introduced by Representative Bill Johnson of Ohio, to 
strengthen the American workforce, for the purpose of adding cosponsors 
and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Delaware?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
