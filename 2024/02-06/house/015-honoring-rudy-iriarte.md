# HONORING RUDY IRIARTE

**Speaking:** Moylan, James C. (M001219) [R-GU]

```
                              {time}  1215
                         HONORING RUDY IRIARTE

  (Mr. MOYLAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MOYLAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to remember and honor a civil 
servant of Guam, the late vice mayor of Sinajana, Rudy Iriarte.
  Rudy was a proud son of Guam whose service to the island and his 
village of Sinajana never went unnoticed. He had served in public life 
for over 30 years in several capacities through the local government 
and his village.
  Today, we recognize the great visions, accomplishments, and selfless 
service Rudy Iriarte made to our community.
  On behalf of a grateful island and Nation, I extend my deepest and 
utmost condolences to the family of the late vice mayor and thank him 
for his service to making Guam a better place.

                          ____________________
```
