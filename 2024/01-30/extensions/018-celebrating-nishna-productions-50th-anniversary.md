# CELEBRATING NISHNA PRODUCTIONS' 50TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Nunn, Zachary (N000193) [R-IA]

```
            CELEBRATING NISHNA PRODUCTIONS' 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ZACHARY NUNN

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 30, 2024

  Mr. NUNN of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate Nishna 
Productions on their 50th anniversary of providing tremendous community 
support and opportunity to individuals with disabilities in Southwest 
Iowa.
  What began as a humble operation in 1974 has continued to grow in 
size and scope over the last five decades. Today, the committed staff 
at Nishna Productions assist individuals with disabilities along the 
path to being as independent as possible in their communities by 
providing them with opportunities to gain vocational skills training, 
community involvement, and socialization as well as the resources to 
find and maintain employment.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Nishna Productions for 
this incredible milestone. I specifically want to recognize their 
incredible staff for selflessly working to better the lives of others. 
Their dedication has not only touched the lives of countless 
individuals and their families but serves as an inspiration to us all.

                          ____________________
```
