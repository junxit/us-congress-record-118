# HONORING RICHARD CUFF

**Speaking:** Wilson, Frederica S. (W000808) [D-FL]

```
                         HONORING RICHARD CUFF

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 30, 2024

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. Richard 
Cuff, a businessman, philanthropist, and friend.
  Born and raised in Miami, the vibrant city within Florida's 24th 
Congressional District, Richard Cuff brought with him a wealth of 
experiences and a deep-rooted sense of community when he relocated to 
Jacksonville. His journey from the heart of Miami to becoming an 
integral part of the Jacksonville community reflects the 
interconnectedness of our great state. His commitment to bridging the 
gap between communities, fostering inclusivity, and celebrating 
diversity is a testament to the values instilled in him from his early 
days in Miami, the place he still calls home.
  Richard Cuff, a dedicated and compassionate resident of Florida, has 
significantly contributed to the lively fabric of our community. 
Richard has selflessly devoted his time to enriching the lives of 
underprivileged youth through the art of music, sharing his passion and 
skill by teaching violin lessons through his Guiding Success Program. 
His comedic talents have not only brought joy and laughter to many but 
also served as a testament to his versatile and creative spirit. 
Richard's commitment to civic engagement has been demonstrated through 
his candidacy for public office, showcasing his unwavering dedication 
to making positive changes within local government.
  Richard Cuff's influence extends beyond the realms of music, comedy, 
and politics. His fervent dedication to community service is evident 
through his leadership in various local initiatives. From spearheading 
neighborhood clean-up projects to organizing charity events, Richard 
consistently demonstrates a profound commitment to the well-being of 
our community.
  In the realm of education, Richard has not only shared his musical 
expertise but has also actively engaged in promoting literacy and 
learning. His involvement in literacy programs for children and adults 
alike underscores his belief in the transformative power of knowledge 
and the importance of fostering a culture of lifelong learning.
  As a staunch advocate for social justice, Richard has fearlessly 
addressed issues of inequality and worked towards building bridges 
between communities. His efforts to foster understanding and unity have 
not only resonated locally but have also inspired others to join the

[[Page E94]]

cause of creating a more inclusive and compassionate society.
  Richard is founder of the Cora Lee Cuff Fund, named for his late 
mother, founder of NASEMBA Business Alliance, and C.E.O. of ConcinTel, 
LLC. He serves as Executive Director of League of Florida Orchestras, 
Inc., a capacity-building nonprofit organization.
  In acknowledgment of these accomplishments and the countless other 
ways Richard Cuff has enriched the lives of our residents, we celebrate 
not only his birthday but also the indelible mark he has left on our 
community. Happy Birthday to Richard, and I thank him for being an 
exemplary force for positive change.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in honoring Mr. Richard Cuff for all he 
has achieved, and for his commitment, dedication and service to our 
community.
```
