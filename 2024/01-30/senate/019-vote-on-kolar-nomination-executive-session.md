# Vote on Kolar Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        Vote on Kolar Nomination

  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Kolar 
nomination?
  Mr. BROWN. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Pennsylvania (Mr. 
Fetterman) and the Senator from West Virginia (Mr. Manchin) are 
necessarily absent.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Wyoming (Mr. Barrasso), the Senator from Idaho (Mr. Crapo), the 
Senator from South Carolina (Mr. Graham), the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Mr. Johnson), the Senator from Utah (Mr. Lee), the Senator from Idaho 
(Mr. Risch), and the Senator from South Carolina (Mr. Scott).
  The result was announced--yeas 66, nays 25, as follows:
```
