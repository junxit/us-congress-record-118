# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 107

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 107

```
            By Mr. CLYDE:
        H.J. Res. 107.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Joint Resolution providing for congressional disapproval 
     under chapter of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Federal Communications Commission relating 
     to ``The Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act: Preventing 
     and Elimination of Digital Discrimination''.
```
