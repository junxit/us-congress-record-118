# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7136

**Measures:** H.R. 7136

```
           By Mr. SCOTT FRANKLIN of Florida:
       H.R. 7136.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to establish a 
     comprehensive standard for timing between referrals and 
     appointments for care from the Department of Veterans Affairs 
     and to submit a report with respect to that standard, and for 
     other purposes.
```
