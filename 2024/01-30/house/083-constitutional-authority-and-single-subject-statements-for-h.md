# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7152

**Measures:** H.R. 7152

```
            By Ms. WEXTON:
        H.R. 7152.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Spending Clause, Article I, Section 8, Clause 1.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The bill would create a national registry of separated 
     Korean-Americans to facilitate reunification with their 
     families in North Korea. The bill also encourages a dialogue 
     between North Korea and the United States.
```
