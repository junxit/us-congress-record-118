# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7587

**Measures:** H.R. 7587

```
           By Mrs. PELTOLA:
       H.R. 7587.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the United States Constitution, 
     specifically clause 3, and clause 18
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Transportation to issue a notice 
     of proposed rulemaking with respect to categorical exclusions 
     of the Maritime Administration, and for other purposes.
```
