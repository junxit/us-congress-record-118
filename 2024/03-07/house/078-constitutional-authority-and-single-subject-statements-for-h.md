# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 7588

**Measures:** H.R. 7588

```
           By Ms. SCHAKOWSKY:
       H.R. 7588.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clauses 3 and 18 of Section 8 of Article 1 of the 
     Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To direct the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the 
     Administrator of the Food Safety and Inspection Service, to 
     reassess certain food ingredients used in the preparation of 
     meat, poultry, and egg products, and for other purposes.
```
