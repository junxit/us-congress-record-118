# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3821, H.R. 7454

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:03 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Alli, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3821. An act to reauthorize the Firefighter Cancer 
     Registry Act of 2018.


                          enrolled bill signed

  At 2:47 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 7454. An act to amend title 49, United States Code, to 
     extend authorizations for the airport improvement program, to 
     amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to extend the funding 
     and expenditure authority of the Airport and Airway Trust 
     Fund, and for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mrs. Murray).

                          ____________________
```
