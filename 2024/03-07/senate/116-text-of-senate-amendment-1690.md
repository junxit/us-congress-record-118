# Text of Senate Amendment 1690

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
  SA 1690. Mr. CORNYN submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4366, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

        At the appropriate place, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  The payment rate for livestock indemnity payments 
     described in section 1501(b)(2) of the Agricultural Act of 
     2014 (7 U.S.C. 9081(b)(2)) shall be 90 percent in the case of 
     losses due to wildfires occurring in the States of Texas and 
     Oklahoma in calendar year 2024.

                          ____________________
```
