                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 740

  At the request of Mr. Boozman, the names of the Senator from Arkansas 
(Mr. Cotton) and the Senator from Maine (Ms. Collins) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 740, a bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
reinstate criminal penalties for persons charging veterans unauthorized 
fees relating to claims for benefits under the laws administered by the 
Secretary of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.

Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 799, a bill to

coverage for all physicians' services furnished by doctors of
