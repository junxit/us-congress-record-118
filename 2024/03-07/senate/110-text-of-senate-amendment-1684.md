# Text of Senate Amendment 1684

**Speaking:** Lee, Mike (L000577) [R-UT]

```
  SA 1684. Mr. LEE submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4366, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in title V of division D, insert 
     the following:


                       limitation on use of funds

       Sec. 5__. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to carry out the final rule of the Corps of Engineers 
     and the Environmental Protection Agency entitled ``Revised 
     Definition of `Waters of the United States' '' (88 Fed. Reg. 
     3004 (January 18, 2023)).
                                 ______
```
