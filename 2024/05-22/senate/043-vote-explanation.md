# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Tester, Jon (T000464) [D-MT]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

  Mr. TESTER. Madam President, I was absent due to a personal matter 
when the Senate voted on vote No. 177 on confirmation of Angela M. 
Martinez, of Arizona, to be U.S. District Judge for the District of 
Arizona. On vote No. 177, had I been present, I would have voted yea.
  Madam President, I was absent due to a personal matter when the 
Senate voted on vote No. 178 on the motion to invoke cloture on Dena M. 
Coggins to be U.S. District Judge for the Eastern District of 
California. On vote No. 178, had I been present, I would have voted 
yea.
```
