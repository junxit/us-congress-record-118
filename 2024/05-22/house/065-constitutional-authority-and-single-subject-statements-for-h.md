# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8491

**Measures:** H.R. 8491

```
           By Mr. CARTWRIGHT:
       H.R. 8491.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (relating to the power of 
     Congress to regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among 
     the several States, and with the Indian Tribes.)
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       The Coal Royalty Fairness and Communities Investment Act of 
     2024 would close loopholes in the federal coal royalty 
     payment system and use royalties to help diversify and 
     strengthen economies of struggling coal communities. (Natural 
     Resources)
```
