# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8501

**Measures:** H.R. 8501

```
           By Ms. JACOBS:
       H.R. 8501.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the issuance of licenses for the exportation of 
     certain defense articles to the United Arab Emirates until 
     the President certifies to Congress that the UAE is no longer 
     providing materiel support to the Rapid Support Forces in 
     Sudan.
```
