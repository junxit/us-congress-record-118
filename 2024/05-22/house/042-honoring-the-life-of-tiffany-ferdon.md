# HONORING THE LIFE OF TIFFANY FERDON

**Speaking:** Newhouse, Dan (N000189) [R-WA]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF TIFFANY FERDON

  (Mr. NEWHOUSE asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to honor the life of a 
dedicated first responder from Okanogan, Washington, Tiffany Ferdon, 
who we tragically lost last month.
  Tiffany was a volunteer firefighter, an EMT for the Tonasket Fire 
Department and Aeneas Valley Fire Department, as well as a member of 
the Samaritan Riders, a group of motorcycle enthusiasts who serve the 
medically challenged and socially disadvantaged children throughout our 
region. Her passion was making the world a better place, a quality 
which was clear in the work that she did.
  Tiffany's death is a loss for the whole community, but her legacy 
will never be forgotten. May her family and community continue to be 
blessed with her memory, and may she rest in peace.

                          ____________________
```
