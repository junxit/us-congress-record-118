# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Parliamentarian will please read a 
communication to the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mrs. 
Murray).
  The Parliamentarian read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                     Washington, DC, May 10, 2024.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule 1, paragraph 3, of the 
     Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable 
     Sheldon Whitehouse, a Senator from the State of Rhode Island, 
     to perform the duties of the Chair.
                                                     Patty Murray,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE thereupon assumed the Chair as Acting President pro 
tempore.

                          ____________________
```
