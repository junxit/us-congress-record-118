# RECOGNIZING THE 2024 LUZERNE COUNTY SPORTS HALL OF FAME INDUCTEES

**Speaking:** Cartwright, Matt (C001090) [D-PA]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE 2024 LUZERNE COUNTY SPORTS HALL OF FAME INDUCTEES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MATT CARTWRIGHT

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Mr. CARTWRIGHT. Mr. Speaker, today I recognize the 2024 Luzerne 
County Sports Hall of Fame inductees. This year's class of sports 
superstars includes multiple U.S. Olympians and All-American athletes, 
an Ironman Triathlete and Ultramarathoner, dedicated coaches, and more.
  Each year, the Luzerne County Sports Hall of Fame honors those who 
have brought fame and recognition to the state of Pennsylvania through 
their outstanding achievements and contributions in athletic endeavors.
  This year's inductees include:
  Francis Xavier Baur of Shavertown--Football and Basketball
  Frederick ``Fritz'' Bleich of Hanover Township--Wrestling
  Qadry ``Missile'' Ismail of Wilkes-Barre--Football and Track & Field
  Raghib ``Rocket'' Ismail of Wilkes-Barre--Football and Track & Field
  Samuel ``Sam'' Hyder of Hanover Township--Karate International 
Competitor
  Sean Ribbons, Esq. of Dallas--Ironman Triathlete and Ultra Marathoner
  Jayson Terdiman of Berwick--U.S. Winter Olympics Luge 2014 and 2018
  Ray Yakavonis of Hanover Township--Football and Track & Field
  Edward Zawatski of Larksville--Swimming and Water Polo Athlete and 
Coach
  Taylor Lillian Dean of Mountaintop--Figure Skating U.S. Olympics 
Committee Representative
  Amanda Faust of Larksville--Field Hockey Athlete and Coach
  Sara Silvetti of Mountaintop--Field Hockey U.S. Olympics Alternative 
2008
  Each person honored has made sports history through their outstanding 
feats of athletic achievement here at home and in many cases around the 
world. They have left an indelible mark on regional athletics, and I am 
pleased to celebrate their contributions to our sports communities in 
Northeastern Pennsylvania.
  It is an honor to recognize these 12 talented individuals on their 
induction into the Luzerne County Sports Hall of Fame. May their 
stories and impressive careers inspire the next generation of 
Pennsylvania athletes striving for excellence.

                          ____________________
```
