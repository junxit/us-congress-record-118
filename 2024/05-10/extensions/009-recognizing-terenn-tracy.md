# RECOGNIZING TERENN TRACY

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING TERENN TRACY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Terenn Tracy of Arvada, Colorado, for winning the Arvada Chamber of 
Commerce's Rising Star Award.
  The Rising Star Award recognizes High School students who have 
exemplified extraordinary skills in entrepreneurship, innovation, 
community impact, and leadership.
  Terenn's teachers and peers alike have recognized her as kind, 
honest, committed, aware, respectful, and empathetic. She has served 
the Arvada community through an internship with Outdoor Lab, offering 
to adapt the curriculum around Native Americans at Windy Peak, and 
through numerous clubs and organizations. Her commitment to the 
environment and public service demonstrate her bright future as an 
advocate for Colorado.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Terenn Tracy for winning the Arvada 
Chamber's Rising Star Award and thank her for her contributions to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
