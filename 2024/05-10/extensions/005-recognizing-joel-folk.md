# RECOGNIZING JOEL FOLK

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                         RECOGNIZING JOEL FOLK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Joel Folk of 
Arvada, Colorado for winning the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's Image 
Award.
  Joel is a retired veterinarian and a nearly 30-year member of the 
Arvada Jefferson Kiwanis. He is also an active volunteer for the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth. Over many years and through 
his countless volunteer hours, Joel has improved the lives of the youth 
and elderly across Jefferson County. Arvada Chamber Image Award winners 
are selected for the positive image of Arvada they portray within the 
community, and Joel has done just that.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Joel Folk for winning this award and 
thank him for his commitment to our community.

                          ____________________
```
