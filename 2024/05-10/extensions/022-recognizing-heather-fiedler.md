# RECOGNIZING HEATHER FIEDLER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING HEATHER FIEDLER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Heather Fiedler 
of Arvada, Colorado, for winning the Image Award from the Arvada 
Chamber of Commerce.
  The Arvada Chamber issues the Image Award to exceptional individuals 
who have a demonstrated commitment to the Arvada community and its 
public image.
  For over 10 years, Heather has been an active member of the Arvada 
Community Garden, leading their efforts to provide fresh food, 
community, and gardening education to all Arvadans. She also plays a 
primary role in the Garrison Garden Paseo project and teaches over 
fifteen gardening classes every year to over four hundred attendees. 
Heather's hard work and dedication to the Arvada gardening community 
has fed and enriched the lives of hundreds of citizens and visitors 
alike. This award is a true testament to the leadership Heather has 
displayed as a gardener and leader in Arvada.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Heather Fiedler for winning the Image 
Award and thank her for her contributions to our community.
```
