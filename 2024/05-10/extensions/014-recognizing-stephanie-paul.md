# RECOGNIZING STEPHANIE PAUL

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                       RECOGNIZING STEPHANIE PAUL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Stephanie Paul 
for earning this year's Young Professional Leadership Award from the 
Arvada Chamber of Commerce.
  For years, Stephanie has shown a commitment to improving Olde Town 
Arvada. As the Director of Marketing and Events for the Arvada Business 
Improvement District, she has helped transform Olde Town into a vibrant 
area that residents and visitors alike can enjoy.
  Stephanie was an instrumental part of bolstering the Olde Town Second 
Saturday summer concert series, doubling attendance since the beginning 
of her tenure. She actively engages with local business leaders and 
community members to ensure projects have input from everyone involved.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Stephanie Paul on receiving the 2023 
Arvada Chamber of Commerce's Young Professional Leadership Award.

                          ____________________
```
