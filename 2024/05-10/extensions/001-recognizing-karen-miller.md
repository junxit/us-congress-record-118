# RECOGNIZING KAREN MILLER

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                        RECOGNIZING KAREN MILLER

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Karen Miller 
for earning this year's Woman of the Year Award from the Arvada Chamber 
of Commerce.
  Karen is a longtime volunteer throughout Jefferson County, especially 
in Olde Town Arvada. She served on the Historic Olde Town Arvada (HOTA) 
Board for many years and was instrumental in the creation of the 
Business Improvement District for Olde Town in 2014. Karen is a 
passionate and dedicated leader and a tremendous asset to the Arvada 
community. She dedicated countless hours to local events including the 
Harvest Festival, St. Patrick's Day, Festival of Scarecrows, A 
Chocolate Affair, and Flicks in the Square. Karen currently sits on the 
Arvada Historical Society Board and manages the Arvada Flour Mill.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Karen Miller on receiving the 2023 
Arvada Chamber of Commerce Woman of the Year Award.

                          ____________________
```
