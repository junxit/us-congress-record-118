# RECOGNIZING ASHLEY PALACIOS

**Speaking:** Pettersen, Brittany (P000620) [D-CO]

```
                      RECOGNIZING ASHLEY PALACIOS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ashley Palacios 
with Hope House Colorado for winning the Arvada Chamber of Commerce's 
Young Professional Leadership Award.
  The Young Professional Leadership Award recognizes an Arvada young 
professional whose leadership and dedication has impacted the world 
around them.
  Ashley is the Director of Operations at Hope House Colorado, an 
Arvada-based resource center and housing provider for teen mothers and 
their children. She is currently working to help open their Early 
Learning Center to give more opportunities to kids in the community. 
Relentless and compassionate, Ashley has also been a dedicated 
volunteer tutor with at-risk youth with Whiz Kids for twelve years, as 
well as a youth soccer coach.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Ashley on her award and thank her for 
all she does to support young people in our community.
```
