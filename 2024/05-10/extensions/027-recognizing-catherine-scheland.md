# RECOGNIZING CATHERINE SCHELAND

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                     RECOGNIZING CATHERINE SCHELAND

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions made to our community by one of my constituents, 
Catherine Scheland, has exemplified outstanding dedication and 
selflessness as a volunteer with a local non-profit, One Community Now. 
Cathy plays a pivotal role in the organization's Veterans program, 
serving as a team leader for Operation Stand Down. Her commitment is 
truly commendable, dedicating over 180 hours each year to the event's 
setup, overseeing the Chaplains' area, and co-directing the 
transportation area. Beyond the event, Cathy personally delivers 
bicycles to veterans in need throughout the year, using her own 
resources to provide locks and lights. She serves as a mentor, offering 
support and using her own funds to assist homeless veterans with 
housing. In the organization's Pack A Sack initiative to fight 
childhood hunger, Cathy is also actively involved, ensuring children 
have food every weekend of the year. She not only contributes her time 
but also financially supports the program. Cathy's engagement extends 
to the agency's annual Pack the Park fundraiser, where she plays a 
vital role in securing sponsors, donations, and volunteers. Cathy's 
dedication isn't confined to our One Community Now; she volunteers with 
other

[[Page E479]]

non-profits and actively participates in her church. Her strong faith, 
character, and compassionate heart make her a hero in our community. 
Additionally, Cathy's role as a prayer warrior and her unwavering 
support for those in need further showcase her exceptional qualities.
  It is clear that Ms. Scheland has made a profoundly positive impact 
on our community, and I am grateful for her service.

                          ____________________
```
