# HONORING REDFORD TOWNSHIP RECREATION & WELLNESS CENTER

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
         HONORING REDFORD TOWNSHIP RECREATION & WELLNESS CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today we recognize a historic milestone for 
Redford Township as they break ground on their community Recreation and 
Wellness Center, serving the residents throughout the township. As the 
Congressperson for Redford Township, I am blessed to have played a 
small part in obtaining funding for the Center.
  This is the first recreation-focused building in Redford Township in 
more than fifty years. The project reflects the hard work and 
contributions of many who helped bring it to life. With a nearly 
50,000-strong population, Redford has made strides to create 
opportunities to enjoy outdoor and recreational activities. I am 
confident that this project will be a treasure and resource for 
countless residents for years to come.
  The 12th Congressional District joins in celebration of this 
momentous occasion with Redford Township and our residents of the 
groundbreaking of its Recreation and Wellness Center.

                          ____________________
```
