# RECOGNIZING WILLIAM LEWIS

**Speaking:** Bilirakis, Gus M. (B001257) [R-FL]

```
                       RECOGNIZING WILLIAM LEWIS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
unique contributions made to our community by one of my constituents, 
William Lewis. A distinguished Army veteran, William, also known as 
Bill, has been an integral part of the Board of Directors for a local 
non-profit, One Community Now, serving as a dedicated volunteer since 
2013. Bill's commitment extends beyond his role at One Community Now; 
he serves as a deacon at the First United Methodist Church of New Port 
Richey and is an active member of the American Legion Post 79. As a 
leader in Operation Stand Down, Bill plays a pivotal role in 
fundraising and establishing connections with various Veteran 
organizations throughout the Tampa Bay area, including American 
Legions, VFWs, Am Vets, Eagles, Elks, and others. His dedication is 
evident in his weekly support for veterans at our Veteran Support 
Center, where he provides lunch and assistance to homeless and at-risk 
veterans. Bill brings his extensive business experience and strong work 
ethic to One Community Now's Board meetings, contributing to the 
organization's strategic direction. At his church, Bill assists his 
wife, Jean Ann, in managing the food pantry, making personal deliveries 
of food and supplies to shut-ins. He actively participates in the Pack-
A-Sack program, leveraging his network to secure donations of food 
items. Bill's readiness to respond to any call for assistance reflects 
his heart for Veterans, children, and anyone facing hunger, driven by 
his commitment to serving the Lord. In addition to his roles at One 
Community Now, his church, and at the American Legion Post 79, Bill 
actively engages in various committees and programs, such as The 
American Legion's Oratorical Contest, the Christmas Wreaths Across 
America and the Cub Scouts of America. Despite approaching his 80th 
birthday in April, Bill's shows no sign of slowing down. He continues 
to demonstrate an unwavering dedication to others.
  It is clear that Mr. Lewis has made a profoundly positive impact on 
our community, and I am grateful for his service.

                          ____________________
```
