# HONORING ANNETTE WOLF'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Frankel, Lois (F000462) [D-FL]

```
                 HONORING ANNETTE WOLF'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOIS FRANKEL

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 2024

  Ms. LOIS FRANKEL of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Annette Wolf for her milestone 100th birthday celebrated on April 1, 
2024. Annette has been a resident of Boynton Beach, Florida since 1999 
and is a proud wife and mother to two great men. What a wonderful 
occasion for Annette, her family, and friends as she reminisces and 
celebrates a life great in years and rich in accomplishments.
  Annette and her husband ran a corrugated box factory business in 
Upstate New York for several years. She started the Women's Business 
and Professional Organization in 1954 in Glens Falls, NY. Annette loves 
to socialize with her friends by playing Mah-Jongg and Canasta. She 
believes in doing everything in moderation and is an avid reader and 
regular viewer of MSNBC and CNN.
  She has lived through extraordinary times in history and has no doubt 
witnessed remarkable changes unimaginable at the time of her birth in 
1924. May this be a joyous time as her family and friends gather to 
celebrate this amazing milestone.
  Annette always has a smile on her face and remains positive in all 
things. Mr. Speaker, it is an honor to congratulate Ms. Annette Wolf on 
her 100th birthday and I wish her good health and great happiness.

                          ____________________
```
