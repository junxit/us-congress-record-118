# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8348

**Measures:** H.R. 8348

```
            By Mr. CARTER of Louisiana:
        H.R. 8348.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Clause, Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1, along with the 
     Necessary and Proper Clause, Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18.
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        To establish in the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure 
     Security Agency of the Department of Homeland Security a task 
     force on artificial intelligence, and for other purposes.
```
