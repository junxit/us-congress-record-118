# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8353

**Measures:** H.R. 8353

```
            By Ms. HOULAHAN:
        H.R. 8353.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Section 1, Article 8 of the Constitution
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        To require the Election Assistance Commission to develop 
     voluntary guidelines for the administration of elections that 
     address the use and risks of artificial intelligence 
     technologies, and for other purposes.
```
