# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8351

**Measures:** H.R. 8351

```
            By Mr. GOLDEN of Maine:
        H.R. 8351.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
        The single subject of this legislation is:
        Requiring the imposition of increased duties on autos 
     imported into the United States that originate in the 
     People's Republic of China.
```
