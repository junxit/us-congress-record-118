# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8340

**Measures:** H.R. 8340

```
           By Mr. SHERMAN:
       H.R. 8340.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     Clause 3 of Section 8 of Article I of the United States 
     Constitution; and Clause 18 of Section 8 of Article I of the 
     United States Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       Amends the United States Housing Act of 1937 to exclude 
     veteran disability benefits from the definition of annual 
     income for purposes of eligibility for housing units.
```
