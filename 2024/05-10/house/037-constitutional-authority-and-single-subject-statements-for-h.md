# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8352

**Measures:** H.R. 8352

            By Mr. GOLDEN of Maine:
        H.R. 8352.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article 1, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution

        Requiring the imposition of additional duties on imports
