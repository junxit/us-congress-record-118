# HONORING FIRE LIEUTENANT BRIAN PEEK

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
                  HONORING FIRE LIEUTENANT BRIAN PEEK

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 8, 2024

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I'm honored and humbled to posthumously honor 
Fire Lieutenant (Lt.) Brian Peek, who tragically passed away in the 
Line of Duty while serving as a member of the Goldsboro Fire Company.
  Lt. Peek was a gift and blessing to our community throughout his more 
than 50 years of service as a Firefighter, who was dedicated to 
mentoring new members, and was tirelessly committed to the Station and 
to the Citizens it serves. He will be greatly missed by all who knew 
him or were blessed to share his life even in a small way.
  Lt. Peek served as Fire Lt. 68-3, Fire Police Lt. 68, and Fire Police 
28-4 in his myriad years of service. Lt. Peek leaves behind his beloved 
family--two sons, two stepsons, his brother, and 18 grandchildren--who 
will carry his legacy.
  Mr. Speaker I'm saddened, honored, and humbled to recognize Fire 
Lieutenant Brian Peek on his tireless, selfless, and unwavering service 
to our communities, Commonwealth, and United States of America. He set 
the standard by which all should be judged.
```
