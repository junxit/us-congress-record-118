# RECOGNIZING THE CATHOLIC DAUGHTERS COURT REGINA COELI NO. 1521 OF ST. ANNE'S PARISH

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE CATHOLIC DAUGHTERS COURT REGINA COELI NO. 1521 OF ST. 
                             ANNE'S PARISH

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 8, 2024

  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Catholic 
Daughters Court Regina Coeli No. 1521 of St. Anne's Parish on their 75-
year anniversary and to thank them for their significant contributions 
to the Porterville community. Catholic Daughters Court Regina Coeli No. 
1521 of St. Anne's Parish, a member of the Catholic Daughters of 
America, was established on October 30, 1949. Since their founding, the 
Catholic Daughters have played a vital role in supporting the parish 
and the broader community through their volunteer work. Guided by a 
mission of faith and charity, the Catholic Daughters have made a 
profound impact on the Tulare County community. Today, the organization 
has 70 members and are active in non-profit and faith-based 
organizations. The Catholic Daughters participate in and host numerous 
events each year to raise funds for parish initiatives. The Christmas 
Bazaar is one of their signature events, and features auction items and 
homemade baked goods. They also host an annual Spring Tea, yard sales, 
bake sales and a Bunco game night. The Catholic Daughters support 
several charities in the community, including the St. Anne's Food 
Pantry, Tulare-Kings Right to Life, and the Homeless Alliance. Each 
year, the organization awards academic scholarships to support recent 
high school graduates from the parish. For 75 years, the Catholic 
Daughters have remained steadfast in their dedication to the well-being 
of St. Anne's Parish and the Porterville Community. The members of the 
Catholic Daughters have always been committed to uplifting each other 
through their faith, long-standing friendships, and shared commitment 
to the betterment of others.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask all my colleagues in the House of Representatives 
to join me in celebrating the Catholic Daughters Court Regina Coeli No. 
1521 of St. Anne's Parish on their 75-year anniversary. Their work to 
enrich the community of Porterville will be felt for generations to 
come.
```
