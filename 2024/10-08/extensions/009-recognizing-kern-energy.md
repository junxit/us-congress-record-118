# RECOGNIZING KERN ENERGY

**Speaking:** Valadao, David G. (V000129) [R-CA]

```
                        RECOGNIZING KERN ENERGY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. DAVID G. VALADAO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, October 8, 2024

  Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker. I rise today to recognize Kern Energy on 
their 90-year anniversary and to thank them for their significant 
contributions to the Central Valley. Kern Energy, established in 1934, 
is an independent, family-owned and operated transportation fuel 
producer in the Southern San Joaquin Valley. For nine decades, Kern 
Energy has been critical to the success of Central Valley agriculture, 
providing the region with the fuel necessary to help transport goods 
across California and the Nation. To date, Kern Energy has produced 
over 58 million gallons of renewable diesel and blended over 74 million 
gallons of biodiesel into its finished fuels. Throughout their history, 
Kern Energy has been a leader of energy innovation, consistently 
overcoming California's operational challenges. Kern Energy is 
dedicated to advancing cleaner transportation fuels by investing in 
innovative solutions for future generations. The company's unwavering 
commitment to meeting the ever-changing energy needs of California has 
guided their mission for nearly a century. Kern Energy has adopted the 
motto of ``small but mighty'' to reflect their significant impact on 
the community. Kern Energy has

[[Page E1021]]

been a steadfast partner to the Central Valley and California, 
supporting the growth and progress of the region. It is because of the 
hard work of Kern Energy and their employees that Valley families, 
small businesses, and producers have the fuel they need to thrive.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to 
join me in celebrating Kern Energy on their 90-year anniversary. Their 
work to provide the Central Valley with reliable, clean energy will be 
felt for generations to come. I wish them continued success in the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
