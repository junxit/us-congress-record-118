# HONORING GIUSEPPE ANOIA'S 90TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

```
                HONORING GIUSEPPE ANOIA'S 90TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. HARRIS. Mr. Speaker, it is a privilege and honor to recognize Mr. 
Giuseppe Anoia as he approaches his 90th birthday this November.
  Born November 19, 1934, Mr. Anoia has been a constituent of 
Maryland's First Congressional District since he immigrated to the 
United States from Italy in 1961. He received a degree in civil 
engineering from Johns Hopkins University in 1970 and worked for 
Baltimore City for 17 years until his retirement in 2000. Mr. Anoia has 
been happily married to Phillis, nee D'Adamo, for over 32 years.
  It is a distinct privilege to recognize Mr. Anoia on his upcoming 
90th birthday and celebrate this wonderful milestone. On behalf of 
Maryland's First Congressional District, I wish Mr. Anoia a very happy 
birthday.

                          ____________________
```
