# HONORING THE 200TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MORRIS COUNTY PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE

**Speaking:** Sherrill, Mikie (S001207) [D-NJ]

```
HONORING THE 200TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE MORRIS COUNTY PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MIKIE SHERRILL

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Ms. SHERRILL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 200th Anniversary 
of The Morris County Prosecutor's Office, an agency in New Jersey's 
Eleventh Congressional District, and its outstanding commitment to 
serving Morris County and improving the quality of life within the 
community. The Morris County Prosecutor's Office works to protect and 
uphold the laws of the State of New Jersey and keep our residents safe.
  On December 20, 1824, George K. Drake was appointed the first 
Prosecutor of the Pleas for Morris County, making Morris County one of 
the first Prosecutor's offices to be established in New Jersey. Since 
its founding, The Morris County Prosecutor's Office has been dedicated 
to keeping Morris County safe and a great place to live, work, and 
raise a family. The Morris County Prosecutor's Office has been able to 
achieve their mission for two centuries by working with members of the 
Morris County community and law enforcement agencies.
  The Morris County Prosecutor's Office is committed to justice, 
fairness, crime reduction, working with law enforcement, and attending 
to the needs of the public. The office leads and supervises chiefs of 
police and municipal prosecutors, ensuring that their mission is 
fulfilled throughout the county on a local level.
  This year marks the bicentennial anniversary of The Morris County 
Prosecutor's Office, and I admire their dedication to protecting and 
serving Morris County. I am grateful for all of their effort and 
accomplishments that have kept Morris County residents safe and look 
forward to seeing their continued work.

                          ____________________
```
