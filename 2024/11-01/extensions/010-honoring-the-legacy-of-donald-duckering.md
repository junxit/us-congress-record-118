# HONORING THE LEGACY OF DONALD DUCKERING

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
                HONORING THE LEGACY OF DONALD DUCKERING

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. COSTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the distinguished life 
and incomparable spirit of Donald ``Don'' Webster Duckering, an 
inspiration and beloved member of his community.
  Born in New York, Donald was the son of Lynn and Mae Duckering. He, 
along with his brother Verle, was raised in Santa Cruz, California, 
where their father was a prominent architect. Donald excelled in 
athletics during his youth, particularly in tennis, snow skiing, golf, 
and polo. His passion for aviation led him to earn a pilot's license at 
the age of 16. During high school, Donald's mechanical skills, 
particularly his work on fast cars, captivated the attention of Hilda, 
who would later become his devoted wife. Together, they shared a 
lifelong love for British Triumph sports cars, even participating in 
the Pebble Beach Sports Car Rallies with their sons, Dale and Lee.
  Donald pursued higher education at the University of Washington, 
where he was an active member of the Delta Tau Delta fraternity. He 
graduated with a bachelor of arts in transportation engineering and was 
commissioned as an ensign through the Naval Reserve Officers Training 
Corps (NROTC) program. A pivotal moment in Donald's life was his 
appointment to naval flight school in Pensacola, Florida, where he 
earned his wings as a naval aviator. After advanced training in 
Hutchinson, Kansas, Donald served as a patrol plane pilot commander, 
leading missions across various global locations.
  A highlight of Donald's distinguished career was his selection to 
attend the Naval Postgraduate School in Monterey, California, where he 
earned a bachelor of science in meteorology. He subsequently served as 
chief meteorologist aboard the USS Oriskany, playing a key role in 
numerous missions in the Middle East.
  In 1963, Donald retired from the Navy as a lieutenant commander and 
transitioned to a successful career with Atmospherics Incorporated in 
Fresno, California. Over nearly four decades, he held significant roles 
as a meteorologist, chief pilot, and vice president.
  Donald's personal life was marked by a loving and enduring marriage 
to his wife, Hilda, lasting 72 years. They treasured their friendships 
at the Sunnyside Swim & Racquet Club and later at the Fort Washington 
Golf & Country Club. Donald's passion for the outdoors led to memorable 
family camping trips, high Sierra backpacking adventures, and a 
successful venture into international fly tying. He also shared a 
special bond with his daughter Judith, racing sailboats
  Donald is survived by his beloved wife Hilda; his children: Lee and 
his wife Debra, Dale and his wife Debbie, and daughter Judith and her 
husband Scott, seven grandchildren, seven great-grandchildren, and 
other close relatives. He was preceded in death by his parents, Lynn 
and Mae Duckering, his brother Verle, and other cherished family 
members.
  In Don's passing, we celebrate a life that has left an indelible mark 
on the world and all who were fortunate enough to know him. His legacy 
will be remembered with respect and admiration by all who knew him.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my esteemed colleagues to join me in paying 
tribute to the memory of Donald Duckering, a true pioneer, and 
adventurer.
```
