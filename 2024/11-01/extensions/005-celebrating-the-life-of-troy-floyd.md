# CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF TROY FLOYD

**Speaking:** Reschenthaler, Guy (R000610) [R-PA]

```
                   CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF TROY FLOYD

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUY RESCHENTHALER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. RESCHENTHALER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the life and 
legacy of Patrolman Troy Floyd, who was killed in the line of duty on 
August 8, 2024.
  A lifelong resident of Lincoln County, Mississippi, Troy graduated 
from Brookhaven High School in 1993. He started his career as a law 
enforcement officer in 1996, dedicating his life to protecting and 
serving the southern Mississippi community.
  Troy spent his years in law enforcement as a Lincoln County jailer 
and deputy. Additionally, he was a certified agent with the Mississippi 
Department of Corrections and former assistant police chief of Wesson, 
Mississippi. He spent his career working to improve the state's law 
enforcement agencies by training officers through many programs. He 
also taught children the dangers of drugs and alcohol through the DARE 
Program and mentored underprivileged youth at Camp Shelby.
  Troy lived a faithful life and served his community and family every 
day. He was dearly loved by many because of his kindhearted nature, 
loyalty, selflessness, and dedication to helping others.
  Troy is survived by his wife, Sage Floyd, three children, four 
siblings, and two grandchildren.
  Mr. Speaker, Mississippi Patrolman Troy Floyd's faith and service to 
his community is a shining example of what it means to be a public 
servant. I wish his family healing and strength during this difficult 
time.
```
