# CONGRATULATING THE LA DODGERS AND FREDDIE FREEMAN

**Speaking:** Steel, Michelle (S001135) [R-CA]

```
           CONGRATULATING THE LA DODGERS AND FREDDIE FREEMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MICHELLE STEEL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mrs. STEEL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the Los Angeles Dodgers for 
bringing home their eighth World Series title. I am especially proud of 
first baseman Freddie Freeman, a California-45 native from Fountain 
Valley, for representing Orange County so well on the national stage 
and bringing home the World Series MVP award.
  Freddie has shown remarkable perseverance in overcoming personal and 
professional trials, and this tremendous accomplishment is a testament 
to his work ethic and drive to succeed.
  A true Orange County hero, Freddie and this Dodgers team have 
excelled at the highest level and added to the rich baseball tradition 
of Southern California. I'm so proud of them for their accomplishments 
and can not wait to see more championships in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
