# HONORING DOUG WIGHT

**Speaking:** Thompson, Mike (T000460) [D-CA]

```
                          HONORING DOUG WIGHT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Doug 
Wight, who was named the 2024 Member of the Year by the Napa County 
Farm Bureau. Mr. Wight is being honored for his decades of outstanding 
contributions to our agricultural community in Napa County.
  Mr. Wight is a fifth-generation farmer in the Napa Valley who 
graduated from St. Helena High School in 1968. He obtained a degree in 
Fruit Science from Cal Poly in 1973 before returning home to plant his 
first vineyard of Cabernet Sauvignon at his family's Lewelling Ranch in 
1974. With Mr. Wight's extensive viticulture knowledge, he is upholding 
the 160-year tradition of continuous family farming on his family 
ranch. Today, Mr. Wight continues to farm and mentors the future 
farmers of his family.
  In 1983, Mr. Wight started his business, Wight Vineyard Management, 
which has been in operation for over 40 years. The company manages 500 
acres of vines across the Napa Valley. Over the course of his career, 
Mr. Wight has also been a longstanding member of the American Society 
of Oenologists and Viticulturists, one of the original members of Napa 
Valley Vineyard Technical Group (Vit Tech) and served on the board of 
directors of the Farm Supply and Grape Growers Association.
  Mr. Wight also made great contributions beyond our agricultural 
community. As Chairman of the St. Helena High School Class of '68 
Scholarship, Mr. Wight has helped ensure educational opportunities for 
our local students, supporting their growth and success. For 25 years, 
he has also served with pride on the St. Helena High School Football 
Chain Gang. Additionally, Mr. Wight was a founding member of the 
committee to reestablish the St. Helena High School FFA program, 
helping to revive and strengthen agricultural education programs in our 
region.
  Mr. Speaker, Doug Wight has continued to uphold his family's legacy 
of humility, generosity, and community in Napa County agriculture. His 
award as Napa County Farm Bureau's Member of the Year recognizes the 
positive impact he has had in Napa County. Therefore, it is fitting and 
proper that we honor him here today.

                          ____________________
```
