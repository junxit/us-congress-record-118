# RECOGNIZING PA-1'S HOMETOWN HERO RITA LENTZ

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
              RECOGNIZING PA-1'S HOMETOWN HERO RITA LENTZ

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
extraordinary individual from Pennsylvania's First Congressional 
District--someone who has gone above and beyond the call of duty in 
service to both our community and our country. It is with great respect 
and admiration, I recognize the late Rita Lentz, one of our district's 
distinguished Hometown Heroes.
  Hometown Heroes are not defined by titles or accolades but by the 
quiet power of their actions. They are distinguished by their 
unwavering integrity, the strength of their character, and their 
steadfast commitment to the people and places they serve. They are the 
foundation upon which our communities stand--the ones who lift others 
up, inspire hope, and, in doing so, call us all to higher standards of 
service and compassion. Throughout her remarkable life, Rita embodied 
this spirit in ways that words can scarcely capture.
  A lifelong Bristol Borough resident, Rita was a beacon of Bristol's 
small-town, neighborly compassion, known for her genuine acts of 
kindness that harkened back to a simpler time. A 1955 Bristol High 
School graduate, Rita went on to serve as the School Secretary at 
Warren-Snyder Elementary. Rita went to work committed to making a 
difference in the students' lives, instilling in them, and sharing the 
values of respect and love. As a lifelong parishioner at St. Ann 
Church, she was a woman of deep faith and a steadfast presence at mass, 
novenas, and all parish events. I looked forward to seeing Rita each 
year at the Annual St. Ann Carnival and Italian Festival, where she 
served as a volunteer since its inception over twenty-five years ago. 
No matter where or when, Rita always made the time to engage and make 
genuine connections with others. As a remarkable testament to her faith 
and compassion Rita made it a priority to attend and was a comforting 
presence at the funerals of generations of Bristolians. A lifetime of 
what may seem like simple acts of kindness has left an indelible and 
extraordinary mark on the Bristol Borough community.
  In times of uncertainty, it is the work of individuals like Rita that 
reminds us of the power of the human spirit. Her service, given without 
expectation of recognition, is testament to the belief that one 
individual's dedication can ripple through the lives of many, sparking 
change, fostering resilience, and inspiring hope. Today, we honor Rita 
not just for her remarkable deeds but for who she was--a true hero in 
every sense of the word.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's First Congressional District, I extend my 
deepest gratitude to the family of Rita Lentz for her extraordinary 
service. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Rita, whose spirit 
of service will inspire generations to come. May her example remind us 
of the boundless potential within each of us to make our communities, 
country, and world a better place.
```
