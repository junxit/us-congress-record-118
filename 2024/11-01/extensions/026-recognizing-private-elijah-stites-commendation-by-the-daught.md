# RECOGNIZING PRIVATE ELIJAH STITES' COMMENDATION BY THE DAUGHTERS OF THE AMERICAN REVOLUTION

**Speaking:** Jordan, Jim (J000289) [R-OH]

```
RECOGNIZING PRIVATE ELIJAH STITES' COMMENDATION BY THE DAUGHTERS OF THE 
                          AMERICAN REVOLUTION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to commend to the House the 
important work of the Lima Chapter of the Daughters of the American 
Revolution. On November 6, the chapter will rededicate a historical 
marker in honor of Private Elijah Stites, Lima's sole veteran of the 
Revolutionary War.
  Elijah Stites was born in Essex, New Jersey, in 1758. During the 
American Revolution, he fought for the Essex County Militia, serving as 
color bearer at the Battle of Yorktown. In 1788, he and two of his 
brothers took part in an expedition along the Ohio River, helping to 
found the settlement of Columbia (modern-day Cincinnati). Later, he 
settled in Warren County with his wife, Rhoda, and their fourteen 
children.

[[Page E1104]]

  Following his death in 1843, Stites was buried in Old Lima Cemetery 
in Allen County. Seventy years ago, the Lima DAR erected a plaque at 
the site of the cemetery recognizing him as the city's only 
Revolutionary War veteran. The organization will rededicate this plaque 
at a special ceremony on November 6.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Ohio's Fourth Congressional 
District, I thank the women of Lima's DAR Chapter for putting together 
this rededication ceremony and for all they do to promote civic 
education. They are true examples of the DAR's high calling to 
``cherish, maintain, and extend the institutions of American freedom.''
```
