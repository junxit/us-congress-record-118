# HONORING THE LATE JERRY WOLOZ

**Speaking:** Torres, Ritchie (T000486) [D-NY]

```
                     HONORING THE LATE JERRY WOLOZ

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, a proud American and a proud New 
Yorker, Jerry Woloz had a profound impact on the lives of many in the 
fields of education and social services. He devoted his life to lifting 
up the most vulnerable among us and educating the next generation. For 
several decades, he served with distinction as a social studies teacher 
and dean in the NYC public school system, educating students about the 
critical importance of our democracy and inspiring them to be civically 
engaged and claim our democracy as their own. Education is the noblest 
profession. Jerry Woloz made it his life's work, and we are all better 
for it.
  Jerry is survived by his wife of 54 years, Marilyn Woloz, and his two 
sons Michael and Jason, as well as his two beautiful grandchildren 
Gabriel and Olivia. The People's House is eternally grateful to Jerry 
Woloz for leaving behind a world better than the one he found.

                          ____________________
```
