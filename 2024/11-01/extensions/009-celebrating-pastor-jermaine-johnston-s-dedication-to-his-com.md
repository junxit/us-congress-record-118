# CELEBRATING PASTOR JERMAINE JOHNSTON'S DEDICATION TO HIS COMMUNITY

**Speaking:** Dingell, Debbie (D000624) [D-MI]

```
   CELEBRATING PASTOR JERMAINE JOHNSTON'S DEDICATION TO HIS COMMUNITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DEBBIE DINGELL

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate 
Pastor Jermaine Johnston of Ypsilanti, Michigan, on the 8th anniversary 
of his Founder's Day. Since 2016, his ministry ``Berea City of Hope'' 
has been a beacon of good will for the community and his work is worthy 
of commendation.
  Pastor Johnston came to Ypsilanti as a young boy when his family 
relocated from Brooklyn, New York. His path to the ministry is not all 
surprising, as he was often referred to as ``the little preacher'' by 
those who knew him. Raised by a single mother, Pastor Johnston grew up 
attending the St. James Church of God in Christ in Ypsilanti. He was a 
devoted church drummer for over 20 years, but despite everyone's 
expectations, he had no desire to preach.
  After attending a transformational service at Burning Bush 
International Ministries, Pastor Johnston decided to heed the calling 
and dedicate his life to the church. Under the guidance of then Pastor 
Don W. Shelby, Jr., Pastor Johnston served as an armorbearer, 
observing, learning and growing spiritually. In 2008, he was licensed 
as a minister, and in 2010, he was ordained as an elder by Bishop John 
H. Sheard. His dedication to the ministry led to his appointment as an 
Assistant Pastor in 2015.
  In the summer of 2016, Pastor Johnston brought the idea of having his 
own ministry to life, opening up the Berea City of Hope in Ypsilanti, 
Michigan. Together with his wife, Lena, the last eight years has seen 
Berea City of Hope truly become a bastion of hope for their community. 
This year, they will be hosting then 6th annual Back to School Fair, 
where they will be giving out free backpacks and school supplies to the 
neediest in the community. They also run a food pantry on the 3rd 
Saturday of each month, a summer literacy program, and a coat drive in 
the winter.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me today in celebrating the 
accomplishments of Pastor Jermaine Johnston and all of the good he has 
done for his community. It is leaders like Pastor Johnston that make 
our communities safer, stronger, and more prosperous. We thank him for 
his service, and are excited to see how his ministry continues to serve 
his community well into the future.
```
