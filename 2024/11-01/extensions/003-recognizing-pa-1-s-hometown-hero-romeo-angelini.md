# RECOGNIZING PA-1'S HOMETOWN HERO: ROMEO ANGELINI

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
            RECOGNIZING PA-1'S HOMETOWN HERO: ROMEO ANGELINI

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
extraordinary individual from Pennsylvania's First Congressional 
District--someone who has gone above and beyond the call of duty in 
service to both our community and our country. It is with great respect 
and admiration, that I recognize Romeo Angelini, one of our district's 
distinguished Hometown Heroes.
  Hometown Heroes are not defined by titles or accolades, but by the 
quiet power of their actions. They are distinguished by their 
unwavering integrity, the strength of their character, and their 
steadfast commitment to the people and places they serve. They are the 
foundation upon which our communities stand--the ones who lift others 
up, inspire hope, and in doing so, call us all to higher standards of 
service and compassion. Romeo has embodied this spirit in ways that 
words can scarcely capture.
  From a young age, Romeo recognized the most valuable gift one can 
give is the gift of giving back. So, since his tenth birthday, he has 
chosen to forgo the usual birthday festivities and dedicate his special 
day to a cause greater than himself. Every year, Romeo organizes and 
hosts food drives and fundraisers to benefit the Family Service 
Association of Bucks County, turning his birthday into an opportunity 
to uplift his community. Through these efforts, he has helped FSABC 
better serve the 50,000 community members who turn to them in their 
times of need. Romeo's remarkable dedication and commitment to serving 
our community have not gone unnoticed. Last October, he was recognized 
with the prestigious Susan N. Altomari Champion for Youth Award--an 
honor reserved for those who have profoundly impacted the lives of 
others in Bucks County.
  The impact of Romeo's work lies not only in the thousands of pounds 
of food he has helped collect but also in the poignant message it 
sends. It is a message of kindness, compassion, and the extraordinary 
power each of us holds to make a difference.
  Mr. Speaker, it is through the work of people like Romeo Angelini 
that our communities thrive and our Nation grows stronger. His service, 
given without expectation of recognition, stands as a testament to the 
belief that

[[Page E1098]]

one individual's dedication can ripple through the lives of many, 
sparking change, fostering resilience, and inspiring hope. Today we 
honor Romeo Angelini not just for his remarkable deeds but for who he 
is--a true hero in every sense of the word.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's First Congressional District, I extend my 
deepest gratitude to Romeo Angelini for his extraordinary service. I 
ask my colleagues to join me in honoring this exceptional individual, 
whose leadership and spirit of service will inspire generations to 
come. May his example remind us of the boundless potential within each 
of us to make our communities, country, and world a better place.

                          ____________________
```
