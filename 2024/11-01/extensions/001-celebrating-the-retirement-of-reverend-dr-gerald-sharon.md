# CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF REVEREND DR. GERALD SHARON

**Speaking:** Ruiz, Raul (R000599) [D-CA]

```
        CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF REVEREND DR. GERALD SHARON

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. RAUL RUIZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. RUIZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a remarkable man of 
faith, a devoted community leader, and a dear friend, Reverend Dr. 
Gerald Sharon. Dr. Gerald Sharon is a rostered pastor in the 
Evangelical Lutheran Church and retired on August 12, 2024, from Hope 
Lutheran Church in Palm Desert, California. After 45 years of faithful 
ministry across numerous churches and denominations, Dr. Sharon leaves 
behind a legacy that has profoundly shaped countless lives.
  When I think of his leadership, I'm reminded of how, in 2015, 
Southwest Church grew under his guidance to become one of the fastest-
growing congregations in the Nation, a milestone recognized by Outreach 
Magazine and Lifeway Research. Additionally, Dr. Sharon traveled 
extensively preaching and training pastors across Latin America, 
Europe, Africa, and Asia. He didn't achieve this by chance; he did it 
through his unwavering commitment to inclusion and his deeply rooted 
belief that every person deserves love and respect.
  It was the good Reverend Gerald Sharon who baptized my wife, Monica, 
who dedicated my daughters in the church, and who has been a friend and 
a guide through life. His Kentucky roots gave us a unique bond over 
bourbon, but it was his courage and transparency that set him apart. 
His openness brought a sense of ease to those who confided in him, and 
his storytelling is legendary. Dr. Sharon has that rare gift to touch 
both hearts and minds, inspiring people to love God, follow Jesus, and 
lead better lives.
  Due to his leadership skills and community involvement, he was 
invited to join the governing board of John F. Kennedy Memorial 
Hospital in Indio, California, where he served as Chairman from 2018 to 
2020. After completing a one-year term as Chairman Emeritus, he was 
asked to continue his role on the board. He also serves on the 
hospital's Bioethics Committee.
  Dr. Sharon received his bachelor of arts and master of arts degrees 
from Georgetown College in Kentucky. His master of divinity and doctor 
of ministry degrees were earned at The Southern Baptist Theological 
Seminary in Louisville, Kentucky.
  In addition to his numerous degrees and accolades, it is his kindness 
and dedication to everyone he encounters that truly defines him. And, 
alongside his beloved wife, Gesa, he has built a beautiful family. 
Together they have four children, three of whom are married, and five 
grandchildren.
  For me, my family, and countless others, we thank Reverend Dr. Gerald 
Sharon for his years of unwavering service, friendship, and for being a 
true beacon of faith and compassion. May his retirement be filled with 
joy and peace.

                          ____________________
```
