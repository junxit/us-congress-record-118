# HONORING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF FRESNO INTERDENOMINATIONAL REFUGEE MINISTRIES

**Speaking:** Costa, Jim (C001059) [D-CA]

```
  HONORING THE 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF FRESNO INTERDENOMINATIONAL REFUGEE 
                               MINISTRIES

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JIM COSTA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. COSTA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor The Fresno 
Interdenominational Refugee Ministries (FIRM) for its 30 years of 
impeccable service to the communities in search of safety and 
opportunity.
  FIRM is a faith-based, nonprofit organization located in the Central 
Valley, dedicated to serving refugees and individuals seeking a fresh 
start and new opportunities.
  Since its establishment in January 1994 through collaborations with 
various congregations, FIRM has assisted over 10,000 people from 
Southeast Asia, Africa, South America, and more than 22 other 
countries. Their involvement with Church World Service and commitment 
to refugee assistance began years before their official founding.
  Today, FIRM supports over 60,000 residents in Fresno County, focusing 
on meeting their needs with a range of comprehensive programs in 
health, education, community building, and youth outreach. These 
initiatives provide essential resources and drive positive change, 
benefiting the community and future generations.
  FIRM's exceptional service has been recognized with numerous awards, 
including the `Outstanding Performance in Community Economic 
Development and Revitalization,' `Outstanding Service and Leadership in 
the Community,' the `Martin Luther King Community Service Award,' and 
the `Vocational Excellence Award,' among other prestigious honors. 
Their impactful work continues to be supported and valued by the local 
community.
  As we celebrate FIRM's 30th anniversary, I commend their leadership, 
dedication to the valley, and their role as a vital resource for those 
striving for a better future.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my esteemed colleagues to join me in honoring 
the Fresno Interdenominational Refugee Ministries. Let us celebrate 
their legacy and their ongoing commitment to prioritizing the valley's 
refugees and providing them a safeguard in which they all call 
``home.''
```
