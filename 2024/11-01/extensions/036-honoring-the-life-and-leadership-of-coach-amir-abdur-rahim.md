# HONORING THE LIFE AND LEADERSHIP OF COACH AMIR ABDUR-RAHIM

**Speaking:** Castor, Kathy (C001066) [D-FL]

```
       HONORING THE LIFE AND LEADERSHIP OF COACH AMIR ABDUR-RAHIM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. KATHY CASTOR

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Ms. CASTOR of Florida. Mr Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart to 
honor the life of Amir Abdur-Rahim who Inspired students and our Tampa 
Bay community with his sincerity, generosity and passion. As the 
vibrant Head Coach for the University of South Florida Men's Basketball 
team, Coach Abdul-Rahim set high moral standards for all and displayed 
an uncommon commitment to students, players and the entire USF Family.
  Amir Abdur-Rahim grew up in Marietta, Georgia, and held his first 
head coach position at Kennesaw State University from 2019 before 
joining the Bulls family in 2023. Coach

[[Page E1107]]

Abdur-Rahim was credited with turning KSU's program into a record-
breaking success. But it is not his incredible professional 
accomplishments that made him the great man he was. He was a servant 
leader with genuine care for those around him. He transformed not only 
records but also the hearts and minds of entire communities.
  Coach Abdur-Rahim was immediately embraced by the USF family. When 
USF Vice President for Athletics Michael Kelly described Coach's impact 
on the team and university, Kelly said, ``he connected this whole 
community.`` Around campus, Coach was known for picking up students' 
coffee tabs, personally greeting camped-out fans in front of the 
Yuengling Center, and dancing with the school band after home game 
wins. His spirit and joy were felt by the entire student body.
  To his players, he was so much more. At the press conference 
following the team's historic first regular season conference 
championship, Coach Abdur-Rahim said, ``It's just great to see growth 
in young people, man, when they start to see themselves as more than 
just basketball players, when they start to see themselves as men.''
  Following last year's historic season, Coach was unanimously voted 
American Athletic Conference Coach of the Year. Yet, he felt an 
individual accolade was unfitting. Coach changed the trophy engraving 
from ``Coach of the Year'' to ``Staff of the Year.'' In all he did, 
Coach Abdur-Rahim found a way to serve those around him.
  Above all else, Coach Abdur-Rahim loved his family. He is survived by 
his wife, Arianne, and their three children, Laila, Lana and Aydin. His 
legacy will live on through his family and all who had the privilege of 
sharing time with him on the basketball court.
  Coach Abdur-Rahim used to say: ``No record is going to tell you what 
impact you have on people around you.'' The impact Coach had on the USF 
community is immeasurable, and his optimism, devotion and servant 
leadership will continue to inspire us all.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of my neighbors across Tampa Bay, I sincerely 
thank Amir Abdur-Rahim for the joy and life he infused into our 
community. We are all eternally grateful.
```
