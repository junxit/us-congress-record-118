# COMMEMORATING THE 43RD ANNIVERSARY OF CARECEN

**Speaking:** Norton, Eleanor Holmes (N000147) [D-DC]

```
             COMMEMORATING THE 43RD ANNIVERSARY OF CARECEN

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. ELEANOR HOLMES NORTON

                        of district of columbia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Ms. NORTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Central American 
Resource Center (CARECEN) Established in the District of Columbia as 
the Central American Refugee Center, CARECEN is celebrating its 43rd 
anniversary this month.
  CARECEN was founded in 1981 to provide legal assistance to Central 
American refugees. Today, in addition to providing such assistance, 
CARECEN provides several services to D.C.'s Latino community, including 
citizenship classes, tenant organizing and legal services. CARECEN also 
advocates for refugees and immigrants from Central America on the 
federal level. Since the 1990s, CARECEN has advocated for granting 
Temporary Protected Status for nationals from Latin American countries.
  I ask the House of Representatives to join me in recognizing CARECEN 
for its contributions to D.C. and D.C.'s Latino community over the last 
43 years.
```
