# RECOGNIZING THE 101ST ANNIVERSARY OF TURKISH REPUBLIC DAY AND THE TURKISH AMERICAN CULTURAL ASSOCIATION OF MICHIGAN

**Speaking:** Dingell, Debbie (D000624) [D-MI]

```
   RECOGNIZING THE 101ST ANNIVERSARY OF TURKISH REPUBLIC DAY AND THE 
           TURKISH AMERICAN CULTURAL ASSOCIATION OF MICHIGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DEBBIE DINGELL

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mrs. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 101st 
Anniversary of Turkish Republic Day and the contributions of the 
Turkish American Cultural Association of Michigan (TACAM) in 
strengthening the relationship between Turkiye and the United States. 
Their steadfast commitment to the improvement of the humanitarian and 
cultural welfare of the Turkish community both locally and 
internationally is worthy of commendation.
  The founding of the Turkish Republic on October 29, 1923, symbolizes 
the immense resilience and determination of the Turkish people. Mustafa 
Kemal Ataturk and his beliefs in the importance of culture and humanity 
through the establishment of a progressive, democratic state are 
representative of the core values of modern-day Turkiye. On this 101st 
Anniversary of Turkiye Republic Day, we celebrate the continued peace 
and security of the region and the unified ties between Turkiye and the 
United States.
  Since 1972, the Turkish American Cultural Association of Michigan has 
been dedicated to upholding the values of Mustafa Kemal Ataturk in 
their work for Turkish-American communities. They have been a strong 
voice for the Turkish people, fostering and executing programs that 
have advanced economic, social, educational, and general welfare for 
Turkish communities. The leadership they have brought to the state of 
Michigan, especially its 6th Congressional District, has enriched our 
communities with Turkish culture, making Michigan a more inclusive and 
thriving state.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me today in honoring the 
101st Anniversary of Turkish Republic Day and the contributions of the 
Turkish American Cultural Association of Michigan in Turkish-American 
communities. The strong ties between Turkiye and the United States are 
made possible by the remarkable work of Turkish-American organizations 
like TACAM and Michigan is proud to be a beneficiary of their efforts. 
I am excited to see the continued accomplishments of TACAM and Turkish-
Americans in Michigan and abroad.
```
