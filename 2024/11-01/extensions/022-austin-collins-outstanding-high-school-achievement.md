# AUSTIN COLLINS' OUTSTANDING HIGH SCHOOL ACHIEVEMENT

**Speaking:** Lopez, Greg (L000604) [R-CO]

```
          AUSTIN COLLINS' OUTSTANDING HIGH SCHOOL ACHIEVEMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG LOPEZ

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 1, 2024

  Mr. LOPEZ. Mr. Speaker, Colorado student Austin Collins from Wray, 
Colorado is a testament and great example of what driven, community 
focused students can offer this country. His achievement throughout his 
high school career in sports, academia, and extra-curricular activities 
have benefited not only him, but his entire community.
  Austin has served in Wray High School's National Honor Society and 
Student Government, working alongside his peers to create a

[[Page E1103]]

strong community and experience for everyone. He has also excelled 
academically, holding a 4.0 GPA and impressive SAT score.
  Athletically, Austin Collins has performed just as exceptionally, 
placing in national championships year after year in wrestling and 
earning Football First team All-Conference and All-State Honorable 
Mention.
  In addition to all of this, Austin finds the time to be a member of 
the Future Business Leaders of America and his local church. He also 
volunteers as a coach in the Wray youth flag football and wrestling 
programs and the Wray Library Book Buddies program.
  It is my distinct privilege as the United States Representative of 
the 4th District of Colorado to commend Austin for his hard work and 
dedication to excellence in every of facet of his life. His service to 
the community of Wray, Colorado is an inspiration of what it means to 
be leader, and I encourage him to continue pursuing excellence in his 
future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
