# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 10081

**Measures:** H.R. 10081

```
           By Mr. STRONG:
       H.R. 10081.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To include unlawful voting in the definition of aggravated 
     felonies under the Immigration and Nationality Act in order 
     to expedite the removal of aliens convicted of such crimes.
```
