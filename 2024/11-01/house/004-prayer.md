# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend Timothy AR Cole, Christ Church, Georgetown, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, our Heavenly Father, we hold up to You all those who 
serve in this Chamber and who seek the well-being, order, peace, and 
safety of this Nation. Grant them wisdom in the making of our laws, 
grace to seek the unity of our people, and the inspiration of Your 
great spirit to hold the care of the people they serve in all they say 
and do.
  As we approach another election, we pray for all who run and all who 
vote. We ask that those who will serve in this place be guided by Your 
justice, Your will for this country, and Your love for all Your 
children, that for all the differences held here in good faith, this 
House may be led by the deeper unity of Your purpose for all the people 
of this land, through Jesus Christ, our Lord.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
