# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 100, H.R. 4206, H.R. 4551, H.R. 7409, H.R. 7437, H.R. 8340, H.R. 8446

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 7409. A 
     bill to amend the Geothermal Steam Act of 1970 to waive the 
     requirement for a Federal drilling permit for certain 
     activities, to exempt certain activities from the 
     requirements of the National Environmental Policy Act of 
     1969, and for other purposes (Rept. 118-722). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.

[[Page H5907]]

  

       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 8446. A 
     bill to amend the Energy Act of 2020 to include critical 
     materials in the definition of critical mineral, and for 
     other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 118-723). Referred 
     to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the 
     Union.
       Mr. McHENRY: Committee on Financial Services. H.R. 4551. A 
     bill to prohibit the Securities and Exchange Commission from 
     requiring that personally identifiable information be 
     collected under consolidated audit trail reporting 
     requirements, and for other purposes; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 118-724). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the state of the Union.
       Mr. McHENRY: Committee on Financial Services. H.R. 4206. A 
     bill to amend the Financial Stability Act of 2010 to require 
     covered financial institutions to include elements of 
     accumulated other comprehensive income when calculating 
     capital for purposes of meeting capital requirements, and for 
     other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 118-725). Referred 
     to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the 
     Union.
       Mr. McHENRY: Committee on Financial Services. H.R. 8340. A 
     bill to amend section 3(b)(4) of the United States Housing 
     Act of 1937 to exclude certain disability benefits from 
     income for the purposes of determining eligibility for the 
     supported housing program under section 8(o)(19), and for 
     other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 118-726). Referred 
     to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the 
     Union.
       Mr. McHENRY: Committee on Financial Services. House Joint 
     Resolution 100. Resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Securities and Exchange 
     Commission relating to ``Cybersecurity Risk Management, 
     Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure'' (Rept. 118-
     727). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. McHENRY: Committee on Financial Services. H.R. 7437. A 
     bill to require certain supervisory agencies to assess their 
     technological vulnerabilities, and for other purposes; with 
     an amendment (Rept. 118-728). Referred to the Committee of 
     the Whole House on the state of the Union.
```
