# HONORING MAYOR KURT HUNSTIGER ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Emmer, Tom (E000294) [R-MN]

```
            HONORING MAYOR KURT HUNSTIGER ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOM EMMER

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 5, 2024

  Mr. EMMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 
distinguished career and dedicated community service of Sauk Rapids 
Mayor Kurt Hunstiger who recently announced his retirement from public 
office.
  Mayor Hunstiger's tenure in public service is truly commendable. Over 
the past 30 years, he has served in various positions, including 18 
years as a city council member and the last eight years as mayor. His 
leadership has been instrumental in shaping the community, as he guided 
Sauk Rapids through times of growth and change.
  Despite retiring from his day job in finance four years ago, Mayor 
Hunstiger continued to dedicate his time and energy to the betterment 
of Sauk Rapids. In a letter to the community and city staff announcing 
his retirement, Kurt expressed his gratitude for their support and 
collaboration over the years. In this next chapter he plans to spend 
more time with his family.
  Under Mayor Hunstiger's stewardship, Sauk Rapids has thrived. He 
leaves behind a legacy of fiscal responsibility and a commitment to 
community engagement. His efforts secured the city's fiscal stability, 
ensuring a strong foundation for the future.
  His dedication, vision, and hard work over the years have made the 
Sauk Rapids community a better place to live, work, and raise a family.
  We are sincerely grateful for Mayor Hunstiger's service and 
contributions to Sauk Rapids and Benton County and wish him a 
fulfilling and joyful retirement.

                          ____________________
```
