# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 163

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 163

```
           By Mr. BALDERSON:
       H.J. Res. 163.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To disapprove of the EPA's rule ``New Source Performance 
     Standards for Greenhouse Gas Emissions From New, Modified, 
     and Reconstructed Fossil Fuel-Fired Electric Generating 
     Units; Emission Guidelines for Greenhouse Gas Emissions From 
     Existing Fossil Fuel-Fired Electric Generating Units; and 
     Repeal of the Affordable Clean Energy Rule''
```
