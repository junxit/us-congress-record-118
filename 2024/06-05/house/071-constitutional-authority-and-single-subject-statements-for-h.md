# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8647

**Measures:** H.R. 8647

```
           By Mr. VAN ORDEN:
       H.R. 8647.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to authorize the 
     Secretary of Veterans Affairs to take certain actions to 
     prevent or resolve a default of a housing loan guaranteed by 
     the Secretary, and for other purposes.
```
