# ADJOURNMENT FROM WEDNESDAY, JUNE 5, 2024, TO FRIDAY, JUNE 7, 2024; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 7, 2024, TO TUESDAY, JUNE 11, 2024

**Speaking:** Mann, Tracey (M000871) [R-KS]

```
ADJOURNMENT FROM WEDNESDAY, JUNE 5, 2024, TO FRIDAY, JUNE 7, 2024; AND 
    ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JUNE 7, 2024, TO TUESDAY, JUNE 11, 2024

  Mr. MANN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Friday, June 7, 2024, 
and further when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet on 
Tuesday, June 11, 2024, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour 
debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Kansas?
  There was no objection.
```
