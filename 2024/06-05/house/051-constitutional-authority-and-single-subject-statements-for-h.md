# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8627

**Measures:** H.R. 8627

```
           By Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina:
       H.R. 8627.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title 38, United States Code, to modify certain 
     requirements relating to the recovery by the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs of overpayments made to eligible persons or 
     veterans relating to educational assistance
```
