# HONORING WILLIAM "BILL" HERBERT HINDS III

**Speaking:** Kim, Young (K000397) [R-CA]

```
                              {time}  1100
              HONORING WILLIAM ``BILL'' HERBERT HINDS III

  (Mrs. KIM of California asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. KIM of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor William ``Bill'' 
Herbert Hinds III, a longtime Saddleback Valley Unified School District 
educational leader from Trabuco Canyon, who recently passed away at 64 
years young.
  Hinds served Saddleback Valley USD for 29 years as a middle school 
history teacher, activity director, and assistant principal at Mission 
Viejo High School, a middle school principal at Alisos Intermediate, 
and principal of Laguna Hills High School for the past 9 years. He was 
also a Saddleback Valley USD graduate himself, graduating from Mission 
Viejo High School in 1988.
  I know Principal Hinds was a pillar of our Saddleback Valley USD 
community, and I join in mourning his passing and in recognizing the 
tremendous impact he has made in the lives of so many students.
  My heart is with his children, Nathan and Alice, and his loved ones 
during this time.

                          ____________________
```
