# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8628

**Measures:** H.R. 8628

```
           By Mr. DONALDS:
       H.R. 8628.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Art 1, Sec 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit the Director of the Bureau of Consumer 
     Financial Protection from issuing new rules or guidance 
     relating to buy now pay later services until the Bureau and 
     the Comptroller General of the United States each conduct a 
     study on such services, and for other purposes.
```
