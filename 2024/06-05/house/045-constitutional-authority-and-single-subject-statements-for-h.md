# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8621

**Measures:** H.R. 8621

```
           By Mr. BACON:
       H.R. 8621.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend part B of title IV of the Social Security Act to 
     support State implementation of Federal standards established 
     under the Indian Child Welfare Act of 1978.
```
