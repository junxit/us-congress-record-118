# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9602

**Measures:** H.R. 9602

```
           By Mr. GARAMENDI:
       H.R. 9602.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Cooperative Forestry Assistance Act of 1978 to 
     authorize States to approve certain organizations to acquire, 
     hold, and manage conservation easements under the Forest 
     Legacy Program.
```
