# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9601

**Measures:** H.R. 9601

```
           By Mr. D'ESPOSITO:
       H.R. 9601.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To prohibit third-party restaurant reservation services 
     from offering or arranging unauthorized reservations for food 
     service establishments, and for other purposes.
```
