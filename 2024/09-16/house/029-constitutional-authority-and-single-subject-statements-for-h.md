# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9606

**Measures:** H.R. 9606

```
           By Mr. LANDSMAN:
       H.R. 9606.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 1 of the United States Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend the Federal Fire Prevention and Control Act of 
     1974 to expand the eligibility of certain recipients for 
     SAFER grants, and for other purposes.
```
