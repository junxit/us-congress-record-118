# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Today's opening prayer will be offered by 
Pastor Laura Viau of First & Central Presbyterian Church of Wilmington, 
DE.
  The guest Chaplain offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy One, Maker of all places and things, You know more than any of 
us that we humans are but one part of this complex, interconnected 
creation, and our lives are but a moment in the vast eternity You 
govern.
  As these leaders gather to serve the people today, grant them resolve 
to lead with humility, courage to speak for the voiceless, and wisdom 
to see beyond tomorrow.
  May justice roll and love abide. Amen.
```
