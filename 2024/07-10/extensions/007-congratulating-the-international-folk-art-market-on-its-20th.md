# CONGRATULATING THE INTERNATIONAL FOLK ART MARKET ON ITS 20TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Leger Fernandez, Teresa (L000273) [D-NM]

```
     CONGRATULATING THE INTERNATIONAL FOLK ART MARKET ON ITS 20TH 
                              ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. TERESA LEGER FERNANDEZ

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 10, 2024

  Ms. LEGER FERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, we all inhabit one planet with 
vibrant, colorful rich traditions of folk art. I rise to congratulate 
the International Folk Art Market, which celebrates its 20th 
anniversary this year for bringing to Santa Fe some of the best of that 
folk art.
  This art gives us a glimpse into the history and cultural traditions 
across our continents, but also is an economic engine for the families 
and communities who create these masterpieces.
  Since 2004, over a thousand volunteers have shared in this vision as 
they join forces to make this major economic driver for the artists and 
the Santa Fe area both possible. For many artists, the revenue feeds 
their family, and for the collective, the profits go to digging water 
wells for the village. The first market hosted 60 participating folk 
artists, and this year my hometown will host 160 artists from over 50 
countries. It is one of the largest folk art markets in the world.
  This next weekend, the Market will transform the Santa Fe Historic 
Rail Yard into an international village, with global music, ethnic 
foods, speakers, and more. The art will come alive in stories, tastes, 
and dance moves.
  Humankind is strongest and kindest when we can celebrate the diverse 
traditions found in our country, and in our world. Happy Birthday 
(Feliz Cumpleanos) to International Folk Art Market on their 20th year 
of bridging cultures.

                          ____________________
```
