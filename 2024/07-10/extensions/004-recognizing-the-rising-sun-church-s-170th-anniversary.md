# RECOGNIZING THE RISING SUN CHURCH'S 170TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Nunn, Zachary (N000193) [R-IA]

```
         RECOGNIZING THE RISING SUN CHURCH'S 170TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ZACHARY NUNN

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 10, 2024

  Mr. NUNN of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 170th 
anniversary of the Rising Sun Church of Christ in Pleasant Hill, Iowa.
  Over the past 170 years, the Rising Sun Church of Christ has been a 
cornerstone of the community, providing spiritual guidance, support, 
and a sense of belonging to countless individuals and families. Their 
mission-based approach centers around building family legacies rooted 
in Christ's truth emphasizing unity in essentials, liberty in non-
essentials, and love in all things. Over the years, the church has 
grown and evolved, embracing new generations while honoring its rich 
history and traditions. This impressive milestone is a testament to the 
enduring faith and dedication of the church's congregation and 
leadership.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking the Rising Sun Church of 
Christ for their commitment to faith, family, and their community and 
congratulate them on this momentous occasion.

                          ____________________
```
