**Speaking:** Davis, Danny K. (D000096) [D-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANNY K. DAVIS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 10, 2024

  Mr. DAVIS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to cast votes on 
July 8th and 9th as I needed to attend the funeral services of a close 
friend.
  Had I been present for the following roll call votes, I would have 
voted Aye on:
  Roll Call No. 336, July 8, 2024: On Motion to Suspend the Rules and 
Pass, as Amended, H.R. 897, Alabama Underwater Forest National Marine 
Sanctuary and Protection Act;
  Roll Call No. 337, July 8, 2024: On Motion to Suspend the Rules and 
Pass H.R. 5441, Long Island Sound Restoration and Stewardship 
Reauthorization Act;
  Roll Call No. 340, July 9, 2024: On Motion to Recommit--H.R. 7637, 
Refrigerator Freedom Act; and
  Roll Call No. 342, July 9, 2024: On Motion to Recommit--H.R. 7700, 
Stop Unaffordable Dishwasher Standards Act.
  Had I been present for the following roll call votes, I would have 
voted No on:
  Roll Call No. 338, July 9, 2024: On Ordering the Previous Question H. 
Res. 1341, Providing for consideration of the bills H.R. 8281, 
Safeguard American Voter Eligibility Act; H.R. 8772, Legislative Branch 
Appropriations Act, 2025; H.R. 7700, Stop Unaffordable Dishwasher 
Standards Act; H.R. 7637, Refrigerator Freedom Act; and the joint 
resolution H.J. Res. 165;
  Roll Call No. 339, July 9, 2024: On Agreeing to the Resolution H. 
Res. 1341, Providing for consideration of the bills H.R. 8281, 
Safeguard American Voter Eligibility Act; H.R. 8772, Legislative Branch 
Appropriations Act, 2025; H.R. 7700, Stop Unaffordable Dishwasher 
Standards Act; H.R. 7637, Refrigerator Freedom Act; and the joint 
resolution H.J. Res. 165;
  Roll Call No. 341, July 9, 2024: On Passage--H.R. 7637, Refrigerator 
Freedom Act; and
  Roll Call No. 343, July 9, 2024: On Passage--H.R. 7700, Stop 
Unaffordable Dishwasher Standards Act.
```
