# RECOGNIZING PAULINE PEARSON'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Nehls, Troy E. (N000026) [R-TX]

```
              RECOGNIZING PAULINE PEARSON'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. TROY E. NEHLS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 10, 2024

  Mr. NEHLS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 100th birthday 
of my constituent Pauline Pearson of Richmond, Texas. Being a pioneer 
resident of the Pecan Grove area for 28 years, it is my honor to wish 
her a Happy Birthday on this momentous milestone.
  She was born a twin sister in Rosebud, North Dakota, and has lived a 
life worthy of many memories. Having grown up working on a family farm 
and studying in a one room prairie schoolhouse in North Dakota, her 
story is truly one of the great American frontier.
  She was the proud wife to her husband, Robert, and a dear mother to 
her daughter, Sharon. They traveled across the country for work in 
California, and eventually moved to the Great State of Texas in 1996. 
She has brought joy and happiness to everyone who has crossed paths 
with, and her life story is a testament to her strength, wisdom, and 
resilience.
  I want to extend my heartfelt gratitude to Ms. Pearson on behalf of 
the people of the great state of Texas. Happy 100th Birthday to 
Pauline. May God continue to bless her and her family with abundant 
health, unending happiness, and boundless love.

                          ____________________
```
