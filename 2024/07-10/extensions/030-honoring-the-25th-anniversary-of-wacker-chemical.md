# HONORING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF WACKER CHEMICAL

**Speaking:** Nunn, Zachary (N000193) [R-IA]

```
            HONORING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF WACKER CHEMICAL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ZACHARY NUNN

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 10, 2024

  Mr. NUNN of Iowa. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate 
Wacker Chemical of Eddyville, Iowa. Wacker Chemical established its 
Eddyville plant in 1999 and recently celebrated its 25th anniversary.
  Wacker Chemical has been at the forefront of cyclodextrin production 
since it was first built. The Eddyville plant plays a central role 
within our global network of Biotech and Biopharma sites.
  From Iowa alone, Wacker produces all the cyclodextrin it sells. 
Eddyville is an important pillar of the BioSolutions portfolio and 
Wacker's growth strategy.
  I extend my sincerest congratulations to Wacker Chemical on a 
tremendous 25 years of business in Iowa and honor its dedication to 
manufacturing for Iowans.
  Mr. Speaker, I invite my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Wacker Chemical of Eddyville, Iowa for its 25th anniversary. I wish 
nothing but the best as they continue their great work in Iowa.

                          ____________________
```
