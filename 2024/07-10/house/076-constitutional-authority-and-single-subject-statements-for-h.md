# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8983

**Measures:** H.R. 8983

```
           By Ms. MACE:
       H.R. 8983.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 17 of the Constitution.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To repeal portions of a regulation issued by the State 
     Superintendent of Education of the District of Columbia that 
     require child care workers to have a degree, a certificate, 
     or a minimum number of credit hours from an institution of 
     higher education.
```
