# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 8977

**Measures:** H.R. 8977

```
           By Mr. CAREY:
       H.R. 8977.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To amend title XI of the Social Security Act to require the 
     Center for Medicare and Medicaid Innovation to test an 
     emergency medical services treatment-in-place model under the 
     Medicare program.
```
