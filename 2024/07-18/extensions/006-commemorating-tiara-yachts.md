# COMMEMORATING TIARA YACHTS

**Speaking:** Huizenga, Bill (H001058) [R-MI]

```
                       COMMEMORATING TIARA YACHTS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BILL HUIZENGA

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 18, 2024

  Mr. HUIZENGA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Tiara Yachts for 50 years of operation in West Michigan. Founded in 
1974 by Mr. Leon Slikkers, this locally owned and operated boat 
manufacturer has remained committed to manufacturing the highest 
quality watercraft.
  As a multi-generational, family-owned and operated business, the 
story of Tiara Yachts is a true representation of the American Dream. 
Mr. Slikkers began building boats in 1946 and founded his own boat 
company, Slickcraft, shortly after. He then sold this company to launch 
S2 Yachts, later known as Tiara Yachts, with his sons David and Robert. 
Together, they worked to revolutionize the marine industry, introducing 
the Tiara Powerboat in 1976.
  With the next generation now at the helm, Tiara Yachts has stayed 
true to the vision Mr. Slikkers first established: designing and 
delivering a legacy of quality, innovation, and unrivaled customer 
experiences that withstand the test of time and continue to evolve with 
their customers' desires. It is evident that this family organization 
remains committed to producing the highest quality, American-made boats 
that provide a lifetime of joy and entertainment on the water.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in congratulating Tiara Yachts as they 
celebrate their 50th Anniversary. I wish them all the best as they 
continue their legacy of success.

                          ____________________
```
