# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.J. Res. 190

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 190

```
           By Mr. PALMER:
       H.J. Res. 190.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To disapprove of the rule submitted by the Bureau of 
     Consumer Financial Protection relating to ``Truth in Lending 
     (Regulation Z); Use of Digital User Accounts to Access Buy 
     Now, Pay Later Loans''.
```
