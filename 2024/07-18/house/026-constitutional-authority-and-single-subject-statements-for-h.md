# Constitutional Authority and Single Subject Statements for H.R. 9066

**Measures:** H.R. 9066

```
           By Mr. GIMENEZ:
       H.R. 9066.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the Constitution stating 
     that Congress has the authority to ``make all laws which 
     shall be necessary and proper for carrying into execution the 
     foregoing powers, and all other powers vested by the 
     Constitution''.
       The single subject of this legislation is:
       To require an assessment by the Department of Homeland 
     Security regarding threats within the United States posed by 
     the Republic of Cuba, and for other purposes.
```
