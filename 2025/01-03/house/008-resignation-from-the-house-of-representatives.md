# RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

```
             RESIGNATION FROM THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
resignation from the House of Representatives:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, January 1, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: As I prepare to be sworn-in as 
     Governor of Puerto Rico, I hereby make official my 
     resignation from the U.S. House of Representatives, effective 
     11:59 p.m. on Wednesday, January 1, 2025. Enclosed is the 
     letter I transmitted to Governor Pedro R. Pierluisi of Puerto 
     Rico.
       It has been an honor to represent the people of Puerto Rico 
     in Congress for the past eight years. It has also been a 
     privilege to serve with you and our colleagues in the House 
     during this time. I look forward to continuing working 
     together in my new role as Governor.
           Sincerely,
                                         Jenniffer Gonzalez-Colon,
     Member of Congress.
                                  ____

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, January 1, 2025.
     Hon. Pedro R. Pierluisi,
     Governor of Puerto Rico,
     San Juan, PR.
       Dear Governor Pierluisi: As I prepare to be sworn-in as the 
     next Governor of Puerto Rico, I hereby submit my resignation 
     as Resident Commissioner effective 11:59 p.m. on Wednesday, 
     January 1, 2025.
       It has been an honor and privilege to represent Puerto Rico 
     in Congress for the past eight years. I am proud of the work 
     and accomplishments my office and I were able to achieve 
     during this time, including securing historic amounts of 
     federal funding to assist with the Island's recovery process, 
     leading negotiations to avert the Medicaid Cliff that would 
     have threatened health care access for 1.5 million people, 
     introducing legislation to provide our 3.2 million American 
     citizens equal treatment under multiple federal laws and 
     programs, and advancing efforts to resolve our political 
     status dilemma.
       It will always be grateful to the people of Puerto Rico for 
     entrusting me to serve them in Congress and for now providing 
     me the opportunity to continue working on their behalf as 
     Governor.
           Sincerely,
                                         Jenniffer Gonzalez-Colon,
                                               Member of Congress.

     

                          ____________________
```
